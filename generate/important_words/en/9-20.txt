Jonathan: Son of King Saul and a close friend of David. He helps David in his flight from Saul's anger.
David: Future king of Israel and a central figure in the Old Testament. In 1 Samuel 20 he is fleeing from King Saul.
Saul: The first king of Israel, who becomes jealous of David and tries to kill him several times.
Covenant: An agreement or commitment between two parties, often with religious connotations. Jonathan and David make a covenant of friendship and loyalty.
Bow and arrow: A weapon consisting of an arrow and a bow. Jonathan uses this as a signal to David that it is not safe to return to Saul's court.
FIELD: An open area where Jonathan and David meet to discuss their situation and plan David's escape.
Meal: An occasion when food is served and eaten. King Saul arranges a meal, and his anger over David's absence reveals his hostility toward David.

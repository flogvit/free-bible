Gibeonites: A Canaanite tribe that tricked the Israelites into making a peace treaty by pretending to come from a distant land.
Peace treaty: An agreement between two parties in which they promise not to attack each other and to live in peace.
Deceit: The use of fraud or manipulation to achieve a goal, as when the Gibeonites lied about their origin to avoid destruction.
Joshua: The leader of the people of Israel after Moses' death, who led them through the conquest of Canaan.
The Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator in Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.
Servitude: Performing work or services for someone, often without payment. The Gibeonites were forced to serve the Israelites as punishment for their deceit.
Ministers of the sanctuary: People who had responsibility for caring for the sanctuary and carrying out ritual duties. The Gibeonites were made ministers of the sanctuary as a result of their agreement with Joshua.

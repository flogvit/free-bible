Joshua: The main character in the book of Joshua and leader of the people of Israel after Moses' death. He leads the Israelites into the promised land.
The Ark: The holy chest containing the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, also known as the Ark of the Covenant. It symbolizes God's presence among the people of Israel.
Jordan: The river that the Israelites must cross to enter the promised land. Miraculously, the waters stop flowing when the priests carry the ark into the river.
Priests: The spiritual leaders of Israel who perform religious ceremonies and rituals. In Joshua 3, they play a central role in carrying the ark across the Jordan River.
The Promised Land: The area that God promised to give to the people of Israel, which Joshua and the Israelites conquer after crossing the Jordan River.
Miracle: A supernatural event that cannot be explained by natural laws. In Joshua 3, a miracle occurs when the Jordan River stops flowing so that the Israelites can cross it.

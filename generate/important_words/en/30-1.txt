Amos: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel.
Lord: Another name for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or consequences for human actions, especially those that break his commands.
Israel: An Old Testament kingdom and God's chosen people, whom Amos spoke to on behalf of God.
Sin: A violation of God's law or moral standards, leading to spiritual separation from God and possible punishment.
Nations: The surrounding countries and peoples over whom Amos prophesied judgment because of their sins and injustice.
Justice: Acting in accordance with God's will and moral standards, including treating others with respect and care.
Injustice: Actions that violate God's moral standards, often linked to oppression and wrongdoing toward others.

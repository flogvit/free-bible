Adultery: An act of breaking a promise or commitment, especially in a religious context, such as when the people of Israel turn away from God.
Punishment: The consequences or response resulting from breaking God's commands and principles, which may involve suffering or loss.
Love: A deep feeling of devotion and care that God has for his people, despite their adultery and disobedience.
Repentance: The feeling of remorse and the desire to correct one's mistakes, leading to a restoration of the relationship between God and humanity.
Israel: The ancient kingdom and people whom God chose to be his special possession and with whom he made a covenant; Hosea's prophecies are directed toward Israel.
Ephraim: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, often used as a representation for all of Israel in the book of Hosea.
Prophet: A person who receives and conveys divine messages and warnings from God, such as Hosea in this case.

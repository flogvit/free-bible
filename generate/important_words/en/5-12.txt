Place: A specific area or location where God commands that offerings and worship should take place.
Offer: An act in which something is given to God as a gift, thanksgiving, or to seek his favor.
Blood: Symbolizes life in the Bible and is an important part of many sacrificial acts.
Idols: False gods or objects that people worship instead of the true God.
Destroy: To remove completely, often used in connection with eliminating idolatry and evil practices.
Inheritance: Land or property given by God to the tribes of Israel as part of his promise.

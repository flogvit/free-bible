Joseph: One of Jacob's twelve sons, who was sold into slavery by his brothers and ended up in Egypt. In Genesis 40 he interprets dreams for fellow prisoners.  
Dreams: Images, feelings, and thoughts that arise in the mind during sleep. Joseph had the ability to interpret the meaning of dreams.  
Cupbearer: A person who serves drinks, especially wine, to a king or high official. The cupbearer in Genesis 40 had a dream that Joseph interpreted.  
Baker: A person who makes bread and other baked goods. The baker in Genesis 40 also had a dream that Joseph interpreted.  
Prison: A place where people are held in custody, often as punishment for unlawful acts. Joseph was in prison with the cupbearer and the baker when he interpreted their dreams.  
Interpret: To explain or translate something, in this case dreams, from a symbolic or unclear language into a clear and understandable message.  
Pharaoh's servants: People who worked for Pharaoh, the supreme leader of ancient Egypt. Both the cupbearer and the baker were servants of Pharaoh.  
Three days: The time period between Joseph's interpretation of the dreams and the fulfillment of his interpretations. The cupbearer was restored to his position, while the baker was executed.

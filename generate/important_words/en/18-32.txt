Elihu: A young man who criticizes Job's three friends and defends God's justice in Job's trials.
Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting in accordance with divine laws and ethical standards.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge, experience, and understanding in an effective and wise manner.
Criticism: Expressing disagreement or pointing out faults and shortcomings in a person's actions, attitudes, or arguments.
Patience: The ability to endure difficulties, suffering, or delays without becoming irritated or upset.
Trials: Difficult situations or challenges that test a person's faith, patience, and character.
Fear of God: A reverent awe and respect for God, leading to obedience to His commands and will.

Nebuchadnezzar: The king of Babylon who erected a golden statue and demanded that everyone worship it.
Statue: A large, human-made sculpture representing a person, god, or other being; in this case, a golden statue of Nebuchadnezzar.
Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego: Three Jewish men who refused to worship the golden statue and were thrown into a blazing furnace as punishment.
Blazing furnace: A very hot oven used for melting metals or making pottery, here employed as an instrument of torture against those who disobeyed the king's command.
Angel: A supernatural being who serves God and carries out his will. In this story, an angel protects Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego from the fire in the furnace.
Miracle: An event that cannot be explained by natural laws and is therefore attributed to divine intervention. In this case, the miracle is that the three men survive the furnace unharmed.
Worship: To honor and show reverence for a deity, often through rituals, prayer, and praise.

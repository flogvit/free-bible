Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience reasonably and effectively in various situations.
Understanding: The ability to grasp and interpret the meaning or significance of something, including ideas and concepts.
Justice: The principle of acting fairly, impartially, and in accordance with moral and ethical standards.
Human being: A person, especially in relation to their inherent qualities, abilities, and limitations as part of God's creation.
Life: The biological process that characterizes living organisms, as well as the existential experience of being alive and conscious.
Elder: A person with significant life experience, wisdom, and authority, often respected and sought for advice and guidance.
Fear of God: A deep reverence, respect, and devotion toward God, often manifested through obedience and adherence to God's will and commands.

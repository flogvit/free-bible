Crucifixion: An execution method where a person is nailed or bound to a cross, often used in Roman times and mentioned as a prophecy about Jesus in Psalm 22.
Helpless: A feeling of being without strength or ability to change a situation, expressed by the psalmist when he prays to God for help.
Despised: To be looked down upon or treated with disgust, as the psalmist describes his own experience among people.
Deliver: To save or rescue someone from a difficult situation, as the psalmist asks God to do.
Praise: A song or poem that expresses worship and gratitude to God, which the psalmist encourages believers to join in.
Tribe: A group of people connected through common ancestors, culture, or language, mentioned in Psalm 22 as part of God's people.
Fear: A feeling of worry, anxiety, or reverence, which can be directed toward God or situations in life.

Joseph: One of Jacob's twelve sons and the main character in Genesis 44, who served as governor in Egypt.
Silver Cup: A valuable drinking vessel that Joseph ordered to be placed in Benjamin's sack to test his brothers.
Brothers: Joseph's eleven brothers, the sons of Jacob, who came to Egypt to buy grain during a famine.
Benjamin: Joseph's youngest brother, whom Joseph wanted to keep with him in Egypt.
Jacob: The patriarch and father of Joseph and his brothers, also known as Israel.
Egypt: The land where Joseph became governor after being sold into slavery by his brothers.
Grain: Food that the brothers came to Egypt to buy because of the famine in Canaan.
Sack: The container the brothers used to carry the grain they bought in Egypt, and where the silver cup was hidden.
Famine: A severe shortage of food that struck Canaan and forced Jacob's family to seek help in Egypt.

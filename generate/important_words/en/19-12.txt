Lord: A title often used for God in the Old Testament, meaning the Almighty and Sovereign One.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, death, and destruction, and giving them eternal life.
Prayer: Communication with God, usually through words, where people express gratitude, concerns, desires, and needs.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose, threaten, or harm someone, often used to describe evil powers against the righteous.
Righteous: People who follow God's will and live a moral and ethical life in accordance with His commands and teaching.
Refuge: A safe place or protection from dangers and threats, often used metaphorically to describe God's protection and care.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and act wisely, based on insight, experience, and knowledge. In the book of Psalms, wisdom is linked to following God's way.
Trust: Belief that someone or something is reliable and will keep promises, especially belief that God will fulfill His promises and remain faithful.

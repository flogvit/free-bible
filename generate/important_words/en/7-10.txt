Tola: A judge in Israel who led the nation for 23 years and helped maintain peace and justice.
Ja'ir: Another judge in Israel who ruled for 22 years and sought to promote justice and stability in the land.
Ammonites: An ancient Semitic people group who were enemies of Israel and often came into conflict with them.
Philistines: Another hostile people group against Israel, known for their military strength and conflicts with the Israelites.
Ba'al: A Canaanite god whom the Israelites began to worship, leading them away from their own God.
Gilead: An area east of the Jordan River inhabited by the Israelites that played an important role in the stories in the book of Judges.
Repentance: Sorrow and regret over past actions, especially regarding turning away from God and worshipping other gods.
Jephthah: A judge and warrior chosen to lead Israel in battle against the Ammonites, who made a vow to God to sacrifice the first person he met upon returning home after victory.
Vow: A commitment or agreement between two parties, in this case between Jephthah and God, involving mutual understanding and expectation of keeping the promise.

Prophecy: Prediction or revelation from God about future events or divine will.
Man of God: A person chosen and led by God to carry out His will and convey His message.
Altar: A holy place, often a structure or table, where offerings are presented to God as part of religious worship.
Jeroboam: The first king of the northern kingdom of Israel after the kingdom was divided into two. He introduced idolatry and led the people away from the proper worship of God.
Levite: A member of the tribe of Levi who had special religious duties and responsibilities in the service of the Israelites, including work in the temple.
False prophet: A person who falsely claims to speak on behalf of God and delivers false prophecies or messages.
Obedience: To obey God's commands and instructions, which is essential for maintaining a close relationship with Him and receiving His blessings.
Punishment: The consequences or reactions resulting from disobedience toward God, often serving as a warning or correction to bring people back to righteous behavior.

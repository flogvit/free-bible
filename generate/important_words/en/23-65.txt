New heaven: A renewed and transformed creation, free from sin and suffering, which God will establish in the future.
New earth: The renewed and restored earth, where righteousness and peace will reign, and God's people will dwell with him.
Seek the Lord: To seek God's presence and guidance through prayer, worship, and obedience to his commands.
Joy: A feeling of great gladness and happiness that comes from being in God's presence and experiencing his blessings.
Bless: To give divine favor and protection; God's positive intervention in people's lives.
Curse: A negative consequence or punishment resulting from sin and disobedience toward God.
Blot out: To remove completely and entirely; in this context, it means that God will eliminate all sin and evil.
Redeem: To buy back or set free from a form of slavery or oppression; here it refers to God's act of saving his people from sin and death.

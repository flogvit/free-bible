Canaan: An area in the Middle East that, according to the Bible, was promised by God to the people of Israel. The region includes parts of present-day Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and Jordan.
Boundaries: The geographical lines defining the extent of Canaan as described in Numbers 34. These boundaries were meant to mark the promised land for the people of Israel.
Inheritance: In this context, inheritance refers to the land God promised to the people of Israel as their possession and homeland. The inheritance also symbolizes God's promise and covenant with His people.
Tribes: The twelve tribes of Israel, each representing one of Jacob's sons. The tribes received their respective portions of the promised land based on instructions given in Numbers.
Eleazar: The high priest and Moses' nephew. He played a key role in leading the people of Israel after Moses' death and helped distribute the land among the twelve tribes.
Joshua: A central figure in the Old Testament and Moses' successor as leader of the people of Israel. He led them into the promised land and was responsible for distributing the land among the tribes.

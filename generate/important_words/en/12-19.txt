Hezekiah: King of Judah who sought God's help against the Assyrians and their king Sennacherib.
Sennacherib: King of Assyria who threatened Hezekiah and Jerusalem, and mocked the God of Israel.
Jerusalem: The capital city of the kingdom of Judah, the site of Solomon's temple and the center of Jewish faith.
The prophet Isaiah: An important prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's words and promises to Hezekiah.
Prayer: A communication with God where people express their desires, gratitude, or concerns.
Temple: A holy place where people worship and offer sacrifices to God; in this case, it refers to Solomon's temple in Jerusalem.
Angel: Spiritual beings created by God to serve him and carry out his will; in this context, an angel destroyed the Assyrian army.
Assyrians: The people from the ancient empire of Assyria, known for their military power and conquests.

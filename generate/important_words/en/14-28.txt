Ahas: A king of Judah who reigned for 16 years and did what was evil in the Lord's sight.
Judah: An Old Testament kingdom, also known as the Southern Kingdom, named after Jacob's son Judah.
Syria: A country in the Middle East that attacked and conquered parts of Judah during Ahas' time.
Edom: An Old Testament kingdom south of Judah, inhabited by descendants of Esau, Jacob's brother.
Philistines: A people who lived in the area along the Mediterranean coast, southwest of Judah. They were often in conflict with the Israelites.
Worship: To show reverence and devotion to a deity or higher power.
Idols: False gods or statues that people worship instead of the true God.
The Lord: A title used to refer to God, especially in the Old Testament.
Prophet: A person who speaks on behalf of God and delivers his message to the people.
Sacrifice: An act where something is given up, often an animal, as a gift to a deity to seek grace, forgiveness, or blessing.

Fall of Man: The term describing humanity's fall from God's grace after Adam and Eve ate from the tree of knowledge.
Adam: The first human created by God, and the ancestor of all mankind.
Eve: The first woman, created by God from Adam's rib, and the matriarch of all mankind.
Tree of Knowledge: The tree in Eden's garden that God forbade Adam and Eve to eat from, as it would give them knowledge of good and evil.
Serpent: The creature who tempted Eve to eat from the tree of knowledge, thus causing the fall of man.
Curse: The punishment God imposed on humanity and the earth after the fall, including pain, death, and separation from God.
Mercy: God's grace and compassion shown toward humanity despite their sin and disobedience.

Book of Psalms 24:
Lord: A title for God that points to his dominion and authority over all things.
Earth: The planet we live on, which according to the Bible was created by God.
Established: To set up or lay the foundation for something; in this context it refers to God's act of creating the earth.
Seas: The vast bodies of water that cover most of the earth's surface.
Rivers: Natural waterways that flow from a higher level to a lower one, often toward the sea.
Jacob's: A reference to Jacob, one of the patriarchs in the Hebrew tradition and ancestor of Israel's twelve tribes.
Purity: Freedom from sin and impurity, both physical and spiritual.
Righteousness: The quality of being just and morally upright; acting in accordance with God's will and commands.
Salvation: Deliverance from sin and its consequences, obtained through faith in God and his grace.
King: A title for God that emphasizes his sovereignty and rule over the entire universe.
Glory: Respect, admiration, and recognition given to someone because of their status, achievements, or virtues.

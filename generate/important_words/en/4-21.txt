Canaan: An area in the ancient Middle East that includes parts of today's Israel, Palestine, Lebanon and Syria. It was the destination of the Israelites' journey after they left Egypt.
Israelites: A people descended from the patriarch Jacob (also called Israel). They became slaves in Egypt before God led them out under Moses' leadership.
Moses: A central figure in Judaism, Christianity and Islam. He led the Israelites out of Egypt and received the Ten Commandments from God on Mount Sinai.
The Bronze Serpent Staff: A staff with a bronze serpent that Moses made according to God's instruction. When the Israelites were bitten by poisonous snakes, they could look at this staff and be healed.
Edom: An ancient kingdom south of the Dead Sea, inhabited by the Edomites, who were descendants of Esau, Jacob's brother.
Arad: A Canaanite king who attacked the Israelites and took some of them captive.
Hormah: A city in Canaan that the Israelites destroyed as revenge for Arad's attack.

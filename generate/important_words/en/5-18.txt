Prophet: A person who receives and conveys messages from God to the people, often concerning future events or divine guidance.
High Priest: The supreme religious leader in Israel, responsible for performing ceremonies and maintaining the sanctuary.
Sacrifice: A gift or act given to God, often in the form of animals, food, or other objects, to show gratitude, ask for forgiveness, or seek blessings.
Diviner: A person who claims to be able to predict the future or reveal hidden knowledge through supernatural powers, but does not necessarily receive messages directly from God.
Necromancer: A person who seeks to communicate with the dead in order to gain information, guidance, or influence living people and events.
Excommunication: An act in which a person or group is expelled or excluded from a community, often due to violations of religious rules or norms.

Jethro: Moses' father-in-law, who was a priest in Midian.
Moses: Led the Israelites out of Egypt and received the Ten Commandments from God.
Israelites: A people descended from the patriarch Israel (Jacob) and freed from slavery in Egypt.
Egypt: The land where the Israelites were slaves before being led out by Moses.
God-fearing: Reverence and respect for God and His commands.
Advisor: A person who provides guidance, counsel, or information to another.
Laws: Rules and regulations given by God to people, especially the Ten Commandments.
Judge: A person who settles disputes and justly enforces the laws.
Delegation: The process of transferring responsibility or tasks to others to ease the workload.
Responsibility: The duty or obligation to act in a certain way or carry out specific tasks.

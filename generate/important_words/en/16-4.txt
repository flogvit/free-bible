The Wall: The structure that was built around Jerusalem to protect the city from enemies and invasions.
Sanballat: An enemy of Nehemiah and the Jews, who tried to prevent the rebuilding of the wall in Jerusalem.
Tobiah: Another enemy of Nehemiah and the Jews, who worked with Sanballat to stop the rebuilding of the wall.
Work: The effort and activities undertaken by Nehemiah and the Jews to rebuild the wall in Jerusalem.
Opposition: Obstacles and challenges that Nehemiah and the Jews faced during the rebuilding of the wall.
Prayer: Communication with God, where Nehemiah and the Jews asked for help and guidance during the rebuilding.
Watchfulness: The security measures put into place by Nehemiah and the Jews to protect the workers and the project from hostile attacks.
Unity: The solidarity and cooperation among Nehemiah and the Jews to complete the rebuilding of the wall despite the opposition they faced.

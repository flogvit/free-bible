Isaiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who proclaimed God's message to the people of Israel.
Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people, also a geographical area in the Middle East.
Jacob: Patriarch of the twelve tribes of Israel, son of Isaac and grandson of Abraham.
Servant: A person who serves and obeys God; in this context it refers to Israel as God's servant.
Redeem: To buy back or set free; here it means that God will deliver his people from their sins and suffering.
Water: Symbolizes life and blessing, often used to describe God's grace and care.
Spirit: Refers to the Spirit of God, a divine power that gives life, wisdom, and guidance.
Hope for the future: The belief that God will act for the good of his people and restore their relationship with him.
Idols: False gods or idols that people worship instead of the true God.

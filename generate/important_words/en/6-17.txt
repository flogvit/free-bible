Joshua: The name of the main character in the book of Joshua, a leader of the people of Israel after Moses' death who led them into the promised land.
Promised Land: The area that God promised to give to Abraham's descendants, including Israel's twelve tribes. This area is also known as Canaan.
Manasseh: One of Israel's twelve tribes, named after Joseph's firstborn son. Joshua 17 describes the allocation of territory to half of the tribe of Manasseh.
Ephraim: Another of Israel's twelve tribes, named after Joseph's second son. Ephraim and Manasseh are often referred to as the tribes of Joseph.
Inheritance: A portion of the land given to each of Israel's tribes, based on God's instructions and Joshua's leadership.
Cities: Settlement areas for Israel's tribes, which were assigned specific territories in the promised land.
Warriors: Men from Israel's tribes who took part in the conquest of Canaan and helped secure inheritances for their respective tribes.

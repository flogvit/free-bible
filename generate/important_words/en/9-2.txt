Hannah: Mother of Samuel and wife of Elkanah, who prayed earnestly to God for a child and promised to dedicate the child to the Lord.
Samuel: The prophet and judge in Israel, son of Hannah and Elkanah, who was dedicated to the Lord's service from childhood.
Eli: High priest and judge in Israel, who raised Samuel in the temple and received God's judgment against his own family because of his sons' wicked actions.
Hophni and Phinehas: Eli's evil sons, who abused their position as priests and acted unjustly toward the people and God.
The Lord: Another designation for God, the almighty Creator and the God of Israel, who governs history and human destiny.
Prophecy: A divine revelation or message given through a prophet, often intended to guide, warn, or correct people and nations.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and the consequences of human actions, whether reward for righteousness or punishment for evil.
Humility: An attitude of modesty and submission before God, recognizing His sovereignty and authority.
Pride: A sinful attitude of self-assertion and arrogance, which places oneself above God and other people.

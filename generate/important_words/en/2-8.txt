Plagues: Unpleasant events or sufferings that God sends to punish Egypt and get Pharaoh to release the people of Israel.
Pharaoh: The Egyptian king who holds the Israelites as slaves and refuses to let them go.
Moses: The prophet chosen by God to lead the people of Israel out of Egypt and slavery.
Aaron: Moses' brother, also a prophet, who helps Moses in his mission.
Frogs: The second plague sent by God, where Egypt is invaded by huge numbers of frogs.
Gnats: The third plague sent by God, where Egypt is infested with myriads of gnats.
Flies: The fourth plague sent by God, where Egypt is attacked by swarms of blood-sucking flies.
Confession: Pharaoh admits his sin and asks for the plague to stop, but he breaks his promise once the plague is over.

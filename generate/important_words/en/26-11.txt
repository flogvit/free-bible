Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Vision: A spiritual experience where a person sees or hears things not accessible to others, often used by God to convey messages.
The Spirit of the Lord: God's power and presence at work in the world and in people's lives.
Jerusalem: The capital city of ancient Israel, a central place for worship and political activity.
Unfaithfulness: Lack of faithfulness and loyalty, especially toward God and His commands.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human behavior, particularly regarding sin and disobedience.
Exile: The condition of being forced to leave one's homeland, often as a penalty or consequence of political or religious conflicts.
Restoration: The process of restoring something to its original state, such as when God restores the people of Israel after exile.
Heart: Symbolizes a person's inner being, including thoughts, feelings, and will. In Ezekiel 11, it refers to God's promise to give the people of Israel a new heart to follow His laws.

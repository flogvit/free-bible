Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Vision: A spiritual experience where Ezekiel sees God's glory and angels.
The Lord's Glory: God's presence and majestic radiance, shown through a shining cloud.
Cherubim: Angelic beings with four faces and wings who serve God and protect His glory.
Wheels: Mysterious objects that represent God's omnipotence and ability to be present everywhere.
Throne: A symbol of God's authority and sovereignty over all creation.
Elohim: The Hebrew name for God, often used in the Old Testament.

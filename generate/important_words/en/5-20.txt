War: An armed conflict between two or more parties, often nations or tribes.
Enemy: A person, group, or nation that is in conflict or opposition with another.
Fear: A feeling of anxiety, worry, or unease caused by a threat or danger.
Lord: A name used to refer to God, the almighty Creator, in Jewish and Christian tradition.
Victory: Success in overcoming an opponent or a challenge, especially in war.
Cities: Larger settlements where people live and work, often surrounded by defensive structures.
Peace negotiations: The process of discussing and reaching agreement to resolve conflicts and establish peace.
Trees: Large, long-lived plants with a trunk and branches that provide shade, food, and other resources.
Women: Adult female humans; often mentioned in connection with family and reproduction in biblical texts.
Children: Offspring of humans; younger individuals who have not yet reached adulthood.

Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment; highly valued in Proverbs.
Understanding: Mental sharpness and the capacity to comprehend, evaluate, and solve problems; closely linked to wisdom.
Woman: In Proverbs 7, a woman is used as a symbol of temptation and sin; a warning to avoid immoral behavior.
Instruction: Knowledge or skills acquired through experience, study, or teaching; essential for developing wisdom and understanding.
Fear: A reverent awe and respect for God, which forms the foundation of wisdom and righteous living.
Listen: To be attentive and receive advice and guidance from others, especially from God and those who possess wisdom and understanding.
Keep away: A warning to stay clear of temptations and bad influences in order to live a righteous life.
Snares: Traps or dangers that can lead to sin and destruction; Proverbs 7 warns against being caught in such situations.

Jacob: Israel's patriarch, father of twelve sons who became the ancestors of Israel's twelve tribes.
Egypt: A land in North Africa where Jacob's son Joseph was sold as a slave and later became a powerful ruler.
Famine: A shortage of food that led Jacob's family to travel to Egypt to buy grain.
The Brothers: The twelve sons of Jacob, including Joseph who was in Egypt.
Benjamin: Jacob's youngest son and Joseph's full brother; the brothers had to take him to Egypt to prove their honesty.
Simeon: One of Jacob's sons who was held as a hostage in Egypt until the brothers returned with Benjamin.
Silver: The metal that the brothers found in their sacks after their first visit to Egypt, which created fear of accusations of theft.
Feast: A meal arranged by Joseph for his brothers, which surprised them since they expected punishment.
Cup: An object that Joseph placed in Benjamin's sack to test the brothers' loyalty and honesty.

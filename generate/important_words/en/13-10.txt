Saul: Israel's first king, chosen by the prophet Samuel and later rejected by God because of disobedience.
Philistines: An ancient people who lived in Canaan and were enemies of Israel. They often fought against the Israelites and posed a threat to their land and people.
Battle: A conflict or fight between two or more parties, especially between armies. In this context, it refers to the battle between the Israelites and the Philistines.
Mount Gilboa: A mountain range in Israel where the battle between Saul's army and the Philistines took place. Saul and his sons died here.
Bow: A weapon that shoots arrows using elastic energy. It is mentioned in the chapter because Saul's sons were killed by the Philistine archers.
Suicide: The act of taking one's own life. Saul committed suicide by falling on his sword to avoid being captured by the Philistines.
God's will: The divine plan or purpose that God has for the world and humanity. In this context, it shows that Saul's death was a result of his disobedience to God's will.

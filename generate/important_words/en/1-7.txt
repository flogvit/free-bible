Noah: A righteous man chosen by God to survive the flood and preserve humanity and animal life.
The Ark: A large boat built by Noah according to God's instructions, which was to house his family and a selection of animals.
The Flood: A massive deluge sent by God to destroy the earth and all life because of human wickedness.
Cleansing: The process of removing evil and impurities, in this case through the flood.
Family: Noah's closest relatives who were also saved from the flood and helped rebuild humanity.
Animals: Living creatures created by God that were to be taken aboard the ark to survive the flood and repopulate the earth.
Pairs: Two individuals of the same species, one male and one female, brought into the ark to ensure the survival of the species.
Rain: Water falling from the clouds, in this case for 40 days and 40 nights, which led to the flood.
Drought: The period after the flood when the waters receded and the earth became habitable again.

Judges: Leaders chosen by God to rescue the people of Israel from oppression and help them settle legal disputes.
Israelites: An ancient people who were descendants of the patriarch Jacob, also known as Israel. They were chosen by God to be His special people.
Canaan: A region in the Middle East that the Israelites conquered and settled after their time in Egypt and their journey through the wilderness.
Baal: A Canaanite god whom the Israelites began to worship, causing them to turn away from God.
Asherah: A Canaanite goddess of fertility whom the Israelites also began to worship alongside Baal.
Cycle: The repeating pattern in the book of Judges where the Israelites sinned, were oppressed, repented, and were saved by a judge.
Covenant: An agreement between God and the Israelites that included promises and obligations from both sides.
Repentance: The feeling of regret and sorrow over having sinned or done wrong, which often led the Israelites to seek God's forgiveness.

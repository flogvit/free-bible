Shechem: A Canaanite prince who raped Dinah, Jacob's daughter, and wanted to marry her.
Dinah: Jacob's only daughter, who was raped by Shechem and then offered as his wife.
Jacob: The patriarch in Genesis, father of Dinah and founder of Israel's twelve tribes.
Hamor: Shechem's father and ruler of the city of Shechem, who negotiated with Jacob and his sons about the marriage between Shechem and Dinah.
Simeon and Levi: Two of Jacob's sons who avenged their sister Dinah by killing all the men in the city of Shechem.
Circumcision: A religious ritual where the foreskin of the penis is removed, proposed by Jacob's sons as a condition for the marriage between Shechem and Dinah.
Deceit: The action carried out by Simeon and Levi when they used the circumcision ritual to weaken the men of Shechem and then kill them.
Rape: The forced sexual act committed by Shechem against Dinah.

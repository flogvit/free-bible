Praise: A song or poem that expresses gratitude and homage to God for his goodness and power.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with moral laws and ethical norms, especially as established by God.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and decision in human affairs, both individually and collectively.
Lord: A title used for God to emphasize his sovereignty and authority over all creation.
Horn: A symbol of strength and power, often used in the Bible to represent God's protection and might for his people.
Humble: To be modest and submissive in attitude and behavior, especially toward God and other people.
Exalt: To raise something or someone in rank, status, or honor, particularly in connection with God's actions toward his people.

The Lord's Face: God's presence and manifestation, which people can experience.
Moses: The prophet and leader of the people of Israel, who received the Ten Commandments from God and led them out of Egypt.
Descendants: A group of people related through common ancestors, often used to refer to a nation or tribe.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love, which he shows toward people despite their faults and sins.
The Tent of Meeting: A holy tent where God met with Moses and the people of Israel, and where they performed religious ceremonies and sacrifices.
The Pillar: A pillar of cloud or fire that symbolized God's presence and guidance for the people of Israel during their journey through the wilderness.
Face to face: A description of a direct and personal meeting between two parties, here between Moses and God.
Joshua: A faithful servant of Moses and later leader of the people of Israel after Moses' death.

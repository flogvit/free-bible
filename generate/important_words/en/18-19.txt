Misery: A state of great distress, suffering, or misfortune that Job experiences due to his trials.
Lord: Another name for God, referring to the Creator and supreme divine authority.
Redeemer: A person or power who saves, delivers, or rescues someone from a difficult situation. For Job, this is a reference to God as his hope for justice and salvation.
Living: In this context, it means that God is active, present, and engaged in human life and destiny.
Skin: Here used as a metaphor for Job's physical body, which has been destroyed by disease and suffering.
Eyes: Used here to describe the ability to see and recognize truth, both literally and symbolically.
Dust: A symbol of mortality and transience, representing humanity's fragile nature and ultimate existence.

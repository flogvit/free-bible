Lord: A title used to refer to God as the sovereign and almighty ruler over all.
Righteous: A person or action that aligns with moral principles, especially those derived from God's will and commandments.
Evil: The opposite of righteousness; wickedness, injustice, and immorality that contradict God's will and commandments.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, suffering, and death, and granting them eternal life.
Prayer: Communication with God, often in the form of a request for help, guidance, or gratitude.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose God's will and His people, and who can create conflict and suffering.
Hide: A place of protection and safety where one can seek refuge from dangers and threats.
Persecute: To pursue, harass, or oppress someone because of their faith, beliefs, or identity.
Humble: A person who shows respect and submission toward God and others, and does not exalt themselves above others.
Praise: To express reverence, gratitude, and admiration for God through prayer, song, and actions.

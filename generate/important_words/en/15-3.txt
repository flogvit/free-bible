Temple: A holy building dedicated to God, where people gather to pray and carry out religious ceremonies.
Rebuilding: The process of restoring and repairing something that was previously destroyed, in this case the temple in Jerusalem.
Altar: A structure or table on which offerings and gifts are presented to God during religious ceremonies.
Offering: A gift or act given to God as part of a religious ceremony, often to seek grace or forgiveness.
Praise songs: Religious songs and hymns sung to praise and honor God.
Priest: A religious leader who performs ceremonies and teaches about the faith, serving as an intermediary between God and people.
Levite: A member of the tribe of Levi in Israel, who had special religious duties and responsibilities, including service at the temple.
Feast: A religious celebration that marks a significant event or commemorates a historical occurrence within the tradition of the faith.

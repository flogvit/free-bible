Abimelech: The king of Gerar who took Sarah, Abraham's wife, not knowing she was married.
Abraham: The patriarch of the Jewish people, an important figure in the Bible and God's chosen one.
Sarah: Abraham's wife and mother of Isaac, regarded as one of the biblical matriarchs.
Prophet: A person who receives and conveys divine messages or revelations from God.
Prayer: Communication with God, often to ask for help, forgiveness, or guidance.
Heal: God's intervention to restore health, whether physical, mental, or spiritual.
Womb: The female reproductive organ where a fetus develops and grows before birth.
Sin: Actions or thoughts that break God's laws and morality, leading to spiritual separation from God.

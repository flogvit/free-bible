Ruben: Jacob's firstborn son and ancestor of the Reubenites, one of Israel's twelve tribes.
Gad: One of Jacob's sons and ancestor of the Gadites, another of Israel's twelve tribes.
Manasseh: One of Joseph's sons and ancestor of the Manassites, a half-tribe of Israel.
Half: The term used to describe a division into two equal parts, here referring to the tribe of Manasseh which was divided into two halves.
Warriors: People who fight in war or conflict, often soldiers or warlords.
Land: A geographic area that is bounded and controlled by a specific group of people or nation.
East: The direction where the sun rises; here it refers to areas east of the Jordan River.
Conquer: To take control of a territory or people by using military force.
Sin: An act that breaks God's laws and morality, resulting in spiritual separation from God.
Punishment: The consequence or response to a sinful act, often carried out by God to correct injustice and restore order.

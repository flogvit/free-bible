Holy: A state of being set apart from the ordinary or unclean, dedicated to God and his service.
Neighbor: The person who lives near another, often used to describe fellow human beings in general.
Honor: To show respect and reverence toward someone, especially parents and elders.
Sabbath: The seventh day of the week, on which work is to cease, and rest and worship of God are to be prioritized.
Offering: A gift or act performed to honor God, often involving animals or food items burned on an altar.
Blood: Symbolizes life, and is used in religious ceremonies to cleanse and sanctify people and places.
Harvest: Crops that are grown and gathered for food and other purposes, including grain, fruit, and vegetables.
Truthful: To be honest and trustworthy, without deceiving or lying to others.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and justly toward others, in accordance with God's laws and commands.

Bound: A commitment or promise, often used in a religious context to describe a covenant between God and humanity.
Grace: God's unmerited goodness and mercy toward people, forgiving their sins and granting them eternal life.
Faithfulness: The ability to be loyal and reliable, especially in relation to God and His promises.
Praise: Expressing gratitude, honor, and reverence for God through prayer, song, and other forms of worship.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, often used as an alternative to God's name.
Throne: Symbolizes God's authority and power over all creation.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with moral principles and God's will, maintaining a high standard of conduct and character.
Justice: The principle of acting fairly and rightly, both toward God and other people.
Strength: God's power and ability to control and influence the world and human lives.
Heaven: The place where God dwells and rules over the universe, often associated with eternal life and salvation.

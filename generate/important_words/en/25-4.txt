Destruction: The process of being ruined or completely destroyed, often used concerning cities and structures.
Suffering: Physical or mental pain or distress, especially over a long period.

Enemies: Persons or groups who are in conflict or war with each other, often used concerning nations or peoples.
Punishment: An action or consequence imposed on someone for a crime or wrongdoing, usually intended to correct or prevent future bad behavior.
Mercy: Showing compassion and grace toward others, especially those who suffer or are in distress.

Elihu: A young man who speaks to Job's friends and criticizes their viewpoints, while defending God's justice.
Justice: A moral principle that involves acting in accordance with divine laws and ethical norms.
Wisdom: The ability to think and act using knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Fear of God: An awe and reverence for God that leads to obedience and devotion.
Punishment: The consequences or response from God toward human sin and disobedience against His will.
Legal case: A metaphor for a situation in which God evaluates human actions and determines their fate.
Power: God's supreme ability and authority over the universe and all that exists.

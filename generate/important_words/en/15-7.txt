Ezra: A Jewish scribe and priest who led the people of Israel back from exile in Babylon.
Artaxerxes: The Persian king who gave Ezra permission to lead the Jews back to Jerusalem and restore the temple.
Temple: A holy place where the Jews worshiped God and offered sacrifices. The first temple was destroyed by the Babylonians, and Ezra was commissioned to rebuild it.
The Law: God's instructions and commands to the people of Israel, including the Ten Commandments and other rules for how they should live and worship God.
Scribe: A person who studied and interpreted the holy scriptures (the Torah) and taught the people God's laws.
Priest: A religious leader who performed ceremonies and sacrifices in the temple on behalf of the people.
Restoration: The process of bringing something back to its original state, which in this case is the rebuilding of the temple and the reestablishment of proper worship of God.

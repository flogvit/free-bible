Nebuchadnezzar: The king of Babylon who had a dream that the prophet Daniel interpreted for him.
Dream: A sequence of images, ideas, feelings, and sensations that arise in the mind during sleep.
Tree: In Nebuchadnezzar's dream there was a great tree that symbolized his kingdom and power.
Watcher of Heaven: An angel or divine messenger who announced judgment against Nebuchadnezzar in the dream.
Seven times: A period referring to seven years, during which Nebuchadnezzar lost his kingdom and became like an animal.
Humility: A trait that involves recognizing one's own limitations and not being arrogant or proud.
Restoration: The process by which Nebuchadnezzar regained his kingdom after being humbled and acknowledging God's sovereignty.
Sovereignty: God's ultimate control and authority over the universe and everything that happens in it.

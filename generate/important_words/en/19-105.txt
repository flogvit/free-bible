Lord: A title referring to God and his authority and power.
Covenant: An agreement or bond between God and people, often connected with promises and blessings.
Signs: Miracles or supernatural events that show God's presence and power.
Wonders: Supernatural acts performed by God that display his power and care for humanity.
Praise: To express gratitude, honor, and respect to God through prayer, song, and actions.
Covenant people: Those who have entered into a covenant with God and follow his ways and commands.
Abraham: The biblical patriarch who made a covenant with God and is the ancestor of the people of Israel.
Isaac: Son of Abraham and father of Jacob, another important patriarch in the Old Testament.
Jacob: Son of Isaac and grandson of Abraham, also known as Israel, and the ancestor of the twelve tribes of Israel.
Joseph: Jacob's beloved son, who was sold into slavery by his brothers and later became a powerful ruler in Egypt.
Egypt: An ancient kingdom in North Africa where the Israelites lived as slaves before being led out by Moses.
Salvation: God's act to save and protect his people from danger, suffering, or destruction.

Covenant: An agreement or bond between God and humanity, especially the people of Israel, involving promises and obligations from both parties.
Disobedience: The act of failing to follow God's commands and instructions, which leads to breaking the covenant with God.
Punishment: The consequences that follow disobedience toward God and breach of the covenant, often in the form of suffering, destruction, or exile.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who received God's word and delivered it to the people of Israel, warning of coming punishment if they did not repent and follow God's way.
Prayer: Communication with God, where people express their gratitude, concerns, desires, and remorse. Jeremiah often prayed to God on behalf of the people.
Repentance: The process of changing one's heart and mind to turn back to God, acknowledge one's own sins, and seek forgiveness.
Nations: Groups of people, often referred to as nations or tribes, mentioned in connection with God's plan and judgment upon them.

Tabernacle: A holy, portable tent structure used by the people of Israel for worship and to house the Ark of the Covenant.
Veil: A thick, decorative curtain that separated the Most Holy Place from the rest of the Tabernacle and symbolized the separation between God and humanity.
Pillars: Vertical supports that held the Tabernacle's structure together and gave it stability.
Coverings: Textiles and materials that covered the Tabernacle's frame and protected it against weather and wind.
Gold hooks: Small gold fasteners used to hang the veil and coverings in the Tabernacle.
Ark of the Covenant: A sacred chest containing the Ten Commandments, Moses' staff, and a jar of manna. It represented God's presence among the people of Israel.
Workers: People who carried out specific tasks and work related to the Tabernacle, including construction and maintenance.

Absalom: Son of King David and his wife Maaka, who rebelled against his father and was killed in battle.
David: The second king of Israel, known for his skill as a warrior and musician, and the author of several psalms.
Joab: David's army commander and nephew, a loyal but also brutal warrior, who killed Absalom despite David's wish to spare him.
Shimei: A man from Saul's tribe who mocked and cursed David when he fled from Absalom, but later asked for forgiveness.
Barzillai: An old and wealthy man from Gilead who helped David during his flight from Absalom and was rewarded for his loyalty.
Amasa: Absalom's army commander and David's cousin, who was appointed by David as army commander after Joab's death, but was killed by Joab.
Israel: The name of the northern kingdom consisting of ten tribes, also used as a general name for the entire nation.
Judah: The name of the southern kingdom consisting of two tribes, also used as a general name for the entire nation.

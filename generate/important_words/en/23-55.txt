Seek: To make an effort to find or attain something, in this context to seek God's presence and guidance.
Lord: A title often used for God, especially in the Old Testament, to show respect and reverence.
Call: A divine invitation or summons to an individual or group of people to carry out a specific task or follow a particular path.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love given to people, even though they do not deserve it.
Thoughts: God's plans, intentions, and purposes, which are often higher and more complex than human thoughts.
Ways: God's methods and ways of acting, which may differ from human expectations.
Rain: A metaphor used to describe God's word and how it brings life and growth, just as rain nourishes the earth.
Fruit: The result or effect of following God's ways and obeying His word, often associated with blessings and spiritual growth.

The Lord's hand: A metaphor for God's power and protection over his people.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and death, and giving them eternal life.
Praise: A form of musical worship and gratitude directed toward God.
Water: Symbolizes salvation and renewal in a spiritual context.
The Holy One of Israel: A title for God that emphasizes his presence and engagement with his chosen people, Israel.
Zion: A mountain in Jerusalem, often used as a symbol for the city and God's dwelling among his people.

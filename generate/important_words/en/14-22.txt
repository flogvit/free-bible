Ahaziah: Son of Joram and Athaliah, and king of Judah. He reigned for one year and ruled wickedly.
Athaliah: Daughter of Omri, mother to Ahaziah and queen of Judah. She killed her own grandchildren to keep power.
Jehosheba: A righteous woman and daughter of King Joram, she rescued Joash from Athaliah's massacre and hid him in the temple.
Joash: Grandson of Athaliah, saved by Jehosheba and later became king of Judah after Athaliah's death.
Priests: Religious leaders who served God and the people by performing ceremonies and upholding God's law.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, they had special religious duties and responsibilities in the temple and in service to God.
The Temple: A holy place where people came to worship God, offer sacrifices, and pray. It was the center of Jewish faith and culture.
The Ark of the Covenant: A sacred chest that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments; it symbolized God's presence and covenant with Israel.

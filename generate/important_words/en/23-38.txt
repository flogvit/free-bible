Hezekiah: King of Judah who became ill and asked God to extend his life; God answered by adding 15 years.
Illness: A poor state of health, in this case Hezekiah's fatal sickness, which led him to seek God's help.
Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God; here it refers to Isaiah, who brought God's answer to Hezekiah.
Sign: A miracle or event that confirms God's promise or intervention; in this case, the shadow on the sundial moving backward as a sign that God had heard Hezekiah's prayer.
Prayer: Communication with God where people ask for help, give thanks, or seek guidance; here it refers to Hezekiah's prayer for healing and extended life.
Healing: The process of becoming well again after illness or injury; in this context, Hezekiah's recovery following his prayer to God.
Psalm: A song or poem expressing gratitude and honor to God; Hezekiah wrote such a psalm after he was healed and granted additional years of life.

Eliphaz: One of Job's friends who attempts to explain and justify God's actions and Job's sufferings.
Suffering: A state of pain, discomfort, or distress, both physical and mental, that Job experiences during his trial.
Righteous: To be morally upright and sincere; a description of Job's character before his trials began.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a sensible way to make good decisions and judgments.
Punishment: A consequence or response to an action, often negative and intended to correct or discipline.
Fear: A feeling of respect and awe, especially toward God, which Eliphaz believes is the beginning of wisdom.

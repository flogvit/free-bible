David: The future king of Israel and the main character in 1 Samuel 30, who leads his men to recover their families and possessions from the Amalekites.
The Amalekites: A hostile tribe that attacked and plundered David's city Ziklag, taking women, children, and property as captives.
Ziklag: A town in Judah given to David by Achish, king of Gath, which was later attacked and plundered by the Amalekites.
Prophet: A person who receives divine messages and conveys them to the people. In this context, David seeks counsel from a prophet before pursuing the Amalekites.
Restore: To get back or regain something that has been lost. In this context, it refers to David's effort to recover his own and his men's families and possessions from the Amalekites.
Plunder: Goods or property taken from someone, often as a result of war or conflict. In 1 Samuel 30, David shares the plunder from the Amalekites fairly among his men and the elders of Judah.

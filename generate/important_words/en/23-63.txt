The Lord's Day: A day of God's intervention and righteous judgment over the nations.
Vengeance: God's just punishment or retribution against those who have done evil, especially against his people.
Salvation: God's act of saving or delivering his people from their enemies and sin.
Cup of Wrath: A symbol of God's anger and punishment to be poured out on the wicked and ungodly.
Treading the winepress: An image describing God's destruction of his enemies, crushing them like grapes in a winepress.
Edom: An ancient nation southeast of Israel, often used as a symbol for God's enemies in general.
The house of Jacob: An expression referring to the people of Israel, God's chosen people.
Mercy: God's grace, love, and care for his people, even when they do not deserve it.
The Spirit: Refers to the Holy Spirit, God's presence and power at work in the world and among his people.

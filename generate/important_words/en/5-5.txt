Commandment: Rules or ordinances given by God to people to regulate their behavior and faith.
Covenant: An agreement between God and humans, often linked with promises and obligations from both parties.
Moses: The prophet who led the Israelites out of Egypt and received the Ten Commandments from God on Mount Sinai.
Mount Sinai: The mountain where Moses received the Ten Commandments from God and made a covenant with Him on behalf of the people of Israel.
Sabbath: A weekly day of rest that is holy to God, where work should be avoided in order to honor God.
Honor: To show respect and submission toward God and one's parents, a central concept in the Ten Commandments.
Servant: A person who works for another, either as an employee, slave, or otherwise subject to another's authority.

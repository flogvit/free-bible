Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Lord: A title used for God, often to show respect and reverence.
Israel: The name of the biblical people whom God chose as his special people, as well as the land where they lived.
Idols: False gods or objects that people worship instead of the true God.
Babylon: An ancient empire in Mesopotamia, known for its power and splendor. The Israelites were taken captive there during the Babylonian exile.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation of human actions and their consequences.
Covenant: An agreement between God and his people, often involving promises and obligations from both sides.
Prophecy: A message or revelation from God, delivered through a prophet.
Sabbath: The seventh day of the week, which God commanded the Israelites to keep holy as a day of rest and as a sign of their covenant with him.

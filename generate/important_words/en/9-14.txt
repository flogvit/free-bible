Jonathan: King Saul's son, a brave and loyal warrior who trusts in God and takes the initiative in battle against the Philistines.
Saul: Israel's first king, chosen by God through the prophet Samuel. He is Jonathan's father and has a complex relationship with God and obedience.
Philistines: A people living in Canaan, Israel's enemies during this period. They controlled iron production and oppressed the Israelites.
Michmash: A strategically important city in the region of Benjamin where Jonathan and his armor-bearer attacked a Philistine garrison.
Armor-bearer: An assistant or servant to a warrior, often responsible for carrying weapons and equipment. In this case, he helps Jonathan in battle against the Philistines.
Ephod: A priestly garment worn by the high priest to communicate with God. Saul uses it to seek God's guidance.
God's will: The term refers to God's plan and purpose for people and the world. Saul tries to understand God's will, but his disobedience leads to conflict.

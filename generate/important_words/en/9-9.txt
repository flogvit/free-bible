Samuel: A prophet and judge in Israel who is introduced in 1 Samuel and plays a central role in the transition from the period of judges to the monarchy.
Saul: The first king of Israel, chosen by God through Samuel, but later rejected because of his disobedience to God's commands.
Prophet: A person who receives and conveys messages from God, often concerning future events or divine guidance for the people.
Kish: Saul's father, a wealthy man from the tribe of Benjamin.
Spirit: An immaterial, invisible power or being, often used to refer to God's Spirit, which gives wisdom, strength, and guidance to chosen individuals.
Sacrifice: A religious act in which gifts, usually food, animals, or other valuable items, are given to God as a way of showing gratitude, reverence, or prayer for help.
Seer: An older term for prophet, a person who could "see" into the future or receive divine insight and guidance from God.

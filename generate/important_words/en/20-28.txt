Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment; highly valued in the book of Proverbs.
Understanding: The mental capacity to distinguish between right and wrong, and to make good decisions based on insight and experience.
Righteousness: The principle of acting justly, honestly, and morally upright; a central theme in the book of Proverbs.
Discipline: Self-control or training used to correct or shape one's behavior and character.
Fear: Respect and reverence for God, often regarded as the beginning of wisdom in the book of Proverbs.
The Lord: A title referring to God, the Almighty Creator, emphasizing His authority and power.
Wealth: Material goods and prosperity, often warned against in the book of Proverbs as something that can lead to temptations and evil deeds.
Humility: Having a modest view of one's own importance and recognizing one's limitations and faults.
Faithfulness: Being loyal and steadfast toward God and other people, as well as keeping promises and fulfilling duties.

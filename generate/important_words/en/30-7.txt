Amos: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel.
Book 7: The seventh chapter in the book of Amos, containing visions and prophecies about judgment on Israel.
Vision: A spiritual experience where a person receives divine revelation or insight.
Locust swarm: A large gathering of locusts that destroys crops and vegetation.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, the almighty Creator.
Plumb line: A tool used to measure distances; here it symbolizes God's righteous judgment.
Temple: A holy place for worship and prayer, often associated with God's presence.
Jeroboam: King Jeroboam II of Israel, who reigned in the 700s BC.
Enemies: Nations or groups that oppose and threaten the people of Israel.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment and consequences for human actions and sin.

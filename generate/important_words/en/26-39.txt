Gog: A symbolic enemy of God's people, often interpreted as a nation or coalition of nations.
Magog: A land or people associated with Gog, who take part in the attack against Israel.
Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel.
Prophecy: Prediction or revelation of future events given by God through a prophet.
The Lord's Day: A period of divine intervention and judgment, where God acts to protect His people and punish their enemies.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people and the geographical area they inhabited in the Old Testament.
Lord of Hosts: A title for God that emphasizes His sovereignty and power over all forces and armies in the world.
Offering: An act in which something is given to God as a gesture of gratitude, devotion, or atonement for sin.
Burnt Offering: A type of offering where an animal was burned on an altar as a holy act to God.
Seven Years: A time period mentioned in Ezekiel 39, symbolizing completeness and divine intervention.

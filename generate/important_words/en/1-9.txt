Noah: The main character in the story of the flood, whom God chose to save along with his family and the animals aboard the ark.
Flood: A great deluge that God sent to destroy the earth and its wickedness, but Noah and his family survived by building an ark.
Ark: A large ship that Noah built according to God's instructions to save his family and a pair of every kind of animal from the flood.
Covenant: A holy agreement between God and people, in which God promises never again to destroy the earth with a flood.
Rainbow: A colorful natural phenomenon that occurs when sunlight is refracted through raindrops. In Genesis 9, the rainbow is a sign of God's covenant with Noah and his descendants.
Blessing: A blessing or expression of goodwill. In this chapter, God gives Noah and his family a blessing and permission to eat meat.
Prohibition: A restriction or limitation. In Genesis 9, God sets a prohibition against eating meat that still has blood in it and against killing other people.

Promise: A binding commitment or vow that a person makes to God, often connected with religious acts and service.
Authority: The right and power to decide, control, and govern over something or someone; in this case, it refers to the head of the household and his influence over vows made by family members.
Woman: An adult female person, discussed in the chapter in connection with vows and their validity based on her civil status and the authority figure in her life.
Father: A male parent or head of a family who, in this context, has the authority to approve or annul his daughter's vows.
Husband: The male partner in a marriage who has the authority to approve or annul his wife's vows according to Numbers 30.
Annul: To declare something invalid or without effect; in this case, it refers to the process by which a father or husband may cancel a vow made by a woman.
Approve: To accept or permit something; in this case, it refers to a father's or husband's right to consent to a vow made by a woman.

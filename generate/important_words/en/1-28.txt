Jacob: One of the patriarchs in the Bible, son of Isaac and Rebekah, brother to Esau.
Bethel: A place in Canaan where Jacob had a dream about a ladder reaching up to heaven; means "house of God".
Dream: An experience during sleep where one sees images and events that may have symbolic meaning.
Ladder: In Jacob's dream the ladder symbolizes the connection between heaven and earth and God's presence.
Angels: Spiritual beings who serve God and carry out his will; in the dream angels go up and down the ladder.
Lord: A title for God that emphasizes his sovereignty and authority.
Promise: An obligation or guarantee given by God to fulfill certain conditions or blessings.
Land: The area that God promised to give to Jacob's descendants, later known as the land of Israel.
Descendants: Offspring or relatives who come from a specific person, in this case Jacob.
Blessing: A special favor or gift from God, often linked with happiness, prosperity and protection.

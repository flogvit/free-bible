Righteousness: A moral quality that involves acting in accordance with God's will and just principles.
Judge: A person appointed to decide right from wrong, often in a legal context. In Psalm 26 this refers to God as the supreme judge.
Test: An examination or challenge to determine one's faithfulness, integrity, or character.
Heart: Symbolizes human emotions, thoughts, and intentions. In Psalm 26 it represents one's inner being.
Redeem: To buy back, deliver, or save someone from an unfortunate situation, often connected with sin and guilt.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy, which he shows toward people even when they do not deserve it.
Altar: A holy place where offerings are presented to God, usually as part of religious ceremonies.
Hymn: A song or poem expressing gratitude, reverence, and praise to God.

Unit: Unity and cooperation among people, especially among God's people.
Brothers: Members of a family or group who share common faith and values; here it refers to believers.
Together: To be in closeness, both physically and spiritually, with others to achieve fellowship and harmony.
Dew: Small water droplets that form on surfaces at night or early morning, symbolizing blessing and fruitfulness.
Hermon: A mountain in the Middle East, often associated with God's presence and blessing.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem, representing God's dwelling place and the center of religious worship in Israel.
Life: A gift from God, representing existence, growth, and development.

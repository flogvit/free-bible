Israel: The name of God's chosen people, who in the book of Hosea represent the nation that has turned away from God and followed idols.
Idols: False gods or objects that people worship instead of the true God, which is strongly condemned in the Bible.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that goes against God's will and laws, leading to spiritual separation from Him.
Punishment: The consequences or sufferings that arise as a result of sin and disobedience toward God.
Repentance: The act of turning away from sin and returning to God with sorrow and renewed faith.
Mercy: God's grace and love shown to people, even when they do not deserve it.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with God's will and moral standards, being just and upright in His sight.

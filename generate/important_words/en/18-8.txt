Eliphaz: One of Job's friends who offers counsel and criticizes Job for his suffering and alleged sin.
Job: The main character in the book, a righteous man who endures great trials and losses but maintains his faith in God.
Suffering: Endurance of pain, loss, or hardship; a central theme in the book of Job.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with moral and ethical principles; Job is described as a righteous man.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good decisions; an important theme in the book of Job.
Fear of God: Respect and awe for God; Job demonstrates this through his endurance and faithfulness.
Unrighteousness: Unethical or immoral behavior; Eliphaz suggests that Job has committed unrighteous acts leading to his suffering.

Nehemiah: A Jewish leader and governor who helped rebuild Jerusalem and strengthen the Jewish nation after the exile in Babylon.
God's people: The term used to refer to the Israelites, those who follow God's laws and have a covenant with him.
Jerusalem: The holy city and capital of Israel, the center of Jewish culture and religion.
Temple: The sacred place where Jews worshiped God and performed religious ceremonies and sacrifices.
Rebuilding: The process of repairing and restoring something that has been destroyed, such as Jerusalem's walls and temple after the exile.
Exile: The period when Jews were forced to leave their homeland and live in foreign lands, especially Babylon.
List: An overview of people, things, or events; in this case, a list of the Jewish families who returned from exile.
Gatekeepers: People responsible for protecting and controlling entry to a city or another important area.
Law: God's instructions and commands given to Moses and the Jewish people, forming the foundation of their faith and practice.

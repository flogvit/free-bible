Moses: An important prophet and leader in Hebrew history, known for receiving the Ten Commandments from God and leading the Israelites out of Egypt.
Pharaoh: The Egyptian king who refused to let the Israelites go free, which led God to send ten plagues upon Egypt.
Israelites: A people descended from the patriarch Israel (Jacob), who were slaves in Egypt and later led out by Moses.
Slavery: A practice where people are treated as property and forced to work under compulsion without freedom.
Building materials: Materials used to construct structures and monuments, such as bricks.
Brick: A building block made from clay and fired in a kiln, often used as construction material in ancient Egypt.
Straw: Dried stalks of grain that were used as a binding agent in brick production.
Workload: The amount of work or tasks imposed on an individual or group of people.
Plagues: A series of disasters and afflictions that God sent upon Egypt to force Pharaoh to release the Israelites.

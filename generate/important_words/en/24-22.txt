King: The supreme ruler of a kingdom, responsible for leading and governing the people according to God's will.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and justly in accordance with moral laws and ethical standards.
Unrighteousness: Actions that break moral laws and ethical standards, leading to injustice and suffering for others.
Jerusalem: The capital city of ancient Israel, a central place for Jewish faith and culture, and also significant for Christianity and Islam.
Prophet: A person who receives messages from God and conveys them to people, often with warnings about the consequences of their actions.
Judgment: God's just punishment or reward based on human actions and choices.
Exile: The expulsion of a people from their homeland, often as a consequence of their sin and disobedience toward God.
Covenant: A pact or agreement between God and his people, in which they promise to follow his commands and he protects and blesses them.

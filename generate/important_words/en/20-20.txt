Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to comprehend and evaluate situations and problems in a clear and rational manner.
Justice: The principle of acting in accordance with moral laws and ethical principles; to be fair and impartial.
Discipline: Correction or training given to teach and improve behavior and character.
Prudence: The practical application of wisdom and understanding in daily life; the ability to make good decisions and act wisely.
Honesty: The quality of being truthful, sincere, and genuine in words and actions.
Humility: The trait of being modest and not arrogant or proud; recognizing one's own limitations and weaknesses.
Work: The effort put forth to accomplish a task or reach a goal; to be diligent and engaged in one's work.
Slothfulness: A lack of effort or energy; failing to do what is required or expected of a person.
Loyalty: Being faithful, reliable, and devoted to someone or something; keeping promises and fulfilling obligations.

Sacrifice: Ritual actions in which gifts, most often animals, are given to God to show gratitude, ask for forgiveness, or seek blessings.
Burnt offering: A type of sacrifice in which an animal is burned on an altar as a holy gift to God.
Sabbath: A special day in the Jewish calendar when people rest from work and celebrate God's creation.
Holy assemblies: Religious meetings where believers gather to worship God and learn about his commands and promises.
Feast of Tabernacles: A Jewish festival that marks the end of the harvest season and the beginning of a new religious year.

Elisha: A prophet in Israel who served God and performed miracles.
Shunammite woman: A wealthy woman from Shunem who helped Elisha by giving him shelter.
Son: The child of the Shunammite woman, who dies and is later brought back to life by Elisha.
Miracle: A supernatural act performed by God through Elisha, such as raising the son and multiplying oil.
Oil: An important resource in biblical times, used both for cooking and lighting. Elisha multiplies a widow's oil to help her out of poverty.
Widow: A woman who has lost her husband and is in financial distress, receiving help from Elisha.
Bread: Food that Elisha miraculously multiplies to feed many people.
Gehazi: Elisha's servant who commits a sin by accepting gifts from Naaman and is punished with leprosy.

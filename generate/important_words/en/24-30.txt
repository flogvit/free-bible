Restoration: The restoration of something to its original state, in this case the people of Israel and their land.
Salvation: Deliverance from sin and its consequences, often used regarding God's saving of humanity.
Jacob's distress: A period of great trouble and suffering for Jacob's descendants, that is, the people of Israel.
The Lord: Another name for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Covenant: An agreement or promise between God and humans, often linked with blessings and obligations.
Punishment: The consequences of breaking God's laws, including suffering and destruction.
Healing: The process of becoming well again, both physically and spiritually, often associated with God's intervention.
Yoke: A symbol of oppression and slavery, used to describe the people of Israel under foreign powers.
David: An important king in Israel's history, often seen as an ideal ruler and a symbol of the Messiah.

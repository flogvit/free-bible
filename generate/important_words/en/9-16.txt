Samuel: A prophet and judge in Israel who plays a key role in the transition from the period of judges to the monarchy.
Saul: The first king of Israel, chosen by God through Samuel, but later rejected due to disobedience to God's commands.
David: A young shepherd and harpist chosen by God to be the next king of Israel. He is known for his skill as a musician and warrior, and later becomes king after Saul.
Son of Jesse: An expression used to describe David as the son of Jesse, who was a Bethlehemite. This highlights David's royal and messianic lineage.
Anointing: A ritual act in which oil is poured or rubbed on a person's head to mark their selection and consecration for a specific role, such as king or priest.
Holy Spirit: God's invisible and active presence in the world, providing guidance, strength, and wisdom to those who believe in him. The Holy Spirit comes upon David after his anointing.
Goliath: A giant and warrior from the Philistine army who challenges the Israelites to single combat. David defeats him with a sling and a stone, leading to an Israelite victory in the battle.
Lyre: A stringed instrument that David plays to soothe King Saul when he is troubled by evil spirits. This marks the beginning of David's service at Saul's court.

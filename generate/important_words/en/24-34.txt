Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who conveyed God's message to the people of Israel.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Babylon: An ancient empire in Mesopotamia, known for its capital city Babylon and King Nebuchadnezzar II.
Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who conquered Jerusalem and led many Israelites into exile.
Jerusalem: The capital of Israel and a holy city for Jews, Christians, and Muslims.
Exile: When individuals or groups are forced to leave their homeland and live in a foreign land.
Covenant: An agreement or promise between two parties, often between God and people in the Bible.
Slavery: A condition in which a person is subject to another's control and power, often as a slave.
Liberation: The act of setting someone free from slavery, captivity, or oppression.
Punishment: Consequences or retribution for actions that break laws, rules, or covenants.

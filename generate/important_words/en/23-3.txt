Lord: A title for God meaning the almighty and sovereign Creator.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem, often used as a symbol for the city and God's people.
Jerusalem: The capital of ancient Israel and a central place for Jewish and Christian faith.
Judgment: God's righteous assessment and punishment of human sin and rebellion.
Leaders: Persons with authority and responsibility to govern and guide society or the people.
Women: In this context, a reference to those who have power and influence in society but misuse it.
Children: May refer to immature and inexperienced persons taking leading roles, which leads to chaos and destruction.
Shame: A feeling of humiliation and degradation due to sin, error, or bad conduct.
Elders: Older and experienced persons who possess wisdom and knowledge, and are often respected in society.
Pride: An excessive self-awareness and arrogance that can lead to God's judgment and punishment.
Justice: The principle of acting in accordance with divine or moral law, and treating others fairly and honestly.

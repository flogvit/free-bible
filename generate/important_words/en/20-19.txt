Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to carefully evaluate situations or matters and make good decisions.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and honestly in all situations, and treating others with respect and equality.
Discipline: Correction or training used to teach someone to behave properly and follow moral principles.
Faithfulness: Being loyal and reliable toward others, especially toward God and His commands.
Humility: The quality of being modest, not considering oneself better than others, but recognizing one's own limitations and weaknesses.
Work: The effort one puts forth to achieve a goal or complete a task, often linked to honest labor and productivity.
Patience: The ability to wait calmly and perseveringly without becoming irritated or upset, especially when facing problems or challenges.
Mouth: The human organ used for speaking and expressing thoughts and feelings. In Proverbs it often symbolizes a person's words and actions.
Wealth: An abundance of material goods and resources, often associated with prosperity and success.
Poverty: A lack of material goods and resources, often associated with hardship and difficult living conditions.

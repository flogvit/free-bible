Heritage land: Land given to the people of Israel by God, and which is to be passed down from generation to generation.
Firstfruits: The first and best harvests gathered, often seen as a gift from God and a symbol of gratitude and trust.
Offering: An act where something is given to God, often food, drink, or animals, as a form of worship, thanksgiving, or atonement for sins.
Levite: A member of the tribe of Levi, who was responsible for carrying out religious ceremonies and duties in ancient Israel.
Foreigners: People who were not born into the people of Israel but lived among them. They were often treated with respect and care, in accordance with God's commands.
Widow: A woman who has lost her husband and who is often in a vulnerable situation in society.
Fatherless: Children who have lost their father and thus lack the traditional provider in the family.

Deliverance: Liberation from distress, danger, or oppression, often described as God's action in the Book of Psalms.
Fortress: A strong and secure place, often used as a picture of God's protection and safety for believers.
Shield: A symbol of protection and defense, representing God's care and safeguarding for His people.
Rock: A solid and unshakable foundation, used as an image of God's stability and strength for believers.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or threaten believers, can also refer to evil spiritual powers.
The Lord: A title referring to God as the supreme authority and ruler over all.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, reverence, and worship directed toward God for His goodness and grace.
Righteous: To act in accordance with moral principles and God's will, often linked to reward from God.

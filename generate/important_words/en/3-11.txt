Clean animals: Animals that according to Leviticus 11 are permitted to be eaten by the Israelites, based on certain criteria (e.g., cud-chewers with split hooves).
Unclean animals: Animals that do not meet the criteria for clean animals and are therefore forbidden to be eaten by the Israelites.
Birds: Flying creatures mentioned in Leviticus 11, where some species are permitted and others are forbidden to be eaten.
Fish: Water-dwelling creatures with fins and scales, which according to Leviticus 11 are permitted to be eaten.
Creeping things: The group of four-legged animals that crawl or climb, which are forbidden to be eaten according to Leviticus 11.
Webbed feet: A characteristic of birds that enables them to swim, and which is a criterion for unclean birds in Leviticus 11.
Abomination: A strongly negative term used in Leviticus 11 to describe animals that are forbidden to be eaten and should be avoided.

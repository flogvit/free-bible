Moses: An important prophet and leader in the Hebrew Bible, who received the law from God and led the Israelites out of Egypt.
Miriam: Moses and Aaron's sister, a prophetess and leader among the Israelites.
Aaron: Moses' older brother, the high priest for the Israelites and an important figure in their religious life.
Disease: In this context, it refers to the skin disease leprosy with which Miriam was punished by God.
Punishment: God's response to human disobedience or sin; in this case, Miriam's and Aaron's criticism of Moses.
Forgiveness: When God chooses not to punish anyone for their sins and gives them a new chance to correct their mistakes.
Healing: The process by which God restores a person's health, either physically or spiritually. In this chapter, God heals Miriam from leprosy.

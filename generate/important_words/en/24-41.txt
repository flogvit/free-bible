Gedaliah: The governor appointed by Babylonian king Nebuchadnezzar to rule over Judah after the fall of Jerusalem.
Ishmael: A member of David's house, who murdered Gedaliah and his followers.
Mizpah: A city in the territory of Benjamin where Gedaliah had his headquarters.
Ammonites: A people who lived east of the Jordan River, often in conflict with the people of Israel.
Johanan: A leader among the Jews who warned Gedaliah about Ishmael's plans and later led a group to avenge Gedaliah's murder.
Eighty men: A group of pilgrims from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria who were attacked by Ishmael, many of whom were killed.
Gibeon: A city where Johanan and his forces caught up with Ishmael and rescued the surviving hostages.
Egypt: The country where Johanan and the rest of the Jews sought refuge after Gedaliah's murder, despite warnings from the prophet Jeremiah.

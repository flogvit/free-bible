Lord: A title often used for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Throne: Symbolizes God's royal power and dominion over the world and humanity.
Peoples: The group of people subject to God's rule, both Israel and other nations.
Righteous: To act in accordance with moral principles and ethical standards, which God expects from his followers.
Merciful: Reflects God's grace, love, and compassion toward people, especially when they turn to him in repentance and conversion.
Praise: An expression of gratitude and reverence to God through prayer, song, or other forms of worship.
Holy: An attribute describing God's purity and perfection, as well as separation from sin and evil.
Jacob's: A reference to the patriarch Jacob, a central figure in Israel's history and God's promises to his descendants.
Strength: God's ability to protect, support, and help his people in difficult times and situations.

Lord: A title used for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority over all creation.
Redemption: The process by which God saves and delivers his people from sin, suffering, and oppression.
Zion: A name for Jerusalem, especially the holy part of the city where the temple stood. It symbolizes God's presence and serves as the center of worship for his people.
Awake: To shake off sleep or indifference, becoming alert and active. Used here as a call to God's people to wake up and recognize God's actions.
Feet: Often used symbolically in the Bible to represent one's journey or path in life. In this context, it refers to those who bring good news of God's salvation.
Good news: The message of God's salvation and deliverance for his people, including the promise of a restored relationship with him.
Holy: To be set apart from what is common or profane, dedicated to God and his service.

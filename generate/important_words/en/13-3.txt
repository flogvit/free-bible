David's sons: The children of David, the second king of Israel, who reigned around 1000 BC.
Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel and an important religious center for Judaism.
Solomon: One of David's sons and his successor on the throne; known for his wisdom and for building the temple in Jerusalem.
Rehoboam: Solomon's son and the first king of Judah after the kingdom was divided into two.
Judah: One of the twelve tribes of Israel and later a separate kingdom after the division of the realm.
The Exile: The period when the Jews were taken captive and lived in Babylon (approx. 586-539 BC).
Shealtiel: A descendant of King David and father to Zerubbabel, a leader during the rebuilding of the temple after the exile.

Covenant: An agreement or bond between God and people, often involving promises and conditions.
Restoration: The act of returning something to its original state; in this context, the rebuilding of Jerusalem and the reunification of the people of Israel.
The Lord's righteousness: This expression refers to God's just actions and judgments, which reveal his character and moral nature.
David's shoot: A metaphor for a future king from David's line, representing the Messiah or Jesus Christ in Christian belief.
Redemption: The process of being set free from sin, guilt, and punishment through God's grace and love.
Fruitfulness: The ability to produce abundant offspring, growth, and prosperity; used as a symbol of God's blessings upon Israel.
The Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, who were responsible for priestly duties and religious obligations in ancient Israel.

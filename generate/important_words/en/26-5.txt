Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Jerusalem: The capital of ancient Israel, regarded as a holy city by Jews, Christians, and Muslims.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or reward for human actions and choices.
Sword: A weapon used to symbolize war, destruction, and God's judgment upon the people.
Famine: A lack of food leading to hardship and suffering, often used as a symbol of God's punishment.
Pestilence: A deadly and contagious disease that can be seen as a form of God's punishment on people for their sins.
Fourth part: Dividing something into four equal parts, here used to describe how God will punish Jerusalem in different ways.
Scattering: The act of spreading something or someone over a large area, here referring to how God will scatter the people of Israel among the nations.
Abomination: A strong feeling of aversion or repugnance, often used to describe God's reaction to human sins and godless actions.
Repentance: The feeling of remorse and sorrow for past deeds, often linked to recognizing one's own sins and the need to turn back to God.

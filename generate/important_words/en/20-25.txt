Proverbs: Short, wise sayings that convey life experience and practical wisdom.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good decisions and act rightly.
Understanding: The capacity to grasp and evaluate situations and people in a clear and discerning way.
Discipline: Training or guidance that helps a person develop good character and morality.
Honor: Respect and recognition resulting from virtues, achievements, or good deeds.
Humility: The quality of being modest and not having an overly high opinion of oneself.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and in accordance with laws and moral rules.
Patience: The ability to wait calmly and perseveringly without becoming irritated or frustrated.
Friendship: A close relationship between two or more people based on mutual love, support, and understanding.
Enmity: Hostile feelings or actions between individuals or groups; the opposite of friendship.

Josaphat: The king of Judah who urged the people to turn back to God and righteousness.
Righteous judges: Individuals appointed by Josaphat to judge fairly and impartially in accordance with God's laws.
Fear of the Lord: A reverent awe and respect for God, leading to obedience to His commands and a rejection of evil.
Spiritual reform: The process of restoring and improving religious practices and beliefs so that they align more closely with God's will.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi who held special religious roles and responsibilities in ancient Israel.
High priest: The supreme religious leader in ancient Israel, responsible for performing sacrifices and other ceremonies in the temple.
Amariah: A priest and prophet during King Josaphat's reign who gave counsel and guidance according to God's word.
Truth: Agreement with reality or actual events; in this context, it also means being faithful to God's commands.
Wholehearted: Doing something with full effort and commitment, without reservation or half-heartedness.

Job: The main character in the book of Job, a righteous man who experiences great suffering and trials.
Suffering: Pain, distress, or discomfort experienced by a person, whether physical, mental, or spiritual.
Friends: Three individuals (Eliphaz, Bildad, and Zophar) who visit Job to comfort and advise him during his trials.
Dialogue: A conversation between two or more people, often used to exchange ideas or viewpoints.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with moral and ethical principles; being honest and fair.
Fear of God: Deep respect and awe for God, often expressed through obedience and trust.
Trials: Difficult situations or challenges that a person must face and overcome.

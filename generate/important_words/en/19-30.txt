Psalm: A holy song or poem often used in worship and praise of God.
Lord: A title for God that expresses respect and submission, often used to acknowledge his sovereignty and power.
Deliver: To save or protect someone from danger or difficulties. In Psalm 30 it refers to God's protection and salvation.
Restore: To bring back to a former state or position. In this psalm it means that God lifts up the one who is crushed or suffering.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love shown to humanity, despite their faults and shortcomings.
Wrath: God's strong displeasure with sin and evil. In Psalm 30 it refers to God's righteous anger against injustice.
Life: In this context, a symbol of God's grace and blessings that he gives to those who seek him and obey his commands.
Weeping: An emotional response to sorrow, pain or suffering. In Psalm 30 it symbolizes human distress and the need for God's help.
Dancing: An expression of joy and gratitude through movement and rhythm. In this psalm dancing represents the joy that comes after God has intervened and helped.

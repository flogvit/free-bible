Nathan: A prophet in Israel who conveyed God's message to King David.
David: The second king of Israel, known for his piety and military success. He desired to build a temple for God.
Temple: A holy place dedicated to God where people can worship and offer gifts.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, often with promises and conditions from both parties.
Lineage: Family or descendants who share a common origin or inheritance.
Throne: Symbol of royal power and authority, here used to refer to King David's position and his descendants.
House of Israel: Term for the Israelite people, including the twelve tribes that make up the nation of Israel.
Enemies: The nations or groups that are in conflict with or oppose Israel and its interests.

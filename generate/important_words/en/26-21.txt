Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
The Sword: Symbolizes God's judgment and punishment upon the people, especially Israel and Jerusalem.
Lord: Another designation for God, often used to show respect and reverence.
Prophecy: Predictions or messages given by God through a prophet, often concerning future events or warnings.
Judgment: God's righteous assessment and consequences for human actions, both individually and collectively.
Jerusalem: The capital of ancient Israel and a central place for Jewish faith and culture.
Ammonites: An ancient people who lived east of the Jordan River; often in conflict with Israel.
Babylon: A powerful empire in antiquity, represented by King Nebuchadnezzar, which conquered Jerusalem and led the Israelites into exile.
Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who conquered Jerusalem and took many Israelites captive to Babylon.

David: A young Israelite who later becomes king of Israel, known for his victory over Goliath and his strong faith in God.
Goliath: A giant from the Philistine army whom David defeats in single combat, demonstrating God's power and strength through the young David.
Saul: The first king of Israel, who gradually becomes jealous of David and attempts to kill him several times.
The Prophet: A person who speaks on behalf of God and conveys His message to the people. In 1 Samuel 22, it is Samuel who serves as the prophet.
Höhle: A hiding place where David seeks refuge from King Saul and his soldiers.
Priests: Religious leaders who perform ceremonies and offer sacrifices to God. In 1 Samuel 22, the priests in the city of Nob are killed by Saul's men.
Ahimelech: The high priest at Nob who helps David and gives him bread and a sword. He is later killed by Saul's men.
Doeg: An Edomite servant of Saul who testifies against Ahimelech and kills the priests at Nob on Saul's orders.
Abiathar: A son of Ahimelech who survives the massacre at Nob and flees to David, where he becomes one of David's closest advisors.

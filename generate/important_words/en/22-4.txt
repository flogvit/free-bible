Bride: The woman who is loved and described in the Song of Songs often symbolizes God's people.
Groom: The man who loves and describes the bride in the Song of Songs often represents God or his love for his people.
Love: A deep feeling of devotion, care, and intimacy between two people; a central theme in the Song of Songs.
Garden: An image of intimacy and beauty where the bride and groom meet and express their love.
Lebanon: A mountainous region in the Middle East mentioned several times in the Song of Songs as a place of beauty and richness.
Pomegranate: A fruit mentioned in the Song of Songs that symbolizes fertility and abundance.
Balm: A fragrant plant mentioned in the Song of Songs as part of the beautiful nature surrounding the bride and groom.

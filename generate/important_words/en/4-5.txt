Purification: The process of removing impurities, both physical and spiritual, in accordance with God's commands.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks God's laws and moral standards.
Offering: A gift to God, often an animal, as a way to show devotion, gratitude, or atonement.
Priest: A religious leader who performs ceremonies and rituals in service to God and the people.
Oath: A solemn promise or vow made by a person to confirm truthfulness or fulfill an obligation.
Guilt: The feeling of having done something wrong, or the actual responsibility for committing a sin.
Restoration: The act of correcting a past mistake, harm, or injustice, often by providing compensation.
Tithe: A portion, usually one-tenth, of one's harvest or income given to God or the priesthood as an expression of thanks and support.

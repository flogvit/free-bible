Misery: A state of great distress, suffering, or misfortune that Job experiences due to his trials and losses.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with moral laws and ethics, which Job claims to have followed throughout his trial.
Hope: The expectation of a better future or outcome, even when facing severe trials and adversity.
Friend: A person who is close and offers support, but in Job's case, someone who criticizes and accuses him of sins he has not committed.
Accusation: Charges or criticism directed at Job by his friends, who believe that his suffering is the result of sin.
Darkness: A symbol of the suffering, despair, and isolation that Job experiences during his trials.
Prayer: Communication with God in which one seeks help, guidance, or comfort, something Job does several times in the book.

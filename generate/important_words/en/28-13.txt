Ba'al: A Canaanite god, often associated with fertility and rain. Hosea criticizes Israel's worship of Ba'al instead of Yahweh.
Ephraim: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, used as a representation of all Israel. Hosea warns Ephraim about the consequences of their sins and idolatry.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks God's laws and commands. Hosea describes Israel's sins and calls them to turn back to God.
Repentance: To show genuine sorrow and regret for sins committed and seek God's forgiveness. Hosea urges Israel to repent of their sins and return to God.
Forgiveness: God's grace and mercy that gives people the opportunity to be cleansed from their sins. Hosea emphasizes the importance of seeking God's forgiveness.
Punishment: Consequences resulting from sin and disobedience toward God. Hosea warns that God's punishment will come upon Israel if they do not turn back to Him.
Mercy: God's loving care and grace shown to people, even when they do not deserve it. Hosea speaks of God's mercy and His desire to forgive and restore His people.

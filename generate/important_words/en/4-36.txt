Lineage Inheritance: Rights and property transferred from one generation to the next within a family or tribe.
Tribes: Groups of people who are related and share a common cultural and historical background, often organized around a shared leader.
Daughters: Female children of a person, used here to refer to the daughters of Zelophehad, who sought inheritance rights.
Marriage: A legal and social contract between two people that binds them together in partnership and commits them to one another.
Inheritance Laws: Rules and regulations that determine how an estate is distributed and transferred within a family or tribe.
Law: A set of rules and ordinances given by God, intended to guide human behavior and societal relationships.
The Lord: Another designation for God, the Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.

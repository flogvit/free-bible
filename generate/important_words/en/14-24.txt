Joash: King of Judah who was crowned at a young age and did what was right in the Lord's eyes under the guidance of priest Jehoiada.
Jehoiada: A high priest in Jerusalem who helped Joash restore the temple and maintain justice in the land.
Temple: A holy place in Jerusalem built by King Solomon to worship God and house the Ark of the Covenant.
Ba'al: A Canaanite god, often worshipped by Israelites despite God's warnings.
Sabaoth: A Hebrew word meaning "hosts" or "armies," often used in connection with God as a description of his power and authority.
Zechariah: A prophet and son of Jehoiada, who spoke out against idolatry in Judah and was stoned on Joash's orders.
Nefuseth: An Egyptian princess who married Joash and gained great influence over him, leading him to turn away from God.
Aram: An ancient region in the Middle East whose army invaded Judah and defeated Joash's forces, leaving him wounded and vulnerable to assassination.

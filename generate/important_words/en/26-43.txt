The Lord's Glory: God's glory and presence revealed in a radiant and powerful form.
Temple: A holy place for worship and sacrifice, central to the Jewish faith.
Altar: A structure where sacrifices and sacred rituals are performed as part of divine service.
Measuring Instruments: Tools and methods used to measure and describe the dimensions of the temple and its surroundings.
Consecration: A ceremony marking the beginning of a new phase or use of a holy place, such as the temple.
Sin Offering: An offering given to atone for sins and achieve reconciliation with God.
Station: The area where priests prepare sacrifices before presenting them to the Lord.
Procedure: Instructions and rules given by God on how sacrifices and rituals should be carried out.

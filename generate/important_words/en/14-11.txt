Rehoboam: Son of Solomon and king of Israel, who succeeded his father on the throne.
Roboam: An alternative name for Rehoboam, used in various Bible translations.
Jeroboam: A former servant under Solomon who became leader of the ten northern tribes and founded the rival kingdom of Israel.
Judah: One of Israel's twelve tribes and the name of the southern kingdom that emerged after the division of the kingdom, with Jerusalem as its capital.
Shechem: The city where Jeroboam was proclaimed king over the ten northern tribes.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, one of Israel's twelve tribes, who had special religious duties and roles in the temple.
The Temple: The holy building in Jerusalem where the Israelites worshiped God and performed sacrifices.
Idols: False gods or deities that people worshiped instead of the true God.
Fortresses: Fortified cities and structures built to protect an area from enemy attacks.
Prophet: A person who speaks on behalf of God and delivers his message to the people.

Sanballat: A Samaritan leader who opposed Nehemiah's work in rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem.
Tobiah: An Ammonite nobleman and enemy of Nehemiah, who also tried to hinder the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls.
Geshem: An Arab ruler who was allied with Sanballat and Tobiah in their attempt to oppose Nehemiah's project.
Prophecy: A revelation or message from God delivered through a prophet. In the book of Nehemiah, false prophets try to deceive Nehemiah.
The Wall: The physical barrier around Jerusalem that Nehemiah and his people worked to rebuild in order to protect the city and its inhabitants.
Letter: Written communication between individuals. In Nehemiah chapter 6, letters are used as a tool to spread lies and rumors about Nehemiah and his work.
Fear: A feeling of anxiety or worry caused by a threat. Nehemiah's opponents tried to create fear among the people to stop the rebuilding of the wall.

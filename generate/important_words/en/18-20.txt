Zophar: One of Job's three friends who came to comfort him and discuss his sufferings.
Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting rightly and justly toward others, in accordance with God's will.
Sins: Actions or thoughts that break God's commands and moral law.
Punishment: Consequences or suffering resulting from breaking God's commands or doing wrong.
Restoration: The process of restoring something to its original condition, often used regarding spiritual healing or reconciliation with God.
Happiness: A feeling of joy, contentment, and well-being, often seen as a reward for living a righteous life.
Corruption: A state of moral decay and destruction, often linked to sin and evil.
The Lord: A title referring to God as the highest authority and creator of all things.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and understanding in an effective and ethical way, regarded as a virtue in religious texts.

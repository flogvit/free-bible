Psalm: A poetic and musical form of prayer or praise, often directed toward God.
Lord: A title referring to God, used to express respect and reverence.
Praise: To express gratitude, honor, and glory to God through prayer, song, or actions.
Enemies: Persons or forces that stand in opposition to what is good, often seen as evil and contrary to God's will.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, evil, and death, bringing them into eternal life in fellowship with Him.
Refuge: A place or person who provides protection, safety, and strength, often used as a metaphor for God's care and shelter.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with God's will and morality, and is therefore good and upright.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that opposes God's will and morality, leading to separation from God and His love.
Merciful: To show compassion, grace, and forgiveness to others, especially those who have done wrong or are in need of help.

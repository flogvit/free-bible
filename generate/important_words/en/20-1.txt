Wisdom: The ability to understand and use knowledge in an effective and moral way; regarded as a virtue in the book of Proverbs.
Understanding: The ability to judge and make good decisions based on insight and experience; necessary for living a righteous life.
Knowledge: Information, facts, and skills acquired through experience or education; highly valued in the book of Proverbs.
Righteousness: The quality of being just, honest, and sincere; an important value in the book of Proverbs for achieving a good life.
Discipline: Correction and training used to teach and guide someone toward proper behavior and attitudes; recommended in the book of Proverbs for raising children and shaping their character.
Fear of God: A deep reverence and respect for God and His commands; considered the beginning of wisdom in the book of Proverbs.

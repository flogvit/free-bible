Solomon's Temple: The building that King Solomon constructed to house the Ark of the Covenant and worship God.
Fire from the Lord: A divine fire that consumes the burnt offering and demonstrates God's presence and acceptance.
Burnt Offering: An offering in which an animal is burned on an altar as a symbolic act to seek God's grace and forgiveness.
Sin Offering: An offering given to make atonement for sins and achieve reconciliation with God.
Intercession: A prayer in which one asks God for help, protection, or blessing on behalf of others.
Ark of the Covenant: A holy chest containing the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, symbolizing God's presence among the people of Israel.
Feast of Seven Days: A religious celebration that lasted seven days, during which the people participated in sacrifices and praise to God.
Dedication: To set apart or consecrate something, such as a temple, for holy use or service to God.
Communion: A ceremony involving eating bread and drinking wine, symbolizing Jesus' body and blood, to remember his sacrifice.

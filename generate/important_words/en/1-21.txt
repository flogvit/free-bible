Abraham: A central figure in Genesis and father to Isaac, regarded as the patriarch of Jews, Christians, and Muslims.
Isaac: Son of Abraham and Sarah, an important figure in the Bible and counted among the patriarchs of Judaism.
Sarah: Abraham's wife, mother of Isaac, and a significant female figure in the Bible.
Hagar: Sarah's Egyptian servant, mother of Ishmael, and a central character in the story of Abraham and his descendants.
Ishmael: Son of Abraham and Hagar, considered the ancestor of the Arabs and an important figure in Islamic tradition.
Covenant: An agreement or bond between God and people, often linked to promises and blessings from God.
Well: A source of water, vital for survival and life in desert regions, also symbolizing God's care and provision.
Philistines: A people who lived in the coastal areas southwest of Canaan, frequently in conflict with Israel in biblical history.

Gideon: A judge and military leader in Israel who led the Israelites to victory over the Midianites.
Midianites: A people group that invaded and oppressed Israel. Gideon defeated them with God's help.
Ephod: A religious garment, often a breastpiece, worn by the high priest in Israel. Gideon made an ephod that later became a source of idolatry.
Zebah and Zalmunna: Two Midianite kings who were captured and killed by Gideon after their defeat against the Israelites.
Abimelech: Gideon's son who declared himself king over Israel and ruled tyrannically for three years before he died.
Shechem: A city in Canaan where Abimelech was proclaimed king and later met his downfall.
Gaal: A rebellious leader who challenged Abimelech's rule, but was defeated and driven out from Shechem.
Tola: A judge who ruled Israel after Abimelech's death and brought stability back to the nation.
Jair: Another judge who ruled Israel after Tola, and helped maintain peace and order in the land.

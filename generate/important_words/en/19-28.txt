Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his authority and power.
Prayer: Communication with God, where one expresses gratitude, asks for help, or seeks guidance.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and giving them eternal life.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or threaten believers and their relationship with God.
Refuge: A place or state of safety and protection, especially in relation to God's care for his people.
Praise: Expressing gratitude, honor, and glory to God through words, song, or action.
Strength: God's power and ability to support, protect, and guide his people in challenging situations.
Shield: A symbol of God's protection and defense of his people against evil and hostile forces.
Heart: The seat of emotions, will, and morality, and a central concept in the relationship between God and humanity.
Cry: An intense prayer or expression of distress, often directed to God for help and support.

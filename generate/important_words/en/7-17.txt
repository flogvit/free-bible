Micah: A man from the hill country of Ephraim, also known as Mika, who is the main character in chapter 17 of the book of Judges.
Idols: Objects or images that represent deities and are worshiped instead of the true God.
Silver: A metallic element often associated with wealth and value. Micah used silver coins to make an idol and other religious objects.
Mother: Micah's mother, who blesses him and encourages him to make religious objects from the silver he returned to her.
Levite: A person from the tribe of Levi, one of the twelve tribes of Israel. Levites had special religious roles and duties, including service in the temple.
Priest: A religious leader who performs ceremonies and sacrifices on behalf of the people. In Judges 17, a young Levite becomes priest for Micah and his family.
Worship: The act of honoring and showing reverence to a deity, usually through prayer, song, and rituals.

Vineyard: A garden or estate where grapes are grown, used as a metaphor for God's people in Isaiah chapter 5.
Lord: A title often used for God, referring to his authority and sovereignty.
Song: A poetic form used to convey the message of this chapter, expressing God's love and disappointment with his people.
Wild grapes: Low-quality grapes, symbolizing Israel's wickedness and disobedience toward God.
Justice: A central concept in the Bible, referring to God's righteous standard and humanity's responsibility to live according to it.
Injustice: The opposite of justice, representing actions and attitudes that contradict God's will and purpose.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human sin and disobedience.
House of the people: A reference to the Israelite community, especially its leaders and those who held power and influence.
Fertile soil: Land suitable for cultivation and crop production, here used as a picture of God's blessings and care for his people.

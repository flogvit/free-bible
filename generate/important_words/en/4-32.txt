Ruben: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, founded by Jacob's firstborn son.
Gad: Another of the twelve tribes of Israel, founded by Jacob's seventh son.
Land: The area assigned to a tribe, in this case east of the Jordan River.
Jordan River: A river in the Middle East that serves as a border between the lands of Canaan and Moab.
Moab: An ancient kingdom east of the Dead Sea and the Jordan River, often in conflict with Israel.
Moses: The prophet and leader of the Israelites who led them out of Egypt and through the wilderness toward Canaan.
Warriors: Soldiers from the tribes of Reuben and Gad who fought in the war against Midian and other enemies.
Inheritance: Land and possessions given to descendants or tribes as part of God's promise.
Promise: A commitment or guarantee given by God to the Israelites to give them a specific territory.

Praise: An expression of gratitude and reverence toward God, often involving song and prayer.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, emphasizing his authority and power.
Deeds: Actions performed by God, demonstrating his power and love for his people.
Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and insight. In the book of Psalms, wisdom is often linked to God's teaching and guidance.
Fear: A deep respect and awe for God, leading to obedience and trust.
Understanding: The capacity to judge and comprehend situations, and to act wisely and justly based on this understanding.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with God's will and morality, and treating others with justice and mercy.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love toward humanity, which brings salvation and blessings.
Covenant: A binding agreement between God and his people, where God promises to protect and bless them, and they promise to be faithful and obedient to him.

Ark: A large boat built by Noah to save his family and the animals from the flood.
Flood: A great deluge sent by God to destroy the earth and all life because of humanity's wickedness.
Noah: The main character in the story, a righteous man chosen by God to build the ark and survive the flood.
Bird: A creature sent out by Noah to find dry land after the waters began to recede.
Olive branch: A symbol of peace and new beginnings, brought back by the dove as a sign that the waters had gone down and life could begin again.
Sacrifice: An act in which something is offered to God as a sign of gratitude, respect, and devotion.
Curse: A negative consequence or punishment resulting from sin or disobedience toward God.

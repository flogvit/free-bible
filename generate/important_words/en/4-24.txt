Balaam: A non-Israelite prophet who was hired by Balak, the king of Moab, to curse the Israelites, but ended up blessing them instead.
Balak: The king of Moab who hired Balaam to curse the Israelites in hopes of weakening them and protecting his own kingdom.
Oracle: A prophecy or divine message, often delivered through a seer or prophet. In Numbers 24, Balaam gives several oracles that bless the Israelites.
The people of Israel: Descendants of the patriarch Jacob (also called Israel) who made up the twelve tribes of Israel. In Numbers 24 they are blessed by Balaam.
Star: A symbol for a prominent leader or king. In Balaam's fourth oracle, a star is mentioned as a symbol of a future king of Israel.
Scepters: Symbols of royal power and authority. In Balaam's oracles, scepters are associated with Israel's victory over their enemies and their future king.
Edom: An ancient kingdom southeast of the Dead Sea, inhabited by descendants of Esau, Jacob's brother. In Balaam's oracles, Edom is subjugated to Israel.
Seir: A mountainous region in Edom. In Balaam's oracles, Seir is also subjugated to Israel.

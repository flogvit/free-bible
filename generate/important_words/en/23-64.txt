Potter: God is compared to a potter who shapes clay, where people are the clay and God forms them according to His will.  
Clay: This concept symbolizes humanity and their frailty, as well as their dependence on God for guidance and protection.  
Heaven: Represents God's dwelling place and the spiritual realm that stands in contrast to the earth, where humans live.  
Tear: To destroy or remove something; in this context, it refers to God's action of tearing open heaven to come down and help His people.  
Mountains: Often symbolize power and stability, and in this context represent the mighty nations and their leaders who can be affected by God's intervention.  
Hide: In this context, it means that God keeps Himself hidden from people and does not intervene directly in their lives, but allows them to experience the consequences of their actions.  
Sins: Actions, thoughts, and attitudes that contradict God's will and morality. Sins lead to spiritual separation from God and can result in punishment.

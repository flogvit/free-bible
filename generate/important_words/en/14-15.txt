Asa: A king of Judah who sought God and carried out religious reforms to remove idolatry.
Seek: To look for or try to find something; in this context, to seek God's guidance and presence.
Prophet: A person who speaks on behalf of God and conveys divine messages to people.
Spirit: In this context, it refers to the Spirit of God, a divine power that gives wisdom and guidance.
Renew: To restore or improve something; here it means to renew the covenant between God and the people.
Covenant: An agreement or commitment between two parties, in this case between God and the people of Israel.
Abolish: To remove or put an end to something; here it means to eliminate idolatry and unjust actions.
Feast: A celebration or observance of a special event; here it refers to a religious ceremony to honor God.

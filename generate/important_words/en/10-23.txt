David: The second king of Israel, known for his bravery and skill as a leader. He was also a poet and musician.
Warriors: David's chosen soldiers, known as "The Thirty," who fought alongside him in many battles.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Covenant: An agreement or promise between two parties, often between God and people, involving certain obligations and blessings.
Righteous: To act in accordance with moral principles and ethical norms, especially in relation to God's will.
Deliverer: A person who frees or rescues someone from oppression, suffering, or danger, often used of God in the Old Testament.
Enemies: Individuals or groups who are in conflict or opposition to one another, often used of nations that Israel fought against.
Fortress: A strong and secure building or structure, often used metaphorically to describe God's protection.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and fairly, especially in relation to God's law and morality.

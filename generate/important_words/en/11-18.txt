Elijah: A prophet in Israel who served under King Ahab and challenged the idolatry of Baal.
Ahab: The king of Israel who married Jezebel and allowed the worship of Baal, which led to God's judgment on the land.
Jezebel: Queen of Israel and wife of Ahab; she promoted the worship of Baal and persecuted God's prophets.
Baal: A Canaanite god of fertility and weather, worshipped by many Israelites at that time, despite God's warnings.
Carmel: A mountain in Israel where Elijah challenged the prophets of Baal and demonstrated God's superior power.
Drought: A long period without rain that God sent over Israel as punishment for their idolatry.
Prophets: People chosen by God to deliver His messages and warnings to the people.
Sacrifice: A gift or act offered to God or gods as part of religious worship.
Fire: A symbol of God's presence and power; in 1 Kings 18, God sends fire from heaven to consume Elijah's sacrifice.
Rain: A sign of God's grace and blessing; after Elijah defeated the prophets of Baal, God sent rain to end the drought.

Temple: A holy place for worship and offerings, central to Jewish faith and culture.
The Lord's house: Another name for the temple, representing God's dwelling among his people.
High priest: The highest religious leader in Israel, responsible for performing sacrifices and ritual acts.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, who had special religious duties and services in the temple.
Sabbath: A weekly day of rest, beginning at sunset on Friday and ending at sunset on Saturday.
Offering: A gift to God, often in the form of animals or food, as part of worship and gratitude.
Holy place: A specially sacred area within the temple, containing the most holy room where God's presence is strongest.
Purification: A process to be cleansed from sin and impurity through prayer, sacrifice, and rituals.
God's servants: Those who serve God and carry out his will, including priests and Levites.

Spies: Persons sent to explore and gather information about a specific area, in this case Canaan.
Canaan: The region God promised to give the people of Israel, later known as the Promised Land.
Moses: The prophet and leader of the people of Israel, who led them out of Egypt and toward the Promised Land.
Joshua: One of the twelve spies sent to Canaan, and a future leader of the people of Israel.
Caleb: Another of the twelve spies, known for his faithfulness and trust in God's promises.
Report: Feedback or information that the spies gave to Moses and the people of Israel after their mission.
Giant inhabitants: Large and strong people who lived in Canaan, which frightened some of the spies and created fear among the people of Israel.
Faith: Trust and conviction that God will keep His promises and give victory to His people.
Fear: A negative feeling that prevents people from trusting God and following His guidance.
Rebellion: The act of going against authority, in this case the people of Israel's lack of faith in God and Moses.

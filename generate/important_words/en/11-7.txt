Solomon: The king of Israel and son of King David, known for his wisdom and the building of the temple in Jerusalem.
Temple: A holy place dedicated to God, where people could come to worship and offer gifts.
Hiram: A skilled craftsman from Tyre, brought by Solomon to work on the decoration of the temple.
Pillars: Large vertical structures that support a building or architectural construction, which can also serve decorative purposes.
Bronze: An alloy consisting mainly of copper and tin, used in producing various objects for the temple.
Molten Sea: A large basin made of bronze and used for ritual cleansing before priests entered the temple.
Lampstands: Objects designed to hold light, often in the form of lamps or candles, used to provide illumination in the temple.
Altar: A structure where offerings and prayers are presented to God. In Solomon's temple there was a large bronze altar for burnt offerings and grain offerings.
Gold: A precious metal symbolizing wealth and purity, used abundantly in the decoration of the temple.

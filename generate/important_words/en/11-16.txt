Baasha: One of the kings of Israel who ruled for 24 years and did evil in the Lord's sight, which led to his dynasty being wiped out.
Elah: Baasha's son and successor as king over Israel; he reigned for two years before being murdered by his servant Zimri.
Zimri: A military commander who killed Elah and seized the throne, but committed suicide after seven days when Omri besieged Tirsa, the city where he was king.
Omri: A military leader who became king after Zimri's death. He fought against Tibni for control over Israel, and after Tibni's death, Omri ruled for 12 years. He established Samaria as the capital of the kingdom.
Ahab: Omri's son and successor as king over Israel. Ahab married Jezebel, a Sidonian princess, and served Baal, which led him to do evil in the Lord's sight more than any other king before him.
Jezebel: A Sidonian princess and Ahab's wife who introduced the worship of Baal into Israel and persecuted the prophets of the Lord.
Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God, often concerning future events or divine guidance.
The Lord: A reference to God, the Almighty Creator, who is rightly and properly worshipped by people.
Baal: A Canaanite god who was worshiped by Ahab and Jezebel, which led them to do evil in the Lord's sight and brought God's wrath upon themselves and Israel.

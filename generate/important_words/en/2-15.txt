Exodus 15 focuses on Israel's victory over the Egyptians and Moses' song of praise to God. Here are some important words and explanations:
Lord: A title often used for God in the Bible, pointing to his authority and power.
Salvation: The term used to describe God's act of delivering his people from danger or oppression.
Enemies: Persons or groups who oppose God's people; in this context, it refers to the Egyptians.
Horse and rider: Symbolizes the Egyptian army and its military strength.
Deep water: Refers to the Red Sea, which God parted so that the Israelites could cross safely.
Miriam: Moses' sister, a prophetess and leader among the Israelites.
Drum and dance: Expressions of joy and gratitude toward God after the victory over the Egyptians.

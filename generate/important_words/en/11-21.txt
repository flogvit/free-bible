Ahab: King of Israel who was married to Jezebel and did evil in the Lord's sight.
Jezebel: Ahab's wife, daughter of Ethbaal king of Sidon, who worshipped idols and encouraged Ahab to do the same.
Naboth: A man from Jezreel who owned a vineyard next to Ahab's palace; he refused to sell or exchange it with Ahab.
The Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator and highest authority in Jewish and Christian faith.
Elijah: A prophet in Israel who spoke on behalf of the Lord and condemned the evil deeds of Ahab and Jezebel.
Judgment: A decision or punishment given by God, often as a result of sin or injustice.
Property: Land, possessions, or resources that belong to a person or group.
Inherit: To receive property, status, or rights from a parent or ancestor after their death.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks God's law and leads to separation from Him.

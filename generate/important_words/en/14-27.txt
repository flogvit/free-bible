Jotham: A king of Judah who ruled righteously and feared God. He was the son of King Uzziah and father of King Ahaz.
Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.
Temple: A holy place where people worshiped God and offered gifts. In Jerusalem stood the first temple built by King Solomon.
High Place: A hill or plateau where people in ancient times performed religious ceremonies and worshiped gods, including idolatry.
Fortress: A military structure or building designed to protect against enemy attacks.
Ammonites: An ancient people who lived east of the Jordan River. They were often in conflict with the people of Israel.
War: An armed conflict between two or more parties, often involving countries, groups, or nations.

Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Book 36: The chapter in Ezekiel containing prophecies about Israel's restoration and renewal of the people.
The word of the Lord: An expression used to describe God's speech or message through the prophet Ezekiel.
Israel: A people chosen by God, also the name of an ancient kingdom and a modern state in the Middle East.
Land: The area promised to the people of Israel by God, including historical Canaan.
Humiliation: The shame and disgrace experienced by the people of Israel as a result of their sins and exile.
Restoration: God's act of bringing the people of Israel back to their land and restoring their national identity.
Cleansing: The process by which God purifies the people of Israel from their sins and impurities.
Heart: A symbol for the inner being of a person, including thoughts, feelings, and will.
Spirit: God's life-giving power that works in human lives, creating transformation and renewal.

Jerusalem: The capital of ancient Israel, destroyed by the Babylonians in Jeremiah 39.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, known for conquering Jerusalem and taking the Jews into exile.
Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who destroyed Jerusalem and took the Jews into exile.
Exile: A condition where a person or group is forced to leave their homeland and live in another country.
Zedekiah: The last king of Judah, who was captured by Nebuchadnezzar and taken to Babylon.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's word and warned about Jerusalem's destruction.
The Lord's house: The temple in Jerusalem, the center of Jewish faith and worship, destroyed by the Babylonians.
Ruins: Destruction or collapse of something, as in this case referring to the fall of Jerusalem.
Godly fear: Fear and respect for God, often linked with obedience to God's will and commands.

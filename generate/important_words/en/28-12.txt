Ephraim: One of Joseph's sons and the ancestor of one of Israel's twelve tribes, often used as a symbol for all Israel.
Jacob: The patriarch of Israel's twelve tribes, also known as Israel after God changed his name. He was the son of Isaac and the grandson of Abraham.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Prophet: A person who receives messages from God and conveys them to the people. Hosea was such a prophet.
Canaan: The historical region in the Middle East that includes parts of present-day Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and Syria. This area was promised by God to Abraham's descendants.
Wickedness: An evil or unjust act, often committed against God's will.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and morally, in accordance with God's will and commands.
Turn back: To repent or turn away from sin and injustice, and seek God's grace and forgiveness.

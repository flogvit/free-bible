Sion: A name for Jerusalem, often used symbolically for God's people and their spiritual home.
The Lord's day: A term for a time of divine intervention, judgment, and salvation.
Purification: The process of being cleansed from sin and impurity, both spiritually and morally.
Glory: A radiant, shining aura that represents God's presence and majesty.
Protection: God's care and safeguarding of His people in times of trouble.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with God's will and moral law.
Survivors: Those who remain after a disaster or trial, often referring to the faithful among God's people.

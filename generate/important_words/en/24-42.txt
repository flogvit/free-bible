Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who received God's word and delivered it to the people.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, known for its power and culture, which conquered Jerusalem.
Fear: A feeling of anxiety or worry caused by a threatening danger, often linked to God's punishment.
Obedient: Being willing to follow instructions or commands, especially from God.
Sword: A symbol of war, destruction, and death, often used as an image of God's judgment and punishment.
Famine: Lack of food, leading to suffering and death; used as a picture of God's punishment.
Plague: A deadly disease that spreads quickly and causes great suffering and death; another image of God's punishment.
Refuge: A safe place where one can seek protection and security, often used as an image of God's care and grace.
Egypt: An ancient empire in North Africa, known for its power and culture, where some Israelites wanted to flee to escape Babylonian rule.

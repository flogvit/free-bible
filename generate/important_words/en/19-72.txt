Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting in a right and fair manner, in accordance with God's will.
Peace: A state of harmony, tranquility, and understanding between people or nations, free from conflict and war.
Judgment: God's ability and right to judge human actions and determine their destiny, based on their obedience to His commands.
Poor: People who live in economic hardship and lack basic resources and opportunities for a dignified life.
Oppressed: People who suffer under injustice, discrimination, or abuse of power from other individuals or systems.
Salvation: The act of delivering someone from danger, suffering, or oppression, often linked to God's intervention to help people in need.
Mercy: God's grace and compassion toward people, especially those who are suffering or in distress, which He shows by forgiving sins and giving help.
Blessing: A positive force or influence that comes from God, bringing happiness, prosperity, and protection to people and their surroundings.
Honor: The highest respect and recognition given to God for His greatness, power, and goodness.
Name: God's identity and character, representing His divine nature and attributes and distinguishing Him from other deities.

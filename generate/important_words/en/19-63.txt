God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Thirst: A strong longing or desire, often used in a spiritual context to describe a yearning for God and His presence.
Soul: The spiritual and immortal part of a human being, considered distinct from the physical body.
Body: The physical and material part of a human being, separated from the spiritual soul.
Power: Authority, strength, or control, especially as exercised by a deity or ruler.
Bless: To express wishes for well-being, happiness, and success for someone, often in a religious context.
Praise: To express admiration, gratitude, and reverence for a deity, usually through prayer, song, or worship.
Right hand: A symbol of God's power and protection, expressing His ability to strengthen and help His followers.

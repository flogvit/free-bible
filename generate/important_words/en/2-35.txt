Moses: The prophet and leader who led the Israelites out of Egypt and received the Ten Commandments from God.
Sabbath day: A holy day of rest to be kept weekly, where no work is to be done but time is to be used to honor God.
Holy place: A special place dedicated to God's worship and presence, often a temple or tabernacle.
Contribution: Gifts or donations given voluntarily for a specific cause, in this case the building of the holy place.
Craftsmen: People with special skills in art, design, and construction who work on making materials and objects.
Ark of the Covenant: A chest made of acacia wood and gold that contained the Ten Commandments, also known as the Ark of the Testimony.
High priest: The supreme religious leader responsible for performing the most important ceremonies and rituals in the holy place.

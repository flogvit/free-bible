Praise: To express admiration and gratitude to God for his goodness and greatness.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, often used as a substitute for God's name in the Old Testament.
God of Jacob: A reference to God as the ancestor of Jacob, one of Israel's patriarchs and forefathers of the twelve tribes of Israel.
Kingdom of Heaven: The spiritual realm where God reigns, and where faithful people will spend eternity with him.
Savior: A person or divine being who rescues others from danger, suffering, or destruction. In Psalm 146, it refers to God as protector and helper of his people.
Widow: A woman who has lost her spouse through death and has not remarried.
Fatherless: A person who has lost their father, often used to describe children without caregivers or support.
The Way: A metaphor for life's journey and the choices and actions we make throughout our lives.

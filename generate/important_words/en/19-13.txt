Psalm: A poetic song or tribute, often used in a religious context to express feelings and thoughts about God and faith.
Lord: A title frequently used for God, referring to his authority and power over all creation.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose an individual or God's will, and which can cause suffering and conflict.
Prayer: Communication with God, in which one expresses gratitude, reverence, concerns, and desires.
Heart: Symbolizes the emotions, intentions, and inner state of a person. In the Book of Psalms, the heart is often used to describe a person's relationship with God.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy toward humanity, even when they do not deserve it.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, death, and eternal separation from him. Salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ.
Praise: A song or tribute that expresses joy, gratitude, and admiration for God and his works.

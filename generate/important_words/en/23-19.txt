The Lord's Day: A time of divine intervention and judgment against the nations.
Egypt: An ancient and powerful empire in North Africa, often used as a symbol of worldly power and resistance to God.
Nile: The longest river in Africa and the lifeline of Egypt, associated with the country's wealth and fertility.
Assyrians: An ancient Mesopotamian empire known for its military strength and brutality, often used as an instrument of God's judgment.
Isaiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel and their kings.
Altar: A holy place where sacrifices and worship were performed in honor of God.
Pillar: A monument or structure that symbolizes a covenant or connection between God and humanity.
Lord of Hosts: A title for God meaning "the Lord of the heavenly armies," emphasizing his sovereignty and power.

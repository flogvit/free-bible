David: A young shepherd and later king of Israel, known for his courage and devotion.
Ziklag: A city in the southern part of the kingdom of Judah, which David and his men received from the Philistine king Achish.
Keilah: A city in Judah where David rescued its inhabitants from a Philistine attack.
Saul: The first king of Israel, who hunted down David to kill him because he saw him as a threat to his rule.
Jonathan: Saul's son and David's close friend, who helped David and protected him from his father.
Priests: Religious leaders who performed ritual acts and sacrifices according to the Jewish faith.
The Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God, often concerning future events or divine guidance.
Ziphites: Inhabitants of the town of Ziph who reported David's location to King Saul.
En-Gedi: A wilderness area where David hid from Saul and his men.

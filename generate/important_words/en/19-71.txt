Refuge: A safe place or protection, especially in a spiritual context. In Psalm 71, the psalmist seeks God's refuge and protection.
Lord: A title used to show respect and reverence toward God. In this context, the Lord is the one who gives strength, protection, and salvation.
Salvation: The term used to describe God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences. Salvation also includes a hope for eternal life in God's presence.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose the psalmist or God's people. Enemies can be both human and spiritual, and the psalmist asks God for protection against them.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and justly according to God's laws and will. The psalmist prays that God's righteousness will be demonstrated through his actions and judgment.
Hope: A positive expectation for the future, based on God's promises and goodness. The psalmist expresses his hope in God and His ability to protect and save.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, honor, and worship to God through song or poetry. The psalmist praises God for His goodness and power, and encourages others to do the same.

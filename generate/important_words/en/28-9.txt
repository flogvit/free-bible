Punishment: God's consequence for the people of Israel due to their sins and idolatry.
Idolatry: The practice of worshipping other gods or deities besides the true God.
Ephraim: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, often used as a representation of all Israel in the book of Hosea.
Prophet: A person chosen by God to convey His message and warnings to the people.
Fertility: The ability to produce offspring, crops, or results; in this context, fertility is a blessing from God.
Exile: Being forced to leave one's homeland, often as a punishment or consequence.
Assyria: An ancient empire in the Middle East that conquered and deported the people of Israel in the 8th century BC.
God's wrath: God's strong displeasure and anger toward sin and injustice.
Ommah: A city in Israel that was destroyed as a result of God's punishment.

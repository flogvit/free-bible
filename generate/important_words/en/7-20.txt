Israelites: A people descended from the twelve sons of Israel who constitute God's chosen people in the Old Testament.
Benjamin: One of the twelve sons of Israel and the ancestor of the tribe of Benjamin, one of the twelve tribes of Israel.
Gibeah: A city in the territory of the tribe of Benjamin where a terrible event occurred that led to civil war between Benjamin and the other tribes of Israel.
Levite: A member of the tribe of Levi, appointed to carry out religious and ceremonial duties in Israel.
Warriors: Groups of fighters or soldiers who fight together in a conflict.
The Lord: Another name for God, often used to show respect and reverence.
Civil war: An armed conflict between groups within the same nation or society.
The Ark of the Covenant: A sacred chest that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments and symbolized God's presence among the Israelites.
Repentance: An action or process in which one turns from sin and seeks forgiveness from God.
Victory: To win or overcome an enemy in a conflict or battle.

Joshua: The main character in the book and leader of the Israelites after Moses' death, who led them into the promised land.
Covenant: A binding agreement or commitment between two parties, here between God and the Israelites.
Serve: To obey and follow an authority, in this case God, and carry out his will.
Idols: False gods or objects that people worship instead of the true God.
Ancestors: Those who came before us in the family line, especially those who founded the nation of Israel, such as Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
Promise: A declaration from God to give something specific to someone, in this case the land of Canaan to the Israelites.
Witnesses: Persons or things that confirm the truth of an event or statement; here used of heaven and earth as witnesses against the Israelites if they break their covenant with God.
Choose: To make a decision or take a stand, here regarding whether to serve God or other gods.

Eliphaz: One of Job's friends who criticizes and gives advice to Job in his trials.
Job: The main character in the book of Job, a righteous man who suffers great losses and afflictions.
Trials: Tests or challenges that people face in life, often to learn or grow.
Righteousness: Acting morally and ethically correct, in accordance with God's will and commandments.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good decisions and live a good life.
God's punishment: God's action to correct or punish people for their sins and injustice.
Listening ear: The ability to be attentive and understand others' perspectives and feelings.
Advice: Guidance or recommendations given to others to help them navigate difficult situations.

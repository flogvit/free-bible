Love: A central attribute of God that involves care, devotion, and loyalty toward people.
Faithfulness: The ability to keep promises and remain loyal to a person, especially in the relationship between God and humanity.
Sin: Actions or thoughts that break God's laws and moral standards, leading to separation from Him.
Punishment: The consequences of sin, often expressed as suffering or misfortune, which God allows to correct and discipline people.
Repentance: A strong expression of sorrow and regret over sin, showing the desire to turn back to God and live according to His will.
Knowledge: Understanding of God's will and plan, as well as insight into how to live a life in harmony with His purpose.
Humility: An attitude of modesty and respect toward God, where one acknowledges their own limitations and dependence on Him.

Judah: One of Jacob's twelve sons and the ancestor of the tribe of Judah, which later became part of the kingdom of Israel.
Perez: Son of Judah and Tamar, and one of the ancestors of King David and Jesus Christ.
Shelah: The third son of Judah and his wife Bathshua. He was a brother to Perez and Serah.
Runners: A group of men known for their speed and skill in warfare, mentioned in connection with the descendants of the Kenites.
Kenites: A people who lived in Canaan before the Israelites entered the land. They were descendants of Cain, and some of them became allies with the Israelites.
Eshton: A leader from the tribe of Judah mentioned in 1 Chronicles 4 as one of the ancestors of several prominent families in Israel.
Asareel: Another leader from the tribe of Judah mentioned in 1 Chronicles 4 as one of the ancestors of several prominent families in Israel.
Jabez: A highly respected man from the tribe of Judah who asked God for blessing and protection, and God answered his prayer.

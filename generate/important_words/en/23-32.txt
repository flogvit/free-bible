Righteousness: A moral principle that involves fair and impartial treatment of all people, and is a central value in the book of Isaiah 32.
Peace: A state of harmony and lack of conflict between people or nations, which the prophet Isaiah promises in his message about a future peaceful kingdom.
King: A monarch or ruler who holds supreme authority in a kingdom. In Isaiah 32 this refers to a righteous and good leader who will govern the people with wisdom and justice.
Protection: Care and security offered by a person or power to prevent harm or danger. In Isaiah 32 God provides protection and safety for His people.
Spirit: In this context, spirit refers to God's presence and power in people's lives. The prophet Isaiah speaks of God's Spirit being poured out over the people, bringing transformation and blessing.
Judgment: A decision or conclusion based on God's righteousness and morality. In Isaiah 32 God's judgment will lead to justice and peace in society.

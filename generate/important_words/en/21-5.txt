Vanity: The term used to describe meaninglessness, emptiness, or futile striving for worldly things, which is a central theme in the book of Ecclesiastes.
Work: The effort people put in to achieve results, but in the book of Ecclesiastes it is often seen as meaningless and futile.
Wealth: An abundance of material goods and money, which according to Ecclesiastes can lead to envy, greed, and dissatisfaction.
Pleasure: The feeling of joy and satisfaction that comes from enjoying life's blessings, but which can also be futile and empty according to Ecclesiastes.
Time: A unit of measurement for the duration of events and situations, which in the book of Ecclesiastes is used to emphasize that everything has its time and season.
Fear of God: Respect and awe for God, which in the book of Ecclesiastes is considered more important than worldly wisdom and wealth.

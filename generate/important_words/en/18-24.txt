Injustice: Unreasonable or wrongful treatment of others, often linked to a lack of justice and morality.
Justice: The principle of acting in accordance with ethical norms and laws, so that all are treated equally and fairly.
The oppressed: People who suffer under unjust conditions, often as a result of abuse of power or discrimination.
The poor: Individuals or groups lacking economic resources and thus having limited opportunities to achieve an acceptable standard of living.
The wicked: Persons who act immorally or harmfully toward others, often with the intent to cause suffering or injustice.
The righteous: People who follow ethical principles and treat others with respect, justice, and compassion.
Punishment: Consequences or reactions resulting from actions that violate moral or legal norms, usually intended to correct or prevent further violations.
Mercy: The ability to show compassion and forgiveness toward others, especially those who are suffering or have done wrong.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to 1 Mosebok, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.

Rehoboam: Son of King Solomon and successor as king over Israel. He increased taxes and forced labor, which led to a division in the kingdom.
Jeroboam: A former officer under King Solomon who became leader of the ten northern tribes of Israel after the kingdom was divided.
Kingdom: A territory or society ruled by a monarch, in this case the kingdom of Israel.
Division: The process by which a kingdom or society is split into smaller units due to disagreements or conflicts.
Prophet: A person who claims to convey divine messages from God to people, often with the purpose of guiding or warning.
Ahijah: The prophet from Shiloh who predicted that Jeroboam would become king over ten tribes and that the kingdom would be divided.
Golden Calves: Two idols that Jeroboam made and placed in the cities of Bethel and Dan to prevent the people from going to Jerusalem to worship.
Sin: An action or thought that goes against God's will and can lead to spiritual separation from God.

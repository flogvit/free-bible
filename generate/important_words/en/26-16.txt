Jerusalem: The city that is central in Ezekiel chapter 16 and symbolizes God's people.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that opposes God's will and moral law.
Grace: God's unconditional love and forgiveness toward people, regardless of their merit.
Covenant: A term describing a pact or agreement between God and his people.
Adultery: A metaphor for turning away from God and worshiping other gods, regarded as unfaithfulness to God.
Punishment: The consequences of sin and rebellion against God, including suffering and destruction.
Restoration: God's promise to restore his people and their relationship with him after a time of punishment and suffering.
Alliance: An agreement between God and his people, where God promises protection and blessings in exchange for obedience and faithfulness.

Job: The main character in the Book of Job, a righteous and wealthy man who experiences great trials and suffering.
Satan: An evil spiritual being who challenges God's authority and accuses Job of serving God only because of his blessings.
Trials: Challenges and difficulties that Job must endure to prove his faithfulness to God.
Wealth: Material prosperity and resources that Job loses as part of his trials.
Children: Job had ten children who died during his trials, which deepened his suffering.
Patience: The ability to endure hardships and adversity without losing faith or hope. Job shows great patience through his trials.

Jerusalem: The capital of ancient Israel, the center for religious and political activity.
Elam: An ancient civilization located east of Mesopotamia, mentioned as an enemy of Jerusalem.
Kir: A city in present-day Syria, noted as an ally of Elam against Jerusalem.
Shebna: A high official in the kingdom of Judah, criticized by Isaiah for his arrogance and abuse of power.
Eliakim: Another official in Judah, appointed by God to replace Shebna as leader.
The Valley of Vision: A symbolic place where the prophet Isaiah received revelations from God concerning Jerusalem's fate.
Sephharad: A place, possibly in Spain, mentioned as a location where the Jews will be gathered after the exile.

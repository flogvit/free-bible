Philistines: A people group who lived in Canaan and were enemies of Israel during several periods of history.
Ark: Also called the "Ark of the Covenant," a holy chest that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, and represented God's presence among the Israelites.
Dagon: The chief god of the Philistines, often associated with fertility and grain.
Ekron: One of the most important cities of the Philistines, mentioned several times in 1 Samuel 6.
Ashdod: Another major Philistine city, where the Ark was first brought after the Philistines captured it from the Israelites.
Tumors: Plagues that struck the Philistines as a result of taking the Ark. May refer to a form of boils or swellings.
Golden rats: Offerings made by the Philistines in gold to make peace with God after taking the Ark and suffering under plagues.
Beth-shemesh: A city in Judah where the Ark was returned to the Israelites after the Philistines sent it back on a cart pulled by cows.

Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to grasp and apply knowledge in a practical and effective way.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and morally, as well as giving each person what they deserve.
Unrighteousness: Actions that are immoral, unjust, or harmful to others.
Discipline: Training, guidance, and correction used to teach and shape a person's character.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, the Almighty Creator.
Humility: The quality of being modest, not proud or arrogant, and recognizing one's own limitations.
Pride: An excessive feeling of self-importance, arrogance, and self-exaltation, often leading to bad actions and consequences.
Work: Effort and activity carried out to reach a goal, achieve results, or earn a living.
Slothfulness: Lack of willingness to work, take initiative, or make an effort in life.

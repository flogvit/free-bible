Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence toward God, acknowledging his authority and power.
Refuge: A place or state of safety and protection. In Psalm 31, the psalmist seeks God's refuge in difficult times.
Deliver: To set free from captivity, oppression, or danger. The psalmist asks God to deliver him from his enemies and suffering.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love toward humanity, despite their sins and failures.
Fear: A feeling of awe and reverence for God's power and holiness. In this context, it also means to obey and trust in God.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or threaten the psalmist. The psalmist asks God for help and protection against his enemies.
Hand: Symbolizes God's power, control, and care. In Psalm 31, the psalmist commits his spirit into God's hand, meaning he trusts God for his salvation and protection.
Righteousness: God's moral standard and principles of right and wrong. The psalmist asks that God act according to his righteousness and guide him on the right path.

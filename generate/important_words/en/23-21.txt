Babylon: An ancient city and kingdom in Mesopotamia, often used as a symbol of evil and idolatry in the Bible.
The Lord: A title for God, the Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Prophecy: A divine revelation or message, usually delivered by a prophet, concerning future events or God's will.
Watchman: A person who keeps watch, monitors, and warns against dangers. In this context, the watchman represents the prophet Isaiah.
Elam: An ancient kingdom in southwestern Iran, mentioned as an ally against Babylon.
Media: An ancient kingdom in northwestern Iran, also mentioned as an ally against Babylon.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or condemnation of human sin and evil.
The Desert: A dry, inhospitable land area, often used as a picture of suffering and trials in the Bible.
The Sea: Can represent peoples or nations in a symbolic sense.

Eliphaz: One of Job's three friends who attempts to explain Job's sufferings and offers counsel.
Righteousness: A moral principle of acting in accordance with divine laws and ethical conduct.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a reasonable way to make good decisions.
Darkness: Symbolizes ignorance, evil, or hidden things that are not easily understood.
Light: Represents knowledge, goodness, and clarity in understanding and insight.
Secrets: Things that are hidden or unknown, which may refer to God's plans or purposes.
Punishment: The consequences of actions that break divine or moral laws, often seen as just retribution.

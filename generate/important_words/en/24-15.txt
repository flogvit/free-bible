Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who received God's message and delivered it to the people of Israel.
Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or consequences for human actions, especially their disobedience toward him.
Repentance: The feeling of regret over past actions, often linked with recognizing sin and a desire to change.
Enemies: Individuals, nations, or forces that oppose God's people and his will.
Exile: The state of being forced away from one's homeland, often as a result of war or God's judgment.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia that conquered Jerusalem and led the Jews into exile.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and mercy toward people, despite their sin and disobedience.
Repentance: The act of turning away from sin and turning toward God in sorrow and faith.
Comfort: To give strength, hope, and encouragement in difficult times, often through God's presence and promises.

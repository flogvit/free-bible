Offer: A ritual act where one gives something of value to God, as a way to show reverence and gratitude.
Burnt offering: A type of sacrifice in which an animal is burned completely or partially on an altar as a gift to God.
Sin offering: An offering given to atone for sins and cleanse the person who has sinned.
High priest: The highest religious leader in ancient Israel, responsible for performing special ceremonies and sacrifices.
Altar: A structure where sacrifices and rituals are carried out as part of religious worship.
Fat: The most valuable and nutrient-rich part of an animal, often offered to God in burnt offerings and sin offerings.
Liver: An internal organ in animals, often used as part of a sacrifice to God.
Kidneys: Internal organs in animals, also used as part of sacrifices to God.
Fire: A symbol of God's presence and power, used in burnt offerings and other rituals.
Cleansing: A process to remove sin and impurities, so that one may be purified and restored in God's sight.

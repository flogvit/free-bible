Temple: A holy building dedicated to God, where people came to worship and offer sacrifices; in the book of Jeremiah chapter 7, the temple in Jerusalem is central.
Shiloh: A city in ancient Israel, formerly the location of the tabernacle and the ark of the covenant; used as a warning that God's protection is not guaranteed.
Hearken: To be attentive and willing to follow God's instructions; in the book of Jeremiah chapter 7, the people of Israel are criticized for not heeding God.
Sacrifice: A gift or act given to God as a form of worship, thanksgiving, or atonement for sins; the book of Jeremiah chapter 7 criticizes the people's empty rituals and lack of sincerity in their sacrifices.
Justice: Ethical and moral principles that reflect God's will; the book of Jeremiah chapter 7 calls on the people of Israel to practice justice in their lives.
Unjust deeds: Actions that contradict God's will and morality; the book of Jeremiah chapter 7 accuses the people of Israel of committing unjust deeds and warns them of the consequences.
Punishment: God's response to human disobedience and sin; in the book of Jeremiah chapter 7, the people of Israel are warned that they will be punished for their godless actions and rejection of God's commands.
Repent: To regret and change one's behavior to return to God's way; the book of Jeremiah chapter 7 calls on the people of Israel to repent from their sinful lifestyle.

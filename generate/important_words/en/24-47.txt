Philistines: An ancient people who lived in the coastal regions southwest of Canaan and were enemies of Israel.
Sword: A long, sharp blade used as a weapon in battle. In this context, it symbolizes God's judgment and punishment.
Lord: A title referring to God, the almighty Creator and Sustainer of the universe.
Prophet: A person chosen by God to deliver His message and warnings to humanity. Jeremiah is such a prophet.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who received God's word and delivered it to the people of Israel and their neighbors.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and consequences for human actions, often linked to punishment or reward.
Gaza: A city in Philistine territory, also known as Gaza, which was struck by God's judgment because of its wickedness.
Ashkelon: Another city in Philistine territory that was also struck by God's judgment.
Destruction: The process of completely destroying something, often as a result of God's judgment over sin and evil.

David: The second king of Israel, who succeeded King Saul and united the twelve tribes.
Jerusalem: A city in the Middle East, also known as Zion, which David made the capital of his kingdom.
Fortress: A fortified structure or building used for defense and protection.
Hiram: The king of Tyre, who helped David build his palace by sending materials and craftsmen.
Philistines: A people who lived in Canaan and were enemies of the Israelites. David defeated them several times.
Baalzebub: A name for a fortress in Jerusalem, meaning "shame of Baal." David captured it from the Jebusites.
Jebusites: The inhabitants of Jerusalem before David conquered the city. They belonged to one of the Canaanite tribes.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.
Ark of the Covenant: A holy relic that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments and symbolized God's presence among the Israelites.

Solomon: The king of Israel and son of King David, known for his wisdom and the building of the temple in Jerusalem.
Temple: The holy place in Jerusalem where the Israelites worshiped God and offered sacrifices; built by King Solomon.
Cities: Places where people live and interact, mentioned in 2 Chronicles as places over which Solomon ruled or had influence.
Hiram: The king of Tyre who helped Solomon obtain materials and labor for the building of the temple.
Fortresses: Fortified buildings or areas used for defense and protection against enemies.
Levy: A group of workers or soldiers recruited from the population to carry out specific tasks, such as building the temple.
Sacrifices: Gifts, usually food or animals, given to God as a form of worship and gratitude.
Priests: Religious leaders in Israelite society who performed ceremonies and sacrifices in the temple.
Sabbath: The seventh day of the week, on which God rested after creating the world, and which the Israelites were to keep holy.

Chosen: In Deuteronomy 7 this refers to the people of Israel whom God has chosen as His special people.
Holy: Means to be set apart or dedicated for a divine purpose; in this context, the people of Israel are called holy because they are chosen by God.
Nations: This refers to other peoples and nations outside of Israel, who were often regarded as pagans.
Alliances: This means partnerships or agreements between two parties, often between nations. In Deuteronomy 7 the Israelites are warned against making such alliances with other nations.
Destroy: To remove completely, ruin, or eliminate. In this context God speaks of destroying the nations occupying the promised land before the Israelites arrive.
Idols: This refers to false gods, statues, or symbols representing false deities. The Israelites are instructed to destroy these when they enter the promised land.
Bless: To grant divine favor, protection, or prosperity. God promises to bless the Israelites if they obey His commands and stay away from other gods.

Nehemiah: A Jewish leader who helped rebuild the walls of Jerusalem and organize the people after the exile in Babylon.
Artaxerxes: The Persian king who allowed Nehemiah to return to Jerusalem to rebuild the city and its defenses.
Jerusalem: The capital of Israel, a holy place for the Jews, where the temple was built and where Nehemiah worked on the rebuilding.
Walls: Structures built around Jerusalem to protect the city and its inhabitants from hostile attacks.
Sanballat: A Samaritan leader who opposed the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls and tried to hinder Nehemiah's work.
Tobiah: An Ammonite official who also opposed the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls and cooperated with Sanballat.
The hand of God: An expression referring to God's guidance, protection, and strength in the work of rebuilding Jerusalem and its defenses.
Prayer: Communication with God where people ask for help or guidance, or give thanks for blessings they have received.

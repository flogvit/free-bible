Psalm: A poetic and musical form of prayer or praise, often directed toward God.
Lord: A title used to show respect and reverence for God; denotes his authority and power.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, evil, and death, and giving them eternal life.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose God and his people, and which can lead to destruction or suffering.
Refuge: A safe place or protection that God gives to those who seek him in difficult times.
Righteousness: God's moral standard and principles, which he desires people to follow and imitate.
Praise: To express gratitude, reverence, and devotion to God through song, prayer, or other forms of worship.
Mercy: God's grace and love toward people, especially when they do not deserve it because of their sins.
Judgment: God's just evaluation and decision regarding human actions and destiny, both in this life and the eternal one.
The Nations: Groups of people, often defined by geographic, cultural, or ethnic boundaries, who may be recipients of God's blessings or judgment.

Inheritance: A right or property transferred from one generation to the next, often through family ties.
Canaan: A historical region in the Middle East that includes parts of present-day Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and Jordan. God promised this land to Abraham's descendants.
Moabites: An ancient Semitic people who lived in Moab, an area east of the Dead Sea and the Jordan River.
Edomites: An ancient Semitic people who lived in Edom, a region south of the Dead Sea and west of the Arabah desert.
Sihon: King of the Amorites who ruled over Heshbon, mentioned in Deuteronomy 2. The Israelites fought against him and conquered his land.
Og: King of Bashan, mentioned in Deuteronomy 2. He was defeated by the Israelites during their journey toward Canaan.
The children of Israel: A term for the Jewish people, descendants of Jacob (also called Israel), son of Isaac and grandson of Abraham.

Plagues: Unpleasant and destructive events that God sends over Egypt to get Pharaoh to release the people of Israel.
Pharaoh: The Egyptian king who holds the Israelites in slavery and refuses to let them go.
Moses: The prophet chosen by God to lead the Israelites out of Egypt and slavery.
Aaron: Moses' brother, who serves as his spokesperson and helps him in meeting with Pharaoh.
Locust: A type of grasshopper that comes as the eighth plague and destroys the crops in Egypt.
Darkness: The ninth plague, where Egypt is covered by a thick darkness for three days.
The Lord: Another name for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Prayer: Communication with God, often to ask for help, guidance, or gratitude.
Obey: To follow instructions, especially those given by God or an authority figure.

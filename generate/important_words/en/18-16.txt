Misery: A state of great distress, suffering, or sorrow, often experienced by Job in his trials.
Comfort: Support and care given to alleviate pain, grief, or worry; lacking in Job's situation.
Accusations: Charges or criticism directed at a person, as Job's friends direct toward him.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and justly; Job asserts his own righteousness throughout the book.
God's wrath: God's strong displeasure or anger, which Job feels is directed against him without cause.
Witness in heaven: A divine observer who can confirm a person's innocence or righteousness; Job longs for such a witness.
Prayer: An appeal to God for help, guidance, or gratitude; Job prays to God throughout his suffering.

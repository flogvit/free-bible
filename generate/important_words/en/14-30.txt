Hezekiah: King of Judah who encouraged the people to turn back to God and restore the Passover celebration.
Passover: A Jewish festival celebrating deliverance from slavery in Egypt under Moses' leadership.
Temple: A holy place for worship and offerings, built by King Solomon in Jerusalem as a dwelling for God's presence.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, chosen by God to serve as priests and assist with temple duties.
Priests: Religious leaders who performed ceremonies and sacrifices in the temple on behalf of the people.
Offering: A gift or act given to God as an expression of worship and gratitude.
Sin: Transgression against God's law, leading to spiritual separation from God and punishment.
Repentance: The act of turning away from sin, seeking forgiveness, and returning to God.
Peace Offering: An offering shared between the priest, the offerer, and others, symbolizing fellowship between God and people.

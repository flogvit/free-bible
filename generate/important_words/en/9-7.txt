Samuel: A prophet and judge in Israel who played a key role in the transition from the period of judges to the monarchy.
Philistines: A people living along the coast of Canaan who were enemies of Israel during several periods of their history.
Ebenezer: A memorial stone set up by Samuel to mark the place where God helped Israel defeat the Philistines.
Mizpah: A city in Israel where Samuel gathered the people to ask for forgiveness and renew their covenant with God.
Ashdod: One of the most important cities of the Philistines, where they brought the ark after defeating Israel.
The Ark: The ark of the covenant, a holy chest containing the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments and symbolizing God's presence.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, often involving conditions and promises of protection and blessing.
Repentance: The process of changing one's mind and heart to turn away from sin and toward God.
Prayer: Communication with God, often used to express gratitude, ask for help, or seek guidance.
Sacrifice: A gift given to God, often in the form of animals, food, or other objects, as an expression of devotion and worship.

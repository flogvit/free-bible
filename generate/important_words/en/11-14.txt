Prophecy: A divine revelation or message often conveyed through a prophet.
Prophet: A person who has received special insight and revelations from God and communicates these messages to people.
Jeroboam: The first king of the northern kingdom of Israel after the kingdom was divided into two.
Rehoboam: King of the southern kingdom of Judah and son of King Solomon.
Ahijah: The prophet from Shiloh who predicted Jeroboam's rise to the throne and foretold his downfall.
Idol: An image or object that is worshiped as a god, but is not the true God.
Sin: A violation of God's laws and commands, leading to spiritual separation from God.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment for human sin and disobedience.
Downward spiral: A process in which a situation gradually worsens and becomes increasingly difficult to escape.

Korah: A Levite who led a rebellion against Moses and Aaron.
Rebellion: The act of opposing authority or control, especially in an organized manner.
Dathan and Abiram: Two Israelites who supported Korah's rebellion against Moses and Aaron.
Earthquake: Sudden and often violent movements in the Earth's crust caused by geological processes.
Open: In this context, it means that the earth splits apart to swallow those involved in the rebellion.
Moses: The leader of the people of Israel and a prophet chosen by God to lead them out of Egypt and into the promised land.
Aaron: Moses' older brother, high priest, and a central figure in the priesthood of the Israelites.
The Lord: Another designation for God, the almighty Creator.
Offering: A gift or act given to God as a sign of reverence, gratitude, or atonement for sins.
Incense: Fragrant substances burned as part of religious ceremonies, symbolizing the prayers of believers rising up to God.
High Priest: The supreme leader of the priesthood, responsible for performing religious ceremonies and offerings.

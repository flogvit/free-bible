Ark: Holy ark that contained Israel's covenant with God, including the Ten Commandments.
Philistines: An ancient people who lived in Canaan and were enemies of the Israelites.
Eli: High priest and judge in Israel who raised the prophet Samuel.
Hophni and Phinehas: Eli's two corrupt sons who abused their priestly authority.
Samuel: Prophet and last judge in Israel who helped with the transition from the period of judges to the monarchy.
Battle of Aphek: A battle between the Israelites and Philistines where the Israelites suffered a great defeat and the ark was captured.
Ichabod: The name of Phinehas' son, meaning "the glory has departed from Israel," symbolizing the loss of God's presence.
Prophecy: God's message delivered through a prophet, often as a warning or guidance for the people.

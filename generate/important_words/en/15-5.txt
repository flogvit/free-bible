Ezra: A Jewish scribe and priest who helped rebuild the temple in Jerusalem after the exile in Babylon.
Book 5: The fifth chapter of the book of Ezra, part of the Old Testament, which describes the rebuilding of the temple and the opposition they faced.
Darius: A Persian king who ruled during the rebuilding of the temple in Jerusalem and gave permission to continue the work.
The Temple: A holy place for the Jews in Jerusalem, the center of their religious practice and worship.
Prophets: God's messengers who conveyed his will and message to people. In Ezra 5, Haggai and Zechariah are mentioned as prophets who supported the rebuilding of the temple.
Rebuilding: The process of rebuilding the temple in Jerusalem after its destruction and the exile in Babylon.
Tattenai: A Persian governor who questioned the rebuilding of the temple and sent a letter to King Darius to confirm permission.
The Letter: The document Tattenai sent to King Darius to obtain confirmation of the permission to rebuild the temple, which led Darius to confirm the authorization and support the project.

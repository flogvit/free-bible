David's Victory: An event where King David, the king of Israel, defeats enemies with God's help.
Philistines: A people who lived in Canaan and were enemies of the Israelites; often in conflict with them.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.
The Lord's Command: God's instructions and orders given to humans through prophets or direct revelation.
Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel and an important religious center for Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.
King: A monarch who rules over a kingdom or country; in this case, King David of Israel.
Prophet: A person who receives and conveys messages from God, usually concerning future events or divine guidance.
Sabaoth: A Hebrew word meaning "hosts" and often used to describe God as "the Lord of hosts."
Temple: A holy place where people worship God and offer sacrifices; in Jerusalem it was built by King Solomon, David's son.

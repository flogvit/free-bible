Cain: Adam and Eve's first son, who killed his brother Abel and thus became the first murderer in the Bible.
Abel: Adam and Eve's second son, who was killed by his brother Cain in an outburst of jealousy.
Offering: A gift or act given to God to show gratitude, reverence, or atonement for sins.
Farmer: A person who works the land to grow plants and produce food.
Shepherd: A person who cares for and manages livestock, especially sheep and goats.
Jealousy: A negative feeling that arises when someone envies another's achievements, possessions, or relationships.
Punishment: Consequences or reactions to bad actions or sins, often given by God or society.
Nod: A land east of Eden where Cain settled after being driven out by God for killing his brother Abel.
Lamech: A descendant of Cain, known for being the first polygamist and for killing a man in self-defense.

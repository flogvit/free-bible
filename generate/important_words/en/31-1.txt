Obadiah: One of the twelve minor prophets in the Old Testament, the author of the book of Obadiah.
Edom: An ancient kingdom southeast of Israel and Judah, inhabited by descendants of Esau.
Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or reward for human actions.
Brotherly strife: A conflict or rivalry between close relatives, especially siblings.
House of Israel: The term for the people of Israel, including the twelve tribes that made up ancient Israel.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem, often used as a symbol of God's dwelling and protection.
Restoration: The process of restoring something to its former state, such as when God restores Israel after exile.
Exile: The condition of being forced to leave one's homeland, often as punishment for disobedience to God.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with God's will and moral law.

Joseph: One of Jacob's sons, known for his ability to interpret dreams and his righteousness.
Potiphar: An Egyptian officer who bought Joseph as a slave and put him in charge of his household.
Potiphar's wife: The woman who tried to seduce Joseph but was rejected by him.
False Accusations: Potiphar's wife accused Joseph of attempting to rape her after he rejected her advances.
Prison: The place where Joseph was sent after being falsely accused by Potiphar's wife.
Dream Interpreter: The ability to interpret dreams, which Joseph was known for having.
Trust: Joseph was entrusted with great responsibility both in Potiphar's house and later in prison.
Integrity: Joseph's moral principles and honesty, which led him to reject Potiphar's wife's advances.
God's Presence: God was with Joseph throughout all his trials and gave him success and progress.

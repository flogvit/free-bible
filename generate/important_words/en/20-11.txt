Wisdom: The ability to think and act wisely, based on experience, knowledge, and sound judgment.
Righteousness: Acting rightly and in accordance with moral principles, laws, or ethics.
Honesty: The quality of being truthful, sincere, and free from lies or deceit.
Humility: Being modest and not having an excessively high opinion of oneself or one's achievements.
Integrity: Steadfastness in following a set of moral or ethical principles, and being wholehearted and honest.
Discipline: Self-control or restraint necessary to achieve a desired goal or behave in an acceptable manner.
Generosity: The willingness to give or share what one has with others, especially those in need.
Diligence: The quality of being industrious and hardworking, showing perseverance and effort in work.
Fear of God: An attitude of respect, reverence, and obedience toward God and His commands.
Truth: That which aligns with reality, facts, or what actually happened; the opposite of lies and deceit.

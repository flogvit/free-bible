Lord: The name used for God in the Old Testament, referring to his authority and power.
Covenant: An agreement or promise between God and people, especially with the nation of Israel.
Children of Israel: The term for the descendants of Jacob (also called Israel), who make up the twelve tribes of ancient Israel.
Egypt: The land where the children of Israel were slaves under the Egyptian pharaoh.
Moses: The prophet and leader whom God chose to lead the children of Israel out of Egypt and into the promised land.
Aaron: Moses' brother and spokesman, who helped him confront Pharaoh and lead the children of Israel out of Egypt.
Pharaoh: The Egyptian king who held the children of Israel as slaves and refused to let them go.
Plagues: The ten disasters that God sent upon Egypt to punish Pharaoh and his people, and to make them release the children of Israel.
Promised Land: The region that God promised to give to the children of Israel as their homeland, also known as Canaan.
Destruction: The term used to describe God's action of destroying enemies or evil forces.

Joab: A military commander and nephew of King David, known for his loyalty and military skill.
Sheba: A rebellious leader from the tribe of Benjamin who challenged David's authority as king.
Amasa: A relative of David and former commander of Absalom's army, appointed by David to replace Joab.
Abishai: Joab's brother and one of David's most powerful warriors, who helped Joab crush Sheba's rebellion.
Bichri: A designation for Sheba's followers, who took part in the revolt against King David.
Abel-Beth-Maacah: A city in Israel where Sheba sought refuge, and where Joab laid siege to him.
Wise woman: An inhabitant of Abel-Beth-Maacah who negotiated with Joab and helped end the siege by handing over Sheba's head.

Sexual Relations: In Leviticus 18 this refers to rules and restrictions concerning sexual behavior and relationships between people.
Close Relatives: This term refers to family members who are closely related, such as parents, siblings, children, and other relatives.
Incest: Sexual relations between close relatives, which is regarded as a serious sin according to the Bible.
Offspring: Children or descendants of a person.
Foreign Woman: A woman who is not a close relative, and thus permitted to enter into a sexual relationship with according to biblical rules.
Sacrifice: An action or object that is offered to God, often as a symbol of devotion, gratitude, or atonement for sins.
The Land of Canaan: The area that the Israelites were to occupy after leaving Egypt, and where they were to live in accordance with God's laws and commands.

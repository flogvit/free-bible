God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Lord: A title for God that shows his authority and power over all things and everyone.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose God and his purposes.
Save: To rescue or deliver from danger, suffering, or destruction.
Victory: To achieve success or dominance over an opponent or a difficult situation.
Mountain: Often symbolizes divine presence and power in the Bible.
Blessing: A favorable gift from God that brings prosperity, happiness, or protection.
Sing: To express praise, gratitude, or joy through song, especially to God.
People: Humans who are part of a community, nation, or God's chosen ones.

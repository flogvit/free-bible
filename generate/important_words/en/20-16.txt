Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The capacity to grasp the meaning of something, especially complex ideas or situations.
Heart: In this context, it refers to a person's inner being, including thoughts, feelings, and will.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with moral laws and ethics; fair treatment and conduct.
Humility: The quality of being modest and having a low view of one's own importance; lack of self-exaltation.
Pride: An excessive sense of self-worth or self-respect, often leading to arrogance and contempt for others.
Plans: Thoughts, intentions, and goals that people have for the future.
The Lord's way: The divine plan and guidance that God gives for human lives.
Discipline: Training, correction, and instruction intended to correct and shape behavior and character.
Blessedness: A feeling of well-being and contentment that comes from living a good and meaningful life.

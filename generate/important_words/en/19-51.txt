Sin: An act or state of doing something wrong, especially in relation to God's law and morality.
Grace: God's goodness and mercy shown toward people, despite their sinful nature and actions.
Repentance: A symbolic action representing turning away from sin and toward God.
Cleansing: The process of being purified from sin and impurities, both physically and spiritually.
Heart: In this context, it refers to a person's inner being, including thoughts, feelings, and will.
Spirit: An immaterial part of a human being, often associated with the person's soul and connection to God.
Renewal: To restore or transform something into a better condition, especially in relation to spiritual growth and renewed commitment to faith.
Offering: An act or gift given to God as an expression of devotion, gratitude, or atonement for sin.
Humility: An attitude of lowliness and respect, especially toward God and other people, and the recognition of one's own limitations and dependence on God.

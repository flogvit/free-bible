Elihu: A young man who speaks in the book of Job and defends God's righteousness and wisdom.
Righteousness: Ethical principles and actions that align with God's will and moral standards.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a reasonable and insightful way, especially in relation to God and His ways.
Listen: To be attentive and receive information or counsel from others, particularly from God or His spokespeople.
Discipline: Correction or punishment intended to amend and improve behavior, often viewed as part of God's educational process.
Humility: A virtue that involves recognizing one's own limitations and submitting to God's will and authority.
Retribution: The act of returning evil for evil, often in the context of God's just judgment upon human sins.

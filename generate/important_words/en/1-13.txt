Abram: An important biblical figure, later called Abraham, with whom God made a covenant and promised numerous descendants.
Lot: Abram's nephew, who traveled with him and settled in various parts of Canaan.
Canaan: A land region in ancient times, designated by God as the promised land for the people of Israel.
Sodom: A city in Canaan that was destroyed by God because of its wickedness and immorality.
Gomorrah: Another city in Canaan that was also destroyed by God along with Sodom.
Strife: Disagreement or conflict, in this case between the herdsmen of Abram and Lot.
Altar: A structure or place where offerings and worship were performed, especially in connection with religious ceremonies.

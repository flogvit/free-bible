Eliphaz: One of Job's three friends who came to comfort and speak with Job during his trials.
Job: The main character in the book, a righteous man who experiences great suffering and seeks to understand God's will.
Righteousness: A moral quality that involves acting in accordance with divine or moral laws and principles.
Suffering: Physical or emotional pain, distress, or discomfort that Job endures throughout the book.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good decisions and judge rightly.
Fear of God: Respect and reverence for God, recognized as an important part of living a righteous life.
Comfort: Support and care that Eliphaz and the other friends try to give Job during his trials.
Trials: The difficult situations and challenges Job must face as a result of his suffering.

Samuel: A young prophet and judge in Israel who was called by God to serve Him. He is the main character in the book of 1 Samuel.
Eli: The high priest at the temple in Shiloh and a mentor to Samuel. He raised Samuel after his mother, Hannah, dedicated him to God's service.
Hannah: Samuel's mother who prayed earnestly to God for a child. After Samuel was born, she kept her vow and gave him to God's service under Eli.
Shiloh: A city in the land of Israel where the tabernacle and the ark stood. It was an important religious center at that time.
Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Call: In this context, it refers to God's communication with Samuel, choosing him to become a prophet and serve Israel.
Prophet: A person who receives messages from God and delivers them to the people. Prophets can also predict future events.
Vision: A spiritual experience in which a person sees or hears something coming from God. In 1 Samuel chapter 3, Samuel receives his first vision from God.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment for human sin and disobedience. In this chapter, Eli's house is judged for their sins.

Locust: A type of grasshopper that attacks and destroys crops.
Swarm: A large group of insects, especially locusts, that gather and move together.
Crop: Plants cultivated and harvested for food, feed, or other purposes.
Offering: An act in which something is given to a deity, often in the form of food, drink, or animals, as an expression of gratitude, reverence, or atonement.
Weeping: An expression of sorrow, pain, or despair through tears and lamentation.
Granary: A place where grain and other agricultural products are stored after harvest.
Worship service: A religious ceremony or ritual that honors and worships God or deities.
High priest: The supreme leader of the priesthood in a religious tradition, responsible for performing important rituals and maintaining order and purity.
Fasting period: A time set aside to abstain from food and/or drink for spiritual discipline, self-reflection, and prayer.
Fertility: The ability to produce offspring, plants, fruit, or new ideas; often associated with abundance and growth.

Uzziah: King of Judah who reigned for 52 years and was known for his military success and rebuilding of the land.
Temple: Holy building where the Israelites worshipped God and offered sacrifices, a central place for religious practice.
High Priest: The supreme leader of the priesthood, responsible for performing important ceremonies and maintaining the sanctuary.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks with God's will and commands, leading to separation from God and punishment.
Leprosy: A contagious skin disease that caused those affected to be considered unclean and required them to be isolated from society.
God's wrath: God's response to human sin and disobedience, which can lead to punishment and negative consequences for those involved.
Repentance: The act of regretting and changing behavior to correct past mistakes, seeking to restore the relationship with God.

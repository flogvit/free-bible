Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who received God's word and delivered it to the people of Israel.
The Lord's Word: An expression referring to the divine messages and instructions given by God to Jeremiah.
Pashur: A priest and leader in the temple who opposed Jeremiah and persecuted him because of his prophecies.
Plague: A term describing God's punishment and judgment upon people or nations that disobey His commands.
Contempt: The feeling of being looked down upon, mocked, or rejected, which Jeremiah experienced from his fellow countrymen due to his prophecies.
Hope: The expectation of a better future, especially in light of God's promises and plans for His people.
Humiliation: The feeling of shame and degradation resulting from being humiliated, dishonored, or insulted.
Comfort: To provide support, help, and encouragement in difficult times, often connected to God's care and love for His people.

Solomon: King Solomon of Israel, who is believed to be the author of Song of Songs and son of King David.
Bride: The woman loved by the man in Song of Songs, often representing God's people or the church.
Groom: The man who loves the bride in Song of Songs, often symbolizing God or Jesus Christ.
Love: A deep devotion and feeling between two people, here between the bride and groom.
Seeking: The act of looking for something or someone, in this case the bride's search for her beloved.
Night: The time of day when it is dark outside, used here as a metaphor for uncertainty and trials.
Day: The time of day when it is light outside, used here as a metaphor for joy and reunion.
Watchmen: People who protect and guard the city; in Song of Songs they help the bride find her beloved.
Palm: A tree symbolizing victory, triumph, and eternal life, used in Song of Songs as an image of love and unity.

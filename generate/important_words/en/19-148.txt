Praise: An expression of giving God glory and thanks for his greatness and goodness.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, also known as Jehovah or Yahweh.
Heavenly realms: The heavenly sphere where God and angels dwell, symbolizing God's kingdom and presence.
Water: The element representing life and cleansing in a biblical context.
Heaven: The physical and spiritual realm above the earth, associated with God's dwelling place and the divine.
Earth: The planet we live on, created by God, and the place of human existence.
Mountains: Large landforms representing strength and stability, often used as symbols of God's power.
Trees: Symbolize growth and life, as well as humanity's connection to nature and God's creation.
Animals: Living creatures on earth, created by God, and part of his creation.
Humans: Created by God in his image, humans are the highest of all creatures and have responsibility to care for the earth and its contents.
Kings: Rulers and leaders of nations, given authority from God and called to govern with justice and wisdom.

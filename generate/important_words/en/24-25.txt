Jeremiah: The prophet who conveys God's message and warnings to the people of Israel in the book of Jeremiah.
Day of Judgment: A day of divine punishment and justice, where God will judge the nations for their sin and disobedience.
Babylon: A powerful ancient empire that God uses as an instrument to punish Israel and other nations for their sins.
70 years: The period during which Israel is to remain under Babylonian rule before returning to their own land.
Cup of Wrath: A symbol of God's anger and judgment against the nations that have acted wickedly and been disobedient toward him.
King Nebuchadnezzar: The king of Babylon whom God appoints to carry out his judgment on Israel and other nations.
Restoration: The process by which God will restore his people after the Babylonian exile and bring in a time of peace and prosperity.

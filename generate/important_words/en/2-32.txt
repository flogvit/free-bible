Golden Calf: An idol made of melted gold that the Israelites worshipped in Moses' absence.
Moses: The prophet and leader of God's people who received the Ten Commandments from God and led them out of Egypt.
Mount Ararat: The place where Moses received the Ten Commandments from God and confronted the Israelites about their idolatry.
Prayer: Communication between humans and God, in which one asks for help, gives thanks, or honors God.
Punishment: Consequences or reactions from God due to human sin and disobedience.
Repentance: A feeling of sorrow and regret for past actions, often linked to sin and disobedience toward God.
Levites: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, known for their role as priests and servants in the Tabernacle and later the temple.
Covenant: An agreement or promise between God and humans that establishes a relationship and sets conditions for how they should live.

Jehu: An Israelite king who ruled in the northern kingdom of Israel and wiped out Ahab's family.
Ahab: A former Israelite king who was married to Jezebel and worshipped Baal, a false god.
Jezebel: Ahab's wife, an evil queen who promoted idolatry and persecuted God's prophets.
Baal: A false god worshipped by the Canaanites and other nations, often associated with fertility and rain.
Samaria: The capital city of the northern kingdom of Israel at that time.
Temple: A holy place where people worshipped gods, including both the true God and false gods.
Prophet: A person chosen by God to deliver His message and guide His people.
Alliance: An agreement or covenant between two parties, often between God and His people.
Wipe out: To completely destroy or remove something, especially an entire family or group of people.

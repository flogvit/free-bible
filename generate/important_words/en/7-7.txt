Gideon: A judge and military leader in Israel whom God chose to save the Israelites from the Midianites.
Midianites: A people group who oppressed and plundered the Israelites, and whom Gideon was called to fight against.
Barak: Another judge and military leader in Israel who fought against the Canaanites.
The Square: The place where Gideon gathered his soldiers before the battle against the Midianites.
The Fleece Test: A test Gideon performed to assure himself that God was with him in the fight against the Midianites.
300 men: The small number of soldiers God chose for Gideon to use in the battle against the Midianites to show His power and strength.
The Dream: A dream one of the Midianites had, which Gideon heard, and which he interpreted as a sign that God would give him victory.
Jars: Objects carried by the 300 men along with torches and trumpets, which they broke and blew to create fear and confusion among the enemy.
Victory: The result of the battle where Gideon and his 300 men overcame the Midianites with God's help.

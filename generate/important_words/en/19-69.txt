Prayer: Communication with God, where one expresses gratitude, asks for help, or seeks guidance.

Shame: A feeling of humiliation or regret due to bad actions or mistakes.
Help: Support or assistance given to someone in need, often in the form of divine intervention.
Grace: God's mercy and love shown toward people, regardless of their merit.
Praise song: A song or poem that expresses praise and honor to God.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with moral principles and ethical standards.
Humble: To be modest and submissive in attitude, especially toward God.

Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
The Spirit of the Lord: The divine power and presence of God that guides and strengthens people.
Son of Man: A title used for Ezekiel, emphasizing his humanity and vulnerability.
Prophecy: A divinely inspired statement or message conveying God's will and plans for the future.
Rebels: Those who oppose God's will and authority, often referring to the people of Israel in the book of Ezekiel.
The Nations: Various peoples and cultures mentioned in the book of Ezekiel, often in connection with judgment and punishment.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human actions, especially their disobedience toward him.
Vision: A supernatural experience where God reveals himself and his will through images and visions.

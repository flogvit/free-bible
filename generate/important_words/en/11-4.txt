Solomon: King of Israel and son of King David, known for his wisdom and wealth.
Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and good judgment.
Stewards: People who are responsible for managing and caring for resources or property.
Province: A geographic region within a country that is subject to central authority.
Household: A group of people living together and sharing resources, often a family.
Supplies: Goods and necessities needed to sustain households and communities.
Workers: People who perform labor or services, often in exchange for payment.
Grain: A type of cereal crop grown to produce food, such as wheat, barley, or corn.
Tributes: Gifts or payments given to a ruler or state as a sign of submission or respect.
Peace: A state of calm and harmony between groups or nations, without conflict or war.

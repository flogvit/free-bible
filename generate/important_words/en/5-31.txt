Moses: The prophet and leader of the people of Israel who received the Ten Commandments and led them out of Egypt and through the wilderness.
Inheritance: The area or property given to the tribes of Israel by God after they entered the promised land.
Book of the Law: A collection of God's laws and instructions given to the Israelites through Moses, including the Ten Commandments and other regulations.
Joshua: Moses' successor as leader of the people of Israel, who led them into the promised land after Moses' death.
Encouragement: To give strength, hope, or courage to someone, especially in difficult situations or challenges.

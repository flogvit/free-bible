Joshua: The main character in the book of Joshua, a leader of the people of Israel after Moses' death who leads them into the promised land of Canaan.
Ai: A Canaanite city that is conquered by the Israelites under Joshua's leadership in chapter 8.
The Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator, who gives instructions and support to Joshua and the people of Israel.
Ambush: A strategy used by Joshua and his army to lure the inhabitants out of Ai and then attack and destroy the city.
Hammer and Anvil: A military tactic where part of the army attacks from the front while another part attacks from the side or rear, resulting in the enemy being surrounded and defeated.
Altar: A place for religious sacrifices and worship, which Joshua builds on Mount Ebal after the victory over Ai to thank God and renew the covenant between God and Israel.
The Law: Refers to the religious laws and regulations given by God to Moses on Mount Sinai, which Joshua reads aloud to the people after the victory over Ai.

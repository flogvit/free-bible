Samuel: A prophet and judge in Israel who helped the people choose their first king, Saul.
Saul: The first king of Israel, chosen by God through Samuel, but later rejected because of disobedience.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Return: An action where someone goes back to a previous state or situation, often used about turning back to God.
Obey: To follow instructions or commands given by an authority, such as God or a leader.
Witness: Someone or something that gives evidence or confirmation of an event, action, or truth.
Fear: A feeling of awe, respect, and submission toward God and His will.
Sign: Events or miracles that show God's power and presence.
Curse: A negative consequence or punishment resulting from disobedience to God.
Blessing: A positive outcome or reward resulting from obedience to God.

Here are some important words from Isaiah chapter 56 and their explanations:
Righteousness: A moral principle that involves fair and ethical conduct, especially in relation to God's laws.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, death, and eternal punishment, and giving them eternal life through faith in Jesus Christ.
Sabbath: The seventh day of the week, Saturday, which is a day of rest for Jews and a time for worship and rest in accordance with God's command.
Sanctuary: A holy place or temple where God dwells and is worshipped. In the Old Testament, it often refers to the temple in Jerusalem.
Foreigners: People who are not of Israelite origin but have joined God's people. They have rights and duties according to God's law and can participate in worship.
Eunuch: A castrated man, often employed in royal or noble households to perform administrative tasks or care for women.
Covenant: A binding agreement between two parties, especially between God and his people. The covenant contains promises and conditions for both sides.
Memorial: A sign or symbol that represents a person, event, or idea, helping people remember and honor what is represented.

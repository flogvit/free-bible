Wisdom: The ability to think and act wisely, based on experience, understanding, and knowledge.
Understanding: The ability to exercise good judgment in various situations and grasp the consequences of actions.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and in accordance with moral and ethical norms, as well as treating others with respect and honesty.
Discipline: Self-control and training necessary to live a right and good life.
Lord: A title often used for God, referring to His authority and power over creation.
Faithfulness: Being loyal and steadfast toward one's principles, promises, and relationships.
Humility: The quality of having a modest and respectful attitude, not considering oneself better than others.
Diligence: Working hard and persistently to achieve one's goals and tasks.
Sloth: Lack of effort and engagement in work or duties, which can lead to negative consequences.
Truth: That which is true, correct, and in agreement with reality; honesty and sincerity in speech and action.
Lying: Saying something that is not true, with the intent to deceive or conceal the truth.

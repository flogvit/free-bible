Wisdom: The ability to think and act wisely, based on experience, knowledge, and understanding.
Understanding: The ability to see connections and meaning in situations, ideas, and concepts.
Knowledge: Insight and information acquired through experience, study, or learning.
Discipline: Correction or guidance given to correct and shape behavior and character.
Lord: A title referring to God, used to show respect and reverence.
Fear: Awe and reverence for God, involving obedience and devotion.
Faithfulness: Being loyal, reliable, and consistent in one's commitments and relationships.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with morality, ethics, and God's will.
Peace: A state of calm, harmony, and absence of conflict, both external and internal.
BLESSING: God's favor and protection, often manifested as material or spiritual well-being.

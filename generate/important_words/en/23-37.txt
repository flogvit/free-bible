Hezekiah: King of Judah who sought God's help against the Assyrians during King Sennacherib's siege of Jerusalem.
Sennacherib: Assyrian king who invaded and threatened to destroy Jerusalem, but was ultimately defeated by God's intervention.
Jerusalem: The capital city of the kingdom of Judah and an important religious center for the Jewish people, where Solomon's temple stood.
The prophet Isaiah: An important prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message and guided King Hezekiah during the siege.
The angel of the Lord: A divine messenger sent by God to protect Jerusalem and destroy the Assyrian army.
Salvation: The term used to describe God's act of saving His people from enemies or dangers, as in this case the Assyrians.
Prayer: Communication between humans and God where one seeks help, gives thanks, or asks for forgiveness.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and the consequences that follow human actions, whether reward or punishment.

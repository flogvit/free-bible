Solomon: A king of Israel, known for his wisdom and wealth, son of David and Bathsheba.
The Queen of Sheba: A legendary queen from Southern Arabia who visited Solomon to test his wisdom.
Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Wealth: An abundance of valuable resources or material possessions, often associated with money and property.
Offering: A gift, often of animals or food, given to a deity as a form of worship or gratitude.
Temple: A sacred place dedicated to a deity, where worship and religious ceremonies take place.
Hiram: King of Tyre, a friend and ally of King Solomon, who helped him with the building of the temple in Jerusalem.
Gold: A precious metal, highly valued for its beauty and rarity, often used for jewelry and decoration.

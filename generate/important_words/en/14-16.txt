Asa: King of Judah who reigned for 41 years and sought God's guidance in the beginning of his reign, but later relied on human help.
Baasha: King of Israel whom Asa fought against and built fortifications at the border between their kingdoms.
Ben-Hadad: King of Aram-Damascus whom Asa asked for help to defeat Baasha and his army.
The Prophet: A person who speaks on behalf of God and delivers God's message to the people. In this chapter, it refers to Hanani.
Hanani: The prophet who confronted Asa with his mistakes and told him that he had acted wrongly by relying on Ben-Hadad instead of God.
Syria: A country in the Middle East that was home to King Ben-Hadad and his people.
Wrath: God's strong displeasure and punishment resulting from human sin and disobedience.
War: Conflict between two or more parties, often involving violence and death. In this case, the war between Asa and Baasha.
Sickness: An illness or weakening of health, here referred to as a disease in Asa's feet that led to his death.

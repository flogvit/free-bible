God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
The people of Israel: A nation and ethnic group originating from the biblical patriarchs Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
Comforter: To give strength, hope, and encouragement to someone who is worried or sad.
Enemies: Individuals or groups that are opponents or rivals, often in a conflict or struggle.
The Lord's servant: A person chosen and called by God to carry out His will and purpose on earth.
Desert nations: Groups of people living in desert regions, often referred to in the Bible as enemies of Israel.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with divine or moral law, especially in relation to other people.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, evil, and death through faith in Jesus Christ.
The offspring of Jacob: An expression referring to the descendants of Jacob, also known as Israel, and their place in God's plan.

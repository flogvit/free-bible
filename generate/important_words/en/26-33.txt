Watchman: A person responsible for warning and protecting the people against dangers and threats.
Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human actions and behavior.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks with God's will and laws, leading to separation from Him.
Repentance: The process by which a person acknowledges their sins, repents, and seeks forgiveness from God.
Righteousness: A state of being morally right and in accordance with God's will and standards.
Life: A gift from God, both physical and spiritual, involving existence and relationship with the Creator.
Death: The end of physical life and a possible consequence of sin, which can lead to eternal separation from God.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people, also referring to the nation and the land they inhabited.

Lord: A title for God meaning the sovereign and highest authority.
Grace: God's unmerited goodness and love toward people, especially in the form of forgiveness and salvation.
Deliverance: The act of setting someone free from oppression, suffering, or captivity.
Refuge: A place of safety and protection, especially in a time of distress or danger.
Trust: To have confidence in or rely on something or someone, often used regarding faith in God.
Eternal: That which lasts forever, without end. In this context, it refers to God's love and grace.
Stone: Symbolizes something solid, strong, and stable, often used as an image of God or his promises.
Cornerstone: An important and foundational part of something, such as a building or a community. In Psalm 118, it refers to Jesus as the foundation of faith and salvation.
Gates: Entrances or openings that provide access to a place, often used as symbols of opportunities and paths to salvation.
Righteousness: The quality of being just and morally upright, especially in relation to God's laws and commands.
Praise: Expressions of gratitude, honor, and worship toward God through words, songs, and actions.

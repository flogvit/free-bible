Solomon: King Solomon was the son of King David and Bathsheba, known for his wisdom and as the builder of the first temple in Jerusalem.
Temple: A sacred building dedicated to God, where the Israelites worshipped and offered sacrifices. Solomon's Temple was the first temple in Jerusalem.
Bronze Sea: A large bronze basin in the temple, used by the priests for ritual cleansing before they performed their duties.
Pillars: Two large bronze pillars, called Jachin and Boaz, which stood at the entrance to the temple as symbols of stability and strength.
Altar: A structure where burnt offerings and other sacrifices were presented to God as part of worship.
Lampstands: Candleholders used in the temple to provide light and symbolize God's presence.
Table: Tables in the temple where the showbread was placed as a symbol of God's eternal presence and care for his people.
Showbread: Bread that was placed on the tables in the temple as an offering and a symbol of God's presence and care for his people.
Altar of Incense: An altar in the temple where incense was burned as part of worship and to symbolize the prayers of the believers rising up to God.

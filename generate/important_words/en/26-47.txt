Temple: A holy place where God dwells and is worshiped, especially the Jewish temple in Jerusalem.
River: A large natural flow of water that empties into the sea, a lake, or another river.
Life-giving: Something that gives or sustains life, often used concerning water, nourishment, or light.
Trees: Large plants with a trunk, branches, and leaves, providing shade, shelter, and food for people and animals.
Leaves: Flat, green parts of a tree or plant that capture sunlight and produce oxygen through photosynthesis.
Healing: The process of becoming well again after illness, injury, or wounds, both physically and spiritually.
Borders: Lines that separate two areas, lands, or properties, often used to describe territories and nations.
Tribes: Subgroups of the people of Israel, the twelve sons of Jacob, each representing a part of the Jewish nation.

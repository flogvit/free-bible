Moses: The prophet and leader of the Israelites, who received the Ten Commandments from God and led the people out of Egypt.
Blessing: A positive force or influence attributed to God, often given to people for their faithfulness and obedience.
Tribes: The twelve groups of Israelites descended from Jacob's twelve sons, each with its own identity and territory.
The Lord: Another designation for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Sinai: The mountain where Moses received the Ten Commandments from God and established a covenant between God and the Israelites.

King: A leader or ruler over a country or people, appointed by God in this context.  
The Law: God's instructions and commandments given to Moses and the people of Israel to guide their lives and society.  
Horses: Large, four-legged animals used for transportation, work, and warfare in ancient times.  
Egypt: The land where the people of Israel were slaves before being led out by Moses under God's guidance.  
The Writings: Sacred texts containing God's word and instructions, including the books of Moses.  
Humility: The quality of being modest and showing respect and obedience to God and His will.

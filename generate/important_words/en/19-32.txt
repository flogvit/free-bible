Sin: An act or condition that breaks God's will and moral law, leading to spiritual separation from God.
Forgiveness: God's gracious action of removing the guilt and punishment of sin, restoring the relationship between people and God.
Guilt: A feeling of responsibility and remorse for having committed a sin or done something wrong.
Accountability: The principle that a person is responsible for their actions and can be held accountable for them.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love toward humanity, expressed through forgiveness and salvation.
Confession: To acknowledge and confess one's sins to God, often in prayer, seeking forgiveness and restoration.
Blessedness: A deep sense of joy, contentment, and well-being that comes from living in harmony with God's will and purpose.
Protection: God's care and defense of His faithful ones against evil and danger, both spiritual and physical.
Justification: God's act of declaring a sinner righteous on the basis of faith in Jesus Christ, so that they are acquitted from sin's punishment and guilt.

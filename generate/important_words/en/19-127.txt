Lord: A title used to refer to God as the supreme authority and protector of humanity.
Builds: To put together or construct something; in this context, it refers to God's role in building and sustaining communities and families.
Watchman: A person or power that protects and cares for something. In Psalm 127, this refers to God's role as the guardian of cities and people.
In vain: When something lacks meaning or purpose. In this psalm, it means that all human efforts are meaningless without God's involvement and blessing.
Labor: The effort and activities that people undertake to achieve a goal or earn their livelihood.
Sleep: A natural state of rest in which the body and mind recover. In this psalm, sleep is a metaphor for trusting in God and recognizing that He provides what we need.
Children: Offspring or descendants of a person. In Psalm 127, children symbolize God's blessings and rewards for those who trust and obey Him.
Arrows: Slender, pointed projectiles shot from a bow. In this psalm, arrows represent children as protection and strength for parents.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or threaten someone. In this context, enemies refer to the powers that work against God's will and plans.

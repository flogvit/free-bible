Dagon: A Philistine deity who was half fish and half human, associated with fertility and harvest.
The Ark: A chest that contained the Ten Commandments, often referred to as "the ark of God's covenant"; a central symbol of God's presence in Israel.
Philistines: An ancient people who lived in Canaan and were frequently in conflict with the Israelites.
Ekron: One of the major Philistine cities, mentioned several times in 1 Samuel.
Tumors: Malignant growths or swellings that occur in the body; in this context, they are a punishment from God against the Philistines for taking the Ark.
Ashdod: Another important Philistine city where the temple of Dagon stood, and to which the Ark was brought after it was taken from the Israelites.

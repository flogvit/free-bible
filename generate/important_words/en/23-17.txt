Damascus: The capital of Syria, mentioned as a place of destruction and judgment in the book of Isaiah 17.
Aroer: A city in ancient Israel, mentioned as a place where people will flee from under the destruction of Damascus.
Ephraim: Another name for the Northern Kingdom of Israel, used here to show that they too will suffer under God's judgment.
The glory of Jacob: A reference to Israel's greatness and prosperity, which will be reduced because of their sins and disobedience toward God.
Gleanings: This term refers to what remains after the harvest. In this context, it means that only a small remnant of the people will survive God's judgment.
Assyria: The Assyrian Empire, a powerful enemy of Israel and Judah, whom God will use as an instrument of His judgment.
Lebanon: A mountainous land north of Israel, known for its cedar trees. Here it symbolizes Israel's destruction, since even the mighty cedars will be cut down.

Israel: The name of God's chosen people, also referring to both Jacob's descendants and the ancient kingdom in the Middle East.
Levi: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, especially known for its role as priests and temple servants in Israelite culture.
Priestly service: A religious role where members from the tribe of Levi performed ritual actions and led prayer and worship in the temple.
Temple: A holy place dedicated to God, where sacrifices and other religious ceremonies were carried out. The first temple was built by King Solomon in Jerusalem.
Singers: Members of the tribe of Levi who were responsible for singing psalms and songs of praise during services in the temple.
Gatekeepers: People from the tribe of Levi who were in charge of guarding the entrances to the temple and ensuring order and security.
Genealogy: An overview of a person's or family's ancestors and descendants, often used to establish identity and kinship within a culture.

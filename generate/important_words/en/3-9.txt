Offer: A ritual act in which animals, food, or other gifts are sacrificed to God as a form of worship and reverence.  
Yppersteprest: The highest religious leader in the Israelite priesthood, responsible for performing sacrifices and other ceremonies in the sanctuary.  
Syndoffer: An offering given to God to atone for sins and cleanse the one who offered it from guilt and punishment.  
Brennoffer: An offering in which the entire animal is burned on the altar, symbolizing total devotion to God and a seeking of forgiveness and grace.  
Herrens Åpenbaring: When God reveals His presence and approves an offering or action by sending fire from heaven to consume the sacrifice.  
Folket: The Israelites, God's chosen people with whom He made a covenant through Moses, and who were to follow His laws and commands.  
Velsignelse: A positive power or influence that comes from God, bringing happiness, prosperity, and protection to those who receive it.

Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to evaluate situations or matters in a wise and insightful manner.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and honestly, in accordance with moral and ethical standards.
Discipline: Training or instruction intended to correct or shape behavior and character.
Happiness: A feeling of contentment, joy, or well-being that comes from living a meaningful and good life.
Work: The effort put forth to perform a task, produce something, or achieve a goal.
Wealth: An abundance of valuable resources, assets, or money.
Poverty: The state of having insufficient financial resources, leading to lack and hardship.
Loyalty: The quality of being loyal, reliable, and faithful toward someone or something.
Speech: Human communication through the use of words, whether spoken or written.

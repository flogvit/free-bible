Cities: Areas established as settlements, here referring to cities of refuge for those who have unintentionally killed someone.
Refuge: A safe place or shelter where a person can seek protection and avoid punishment or revenge.
Murderer: A person who has taken the life of another human being, either intentionally or by accident.
Avenger: A person who seeks to inflict harm or punish someone for a wrong they have committed, especially in connection with murder.
Boundary: A geographical line that separates two areas, here referring to the territorial borders of Israel's tribes.
Witness: A person who gives information about an event they have seen, heard, or experienced, especially in a legal context.
False testimony: Giving incorrect information or lying in a court case, which can lead to innocent people being convicted.
Two or three witnesses: The requirement that at least two or three witnesses must be present to confirm an accusation or event.

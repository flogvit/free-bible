Temple: A holy building dedicated to God, where people came to worship and offer gifts.
Solomon: King of Israel and son of King David, known for his wisdom and wealth. He built the first temple in Jerusalem.
Ark: The ark of the covenant, a sacred chest that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, Aaron's staff, and a jar of manna.
The Lord's house: Another name for the temple in Jerusalem, the place where God was present among his people.
Cloud: A symbol of God's presence and protection, which covered the temple when the ark of the covenant was brought into the Most Holy Place.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, chosen by God to serve as priests and musicians in the temple.
Singers: Praise songs and hymns sung by the Levites during worship services and ceremonies in the temple.
Offerings: Gifts and sacrificial animals presented to God as a sign of gratitude, devotion, and obedience.
Most Holy Place: The innermost room of the temple, where the ark of the covenant was kept and where God's presence was strongest.

Ariel: A symbolic designation for Jerusalem, often used as a metaphor for the city and its inhabitants.
The Lord's house: An expression referring to the temple in Jerusalem, the place of worship in ancient Israel.
Spiritual blindness: A metaphor for the inability to understand or recognize God's will and truth.
Vision: Prophetic revelations or insight given by God to the prophet Isaiah concerning future events and God's plans.
Lips: Used here as a metaphor for outward religious piety without inner conviction or sincerity.
Heart: Symbolizes human inner life, thoughts, feelings, and will, especially in relation to their relationship with God.
The Lord: A title often used in the Old Testament to refer to God, the almighty Creator.
Enemies: The nations or peoples threatening Israel and Jerusalem, often seen as punishment from God for the people's disobedience.
Miracles: Supernatural acts performed by God to demonstrate His power and authority.
Deliverance: God's act of saving and protecting His people from hostile nations and threats.

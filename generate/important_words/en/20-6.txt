Proverbs: Abbreviation for the Book of Proverbs, a collection of proverbs and wisdom literature in the Bible.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a reasonable and prudent way to make good decisions.
Understanding: The ability to comprehend and evaluate situations and problems in an intelligent and insightful manner.
Discipline: Training or correction aimed at teaching and improving behavior.
Slothfulness: A lack of willingness or energy to work, often viewed as a negative trait.
Mire: A wet, muddy area with poor drainage, frequently used as a metaphor for difficult or dangerous situations.
Predator: An animal that hunts and eats other animals, used as a metaphor for people who exploit and harm others.
Lie: A false statement or story intended to deceive or mislead.
Falsehood: The quality of being dishonest, untrustworthy, or inauthentic.
Enemy: A person or group opposed or hostile toward another; an adversary.

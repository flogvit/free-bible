Amalekites: A nomadic tribe that attacked the Israelites during their journey through the wilderness.
Rephidim: A place in the wilderness where the Israelites camped and experienced a lack of water.
Massah: The name given to the place where the Israelites complained about the lack of water, meaning "test" or "trial."
Meribah: The name given to the place where Moses struck the rock and water flowed out, meaning "quarrel" or "contention."
Moses: The prophet and leader of the Israelites who led them out of slavery in Egypt and through the wilderness.
Joshua: A young Israelite warrior and successor to Moses who led the army of Israel in battle against the Amalekites.
Hur: An Israelite who, together with Aaron, held up Moses' arms during the battle against the Amalekites.
Aaron: Moses' older brother and high priest for the Israelites.

Repentance: A term denoting sorrow and acknowledgment of sin, leading to conversion and reconciliation with God.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and mercy toward people, despite their sins and shortcomings.
Forgiveness: God's act of removing guilt and punishment for sin, so that people can restore their relationship with Him.
Repentance: A sincere act of sorrow and change in behavior, resulting from the recognition of one's sin and the need for God's grace.
Healing: God's power to restore both physical and spiritual health, as well as relationships between people and Himself.
Blessing: God's favor and protection, bringing prosperity, joy, and spiritual growth to those who follow His way.
Fruitfulness: A symbol of life, growth, and productivity in both a spiritual and physical sense, resulting from God's blessing.

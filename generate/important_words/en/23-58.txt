Fast: A religious practice where one abstains from food and drink for a specific time, often as a form of spiritual discipline and prayer.
Ydmykhet: An attitude or trait that involves being humble and submissive, and recognizing one's own limitations and dependence on God.
Rettferdighet: Acting in accordance with God's will and morality, and working for justice and equality among people.
Barmhjertighet: Showing compassion, grace, and care for others, especially those who are in need or suffering.
Lys: A symbol of God's presence, guidance, and truth, bringing hope and life to people.
Helbredelse: God's power to restore physical, spiritual, and emotional health and well-being.
Sabbat: The seventh day of the week, which according to Jewish tradition is to be kept holy and observed as a day of rest and worship of God.

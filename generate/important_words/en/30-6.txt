Self-satisfaction: An attitude of being content with oneself or one's situation, often without awareness of potential dangers or shortcomings.
Wealth: Economic success and material abundance; in the book of Amos chapter 6 it is criticized for leading to pride and indifference toward God's commands.
Destruction: Total ruin or collapse, especially of a nation or institution. In the book of Amos chapter 6 it is the result of Israel's disobedience to God.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem that symbolizes God's presence and protection. In the book of Amos chapter 6 Zion is warned of impending judgment and destruction.
Samaria: The capital city of the northern kingdom of Israel, also associated with sin and idolatry. Amos warns Samaria of God's punishment and destruction.
The prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God. Amos is a prophet who warns Israel of God's coming judgment and punishment.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment for human sins and transgressions. In the book of Amos chapter 6 this is a consequence of Israel's disobedience and moral decay.

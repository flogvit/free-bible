Philistines: A people group who lived in the area around present-day Israel and Palestine, often in conflict with the Israelites.
David: An important figure in the Bible, later king of Israel. In 1 Samuel 27 he lives among the Philistines to avoid King Saul's persecution.
Achish: The king of Gath, a Philistine city. David seeks refuge with Achish and becomes his vassal.
Saul: Israel's first king, who hunts down David out of jealousy and fear that David will take the throne from him.
Ziklag: A town that Achish gives to David as a place to live. David uses Ziklag as a base for his raids against various peoples.
Raids: Attacks or plundering expeditions carried out by David and his men against various peoples to obtain resources and strengthen their position.

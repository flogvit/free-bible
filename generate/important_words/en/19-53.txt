Sinners: A person who breaks God's laws and commands, and acts in opposition to his will.
Evil: Actions, thoughts, or attitudes that are morally wrong, harmful, or destructive, contrary to God's nature and will.
Righteousness: To act in accordance with God's will and morality, to be upright and good.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from the consequences of sin and bringing them into a close relationship with him.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, emphasizing his authority and dominion over all things and everyone.

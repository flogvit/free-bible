Restoration of Zion: An expression referring to the restoration of Jerusalem, especially after the exile in Babylon.
Lord: Another designation for God, the almighty Creator and Sustainer of the universe.
Turned: To turn or change direction, here used concerning God's action of bringing His people back from exile.
Fate: An unavoidable event or situation, often linked to divine will or predestination.
Joy: A feeling of great happiness, here connected with Israel's reunification and rebuilding of their homeland.
Tears: A physical manifestation of sorrow or sadness, here used as a contrast to joy and laughter.
Sow: To plant seeds in the ground so that they grow into plants, here used as an image for labor and effort.
Reap: To gather crops after they have grown and matured, here used as an image for reward and success.
Carry: To hold or support something, here referring to carrying grain sacks as a symbol of the result of hard work and patience.

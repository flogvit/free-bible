Abijah: Son of Rehoboam and king over Judah, who fought against Jeroboam and his forces from Israel.
Jeroboam: First king of the northern kingdom of Israel after the kingdom was divided into two; known for his disobedience to God and introduction of idol worship.
Judah: One of the twelve tribes of Israel and the name of the southern kingdom after the division of Israel's kingdom.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, who were responsible for serving in the temple and performing priestly duties.
God's covenant: An agreement between God and his people, where God promises protection and blessings in exchange for obedience and faithfulness.
Altar: A structure or table where offerings and holy acts are performed as part of religious worship.
Golden calves: Idols shaped like calves, made of gold; Jeroboam introduced them in Israel as part of his idol worship.
The war: The conflict between Abijah and Jeroboam, where God gave victory to Abijah and Judah.
Prophet: A person who speaks on behalf of God and delivers his message to the people.

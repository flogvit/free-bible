Solomon: King David's son and successor as king of Israel, known for his wisdom and wealth.
Sacrifice: An act in which people give gifts, often animals or food, to God as a form of worship or gratitude.
Gibeon: A city in Israel where the tabernacle and altar were located before the temple was built in Jerusalem.
Dream: An experience that occurs while sleeping, in which thoughts, images, and feelings can seem real and vivid.
Wisdom: The ability to think and act with sound judgment, knowledge, and insight, especially in practical and moral matters.
Wealth: An abundance of valuable resources or material possessions, such as money, property, and other assets.
Army: Military forces gathered to defend a country, attack enemies, or carry out other military operations.

Rahab: A woman from Jericho who helped Israelite spies and later became part of their people.
Spies: People sent by Joshua to explore Jericho and gather information about the enemy.
Jericho: An ancient city in Canaan that the Israelites conquered under Joshua's leadership.
Canaan: The promised land that God pledged to give to the children of Israel, Abraham's descendants.
Faithfulness: Loyalty and trust in God and his promises, shown by both Rahab and the Israelites.
Red cord: A red thread that Rahab hung out of her window to identify her house and protect her family during the destruction of Jericho.
Conquest: The process of taking control of an area or city, as the Israelites did with Jericho and other cities in Canaan.
God's promises: Commitments that God gives to people, such as the promise of the promised land to the children of Israel.

Benjamin: Youngest son of Jacob and Rachel, founder of the tribe of Benjamin among Israel's twelve tribes.
Sons: Offspring or descendants, used to describe family relationships and inheritance.
Brothers: Siblings of the same sex, also used for close relatives or members of the same tribe.
Tribe: A group of people related through common ancestors who share a cultural and social identity.
Exile: The state of being forced to leave one's homeland, often as a result of war, persecution, or punishment.
Return: To go back to a former place, especially to the homeland after exile.
Firstborn: The first person born into a family, often given special rights and responsibilities according to tradition and culture.
Rulers: Persons who have authority and power over others, often as leaders or kings.

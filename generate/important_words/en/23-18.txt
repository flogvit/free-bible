Prophecy: A prediction or revelation about future events given by God through the prophet Isaiah.
The Day of the Lord: A time of God's judgment and intervention in human history, often linked to the end times.
Zion: A designation for Jerusalem as a central place of worship and God's presence in the Old Testament.
Ethiopia: An ancient African kingdom mentioned in Isaiah 18, representative of nations located outside Israel.
Ambassadors: Envoys or representatives sent from one nation to another to convey messages or make agreements.
Trumpet: A musical instrument used as a signal and call to action in biblical times, often associated with important events and God's intervention.
Banner: A symbol or emblem representing a nation, group, or cause, used to gather people and identify with a common goal.

Joshua: The main character in the book of Joshua, who succeeded Moses as leader of the people of Israel and led them into the promised land.
Promises: God's promises to the Israelites to give them the land of Canaan and protect them from their enemies, as long as they remain obedient to him.
Canaan: The region that God promised to give to the people of Israel, which Joshua helped them conquer. This area corresponds to parts of present-day Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and Jordan.
Covenant: An agreement between God and his people, in which God promises to protect and bless them if they follow his commands and avoid idolatry.
Enemies: The nations that lived in Canaan before the Israelites arrived there, often in conflict with them due to territorial claims and cultural differences.
Idolatry: The practice of worshipping other gods besides the one true God, which was strictly forbidden according to the covenant between God and the people of Israel.
Obedient: To follow God's commands and instructions, which is necessary to maintain the covenant and receive God's protection and blessings.

Lord: A title used for God, referring to his authority and power over all things.
Prayer: A form of communication between people and God, where one asks for help, guidance, or gives thanks for what has been received.
Salvation: The term used to describe God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, often linked to eternal life after death.
Name: In this context, it refers to God's character and nature, and is seen as a source of strength and protection for believers.
Offering: A gift or action given to God as a way to show devotion, gratitude, or to ask for forgiveness.
Heart: In a religious context, the heart often symbolizes the seat of emotions, will, and morality.
Victory: A triumph or success in a conflict or challenge, often linked to God's help and strength.

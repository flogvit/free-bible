Ish-Bosheth: A son of King Saul who succeeded him as king over Israel after his death.
Baanah and Rekab: Two brothers and mercenaries who served under Ish-Bosheth and later murdered him.
Mephibosheth: A son of Jonathan, Saul's son, who was lame in both feet. He was spared by David.
David: The future king of Israel, who had a close relationship with Saul's son Jonathan and received the news of Ish-Bosheth's death.
Hebron: The city where David was anointed king over Judah and where he ruled before becoming king over all Israel.
Gibeah: The place where Baanah and Rekab killed Ish-Bosheth and brought his head to David as a sign of loyalty.
Amasa: A military leader and cousin of Joab, who was later appointed by David as commander-in-chief of the army.

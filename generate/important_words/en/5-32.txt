Lord: A title for God that emphasizes his sovereignty and authority over all creation.
Mountain: In this context, a symbol of stability, strength, and eternity, often linked to God's presence.
Praise: A poetic expression of devotion, gratitude, and reverence toward God, often sung or recited by believers.
People: Refers to the people of Israel, God's chosen nation, with whom he made a covenant and gave his laws and commands.
Nation: A political and geographical entity consisting of people who share a common culture, traditions, and history.
Enemies: Those who oppose God and his people, whether other nations, gods, or evil forces.
Punishment: God's righteous act of bringing suffering or negative consequences upon those who break his laws and commands.
Righteousness: A principle meaning that God acts in accordance with his own moral perfection and the requirements of his covenant with his people.
Mercy: God's loving and compassionate response to human weakness and sin, offering forgiveness and grace.
Eagle: A symbol of God's protection and care for his people, as well as his ability to lift them up and carry them through difficulties.

Bildad: One of Job's three friends who comes to comfort and advise him during his trials.
Night: A metaphorical reference to darkness, misery, or ignorance.
Stars of heaven: A reference to the celestial bodies that represent God's greatness and creation.
Man: Refers to humanity in general, and in this context specifically to Job.
Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting rightly and justly according to God's will and commandments.
Purity: A state of being free from sin, injustice, and moral defilement.
Earth: The earth or the world as a whole, often used to represent earthly life and its challenges.
Worm: A symbol of low status, weakness, or contempt, here used to emphasize human limitations compared with God.

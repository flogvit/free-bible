Abimelech: A son of Gideon who killed his brothers and proclaimed himself king over Israel, but was later killed.
Shechem: A city in Canaan where Abimelech was made king; also the name of a person in the story who helped Abimelech become king.
Gaal: An opposition leader who challenged Abimelech's rule and tried to overthrow him, but failed.
Mount Gerizim: A mountain near Shechem where Jotham, the only surviving brother of Abimelech, told a parable about trees choosing a king.
Parable: A short story that uses comparisons to convey a moral or lesson, such as Jotham's speech about the trees.
Jotham: The youngest son of Gideon who escaped being killed by his brothers and warned the people of Shechem against Abimelech through his parable.
Gideon: A judge in Israel and father of Abimelech, known for his military victories and leadership.
Jerubbaal: Another name for Gideon, meaning "let Baal contend," referring to a pagan god in Canaan.
Thebez: A city where Abimelech attacked and besieged the inhabitants, but was mortally wounded by a woman who dropped a millstone on him.

Aaron: Brother of Moses and the first high priest in Israel, who belonged to the tribe of Levi.
High Priest: The supreme religious leader in Israel, responsible for performing sacrifices and ritual acts.
Levi: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, appointed to serve God and the Israelite community as priests and temple servants.
Service Groups: The division of the Levites into 24 groups who were to perform service at the temple by rotation.
Urim and Thummim: Mysterious objects used by the high priest to seek counsel from God in important matters.
Lot: A method for making decisions using chance, often used in the Bible to determine God's will.
Eleazar: One of Aaron's sons, who succeeded him as high priest.
Ithamar: Another of Aaron's sons, also a priest in Israel.
Genealogy: A list of family members and their ancestors, important for preserving family and tribal identity.

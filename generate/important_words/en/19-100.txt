Praise: A tribute or expression of gratitude and reverence to God, often in the form of song or poetry.
Joy: A feeling of happiness, contentment, and well-being, especially in connection with religious devotion.
Serve: To dedicate oneself to God's will and work, carrying out actions in His name.
The Lord: A title used to refer to God, acknowledging His sovereignty and power over all things.
Enter: To approach or participate in God's presence, often through prayer, praise, or meditation.
Know: To recognize or understand the truth about God's existence, nature, and love.
Creator: God as the almighty power who created the universe and all life.
People: Human beings generally, especially those who believe in and worship God.
Gates of entry: A symbolic representation of the entrance to God's kingdom or presence.
Worship: The act of expressing admiration, respect, and gratitude to God, often through song or prayer.
Thanksgiving: An expression of gratitude and acknowledgment of God's goodness and grace.

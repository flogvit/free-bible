Joash: A young king of Judah who ruled righteously and worked to repair the temple.
Jehoiada: A high priest who guided and supported Joash in his rule and reforms.
Temple: A holy place where the Israelites worshiped God and offered sacrifices.
Repair: The work of restoring or fixing something that is damaged, in this case the temple.
Treasury: A room or building where valuable items and money are kept; here it refers to the temple treasury.
Collection: Gathering money or resources from the people for a specific purpose, here to fund the repair of the temple.
Workers: People who perform manual labor, in this context those who repaired the temple.
Craftsmen: Skilled professionals working with specialized abilities, such as carpenters, stonecutters, and metalworkers, involved in the temple's repair.

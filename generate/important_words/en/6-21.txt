Levites: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, appointed as priests and given responsibility to serve God and the people.
Inheritance: A portion of the land given to each of the tribes of Israel as their property and heritage.
Cities: Places where people settled and organized communities, given to the Levites as dwellings and centers for worship.
City of Refuge: A safe place, especially for those who unintentionally killed someone, where they could seek protection and avoid vengeance.
Caleb: An important figure in the book of Joshua, one of the twelve spies sent by Moses to explore Canaan, and one of the few who urged the Israelites to trust God and take possession of the land.
Eleazar: The high priest and son of Aaron, who together with Joshua led the distribution of the land among the tribes of Israel.

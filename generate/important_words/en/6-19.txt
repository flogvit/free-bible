Joshua: The main character in the book, an Israelite leader who leads the children of Israel into the promised land after Moses' death.
Promise: A commitment or guarantee given by God to the people of Israel regarding a specific land area as their inheritance and home.
Land: The region that God promised to give the children of Israel, also known as Canaan; includes territories and cities.
Inheritance: The portion of the land that each Israelite tribe receives as its property and rightful possession.
Tribe: A group of people related through common ancestors, forming part of the nation of Israel.
Territory: A geographic region assigned to a specific tribe as their inheritance and dwelling place.
City: An inhabited place with an organized society and infrastructure, often part of a tribe's territory.
Boundary: A limit or dividing line that defines the extent of a territory belonging to a tribe.
Casting lots: A method for assigning territories to the tribes by drawing lots, which is considered to be guided by God's will.

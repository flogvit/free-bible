Lord: A title for God meaning the almighty, sovereign Creator and Ruler of the universe.
Blessed: To receive divine favor or protection, leading to happiness, prosperity, and success.
Fear: To show reverence and respect toward God, acknowledging His authority and keeping His commands.
Labor: Effort, strength, or energy used to accomplish a task or reach a goal.
Wife: A married woman in relation to her husband, symbolizing family and unity.
Children: Offspring of humans, representing the future and the legacy left by parents.
Olive plants: Symbolize fruitfulness, growth, and blessing, often used as an image for children and their potential.
Jerusalem: The holy city in Israel, the place of God's temple and the center of Jewish faith and culture.
Peace: A state of harmony, freedom from conflict and turmoil, both inward and outward.

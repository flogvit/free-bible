Vanity: A feeling of meaninglessness and emptiness, which the Preacher describes as a central part of human experience.
Earthly: Everything connected to the world and physical life, in contrast to the spiritual or eternal.
Futility: The shortness and transience of everything in the world, which the Preacher points out to show the meaninglessness of life.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and judge rightly, something the Preacher seeks but finds unsatisfying in light of life's meaninglessness.
Work: Effort and activity carried out to achieve a goal, often linked to one's profession. The Preacher sees work as a source of emptiness and meaninglessness.
Time: A continuous sequence of events and moments, which the Preacher claims moves in cycles and repeats itself.
Sun: A symbol of nature's cyclical patterns and the transience of human life, which the Preacher uses to emphasize life's emptiness.

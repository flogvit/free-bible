Sion: A mountain in Jerusalem, often used as a symbol of God's presence and protection.
The Lord: A title for God, the almighty Creator of the universe and all life.
Surrounds: To be around or on all sides, here referring to God's protection and presence.
Watches over: To protect and preserve, here it refers to God's care for His people.
Righteous: People who live according to God's will and moral standards.
Unrighteous: People who do not follow God's will and moral standards, and act sinfully.
Peace: The absence of conflict and strife, as well as harmony and well-being among people and with God.

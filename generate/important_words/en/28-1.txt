Hosea: The prophet through whom God spoke and the author of the book of Hosea in the Old Testament.
Gomer: Hosea's wife, an unfaithful woman who symbolizes Israel's adultery against God.
Jezreel: A city and valley in Israel, the site of many historical events and prophetic symbols.
Lo-Ruhama: The name of Hosea's first daughter, meaning "not loved," symbolizing God's lack of mercy toward Israel.
Lo-Ammi: The name of Hosea's second son, meaning "not my people," representing the breach between God and Israel.
Adultery: A violation of faithfulness and trust, especially in the marriage between Hosea and Gomer, and between God and Israel.
Repentance: The process of turning away from sin and returning to God, a central theme in the book of Hosea.
Mercy: God's grace and forgiveness toward people despite their sins and failures.

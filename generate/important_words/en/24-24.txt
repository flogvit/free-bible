Fig: Fruit from the fig tree, used as a symbol in Jeremiah chapter 24 to represent different groups of God's people Israel.
Good figs: In Jeremiah chapter 24, the good figs symbolize those Israelites who are regarded as righteous and faithful toward God.
Bad figs: In Jeremiah chapter 24, the bad figs symbolize those Israelites who are not faithful to God and have turned away from His ways.
Jerusalem: The capital city of ancient Israel and Judah, a central place for worship and political power.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, known for its power and influence. In Jeremiah chapter 24, Babylon refers to the place where many Israelites were taken into exile.
Exile: The condition of being forced to leave one's homeland, often as punishment or because of war. In Jeremiah chapter 24, the exile represents a consequence of God's people Israel's disobedience toward Him.
Restoration: The process of restoring something to its original state. In Jeremiah chapter 24, restoration is a promise that God will restore His people after the exile.

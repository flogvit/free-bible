Covenant: An agreement or bond between two parties, in this case between God and the people of Israel, which involves promises and obligations from both sides.
Blessings: Good things or benefits that God gives to his people as a result of their obedience to his commands and covenant.
Punishment: Negative consequences or sufferings that God brings upon his people when they break his commands and covenant, in order to correct and discipline them.
Prosper: To achieve success, growth, or progress, especially as a result of following God's way and being obedient to his commands.
Enemies: Persons or groups who are opposed to or hostile toward the people of Israel, and who may threaten their safety, peace, and well-being.
Land: The area that God has promised to give to the people of Israel, and in which they are to live and cultivate according to his will and covenant.
Sin: An action or condition that goes against God's law and will, leading to separation from him and his blessings.
Repent: To change one's mind, heart, and life so as to turn away from sin and evil, and turn to God to seek his forgiveness, grace, and help.

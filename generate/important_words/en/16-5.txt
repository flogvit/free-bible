Usurer: Persons who lend money to others and charge high interest, often in an unfair manner.
Repayment: The process of paying back money that has been borrowed, usually including interest and fees.
Slavery: A condition of being bound or under the control of another person, often in the form of servitude.
Interest: The payment required for borrowing money, typically expressed as a percentage of the amount borrowed.
Collateral: Property or an item pledged as security for a loan, which may be taken by the lender if the loan is not repaid.
Restoration: The act of restoring something to its original condition, such as financial stability or justice.
Fear of God: Respect and reverence for God, leading to obedience to his commands and a desire to follow his ways.
Nehemiah: A central figure in the book of Nehemiah, governor of Jerusalem and leader of Jewish rebuilding after the exile in Babylon.

David: The king of Israel who reigned around 1000 BC and is known for his skill as a warrior, poet, and musician.
Census: A process in which an official count of the population is carried out, often for tax purposes or military recruitment.
Satan: An evil spiritual being who represents evil and temptation, and who according to the Bible tries to lead people away from God.
Wrath: Intense anger or indignation, especially in connection with God's response to human sin and disobedience.
Punishment: The consequences or retribution for an action, especially regarding God's righteous judgment on sin.
Angel: A supernatural spiritual being who serves as God's messenger and servant, carrying out His will.
Jerusalem: The capital of ancient Israel and a central place for Jewish and Christian faith, where Solomon's temple was built.
Altar: A holy place or structure where religious rituals, sacrifices, and prayers are performed as part of worship and devotion to God.
Ornan: A Jebusite who owned a threshing floor in Jerusalem, where David built an altar to God and offered sacrifices after the plague had stopped.

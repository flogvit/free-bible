Here are some important words and their explanations from Numbers 10:
Tabernacle: A temporary holy place where the Israelites worshiped God during their wilderness journey.
Carrying poles: Poles used to carry the tabernacle when the Israelites moved camp.
High priest: The highest religious leader among the Israelites, responsible for performing sacrifices and other ceremonies.
Two silver trumpets: Instruments made of silver that were used to call the assembly together and signal the movement of the camp.
Wilderness journey: The period during which the Israelites wandered in the wilderness after leaving Egypt and before entering the promised land.
Cloud: A manifestation of God's presence that led the Israelites through the wilderness.

Elijah: One of Israel's prophets, known for his zeal and faithfulness to God. He performed many miracles and warned the people to turn away from idol worship.
Elisha: Another prophet in Israel, who was Elijah's disciple and successor. He received a double portion of Elijah's spirit and continued his work by encouraging the people to follow God.
Chariot of Fire: A symbolic description of the divine intervention that took Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind. This highlights Elijah's special relationship with God and his exalted status among the prophets.
Jordan: A river in the Middle East that holds great significance in biblical history. Elijah and Elisha crossed this river miraculously by God's power before Elijah was taken up to heaven.
Schools of the Prophets: Groups of young men who studied under a prophet to be trained and prepared to serve God. Elisha visited these schools after Elijah was taken up to heaven, where he taught and guided them.
Double Portion of the Spirit: A prayer Elisha asked for, requesting twice as much of God's Spirit as Elijah had possessed. This shows his desire to be an even more effective servant of God and to continue Elijah's work.

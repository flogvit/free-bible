Bezalel: A skilled artist and craftsman chosen by God to lead the work of building the Tabernacle.
Oholiab: Another skilled artist and craftsman who worked together with Bezalel on the Tabernacle.
Tabernacle: A portable sanctuary tent that the Israelites used for worship during their journey through the wilderness.
Offering: A gift or act performed to honor, thank, or seek favor from God; often involved animals, food, or other valuable items.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a good and effective way, especially in moral and spiritual matters.
Heart: In this context, a person's inner being, including thoughts, feelings, will, and morality.
Work: The effort and activities involved in making, building, or producing something, especially for a specific purpose.

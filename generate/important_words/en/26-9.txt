Marked: A sign placed on the foreheads of the righteous in Jerusalem to protect them from God's judgment.
Weeping: An expression of grief and repentance over the sins committed by the people of Jerusalem.
Vision: A divine revelation or prophetic dream given to Ezekiel by God to convey His message.
The glory of the Lord: God's presence and majesty, often associated with His power and authority.
City gate: The entrance to a city, often used as a symbol for access to protection and safety.
Elders: Leaders and wise men in ancient Israel, responsible for guiding and governing the community.
Six men: Six angels or heavenly beings sent by God to carry out His judgment on Jerusalem.
Outpouring of wrath: God's punishment and anger poured out upon the wicked and unjust in Jerusalem.

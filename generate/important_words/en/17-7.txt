Esther: A Jewish woman who became queen of Persia and the main character in the book of Esther.
Mordecai: Esther's adoptive father and a central figure in the story, who helps her save the Jewish people.
Haman: A high-ranking Persian official who seeks to destroy the Jews but is ultimately punished for his evil plans.
Ahasuerus: The king of Persia, also known as Xerxes, who is married to Esther and plays a key role in the story.
Purim: A Jewish festival celebrated in remembrance of the events in the book of Esther, when the Jews were saved from Haman's wicked plot.
Decree: An official order or law, especially one issued by a king or other authority.
Gallows: A tall pole or structure used to hang people as a form of execution. In the book of Esther, it is Haman who is hanged on the gallows he had built for Mordecai.

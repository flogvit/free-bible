Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting in accordance with divine laws and ethical norms.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience effectively to make good decisions and solve problems.
The Lord: Another designation for God, emphasizing his sovereignty and authority over humanity and the universe.
Punishment: The consequences or reactions that follow from breaking divine laws or moral rules.
Fear: A deep respect and reverence for God and his power, often associated with the desire to obey his commands and avoid his wrath.
The Unrighteous: People who do not follow God's principles of righteousness and who act in opposition to his will and ethical norms.
Wrath: God's strong displeasure and anger toward sin and evil, which can result in punishment and suffering for the guilty.

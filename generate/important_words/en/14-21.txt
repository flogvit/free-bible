Jehoram: A king of Judah who ruled in the 9th century BC, known for his wickedness and idolatry.
Judah: One of the twelve tribes of Israel and a kingdom in the southern part of ancient Israel.
The prophet Elijah: An important prophet in the Old Testament, who performed miracles and spoke God's word to the people.
Edom: An ancient kingdom southeast of Israel, often in conflict with Israel and Judah.
Libnah: A city in ancient Israel that rebelled against Jehoram's rule because of his wickedness.
Sickness: Jehoram was struck by a severe intestinal disease as punishment from God for his evil deeds.
Death: Jehoram died after two years of suffering from the illness, and his death was not mourned by the people of Judah.

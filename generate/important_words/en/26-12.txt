Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Chapter 12: The twelfth chapter of the book of Ezekiel containing prophecies and visions concerning the people of Israel and their leaders.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Vision: A spiritual experience in which a person sees or hears something hidden from others, often carrying a message from God.
Prophecy: A prediction or revelation about future events given by God through a prophet.
Exile: A condition in which people are forced to leave their homeland, often due to war, persecution, or punishment from God.
Jerusalem: The capital of Israel and a holy city for Jews, Christians, and Muslims. In Ezekiel chapter 12, it is central to the prophecies about judgment and exile.
Leaders: People with authority and power over a nation, often referred to as kings, princes, or elders in the Bible.
Judgment: God's just punishment or discipline for human sin and disobedience.
Hope: The belief that God will intervene and change the situation for the better, even in difficult times.

Moses: The prophet and leader of the people of Israel who received the law from God and led them out of Egypt and through the wilderness.
The Law: The divine instructions and commandments given by God to Moses on Mount Sinai, including the Ten Commandments.
Inheritance: The land and blessings that God promised to give to the people of Israel, especially Canaan, the promised land.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks God's will and moral law, leading to separation from God.
Humility: An attitude of modesty and submission, where one acknowledges their own limitations and dependence on God.

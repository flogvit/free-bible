Levitical cities: Cities that were given to the Levites, one of the twelve tribes of Israel, to live in and use as places of refuge.
Levites: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, chosen by God to carry out special religious duties and services in the tabernacle and later in the temple.
Avenger of blood: A person from the victim's family who was responsible for killing a murderer in case of homicide, as a form of vengeance and justice.
City of refuge: A safe place where someone who had committed unintentional manslaughter could seek protection from avengers of blood.
Murder: An intentional act in which a person takes another person's life with premeditation.
Unintentional killing: An accidental act in which a person takes another person's life without premeditation.
Priest: A person who performed religious ceremonies and sacrifices on behalf of the people in ancient Israel.

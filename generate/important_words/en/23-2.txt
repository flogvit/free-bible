Judgment Day: A day in the future when God will judge humanity and the world, and justice will be restored.
Kingdom of Peace: A time of peace and harmony that will come after God's intervention, where nations will lay down their weapons and live together in peace.
Mount Zion: A mountain in Jerusalem that symbolizes God's presence and the place of his holy temple.
Light: A symbol of God's guidance and truth, helping people to find the right path in life and avoid evil.
Darkness: A symbol of ignorance, evil, and spiritual blindness that leads people away from God's way.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and in accordance with God's will, which is the foundation for a good society and human relationships.
Humility: The quality of being modest and submitting to God's will, considered a virtue in religious faith.
Pride: A sinful attitude characterized by arrogance, self-importance, and self-exaltation, which opposes God's desire for humility and obedience.

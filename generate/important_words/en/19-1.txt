Psalm: A poetic and musical form of prayer, praise, or lament in the Old Testament.
Lord: A title often used for God in the Old Testament, representing his sovereignty and authority.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, honor, and worship directed toward God for his goodness and grace.
Righteous: A person who lives in accordance with God's will and legal requirements, and is therefore accepted by God.
Sinner: A person who breaks God's law and morality, and is therefore separated from God and his blessings.
Blessed: To be favored and protected by God, receiving his grace and care.
Law: God's instructions and commandments given to humanity, especially the Ten Commandments and other regulations in the books of Moses.
Meditation: Deep and focused thinking, often used to reflect on God's word and his works.
Throne: A symbol of royal power and authority, often used metaphorically to describe God's dominion.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human actions, both in this life and after death.

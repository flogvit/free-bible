Sem: One of Noah's three sons, ancestor of the Semites.
Ham: Another of Noah's sons, ancestor of the Hamites.
Japheth: The third son of Noah, ancestor of the Japhetites.
Peoples: Groups of people who share a common culture and language, often regarded as descendants of a common ancestor.
Tongue: A language or dialect spoken by a particular people.
Land: A geographic area inhabited by a people or nation.
Nation: A large group of people united by common origin, history, culture, or language, usually living in a specific territory.
Ancestors: The first members of a family or ethnic group regarded as founders or progenitors.

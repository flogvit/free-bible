Solomon: David's son and successor as king over Israel, known for his wisdom and wealth.
David's instructions: Advice and guidance given by David to Solomon before he dies, about how to rule the kingdom and deal with enemies.
Joab: David's former military commander, who is executed by Solomon because of his past actions and disloyalty.
Abiathar: A former priest who is removed from office by Solomon because of his support for Adonijah, Solomon's rival.
Adonijah: Half-brother to Solomon who claims the throne, but is eventually killed on Solomon's orders.
Benaiah: A loyal military commander under Solomon who carries out executions and missions on behalf of the king.
Shimei's house: A man who had previously cursed David, but is pardoned by Solomon on condition that he never leaves Jerusalem. He breaks this agreement and is executed.

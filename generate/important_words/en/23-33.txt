Zion: A hill in Jerusalem that symbolizes God's dwelling place and protection for His people.
The glory of the Lord: A term describing God's presence, power, and majestic nature.
Plunder: Spoils or property taken from enemies after a victory, often used as a symbol of God's righteous judgment.
Destruction: The process of destroying or bringing something to an end, frequently used in prophetic texts to describe God's punishment against wicked nations.
Righteousness: Moral conduct and principles that align with God's will and commands.
Peace: A state of harmony and absence of conflict, both individually and between nations, resulting from following God's way.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and apply knowledge in a way that leads to good choices and actions, especially in relation to God's will.
Understanding: The capacity to recognize and interpret information, ideas, and situations accurately and insightfully.

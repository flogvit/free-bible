Immanuel: A name meaning "God is with us," and a symbol of God's protection and presence among His people.
Ahaz: The king of Judah during Isaiah's time, who sought help from the Assyrians instead of trusting in God.
Prophecy: A divinely inspired statement or message about future events or God's will, delivered through a prophet such as Isaiah.
Judah: An Old Testament kingdom in southern Israel, whose inhabitants were descendants of Jacob's son Judah.
Assyria: A powerful ancient nation in Mesopotamia that threatened both Israel and Judah during Isaiah's time.
Syria: A neighboring country to Israel and Judah, often in conflict with them and allied with other nations against them.
Ephraim: One of the tribes of Israel and also a name used to refer to the northern kingdom of Israel.
Alliance: A union or agreement between two or more parties to cooperate against common enemies or achieve shared goals.
Sign: A miracle, event, or object that confirms God's promises and presence, giving hope and guidance to people.
Trust: To rely on God's protection, care, and plan, even when circumstances look dark and human solutions fail.

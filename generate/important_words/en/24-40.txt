Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who conquered Jerusalem and led the Jews into exile.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's words and warnings to the people of Israel.
Gedaliah: Appointed by Nebuchadnezzar as governor over Judea after the fall of Jerusalem.
Ishmael: A member of the Jewish royal family who planned and carried out the murder of Gedaliah.
Mizpah: A town in the region of Benjamin where Gedaliah established his administration.
Babel: Another name for Babylon, the capital of the Babylonian empire.
Exile: Forced removal from one's homeland, often as punishment or political control.
Prophecy: A message or revelation from God concerning future events or divine guidance.

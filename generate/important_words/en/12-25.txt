Jerusalem: The capital of the ancient kingdom of Judah, which was destroyed by the Babylonians in this account.
Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who conquered and destroyed Jerusalem, leading many of its inhabitants into exile.
Zedekiah: The last king of Judah, installed by Nebuchadnezzar, but he rebelled against him, leading to Jerusalem's fall.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia that conquered and destroyed Jerusalem during the reign of Nebuchadnezzar.
Exile: The condition of being forced away from one's homeland, as the Jews experienced after Jerusalem was destroyed by the Babylonians.
The Temple: The holy place in Jerusalem where the Jews worshipped God, which was destroyed by the Babylonians during the siege.
Seraiah: The high priest in Jerusalem who was executed by the Babylonians after Jerusalem fell.
Gedaliah: A Jewish leader appointed by the Babylonians to govern the remaining Jews in Judah after Jerusalem's fall, but he was murdered.
Jehoiachin: Former king of Judah who was taken into exile in Babylon by Nebuchadnezzar, but later released and treated well.

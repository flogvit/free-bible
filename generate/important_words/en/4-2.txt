Tabernacle: A holy tent that housed the Ark of the Covenant and served as a place for worship and offering sacrifices in the wilderness.
Levi: One of Israel's twelve tribes, chosen by God to serve as priests and assist with the service at the tabernacle.
Ark of the Covenant: A sacred chest containing the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, symbolizing God's presence among the Israelites.
Tribes: The twelve groups of Israelites descended from Jacob's twelve sons; they were organized by tribe during their journey through the wilderness.
Numbering: A census of the Israelites commanded by God to organize them by tribe and family.
East, west, north, south: The directions in which the tribes were positioned around the tabernacle; each tribe had its own assigned place.
Commander: The leader of each tribe responsible for organizing and leading the tribe during their journey through the wilderness.

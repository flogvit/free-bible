Elijah: A prophet in Israel who spoke God's word and performed miracles.
Ahaziah: King of Israel who sought counsel from Baal's prophets instead of consulting God.
Ba'al-Zebub: A Canaanite god, also known as the "god of flies," whom Ahaziah consulted for advice.
Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God to people.
Angel: A supernatural being who serves God and helps carry out his will on earth.
Fire: A symbol of God's power and presence, used by Elijah to destroy enemy soldiers.
Ascension: The term used to describe Elijah's being taken up to heaven in a chariot of fire.

Lord: A title referring to God as the supreme authority and leader of his people.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, reverence, and honor to God for his goodness and grace.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and death, granting them eternal life through faith in him.
Enemies: Those who oppose God's will or work against his people, whether human beings or spiritual forces.
Prayer: Communication with God where one expresses thoughts, feelings, and needs, seeking divine guidance and help.
Refuge: A place or state where one finds safety, protection, and comfort in God's presence and care.
Righteousness: God's moral standard, which he expects his people to follow and uses to judge the world.
Sin: Actions, thoughts, and attitudes that break God's law and create separation between people and God.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love given to people despite their sinful nature.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and act in accordance with God's will and principles, leading to a good and meaningful life.

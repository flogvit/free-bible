Tyre: An ancient Phoenician city described as a major trading center and powerful maritime nation in Isaiah 23.
The hand of the Lord: A metaphor for God's power and influence, used to describe how He punishes or rewards people and nations.
Destruction: In this context, it refers to God's judgment on Tyre, where He will destroy the city and its wealth because of their pride and sin.
Seventeen years: The period mentioned in Isaiah 23 during which Tyre must suffer under destruction before it can rise again as a trading city.
Forgotten harp: A symbol of forgetfulness and loss of culture and identity resulting from the destruction of Tyre.
Treasure: The wealth and resources gathered from trade and shipping, which after Tyre's restoration will be dedicated to the Lord.
The Lord: Another name for God, the Almighty Creator who, according to the Bible, rules over the universe and all its creatures.

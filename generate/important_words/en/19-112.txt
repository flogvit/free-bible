Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with moral principles and God's will, treating others with justice and respect.
Fear: A reverent awe and devotion toward God, involving obedience to his commands and living a godly life.
Grace: God's undeserved love and mercy, which he gives to people despite their sins and failures.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and understanding in a practical and effective way, especially regarding moral and spiritual matters.
Light: A symbol of truth, wisdom, and divine guidance that helps people navigate through life's darkness and challenges.
Wealth: Material prosperity and abundance, but also spiritual riches and deep, lasting values.
Inheritance: What a person leaves behind for their descendants or close relatives, whether material possessions, wisdom, faith, or good examples.
House: A reference to both the physical home and the spiritual family, including relatives and friends who share common faith and values.

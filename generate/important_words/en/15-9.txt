Ezra: A priest and scribe who led the people of Israel back from exile in Babylon and taught them God's law.
Confession: A public acknowledgment of sins, faults, or transgressions, often followed by repentance and a desire for reconciliation.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks with God's will and commandments, leading to spiritual separation from God.
Grace: God's undeserved favor and love, offered to people despite their sins and weaknesses.
Covenant: A sacred agreement between God and his people, in which both parties commit to certain conditions and promises.
Exile: The period when the people of Israel were taken away from their homeland and held as captives in a foreign land, in this case Babylon.
Reconciliation: The process of restoring a broken relationship, especially between God and humanity, through repentance, forgiveness, and grace.

Lord: Another designation for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Passover: A Jewish festival celebrating Israel's deliverance from slavery in Egypt.
Firstborn: The first person or animal born to a mother; in this context, it refers to the eldest son in a family.
Consecrate: To set something or someone apart for a special purpose or holy service.
Unleavened bread: Bread without yeast, symbolizing the haste with which the Israelites left Egypt and had no time to let the dough rise.
Memorial: A reminder of an important event or experience, often connected to a ceremony or celebration.
Outstretched hand: An expression describing God's power and might, especially in connection with miracles and intervention in human affairs.

Sun: The nearest star to Earth that provides light and warmth, and is essential for life on the planet.
Time: A continuous progression of events from the past, through the present, and into the future.
Work: Physical or mental effort undertaken to achieve a goal or create something.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good decisions and judge rightly.
Joy: A feeling of happiness, pleasure, or contentment experienced by an individual.
Youth: An early stage in a person's life, marked by growth, development, and learning.
Old age: A later stage in a person's life, characterized by physical and mental changes resulting from aging.
Life: The state or process of being alive, which includes growth, reproduction, and response to stimuli.
Death: The cessation of all biological functions that define a living organism.
Fate: An inevitable or predetermined sequence of events or consequences.
Fear of God: A reverent respect and awe for God, leading to obedience and godliness.

Samuel: A prophet and judge in Israel who served God and led the people.
The people of Israel: A collective term for the twelve tribes of Israel, a people chosen by God to be his special possession.
Elders: Experienced and respected leaders in society who were responsible for making important decisions and guiding the people.
King: A monarchical leader who holds supreme authority over a country or kingdom. The people of Israel wanted a king to be like other nations.
Nations: Different groups of people that make up various societies, cultures, and geographic regions.
The Lord: Another name for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Warning: A message or action that conveys a potential danger or negative consequence.
Obey: To follow instructions, rules, or requests from an authority.
Servants: Persons who are subordinate and work for someone of higher status, often without pay or with very low wages.
Sons: Offspring or descendants of a person, used both literally and symbolically to refer to relationships between generations.

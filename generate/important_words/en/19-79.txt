The Lord's temple: A term for the place where God is worshiped and honored, often referred to as the temple in Jerusalem.
Salvation: To be saved from sin, death, or destruction, especially with regard to the spiritual salvation that God gives.
Nations: Groups of people who share a common culture, tradition, or geographic area, often used to describe non-Israelite peoples.
Desecration: An act that dishonors, damages, or destroys something holy or valuable, such as a temple or religious object.
Restoration: The process of bringing something back to its original state, here used in connection with the land and people of Israel.
Vengeance: The act of punishing someone for a wrong they have committed, often understood as God's just punishment for sin.
Praise: A song or expression of gratitude, honor, and worship to God, often used in prayer and worship services.

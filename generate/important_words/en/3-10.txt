Nadab and Abihu: Two of Aaron's sons who performed an unauthorized offering and were punished with death by God.
Aaron: The high priest and brother of Moses; father of Nadab and Abihu.
Holy fire: A sacred fire used to burn offerings to God.
Strange fire: Unauthorized or unholy fire that was not to be used in offerings.
Tabernacle: A special place dedicated to God's honor, where offerings and religious ceremonies take place.
High priest: The supreme religious leader in Israel, responsible for performing offerings and ceremonies.
Mourning: A period of grief and lament over the loss of someone, usually accompanied by tears, clothing, and behavior that shows sorrow.

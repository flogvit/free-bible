Joshua: The main character in the book and leader of Israel's people after Moses, who helps them take possession of the promised land.
Promised Land: The area God had promised to give to Israel's people, also known as Canaan.
Altar: A structure used for offering gifts and sacrifices to God as part of religious rituals and ceremonies.
Tribes: The twelve groups of Israel's people, each descending from one of Jacob's sons.
Peace: A state of harmony and absence of conflict between people or nations.
Reconciliation: The process of restoring a good relationship between people or groups who have been in conflict.
Obedience: The act of following God's commands and instructions, which is important for maintaining a good relationship with him.
Inheritance: Land and possessions given to Israel's tribes as part of God's promise.

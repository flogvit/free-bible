Love: A deep devotion or feeling of dedication, especially between God and people in the book of Hosea chapter 6.
Return: In the book of Hosea chapter 6, the process of turning back to God after a period of falling away or sin.
Repentance: A change of heart and mind that leads to a change in behavior, often related to a deeper understanding of God's will and a desire to correct past mistakes.
Knowledge: Understanding of God's will and his plans for people, as well as insight into how to live a righteous life.
Mercy: God's grace and compassion toward people, especially regarding the forgiveness of sins and offering the possibility of reconciliation and salvation.
Sacrifice: The act of giving up something valuable as an offering to God, to show devotion, gratitude, or seek forgiveness for sins.
Faithfulness: The ability and willingness to keep promises and remain loyal, especially in the relationship between God and people in the book of Hosea chapter 6.

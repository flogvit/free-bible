Michael: The archangel who protects the people of Israel and fights against evil forces.
End times: The end of the present age, when God's judgment will come and righteousness will prevail.
Resurrection: The process in which the dead are raised to life and receive either eternal life or eternal punishment, depending on their deeds and faith.
Righteous ones: Those who follow God's laws and live morally good lives.
Evil ones: Those who act against God's will and commit sins.
Knowledge: Understanding and insight into God's plan and his purposes for humanity.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge in a way that aligns with God's will and purpose.
Trials: Events and situations that test people's faith and character.
Sealing: The act of hiding or protecting information until a specific time.

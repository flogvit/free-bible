Joshua: The main character and leader of the people of Israel after Moses' death, who leads them into the promised land of Canaan.
Canaan: The area that God had promised to give to the people of Israel, located in present-day Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and parts of Jordan and Syria.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Miracle: A supernatural event or intervention from God that breaks the laws of nature.
Hazor: A Canaanite city that was one of the largest and most powerful in the region, which Joshua and the people of Israel conquered.
Allies: Several kings and peoples who join together to fight against a common enemy, in this case against Joshua and the people of Israel.
Conquer: To take control of a land or city by using military force.
Challenge: A difficult task or situation that requires courage, endurance, and determination to overcome.

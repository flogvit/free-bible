Adam: The first human created by God, the ancestor of all humanity.
Eva: Adam's wife and the first woman, mother of Cain, Abel, and Seth.
Seth: The third son of Adam and Eve, regarded as the ancestor of the righteous line of people.
Noah: A righteous man chosen by God to build the ark and survive the flood with his family and the animals.
Shem, Ham, and Japheth: Noah's three sons, from whom all nations on earth descended after the flood.
Abraham: The ancestor of the Israelites, who made a covenant with God that his descendants would become a great nation.
Isaac: Abraham's son by Sarah, father of Jacob and Esau, blessed by God and part of the covenant.
Jacob: Isaac's son, known as Israel, father of the twelve tribes of Israel.
Esau: Isaac's son and Jacob's twin brother, ancestor of the Edomites.
Joseph: One of Jacob's twelve sons, sold into slavery by his brothers, but later became a powerful leader in Egypt.
Covenant: A sacred agreement between God and people, often connected with promises and blessings.

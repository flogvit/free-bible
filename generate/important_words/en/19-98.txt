Psalm: A poetic religious song, often used in worship and prayer.
Lord: Another name for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Acts of salvation: God's actions to save and deliver humanity from sin and evil.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a good and righteous way.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and justly, in accordance with moral and ethical standards.
Mercy: God's grace and compassion, especially toward those who suffer or are in need.
Hymn: A song or praise expressing gratitude and honor to God.
Nations: The various peoples and cultures on earth.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human behavior.
Creation: The entire universe and everything that exists, including nature and all life.

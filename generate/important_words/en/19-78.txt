Asaph: One of the leading musicians and poets in King David's time, who wrote several psalms, including Psalm 78.
Teaching: Instruction or guidance given to convey knowledge, especially concerning God's laws and will.
Miracles: Supernatural acts performed by God that display his power and care for his people.
Egypt: The ancient kingdom in North Africa where the Israelites were enslaved before the exodus led by Moses.
Plagues: A series of disasters and afflictions sent by God upon Egypt to compel Pharaoh to let the Israelites go free.
Chosen: The term describing God's special relationship with the Israelites, whom he selected as his own people.
Lead: To show the way or guide someone in a particular direction, often used regarding God's guidance of his people.
Shepherd: A person who cares for and leads sheep; in Psalm 78 it is used metaphorically to describe God's care for his people.
David: The second king of Israel, known for his skill as a warrior, musician, and poet, and the author of many psalms.

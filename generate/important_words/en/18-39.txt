Wild animals: Animals that live freely in nature, without human control or domestication.
Mountain goat: A type of goat that lives in mountainous areas and is known for its agility and climbing ability.
Hind: Another name for a female deer, an animal belonging to the deer family that lives in forests and open landscapes.
Wild donkey: A wild-living donkey found in desert and steppe regions, known for its endurance and adaptability.
Ostrich: A large, flightless bird known for its speed and ability to run quickly over long distances.
Horse: A large, four-legged mammal often used by humans as a work and riding animal.
Grasshopper: An insect that can jump long distances using its powerful hind legs and often gathers in large swarms.
Eagle: A large bird of prey known for its sharp vision and ability to soar high in the sky.
Sea eagle: A special type of eagle that lives near the coast and hunts fish and other sea creatures.

Law: Rules and instructions given by God to humanity, especially through Moses in the Old Testament.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, often with promises and obligations from both parties.
Testimony: God's revelation and instruction to humanity, particularly as recorded in the Scriptures.
Commandments: Specific commands and instructions given by God, such as the Ten Commandments.
Righteousness: The quality of being morally right and just, especially in relation to God's will and standards.
Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Understanding: The ability to comprehend and judge situations and actions in a wise and insightful way.
Guidance: Advice and instruction given by God or other wise people to help individuals navigate life and make good decisions.
Blessedness: A feeling of joy and contentment that comes from living a good and meaningful life, especially in harmony with God's will.

Eliphaz: One of Job's three friends who criticized and condemned him in his suffering, claiming that his problems were the result of sin.
Light: Symbolizes knowledge, wisdom, and insight. In this context, it refers to God's ability to reveal truth and justice.
Darkness: Represents ignorance, evil, and sin. In Job 18, darkness is used as a metaphor for the fate and destruction of the wicked person.
Snare: A trap or obstacle. Eliphaz uses this term to describe how the wicked are caught and punished by God for their sins.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with moral principles and God's will. In Job 18, there is a contrast between the righteous person and the wicked person.
Wicked: A person who acts against moral principles and God's will. Eliphaz claims that the wicked person will suffer and be punished by God.
Punishment: The consequences of bad actions and sins, especially regarding God's judgment. Eliphaz asserts that God will punish the wicked for their sins and injustice.

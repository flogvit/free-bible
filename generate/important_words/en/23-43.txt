Herod: King of Judea known for his cruelty and political intrigues.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, often used as a symbol of worldly power and evil.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people and the nation they belong to, also called Jacob's descendants.
Salvation: God's act of saving humanity from sin and death, often linked to the Messiah.
Water: A symbol of life and cleansing, used here to show God's protection and care for His people.
Loved: Describes God's deep love and devotion to His chosen people, Israel.
Ways: God's plans and purposes for His people and the world, sometimes incomprehensible to humans.

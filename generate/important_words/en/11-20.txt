Ahab: The king of Israel who is involved in several wars and conflicts in 1 Kings 20.
Ben-Hadad: The king of Aram who attacks Ahab and Israel, but is ultimately defeated.
Prophet: A person who receives messages from God and conveys them to the people. Prophets play an important role in this story by giving advice and warnings to Ahab.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence. In 1 Kings 20, the Lord helps Ahab defeat Ben-Hadad.
Samaria: The capital city of the kingdom of Israel, which Ben-Hadad besieges in 1 Kings 20.
Servant: A person who performs services or work for another person, often a superior. In this context, it refers to the prophet's servant, who carries out tasks on behalf of the prophet.
Defeated: When one party wins over another in a conflict or war. In this chapter, Ben-Hadad and his army are defeated by Ahab and the army of Israel.
Covenant: An agreement or commitment between two parties. Ahab makes a covenant with Ben-Hadad after defeating him.

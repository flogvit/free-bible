Achan: A man from the tribe of Judah in Israel who committed a sin by taking forbidden items during the conquest of Jericho.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks God's will and commandments, leading to separation between humanity and God.
Curse: A negative consequence or punishment resulting from sin or disobedience toward God.
Jericho: An ancient city in Canaan that was destroyed by the Israelites under Joshua's leadership after they crossed the Jordan River.
Ai: Another Canaanite city that the Israelites attacked after Jericho. They suffered defeat on their first attempt because of Achan's sin, but later captured the city after making atonement for the sin.
Stoning: Used as a method of execution in the Old Testament, especially for serious sins. Achan and his family were stoned to death as punishment for his sin.
Lotting: A method used to determine God's will by casting objects such as lots or small stones. Lots were cast to identify Achan as the guilty party in the book of Joshua chapter 7.

Covenant: An agreement or promise between two parties, in this case between God and the people of Israel, involving obligations and blessings.
Law: God's instructions and commandments given to Moses on Mount Sinai, forming the foundation for the Israelites' faith and practice.
Obligations: Requirements or responsibilities that one party has toward another; here it refers to the duties of the Israelites according to their covenant with God.
Temple: The holy place in Jerusalem where the Israelites worshipped God and performed sacrifices, built by King Solomon.
Sacrifice: A gift or act given to God as an expression of gratitude, loyalty, or atonement for sins.
Tithe: One-tenth of income or harvest given to the temple and priests as a form of support and thanksgiving to God.
Sabbatical Year: Every seventh year when the land was to lie fallow and debts were to be forgiven, part of God's law to ensure justice and rest.

Lightbringer: A title given to the fallen angel, Lucifer, meaning "bearer of light." In Isaiah 14 it refers to the king of Babylon and his fall from power.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, known for its pride and arrogance. In the book of Isaiah 14, Babylon is a symbol of worldly power and oppression.
Lord: Another designation for God, referring to His authority and dominion over creation.
Israel: The ancient people and nation whom God chose as His special people. In Isaiah 14, Israel's deliverance from Babylonian captivity is foretold.
Deliverance: The process of being set free from slavery, oppression, or captivity. In this context it refers to Israel's release from Babylonian exile.
Mockery: Derision or contempt expressed toward a person or what they stand for. In Isaiah 14 the Lord mocks Babylon and its ruler after their fall.
Humiliation: The reduction of someone to a lower position, status, or dignity. In the context of Isaiah 14 it refers to the humiliation of Babylon and its ruler.

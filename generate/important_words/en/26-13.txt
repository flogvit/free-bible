Prophecy: A revelation or message from God, often delivered through a prophet.
Ezekiel: One of Israel's prophets who wrote the book of Ezekiel in the Old Testament.
False prophets: People who claim to speak on behalf of God but actually promote their own interests or false teachings.
Vision: A spiritual experience in which a person sees or hears something normally beyond human senses, often used by God to communicate with prophets.
The Wall: In this context, it refers to a symbolic wall built by false prophets to hide the truth and lead people away from God's way.
Whitewash: A material that false prophets use to cover up weaknesses in the wall so it appears solid, but which is actually weak and will collapse.
The Lord's Wrath: God's righteous anger and judgment against sin and evil, especially directed at false prophets and their actions.
Storm: A metaphor for God's judgment and destruction coming upon false prophets and their works.

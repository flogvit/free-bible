Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, the almighty Creator.
Righteousness: God's righteousness, which is his ability to judge and act in accordance with what is right and good.
Joy: A feeling of happiness and contentment, especially in relation to God's presence and actions.
Earth: The planet we live on, and in this context often refers to humanity and all life on earth.
Heaven: The spiritual realm where God and angels dwell, often associated with a place of perfection and harmony.
Mountains: Large natural formations on the earth, used symbolically to represent power and stability.
Islands: Landmasses surrounded by water, used here as part of a poetic description of the whole earth testifying to God's greatness.
Enemies: Those who oppose God and his ways, often used to describe evil forces or persons.
Light: Symbolizes God's goodness, truth, and guidance, standing in contrast to darkness which represents evil and ignorance.
Hiding places: Places where people seek protection and safety, but in this context used to show that no one can escape God's judgment.

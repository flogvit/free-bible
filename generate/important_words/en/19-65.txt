Psalm: A poetic and musical form of prayer or praise, often directed toward God.
Praise: A song or tribute that expresses love, gratitude, and honor to God.
Refuge: A place or person who provides protection, safety, and comfort in difficult times.
Quiet: To bring calm, peace, and harmony to a situation or place.
Bless: To ask for divine protection, favor, and well-being for someone or something.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with God's will and morality, and who is upright and honest.
Deed: An action or achievement, especially one that has moral or spiritual significance.
Fear: A feeling of awe, reverence, and submission toward God and His power.
Earth: The planet we live on, which also refers to the physical landscape and nature around us.

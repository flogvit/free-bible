Immanuel: The name means "God with us" and is used as a symbol of God's presence and protection of his people.
Assyrians: A powerful ancient empire in the Middle East that invaded and conquered many nations, including Israel's northern kingdom.
The Lord's hand: A metaphor for God's power and influence, often used to describe his intervention in human affairs.
Darkness: Symbolizes evil, despair, and ignorance, and is used here to describe the spiritual and moral condition of the people.
Light: Represents God's presence, guidance, and blessings. It stands in contrast to darkness and symbolizes hope and salvation.
Water: Symbolizes life, cleansing, and fruitfulness. In this context, it can also represent the threat of destruction, as when the Euphrates River overflows its banks and invades the land.
Stone: Can symbolize God's firmness and stability, but also danger and judgment when people stumble over it or collide with it.

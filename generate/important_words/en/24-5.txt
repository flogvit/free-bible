Judgment: God's righteous punishment or consequences resulting from human disobedience and sin.
Sin: Transgression of God's law and morality, leading to separation from God and His blessings.
Repentance: A change in mindset and actions, where a person turns away from sin and toward God.
Prophet: A person who receives messages from God and conveys them to the people.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people, as well as a geographical area in the Middle East.
Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel and a central place for Jewish and Christian faith.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Injustice: Unfair treatment of others, which goes against God's laws and principles.
Discipline: Correction or punishment given by God to guide and correct people.

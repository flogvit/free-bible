Moses: A prophet and leader of the people of Israel, who received the law from God and led them out of Egypt.
Inheritance: The transfer of property, rights, or position from one person to another, often after death.
Daughters: Female children of a person; in this context, the daughters of Zelophehad who sought inheritance rights.
Law: A rule or principle given by God to Moses that governs the behavior and faith of the people of Israel.
Priest: A religious leader who performs ceremonies and sacrifices according to God's law.
Joshua: An important figure in Israel's history, Moses' successor as leader of the people of Israel who led them into the promised land.
Hands: Symbolize the transfer of authority, power, or blessing from one person to another through physical contact.

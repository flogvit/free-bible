Drought: A long period with little or no rainfall, leading to water shortages and poor harvests.
Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel and a holy city for Jews, Christians, and Muslims.
Prophet: A person who receives revelations from God and communicates them to the people.
Sorrow: A feeling of sadness and pain, often connected with loss or suffering.
Prayer: Communication with God, in which one asks for help, guidance, or gives thanks for gifts and blessings.
Judgment: God's just punishment for human sin and disobedience.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks God's commands and moral law.
Repentance: To turn away from one's sins and change one's behavior to follow God's way.
False prophets: People who claim to speak on behalf of God but actually spread lies and deception.
Punishment: The consequences or response to an action, especially regarding sin and disobedience toward God.

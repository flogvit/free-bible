Esther: The main character in the book, a young Jewish woman who becomes queen of Persia and saves her people from destruction.
Mordecai: A Jewish man and Esther's adoptive father; he exposes a conspiracy against the king and later becomes a high official.
Haman: An evil Persian nobleman who plans to destroy the Jews but is ultimately punished for his actions.
Xerxes: The king of Persia, who chooses Esther as his queen and supports her efforts to save the Jewish people.
Purim: A Jewish festival that celebrates victory over Haman's evil plan and the deliverance of the Jewish people.
Beauty Contest: A competition in which King Xerxes chose his new queen after his former queen Vashti had been deposed.
The Fast: A period of fasting and prayer that Esther and her people observed before she went to the king to ask for protection for the Jews.
The Gallows: An execution device that Haman built to hang Mordecai, but which he himself ended up being hanged on.

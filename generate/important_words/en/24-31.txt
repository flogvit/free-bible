New Covenant: A renewed agreement between God and his people, involving forgiveness of sins and a new inner relationship with God.
Israel: The name of the ancient Hebrew people, also referring to the twelve tribes that made up the nation of Israel in the Old Testament.
Ephraim: One of Israel's twelve tribes and a term often used to represent the entire Northern Kingdom of Israel.
Jerusalem: The holy city and capital of ancient Israel, the center of Jewish faith and worship.
Zion: Another name for Jerusalem, especially used to refer to the spiritual significance of the city as God's dwelling place.
Gather: The action performed by God when he brings his people together again after a time of separation or exile.
Restore: The process by which God restores the relationship with his people and heals their land and community.
Heart: Symbolizes the inner being of a person, including thoughts, feelings, and will. In Jeremiah 31 it represents the place where God's law is written.
Law: God's instructions and commands to his people, given through Moses and other prophets in the Old Testament.

Lord's Servant: A designation for a person chosen by God to carry out His will and mission, often associated with prophets or the Messiah.
Redemption: The process by which God saves humanity from sin and its consequences, which may include forgiveness, reconciliation, and transformation.
Light to the Gentiles: An image showing that God's salvation and truth are meant to reach all nations and peoples, not only Israel.
Gather: To bring together God's people, whether it be scattered Israelites or the inclusion of other nations in God's kingdom.
Comfort: To provide support, encouragement, and hope to those who suffer or are in distress, especially in connection with God's promises of salvation and restoration.
Inherited Land: The land that God promised to give to the people of Israel, often referred to as Canaan or the Promised Land.
Salvation: The act or state of being saved from sin, evil, and death, and being reunited with God through His grace and love.

Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who received God's word and delivered it to the people.
The Lord's house: An expression referring to the temple in Jerusalem, a holy place for Jewish worship.
Prophecy: A message or revelation from God, often concerning future events, given through a prophet.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or reward for human actions, often linked to nations and individuals.
Turn back: To change course or direction, especially regarding moral or spiritual behavior; to repent.
Listen: To hear and obey God's commands and instructions, which is necessary to avoid judgment and destruction.
False prophets: People who claim to speak on behalf of God but have not been called or appointed by him.

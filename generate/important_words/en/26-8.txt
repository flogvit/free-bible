Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Vision: A spiritual experience in which a person sees or perceives divine revelations.
The glory of the Lord: God's presence and majesty, often described as radiant and overwhelming light.
Idolatry: The practice of worshipping and honoring other gods or idols instead of the true God.
Temple: A holy place where people worship and offer sacrifices to God; in Ezekiel's time, Solomon's temple in Jerusalem was the central place of worship.
Sin: An act or condition of breaking God's law or moral standards; sin leads to separation from God and can result in punishment.
Weeping women: Women who mourned and wept over the Babylonian god Tammuz, an example of idolatry occurring in the temple.
Sun worshippers: Leaders who worshipped the sun, another form of idolatry that took place in the temple.
Judgment: God's righteous assessment and punishment for human sin and wickedness.

Leprosy: A serious skin disease, also known as leprosy, which in the Bible is regarded as a form of impurity and a cause for social isolation.
Priest: A religious leader in Israelite society who was responsible for performing ritual actions and sacrifices on behalf of the people.
Cleansing process: A series of ritual actions and sacrifices that were to be performed to cleanse a person from impurity, which in this case is leprosy.
Sacrifice: A gift or action given to God to seek his favor, gratitude, or forgiveness for sins and impurity.
Blood: In a biblical context, blood symbolizes life and cleansing, and was used in various rituals and sacrifices.
Hyssop: An instrument used by the priest during cleansing rituals to apply blood and oil on the former person with leprosy.
Oil: In a biblical context, oil symbolizes anointing and blessing, and was used in cleansing rituals.

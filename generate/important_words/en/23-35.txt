Desert: A dry, inhospitable land area with little or no vegetation.
Bloom: The process by which plants produce flowers, often as a sign of growth and fruitfulness.
Joy: A feeling of happiness, contentment, or well-being.
Glory: Great honor, recognition, or fame; in a religious context, the radiant presence of God.
Strength: The ability to withstand pressure or carry out actions; here, an encouragement to be brave and trust in God.
Lame: Persons unable to use one or more limbs, often due to illness or injury.
Deaf: Persons who cannot hear or have significantly impaired hearing.
Warrior: A fighter, soldier, or person who fights for a cause.
Holy Way: A symbolic path representing purity and holiness, leading to God's kingdom.
Predator: Animals that hunt and kill other animals for food; here, a metaphor for evil and destruction.

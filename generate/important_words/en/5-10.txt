Commandment: God's commands and instructions to people to live righteously and in harmony with his will.
Covenant: An agreement or bond between God and people, where both parties have obligations and promises toward each other.
Stone tablets: The two stone plates that contained the Ten Commandments, given by God to Moses on Mount Sinai.
Circumcision: A religious ceremony in which the foreskin of the penis is removed as a sign of covenant and belonging to God's people.
Humility: An attitude of respect and submission toward God, involving recognition of one's own limitations and dependence on God's grace.

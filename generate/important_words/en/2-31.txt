Besalel: A skilled Israelite craftsman chosen by God to lead the work of building the tabernacle.
Oholiab: Another skilled Israelite craftsman who was chosen by God to help Besalel build the tabernacle.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a wise and insightful way, given by God to selected individuals.
Craftsmanship: Professional skill within a specific trade or art, such as carpentry, jewelry design, weaving, and engraving.
Sabbath: The seventh day of the week, a holy day for rest and worship, which God commanded the Israelites to keep holy.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, often accompanied by promises and conditions from both parties.
Broke: The act of breaking an agreement, covenant, or promise, leading to consequences and punishment from God.

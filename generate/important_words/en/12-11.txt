Athaliah: Queen and mother of King Ahaziah of Judah, who seized power after her son's death and attempted to wipe out the line of David.
Joash: Son of Ahaziah, rescued from Athaliah's massacre and later crowned king of Judah at the age of seven.
Jehosheba: Half-sister of Ahaziah and aunt of Joash, who saved him from Athaliah's massacre and hid him in the temple.
Jehoiada: High priest in the temple and husband of Jehosheba, who led the revolt against Athaliah and helped Joash become king.
Covenant: An agreement or commitment between two parties, in this case between God and the people of Judah, promising loyalty and obedience.
Temple Guards: Soldiers who protected the temple and the king, playing a key role in overthrowing Athaliah and installing Joash as king.

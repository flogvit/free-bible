Refuge: A safe place or protection, often used to describe God's care and safeguarding.
Enemies: Persons or forces that are against or wish to harm someone; in this case, it refers to those who oppose God's people.
Lord: A title used to show respect and reverence toward God, acknowledging his authority and power.
Guide: To give advice, instructions, or direction; here it refers to God's guidance of his people through life and faith.
Truth: That which is real and correct, often used to describe God's word and teaching.
Grace: God's undeserved love and mercy that he gives to people, despite their faults and sins.
Sin: An action or thought that goes against God's will and morality, leading to separation from God and his love.
Salvation: Deliverance from sin and its consequences, achieved through faith in God and his grace.

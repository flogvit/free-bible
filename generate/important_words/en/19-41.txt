Merciful: To show compassion and grace, especially toward those who suffer or are in distress.
Enemy: A person, group, or power that opposes another, often used to describe evil forces or persons who stand against God's will.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks God's law and contradicts his holy nature.
Lord: A title often used for God in the Old Testament, referring to his sovereign authority and power.
Bless: To ask for or give divine favor and protection, often linked with material or spiritual prosperity.
Forgive: To remove guilt or punishment for a sin, so that the one who has done wrong can restore their relationship with God.
Raised up: In this context, it refers to being restored from sickness, suffering, or moral failure, and can also mean being strengthened by God.

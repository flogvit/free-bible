Moab: An ancient kingdom east of the Dead Sea, known for its hostility toward Israel and Judah.
Sion: Another name for Jerusalem, especially used to refer to God's dwelling place and the spiritual center of the Jewish people.
Refugees: People who flee from danger, persecution, or conflict, seeking protection in another country or region.
Lord of Hosts: A reference to God's divine power and authority, often used to describe his protection of his people and his judgment on their enemies.
Counsel: Guidance, instruction, or wisdom given by someone with insight or experience, often seen as a gift from God.
Mercy: God's grace and compassion toward people, especially those who suffer or are in distress, and his willingness to forgive sins and grant salvation.
Fertility: The ability to produce abundant crops, life, and growth, often viewed as a sign of God's blessing and care.
Destruction: The total ruin or devastation of something, especially as a result of God's punishing actions against sin and evil.

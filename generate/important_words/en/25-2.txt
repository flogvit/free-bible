The Lord's wrath: God's strong displeasure and anger toward the people of Israel because of their sins and disobedience.
Jerusalem: The capital city of ancient Israel, a central place for worship and cultural identity for the Israelites.
Temple: The holy place where the Israelites worshipped God, which was destroyed during the fall of Jerusalem.
Enemies: The nations and peoples who attacked and conquered Jerusalem, often used as instruments of God's judgment.
Lamentation: A poetic genre characterized by grief and mourning over loss, suffering, and destruction.
Prophet: A person chosen by God to deliver his message and warnings to the people.
Judgment: The consequences of the sins and disobedience of the people of Israel, including the destruction of Jerusalem and the temple.
Sin: Transgression against God's laws and commands, which leads to his wrath and judgment.
Mercy: God's grace and compassion toward people, despite their sins and failures.
Hope: The belief that God will restore and heal after suffering and destruction.

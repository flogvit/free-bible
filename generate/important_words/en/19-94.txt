Lord: A title used to refer to God as the supreme authority and ruler over all.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or work against God's will and plan.
Righteousness: A moral quality that involves acting in accordance with God's laws and principles.
Day of Judgment: The day when God will judge all people based on their deeds and faith, granting them eternal reward or punishment.
Help: Support or assistance that God gives to those who seek Him and trust in His protection and guidance.
Comfort: To give solace and relief to those who suffer or are in distress, often through God's love and care.
Defender: A person or power that protects and stands up for the weak, innocent, and oppressed, often a reference to God Himself.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, reverence, and worship toward God through song, prayer, or other forms of artistic expression.

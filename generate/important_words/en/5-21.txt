War: An armed conflict between two or more parties, often nations or ethnic groups.
Women: Adult persons of the female sex; in this context, it refers to female prisoners of war.
Inheritance: The transfer of assets, property, and rights from one person to another after death.
Murder: The unlawful and intentional killing of a human being; in Deuteronomy 21, it concerns unsolved murders.
Firstborn: The first person born among siblings, with special rights and duties according to Jewish law.
City: A densely populated area where people live, work, and interact.
Worship: A religious ceremony or act performed to honor and worship God.

Absalom: One of King David's sons who rebelled against his father and tried to take the throne.
David: King of Israel, a central figure in the books of Samuel, known for his faith in God and his military conquests.
Hushai: A friend and advisor of David who helped him fight Absalom's rebellion by giving false advice to Absalom.
Ahithophel: A former advisor to David who switched sides to join Absalom and gave him counsel during the rebellion.
Zadok: A priest who remained loyal to David and helped him fight against Absalom's rebellion.
Abiathar: Another priest who was loyal to David and helped him fight against Absalom's rebellion.
Counsel: Recommendations or guidance given by one person to another, often regarding political or military decisions.

Sara: Wife of Abraham and mother of Isaac, she dies in this chapter and her death is described.
Abraham: The patriarch who makes a covenant with God and is the father of Isaac. He wishes to buy a burial place for Sara.
Ephron: A Hittite who owns land at Machpelah, where Abraham wants to bury Sara.
Machpelah: A piece of land with a cave near Mamre, which Abraham buys from Ephron to bury Sara.
Cave: The place where Abraham buries Sara, and later he himself and his descendants are buried here.
Silver: The currency that Abraham uses to buy the burial site from Ephron.
Negotiate: The process in which Abraham and Ephron discuss the price of the land before agreeing on a sum.

Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment; highly valued in the book of Proverbs.
Understanding: The ability to judge situations or people in a wise and insightful manner; closely linked to wisdom.
Righteousness: The principle of being just and morally upright; acting in accordance with ethical norms and laws.
Discipline: Correction or training used to guide and shape behavior, especially among children and young people.
Knowledge: Information, facts, principles, and ideas acquired through experience, observation, and learning.
Contempt: A strong feeling of disapproval and disgust toward something or someone; often mentioned as a negative attitude in the book of Proverbs.
Laziness: Lack of willingness to work or exert effort; regarded as a bad trait that can lead to poverty and misery.
Fear of God: Deep respect and awe for God; considered the foundation of wisdom and a good life.

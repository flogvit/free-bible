Prayer: A communication between people and God, where one asks for help, guidance, or gives thanks for blessings.
Grace: God's undeserved love and mercy given to people, despite their sins and failures.
Lord: A title used to show respect and reverence toward God, acknowledging his sovereignty and power.
Despair: A feeling of hopelessness and sorrow, often experienced when facing great trials and challenges in life.
Comfort: To give strength, hope, and encouragement to someone in distress or grief, often through God's presence and support.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, death, and eternal destruction, through faith in Jesus Christ.
Tears: A physical and emotional response to pain, sorrow, or joy, often used as a symbol of human suffering and vulnerability.

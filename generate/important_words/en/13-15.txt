Ark: A chest that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, and was a symbol of God's presence among the Israelites.
David: The second king of Israel, a central figure in the Old Testament, known for his faith in God and his military victories.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, who were responsible for serving in the temple and carrying out religious duties.
Bearers: Persons chosen to carry the Ark on their shoulders using poles, as described in the law given by Moses.
Singers: Persons chosen to sing and play music during religious ceremonies and worship.
Offering: A gift or act that is given to God, often as thanks or to seek his favor.
Feast: A celebration or gathering where people come together to honor God and rejoice in his blessings.
Holy: Something that is set apart from the ordinary and dedicated to God, and thus has a special meaning and value.

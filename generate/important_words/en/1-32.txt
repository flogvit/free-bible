Jacob: One of the patriarchs in the Bible, son of Isaac and Rebekah, and father of the twelve tribes of Israel.
Esau: Jacob's older twin brother, whom Jacob deceived for both the birthright and the blessing from their father Isaac.
Angels: Spiritual beings who serve God and carry out his will; in this context, Jacob meets a group of angels.
Camp: The place where Jacob sets up camp after crossing the Jabbok River and sending his servants, family, and possessions across the river.
Jabbok: A tributary of the Jordan River, which Jacob crosses before meeting Esau again after many years.
Blessing: A divine gift or favor that may include protection, prosperity, fertility, or other benefits.
Struggle: Jacob wrestles with a man (assumed to be an angel or God himself) through the night and is blessed by him.
Israel: The name given to Jacob after his struggle, meaning "God struggles" or "he who struggles with God".

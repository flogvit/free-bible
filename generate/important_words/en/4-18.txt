Aaronites: One of the families of the tribe of Levi, descendants of Aaron, who had priestly duties in the service of the Israelites.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, one of the twelve tribes of Israel, who were responsible for carrying out religious and cultic tasks in the tabernacle and later in the temple.
Tithe: A tenth part of the harvest or income given to priests and Levites as a form of tax and support for their service.
Offering: A gift or act given to God as a way to honor, thank, or seek forgiveness from Him. It could consist of food, animals, incense, or other items.
High Priest: The supreme leader of the priesthood in Israel, responsible for performing the most important ceremonies and representing the people before God.
Firstfruits: The first portion of a harvest offered to God as thanks for His blessings and protection.
Sin Offering: An offering given to atone for sins and impurities, typically in the form of an animal that was slaughtered and burned on the altar.

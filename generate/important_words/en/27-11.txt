Prophecy: Predictions about future events, often given by God through a prophet.
Persian Empire: An ancient empire that dominated large parts of the Middle East, including Iran and parts of present-day Iraq, Turkey, Afghanistan, and Pakistan.
Greco-Macedonian Empire: The empire founded by Alexander the Great, which stretched from Greece to Egypt and India.
King: A ruler over a country or territory, often inheriting the position and holding absolute power.
Northern Kingdom: A reference to the Seleucid Empire, one of the Hellenistic states after the death of Alexander the Great, controlling areas in Anatolia, Persia, Mesopotamia, and the Levant.
Southern Kingdom: A reference to the Ptolemaic dynasty, which ruled Egypt and parts of the Middle East after the death of Alexander the Great.
End Times: The final period in human history before God's final judgment and the establishment of a new heavenly kingdom.
Antichrist: An evil and powerful person who will oppose God and His people in the end times.
People of Israel: A term for the descendants of the patriarch Jacob, also known as Israel. In this context, it refers to the Jews and their nation.
Sanctuary: The Temple in Jerusalem, which was the center of Jewish religious practice and a symbol of God's presence among His people.

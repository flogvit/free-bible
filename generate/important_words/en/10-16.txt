Absalom: Son of King David and Maaka, who rebels against his father and seizes the throne in Jerusalem.
David: The second king of Israel, known for his skill as a warrior, musician, and poet. He is the author of many psalms.
Sheba: A Benjamite who led a rebellion against King David after Absalom's death.
Shimei: A member of Saul's family who cursed and threw stones at David while he fled from Absalom.
Hushai: David's friend and advisor, sent back to Jerusalem to spy on Absalom and give him bad advice.
Ahithophel: Former advisor to David who betrays him and supports Absalom in his rebellion.
Ziba: Servant of Mephibosheth, Saul's grandson, who provides David with food and drink during his flight from Jerusalem.
Mephibosheth: Grandson of King Saul and son of Jonathan, accused by Ziba of betraying David.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.

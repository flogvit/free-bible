Prayer: A communication with God, often in the form of a request for help, guidance, or gratitude.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or threaten an individual, often used to describe those who are against God's will.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with moral principles and God's will, often associated with just judgment and reward.
Protection: The act of guarding or securing someone from harm or danger, often asked of God.
Heart: Symbolizes one's inner feelings, thoughts, and intentions; in a religious context, it can also refer to one's soul or spirit.
Refuge: A safe place or shelter, often used metaphorically to describe God's protection and care.
Test: To examine or evaluate something, in this context used to describe God's testing of people's faith and character.

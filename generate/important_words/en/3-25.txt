Jubilee Year: A special year celebrated every 50th year, when debts were forgiven, slaves set free, and land returned to its original owners.
Sabbatical Year for the Land: A period of one year during which the land was to rest and not be cultivated, occurring every seventh year.
Redeem: To buy back something or someone, such as property or a slave, often by paying a sum of money.
Kinsman: A person who is related to another person, either by blood or through marriage.
Freeman: A person who was formerly a slave but has now been set free and restored their rights.
Lease: An agreement in which one person is granted the use of property or services from another person in exchange for payment.
Tenancy: A contract between two parties where one party grants the other the right to use their property, usually agricultural land, in return for payment.

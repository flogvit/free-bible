Absalom: Son of King David and Maaka, who rebels against his father to seize the throne of Israel.
David: The second king of Israel, known for his wisdom and military skill, as well as being the author of several psalms.
Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel and a holy city for the Jews, where David's palace and the temple are located.
Hushai: A friend and advisor to King David, who is sent back to Jerusalem to spy on Absalom and give him bad advice.
Ahithophel: A former advisor to King David, who switches sides and supports Absalom's rebellion.
Daughter of Zion: A poetic name for Jerusalem, often used in the Bible to describe the city's beauty and significance.
The Lord: Another term for God, frequently used in the Old Testament to refer to the Almighty Creator.

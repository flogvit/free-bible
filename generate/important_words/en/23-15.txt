Moab: An ancient nation and region east of the Dead Sea, often in conflict with God's people Israel.
Prophecy: A prediction or message from God through a prophet concerning future events or divine guidance.
Isaiah: One of the major prophets in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel.
Sorrow: The feeling of sadness, pain, or loss, often connected with personal or national tragedies.
Lament: An expression of grief, pain, or regret over an event or situation.

Kir-Hareseth: An important city in Moab, also known as Kir-Moab, which was attacked and destroyed.

Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting rightly and justly in accordance with ethical norms and God's will.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience effectively and reasonably, especially in practical situations and when making decisions.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks God's law and morality, leading to separation from God and potential negative consequences.
Punishment: The consequence of breaking God's laws and morality, often referred to as God's righteous judgment on sinners.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy that he gives to people, even though they do not deserve it. Grace includes forgiveness and salvation from sin and punishment.

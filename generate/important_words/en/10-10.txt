David: The king of Israel, who succeeded King Saul and united the twelve tribes.
Ammonites: A people group who lived east of the Jordan River, often in conflict with the people of Israel.
Hanun: King of the Ammonites, son of Nahash, who insulted David's envoys and started a war.
Joab: David's military commander and nephew, who led Israel's army in the war against the Ammonites and their allies.
Arameans: A people group who lived north of Israel, who allied with the Ammonites in the war against David.
The Battle of Helam: A decisive battle in the war between Israel and the Ammonites, where Joab and his army defeated the Arameans and the Ammonites.
Peace: The state achieved when hostilities and conflicts between parties cease, which was the goal of David's actions after the war.

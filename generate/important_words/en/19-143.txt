Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence toward God, acknowledging his authority and power.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or threaten the believer, either spiritually or physically.
Spirit: The immaterial part of a person, often connected with the soul and the principle of life.
Prayer: Communication with God, in which one asks for help, guidance, or gives thanks for blessings.
Righteousness: The quality of being morally right and just in action and character.
Faithfulness: Being loyal and reliable, especially in relation to God and his promises.
Guidance: Direction and leadership from God that helps the believer navigate life's challenges.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love toward people, forgiveness of sin, and salvation.
Landscape: A metaphor for life's journey and the various situations one may encounter.

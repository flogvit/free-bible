Preacher: Author of the book of Ecclesiastes, believed to be King Solomon, who shares his wisdom and reflections on the meaning of life.
Vanity: A central theme in the book of Ecclesiastes, used to describe the futility and transience of human endeavors and worldly pleasures.
Time: Another important theme in the book, emphasizing that there is a time for everything under heaven and that God has appointed a time for every purpose.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and apply knowledge in a way that leads to good choices and actions. The Preacher values wisdom but also recognizes its limitations.
Work: Human effort and activity aimed at achieving a goal. The Preacher reflects on the meaning of work and its outcomes, both positive and negative.
Pleasure: Enjoyment and joy that people seek in life. The Preacher explores the value of pleasure and its place in the search for life's meaning.
Fear of God: Respect and awe toward God, regarded as the foundation of a meaningful life and wisdom. The Preacher recommends fearing God and keeping His commandments.
Judgment: God's just evaluation and assessment of human actions. The Preacher reminds us that God will judge everything, both good and evil.

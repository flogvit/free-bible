Jacob: Younger son of Isaac and Rebekah, brother to Esau, who becomes one of the patriarchs in the Hebrew tradition.
Esau: Jacob's older twin brother, son of Isaac and Rebekah, and father of the Edomites.
Meeting: The event where Jacob and Esau are reunited after many years of strife and conflict.
Reconciliation: The process by which two parties resolve their conflicts and restore a good relationship; here it refers to the reconciliation between Jacob and Esau.
Gifts: Objects or actions given to express gratitude, friendship, or love; in this case, Jacob gives gifts to Esau as part of the reconciliation process.
Sukkot: A place mentioned in Genesis 33, where Jacob builds a house and makes shelters for his livestock after meeting Esau.
Shechem: An ancient city in Canaan (present-day Israel) where Jacob settles after meeting Esau and buys land from the local inhabitants.

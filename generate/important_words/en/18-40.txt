Behemoth: A large and mighty creature, possibly a mythological being, which God uses to illustrate His power and greatness.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence toward God and His authority.
Mock: Another word for laughing at or ridiculing, used in Job 40 to describe how God challenges Job and his complaints.
Justify: To declare or prove oneself righteous, upright, or morally correct.

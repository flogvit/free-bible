Abner: A powerful military commander who served King Saul and later his son Ish-Bosheth.
Ish-Bosheth: King Saul's son who was appointed king over Israel after his father's death, but held a weak position.
David: The young shepherd who was anointed by the prophet Samuel and later became king of Israel.
Hebron: A city in Canaan where David was anointed king over Judah and established his first kingdom.
Joab: David's nephew and military commander, who killed Abner in revenge for his brother's death and strengthened David's position.
Asael: Joab's brother who was killed by Abner during a clash between their respective armies.
Alliance: An agreement or covenant between two parties, often between nations or groups, to achieve common goals or security.
Wives: In this context, it refers to David's wives, of whom he had several according to the customs of that time.

Mercy: God's grace and care, expressed through his forgiveness and love for humanity.
Refuge: A safe place or protection, often used metaphorically to describe God's protection and support.
Shadow: An image representing protection and closeness; in this context, it refers to God's presence and care.
Heaven: The spiritual realm where God dwells, as well as the physical sky above the earth.
Glory: God's greatness, honor, and power, shown through his creation and actions.
Praise: A form of musical prayer or poetry expressing gratitude, worship, and reverence to God.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or threaten believers, often used metaphorically for evil and sin.
Righteousness: God's moral perfection and just standard, which he calls his followers to imitate.

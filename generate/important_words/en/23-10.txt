Assyrians: An ancient people and kingdom in the Middle East that served as God's instrument to punish Israel.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, the almighty Creator and Sustainer of the universe.
Punishment: Consequences or suffering resulting from a person's actions, often given by God to correct behavior.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and mercy that he gives to people, despite their sins and failures.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem, often used as a symbol of God's dwelling place and the location of his chosen people.
House of Israel: The term for the ancient Israelite community consisting of twelve tribes, often referred to as God's chosen people.
Restoration: The process of restoring something to its former state; here it refers to Israel's restoration after punishment and exile.

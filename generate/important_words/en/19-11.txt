Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with moral principles and God's will, thereby gaining God's approval.
Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Temple: A holy place where people worship and honor God, often associated with prayer and sacrificial offerings.
Test: A situation or event that challenges a person's faith, character, or endurance, and which can lead to spiritual growth.
Heart: In biblical context, it often refers to a person's inner being, including thoughts, feelings, will, and morality.
Violent man: A person who commits violence and evil against others, standing in contrast to righteous people.
Eyes: Symbolize God's ability to see everything, showing that nothing is hidden from his attention and judgment.
Foundation: The basis of something, both concrete and abstract. In Psalm 11 it refers to God's righteous foundation for the world.
Sinner: A person who breaks God's commands and lives in opposition to his will, and therefore needs forgiveness and repentance.

Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to grasp or interpret the meaning of something, especially abstract or complex ideas.
Knowledge: Information, facts, and skills acquired through experience, education, and observation.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with morality, ethics, and the law; being upright and honest.
Discipline: Self-control and the ability to follow rules, guidelines, and principles to achieve goals.
Listen: To be attentive and receive information, advice, or instructions from others.
Guidance: Advice or instructions given to help someone make a decision or solve a problem.
Protection: The action or process of keeping someone or something safe from harm, loss, or danger.
Unrighteous: People who act in violation of morality, ethics, and the law; unjust and dishonest.
Path: A metaphor for life's journey, where choices and actions lead to different outcomes and consequences.
Heart: In this context, the center of thoughts, feelings, and will; a person's inner being.
Fountain of Life: A metaphor for wisdom and understanding that nourishes and strengthens the soul.

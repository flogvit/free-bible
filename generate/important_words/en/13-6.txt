Levi: One of Jacob's twelve sons and the ancestor of the Levites, who had special religious duties in Israel.
Aaron's sons: The descendants of Aaron, Moses' brother, who formed the priesthood. They were responsible for performing ritual actions in the temple.
Temple: A holy place where the Israelites worshiped God and carried out sacrificial offerings.
Singers: Levites who were responsible for singing and playing music during worship services in the temple.
God's service: Religious duties and tasks performed by the priests and Levites in the temple.
The cities: Places where the Levites and priests lived, including the six cities of refuge that protected those who killed someone accidentally.
Land areas: Territories given to the tribes of Israel as their inheritance and property.

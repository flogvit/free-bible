Bride: The woman described in Song of Solomon, loved by the man and often compared to beautiful elements from nature.
Groom: The man described in Song of Solomon, who loves the woman and is often referred to as king or shepherd.
Love: A deep feeling of devotion, passion, and care between two people, here used to describe the relationship between the bride and groom.
Garden: A symbol of beauty and fruitfulness, often used in Song of Solomon to describe the bride and her body.
Pomegranate (Granateple): A fruit that symbolizes fruitfulness and love, mentioned several times in Song of Solomon.
Sister: An affectionate and respectful expression used by the groom to refer to the bride, indicating their close bond and relationship.
Solomon: The king of Israel who has traditionally been credited with authorship of Song of Solomon, although it is uncertain whether he actually wrote it.

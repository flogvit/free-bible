Nehemiah: The main character in the book, a Jewish leader who helps his people rebuild the walls of Jerusalem.
Jerusalem: The holy city for Jews, Christians, and Muslims; an important place in the book where Nehemiah works to rebuild the walls.
Walls: Physical barriers around Jerusalem that serve as protection against enemies; Nehemiah works to repair these after their destruction.
Artaxerxes: A Persian king who gives Nehemiah permission to go to Jerusalem to help his people with the rebuilding.
Prayer: Communication with God, often asking for help or guidance; Nehemiah prays to God throughout the book.
God's people: The Jews, chosen by God and having a special covenant with him; Nehemiah helps them rebuild their community and faith.
Enemies: Groups or individuals who oppose Nehemiah and the Jews in their efforts to rebuild Jerusalem.
Tobiah: One of Nehemiah's and the Jews' enemies, who tries to stop the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls.
Sanballat: Another enemy of Nehemiah and the Jews, who also tries to prevent the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls.

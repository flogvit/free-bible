Redeemer: An ancient custom where a relative redeems property, pays off a debt, or marries the widow of a deceased family member to secure the family's inheritance and name.
Boaz: A wealthy man from Bethlehem who becomes Ruth's husband and redeems her in-laws from poverty.
Ruth: A Moabite woman who converts to Judaism and becomes the great-grandmother of King David.
Naomi: Ruth's mother-in-law who returns to Bethlehem with Ruth after both have lost their husbands.
Kinsman-Redeemer: A close relative who has the right and duty to redeem property and marry the widow of a deceased family member to secure the family's inheritance and name.
Family Lineage: A series of relationships showing how people are connected through generations.
Clan: A group of people related by common ancestors, often linked to a specific family, clan, or tribe.

Jacob: Youngest son of Isaac and Rebekah, brother to Esau, and ancestor of the twelve tribes of Israel.
Leah: Jacob's first wife, older sister to Rachel, and daughter of Laban. She bears six of Jacob's sons and one daughter.
Rachel: Jacob's favored wife, younger sister to Leah, and daughter of Laban. She is the mother of Joseph and Benjamin.
Laban: Father of Leah and Rachel, and uncle to Jacob. He makes agreements with Jacob regarding work and marriage.
Mandrakes: A plant associated in the Bible with fertility. Leah receives mandrakes from her son Reuben and trades them for a night with Jacob.
Barrenness: Involuntary inability to have children. Rachel struggles with barrenness before finally becoming the mother of Joseph and Benjamin.
Concubines: Bilhah and Zilpah, the servant women of Rachel and Leah, who also bear children by Jacob to increase the size of their respective families.
Worship: Faith in and devotion to God. Jacob and his family practice worship and acknowledge God's influence in their lives.
Covenant: Agreement between two parties. Jacob and Laban make a covenant concerning wages and possessions after Jacob has worked for him for several years.

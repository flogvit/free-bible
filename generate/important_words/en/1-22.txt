Abraham: Biblical patriarch and ancestor of the people of Israel, whom God chose to make a covenant with.
Isaac: Abraham's son, born to his wife Sarah. Isaac is a central figure in Genesis and is regarded as one of the patriarchs in Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.
Sacrifice: A holy act in which something is given to God, often as a symbolic gesture to show gratitude, devotion, or obedience.
Moriah: A mountain region mentioned in Genesis 22, where Abraham was asked to sacrifice his son Isaac as a sign of his faith and obedience to God.
Angel: A supernatural being who serves as God's messenger and servant. In this story, an angel stops Abraham from sacrificing Isaac and shows him an alternative offering.
Ram: A male sheep found by Abraham and sacrificed in place of Isaac after the angel intervened.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, often connected with promises and conditions. In this case, God confirms his covenant with Abraham and promises to bless him and his descendants.

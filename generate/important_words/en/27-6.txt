Daniel: A prophet in Babylon and one of the Jewish youths taken captive by Nebuchadnezzar. He is known for his wisdom and faithfulness to God.
Lions' Den: A place where lions were kept, into which Daniel was thrown as punishment for praying to God instead of King Darius.
King Darius: Ruler over the Persian Empire that conquered Babylon. He respected Daniel but was tricked into signing a law that led to Daniel's punishment.
Prayer: Communication between people and God, which Daniel faithfully practiced three times a day, even when it was forbidden.
Angel: Supernatural beings who serve as God's messengers and servants. An angel protected Daniel in the lions' den.
God's Protection: Daniel's unwavering faith in God led Him to protect him from the lions in the lions' den.
Conspiracy: A plot by some of Darius' officials who wanted Daniel removed from his position by manipulating the king into issuing a law against prayer.

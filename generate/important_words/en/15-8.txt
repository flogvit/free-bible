Ezra: A priest and scribe who led the Jews' return from exile in Babylon to Jerusalem to restore their cultural and religious identity.
Book 8: The eighth book of Ezra's book, which describes the journey back to Jerusalem and the preparations for rebuilding the temple.
God's hand: An expression symbolizing God's protection, guidance, and strength throughout the entire process of rebuilding.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, who had special religious duties and roles in Jewish society, including service in the temple.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy toward humanity, given without regard to merit or good works.
Temple: The holy place where Jews worshiped God and performed religious rituals, which was destroyed by the Babylonians and later rebuilt.
Sacrifice: Religious acts in which gifts, usually food or animals, are offered to God as a way of showing gratitude, devotion, and submission.

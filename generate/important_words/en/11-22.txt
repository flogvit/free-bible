Ahab: The king of Israel who reigned in Samaria, married to Jezebel and known for his wickedness and idolatry.
Jehoshaphat: The king of Judah, known for his righteousness and seeking God's guidance.
Micaiah: A prophet of God who spoke the truth and warned Ahab about his impending death.
Ramoth-Gilead: A city east of the Jordan River that was a point of conflict between Israel and Aram.
The king of Aram: The ruler over the Aramean kingdom, an enemy of Israel and allied with Ahab in the war against Ramoth-Gilead.
Prophets: Religious leaders who claim to receive divine insight and convey God's message to the people.
A lying spirit: A spirit that God permits to deceive the prophets, so that Ahab is led to his own destruction.
The battle: The final confrontation between the armies of Israel and Aram at Ramoth-Gilead, where Ahab was killed.

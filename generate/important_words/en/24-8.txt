Sorrow: A feeling of sadness, pain, or loss experienced by people and expressed in the book of Jeremiah 8.
Punishment: God's response to human disobedience and sin, leading to suffering and difficulties for them.
Sin: Actions or thoughts that break God's laws and principles, creating a separation between people and God.
Repentance: A feeling of deep regret and sorrow over past sins, along with a desire to change and do what is right.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a way that aligns with God's will and purpose.
False prophets: People who claim to speak on behalf of God but actually spread lies and mislead the people.
Harvest: A metaphor for final judgment and the consequences of human actions, both good and bad.

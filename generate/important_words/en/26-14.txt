Prophecy: Prediction or revelation of future events, often given by God through a prophet.
Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Idol: An image or object that people worship as a god instead of the true God.
Sin: Violation of God's law, moral failure, or evil action.
Punishment: Consequences or suffering resulting from sin or injustice.
Repent: To change one's mind, attitude, or behavior, especially toward God and His ways.
Righteous: To be morally upright and act in accordance with God's will.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from punishment and the consequences of their sin.
Judgment: God's evaluation and decision regarding human actions and their eternal destiny.

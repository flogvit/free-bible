Lord: A title for God that refers to his authority and sovereignty over all creation.
Servant: In this context, it refers to the chosen one (the Messiah) whom God sends to carry out his will and save humanity.
Righteousness: The principle of just and moral conduct that God requires from his creatures, which the Messiah is to establish on earth.
Light: A symbol for truth, guidance, and revelation from God. In Isaiah 42, light represents the salvation and hope that the Messiah brings to the world.
Nations: This word refers to all nations and ethnic groups in the world, emphasizing that God's salvation is universal and available to all people.
Blind: A metaphor for spiritual blindness or lack of spiritual insight. Isaiah 42 describes how the Messiah will help the spiritually blind to see and understand God's truth.
Prisoner: A person who is bound or oppressed, either physically or spiritually. The Messiah has the task of freeing the oppressed and bringing liberty.
Destroyer: An enemy or evil power that seeks to harm God's people and oppose his plans. Isaiah 42 promises that God will protect his people from destroyers.

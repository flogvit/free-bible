Lord: A title used to show respect and reverence toward God, who is the supreme authority and source of all power.
Humble: To be modest and not arrogant, acknowledging one's own limitations and dependence on God.
Quiet: To be calm and free from anxiety or disturbance, often associated with inner peace and harmony.
Heart: In this context, it refers to a person's inner being, emotions, thoughts, and will.
Like a weaned child: A metaphor describing a child who has stopped nursing and is calm and content in the mother's arms, symbolizing trust and rest in God's care.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people in the Old Testament, also used as a metaphor for believers who seek God and His promises.
Hope: Expectation and confidence that God will fulfill His promises and give strength, comfort, and salvation.

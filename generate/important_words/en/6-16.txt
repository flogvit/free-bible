Joshua: The main character in the book of Joshua, leader of the people of Israel and Moses' successor.
The Land: The area that God promised to give to the people of Israel as their inheritance and homeland.
Inheritance: A gift or property passed from one generation to another, especially within the tribes of Israel.
Ephraim: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, descendants of Joseph's son Ephraim.
Manasseh: Another of the twelve tribes of Israel, descendants of Joseph's son Manasseh.
Territory: Geographic areas assigned to each of the tribes of Israel as their inheritance and place of settlement.
Borders: Lines that mark the boundary between different territories or regions.
Cities: Populated areas where people live and work together, often protected by defensive walls.
Canaan: A historical region in the Middle East that includes parts of present-day Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and Syria. It was inhabited by the Canaanites before the Israelites conquered it.

Lord's Spirit: The divine power that works in the world, and especially through God's chosen people.
Anointing: An act symbolizing the consecration or sanctification of a person for a special mission or service.
Joy: A deep feeling of happiness and contentment that comes from being in harmony with God and his will.
Comfort: To give support, help, and relief to those who suffer or are in distress.
Sorrow: A feeling of sadness and pain that often arises as a result of loss, suffering, or injustice.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and fairly, in accordance with God's law and morality.
The Nations: The various peoples and cultures of the world, all created by God and loved by him.
Restoration: The process of bringing something back to its original state, especially in relation to God's people and their relationship with him.
Inheritance: What one receives from ancestors or predecessors, especially in relation to faith, culture, and tradition.

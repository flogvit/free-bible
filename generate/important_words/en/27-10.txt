Daniel: The prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and interpreted dreams, the author of the book of Daniel.
Vision: A revelation or divine insight that Daniel received through dreams or visions.
Angel: A supernatural being, messenger and servant of God, who helps and protects people.
Michael: One of the chief angels, an archangel, who fights on God's side and protects Israel.
Persia: An ancient empire in the Middle East that played a significant role during Daniel's time, now modern-day Iran.
Greek: A reference to ancient Greece, which became a major power after the fall of Persia and influenced Daniel's prophecies.
Fast: A period of self-denial and prayer, where Daniel sought divine guidance and revelation.
Conflict: A struggle or battle between forces, both spiritual and earthly, that affects human destiny.

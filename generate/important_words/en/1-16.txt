Hagar: An Egyptian slave woman who was the servant of Sarah, Abraham's wife.
Sarah: Abraham's wife, who could not have children and therefore gave Hagar to Abraham to bear a descendant.
Abraham: The patriarch of the Jewish people, who made a covenant with God and received the promise of numerous descendants.
Pregnant: The condition in which a woman carries an unborn child in her womb.
Contempt: A feeling of strong disapproval or disgust toward someone or something.
Desert: A dry, inhospitable landscape with little vegetation and rainfall, where Hagar fled.
Angel: A divine messenger who conveys God's will and protection.
Ishmael: Son of Abraham and Hagar, and the ancestor of the Ishmaelites, an Arab people.
Bless: To give divine favor, protection, or prosperity to someone or something.
Fertile: The ability to produce many offspring, often used as a metaphor for abundance and growth.

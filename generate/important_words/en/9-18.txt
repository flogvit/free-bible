David: A young Israelite who later becomes king of Israel, known for his bravery and musical abilities.
Jonathan: King Saul's son and David's close friend, known for his loyalty and courage.
Saul: Israel's first king, who gradually becomes jealous of David and attempts to kill him.
Goliath: A Philistine warrior, a giant who is defeated by David in a duel.
Friendship: A strong bond between two people, here between David and Jonathan, who stand together despite Saul's hostility.
Jealousy: A negative feeling that arises when someone feels threatened by another person's success or relationship, here Saul's jealousy toward David.
Covenant: An agreement or promise between two parties, here between David and Jonathan, who swear loyalty and friendship to each other.
Michal: Saul's daughter and David's first wife, who helps David escape from her father.

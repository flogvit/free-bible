Love: A deep devotion and care for God, which requires following his commands and keeping his covenant.
Commandments: Rules and instructions given by God to people, which must be followed to maintain a good relationship with God and others.
Covenant: A holy agreement between God and his people, where God promises blessings and protection in exchange for obedience and faithfulness.
Blessing: A favorable gift or reward from God, often associated with happiness, prosperity, and success.
Punishment: Negative consequences resulting from disobedience to God's commands and breaking his covenant; it can include suffering, defeat, and destruction.
Obedience: To obey and follow God's commands and instructions, which is necessary to maintain a good relationship with God and receive his blessings.
Conquest: Taking control of and settling in the promised land, Canaan, as part of God's promise to the people of Israel.

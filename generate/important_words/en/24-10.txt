God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel.
Idols: False gods or images that people worship instead of the true God.
Trinitarian: Something consisting of three parts or aspects.
Craftsmen: People who work at making or repairing things, especially using their hands and simple tools.
Silver: A chemical element and precious metal often associated with wealth and dignity.
Hammer: A tool used to strike or beat on objects, often to shape them or fasten them together.
Terrified: To be afraid, anxious, or uneasy because of a perceived threat or danger.
Plant: A living organism that produces its own food through photosynthesis and typically grows in the soil.

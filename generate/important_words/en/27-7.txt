Daniel 7 is a chapter in the Old Testament that contains prophetic visions and symbols. Here are some important words and explanations:
Prophecy: Predictions or revelations about future events, often given by God through a prophet.
Vision: A supernatural experience where a person sees images or scenes, often with symbolic meaning.
Beasts: Symbols for various kingdoms and nations described in Daniel's visions. Each beast represents a specific kingdom.
Lion: The first beast in Daniel's vision, symbolizing the Babylonian empire.
Bear: The second beast, representing the Medo-Persian empire.
Leopard: The third beast, standing for the Greek empire under Alexander the Great.
Fourth beast: A terrifying and powerful beast that represents the Roman empire.
Ten horns: Part of the fourth beast, symbolizing ten kings or kingdoms that will arise from the Roman empire.
The little horn: An eleventh horn that appears among the ten, representing an evil power or antichrist.
The Ancient of Days: A title for God, describing his eternal existence and authority.
Son of Man: A messianic figure who comes on the clouds and receives an everlasting kingdom from the Ancient of Days.
Judgment: A time when God will judge the nations and humanity, and establish his eternal kingdom.

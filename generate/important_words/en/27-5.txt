Belteshazzar: The name of the king of Babylon in the book of Daniel, also known as Belshazzar.
Writing: Mysterious signs that suddenly appear on the wall during a feast at Belshazzar's palace, which no one can interpret.
Daniel: The prophet who is the main character in the book and who is called to interpret the writing on the wall.
Mene: The first word of the writing on the wall, meaning that God has numbered and weighed Babylon's king and found him wanting.
Tekel: The second word of the writing on the wall, meaning that the king has been weighed and found wanting.
Peres: The third word of the writing on the wall, meaning that the kingdom will be divided and given to the Medes and Persians.
Medes and Persians: The peoples who take over Babylon after the writing on the wall is interpreted and assume power from the Babylonians.

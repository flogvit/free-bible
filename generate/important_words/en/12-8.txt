Elisha: A prophet in Israel who performed miracles and conveyed God's message to the people.
Seven years: The time period of a famine that Elisha predicted and warned about.
The Shunammite woman: A woman who helped Elisha and received a promise that she would have a son.
The king: The ruler over Israel whom Elisha contacted and influenced through his prophecies.
Hazael: An officer in the Aramean army who was anointed by Elisha to become king over Aram.
Ben-Hadad: King of Aram whom Hazael killed to take the throne.
Joram: King of Israel who was wounded in battle and succeeded by his brother Ahaziah.
Ahaziah: King of Judah who ruled together with his mother Athaliah after his father's death.
Athaliah: The queen mother who killed all her grandchildren to keep power, but was eventually killed.
Joash: A grandson of Athaliah who survived and became king of Judah at seven years old.

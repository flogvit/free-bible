Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The capacity to make good decisions and show sound judgment in various situations.
Discipline: Training or correction that helps a person develop moral and ethical character.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and uprightly, both toward other people and God.
The Lord: Another designation for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Humility: The quality of being modest and not considering oneself better than others.
Fear: A reverent respect and submission toward God, leading to obedience and trust.
Foolishness: Lack of reason, wisdom, and understanding, often resulting in poor choices and consequences.
Truth: That which is true, correct, and in agreement with reality; the opposite of lies and deceit.
The Woman: In the book of Proverbs, the woman is often used as a symbol of wisdom and virtue.

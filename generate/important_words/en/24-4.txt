Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who conveyed God's message to the people of Israel.
Day of Judgment: A symbolic day representing God's final punishment and destruction of the wicked and unjust.
Repentance: The feeling of regret for past actions and a desire to make things right, often connected with sin.
Conversion: The process by which a person acknowledges their sins, repents, and seeks forgiveness from God.
Jerusalem: The capital city of ancient Israel and a central place for Jewish faith and culture.
Invasion: When a foreign power takes control over another country or territory, often through military force.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, known for its conquest of Jerusalem and the exile of the Jews.
The Lord's Voice: A metaphor for God's communication with people, often through prophets like Jeremiah.
Destruction: The result of God's judgment, where evil and injustice are removed and society is cleansed.

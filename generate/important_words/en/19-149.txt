Praise Song: A song or hymn that praises and honors God for His greatness and deeds.
Lord: Another name for God, the Almighty Creator.
New Song: A fresh and original praise song to God, expressing joy and gratitude.
Assembly: A group of people gathered for religious purposes, especially to worship God.
Israel: God's chosen people in the Old Testament, the ancestors of the Jewish nation.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem, often used as a symbol for the city and God's people.
Shout of Joy: A loud expression of joy, gratitude, and admiration, often directed toward God.
Dance: A physical movement that expresses joy, gratitude, and reverence toward God.
Drum: A musical instrument used to keep rhythm and emphasize the praise song.
Harp: A stringed instrument used to create melodies and accompany the praise song.
Vengeance: God's just punishment of evil and injustice.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and decision regarding human actions and their consequences.
Kings: Rulers over nations, who are subject to God's authority and judgment.
Chains: Symbols of restriction and submission, used to describe God's power over His enemies.
Scripture Passages: Holy texts that make up the Bible and convey God's word and will.

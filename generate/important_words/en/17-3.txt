Esther: The main character in the book, a Jewish woman who becomes Persian queen.
Mordecai: Esther's adoptive father and Jewish leader, helps Esther and warns the king of a conspiracy.
Haman: The king's highest official, plans to destroy the Jews, but is eventually hanged himself.
King Ahasuerus: Persian king who marries Esther and later saves the Jews because of her intervention.
Fasting: A period of abstaining from food and drink, which Esther asks Mordecai and the Jews to do to seek God's help.
The gallows tree: A tall tree where Haman had planned to hang Mordecai, but ends up being hanged there himself.
Purim: Jewish festival that celebrates the deliverance of the Jews from Haman's evil plans.

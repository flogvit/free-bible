David: King of Israel and a central figure in 1 Chronicles, chosen by God to be the leader of the Israelites.
Temple: A holy place for worship and offerings, where God dwells among his people. In this context, it refers to the first temple in Jerusalem that was to be built by Solomon.
Solomon: David's son and successor as king over Israel, known for his wisdom and wealth. He received the task of building the temple in Jerusalem.
Lord: Another designation for God, emphasizing his authority and power.
Plans: Detailed instructions and drawings that David gives to Solomon for the construction of the temple, inspired by God.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, often connected with promises and blessings. In this chapter, it is the covenant between God and David, where God promises that David's throne will endure forever through his descendants.
Heart: Symbolizes a person's inner being, including thoughts, feelings, and will. Here it is used to describe David's and Solomon's attitude and commitment in their service to God.
Obey: To follow or obey God's commands and instructions, which is important for maintaining a good relationship with God and receiving his blessings.

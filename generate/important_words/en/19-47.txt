The following are some important words from Psalm 47 and their explanations:
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Peoples: Groups of people that make up different nations, cultures, and ethnicities.
Lord: Another designation for God, often used to show respect and reverence.
Most High: An adjective describing God's sovereign and supreme position over all other powers and beings.
King: A leader or ruler who has authority and power over a territory or people.
Praise song: A song or poem that expresses praise, gratitude, and honor to God.
Nations: Political entities or states consisting of a specific territory and population.
Victory: Success or triumph in a conflict, battle, or challenge.
Those under the earth: A reference to the dead or the spirit world, which lies beneath the surface of the earth.

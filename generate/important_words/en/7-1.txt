Canaan: The area in the Middle East that the Israelites were to conquer and settle in, according to God's promise to Abraham.
Israelites: A people descended from the patriarch Israel (Jacob), who make up the twelve tribes of the nation of Israel.
Judge: A leader chosen by God to rule over and protect the Israelites against their enemies, and to bring justice and order.
Jerusalem: A city in Canaan that was conquered by the Israelites and later became the capital of the kingdoms of Israel and Judah.
Judah: One of the twelve tribes of Israel; also the name of the southern kingdom after the nation was divided.
Enemies: Various peoples and nations who fought against the Israelites and threatened their existence and security.
Alliance: A covenant or agreement between two or more parties, often formed to cooperate and strengthen one another.
Conquer: To take control of an area or city by means of military force, as the Israelites did in Canaan.
Faithfulness: Loyalty and trust in God and his promises, along with obedience to his commands and instructions.

Wisdom: The ability to think and act wisely, based on knowledge, experience, and understanding.
Understanding: The ability to judge and make good decisions using insight and wisdom.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and morally, both toward oneself and others.
Wealth: An abundance of material goods and resources, often associated with financial prosperity.
Poverty: A lack of material goods and resources, which can lead to difficult living conditions.
Humility: To be modest and not boast about one's own success or worth, but to acknowledge others and God's contribution.
Work: The effort and activities a person carries out to achieve a goal, often linked to occupation and income.
Slothfulness: A lack of initiative and willingness to work, which can lead to poverty and suffering.
Discipline: Correction and training used to guide and shape a person's character and behavior.
Friendship: A close relationship between two or more people, characterized by mutual support, trust, and care.
Enmity: A conflict or opposition between two or more parties, which can lead to strife and unrest.

Nazirite Vow: A vow that a person makes to God to dedicate themselves to Him for a specific period. It involves special rules and restrictions.
Abstinence: Avoiding certain things, such as alcohol, as part of a religious vow or principle.
Sin Offering: An offering sacrificed in ancient Israel to obtain cleansing from sin and impurity.
Burnt Offering: A type of sacrifice in ancient Israel where animals were burned on the altar as a holy act to God.
Peace Offering: An offering made to express gratitude to God and to celebrate peace and harmony within the community.
The Lord's Blessing: A prayer asking that God bless and protect His people. This blessing is also called the "Aaronic Blessing" and is a central part of Jewish and Christian liturgy.

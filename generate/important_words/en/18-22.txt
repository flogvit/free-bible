Eliphaz: One of Job's three friends who criticized and accused him of having sinned and thus caused his own suffering.
Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting in accordance with divine laws and ethical norms.
Sin: An action or omission that breaks God's will and moral laws, leading to spiritual separation from God.
Condemnation: Criticism or rejection of a person based on their perceived faults or sins.
Blessing: A feeling of well-being and contentment that comes from living a good life in accordance with God's will and moral principles.
Prosperity: Money or material goods earned or received as a reward for work, investment, or other activities.
Restoration: The process of returning to a previous state of well-being, health, or prosperity after a period of suffering or loss.
Prayer: A form of communication with God in which one expresses gratitude, asks for help, or seeks guidance.

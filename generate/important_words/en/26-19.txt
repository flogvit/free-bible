Lioness: A metaphor for the nation of Israel, representing strength and power.
Cubs: Refers to the leaders and kings of Israel, who were the offspring of the lioness.
Eagle: A symbol of Nebuchadnezzar II, king of Babylon, who invaded and destroyed Jerusalem.
Branch: Represents Zedekiah, the last king of Judah, who was taken captive by the Babylonians.
Vines: Symbolize the people of Israel and their community, suffering under oppression and exile.
Drought: Signifies spiritual dryness and turning away from God, leading to Israel's weakening and vulnerability.
Cry: Expresses the people's lament and plea for help in their distress and suffering.
The Lord: A reference to God, the Almighty Creator who controls human destiny.

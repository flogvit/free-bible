Law: God's instructions and commandments given to the people of Israel to guide their lives and society.
Covenant: An agreement between God and humans, often with promises and conditions from both parties.
Moses: The prophet and leader who led the people of Israel out of Egypt and received the law from God on Mount Sinai.
Forbidden images: Idols or images made to represent deities, which are forbidden according to God's law.
Inheritance: The land and blessings that God promised to give the people of Israel as part of the covenant.

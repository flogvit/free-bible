Tabernacle: A holy tent where God dwells among his people, a place for worship and offerings.
Ark: A chest made of acacia wood and overlaid with gold, containing the two stone tablets with the Ten Commandments.
Mercy Seat: The cover of the ark, made of pure gold and surrounded by two cherubim, where God would meet Moses.
Cherubim: Heavenly beings, often depicted with wings, who protect and serve God.
Lampstand: A gilded lamp holder with seven lamps, symbolizing God's light in the world.
Bread: The twelve loaves placed before the tabernacle as a continual offering to God.
Acacia Wood: A durable and resistant type of wood, often used in building the tabernacle and its furnishings.
Gold: A precious metal that symbolizes purity and divinity, widely used in the tabernacle and its furnishings.

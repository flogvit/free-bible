Stones: Large, flat stones that were used to build an altar and memorials according to God's instructions.
Altar: A sacred structure where offerings and prayers were given to God as part of the ancient Israelite cult system.
The Law: God's instructions and commandments given to Moses and the people of Israel to guide their lives and society.
Blessing: A positive statement or action from God that brings prosperity, happiness, and protection to the recipient.
Curse: A negative statement or action from God that brings misfortune, suffering, and punishment to the recipient.
Tribes: The twelve groups of the people of Israel descended from Jacob's twelve sons.
Gershonites: A subgroup of the Levites, one of the twelve tribes of Israel, who had specific duties in the tabernacle and later in the temple.

Moab: An ancient nation located east of the Dead Sea, known for its resistance against the kingdom of Israel.
Mesha: King of Moab mentioned in 2 Kings 3, who led a revolt against Israel after Ahab's death.
Jehoram: Son of Ahab and Jezebel, became king over Israel and sought an alliance with Judah and Edom to fight against Moab.
Jehoshaphat: King of Judah, agreed to help Jehoram in the war against Moab.
Edom: An ancient nation southeast of the Dead Sea, allied with Israel and Judah in the war against Moab.
Elisha: The prophet who succeeded Elijah, gave counsel and divine guidance to the kings of Israel, including Jehoram.
Water: A central element in the story; Elisha prophesied that the valley would fill with water as a sign of God's help.
Sacrifice: An act where something is given to a deity, often as a prayer for help or thanks for help received.

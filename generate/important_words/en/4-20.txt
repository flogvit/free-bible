Miriam: Moses and Aaron's sister, a prophetess and leader of the people of Israel.
Death: The end of life, when the body stops functioning and the soul leaves it.
Kadesh: A city in the wilderness where the Israelites camped during their journey toward Canaan.
Water: A basic resource and necessity for life, often scarce in desert areas.
The Rock: A large stone or rock formation that God commanded Moses to strike to bring forth water.
Staff: A long, straight rod that Moses used to perform miracles and demonstrate God's power.
Book of Numbers: The fourth book of the Old Testament, which tells about the laws given to Moses and the Israelites.
Aaron: Moses' older brother and Israel's first high priest, responsible for the priesthood and the sanctuary.
Edom: A kingdom southeast of the Dead Sea, inhabited by descendants of Esau, Jacob's brother.

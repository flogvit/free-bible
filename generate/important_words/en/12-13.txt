Joash: A king of Israel who ruled for 16 years and fought against the Arameans to restore cities in Israel.
Elisha: A prophet in Israel, successor to the prophet Elijah, who performed miracles and gave counsel to the kings of Israel.
Jehoahaz: Father of Joash and king of Israel before him, who did evil in the Lord's sight and suffered under Aramean oppression.
Hazael: King of Aram, who oppressed Israel and fought against Joash and Jehoahaz.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.
Bow: A weapon used in war, symbolizing here Israel's power and ability to fight enemies.
Arrows: Part of the bow, representing the individual battles and victories that Israel could achieve with God's help.
Sin: Broke God's commands and lived in a way that was not in accordance with His will.
Arameans: People from Aram, an ancient kingdom in the Middle East that was often in conflict with Israel.

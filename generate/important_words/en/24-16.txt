Prophecy: A prediction or revelation from God about future events, often communicated through a prophet.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel and warned them of the consequences of their sins.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks with God's will and moral law.
Punishment: Consequences or suffering resulting from sin, often imposed by God to correct injustice and encourage repentance.
Repentance: A change of heart and mind that leads a person to turn away from sin and seek forgiveness and reconciliation with God.
Land: In this context, it refers to the nation of Israel and its territory.
Captivity: The state of being taken captive and held under compulsion, often as punishment for the people of Israel for their disobedience to God.
The Lord's hand: An expression symbolizing God's power and authority over human destiny and history.
Curse: A negative force or effect imposed on someone or something by God because of sin and disobedience.
Blessing: A positive force or effect given by God to those who are obedient and faithful to His will and commands.

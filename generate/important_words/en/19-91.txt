Here are some important words from Psalm 91 and their explanations in Norwegian Bokmål:
Lord: A title often used for God in the Bible, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Refuge: A place or person one seeks protection and safety with. In Psalm 91 it refers to seeking shelter in God.
Fear: A feeling of awe and reverence toward God, as well as a healthy fear of his power and justice.
Wings: An image used to describe God's protection and care, like a bird protecting its young under its wings.
Shield: A symbol for protection and defense. In Psalm 91 it means that God is our protector against dangers and threats.
Trap: A situation or thing that can lead to harm or destruction. In Psalm 91 it speaks of being rescued from the traps life may bring.
Lion: A powerful and dangerous animal that symbolizes threats and dangers. In Psalm 91 it means that God will protect us from such dangers.
Plague: A disease that spreads quickly and causes death and suffering. In Psalm 91 it points to God's protection against sickness and suffering.
Comfort: To give support, encouragement, and relief to someone who is suffering or worried. In Psalm 91 it means that God will comfort and strengthen us in difficult times.

Circumcision: A surgical procedure in which the foreskin of the penis is removed, usually as part of religious rituals in Judaism.
Reproduction: The process by which living organisms produce offspring, often linked to sexual reproduction.
Uncleanness: A state of spiritual or ritual impurity according to religious laws, which may require cleansing before one can take part in certain religious acts.
Foreigner: A person who is not part of the Israelite community and comes from another country or culture.
Prostitution house: A place where prostitution is practiced, considered unacceptable under the religious laws in Deuteronomy.
Tithe: A tax or offering, usually 10% of crops or income, given to the temple or priesthood to support religious activities and help those in need.
Vow: A sacred commitment or oath, often made to God, that binds a person to fulfill a promise or action.

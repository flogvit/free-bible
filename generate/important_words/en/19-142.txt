Lament: A form of expression in which one voices distress, sorrow, or suffering.  
Lord: A title used to refer to God as the supreme authority and protector.  
Ear: Symbolizes God’s attentiveness and ability to hear prayers and laments.  
Cry: A strong expression of distress, often used to ask for help or intervention from God.  
Refuge: A place or person that offers protection, safety, and support in difficult times.  
Prisoner: A person who is captured, confined, or oppressed, either physically or spiritually.  
Freedom: The absence of restrictions, oppression, or control; the ability to act, think, and choose freely.

Psalm 4 is a short psalm with few key words, but here are some important words and their explanations:
Lord: A title used to refer to God, showing respect and reverence.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with moral principles and divine law.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and mercy toward people.
Hear: To listen or pay attention to someone; in this context, it means asking God to listen to prayers.
Security: A feeling of safety and protection, often linked to God's care and presence.

Nebuchadnezzar: The king of Babylon who conquered Jerusalem and took many Jews into captivity in Babylon.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who received God's word and delivered it to the people of Israel.
Egypt: A country in North Africa where some Israelites sought refuge after the fall of Jerusalem.
Baruch: Jeremiah's scribe and assistant, who wrote down the prophecies that Jeremiah received from God.
Tahpanhes: An Egyptian city where the Jews settled after fleeing from Babylon.
The word of the Lord: A term for God's message or instructions given through the prophet Jeremiah.
Punishment: The consequences of not following God's will, often in the form of suffering or destruction.

Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Book 6: A chapter in the book of Ezekiel containing a prophecy about judgment on the mountains of Israel.
Mountains: In this context, symbolize places of idol worship and sin among the people of Israel.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment for sin and disobedience to His commands.
Idols: False gods that the people of Israel worshipped instead of the one true God.
Sword: A symbol of war, destruction, and God's punishing hand.
Scattered: The act of scattering the people of Israel among the nations as a result of their disobedience.
Repentance: Turning away from sin and seeking forgiveness from God.

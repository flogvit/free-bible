Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with moral principles and maintains a good character.
Trials: Difficult situations or sufferings that some experience, often to test their faith or character.
Lament: To express dissatisfaction, pain, or suffering, usually toward God, especially in the context of trials.
Comfort: Support, encouragement, or help given to someone who is suffering or going through difficulties.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a sensible way to make good decisions and act rightly.
God's ways: The manner in which God governs the world and human lives, including his plans and purposes.

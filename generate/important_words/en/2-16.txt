Manna: A miraculous bread-like substance that God sent from heaven to feed the Israelites in the wilderness.
Evening: The time of day when the sun sets and it becomes dark; often used to mark the end of a day.
Morning: The time of day when the sun rises and it becomes light; often used to mark the beginning of a day.
Sabbath: A weekly day of rest, usually the seventh day of the week, which God commanded the Israelites to observe and keep holy.
Israelites: A people descended from the twelve sons of Israel; they were God's chosen people and were led out of Egypt under Moses' leadership.
Wilderness: A dry and inhospitable landscape with little water and vegetation; the Israelites wandered through the wilderness after fleeing Egypt.
Omer: A unit of measurement for volume, about 3.5 liters; used to measure the manna that the Israelites gathered.
Worship service: A gathering of people to worship and honor God, often including prayer, singing, and teaching.

Psalm: A poetic religious song or hymn, often used in worship and prayer.
Lord: Another name for God, referring to his sovereignty and power over all creation.
Waited: To have patience and endurance while waiting for something to happen, often with reference to God's timing and promises.
Cry: To express a strong feeling, such as prayer or lament, directed to God for help, guidance, or comfort.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and death through faith in Jesus Christ and his atoning work.
Praise song: A song or hymn that expresses gratitude, reverence, and praise to God for his goodness and grace.
Deeds: Actions or behavior that reflect one's faith and morality, often resulting from God's grace and guidance in one's life.
Established: To be confirmed, strengthened, or founded, often with reference to one's faith and trust in God.

God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Jeremiah: The prophet who delivered God's message to the people of Israel and author of the book of Jeremiah.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia where the Israelites were taken into captivity.
Captivity: The period when the Israelites were held as prisoners in Babylon and their homeland was destroyed.
Plans: God's intentions and purposes for His people, often linked to blessing and prosperity.
Hope: Expectation of a better future and trust in God's promises.
Future: The time that comes after the present, which God controls and knows fully.
Prayer: Communication with God, often expressed through thanksgiving, praise, repentance, or requests for help.
Seek: To make an effort to find or obtain something, such as seeking God and His will.

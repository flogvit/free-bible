Tabernacle: The mobile sanctuary tent that the Israelites built and used during their wilderness journey after leaving Egypt.
Offering: A gift to God, often in the form of animals, food, or other items, as an expression of gratitude, repentance, or worship.
Tent of Meeting: Another name for the tabernacle, where God revealed Himself to Moses and the Israelites.
Nazirite: A person who has taken a special vow of dedication to God, thereby accepting certain restrictions and duties.
Leaders: High-ranking individuals among the twelve tribes of Israel who were responsible for leading and guiding the people.
Consecration: A ceremony in which something or someone is set apart for holy service or a sacred purpose.
Altar: A structure where offerings and rituals were performed as part of religious worship.
Obedience: To obey and follow God's commands and instructions.

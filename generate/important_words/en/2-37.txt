Ark: A chest made of acacia wood, covered with gold, which contained the Ten Commandments and represented God's presence among the Israelites.
Mercy Seat: A gold-covered lid on the ark, where two cherubim stand facing each other. The mercy seat symbolized God's throne and grace, and was the place where God spoke to Moses.
Cherubim: Angelic beings in Jewish and Christian tradition, often depicted with wings and faces. In Exodus 37 they guard the mercy seat and God's presence.
Lampstand: A gilded lamp holder with seven branches, used in the tabernacle to provide light. It symbolized God's light and presence among his people.
Tabernacle: A portable sanctuary tent that the Israelites used for worship during their wilderness journey after leaving Egypt. The tabernacle contained the ark and other holy objects.
Bezalel: A skilled craftsman chosen by God to make the ark, the mercy seat, the lampstand, and other items for the tabernacle.

Praise: To express admiration and gratitude to God for his greatness and grace.
Lord: Another designation for God, emphasizing his sovereignty and authority.
Fear: Awe or reverence for God, arising from the recognition of his power and holiness.
Hope: Trust in and expectation of God's promises and plans for the future.
Mercy: God's loving and compassionate treatment of people, especially those in need or suffering.
Justice: God's moral standard, which he expects people to uphold.
Creative Power: God's ability to create and sustain the universe and all life.
Wisdom: God's perfect knowledge and understanding, which he gives to those who seek him.
Faithfulness: God's reliability and loyalty in keeping his promises and standing by his word.

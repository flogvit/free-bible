Lord: The title used to refer to God in the book of Amos; meaning the almighty and sovereign Creator.
Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God, often concerning future events or divine guidance.
Israel: An Old Testament people and nation whom God chose for a special relationship and mission.
Samaria: The capital city of the northern kingdom of Israel in Amos' time, often associated with idolatry and sin.
Judgment: Consequences or suffering resulting from a person's actions, especially regarding violations of God's laws.
Cry: A strong expression of distress, complaint, or warning, often used by prophets to awaken the people's attention.
Lion: A symbol of strength and authority, used metaphorically to describe God's power and judgment.
Two: The number of witnesses required to confirm a message or accusation, according to Old Testament law.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and restoring their relationship with Him.

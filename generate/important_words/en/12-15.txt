Azariah: A king of Judah who is also known as Uzziah; he reigned for 52 years and did what was right in the Lord's eyes.
Zechariah: A king of Israel who reigned for six months; he was murdered by Shallum, who then took the throne.
Shallum: A conspirator who murdered King Zechariah and ruled over Israel for one month before being murdered himself by Menahem.
Menahem: A king of Israel who reigned for ten years; he did evil in the Lord's eyes and was succeeded by his son Pekahiah.
Pekahiah: A king of Israel who reigned for two years; he did evil in the Lord's eyes and was murdered by Pekah, who then took the throne.
Pekah: A king of Israel who reigned for 20 years; he did evil in the Lord's eyes and was murdered by Hoshea, who then took the throne.
Hoshea: A king of Israel who reigned for nine years; he did evil in the Lord's eyes and was eventually taken captive by the Assyrians.
Jotham: A king of Judah who reigned for 16 years and did what was right in the Lord's eyes; he was succeeded by his son Ahaz.
Ahaz: A king of Judah who reigned for 16 years; he did evil in the Lord's eyes and was succeeded by his son Hezekiah.

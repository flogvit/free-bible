Rene: To be free from impurities, moral and spiritual purity.
Urene dyr: Animals that according to the Mosaic laws are not considered clean and therefore not suitable for consumption.
Tiende: A tax or levy of ten percent on income or harvest, given to God or the temple.
Levittene: Members of the tribe of Levi, one of Israel's twelve tribes, with special religious duties and rights.
Fattige: Persons who lack economic resources and material goods, often referred to in the Bible as those who need help and support from society.
Enke: A woman whose husband is dead and who has not remarried.
Farløs: A person, usually a child, who has lost their father and has no male guardian.
Fremmede: Persons who come from another country or culture and live temporarily or permanently in Israel.

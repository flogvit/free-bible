Zion: A mountain in Jerusalem, often used as a symbol of God's dwelling place and the spiritual center of Israel.
The Lord's city: An expression referring to Jerusalem, regarded as God's chosen place and Israel's capital.
Fortress: A strong and protective structure, here used metaphorically to describe God's protection of His people.
God's love: The deep, unconditional love that God has for His people and all creation.
Righteousness: Moral conduct that aligns with God's will and commands.
Praise: To honor or glorify God through song, music, or words, as a form of worship and gratitude.

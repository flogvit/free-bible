Sin: An act or condition that breaks God's will and morality, leading to spiritual separation from God.
Confession: To admit one's sins and faults before God, and ask for forgiveness and grace.
Covenant: A holy agreement between God and people, where God promises blessings and protection in exchange for obedience and faithfulness.
Intercession: To pray on behalf of others, often for their spiritual, physical, and emotional needs.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love, given to people despite their sin and injustice.
Deliverance: God's act of saving His people from slavery, oppression, or danger, often through miraculous means.
Humility: An attitude of reverence and respect toward God, recognizing one's own limitations and dependence on God.
Praise: To express gratitude and honor to God for His goodness, grace, and power.

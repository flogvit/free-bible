God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Jeremiah: The prophet who wrote the book of Jeremiah and delivered God's message to the people of Israel.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people and the nation they inhabited in the Old Testament.
Idols: False gods or objects worshipped instead of the true God, which was forbidden according to the Ten Commandments.
Wedding day: A symbol of the relationship between God and Israel, where God is the bridegroom and Israel is the bride.
Unfaithfulness: A metaphor for Israel's spiritual adultery against God by worshipping other gods and breaking the covenant.
Source: A reference to God as the living fountain of water, and idols as broken cisterns that hold no water.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, especially between God and Israel, where both parties have obligations and promises.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment and consequences for Israel's sins and unfaithfulness.

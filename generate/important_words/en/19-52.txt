Evil: A moral failure or bad action, often in conflict with God's will.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with ethical and moral principles, especially in line with God's teaching.
Tongue: Used metaphorically to describe human speech and communication, both good and evil.
Deceit: The act of deceiving, cheating, or being dishonest, which is condemned in the book of Psalms.
House of God: A place for worship and holy fellowship, often referred to as a church or temple.
Olive: Symbolizes peace, fruitfulness, and blessing; often used as an image of righteousness and God's grace.
Trust: The belief that God will protect, guide, and give strength in difficult times.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, reverence, and devotion to God through song and prayer.

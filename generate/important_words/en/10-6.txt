Ark: A holy object containing the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, symbolizing God's presence and covenant with Israel.
Uzzah: A man from the tribe of Judah who died when he touched the Ark during its transport to Jerusalem.
David: The second king of Israel, known for his skill as a leader and warrior, as well as for composing many psalms.
Jerusalem: The capital city of ancient Israel and David's city, an important religious and political center for the Jews.
Michal: Daughter of King Saul and David's first wife, who criticized David for his dancing before the Ark.
Dance: A physical form of expression that David used to honor and rejoice in God's presence and blessings.
Blessing: A divine gift or favor, often associated with protection, prosperity, and success.

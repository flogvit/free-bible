Fool: A person who lacks reason, wisdom, or judgment, and acts recklessly or carelessly.
Deny: To reject, disavow, or refuse to believe in something; in this case, the existence of God.
Wicked: Something morally wrong, harmful, or evil; contrary to goodness and justice.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with moral principles and ethics; honest and just.
Lord: A title for God that emphasizes His authority and dominion over all things and everyone.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and granting them eternal life.

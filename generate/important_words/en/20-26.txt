Proverbs: Short, wise sayings that give advice and guidance in life.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a reasonable and prudent way.
Foolishness: A lack of good judgment or wisdom; unreasonable behavior.
Slothfulness: An unwillingness to work or exert oneself; a lack of initiative.
Tongue: A symbol for speech and communication; it can be used for both good and evil purposes.
Enemy: A person who is opposed to or wishes harm upon another; an adversary.
Lie: A false statement or action intended to deceive or mislead.
Deceitful: Something or someone that is unreliable, dishonest, or treacherous.
Friend: A person with whom one has a close and trusting relationship; an ally.
Chestnut: A metaphor for an old, well-known matter or situation.

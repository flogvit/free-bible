Balaam: A prophet who is asked to curse the Israelites, but God instructs him not to do so.
Balak: The king of Moab who asks Balaam to curse the Israelites out of fear that they will invade his land.
Israelites: God's chosen people who are on their way to the promised land after being led out of Egypt.
Donkey: The animal Balaam rides, which sees an angel and refuses to move forward. The donkey is given the ability to speak by God and confronts Balaam.
Angel: A divine messenger sent by God to prevent Balaam from cursing the Israelites.
Curse: A negative force or unfavorable situation that Balak wants Balaam to bring upon the Israelites.
Blessing: The opposite of a curse; Balaam ends up blessing the Israelites instead of cursing them.

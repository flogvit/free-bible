Aaron: Brother of Moses and high priest in Israel, chosen by God to serve in the priesthood.
Levites: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, selected by God to serve in the tabernacle and later the temple as assistants to the priests.
Tabernacle: A temporary sanctuary and meeting place between God and his people, where sacrifices and ceremonies were performed.
Gershon: The eldest son of Levi, and one of the three main families among the Levites.
Kohath: The second son of Levi, and one of the three main families among the Levites.
Merari: The third son of Levi, and one of the three main families among the Levites.
High Priest: The supreme leader of the priesthood, responsible for performing important ritual actions and sacrifices.
Mercy Seat: The top cover of the Ark of the Covenant, where God's presence was to rest and where the high priest was to offer the atonement sacrifice.

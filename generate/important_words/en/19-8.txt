Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, used to acknowledge his sovereignty and power.
Heaven: The upper part of the universe, often associated with God's dwelling place and symbolically representing divine presence and protection.
Earth: The planet we live on, created by God and given to humanity as their home.
Son of Man: An expression that refers to people in general, emphasizing our frailty and dependence on God.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose God's will and plans, and who can be a source of evil and suffering in the world.
Mouth: Used metaphorically to represent speech and communication, especially when it comes to praising and honoring God.
Children: Young people, symbolizing innocence and purity, and serving as an image of the trust and dependence we should have toward God.
Fingers: Represent God's creative power and skill, and how he has shaped and designed everything in the universe.
Stars: Luminous celestial bodies that represent God's greatness and beauty, and serve as signs of his infinite wisdom and creativity.

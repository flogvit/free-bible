Psalm 77 is a lament in which the author expresses distress and seeks comfort in God's past deeds. Here are some key words and their explanations:
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.
Cry out: To express oneself loudly and passionately, especially in prayer or distress.
Night: Darkness or the period between sunset and sunrise, often associated with sorrow, loneliness, or fear.
Hand: Symbolizes God's power and protection, as well as his ability to act in the world.
Miracles: Supernatural acts performed by God that demonstrate his power and love.
Lord: A title showing respect and awe for God, acknowledging his sovereignty and authority.
Salvation: God's action to save or deliver people from sin, evil, and death.

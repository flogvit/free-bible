Philistines: An ancient people group who lived in the coastal regions of Canaan, present-day Israel and Gaza. They were often in conflict with the Israelites.
Achish: The Philistine king of the city of Gath, whom David sought refuge with when he fled from King Saul.
David: Future king of Israel and Judah, known for his bravery and skill as a warrior. He is also the author of several psalms in the Book of Psalms.
Saul: The first king of Israel, chosen by the prophet Samuel. His disobedience to God led God to reject him as king and choose David instead.
Aphek: A city in Canaan where the Philistine army gathered before going into battle against the Israelites.
Malakites: A nomadic tribe that lived south of Canaan and had frequent clashes with the Israelites.
Amalekites: A people group who lived in the Negev desert and parts of Sinai. They were enemies of the Israelites and had several conflicts with them.
Ziklag: A city in southern Israel that was given to David by Achish as a base for him and his men.

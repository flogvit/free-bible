Lord's Temple: The holy place where God is worshiped and honored, often associated with Solomon's temple in Jerusalem.
Seraphim: Heavenly beings with six wings who surround God's throne and continually praise him.
Holy: A term used to describe something that is divine, pure, and set apart from the ordinary or profane.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks God's will and moral law.
Sacrificial animal: An animal offered to atone for human sins and restore the relationship between God and humanity.
Cleansing: The process of being freed from sin and impurity so that a person can be reunited with God.
Prophet: A person who receives messages from God and conveys them to the people, often with the purpose of guiding, warning, or encouraging.
Calling: God's special mission or purpose for an individual's life, especially within service and leadership.

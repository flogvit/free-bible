Prayer: A communication between people and God, where one asks for help, gives thanks, or praises God.
Lord: Title used to refer to God as the supreme authority and ruler over the universe.
Merciful: Attribute describing God's grace, love, and compassion toward humanity.
Grace: Unmerited goodness and mercy that God shows people, despite their faults and sins.
Forgive: To remove guilt and punishment linked to a person's actions, often in a religious context where God forgives sins.
Listen: To be attentive to and receive information from someone, here used in the context of God listening to people's prayers.
Keep safe: To protect and hold secure; in this context, it refers to God's protection of people.
Faithful: Characteristic of God as reliable and loyal, always fulfilling promises and keeping His word.

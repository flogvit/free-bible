Praise Song: A song or poem that expresses praise, gratitude, and honor to God.
The Lord: Another name for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority over all creation.
Proclaim: To communicate or spread a message, especially concerning God's works and salvation.
Salvation: God's act of saving humanity from sin and its consequences, particularly through Jesus Christ.
The Nations: All peoples and cultures in the world, emphasizing that God's salvation is universal and available to all people.
Judge: God's righteous evaluation and decision regarding human actions, both individually and collectively.
Righteousness: God's moral perfection and standard, which people are encouraged to imitate.
Creation: The entire universe and all life that God has created, including the earth, the heavens, and every living being.

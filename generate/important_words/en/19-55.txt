Prayer: A communication with God where one asks for help, guidance, or gives thanks for blessings.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or wish to harm someone, often used in a religious context to describe evil powers.
Flee: To quickly leave a place, situation, or conflict to avoid danger or discomfort.
Fear: A feeling of anxiety, unease, or worry that arises when one is afraid of something or someone.
The Lord: Another term for God, referring to his authority and power.
Lament: To express dissatisfaction, grief, or suffering, often directed toward God in prayer for help and comfort.
Tears: Drops of fluid coming from the eyes, usually as a result of sorrow, pain, or strong emotions.
Trust: Belief that someone or something will act rightly and be reliable, especially in relation to God.
Friendship: A close relationship between two or more people involving love, support, and understanding.

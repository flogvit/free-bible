Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who conveyed God's message to the people of Israel.
Warning: Jeremiah warns the people about the consequences of their actions and urges them to turn back to God.
Idolatry: The worship of false gods, which was strictly forbidden according to Israelite faith.
Punishment: God's response to the disobedience and idolatry of the people of Israel, often in the form of destruction and suffering.
Exile: The banishment of the people of Israel from their homeland as a consequence of their disobedience toward God.
Confession: Acknowledgment of one's sins and an expression of repentance, a necessary step to seek God's forgiveness.
Upheaval: Major changes and adjustments resulting from God's intervention in human lives and history.
Salvation: God's promise to save his people from destruction and give them eternal life if they turn away from sin and follow his way.

Aron: Brother of Moses and high priest in Israel, chosen by God to serve as a priest in the tabernacle.
Staff: A rod or stick that symbolized authority and leadership, especially for the leader of one of Israel's twelve tribes.
Buds: Growths on the staff that represented life and fruitfulness, serving as a sign of God's choice of Aaron as high priest.
Flowers: Blooming parts of the staff that also symbolized life and growth, confirming God's choice of Aaron.
Almond tree: A tree known for its early blooming, which in this context symbolizes God's choice of Aaron and his descendants as priests.
Rebellion: The act of opposing the authority of Moses and Aaron, which led to God's punishment and demonstration of His choice through the staff that blossomed.
Tabernacle: The temporary holy tent where the Israelites worshiped God during their wilderness journey, and where Aaron and his sons carried out their priestly duties.

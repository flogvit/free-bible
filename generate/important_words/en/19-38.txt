Sin: A violation of God's laws or moral principles, leading to spiritual separation from God.
Punishment: The consequences or sufferings resulting from sin, whether inflicted by God or as a natural outcome of actions.
Prayer: Communication with God in which one asks for help, guidance, forgiveness, or gives thanks for blessings.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and mercy toward people, especially regarding forgiveness and salvation.
Repentance: Sincere regret for past sins and a desire to change one's behavior and make things right.
Healing: The process of becoming well or restored from illness, injury, or suffering, whether physical, mental, or spiritual.
Enemies: Individuals or forces that oppose, threaten, or cause harm, often used in a religious context to describe evil powers.
Trust: Belief in God's ability and willingness to protect, guide, and strengthen the believer when facing challenges and trials.

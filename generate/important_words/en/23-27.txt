Isaiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, the almighty Creator.
Israel: The name of the biblical people and land with whom God had a special relationship.
House of Jacob: An expression referring to the descendants of Jacob, also known as the twelve tribes of Israel.
Fruits: Symbolize the results or consequences of actions and decisions.
Enemies: Those who oppose or work against God's plan and his people.
The serpent: A symbol of evil and temptation, often associated with Satan.
Vineyard: Means vineyard in English, and symbolizes God's care and protection of his people.
Punishment: The consequences that follow from breaking God's commands and going against his will.
Salvation: God's act to save people from sin and death through faith and repentance.

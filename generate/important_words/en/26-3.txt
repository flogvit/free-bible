Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Vision: A spiritual experience where a person sees or hears something supernatural, often with a message from God.
Scroll: A rolled manuscript, an ancient writing medium, containing God's words and messages to Ezekiel.
Sweet: A description of the taste of God's word when Ezekiel eats the scroll, symbolizing that God's message is good and desirable.
Watchman: One who warns and protects others from danger. Ezekiel is given the role of watchman to warn the people of Israel about God's judgment.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment and consequences for human sin and disobedience.
Sin: Transgression of God's laws and commands, leading to separation from God and his blessings.
Repentance: The feeling of remorse and sorrow over sin, which can lead to turning away from sin and receiving forgiveness from God.
Conversion: To change one's life, thoughts, and actions by turning away from sin and turning toward God.
Forgiveness: God's grace and mercy that removes the guilt of sin and restores the relationship between God and humanity.

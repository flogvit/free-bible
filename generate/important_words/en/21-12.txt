Preacher: A wise man in the Old Testament who shares his wisdom and reflections on life.
Vanity: A central theme in the book of Ecclesiastes, referring to meaninglessness and transience in the world.
Time: Another important theme in the book, emphasizing that there is a time for everything under heaven and that human life is short-lived.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and apply knowledge in a reasonable way. The Preacher values wisdom but acknowledges its limitations.
Work: Human effort and activity to achieve a goal. The Preacher discusses the meaning and significance of work in life.
Pleasure: The joy and satisfaction one gains from various aspects of life. The Preacher recommends enjoying life's blessings but recognizes that this too is fleeting.
Fear of God: Respect and awe for God. The Preacher concludes that this is humanity's primary duty and the path to a meaningful life.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation of human actions. The Preacher reminds us that God will judge everyone for their deeds.

Isaac: One of the patriarchs in the Jewish tradition, son of Abraham and father to Jacob and Esau.
Rebekah: Isaac's wife and mother to Jacob and Esau.
Esau: Isaac's firstborn son, twin brother to Jacob, known for his endurance as a hunter.
Jacob: Isaac's younger son, twin brother to Esau, known for his cunning and later becomes the ancestor of Israel's twelve tribes.
Blessing: A sacred act where God or a person gives another person a spiritual gift or favor, often linked to prosperity, fruitfulness, or protection.
Deception: The act of deceiving someone by hiding the truth or giving false information, which Jacob does to obtain Isaac's blessing.
Birthright: The special status and inheritance rights belonging to the firstborn son in a family, including authority and material wealth.
Lentil stew: The dish Jacob prepares and trades for Esau's birthright, symbolizing worldly needs and short-term priorities.

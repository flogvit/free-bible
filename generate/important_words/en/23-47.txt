Babylon: An ancient city and kingdom in Mesopotamia, often used as a symbol of evil and idolatry in the Bible.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or evaluation of human actions, especially in relation to their faithfulness toward him.
Lord: Another name for God, emphasizing his sovereignty and authority over all creation.
Virgin: A metaphor describing Babylon as an innocent woman, which is ironic since Babylon was known for its evil and idolatry.
Humiliation: Degradation and loss of dignity, often resulting from God's judgment and punishment.
Sorcery: The practice of magic and supernatural powers, often associated with evil and idolatry.
Sorrow: Pain, suffering, and hardship experienced by people, especially as a result of God's judgment.
Rivers: Large waterways, often used in the Bible as symbols of power and purity.

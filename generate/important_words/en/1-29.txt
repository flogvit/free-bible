Jacob: Grandson of Abraham and son of Isaac, who plays an important role in Genesis and is one of the patriarchs in Jewish tradition.
Laban: Uncle to Jacob and father of Rachel and Leah, two of Jacob's wives.
Rachel: Laban's younger daughter and Jacob's beloved wife, mother of Joseph and Benjamin.
Leah: Laban's older daughter and Jacob's first wife, mother of six of his sons and a daughter.
Seven years: The time Jacob worked for Laban to be allowed to marry Rachel, but he was tricked into marrying Leah first.
Wedding: The ceremony where Jacob marries both Leah and later Rachel, after completing his service with Laban.
Service: The work Jacob performs for Laban as payment in order to marry his daughters.

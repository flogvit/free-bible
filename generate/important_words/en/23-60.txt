The Lord's glory: God's radiant presence and power displayed in a magnificent way.
Nations: Groups of people organized under a common system of government, often linked to a specific geographic area.
Light: Symbolizes God's guidance, truth, and spiritual insight.
Darkness: Represents ignorance, evil, and spiritual blindness.
Gold and frankincense: Precious gifts offered to God in biblical times, symbolizing devotion and reverence.
Foreigners: People who do not belong to the original community or people, often used to describe non-Israelites.
Daughter of Zion: A poetic expression for Jerusalem, Israel's capital and center of religious worship.
Peace: A state of harmony and absence of conflict, both physical and spiritual.

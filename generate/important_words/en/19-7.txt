Psalm: A poetic and musical form of prayer, praise, or lament found in the Bible.
Lord: A respectful name for God that acknowledges his authority and power.
Refuge: A place of protection and safety, often used metaphorically to describe God's care and shelter.
Righteousness: God's moral perfection and just judgment, as well as a person's obedience to God's will and moral standards.
Enemies: Persons or forces who oppose, threaten, or harm someone, often used in the Psalms to describe wicked people or spiritual powers.
Prayer: Communication with God through speech or thought, where a person may ask for help, give thanks, worship, or confess sin.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, death, and eternal separation from him, especially through faith in Jesus Christ.

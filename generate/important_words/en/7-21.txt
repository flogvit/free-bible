Benjamin: One of the twelve tribes of Israel who was involved in the conflict described in Judges 21.
Gibeah: A city in the tribe of Benjamin where a terrible crime was committed, which led to a war between Benjamin and the other Israelite tribes.
Sons of Israel: A term for the people of Israel or the Israelite tribes.
Ed: An altar by the Jordan River where the Israelites gathered to discuss the situation with the tribe of Benjamin and swear loyalty to one another.
Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator in Jewish and Christian faith.
Jabesh-Gilead: A city east of the Jordan River that was attacked by the Israelites because it did not take part in the war against the tribe of Benjamin.
Daughters of Jabesh: Young women from Jabesh-Gilead who were captured by the Israelites and given as wives to the surviving men from the tribe of Benjamin.
Shiloh: A city in the land of Ephraim where an annual festival took place, and where the Benjaminites kidnapped some women to take them as wives.

Temple Area: The holy place where God dwells and is worshiped, central to Ezekiel's vision of future Israel.
Sacrifice: Ritual acts in which animals or food are offered to God as a form of worship and atonement for sin.
Priests: The religious leaders in Israel who performed sacrificial rites and served in the temple on behalf of the people.
Levites: One of the twelve tribes of Israel with special duties and responsibilities connected to the temple and its services.
Holy Place: The innermost part of the temple where God's presence was strongest, also called the Most Holy Place.
Land Distribution: The process of dividing the land of Israel among the twelve tribes, described in detail in Ezekiel 45.
Prince: A leader or ruler in Israel, often referred to as a king or prince. In Ezekiel 45 this may refer to a messianic figure.
Festivals: Religious celebrations and feasts in Israel, such as Passover and the Feast of Tabernacles, where the people gather to worship God and remember his deeds.

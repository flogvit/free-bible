Jacob: The patriarch who is the son of Isaac and father to twelve tribes of Israel, gives blessings and prophecies to his sons before he dies.
Sons: The twelve sons of Jacob who represent the twelve tribes of Israel.
Blessing: A holy statement that conveys God's favor and protection over a person or group of people.
Prophecy: A divinely inspired statement that predicts future events or gives guidance and instruction.
Reuben: Jacob's firstborn son, loses his birthright because of his behavior.
Simeon and Levi: Two of Jacob's sons, criticized for their violent actions and scattered among the tribes of Israel.
Judah: One of Jacob's sons, promised rule and leadership among his brothers, and his descendants include King David and Jesus.
Zebulun: One of Jacob's sons, receives a blessing connected to seafaring and trade.
Issachar: One of Jacob's sons, described as a hardworking and patient person.
Dan: One of Jacob's sons, predicted to become a judge and leader for Israel.
Gad: One of Jacob's sons, described as a warrior who will fight against enemies.
Asher: One of Jacob's sons, blessed with wealth and abundance.
Naphtali: One of Jacob's sons, described as a swift and skilled messenger.
Joseph: One of Jacob's favorite sons, blessed with fruitfulness and protection from enemies. He is also known for his dream interpretation and leadership in Egypt.
Benjamin: Jacob's youngest son, described as a wolf who will share the spoil with his brothers.

Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Book 48: The final chapter of the book of Ezekiel, which describes the future division of the land of Israel among the twelve tribes.
Land: The area that God promised to give to the people of Israel, also known as Canaan or the Promised Land.
Tribes: The twelve groups of the people of Israel, each with its own origin and inheritance, descending from Jacob's twelve sons.
Allotment: The distribution of the land among the twelve tribes, described in detail in Ezekiel 48.
Holy Place: A special area within the land dedicated to God's service and worship, usually associated with the temple.
Temple: A holy place where God's presence dwells and where worship and sacrifices are performed.
City Gates: The entrances to the cities of Israel, mentioned in Ezekiel 48, representing the twelve tribes.

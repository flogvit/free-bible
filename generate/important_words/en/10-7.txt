Nathan: The prophet who conveys God's message to King David in 2 Samuel 7.
David: The second king of Israel and a central figure in 2 Samuel, known for his piety and military skill.
Temple: A holy place dedicated to God, where worship and sacrifices take place. In this context, it refers to the temple David wishes to build for God.
Covenant: An agreement or promise between two parties, in this case between God and David. God promises to establish David's kingdom and his descendants as rulers over Israel.
Rest: The term refers to a time of peace and stability for Israel, which God promises David and his people after they have been delivered from their enemies.
Throne: Symbolizes royal power and authority. In 2 Samuel 7, God promises that David's throne will be established forever.
Descendant: A person who comes from a specific lineage or family. God promises that one of David's descendants will rule over Israel and establish an eternal kingdom.

Absalom: Third son of King David, known for his beautiful hair and rebellion against his father.
Joab: David's nephew and army commander, played a key role in conflicts and political intrigues during David's reign.
Tekoa woman: A woman from the town of Tekoa who was sent by Joab to David to present a parable aimed at bringing Absalom back from exile.
Parable: A story that conveys a moral or spiritual lesson, often using metaphors and symbolism.
Exile: Being forced to leave one's homeland, usually due to political or legal reasons.
Reunion: The process of bringing together family members or friends who have been separated, especially after a long period.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy toward people, regardless of their actions or merit.

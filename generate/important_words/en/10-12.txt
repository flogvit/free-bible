Nathan: The prophet who conveys God's message to King David and confronts him with his sins.
David: The king of Israel who commits a series of sins, including adultery and murder.
Bathsheba: The woman David has an affair with and later marries after arranging for her husband to be killed.
Uriah: Bathsheba's husband, a military commander sent to his death on David's orders.
Sin: An action or behavior that goes against God's will and moral law.
Punishment: The consequences that follow from committing sin, often described as God's righteous judgment.
Repentance: The feeling of sorrow and regret for one's own sins and the desire to turn away and change one's ways.
Forgiveness: God's grace and mercy toward those who repent of their sins and seek reconciliation.

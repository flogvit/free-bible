Circumcision: A religious ceremony involving the removal of the foreskin from the penis, marking the establishment of a covenant between God and the people of Israel.
Gilgal: A place near Jericho where the Israelites camped after crossing the Jordan River. Here Joshua performed circumcision on the new generation of Israelites.
Manna: Miraculous food that God provided to the Israelites during their journey through the wilderness. The manna ceased when they reached Canaan, the promised land.
Passover Lamb: A sacrificial lamb eaten by the Israelites during the Passover celebration to commemorate their deliverance from slavery in Egypt.
Canaan: The promised land that God designated for the Israelites. This area is located in present-day Israel, Palestine, and parts of Lebanon, Syria, and Jordan.
Joshua: The leader of the Israelites after Moses' death. He led the people into Canaan and guided them in conquering the land.

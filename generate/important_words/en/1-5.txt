Adam: The first human created by God, and the ancestor of all people.
Eve: The first woman, created by God from Adam's rib, and mother to all people.
Seth: Third son of Adam and Eve, born after Cain and Abel; regarded as the ancestor of the righteous line of humanity.
Enosh: Son of Seth and father of Kenan; represents the continuation of humanity after Cain's evil deeds.
Kenan: Son of Enosh and father of Mahalalel; part of the righteous line in Adam's family.
Mahalalel: Son of Kenan and father of Jared; another leader in the righteous genealogy.
Jared: Son of Mahalalel and father of Enoch; his generation led to Enoch, who had a special relationship with God.
Enoch: Son of Jared, known for his close relationship with God; was taken up to heaven without dying.
Methuselah: Son of Enoch and father of Lamech; known as the oldest person ever to have lived, at 969 years old.
Lamech: Son of Methuselah and father of Noah; part of the righteous genealogy before the flood.
Noah: Son of Lamech, chosen by God to build the ark and save his family and the animals from the flood; ancestor of humanity after the flood.

Feast: A special occasion or festival dedicated to honoring God and celebrating his works.
Sabbath: A weekly day of rest to honor God, usually observed from sunset on Friday to sunset on Saturday.
Passover: An important Jewish feast that celebrates the deliverance of the people of Israel from slavery in Egypt under the leadership of Moses.
Unleavened Bread: A seven-day festival that follows Passover, where Jews eat unleavened bread (matzah) to remember their hasty departure from Egypt.
Firstfruits: A religious ceremony in which the first and best fruits and vegetables from the harvest were offered to God as thanks for his blessings.
Pentecost: A Jewish feast celebrated 50 days after Passover, also known as Shavuot. It marks the time when God gave the Torah to Moses on Mount Sinai.
Feast of Tabernacles: A seven-day feast, also known as Sukkot, celebrated in autumn to remember Israel's 40 years of wandering in the wilderness and God's protection.
Yom Kippur: A Jewish holy day, also known as the Day of Atonement, where Jews ask for forgiveness for their sins and fast for 25 hours.

Lord: A title used to refer to God, the almighty creator of the universe.
Feast: A large meal arranged to celebrate a special event or festival, often with religious significance.
Death: The end of life; a transition from earthly life to eternity.
Tear: A drop of fluid formed in the eye, often as a result of emotions such as sorrow, joy, or gratitude.
Veil: A thin covering or screen that hides or protects something.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and giving them eternal life.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human actions.
Fortress: A protective structure or barrier, such as a wall or stronghold, built to defend against enemies.
Refuge: A place where one seeks protection, safety, or comfort, especially in difficult times.

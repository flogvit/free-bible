Heshbon: A city in Moab that was conquered by the Israelites and given to the tribe of Reuben as part of their inheritance.
Bashan: An area east of the Jordan, known for its fertility and rich pastures; conquered by the Israelites under Moses' leadership.
Og: The king of Bashan who was defeated by the Israelites under Moses' leadership; his defeat symbolizes God's power and protection of His people.
Sirion: Another name for Mount Hermon, a high mountain in the Anti-Lebanon range, which formed part of the boundary between Canaan and other regions.
Inheritance: A term referring to the land and resources that God gave to the Israelites after their departure from Egypt; this includes the territories distributed among the twelve tribes.

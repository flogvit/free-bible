Love: An intense feeling of devotion, closeness, and care between people, a central theme in the Song of Songs.
Bride: The woman who is loved by the man in the Song of Songs, a symbol of the loving relationship between God and his people.
Groom: The man who loves the bride in the Song of Songs, also representing God's love for his people.
Vine: A plant that produces grapes, symbolizing fruitfulness and growth in the Song of Songs.
Pomegranate: A fruit often used as a symbol of fertility and love in the Song of Songs.
Sister: A close relative or friend, used in the Song of Songs to describe a close and loving relationship.
Seal: A symbol of ownership and protection, used in the Song of Songs to express the strength and permanence of love.
Flame: An image representing passion and intensity, used in the Song of Songs to describe the power of love.

Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his sovereignty and power.
Praise: A form of musical or poetic expression that gives honor, thanks, and glory to God.
Mercy: God's grace, love, and compassion, especially toward people in need or suffering.
Truth: That which is real, correct, and reliable; here it often refers to God's word and promises.
Humble: A person who is modest and not arrogant, recognizing their dependence on God and others.
Pride: A negative trait that involves self-exaltation, arrogance, and an inability to accept help or guidance from God.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin, death, and eternal separation from him, often associated with faith in Jesus Christ.

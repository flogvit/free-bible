Here are some important words from Isaiah chapter 54 and their explanations:
Widow: A woman who has lost her spouse through death and has not remarried.
Childless: A person who has no children, either by choice or due to biological or other reasons.
Tent: A temporary dwelling made of cloth or other material, often used by nomads or in connection with refugee camps.
Mount Zion: A hill in Jerusalem, also known as the Temple Mount, which is a holy place for Jews, Christians, and Muslims.
The Lord: A title often used for God in the Old Testament, especially in the Hebrew Bible.
Forsaken: A person who has been abandoned by their spouse, family, or community, and may feel lonely and helpless.
God of salvation: A title referring to God's role as savior and protector of His people.
Righteousness: A concept denoting just and moral conduct, often linked to God's will and standards.
Peace: A state of calm, harmony, and absence of conflict, both individually and between groups.

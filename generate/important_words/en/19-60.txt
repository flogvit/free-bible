God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Psalm: A poetic text from the Bible, often a prayer or praise to God.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and punishment, and giving them eternal life.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose God's people, often symbolizing evil and sin.
The Lord's hand: An expression of God's power and influence in the world.
Banner: A symbol of unity and fellowship among God's people, as well as their shared faith and purpose.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy toward people, despite their sins and failures.
Help: Support and protection given by God to his people in times of trouble and testing.

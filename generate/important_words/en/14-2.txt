Solomon: King David's son and successor as king of Israel, known for his wisdom and the building of the temple in Jerusalem.
Temple: A holy place dedicated to God's worship, especially the first temple in Jerusalem built by King Solomon.
Hiram: The king of Tyre who helped Solomon obtain materials and labor for the construction of the temple.
Tyre: An ancient city in present-day Lebanon, an important trading partner for Israel at that time.
Cedar trees: Tall, straight trees with fragrant wood, valued as building materials and often mentioned in the Bible.
Workers: People who perform physical or manual labor, in this case for the construction of the temple.
Building project: An organized effort to construct a structure, in this case the temple in Jerusalem.
Offerings: Gifts, usually food or animals, given to God in worship and thanksgiving.

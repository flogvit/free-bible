Ezra: A Jewish scribe and priest who helped restore the law and rebuild the temple in Jerusalem after the exile in Babylon.
Babylon: An ancient empire in Mesopotamia where the Jews were held captive after Jerusalem fell in 586 BC.
Rebuilding: The process of restoring something that has been destroyed; here it refers to the reconstruction of the temple and community in Jerusalem.
Temple: A holy place for Jewish worship, built to house the Ark of the Covenant and serve as God's dwelling on earth. The first temple was destroyed by the Babylonians, and Ezra was involved in rebuilding the second temple.
Return: The process by which the Jews returned to Jerusalem from exile in Babylon to rebuild the temple and community.
List: In the book of Ezra chapter 2 there is a list of the names of the Jews who returned to Jerusalem after the exile in Babylon.
Ark of the Covenant: A sacred relic in Judaism that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, which Moses received from God on Mount Sinai.

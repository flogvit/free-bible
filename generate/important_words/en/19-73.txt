Asaph: The author of Psalm 73, a Levitical musician and prophet who led temple choirs and wrote several psalms.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Heart: Used metaphorically to represent human inner feelings, thoughts, and will.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with God's will and morality, behaving justly and uprightly.
Wicked: Describes actions or people that are morally bad, unjust, or harmful.
Envious: To have a negative feeling or desire for others' possessions, success, or advantages.
Understand: The ability to grasp and recognize the meaning of something, especially spiritual or moral principles.
Comfort: To give support, relief, or encouragement to someone who is suffering or in distress.
Strength: Power or ability to carry out actions, whether physical, mental, or spiritual.
Sanctuary: A holy place dedicated to God, often used as a reference to the temple in Jerusalem.
Fate: The final outcome or consequences of a situation, often seen as determined by God.

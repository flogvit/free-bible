Micah: A Levite who served as a priest for the tribe of Dan and played a central role in Judges 18.
Tribe of Dan: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, which sought new territory due to pressure from enemies.
Laish: A city in Canaan that was conquered and settled by the tribe of Dan, renamed Dan.
House of God: A holy place where God's presence was acknowledged and religious rituals were performed.
Idol: A physical representation of a deity, often used in religious worship.
Conquer: To take control of an area or people by means of military force.
Settle: To establish a community or permanent home in a new area.

Purification: The process of getting rid of impurities, both physical and spiritual, according to religious regulations.
Skin disease: A skin condition that can be a sign of uncleanness and require ritual cleansing.
Menstruation: A woman's monthly bleeding, which according to Leviticus makes her ritually unclean for a period.
Discharge of semen: A man's release of semen, which also leads to ritual uncleanness for a period.
Offering: A gift or act given to God to seek forgiveness, blessing, or gratitude.
Priest: A person appointed by God to perform religious ceremonies and maintain the sanctuary.
Sanctuary: A special place dedicated to God where religious ceremonies and offerings take place.
Sin offering: An offering given to atone for sins and impurities, restoring purity and fellowship with God.

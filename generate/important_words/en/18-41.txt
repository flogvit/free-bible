Leviathan: A great sea monster or creature described in the book of Job 41, used as a metaphor for God's invincible power.
Ineffable: An adjective meaning that something is so extraordinary or overwhelming that it cannot be described with words.
Crocodile: A reference to Leviathan as a large, dangerous, and powerful animal, like a crocodile, to emphasize God's sovereignty and might.
Scales: Protective plates covering Leviathan's body, symbolizing strength and immortality.
Breath: Leviathan's breath is described as a beam of light, pointing to his fearsome and mighty nature.
King of the sea: A title given to Leviathan representing his dominance and authority over the sea and all life within it.

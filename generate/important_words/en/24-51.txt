Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, known for its power and wealth; in Jeremiah 51 it is a symbol of evil and destruction.
The Lord: A title used for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority over all things.
God of hosts: A designation for God that emphasizes his power and control over all forces, both heavenly and earthly.
Hammer: An image representing God's power and justice, used to crush enemies and destroy evil.
Flood: A symbol of destruction and God's judgment, often used to describe a sudden and overwhelming catastrophe.
Redeemer: One who frees or saves someone from captivity, suffering, or danger; in this context it refers to God's action to deliver his people from Babylon.
Captivity: The state of being captured, oppressed, or held in bondage; in Jeremiah 51 it refers to Israel's exile in Babylon.
Sword: A weapon and symbol of war, used here to describe God's punishment and judgment over Babylon.
Nebuchadnezzar: A Babylonian king who conquered Jerusalem and led the Israelites into exile in Babylon.

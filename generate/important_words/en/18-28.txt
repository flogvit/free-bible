Wisdom: A deep understanding and insight into life and reality, which the book of Job 28 highlights as an invaluable gift from God.
Hidden places: Places that are inaccessible or unknown to humans, used as a metaphor for the inaccessibility and mystery of wisdom.
The depths: This word refers to the deepest parts of the earth and sea, and is also used as a metaphor to describe how difficult it is to find wisdom.
Silver: A valuable metal that symbolizes wealth and prosperity, but which cannot be compared with the value of wisdom.
Mining: The process of extracting minerals and precious metals from the earth, used as a parallel to the search for wisdom.
Fear of the Lord: An awe and reverence for God that is the foundation of wisdom, and involves humility and obedience to God's will.
Understanding: The ability to see connections and meaning in various aspects of life, which is necessary to attain wisdom.

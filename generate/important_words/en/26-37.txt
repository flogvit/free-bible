Prophecy: A divinely inspired message, often delivered through a prophet, that provides insight into future events or God's will.
Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God, including the prophecy about the dry bones in chapter 37.
Dry bones: A symbol of the people of Israel in exile, who felt hopeless and lifeless. God used this image to show that He would restore them and give them new life.
Revival: The process of bringing something back to life or restoring it to its former state. In Ezekiel 37, it represents God's promise to restore Israel and give them new life.
Israel: God's chosen people and the nation founded by Jacob's descendants. In Ezekiel 37, it refers to the two kingdoms, Judah and Israel, which are to be reunited under one king.
Restoration: The gathering of separated parts or groups. In Ezekiel 37, it describes the process by which God will gather His people from all nations and restore them as a united nation.
Spirit: In this context, it refers to God's life-giving power that will bring new life to Israel and their dry bones. It is also a symbol of God's presence and activity among His people.

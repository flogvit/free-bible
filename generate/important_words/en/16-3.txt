The Wall: The structure that surrounds and protects Jerusalem, which Nehemiah was commissioned to rebuild.
Rebuilding: The process of repairing and restoring something that has been destroyed or damaged, such as the wall around Jerusalem in the book of Nehemiah.
Nehemiah: A Jewish leader and governor who helped rebuild the wall around Jerusalem after the exile in Babylon.
Jerusalem: The holy city for the Jews and the capital of Israel; the center of Jewish faith and culture.
Exile: The period when the Jews were forced to leave their homeland and live in Babylon.
Babylon: The ancient kingdom and city where the Jews were held in exile before returning to Jerusalem to rebuild the city and its walls.
City Gates: The entrances to the city of Jerusalem that were part of the wall and had to be rebuilt along with the wall itself.
Workers: The volunteers who helped Nehemiah rebuild the wall and the city gates around Jerusalem.
Priests: Religious leaders within Judaism who also participated in the work of rebuilding the wall.

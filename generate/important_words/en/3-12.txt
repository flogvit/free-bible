Cleansing: The process of getting rid of impurities, both physical and spiritual, according to religious regulations.
Woman after childbirth: A woman who has recently given birth and is in a period of recovery and ritual purity.
Uncleanness: A state of being spiritually or ritually unclean according to religious laws and regulations.
Offering: A gift or act given to God as a form of worship, gratitude, or atonement for sins.
Sin offering: An offering given to make atonement for sins and receive forgiveness from God.
Burnt offering: An offering in which something, usually an animal, is burned on an altar as a holy act and a gift to God.
Priest: A person authorized to perform religious ceremonies and functions on behalf of God and people.

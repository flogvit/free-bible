Time: A period or moment when events occur, the Preacher emphasizes that there is a time for everything in life.
Purpose: The goal or intention behind something, the Preacher explores humanity's purpose and the meaning of life.
Vanity: The transience of life and the world, the Preacher stresses that all is fleeting and temporary.
Work: The effort and activities people undertake to achieve a goal, the Preacher reflects on the value and usefulness of labor.
Wisdom: Knowledge and understanding that enable one to make good choices, the Preacher acknowledges the worth of wisdom, yet also its limitations.
Joy: A feeling of well-being and contentment, the Preacher seeks joy and meaning in life.
Fear of God: Reverence and awe toward God, the Preacher teaches that this is the foundation of wisdom and the meaning of life.
Judgment: God's evaluation and justice, the Preacher points out that God will judge all actions, both good and evil.

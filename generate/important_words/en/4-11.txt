Quota: A distribution or allocation of something, such as food or resources, among a specific number of people.
Dissatisfaction: A feeling of discontent, irritation, or complaint about something that does not meet one's desires or expectations.
Manna: A miraculous food that God gave the Israelites while they wandered in the wilderness after fleeing from Egypt.
Meat: In this context, it refers to food, especially meat from animals, which the Israelites longed to eat instead of manna.
Elders: Experienced and respected members of a group or community, often chosen to make decisions and guide others.
Prophesy: To convey or express God's message and will, usually through inspired speech or action.
Wrath: Intense anger or rage, especially when directed at someone or something due to perceived injustice or sin.
Punishment: An act of inflicting suffering, pain, or loss as a result of bad behavior, disobedience, or sin.

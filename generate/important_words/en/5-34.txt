Moses: An important prophet and leader in Judaism, who received the Ten Commandments and led the Israelites out of Egypt.
Neviim: One of the three main sections of the Hebrew Bible, containing the writings of the prophets.
Inheritance: The transfer of property, rights, or positions from one person to another, often after death.
Joshua: Moses' successor as leader of the Israelites, known for leading them into the Promised Land.
Pisgah: A mountain in the region of Moab, where Moses climbed to see the Promised Land before he died.
The Promised Land: The area God promised to give to Abraham's descendants, including the Israelites.

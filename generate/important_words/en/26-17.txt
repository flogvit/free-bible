Eagle: Symbolizes rulers or nations; in this book it specifically refers to Babylon and Egypt.
Lebanon: A geographical area representing Jerusalem and its people.
Top shoot: Means a royal descendant from the house of David; here it refers to the king of Israel.
High mountain: Represents a place of power and authority, usually an empire or kingdom.
Fear of God: A respectful awe and obedience toward God and his commands.
Alliances: Agreements between nations to secure support, protection, and cooperation.
Thirst: In this context it means suffering under difficult conditions, often as a result of punishment from God.
Fruitfulness: The ability to grow and thrive, both physically and spiritually.

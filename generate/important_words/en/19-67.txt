God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Gracious: Refers to God's love, mercy, and goodness toward humanity.
Bless: To give divine favor, protection, or prosperity to someone or something.
Nations: Groups of people who share a common culture, language, or ethnicity.
Righteousness: God's righteousness, which means He acts in accordance with His own nature and laws.
Lord: Another designation for God, often used to show respect and reverence.
Hymn: A song or poem that expresses gratitude, honor, and praise to God.

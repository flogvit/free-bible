Jehoshaphat: A king of Judah who sought God's guidance and strength in the face of hostile nations.
Moabites: A people from the land of Moab, an enemy of Israel and Judah.
Ammonites: A people from the land of Ammon, also an enemy of Israel and Judah.
Seir: An area inhabited by the Edomites, another hostile people against Israel and Judah.
Prophet: A person who conveys God's message and will to the people.
Hymn: A song or hymn expressing gratitude, reverence, and praise to God.
Fear: A feeling of respect, awe, and submission before God's power and authority.
God's Spirit: The divine power and presence of God at work in the world and in human lives.
Enemies: Nations or groups that are in conflict with or threaten Israel and Judah.
Victory: To achieve triumph or dominance over an enemy or challenge, often with God's help.
Prayer: Communication with God in which one asks for help, guidance, protection, or gives thanks for blessings.

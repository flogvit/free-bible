Servant: A person who works for another, often in a subordinate position and without freedom to leave the service.
Hebrews: Members of the ancient Israelite people, descendants of the patriarch Jacob and his son Joseph.
Buy: To acquire ownership of something by paying a price or compensation.
Release: To set someone free from service, slavery, or captivity; to let go.
Eye for eye: An Old Testament principle of just retribution, where the punishment should match the harm inflicted on the victim.
Life: Here it refers to human life and the principle that human life is sacred and valuable.
Atonement: To make amends for a sin, fault, or wrong by providing some form of compensation or through acts of repentance.
Donkey: A domestic animal closely related to the horse, used as a beast of burden and for transportation.

Joshua: The main character in the book of Joshua, who leads the people of Israel into the promised land after Moses' death.
Canaan: A region in the Middle East that makes up the promised land for the people of Israel, which they conquer under Joshua's leadership.
Tribes: The twelve tribes of Israel, each representing one of Jacob's sons, and who divide Canaan among themselves.
Territories: Geographic areas that are distributed among the twelve tribes of Israel in the book of Joshua.
Moses: The prophet and leader of the people of Israel before Joshua, who led them out of Egypt and received the Ten Commandments from God.
Inheritance: The term used to describe the territories given to each of the twelve tribes of Israel as their allotted portion.
God's command: The instructions and requirements that God gives to Joshua and the people of Israel through prophets and visions.
Enemies: Peoples and nations living in Canaan before Israel's entry, whom they must fight against to take control of the land.

Levite: A man from the hill country of Ephraim who is the main character in the story and who takes in a guest and his companion.
Guest: A traveling man from Bethlehem seeking shelter for the night with his companion.
Companion: The guest's concubine who becomes the victim of a cruel act carried out by the men of Gibeah.
Gibeah: A city in the tribe of Benjamin where the events of chapter 19 take place.
Tribe of Benjamin: One of the twelve tribes of Israel that is strongly criticized and punished because of their evil and violent actions in this story.
Rape: A brutal and unacceptable sexual act committed by the men of Gibeah against the guest's companion.
Vengeance: The act of seeking justice and punishing those guilty for their wicked deeds, which leads to a bloody conflict between the tribes of Israel.

Hezekiah: A king of Judah known for his piety and reforms in the kingdom.
Temple: A holy place dedicated to God, where people came to worship and offer sacrifices.
Priests: Religious leaders responsible for performing ceremonies and teaching about God's laws.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi who had special religious duties and services in the temple.
Sacrifices: Gifts or actions given to God to show gratitude, ask for forgiveness, or seek blessings.
Tithes: A tenth of income or harvest given to the temple and priesthood to support their work and help those in need.
Purity: A state of being free from sin and impurities, both physical and spiritual, which is necessary to approach God.

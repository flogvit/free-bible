Asaph: One of the leading musicians and poets in King David's time, who wrote several of the Psalms.
Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Shout of joy: An expression of gladness and gratitude, often used in connection with praising and worshipping God.
Raised horn: A symbol of strength and victory, usually associated with God's power and protection of his people.
Land of Joseph: A reference to Israel, especially the northern kingdom, which was inhabited by Joseph's descendants.
Egypt: The country where the Israelites were slaves before God freed them through Moses.
Deliverance: The process of being set free from oppression or slavery, often linked with God's saving of his people.
Commandments: Laws and orders given by God to his people so that they may live righteously and in harmony with his will.

David: The king of Israel, who united the twelve tribes and established Jerusalem as the capital.
Warrior: A person who fights in armed conflicts, often as a member of an army or militia.
Enemies: Individuals, groups, or nations that are opponents or rivals and may pose a threat.
Victory: Success or triumph over an enemy or in a competition; winning a battle or conflict.
Spoils: Goods, resources, or possessions taken from an enemy as part of the victory in a war or conflict.
Hammanites: A people mentioned in 1 Chronicles 18, likely referring to the Arameans, who were enemies of Israel.
Gold: A precious metal of great value, used both for ornamentation and as currency.
Silver: Another precious metal that also holds value and is used for decoration and as money.
Bronze: An alloy of copper and tin, used in weapons, tools, and artworks.
Submission: Accepting the authority or control of others, often following a conflict or battle.

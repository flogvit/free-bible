Praise: A song or hymn that expresses gratitude and reverence to God for his greatness and goodness.
Shout: A loud expression of joy, enthusiasm, or thanksgiving, often directed toward God in prayer or praise.
Savior: A person or deity who saves or delivers people from sin, suffering, or oppression. In Christianity, Jesus Christ is the ultimate Savior.
Rock: A symbol of stability, strength, and protection, often used metaphorically to describe God and his care for his people.
Heart: Used metaphorically to represent emotions, intentions, and will in a person or in God.
Test: To put someone or something to a test in order to evaluate their faithfulness, obedience, or endurance, as when God tested the people of Israel in the wilderness.
Wilderness: A dry and inhospitable landscape, often used as a symbol of trials, temptations, and spiritual barrenness in the Bible.
Meribah: The name of a place in the wilderness where the Israelites complained about lack of water and challenged Moses and God's authority.
Massah: Another name for Meribah, also associated with the Israelites' doubt and complaints during their journey through the wilderness.

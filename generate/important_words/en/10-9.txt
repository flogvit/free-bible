Mephibosheth: A son of Jonathan and grandson of King Saul, who was lame in both feet. He was taken into King David's household.
David: The king of Israel and a central figure in 2 Samuel, known for his piety, skill as a warrior and musician, and as the author of many psalms.
Jonathan: King Saul's eldest son and a close friend of David. Jonathan died in battle together with his father.
Saul: Israel's first king and father of Jonathan. He was rejected by God because of his disobedience and replaced by David as king.
Ziba: A servant of Mephibosheth who was appointed to manage Mephibosheth's estates and assist him after he was taken into David's household.
Lo-debar: A town east of the Jordan River where Mephibosheth lived before being brought to David.
Grace: God's unconditional love and favor. In this context, it refers to David's kindness and grace toward Mephibosheth, despite his connection to Saul's house.

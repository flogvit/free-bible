Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks with God's will and morality. It creates a spiritual separation between people and God.
Righteousness: God's moral standard that people are called to follow. It involves acting rightly and justly in relation to both God and other people.
Salvation: God's grace and mercy that saves people from the consequences of sin and restores their relationship with God.
Spirit: God's invisible and immaterial presence that gives power, guidance, and comfort to people.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation of people's actions and thoughts, and the dispensing of reward or punishment based on their adherence to His standards.
Redeemer: A term for Jesus Christ, who according to Christian faith sacrificed his life to free people from sin and death and restore their relationship with God.
Truth: That which is in agreement with reality and God's revelation. Truth is a central concept in the Bible and is the foundation of a right relationship with God and other people.

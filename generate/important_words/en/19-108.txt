God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Praise song: A song or poem that expresses praise, gratitude, and honor to God.
Enemies: Persons or groups who oppose or are in conflict with someone, often used to describe those who resist God's will.
Victory: To achieve a triumph or overcome resistance, often used in a religious context to symbolize God's power and protection.
Help: Support or assistance given to someone, especially in the form of divine intervention from God.
Salvation: Being saved from sin, evil, or destruction, often through God's grace and mercy.
Faithfulness: Loyalty and trust in God and His promises, as well as the ability to rely on His protection and guidance.

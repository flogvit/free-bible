Penalty: God's reaction to human disobedience and sin, often involving suffering and difficulties.
Israel: An Old Testament people with whom God made a covenant; in the book of Amos, it refers to the northern tribes of the kingdom of Israel.
Lord: A title used for God, emphasizing his sovereignty and authority over all.
About repentance: A change of heart and mind where a person turns away from sin and toward God.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and uprightly, both in relation to God and other people.
Judgment: God's fair evaluation and handling of human behavior, especially in relation to sin and disobedience.
Famine: Lack of food as a result of God's punishment and warning to the people to turn from their sins.
Drought: Lack of rain and water, leading to poor harvests and suffering; also part of God's punishment and warnings.
Plague: A contagious and deadly disease that affects humans and animals, which in the book of Amos is part of God's punishment.

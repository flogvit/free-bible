Canaan: The area that God promised to give to the people of Israel, which Joshua and his army conquered.
Caleb: One of the twelve spies whom Moses sent to explore Canaan. He remained faithful to God and was rewarded with a portion of land in Canaan.
Hebron: A city in Canaan that Caleb received as an inheritance for his faithfulness to God.
Anakim: A people who lived in Canaan and were known for being large and strong warriors.
Joshua: The leader of the people of Israel after Moses' death, and he led them into Canaan.
Inheritance: A gift or property passed from one generation to another, often used in a biblical context to describe the lands God gave to the tribes of Israel.

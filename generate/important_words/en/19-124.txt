Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, the almighty Creator.
Name: The identification or designation of God, expressing his character and attributes.
Heaven: The spiritual realm where God dwells and reigns, often associated with the place of divine presence.
Earth: The physical world we live in, including landmasses and all life upon them.
Bird's nest: An image used to illustrate the protection and safety that God gives his people.
Trap: A snare or device for catching, here used metaphorically to represent dangers or difficulties people face.
Break: To shatter or destroy, here used to describe how God helps his people escape from traps and dangers.
Help: Support, guidance, or assistance that God gives his people in their distress and trials.

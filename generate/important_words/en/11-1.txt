Solomon: Son of King David and Bathsheba, who is chosen to succeed his father as king over Israel.
Adonijah: Another son of King David who attempts to seize the throne before Solomon, but ultimately fails.
David: The king of Israel who is near death at the beginning of 1 Kings; he gives advice to his son Solomon on how to rule the kingdom.
Bathsheba: David's wife and Solomon's mother, who helps ensure that Solomon becomes king.
Nathan: A prophet who supports Solomon and warns David about Adonijah's plans to take the throne.
Zadok: A priest who also supports Solomon and helps install him as king.
Abishag: A young woman who serves and cares for the aging King David, but has no sexual contact with him.

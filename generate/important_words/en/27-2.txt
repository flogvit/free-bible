Nebuchadnezzar: The king of Babylon who had a dream he wanted interpreted in the book of Daniel 2.
Dream: A series of images, ideas, and feelings that arise in the mind during sleep. Nebuchadnezzar's dream is central to chapter 2.
Statue: A large image of a person or object. The dream contains a statue made of different metals symbolizing various kingdoms.
Head of Gold: Represents the Babylonian kingdom led by Nebuchadnezzar, the first kingdom in the dream.
Chest and Arms of Silver: Symbolize the powerful Medo-Persian kingdom that followed Babylon.
Thighs of Bronze: Represent the Greek kingdom under Alexander the Great.
Legs of Iron: Symbolize the Roman kingdom known for its military strength.
Feet of Clay: Signify a fragile and divided kingdom that comes after the Roman kingdom.
The Stone: A stone cut out without human hands that crushes the statue and becomes a mountain. This represents God's eternal kingdom.
Daniel: A prophet and advisor in the Babylonian kingdom who interprets Nebuchadnezzar's dream through divine insight.

Job: The main character in the Book of Job, a righteous man who undergoes great trials and suffering.
Trials: Tests or challenges intended to strengthen faith and character.
Suffering: Pain, distress, or discomfort experienced by an individual.
Satan: An evil spiritual being who opposes God and tempts people to sin.
God's permission: God's acceptance or consent for something to happen, even if it may be painful or difficult.
Righteousness: Acting in a morally correct way and in accordance with God's will.
Complaint: Expressing dissatisfaction, grief, or suffering, often directed toward God.
Friends: Three individuals who visit Job to comfort him but end up accusing him of having sinned.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience effectively and wisely.
God's answer: God's response to Job's complaints, in which He highlights His own power and wisdom.

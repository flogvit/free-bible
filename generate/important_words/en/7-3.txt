Ehud: A left-handed judge from the tribe of Benjamin who killed the Moabite king Eglon and freed Israel from Moab's oppression.
Deborah: A prophetess and judge in Israel who, together with Barak, led the Israelites to victory over the Canaanites and their general Sisera.
Barak: Israel's military leader who, at Deborah's urging, gathered an army and defeated the Canaanites under Sisera.
Sisera: General of the Canaanite king Jabin who was killed by Ja'el after his army was defeated by Barak and Deborah.
Ja'el: A woman from the Kenite tribe who killed Sisera by driving a tent peg through his head while he slept.
Gideon: A judge and military leader who defeated the Midianites and Amalekites, and then reduced his army to show that it was God who gave the victory.
Midianites: A nomadic tribe that invaded and oppressed Israel, but were ultimately defeated by Gideon and his army.
Samson: A Nazirite and judge in Israel known for his immense strength. He fought against the Philistines and died with them by pulling down a temple.
Delilah: A Philistine woman who became Samson's lover and betrayed him by revealing the secret of his strength.
Philistines: A people who lived in the area around the Gaza Strip and were Israel's enemies. Samson fought against them throughout his time as judge.

Lord's Angel: A divine messenger sent by God to lead and protect the people of Israel.
Covenant: An agreement between God and humans, often linked with promises and obligations from both parties.
Ark of the Covenant: A holy chest that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, a symbol of God's presence and covenant with Israel.
Sabbatical Year: Every seventh year when the land was to lie fallow and not be cultivated, so that it could rest and the poor could harvest what grew on its own.
Festivals: Religious celebrations marking special events or times in the Jewish year, such as Passover, Pentecost, and the Feast of Tabernacles.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and fairly, both toward God and other people.
Destruction: Complete devastation or extermination, here used concerning God's judgment over hostile nations.
False Witness: To lie or give false information in a legal case, which is strictly forbidden in the books of Moses.

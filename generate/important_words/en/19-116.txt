God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Love: A deep devotion, care, and dedication toward someone or something.
Death: The end of life; when an organism ceases to exist biologically.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and mercy toward people.
The Lord: Another name for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Tears: Drops of fluid that come from the eyes, often as a result of emotions such as sorrow or joy.
Salvation: The act of being saved or delivered from sin, evil, or danger.
Servant: A person who performs services or work for another, especially in a subordinate position.
Promise: A formal declaration or assurance that one will do something or that something will happen.
Temple: A holy place for worship and prayer dedicated to a deity, often used in connection with Jewish and Christian faith.

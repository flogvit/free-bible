Laban: A person in Genesis, father of Rachel and Leah and father-in-law to Jacob.
Jacob: One of the patriarchs in the Bible, son of Isaac and Rebekah, and father of the twelve tribes of Israel.
Rachel: Jacob's favorite wife and mother of Joseph and Benjamin, younger sister of Leah.
Leah: Jacob's first wife and mother of six of his twelve sons, older sister of Rachel.
Rodentree: A tree mentioned in Genesis 31, used by Jacob to influence the livestock offspring.
Covenant: An agreement between two parties, often with religious significance, as in this case between Jacob and Laban.
Heap-stone: A type of stone mentioned in Genesis 31, likely used as a boundary marker or witness in the covenant between Jacob and Laban.
Gilead: A geographical area east of the Jordan River, where Jacob and Laban made their covenant.

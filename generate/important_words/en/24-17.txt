Sin: An act or state of breaking God's laws, often linked to moral evil and rebellion against God.
Heart: In this context, it refers to the inner being of a person, including thoughts, feelings, and will. It is also described as the source of human sin and evil.
Curse: A negative consequence or punishment from God due to sin and disobedience.
Blessing: A positive consequence or reward from God for obedience and righteousness.
Trust: To rely on or have faith in someone, in this case God, and His promises and plans.
Lord: A title used to show respect and reverence for God, acknowledging His sovereignty and authority.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people in the Old Testament, which also refers to a nation and a geographical area.
Judah: One of Israel's twelve tribes and the name of the southern kingdom after the kingdom was divided.

Psalms: A collection of religious songs, prayers, and poems that form part of the Bible.
The Lord's house: A term referring to the temple in Jerusalem, a holy place for worship and sacrifices.
Hymn: A song or poem expressing gratitude and praise to God.
Strength: Power, courage, and endurance, both physical and spiritual.
Blessing: A favorable gift from God, which may include protection, grace, and prosperity.
Pilgrimage: A religious journey to a holy place, often sought for spiritual growth and closer connection with God.
Shield: A metaphor for God's protection and defense against evil and enemies.
Anointing: A ritual act in which oil is applied as a sign of consecration, blessing, or healing.

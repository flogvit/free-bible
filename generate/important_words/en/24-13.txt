Loincloth: A garment, like a belt or loincloth, which God instructed the prophet Jeremiah to use as a symbol of Israel's relationship with God and their coming destruction.
Jar: An earthenware container that Jeremiah was told to buy and hide the loincloth in. This symbolized how Israel's pride would be destroyed.
Pride: A negative trait that characterized the people of Israel and Judah, leading them to turn away from God and follow false gods.
Thirst: A metaphor for the spiritual emptiness and need for God's presence that arises when people turn away from Him.
Destruction: The punishment God warned about through Jeremiah, resulting from Israel's and Judah's disobedience and idolatry.
Exile: The final outcome of God's judgment on Israel and Judah, where the people were taken captive and deported to Babylon.

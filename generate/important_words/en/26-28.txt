King of Tyre: A ruler from the ancient city of Tyre, used as an allegory for human pride and sin.
Eden: A symbolic garden representing God's perfect creation, where Adam and Eve lived before the fall.
Cherub: A type of angel with high status in the heavenly hierarchy, often described as God's protectors or servants.
Holy mountain: A symbol of God's divine presence and sovereignty, often associated with the spiritual realm.
Beauty: An attribute attributed to God's creation and beings, but which can lead to pride and sin when misused.
Pride: A sin involving excessive arrogance, self-satisfaction, and lack of humility, leading to God's judgment.
Wisdom: A virtue involving deep understanding, insight, and righteousness, but which can be corrupted by pride and selfishness.
Judgment: God's just punishment for sin and injustice, expressed through prophetic warnings and actions.

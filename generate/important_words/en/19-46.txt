God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Refuge: A safe place or protection, often used to describe God's care and protection for people.
Strength: Power or ability, both physical and spiritual; here it refers to God's power and capacity to help and support believers.
River: A natural flow of water; in Psalm 46 it symbolizes God's presence and life-giving power.
City: A place where people live and interact; in this context it represents God's kingdom and the community of believers.
Nations: Groups of people, often defined by nationality, culture, or ethnicity; here it refers to all nations and peoples on earth.
Lord: A title for God that emphasizes his authority and sovereignty over all creation.
Hosts: Large groups of angels, often associated with God's power and protection.
Peace: Absence of conflict and unrest; in this context it means a deep inner calm and harmony that comes from trust in God.

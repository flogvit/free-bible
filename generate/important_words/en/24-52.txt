Jerusalem: The capital city of the kingdom of Judah, which was destroyed by the Babylonians under Nebuchadnezzar II.
Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon, who conquered and destroyed Jerusalem and led the Jews into exile.
Exile: The condition of being forced to leave one's homeland and live in a foreign land, as the Jews experienced after the fall of Jerusalem.
Temple: The holy place in Jerusalem where the Jews performed their religious ceremonies and sacrifices. Destroyed by the Babylonians.
Zedekiah: The last king of Judah, who was captured by Nebuchadnezzar and taken to Babylon.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, known for its power and cultural influence. The place where the Jews were led into exile.
Jehoiachin: Former king of Judah, who also was captured by Nebuchadnezzar and taken to Babylon.
Evil-Merodach: Son of Nebuchadnezzar and king of Babylon, who released Jehoiachin from captivity and treated him with respect.

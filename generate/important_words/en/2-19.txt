Sinai: A mountainous region on the Sinai Peninsula where God gave Moses the Ten Commandments.
Moses: The prophet and leader of the Israelites who led them out of slavery in Egypt and received the Ten Commandments from God.
Israelites: The Hebrew people, descendants of Jacob, who were freed from slavery in Egypt under the leadership of Moses.
Covenant: A sacred and binding agreement between God and humans, often involving promises and conditions.
Third day: The specific day when God would come down on Mount Sinai and speak to Moses and the Israelites.
Holy: Dedicated or set apart for God; something that is spiritually pure and worthy.
Boundary: A limit or barrier that separates the holy area around Mount Sinai from the common or profane.

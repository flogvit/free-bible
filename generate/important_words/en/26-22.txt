God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who conveys God's message and judgment over Israel and other nations.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks with God's will and morality, leading to separation from God.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment for human sins and their rejection of His commands and guidance.
Purification: The process of removing sin and impurities to restore purity and holiness.
Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel and a central symbol of God's people and their relationship with Him.
The sword: A symbol of God's judgment and punishment upon the people for their sins and rebellion.
Bloodguilt: The sin associated with having killed another person, either directly or indirectly.
The crucible: An image used to describe God's trials and purification process for His people.

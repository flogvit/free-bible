Plagues: Unpleasant and destructive events that God sends over Egypt to force Pharaoh to release the Israelites from slavery.
Pharaoh: The ruler of ancient Egypt, who in this context refuses to let the Israelites leave.
Moses: The prophet and leader of the Israelites who serves as God's spokesperson and conveys His message to Pharaoh.
Aaron: Moses' brother who helps him carry out God's commands and confront Pharaoh.
Livestock: Domestic animals kept by Egyptians and Israelites for food, labor, and religious ceremonies. Affected by several plagues.
Hail: One of the ten plagues, where large chunks of ice fell from the sky, destroying crops and injuring people and animals.
Frogs: Another plague, in which a vast number of frogs invaded Egypt, creating chaos and discomfort.
Pestilence: A contagious disease that struck the Egyptians' livestock, causing great destruction and loss of resources.
Swarms: A plague where huge numbers of flies invaded Egypt, causing discomfort and sickness.
Darkness: One of the ten plagues, in which Egypt was covered by thick darkness for three days, causing fear and confusion.

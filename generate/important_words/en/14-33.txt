Manasse: An evil king of Judah who reigned for 55 years and did what was evil in the Lord's sight, including practicing idolatry and sorcery.
Amon: Manasseh's son who succeeded him as king of Judah and continued to do what was evil in the Lord's sight.
Omri: King of Israel whom Manasseh was compared to because of his wickedness and idolatry.
Assyrians: An ancient empire and people from Mesopotamia who invaded and conquered parts of Israel and Judah. Manasseh was taken captive and led away to Babylon by the Assyrians.
Babylon: An ancient city and kingdom in Mesopotamia where Manasseh was held as a prisoner.
Repentance: The feeling of sorrow and regret over past actions. Manasseh repented of his sins and prayed to God for forgiveness while he was in captivity.
Forgiveness: God's grace and mercy toward those who repent of their sins and seek reconciliation. Manasseh was forgiven and restored as king after he repented.
Reformation: The process of changing or improving something. Manasseh carried out a reformation in Judah by removing idols and restoring the worship of the Lord.

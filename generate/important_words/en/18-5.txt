Job: The main character in the book of Job, a righteous man who experiences great trials and suffering.
Satan: An evil spiritual being who challenges God and receives permission to test Job.
Trials: Difficult situations or sufferings that people may face in life.
Patience: The ability to endure and remain faithful under difficult circumstances.
Wisdom: Deep knowledge and understanding, often connected with moral and ethical questions.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with moral and ethical principles; goodness and honesty.
Blessings: Gifts and benefits given by God, often as a reward for faithfulness and obedience.

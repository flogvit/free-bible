Here are some important words from Psalm 113 and their explanations in Norwegian Bokmål:
LORD: The name often used for God in the Old Testament, the almighty Creator.
Praise: To express admiration or gratitude for something, especially toward God for His deeds and character.
Servant: A person dedicated to serving or carrying out tasks for another, here referring to believers who serve God.
Sunrise: The time when the sun rises above the horizon in the morning, symbolizing the beginning of a new day.
Sunset: The time when the sun sinks below the horizon in the evening, marking the end of the day.
Name: A designation that identifies and represents a person; here it refers to God's name and character.
High: An adjective describing something far above the ground or above a certain level, used here to describe God's position and greatness.
Low: An adjective describing something near the ground or below a certain level, used here to describe human position compared with God.
Widow: A woman whose spouse has died and who has not remarried, often seen as a vulnerable group in society.
Fatherless: A person who has lost their father or does not have a father to take responsibility for them, also regarded as a vulnerable group.

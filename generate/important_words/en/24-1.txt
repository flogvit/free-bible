Call: God's invitation or commission to a person to carry out a specific service or task.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who was called by God to deliver His message to the people of Israel.
Prophecy: A divinely inspired statement or message, often concerning future events, given through a prophet.
The Nations: Various peoples and lands, especially those surrounding Israel at that time.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment over sin and evil, often expressed through destruction or suffering.
Holy: Dedicated to God; something or someone set apart from the ordinary and assigned for divine use.
Lord: A name often used to refer to God in the Old Testament, especially in His role as Israel's God and protector.

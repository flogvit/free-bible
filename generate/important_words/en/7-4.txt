Deborah: A prophet and judge in Israel who led the Israelites to victory over the Canaanites.
Barak: An Israeli military leader who, together with Deborah, defeated the army of the Canaanites under Sisera.
Sisera: The commander of the Canaanite forces who was defeated by Barak and Deborah. He was killed by Jael while seeking shelter in her tent.
Jael: A woman from the Kenite people who killed Sisera by driving a tent peg through his head while he slept.
Canaanites: A people group who lived in Canaan, an area later known as Israel and Palestine. They were enemies of the Israelites in the book of Judges 4.
Kishon River: A river in Canaan where the battle between the Israelites and the Canaanites took place. The river played a key role in the Israelite victory.
Judge: A leader in ancient Israel who held both political and religious authority. Deborah was such a judge.
Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God. Deborah was also a prophet.

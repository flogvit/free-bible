God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
The Deep: The great, endless sea or waters, often associated with chaos and darkness.
North: The direction north, but also used to describe the highest heaven where God dwells.
Pillars: A symbolic reference to the supports that hold up the earth or the heavens, representing God's power and stability.
Spirit: God's invisible power and presence, which gives life and movement to creation.
Heaven: The upper part of the universe where God dwells, as well as angels and other heavenly beings.
Clouds: A representation of God's presence and power, especially when they surround his throne.
Horizon: The boundary between earth and heaven, where sky and land meet and are separated from each other.
Leviathan: A symbol of evil and Satan, also called Leviathan, a sea monster representing chaos and destruction.

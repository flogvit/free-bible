Lord: A title for God, the almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.
Vision: A revelation or sight given by God, often used to convey divine insight or message.
Children of Israel: An expression referring to the people of Israel, descendants of the patriarch Jacob, also called Israel.
Feast: A religious celebration or festival held in honor of God, often with special rituals and ceremonies.
Remnant: A small remaining group of people, often used in prophetic texts to describe the faithful who survive God's judgment.
Restore: To bring something back to its original state or condition, often used regarding God's promises of renewal and restoration.
The fallen hut of David: A metaphor referring to the destroyed kingdom of Israel, with David as a former king and founder of the kingdom.
Nations: Large groups of people united by common culture, language, or territory, often referred to as lands in biblical texts.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human actions, often manifested through natural disasters, war, or other events.

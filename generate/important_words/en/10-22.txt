God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Savior: A person or divine being who saves or delivers someone from danger, suffering, or sin.
Enemies: Individuals or groups who are opponents, competitors, or in conflict with one another.
Establish: To make something stronger, more secure, or better protected, especially regarding defense.
Rock: A symbol of strength, stability, and protection, often used as a metaphor for God in the Bible.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, also known as Yahweh or Jehovah.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, respect, and honor toward God, often through song, prayer, or worship.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with moral laws, ethics, and justice; connected to God's nature and human behavior.
Discipline: Training, correction, or instruction given to improve or change behavior.

Solomon: The third king of Israel, known for his wisdom and the building of the temple in Jerusalem.
Hiram: King of Tyre, who helped Solomon obtain materials and craftsmen for the construction of the temple.
Cedar trees: Large, valuable trees from Lebanon, used in the building of the temple and other structures in Israel.
Construction work: The process of constructing or assembling a building, in this case the temple.
The Temple: A holy place for worship and sacrifices, built by Solomon in Jerusalem as a house for God's name.
Labor force: People who work on a specific task; here it refers to those who worked on the temple.
Quarries: Places where stone is extracted from the ground, used as building material for the temple.

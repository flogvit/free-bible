Rock: A solid, natural formation of stone, often used as a symbol of strength and protection in biblical literature.
Savior: A person or divine being who rescues or delivers others from danger, suffering, or sin. In the book of Psalms, this usually refers to God himself.
Lord: A title for God that expresses respect and submission; denotes God's sovereignty and authority over all creation.
Hear: A verb meaning to perceive sounds with the ears, but also to pay attention to and take heed of someone or something. In this context, it is a plea for God to listen to the prayer.
Prayer: Communication with God in which people express gratitude, reverence, concerns, and desires.
Inheritance: The transfer of property, rights, or privileges from one generation to another. In the book of Psalms, it can also refer to God's promises and blessings given to his people.
Intercession: The act of praying to God on behalf of others, especially for their well-being and protection.

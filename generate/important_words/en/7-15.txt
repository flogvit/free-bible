Samson: A biblical judge and national hero in Israel known for his supernatural strength, given by God.
Philistines: An ancient people who lived in the coastal regions of Canaan and were enemies of Israel during several periods.
Jawbone of an donkey: The weapon Samson used to kill a thousand Philistines, serving as a symbol of his divine strength and victory over the enemy.
Lehi: The place where Samson defeated the Philistines with the jawbone of a donkey; it means "jaw" in Hebrew.
En-hakkeshet: A miraculous spring that appeared after Samson prayed to God for help when he was thirsty following his battle against the Philistines.
Saul: A town mentioned in Judges 15, where Samson was held captive by his own countrymen before being handed over to the Philistines.
TRAP: A technique or strategy used to deceive someone or cause them to be caught, as when the Philistines tried to capture Samson.

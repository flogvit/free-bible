Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to evaluate situations or people in a wise and reasonable manner.
Discipline: Training, instruction, or correction intended to teach and improve behavior.
Prudence: The quality of being wise and insightful, often linked to skill in handling complex situations.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and honestly, in line with moral and ethical standards.
Humility: The trait of being modest and not having excessive pride or self-confidence.
Speech: The way we communicate and express ourselves through language and words.
Friendship: A close relationship between two or more people based on mutual devotion, trust, and support.
Strife: A conflict, disagreement, or quarrel between individuals or groups.
Tongue: The manner in which one speaks, including word choice and tone, which can greatly affect how a message is received.

Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his authority and power.
Savior: One who saves or delivers from danger or destruction. In Psalm 144 it refers to God as the protector and deliverer of his people.
War: An armed conflict between two or more groups. In this context it refers to the battles the people of Israel had to fight against their enemies.
Enemies: Persons or groups who are opposed to or wish harm upon another person or group. In Psalm 144 it refers to the nations that threatened Israel.
Strength: Power, energy, or ability to carry out actions. In this context it refers to God's strength that helps people in their struggles.
Hand: A part of the human body used to grasp and manipulate objects. In this context it represents God's protective and helping hand.
Sword: A weapon with a long blade used for cutting and thrusting. In this context it symbolizes war and battle, and God's protection against enemies.
Praise: A song or poem that expresses gratitude, honor, and glory to God for his goodness and grace.
Prosperity: A state of material wealth and well-being. In Psalm 144 David asks that God would bless his people with prosperity and security.

Psalm: A poetic religious song or poem, often used in worship services and devotion.
Lord: A title for God expressing respect and reverence; denotes God's sovereignty and authority.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and granting them eternal life.
Enemies: Persons or forces opposed to God and His people; symbolize evil and resistance.
King: A leader with supreme authority in a kingdom; in Psalm 21 it refers both to an earthly king and to God as the ultimate King.
Strength: God's power and ability to protect, support, and help His people.
Life: The existence of humans and creatures; in a spiritual context it can also refer to eternal life with God.
Joy: A feeling of happiness and contentment that comes from being in God's presence and experiencing His blessings.
Bless: To ask for God's favor and protection over someone or something; also a way to express gratitude for God's gifts.

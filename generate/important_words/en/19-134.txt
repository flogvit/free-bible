Bless: To wish or pray for divine protection, happiness, and well-being for someone.
LORD: The name of God in the Old Testament, often written as "Yahweh" or "Jehovah". It represents the Almighty God in Judaism and Christianity.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem that holds great significance in both Judaism and Christianity. It symbolizes God's dwelling place and the holy site where God is worshiped.
Night: The period between sunset and sunrise, when it is dark. In this context, it refers to serving and worshiping God throughout the entire day and night.
Sanctuary: A sacred place dedicated to worship and prayer, often a building or temple.
Lift: To raise something upward; in this context, to lift hands in prayer and worship as a sign of reverence and devotion toward God.

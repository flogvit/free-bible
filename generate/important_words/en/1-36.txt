Esau: One of the twin sons of Isaac and Rebekah, brother to Jacob, and ancestor of the Edomites.
Edom: An ancient kingdom south of the Dead Sea, inhabited by descendants of Esau.
Se'ir: A mountainous region in Edom where Esau and his descendants settled.
Patriarchs: Founders of a people group or nation; here it refers to Esau and his sons.
Canaanite: Related to Canaan, an area in the ancient Middle East that includes parts of today's Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and Jordan. Here it refers to women from Canaan whom Esau married.
Alvah: One of the sons of Esau and his descendants, leader of one of the Edomite clans.
Korah: Another son of Esau and his descendants, leader of one of the Edomite clans.
Hori: A native Seirite who was the son of Se'ir and ancestor of one of the Edomite clans.

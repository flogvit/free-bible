Judgment Day: A day of final judgment and punishment for humanity, where the righteous are rewarded and the unrighteous are punished.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem that symbolizes God's dwelling place and the spiritual center for the Jews.
The Lord: A title often used for God in the Old Testament, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
God's people: An expression referring to believers, especially the Israelites, who have a special covenant and relationship with God.
The nations: The term is used to describe all peoples and lands outside of Israel, which are often contrasted with God's people.
The war: A conflict between the nations and God's people, where God intervenes to protect his people and judge their enemies.
Valley of Jehoshaphat: A place mentioned in Joel 3, where God will gather the nations to hold judgment over them.
The harvest: An image symbolizing God's final judgment upon humanity, where the righteous and unrighteous are separated from one another.

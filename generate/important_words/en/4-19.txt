Purification water: Water used in ceremonies to cleanse people from sin and impurity.
Red heifer: A rare, red-colored young cow without blemish or defect, sacrificed according to God's instructions.
Ash: The remains left after burning the red heifer, used to make purification water.
Impurity: A state of ritual uncleanness resulting from contact with dead bodies or other unclean objects.
Seven days: The duration of the cleansing process for a person who has become ritually unclean.
Hyssop: A plant used in cleansing rituals to sprinkle purification water on people and objects.
Tent: The dwellings of the Israelites during their journey through the wilderness, which could also become unclean through contact with dead bodies.

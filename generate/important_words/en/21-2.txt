Emptiness: A feeling of meaninglessness and futility, describing life's apparent lack of lasting value and purpose.
Earthly: Related to the material and worldly, as opposed to the spiritual or heavenly.
Pleasure: Pleasant experiences and joys, often associated with physical and sensory delights.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and apply knowledge, experience, and insight in an effective and wise manner.
Work: Physical or mental effort undertaken to produce or achieve something; can also refer to occupations and daily duties.
Time: A continuous progression where events occur in sequence from the past, through the present, into the future.
Fleetingness: The quality of being short-lived, transient, and perishable, implying that everything has a beginning and an end.
Fear of God: An attitude of awe, reverence, and obedience toward God, which is the foundation for a moral and meaningful life.
Judgment: God's righteous assessment and decision regarding human actions and their eternal destiny.

Hannah: A woman who prayed earnestly to God for a child and promised to dedicate the child to God's service.
Elkanah: Hannah's husband, a devout man who supported Hannah in her prayer to God.
Peninnah: Elkanah's other wife, who mocked Hannah for her barrenness.
Eli: The high priest at the temple in Shiloh, who ultimately blessed Hannah's prayer for a child.
Samuel: The son born to Hannah and Elkanah, who was dedicated to God's service and became an important prophet and judge in Israel.
Prayer: A form of communication between people and God, where they express their desires, gratitude, or concerns.
Vow: A commitment or declaration that something will be done, especially in a religious context.
Service: Work or tasks performed for God or within a religious setting.

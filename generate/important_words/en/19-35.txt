Enemies: Persons or forces who stand against or are opponents of a person, often referred to in Psalms 35 as those who oppose the psalmist and God.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, the almighty Creator, who helps and protects believers.
Righteousness: The concept used to describe acting rightly and morally, in accordance with God's will and commandments.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from their sins and punishment, and giving them eternal life.
Witnesses: Persons who give testimony or confirmation of events or actions, often used in Psalms 35 to describe false witnesses who spread lies about the psalmist.
Shield: A symbol of protection and defense, often used to describe God's role as protector of believers.
Praise: An expression of gratitude and honor to God through song, music, or prayer.

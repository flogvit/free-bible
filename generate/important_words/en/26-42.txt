Temple: A sacred building dedicated to God, where worship and religious ceremonies take place.
Measurements: Sizes and dimensions that describe the temple structure and its rooms in detail.
Chambers: Small rooms in the temple, used for various purposes such as storage or for priests to rest.
Outer wall: The outermost wall surrounding the entire temple, marking the boundary between holy and common areas.
Priests: Religious leaders who perform ceremonies and rituals, and are responsible for maintaining temple service.
Holy Place: The most sacred room in the temple, usually reserved for God's presence and worship.
Offering: Gifts and actions given to God as a way to show gratitude, reverence, and loyalty.
Entrance: The door or passage that provides access to the temple or its various rooms.

Samson: One of Israel's judges, known for his supernatural strength that came from his long hair.
Philistines: A people in Canaan who were enemies of the Israelites and controlled parts of the land at that time.
Delilah: A Philistine woman whom Samson loved and who betrayed him by getting him to reveal the source of his strength.
Pillar: An architectural structure that supports a building; Samson collapsed two pillars in the Philistines' temple and killed many enemies along with himself.
Nazarite vow: A religious vow that some Israelites took, which included abstaining from alcohol, avoiding contact with dead bodies, and not cutting one's hair.
Hair: The source of Samson's strength; he lost his power when Delilah had it cut off.
Blindness: The condition Samson ended up in after the Philistines captured and blinded him.
Vengeance: The act Samson carried out against the Philistines by destroying their temple and killing many of them along with himself.

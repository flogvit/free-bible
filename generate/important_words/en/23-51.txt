Lord's arm: God's power and strength that he uses to carry out his will and protect his people.
Comfort: To give support, encouragement, and hope to someone who is suffering or in distress.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and justly, which God requires of his people and which he himself practices.
Salvation: God's act of saving humanity from sin and death by offering eternal life through faith in Jesus Christ.
Zion: Another name for Jerusalem, often used symbolically to represent God's people and his kingdom.
Wilderness: A dry and inhospitable landscape, often used as a picture of spiritual barrenness and suffering.
Joy: A feeling of happiness and contentment that comes from knowing and following God.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his sovereignty and power.
Restoration: The restoration of something that has been lost or damaged, especially in relation to God's people and their relationship with him.

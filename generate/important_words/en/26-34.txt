Shepherd: A leader or spiritual guide who has the responsibility to care for and protect their flock, often used as a metaphor for God's care for His people.
Flock: Members of God's people, compared to sheep in a herd, symbolizing their dependence on God and their shepherd for protection and guidance.
Lost: Describes those who have wandered away from God's path and need help to return to righteousness and salvation.
Righteousness: A concept that involves moral correctness, integrity, and obedience to God's laws.
Punishment: The consequences resulting from sin and disobedience toward God, including both physical and spiritual suffering.
Restoration: The process by which God brings His people back to righteousness and healing after periods of sin and punishment.
David: King David from the Old Testament, here used as a prophecy about the coming Messiah, who will be an ideal shepherd for God's people.

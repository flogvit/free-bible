Land: The area or territory being referred to, especially in connection with God's promises to his people.

Covenant: An agreement or commitment between two parties, especially between God and his people.
Exile: The state of being taken as a captive or slave, often used in connection with the people of Israel in captivity.
Restoration: The process of bringing something back to its original condition, such as restoring a people to their land after exile.
Hope: An expectation that something positive will happen in the future, especially in connection with God's promises and plans for his people.

Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, the almighty Creator.
Throne: A symbol of royal authority and power, used here to describe God's sovereign rule over the world.
Firm: Describes something solid, stable, and unshakable. In this context, it refers to God's kingdom and his unchanging nature.
Rivers: Large streams of water that represent life and power. In Psalm 93, the rivers symbolize God's overwhelming strength and ability to bring order out of chaos.
Waves: The waves of the sea or rivers, representing instability, chaos, and threats. Here it refers to challenges and opposition that God overcomes.
Holy: Something set apart from the ordinary and dedicated to God. In this context, it refers to God's purity and holiness, which sets him apart from everything else.

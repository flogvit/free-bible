David: A young shepherd who later becomes king of Israel and a central figure in 1 Samuel.
Saul: The first king of Israel, who hunts David because he is jealous of his success and fears that he will take the throne.
Ziph: A wilderness region southeast of Hebron where David hides from Saul.
Abner: Commander and cousin of King Saul, who protects and supports him in the pursuit of David.
Ahithophel: A wise counselor who first serves Saul and later David.
Nabal: A rich man who refuses to help David and his men, leading to conflict.
Abigail: Nabal's wise and beautiful wife who prevents a bloody conflict between David and Nabal by giving them food and drink.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.

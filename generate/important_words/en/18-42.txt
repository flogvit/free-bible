Job: The main character in the Book of Job, a righteous man who endures many trials and losses.
Satan: An evil spiritual being who challenges Job and his faith in God by inflicting suffering upon him.
Trials: Events or situations that test a person's character, patience, or faith.
Righteous: Describes a person who is morally upright and honest in their behavior and attitudes.
Restoration: The process of regaining something that has been lost or destroyed, such as health, property, or relationships.
Blessing: A favor or gift from God that brings happiness, success, or prosperity.

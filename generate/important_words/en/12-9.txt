Jehu: An Israelite military commander who was anointed king of Israel by God's command to destroy the house of Ahab and Jezebel.
Anointed: The act of applying oil to someone, often to consecrate them for a special service or position, in this case as king.
Ahab: The seventh king of Israel who ruled from Samaria and was married to Jezebel. He is known for his idolatry and injustice.
Jezebel: Queen of Israel, wife of King Ahab. She was known for her wickedness, idolatry, and persecution of God's prophets.
Prophet: A person who receives messages from God and delivers them to the people. Prophets played an important role in Israel's history.
Nabot: An Israelite who owned a vineyard next to Ahab's palace. Ahab wanted to buy the vineyard, but Nabot refused. Jezebel then conspired to have him killed so that Ahab could take the vineyard.
Jezreel: A city in northern Israel where much of the action in 2 Kings 9 takes place, including the killings of Joram, Ahaziah, and Jezebel.
Joram: Son of Ahab and Jezebel; he was king of Israel before Jehu was anointed king and killed him.
Ahaziah: King of Judah who was related to Ahab and Jezebel through his mother Athaliah. He was also killed by Jehu.
God's judgment: God's righteous punishment for sin and evil, especially in relation to the family of Ahab and Jezebel as a result of their idolatry and wickedness.

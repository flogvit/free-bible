Rehoboam: Son of King Solomon and successor as king over Israel. He ruled over the kingdom of Judah.
Shishak: An Egyptian pharaoh who invaded and plundered Jerusalem during Rehoboam's reign.
Jerusalem: The capital city of ancient Israel and Judah, an important religious and political center.
The house of the Lord: Also known as Solomon's temple, a holy place for the Jews where they worshiped God.
The prophet Shemaiah: A prophet who delivered God's message to King Rehoboam and the leaders in Judah.
To humble oneself: To submit or show respect and reverence, especially toward God.
God's anger: His strong displeasure and wrath, often directed at people who have sinned or acted unjustly.

Prayer: A communication with God, where one asks for help, guidance, or gives thanks for what has been received.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or wish to harm a person, often used in a spiritual context.
Grace: God's undeserved love and mercy toward humanity, forgiveness of sin, and salvation.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and death, and giving them eternal life through faith in Jesus Christ.
Lord: A title showing respect and submission, often used for God as the highest authority.
Praise song: A song or expression of praise and honor to God for His greatness and goodness.
Sin: Actions, thoughts, or attitudes that break God's will and law, leading to separation from God.
Repentance: A feeling of sorrow and regret for one's sins, accompanied by a desire to change and seek forgiveness from God.

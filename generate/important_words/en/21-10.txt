Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a reasonable and prudent way to make good decisions and act rightly.
Foolishness: A lack of reason, wisdom, or judgment that leads to poor choices and actions.
Work: The effort and activities people carry out to achieve a goal, often related to their profession or daily life.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly in accordance with moral and ethical norms, as well as treating others fairly and with equal worth.
Injustice: Actions that violate the principle of justice and lead to unfair treatment of others.
Patience: The ability to wait calmly and perseveringly without becoming irritated or frustrated, even in difficult or challenging situations.
Impatience: The inability to wait calmly and perseveringly, which can lead to irritation, frustration, and impulsive actions.
Happiness: A feeling of well-being, joy, and contentment that comes from living a meaningful and good life.
Frustration: The feeling of dissatisfaction, irritation, and disappointment that arises when one fails to reach their goals or fulfill their desires.

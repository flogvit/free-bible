Love: A deep devotion and care for someone, used in the book of Hosea 3 to describe God's love for Israel.
Israel: A people and a nation that according to the Bible is God's chosen one. Hosea uses the marriage between himself and his unfaithful wife as an image of the relationship between God and Israel.
Unfaithfulness: Breaking the promise of loyalty to a partner, often through sexual relationships with others. In the book of Hosea 3 it symbolizes Israel's turning away from God by worshiping other gods.
Redemption: The act of buying back or ransoming something or someone. Hosea buys back his unfaithful wife, which symbolizes God's willingness to forgive and restore His relationship with Israel.
Faithfulness: Holding fast to promises and being loyal to someone, especially in a marriage. God calls Israel to return to Him and remain faithful to Him.
Longing: A strong desire or need for something or someone. Hosea describes Israel's longing for God and their future reunion with Him.

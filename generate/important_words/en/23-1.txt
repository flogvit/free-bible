Sin: Actions or thoughts that break God's commands and morality, leading to spiritual separation from God.
Discipline: Correction or training given by God to guide people toward righteousness and obedience.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, emphasizing his authority and power over creation.
Zion: A mountain in Jerusalem, often used as a symbol for God's people and their spiritual home.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human actions, especially those that are unjust or evil.
Righteousness: The quality of being morally right and just, in harmony with God's will and commands.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy toward people, despite their sins and shortcomings.
Repentance: To change one's behavior and attitude by turning away from sin and seeking God's grace and forgiveness.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from the consequences of sin and giving them eternal life with him.

Saul: The first king of Israel who was chosen by God through the prophet Samuel.
Samuel: A prophet and judge in Israel who anointed both Saul and David to be kings.
Witch: A person who claims to have supernatural powers and abilities, such as communicating with spirits.
Endor: A village in Israel where Saul visited a witch to contact the spirit of Samuel.
Spirit: An immaterial being, often associated with a human soul or divine beings.
Necromancy: The practice of communicating with the dead to gain information or guidance.
Philistines: An ancient people group who were enemies of Israel and lived in Canaan.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.

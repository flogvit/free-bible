Ammonites: A people who lived east of the Jordan River, often in conflict with Israel.
Hanun: King of the Ammonites, son of Nahash, who insulted David's envoys.
David: The second king of Israel, a central figure in the Old Testament.
Joab: David's military commander and nephew, played an important role in many of David's military campaigns.
Syria: A country northeast of Israel, allied with the Ammonites against David and his army.
Abishai: Joab's brother and one of David's most powerful warriors, helped lead Israel's army against the Ammonites and Syrians.
Nahash: Former king of the Ammonites, father of Hanun, had a good relationship with David before his death.
Army: An organized military force consisting of soldiers and officers who fight for a nation or a leader.

Temple Gatekeepers: Persons who were responsible for protecting and preserving the sanctuary in the temple, as well as controlling access to it.
The Korahites: A group of Levites descended from Korah, a relative of Moses and Aaron. They had special duties related to temple service.
Merari: One of the three branches of the tribe of Levi, also known as the Merarites. They were responsible for certain parts of the tabernacle and later the temple.
Obed-Edom: A Levite and temple gatekeeper who housed the ark of the covenant in his home for three months before it was moved to Jerusalem.
Shift System: A method for organizing the work among temple workers by dividing them into groups that served in periods or shifts.
Casting Lots: A practice used to decide important matters or allocate responsibilities, based on the belief that God controlled the outcome.
Treasury Collectors: Persons who were responsible for collecting money and gifts for the temple and the priesthood.

Jacob: One of the patriarchs in the Bible and son of Isaac and Rebekah. He is also known as Israel after God changed his name.
Bethel: A city in Canaan, where Jacob had a meeting with God. The name means "house of God" in Hebrew.
Israel: The new name God gave to Jacob after a struggle with an angel. The name means "he who struggles with God" or "God struggles".
Rachel: Jacob's beloved wife and mother of Joseph and Benjamin. She died during the birth of Benjamin and was buried near Bethel.
Benjamin: The youngest of Jacob's twelve sons and Rachel's second son. His name means "son of my right hand" or "son of the south".
Ephraim: A city in Canaan where Jacob lived after leaving Bethel. The place is also known as Mamre and Kiriath-Arba.
Isaac: Jacob's father and son of Abraham and Sarah. He was one of the three patriarchs in the Bible and father to Esau and Jacob.

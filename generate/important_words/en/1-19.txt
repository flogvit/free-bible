Lot: Abraham's nephew who lived in Sodom and was saved by God before the city's destruction.
Sodom: A biblical city destroyed by God because of its sinful inhabitants.
Gomorrah: Another city destroyed together with Sodom by God for the same reason.
Angels: Heavenly beings sent by God to rescue Lot and his family and to destroy Sodom and Gomorrah.
Sin: Violation of God's commands and morality, which led to the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah.
Pillar of salt: What Lot's wife was turned into when she looked back at Sodom, against the angels' warning.
Cave: The place where Lot and his daughters sought refuge after the destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah.
Incest: Sexual relations between close relatives, which occurred between Lot and his daughters in order to continue their lineage.

Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his authority and power.
Shepherd: A person who looks after and cares for sheep. In Psalm 23, God is described as a shepherd who cares for people.
Green pastures: Lush and fertile areas where sheep can graze and rest. Symbolize God's care and provision of human needs.
Still waters: Calm and safe water, symbolizing God's peace and tranquility in life.
Paths of righteousness: The way that leads to justice and goodness, guided by God's principles and laws.
Valley of the shadow of death: Periods of trials, suffering, and difficulties in life. God is present and offers comfort and protection.
Evil: Wicked actions, thoughts, and intentions that oppose God's will and morality.
Table: A place where food and drink are served, representing God's abundant blessings and care.
Oil: A symbolic act of pouring oil on the head, representing anointing, healing, and blessing from God.
Cup: A vessel containing drink, symbolizing abundance and joy that God gives.
Goodness and mercy: God's love, grace, and compassion that follow a person throughout life.
House of the Lord forever: An expression of God's presence and eternal dwelling, where one can experience fellowship with God.

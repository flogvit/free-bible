Solomon: Son of King David and Bathsheba, chosen by God to build the temple in Jerusalem.
The Temple: A holy place for worshiping God, planned by King David and built by his son Solomon.
Lebanon: A country north of Israel, known for its cedar trees that were used to build the temple.
Cedar Trees: Large, fragrant trees with durable wood, brought from Lebanon to build the temple.
Hiram's Workers: Workers from King Hiram of Tyre who helped Solomon build the temple.
Bronze: Metal used in the construction of the temple, especially for making tools and decorations.
Peace: A time without war or conflict, which God promised David that Solomon would experience during his reign.
The Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator.

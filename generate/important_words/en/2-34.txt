Covenant: An agreement or bond between God and people, often involving promises and conditions.
Merciful: To show compassion, grace, and love, especially toward those who suffer or are in distress.
Gracious: To be generous and willing to forgive faults and shortcomings, as well as to give unearned gifts and blessings.
Patient: To have great patience and endurance, particularly when tolerating the mistakes and weaknesses of others.
Truth: That which is correct, real, and reliable; in a religious context, it can also refer to God’s unchanging and eternal nature.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks God’s will and moral law, leading to separation from God and other people.
Punishment: A consequence or response to sin or an unjust act, often given by God to correct the situation and teach the guilty party.
Stone tablets: Flat stone slabs on which God wrote the Ten Commandments, received by Moses on Mount Sinai as part of the covenant between God and the people of Israel.

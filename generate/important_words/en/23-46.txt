Babel: An ancient city in Mesopotamia, often associated with pride and rebellion against God.
Idols: Objects or statues that people worship as gods, but which are false and powerless.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, emphasizing his sovereignty and authority.
Savior: The one who saves or delivers; in this case, it refers to God as Israel's protector and rescuer.
Jacob: The patriarch of the twelve tribes of Israel and grandson of Abraham. Also known as Israel.
Bear: In this context, it means that God supports and helps his people through life's trials.
Old age: Refers to a person's advanced years, symbolizing experience and wisdom.
Knowledge: God's ability to know all things, including the future and human thoughts and actions.

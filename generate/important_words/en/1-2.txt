Rest: A concept describing God's rest after six days of creation, symbolizing the completion of the work of creation.
Garden of Eden: A paradisiacal place created by God for Adam and Eve to live in, containing the tree of life and the tree of knowledge of good and evil.
Adam: The first man created by God from the dust of the ground, regarded as the ancestor of humanity.
Eve: The first woman created by God from Adam's rib, regarded as the mother of humanity.
Tree of Life: A tree in the Garden of Eden representing eternal life and God's blessing.
Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil: A tree in the Garden of Eden representing human moral consciousness and the ability to distinguish between good and evil.
Covenant: An agreement or pact between God and people, in this case a prohibition against eating from the tree of knowledge of good and evil.

Jerusalem: The capital city of ancient Israel and Judah, a central place for worship and political power.
Nehemiah: A Jewish leader who helped rebuild Jerusalem and strengthen the community after the exile in Babylon.
Rebuilding: The process of restoring something that has been destroyed or damaged, in this case Jerusalem and its wall.
Temple: A holy place where people worshipped God, especially the first temple in Jerusalem built by King Solomon.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi who had special religious duties and responsibilities connected to the temple and worship.
Priests: Religious leaders from the line of Aaron who were responsible for performing ceremonies and sacrifices in the temple.
Residents: People living in a specific area or city, in this case those who lived in Jerusalem and other towns in Judah.
Lot casting: A method for randomly selecting people or things, used in Nehemiah 11 to determine who should live in Jerusalem.
Worship: Showing reverence and devotion toward a deity, in this case the Almighty God.

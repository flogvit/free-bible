Saul: Israel's first king, chosen by God through the prophet Samuel.
Samuel: Prophet and judge in Israel, who served as a mediator between God and the people.
Amalekites: A hostile tribe that attacked the Israelites during their exodus from Egypt.
Lord: Another name for God, emphasizing his sovereignty and authority.
Obey: To follow instructions and commands given by a higher power, in this case God.
Repent: To show regret or sorrow over past actions, especially those that are sinful or contrary to God's will.
Agag: The king of the Amalekites, whom Saul captured instead of killing him, in violation of God's command.
Chosen: Individuals or groups selected by God for a specific purpose, as was the case with Saul and Israel.
Rejection: When God no longer accepts an individual or group due to their disobedience or sin.

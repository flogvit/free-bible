Wedding: A ceremony where two people enter into marriage, often used as a picture of the relationship between God and His people.
Adultery: When someone breaks vows or commitments in a relationship, especially marriage. In Hosea chapter 2 this refers to the people of Israel turning away from God.
Punishment: Consequences or reactions resulting from bad actions or behavior, often given by God to correct and teach people.
Grace: God's love and mercy toward people, even when they do not deserve it.
Restoration: The process of bringing something back to its original state, especially the relationship between God and people.
Covenant: An agreement between two parties, here between God and Israel, where both have commitments and promises toward each other.
Fruitfulness: The ability to produce offspring, plants, or results; in this context it symbolizes God's blessings and care for His people.

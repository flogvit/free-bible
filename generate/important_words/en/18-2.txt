Job: The main character in the Book of Job, a righteous and wealthy man who undergoes enormous trials and suffering.
Satan: An evil spiritual being who challenges Job and his faithfulness to God by inflicting suffering upon him.
Trials: The great difficulties and challenges that Job faces throughout the story, including loss of wealth, health, and family.
Faithfulness: Job's enduring trust and loyalty to God, even when he confronts seemingly unexplainable suffering.
Friends: Three individuals (Eliphaz, Bildad, and Zophar) who visit Job to comfort and advise him during his trials. They represent different perspectives on why Job suffers.
Elihu: A younger man who offers his opinion on Job's situation and criticizes both Job and his friends for their lack of understanding of God's wisdom.
God: The Almighty Creator who ultimately speaks to Job and his friends, revealing insight into His sovereignty and wisdom.
Wisdom: A central theme in the Book of Job concerning seeking and understanding God's will and purposes, even when they do not immediately make sense to human reason.

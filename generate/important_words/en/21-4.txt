Under: Symbolizes a lower position or status in relation to something else.
Time: A continuous progression of events that provides a framework for measuring and comparing experiences.
Work: Physical or mental effort undertaken to produce or achieve something.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a wise and reasonable manner.
Joy: A feeling of happiness, pleasure, or contentment.
Vanity: The quality of being temporary, short-lived, or fleeting.
Sun: The star closest to Earth, which gives light and warmth and is the center of the solar system.
Wind: Movement of air from a high-pressure area to a low-pressure area; a natural phenomenon.
River: A natural watercourse flowing toward the sea, a lake, or another river.
Human: An individual of the human species, Homo sapiens; a person.
God's hand: An expression symbolizing God's power, care, and influence in the world.

Jeremiah: The prophet who conveys God's message to the people of Israel in the book of Jeremiah.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people and the nation they belong to, often referring to both the Northern Kingdom and the Southern Kingdom (Judah).
Return: The process by which Israel turns back to God after turning away from Him and following idols.
Repentance: A feeling of deep sorrow and regret over sin and disobedience toward God, leading to a change in heart and actions.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, especially with Israel, where God promises protection and blessing in exchange for obedience and faithfulness.
Idols: False gods or objects that are worshiped instead of the true God.
Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Faithfulness: Being loyal, consistent, and reliable in one's relationship with God and His commands.

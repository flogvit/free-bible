Absalom: One of King David's sons who rebelled against his father and tried to seize the throne.
David: King of Israel and the main character in 2 Samuel, known for his strong leadership and faith in God.
Joab: David's army commander, a loyal and skilled warrior who helped David fight his enemies, including Absalom.
Amasa: A relative of David and leader of Absalom's army, later appointed by David as the new army commander after Joab's fall.
Ahimaas: Son of the priest Zadok and one of David's messengers who reported news about the battle against Absalom.
The Battle at the Forest of Ephraim: A conflict between David's and Absalom's forces, where David won and Absalom was killed.
Tamar: David's daughter and Absalom's sister, who was raped by her half-brother Amnon, which led to Absalom's revenge and rebellion.

Jerusalem: The capital of ancient Israel, the center of religious and political power.
Invasion: A military action in which a foreign power attacks and occupies another country or territory.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of people based on their actions and faith.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, especially in the Old Testament.
False prophets: People who claim to speak on behalf of God but deliver false messages and lead people away from the truth.
Repentance: The feeling of regretting one's sins and desiring to change one's behavior to follow God's way.
Righteousness: A concept within Christianity that involves acting in accordance with God's will and morality.
Punishment: The consequences that God imposes on people for their sinful actions and disobedience toward him.

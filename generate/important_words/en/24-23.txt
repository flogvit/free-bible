Lord: A title used for God that expresses his authority and power over all things and everyone.
Prophet: A person commissioned by God to convey his message to people, often concerning future events or warnings.
Righteousness: A concept describing acting rightly and morally, and following God's laws and principles.
Shepherds: Leaders, especially religious leaders, who are responsible for guiding and caring for God's people.
Scattered: Describes the situation where the people of Israel are divided and exiled from their homeland.
Gather: God's action of bringing together and reuniting his people after a time of division and exile.
Branch of David: A designation for the Messiah, who according to prophecies was to come from King David's lineage and rule righteously.
False prophets: People who claim to speak on behalf of God but have not received such a commission and spread lies and false teachings.
Vision: A divine revelation, often in the form of a sight, given to a prophet to convey God's message.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment and consequences for human actions, especially those who have turned away from him and followed false prophets.

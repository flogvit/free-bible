The book of Job contains many important words and concepts, but here are some of the most central ones:
Job: The main character in the book of Job, a righteous man who is subjected to suffering and trials.
Eliphaz, Bildad, Zophar: Job's three friends who visit him during his suffering and try to find an explanation for his situation.
Suffering: Physical or emotional pain and distress that Job experiences.
Righteousness: The concept describing moral correctness and adherence to God's laws. Job asserts his own righteousness throughout the book.
Trials: Tests and challenges that Job must endure, often permitted by God to test his faith and character.
Fear of God: Deep respect and awe for God, which Job demonstrates despite his suffering.

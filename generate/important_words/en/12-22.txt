Josiah: A young king of Judah who introduced religious reforms and removed idolatry from the kingdom.
Hilkiah: High priest in the temple during King Josiah's reign, who found the Book of the Law (the Torah).
Shaphan: Scribe and official in King Josiah's court, who helped convey the discovery of the Book of the Law.
Book of the Law: A scroll containing God's laws and commands, likely part of the books of Moses, which was found in the temple.
Reform: A change or improvement of something, in this case Josiah's attempt to restore monotheism and remove idolatry.
Idolatry: Worship of false gods or idols, which was forbidden according to the discovered Book of the Law.
Huldah: A prophetess who confirmed the authenticity of the Book of the Law and predicted that God's judgment would come upon Judah.

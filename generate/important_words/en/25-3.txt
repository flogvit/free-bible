Lord: A title showing respect and submission, often used to refer to God in Lamentations 3.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy toward people, even when they do not deserve it.
Hope: Expectation of something good or a positive change in the future, especially in the context of God's promises.
Patience: The ability to wait calmly and without frustration, especially when trusting in God's plan and timing.
Prayer: Communication with God, where one expresses gratitude, asks for help, or seeks guidance.
Humility: Recognizing one's own limitations and need for God's grace and help, as well as showing respect toward others.
Comfort: The feeling of relief and support, especially from God, in difficult times and trials.
The Light: A symbol of God's presence, guidance, and truth in the darkness of suffering and despair.

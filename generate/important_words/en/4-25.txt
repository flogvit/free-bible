Baal-Peor: A Canaanite god, often associated with fertility and idolatry. The people of Israel began to worship this god in Numbers 25.
Midianite woman: A woman from the Midianite people whom the Israelite Zimri brought into the camp, provoking God's anger.
Zimri: An Israelite from the tribe of Simeon who took a Midianite woman into the camp and made God angry.
Phinehas: A priest and grandson of Aaron who killed Zimri and the Midianite woman to stop God's anger and the plague that was ravaging the Israelites.
Plague: A deadly disease that God sent as punishment for the Israelites' idolatry and immorality.
Alliance: A covenant or agreement between two parties. In this case, a covenant between God and Phinehas as a result of his actions.

Moses: An important prophet and leader of the people of Israel, who received the law from God and led the Israelites out of Egypt.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Children of Israel: A term for the people of Israel, descendants of Jacob's twelve sons, who form the twelve tribes of Israel.
Midianites: A group of people who lived in Midian, often in conflict with the people of Israel in the Old Testament.
Commander: A military leader or officer responsible for leading an army force.
Gold: A precious metal that was highly valued and used for jewelry, decoration, and as currency.
Sanctuary: A special place dedicated to worship and religious ceremonies.
Tithe: A tax or contribution, usually 10% of income or property, given to the church or temple.

Lord: A title for God meaning "the highest authority" and referring to his sovereignty over all things.
Salvation: Deliverance or rescue from danger, suffering, or destruction, especially in a spiritual context.
Refuge: A place of protection, safety, and rest, often used to describe God's protection.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose, threaten, or harm someone; here it refers to David's enemies.
Trust: Belief in or reliance on someone, especially God, when facing uncertainty or difficulties.
Seek: To try to find or obtain something; here it refers to seeking God's presence and guidance.
Temple: A holy place for worship and prayer dedicated to God.
Sacrifice: An act of giving something valuable as a gift or tribute to God, often in thanksgiving or to seek his favor.

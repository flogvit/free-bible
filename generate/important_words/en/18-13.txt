Job: The main character in the Book of Job, a righteous and wealthy man who undergoes great trials.
Eliphaz: One of Job's three friends who comes to comfort him and discuss his situation.
Bildad: Another of Job's friends who participates in the dialogues.
Zophar: The third friend who visits Job and engages in the conversation about his sufferings.
Righteousness: Ethics and morality related to acting rightly and justly, especially in relation to God and other people.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a reasonable and insightful way, often connected to moral and ethical decisions.
Suffering: Pain, distress, or discomfort, whether physical or mental. In the Book of Job, suffering represents Job's trials and losses.
Trials: Challenges or difficulties that test a person's character, patience, and faith.

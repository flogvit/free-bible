Burning Bush: A miracle where God spoke to Moses through a bush that burned without being consumed by the flames.
Moses: The prophet and leader whom God chose to free the Israelites from slavery in Egypt.
Egypt: The ancient kingdom in Northeast Africa where the Israelites were held as slaves under Pharaoh's rule.
Pharaoh: The ruler of ancient Egypt, who refused to let the Israelites leave until God sent ten plagues.
Israelites: God's chosen people, descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who suffered under slavery in Egypt.
Challenge: To question or oppose something, as when Moses challenged Pharaoh to release the Israelites.
Free: To set free from captivity, slavery, or oppression, as when God freed the Israelites from Egypt.
Sign: Miracles or supernatural events that show God's power, such as the ten plagues.
Plagues: The ten disasters God sent upon Egypt to force Pharaoh to release the Israelites.
Promise: A declaration of intention or commitment, such as God's promise to lead the Israelites to a land flowing with milk and honey.

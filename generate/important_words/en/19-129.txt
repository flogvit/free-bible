Enemies of Israel: Groups or nations that oppose or threaten the people of Israel and their interests.
The Lord: Another designation for God, the almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.
Plowmen: A metaphor describing the actions of Israel's enemies, who try to harm and destroy Israel.
Righteousness: The moral principle of acting rightly and in accordance with ethical rules and laws.
Bind: To hold fast, restrict, or control something or someone, often used figuratively.
Shame: A feeling of humiliation or degradation resulting from bad actions or mistakes.

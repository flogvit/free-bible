Psalm: A poetic religious song or prayer, often used in worship and praise of God.
Lord: A title used to show respect and reverence toward God, acknowledging his sovereignty and power.
Ears: The part of the body that hears sound; used here symbolically to ask God to listen to the prayer.
Mouth: The part of the body used for speaking; used here symbolically to describe how one communicates with God through prayer.
Watchman: A person or divine force who protects and keeps watch over something or someone; here the psalmist asks God to be a watchman over the mouth and heart.
Heart: The inner center of emotions and will in a person; used here symbolically to represent the moral and spiritual center of a person's life.
Evil: Something that is morally wrong, harmful, or destructive; here it is used to describe actions or thoughts that contradict God's will.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with God's will and morality; here it refers to one who lives a good and godly life.

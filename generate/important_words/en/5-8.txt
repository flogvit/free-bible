Lord: A designation for God, referring to his sovereignty and authority.
Land: The area that God promised to give the people of Israel as their homeland.
Promise: To give an assurance or pledge about something; in this case, God's promise of the land to the Israelites.
House of Egypt: A reference to the time when the Israelites were slaves in Egypt before the exodus.
Wilderness: A dry, inhospitable landscape where the Israelites wandered for 40 years after leaving Egypt.
Test: A trial or challenge that God gave the Israelites to see if they would follow his commands and learn to trust him.
Manna: A miraculous food product that God provided to the Israelites while they were in the wilderness.
Humble: To bring someone to a lower position, often to teach them to depend on God.
Discipline: To correct or guide someone to help them learn and grow.
Forgetfulness: The state of forgetting or not remembering something; in this case, God's deeds and commands.

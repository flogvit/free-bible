Levites: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, responsible for service in the tabernacle and later the temple.
Kohathites: A subgroup of the Levites, descendants of Kohath, one of Levi's sons. They were responsible for carrying the holy objects in the tabernacle.
Gershonites: Another subgroup of the Levites, descendants of Gershom, also one of Levi's sons. They were responsible for transporting and maintaining the structures and textiles of the tabernacle.
Merarites: The third subgroup of the Levites, descendants of Merari, another of Levi's sons. They were responsible for transporting and maintaining the frames, pillars, and bases of the tabernacle.
Tabernacle: A portable sanctuary tent used by the Israelites as a place for worship and offerings before Solomon's temple was built.
Holy objects: Items used in the tabernacle and later the temple for worship and rituals, including the ark, the lampstand, the altar, and the table with the bread of the Presence.
Aaron: Brother of Moses and the first high priest in Israel, who led the priestly service in the tabernacle.

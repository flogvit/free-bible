Underworld: A description of a dark and gloomy place, often used to symbolize evil and despair.
Light: A symbol of hope, wisdom, and God's presence; used in contrast to underworld to show that God brings light and salvation to the people.
Joy: A feeling of happiness and contentment that comes from experiencing God's love and grace.
Burden: A heavy load or responsibility that people carry, often linked to sin and guilt.
Staff: A symbol of authority and power, representing God's control over the world and his protection of his people.
Warrior: A person who fights in war, here used to describe those who fight against evil and stand with God.
Prince of Peace: A title given to Jesus Christ, representing his role as the one who brings peace and reconciliation between God and humanity.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with God's will and morality, and treating others with justice and respect.
Faithfulness: Being loyal and steadfast in one's faith in God and commitment to his service.

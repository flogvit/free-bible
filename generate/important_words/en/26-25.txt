Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
The word of the Lord: An expression referring to God's revelation or instructions given to the prophet Ezekiel.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment for nations' wickedness and disobedience toward Him.
Ammonites: A people who lived east of the Jordan River, often in conflict with the people of Israel.
Moab: A nation east of the Dead Sea, related to the Ammonites and often hostile toward Israel.
Edom: A kingdom south of Israel, known for its hostility toward the Israelites and their alliances with other enemies of Israel.
Philistines: A people who lived along the coast of Canaan and were known for their conflicts with the Israelites.
Retribution: God's act of punishing a nation or people for their sins and injustice.
Sanctuary: A place dedicated to worship and honor of God, often referring to the temple in Jerusalem.

Joshua: The main character in the book of Joshua, who succeeds Moses as leader of the people of Israel.
Inheritance: The land and territories that God has promised to give to the tribes of Israel.
The Law: The divine instructions and commandments given by God to Moses on Mount Sinai, including the Ten Commandments.
Courage: Bravery and determination in the face of difficulties and challenges; a quality Joshua is encouraged to have.
Prosper: To reach goals or achieve success, especially when it comes to leading the people of Israel into the promised land.
Cross: To move from one place to another, specifically across the Jordan River, which separates the wilderness from the promised land.
Foot: A metaphor for presence and control, used to express that wherever Joshua's foot treads, he will gain dominion over it.

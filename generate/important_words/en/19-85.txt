Lord: Another designation for God, emphasizing his sovereignty and authority.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy shown to people, even though they do not deserve it.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and giving them eternal life.
Truth: That which is real, correct, and in accordance with God's will and character.
Righteousness: God's just and moral standard, which he expects people to follow.
Peace: A state of harmony and well-being, both among people and between people and God.
Prayer: Communication between people and God, where one asks for help, gives thanks, or praises God.
Land: In this context, it refers to Israel, God's chosen people and their homeland.

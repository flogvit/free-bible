Sion: A biblical name for Jerusalem, representing God's people and the place of his presence.
The Lord's day: A future time when God will intervene in history to judge the nations and establish his kingdom.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, restoring them to a right relationship with him.
Righteousness: The moral standard that God requires of people, and the status they receive when accepted by God because of their faith.
Bridegroom: An image used to describe God's loving relationship with his people, often associated with Jesus Christ.
Bride: A symbol of God's people, united with God through a covenant and participating in his plan of salvation.
Watchmen: Prophets or leaders whom God has appointed to pray and work for Zion's good and to remind God of his promises.
Walls: A symbolic reference to the protection and security that God gives his people.

Elihu: A young man who speaks to Job and his friends, presenting another perspective on God's justice and Job's suffering.
Thunder: A powerful sound caused by lightning, used in this context as a symbol of God's power and majesty.
God's voice: A metaphor for God's communication or divine guidance, often linked with natural phenomena.
Snow: Frozen precipitation falling from the clouds, used here as an image of God's creative powers and ability to change the weather.
Rain: Water falling from the clouds in droplets, another example of God's control over natural forces and the weather.
Clouds: Gatherings of water droplets or ice crystals in the atmosphere, represented here as part of God's creation and power.
The wind: Air in motion, also used as an image of God's power and influence over nature.
Righteous: To act morally and ethically correct, describing here God's character and actions.

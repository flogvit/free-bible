Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who reigned from 605 BC to 562 BC, and played a central role in the book of Jeremiah chapter 27.
Slavery: A condition in which a person is forced to work for another, often in the form of bondage.
Yoke: A device used to bind animals together for plowing or other labor, here symbolizing submission and servitude.
Prophets: Religious leaders who claim to receive messages directly from God, and convey these messages to the people.
Jeremiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who warned the people of Israel about coming destruction and urged them to turn back to God.
Deceit: A false or fraudulent act, often used in connection with false prophets who give misleading information or promises.
Land: Geographical areas that are subject to political control and rule, often used to refer to nations and kingdoms.
Serve: To perform work or duties for another person or organization, often in a subordinate position.
Seven years: A time period representing a full cycle of sabbatical years, which was important in Jewish tradition.

Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to comprehend and evaluate situations and problems in a clear and rational manner.
Discipline: Training or correction used to teach or guide someone, especially children.
Righteousness: The quality of being just, honest, and morally upright in one's actions and decisions.
Wealth: An abundance of valuable resources, assets, or money that provides financial security and prosperity.
Fear: A feeling of awe, reverence, and submission toward God, leading to obedience and piety.
Prudence: The ability to make good decisions based on wisdom, experience, and an understanding of consequences.
Faithfulness: Being loyal, reliable, and steadfast in one's commitments, promises, and relationships.
Humility: The quality of being modest, not proud or arrogant, and recognizing one's limitations.
Work: The effort and activities undertaken to achieve a goal, create something, or earn an income.

Saul: Israel's first king, chosen by God and anointed by the prophet Samuel.
Samuel: Prophet, priest, and judge in Israel who anointed both Saul and David as kings.
Jonathan: Saul's son and a skilled warrior, close friend of David.
Philistines: A people living along the coast of Canaan who were enemies of Israel in several conflicts.
Gilgal: A city in Israel where Saul was appointed king and later offered an unlawful sacrifice.
Sacrifice: An act in which people offered animals, food, or other items to God to seek his favor or forgiveness.
Unlawful: Something that breaks God's laws, commands, or instructions.
Dissatisfaction: A feeling of discontent, often resulting from some form of failure or injustice.

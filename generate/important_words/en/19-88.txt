Lord's: A reference to God as the highest authority and ruler over all.
Prayer: Communication or conversation with God, usually expressed in the form of thanksgiving, praise, or requests.
Night: The time of day when the sun is below the horizon, often used symbolically for darkness, sorrow, or trials.
Soul: The spiritual and immortal part of a person, often regarded as that person's true essence.
Life: The biological process that characterizes living organisms, but also used metaphorically to describe a person's experiences and existence.
Grave: A place where the dead are buried, often used as a symbol of death and final rest.
Wrath: God's strong displeasure or anger, often directed toward sin and injustice.
Hand: A body part that represents God's power and influence in the world and in people's lives.
Darkness: Absence of light, often used as a metaphor for evil, suffering, and hopelessness.

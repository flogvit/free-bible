Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his authority and power.
Eyes: Used here metaphorically to express attention and care from God.
Servant: A person dedicated to serving God and following his guidance and instructions.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love that he gives to people.
Contempt: A strong negative feeling or attitude toward something or someone, often associated with rejection and humiliation.
Mercy: God's compassion, grace, and love shown toward people in their distress and suffering.

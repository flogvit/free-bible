Esther: A Jewish woman who becomes Persian queen and the main character in the book of Esther.
Mordecai: Esther's adoptive father and a central figure in the story, helps Esther save the Jewish people.
Haman: An evil advisor to King Ahasuerus, wants to destroy all Jews in the Persian Empire.
Ahasuerus: The king of the Persian Empire, married to Esther, unaware of her Jewish background.
Purim: A Jewish festival celebrating the deliverance of the Jewish people from Haman's plan to exterminate them.
The Fast: A period when Esther and the Jewish people fast and pray to God for protection and guidance.
Gallows: An execution device that Haman builds to hang Mordecai, but ends up being hanged on it himself.

Incense: A mixture of aromatic substances burned as an offering to God, symbolizing prayer and devotion.
Anointing oil: A special oil used to anoint or consecrate people and objects in the temple, representing God's presence and blessing.
Atonement: An act to make amends for sins and mistakes, restoring the relationship between God and humanity.
The treasury: A tax or gift given to the temple to support the priests and maintain its services.
The Ark: A special chest that contained the Ark of the Covenant, where God's presence was said to rest. The Ark symbolized God's covenant with the people of Israel.
Priest: A person who performs religious ceremonies and functions on behalf of the people, serving as a mediator between God and humanity.
Holy: Something set apart for God and his service, distinct from what is common or profane.

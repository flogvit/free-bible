Moses: The prophet and leader of the people of Israel, who received the law from God on Mount Sinai.
The Law: The instructions and commandments given by God to Moses and the people of Israel to guide their lives and society.
The Land: The promised land of Canaan, where God led the people of Israel after leaving Egypt.
Mountain: Mount Sinai, the place where Moses received the law from God.
The People: The people of Israel, also known as Hebrews, who were led by Moses through the wilderness toward the promised land.

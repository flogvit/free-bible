David: The young shepherd who later becomes king of Israel, known for his courage and devotion.
Saul: Israel's first king, consumed by jealousy and seeking to kill David.
The Cave: The place where David hides from Saul and has the opportunity to kill him, but chooses not to do so.
The Lord: Another name for God, referring to the Almighty Creator and the God of Israel.
Conscience: A person's inner moral compass that helps them decide what is right and wrong.
Mercy: The ability to show compassion, grace, and forgiveness toward others, even when they do not deserve it.
Covenant: A pact or agreement between two parties, often used in a biblical context to describe the relationship between God and people.
Persecution: The act of chasing, harassing, or oppressing someone because of their faith, beliefs, or position.
Respect: Showing honor, reverence, and consideration toward another person or their wishes and needs.

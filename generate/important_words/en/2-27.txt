Altar: A structure for offerings and worship of God, built according to his instructions in Exodus 27.
Bronze: A metal made of copper and tin, used to make details and equipment for the altar and courtyard.
Courtyard: An enclosed area around the tabernacle where people could come to worship God and bring offerings.
Pillars: Supporting structures that held up the entrance to the courtyard and the hangings that surrounded it.
Hangings: Textiles, often made of fine linen or other fabric, used to create a barrier around the courtyard.
Olive oil: Oil pressed from olive fruits, used to keep the lamp in the tabernacle burning continuously.
Lamp: A container for oil with a wick, designed to provide light inside the tabernacle.

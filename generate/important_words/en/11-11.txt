Solomon: King of Israel and son of David, known for his wisdom and wealth.
Foreign women: Women from other nations whom Solomon married, in violation of God's command.
Apostasy: Turning away from God and His commands, worshiping other gods or following false teachings.
Ashtoreth: A goddess of fertility who was worshiped in several Canaanite and Near Eastern cultures.
Milcom: An Ammonite god, also known as Molech, often worshiped through human sacrifice.
Jeroboam: An Israelite chosen by God to rule the ten northern tribes after the kingdom was divided.
The prophet: A person who speaks on behalf of God and delivers His message to the people.
The Kingdom: The kingdom of Israel, which during Solomon's reign was united but later split into two.

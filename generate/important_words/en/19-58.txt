Righteous: To act in accordance with morality, ethics, and God's laws; to be right and upright.
Judge: A person or divine power who decides what is right and wrong, and administers justice and punishment.
Wrath: Intense anger, especially when directed against injustice or evil.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Human: A person or individual of the human race, often used to describe all humanity.
Corruption: A state of moral decay or destruction, often associated with sin and evil.
Trial: A process in which a case is examined and decided, often linked to justice and judgment.
Heart: In a biblical context, this often refers to a person's inner being, including thoughts, feelings, and will.
Lie: An untruth, deception, or falsehood, often used to describe actions and words that oppose God's truth.
Poison: A harmful substance, here used metaphorically to describe evil words and deeds that harm others.
Snake: A reptile, often used as a symbol of evil and temptation in biblical texts.
Living: To be alive, to have life and consciousness; in this context, used to describe God's presence and work in the world.

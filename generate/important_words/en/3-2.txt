Grain Offering: A gift consisting of grain or flour that was offered to God to show gratitude and submission.
Unleavened: Food, especially bread, that has not undergone the fermentation process, thus symbolizing purity and holiness.
Oil: An ingredient in the grain offering representing God's Spirit and blessing.
Frankincense: Fragrant substances burned to release a pleasing aroma, symbolizing prayers and devotion to God.
Burnt Offering: A part of the gift that is burned on the altar as a symbolic act to give something back to God and seek his favor.
High Priest: The highest religious leader among the Israelites, responsible for performing ritual duties and sacrifices in the temple.
Salt: A symbol of an enduring covenant and loyalty between God and his people, used in the grain offering.

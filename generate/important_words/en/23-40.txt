The Lord's comfort: God's encouragement and strength offered to his people in difficult times.
The Lord's voice: God's voice or message, often delivered through prophets.
Desert: A dry and inhospitable landscape that symbolizes spiritual dryness and trials.
Highway: A road or path representing God's guidance and direction for his people.
Glory: God's majesty, greatness, and divine presence.
Grass: Symbolizes human frailty and the brevity of life.
Flower: Represents the beauty and transience of human life and achievements.
Hand: God's power and ability to act on behalf of his people.
Circle of heaven: The universe and all it contains, created and controlled by God.
Sheep: A symbol of God's people, whom he cares for and leads.
Shepherd: God's care and leadership for his people, as a shepherd tends his flock.
Arm: God's strength and protection for his people.

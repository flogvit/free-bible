Daniel: A young Jewish man who was taken to Babylon and served King Nebuchadnezzar; the main character in the book of Daniel.
Babylon: An ancient city in Mesopotamia, the center of the Babylonian empire, where Daniel and his friends were taken as captives.
Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who took Daniel and other Jews as captives and had dreams that Daniel interpreted.
Wisdom: The ability to think and act with insight, understanding, and judgment; Daniel and his friends were given wisdom from God.
Dreams: Images, thoughts, and feelings that arise in the mind during sleep; Daniel interprets the king's dreams and gains recognition.
Interpretation: To explain or give meaning to something, especially dreams and prophecies; Daniel was known for his ability to interpret dreams.
Godliness: Respect and reverence for God; Daniel and his friends practiced godliness by holding fast to their religious beliefs.
Prophecy: A prediction or announcement of future events, often given by God; the book of Daniel contains several prophecies.

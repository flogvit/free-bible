Trust: A feeling of safety and faith in God, showing that one believes He will protect and lead His people.
Enemies: Persons or forces that are against a person, often used in the Book of Psalms to describe those who stand against God's people or God's will.
Deliver: To set someone free from captivity, oppression, or danger. In Psalm 56 it refers to God's act of saving His people from their enemies.
Tears: A symbol of sorrow, pain, or suffering. In Psalm 56 tears represent the hardship and adversity that God's people experience.
Promises: Commitments or guarantees given by God to His people, often linked with protection, blessing, and salvation.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, reverence, and devotion to God through song, music, or poetry.

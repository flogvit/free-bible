Holy: Something that is specially set apart or dedicated to God, and therefore should be treated with respect and reverence.
Lord: Another name for God, used to emphasize his authority and power.
Abominations: Actions or things regarded as morally detestable and offensive in God's sight.
Punishment: The consequences of breaking God's commands and rules, often involving suffering or death.
Incest: Sexual relations between close relatives, which is strictly forbidden according to God's laws.
Offspring: Descendants, children, or progeny of a person or people.
Death penalty: The most severe punishment, where the guilty party is executed for their crime.
Destruction: Complete ruin or removal of something, often used regarding God's judgment on wicked people or nations.

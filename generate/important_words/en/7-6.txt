Gideon: A judge in Israel whom God chose to free the Israelites from Midianite oppression.
Midianites: A people group who oppressed the Israelites in Judges 6, and whom Gideon was called to fight against.
Ba'al: A Canaanite god whom the Israelites worshiped instead of Yahweh, which led to God's displeasure and punishment.
Altar: A holy place or structure where sacrifices and worship are performed.
Fleece: Wool from a sheep or goat skin, which Gideon used as a sign from God to confirm his calling.
Sign: A supernatural event or manifestation that confirms God's will or presence.
Jerubba'al: Another name for Gideon, meaning "let Ba'al fight against him," given after he destroyed Baal's altar.
Choice: God's selection of special people to fulfill His plan and purpose.

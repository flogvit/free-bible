Job: The main character in the Book of Job, a righteous man who endures great trials and losses.
Satan: An evil spiritual being who challenges God's authority and accuses Job of causing his own misfortune.
Trials: Various difficulties and sufferings that Job experiences to test his faith and loyalty.
Loyalty: Job's ability to remain faithful to God, even when facing severe trials and suffering.
Friends: Three individuals (Eliphaz, Bildad, and Zophar) who visit Job and discuss his situation and theological questions.
Dialogue: Conversations between Job and his friends, where they exchange views on God's justice and human suffering.
God's answer: God's response to Job's complaint, in which He highlights His power and wisdom, and questions humanity's understanding of justice.
Humility: Job's recognition that he cannot understand God's plan and his acceptance of his place in the universe.
Restoration: The restoration of Job's wealth and blessings after his trials and humility before God.

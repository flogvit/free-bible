Canaan: The area in the Middle East that according to the Bible was promised by God to the people of Israel. It covers parts of today's Israel, Palestine, Lebanon and Jordan.
Joshua: A central figure in ancient Israel, the leader who succeeded Moses and led the Israelites into Canaan.
Caleb: One of the twelve spies sent by Moses to explore Canaan, and one of the two who gave a positive report. He was from the tribe of Judah and was rewarded with land in Canaan.
Judah: One of Israel's twelve tribes, named after Jacob's son Judah. In Joshua 15 the boundaries of Judah's territory in Canaan are described.
Hebron: An ancient city in Canaan that is important in Jewish, Christian and Muslim tradition. The city was given to Caleb as part of his inheritance and later became one of David's royal cities.
Deborah's palm: A well-known landmark mentioned in Joshua 15, assumed to be a holy place or a site for worship. The place is connected with the prophetess Deborah from Judges.

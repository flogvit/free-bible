Praise: To express admiration, gratitude, and honor to God for His greatness and deeds.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, showing respect and acknowledgment of His authority.
Sanctuary: A holy place dedicated to God, often a temple or church, where worship and services take place.
Mighty acts: God's powerful actions and miracles, demonstrating His omnipotence and love for humanity.
Harp: A stringed musical instrument with many strings, often used in biblical times to accompany singing and worship.
Trumpet: A wind instrument used in the Bible both as a signal device and to accompany praise and worship.
Dance: A form of movement and bodily expression used in the Bible to show joy, gratitude, and worship of God.
Cymbals: Percussion instruments consisting of two flat metal plates struck together, used to create rhythm and emphasize music.

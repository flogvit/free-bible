Lord: A title that refers to God and his sovereign authority over all creation.
Servant: A person dedicated to serving and obeying God. In Isaiah 50, this refers to the Messiah, Jesus Christ, who is God's perfect servant.
Light: A symbol of divine guidance, truth, and wisdom. In this context, light represents God's presence and help in dark times.
Ear: Used metaphorically to describe the ability to hear and obey God's word and guidance.
Cheek: In this context, a symbol of humility and suffering. To offer the cheek to one who strikes means to accept persecution without taking revenge.
Righteousness: God's righteous standard and morality, which people should pursue and which the Messiah fulfills completely.

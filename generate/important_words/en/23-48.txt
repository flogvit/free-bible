Herod: King of Judea during Isaiah's time, known for his cruelty and oppression.
Prophecy: A prediction or revelation about future events given by God through a prophet.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, often used as a symbol of evil and idolatry.
Redemption: God's act to free people from sin and punishment, especially through the sacrifice of Jesus Christ.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people, including the twelve tribes that made up ancient Israel.
Zion: Another name for Jerusalem, particularly the holy part of the city where the temple stood.
The Lord's Servant: A title often referring to the prophet Isaiah, but also to the Messiah, Jesus Christ.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human actions, both individually and collectively.

Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting rightly and justly in accordance with God's will and laws.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of human actions, especially those that break his commands and decrees.
Mourn: An expression of grief, sadness, or regret over something that has happened or been lost, often used in connection with the judgment and destruction coming upon Israel.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people, descendants of Jacob, which includes twelve tribes and their land.
Samaria: The capital city of the northern kingdom of Israel, often mentioned as a symbol of the nation's sin and idolatry.
Bethel: A holy city in the southern kingdom of Judah, known for its religious significance and as a place of worship and sacrifice.
The day of Zion: A reference to God's coming judgment and punishment upon Israel and other nations, also known as "the day of the Lord".
Repentance: A change of heart and mind that leads a person to turn away from sin and turn to God for forgiveness and salvation.

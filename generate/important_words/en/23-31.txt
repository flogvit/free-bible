Egypt: A land in North Africa, often referred to as a source of strength and help, but in Isaiah chapter 31 it is warned against trusting in Egypt.
Horses: Symbolize military strength and power. In this context, they refer to Egypt's army and its false security.
Salvation: The term used to describe God's act of saving His people from their enemies and troubles.
The Lord: Another designation for God, emphasizing His authority and sovereignty.
Zion: A mountain in Jerusalem that symbolizes God's presence and protection of His people.
Turn back: To change direction or attitude, especially regarding one's life and faith. In Isaiah 31, the people of Israel are urged to turn away from their trust in Egypt and instead rely on God.

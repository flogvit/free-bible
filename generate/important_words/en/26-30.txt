Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received revelations from God and delivered His message to the people of Israel.
Prophecy: Predictions or messages given by God through a prophet, often concerning future events or warnings.
Egypt: An ancient kingdom in Northeast Africa that plays an important role in biblical history, especially in connection with the enslavement and exodus of the Israelites.
Lord: Another designation for God, emphasizing His sovereignty and authority over creation.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or consequences for human actions, particularly their disobedience to His commands.
Sword: Symbolizes war, destruction, and God's punishing judgment upon nations.
Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who conquered Jerusalem and led the Jews into exile; also used as an instrument of God's judgment in Ezekiel chapter 30.
Babylon: A powerful ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia that played a central role in several biblical narratives, including the Jewish exile.
Nations: Refers to the various peoples and countries involved in or affected by God's plan and judgment in Ezekiel chapter 30.

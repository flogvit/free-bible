Covenant: An agreement or bond between God and people, often with promises and conditions from both parties.
Alliance: Another word for covenant, describing an agreement between two or more parties, especially between God and the people of Israel.
Blessing: God's favor and protection, often given as a reward for obedience to his commands and covenants.
Curse: A negative consequence or punishment resulting from disobedience to God's commands and covenants.
Obedience: To follow and obey God's commands and instructions, which often leads to blessings and protection.
Disobedience: To fail to follow or obey God's commands and instructions, which can result in curses and negative consequences.
The people of Israel: The nation descended from the patriarch Jacob (also called Israel), including the twelve tribes that made up ancient Israel.
Moses: The prophet and leader of the people of Israel, who led them out of slavery in Egypt and received God's law, including the Ten Commandments.

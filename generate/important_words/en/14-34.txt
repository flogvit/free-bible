Josiah: King of Judah who carried out religious reforms and removed idolatry.
Reform: A change or improvement, especially in politics, religion, or society.
Temple: A holy place for worship and prayer, in this case Solomon's temple in Jerusalem.
Ark of the Covenant: A sacred chest that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, a central part of Israel's history and faith.
Hilkiah: High priest in the temple during Josiah's reign; he found the book of the law which led to the reforms.
Book of the Law: A book containing God's laws and commands, likely part of the books of Moses.
Prophetess: A woman who speaks on behalf of God and conveys His message.
Huldah: A prophetess who confirmed the authenticity of the book of the law and predicted the consequences if the people did not follow God's laws.
Idolatry: The practice of worshipping and honoring other gods besides the true God.
Removal: The act of removing or eliminating something; here it refers to Josiah's actions to remove idolatry from the kingdom.

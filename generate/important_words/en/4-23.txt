Balaam: A pagan prophet who was hired by Balak, the king of Moab, to curse the people of Israel, but ended up blessing them instead.
Balak: The king of Moab who hired Balaam to curse Israel, but failed in his attempt.
Blessing: A positive statement or action from God or humans that brings happiness, success, and prosperity to the recipient.
Curse: A negative statement or action from God or humans that brings misfortune, defeat, and suffering to the recipient.
Sacrifice: A holy act in which animals, food, or other items are offered to God as a form of worship, gratitude, or atonement for sins.
The people of Israel: Descendants of the patriarch Jacob (also called Israel) and his twelve sons, who formed the twelve tribes of Israel.

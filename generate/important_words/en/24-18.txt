Canvas: A metaphor referring to humanity, where God shapes and influences the lives of people.
Potter: A description of God as the one who forms human lives, like a potter shaping clay.
Clay: Symbolizes people and their malleability in God's hands, able to shape them into something new or destroy what is old.
Transformation: The process by which God changes or transforms people's lives and circumstances based on their actions and choices.
Nation: A people or community that is the object of God's judgment and grace, depending on their obedience or disobedience toward Him.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment for human evil and disobedience.
Repentance: A change of heart and mind that leads an individual to turn away from sin and seek God's forgiveness and grace.
Grace: God's love and mercy offered to people, even though they do not deserve it.

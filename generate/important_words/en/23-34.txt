Herod: King of Judea who ruled during the time of the prophet Isaiah and was known for his cruelty and tyranny.
Judgment Day: A future day when God will judge all people, both living and dead, and reward or punish them according to their deeds.
Edom: An ancient kingdom southeast of Israel, often described as an enemy of the Israelites. In Isaiah 34, Edom represents God's judgment against all nations that oppose his people.
Sword: A symbol of God's judgment and punishment, used to describe how he will destroy the wicked and protect the righteous.
Sacrifice: A ritual act in which animals or other objects are offered to God as a form of worship and gratitude. In Isaiah 34, sacrifice is a symbol of God's judgment and punishment upon the wicked.
Heaven: The upper part of the universe, regarded as God's dwelling place and the location where angels and holy people reside after death.
Earth: The planet we live on, created by God and given to humanity as their home and responsibility.
Life: The vital force that enables organisms to grow, reproduce, and sustain themselves. God is the source of all life.

Shepherd: A person who cares for and leads sheep or other animals; in this context, it refers to God as the one who guides and protects His people.
Vine: A plant that produces grapes, used here as a symbol for the people of Israel, whom God has planted and tended.
Throne: A royal seat where a ruler sits; in this context, it represents God's authority and power.
Prayer: Communication with God, often to ask for help, guidance, or to express gratitude.
Salvation: The term referring to God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, so that they may have eternal life and a close relationship with God.

Enemies: Persons or forces who oppose or wish to harm another; in the book of Psalms 59 this often refers to those who stand against God and his people.
Watch: A person or force that protects or keeps guard over something or someone; here it is used of God as he protects his people from enemies.
Salvation: The term used to describe God's act of saving, protecting, and delivering his people from their enemies and sin.
Strength: Power or ability to do something, whether physical, mental, or spiritual; in this context it refers to God's power and ability to protect and save his people.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, honor, and glory to God through song, music, and poetry; in the book of Psalms 59 it is used to praise God for his protection and salvation.
Morning: The time when the day begins and the sun rises; in the book of Psalms 59 it symbolizes a new beginning and God's faithfulness each day.
Mercy: God's love, grace, and compassion toward people, especially regarding forgiving sins and giving help in times of trouble.

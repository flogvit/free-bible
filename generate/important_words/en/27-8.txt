Daniel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions from God about the future.
Chapter 8: The chapter in the book of Daniel where he describes a vision he received concerning the end times and world powers.
The Ram: A symbol representing the powerful Medo-Persian empire, with two horns symbolizing the two kingdoms of Media and Persia.
The Male Goat: A symbol representing the Greek empire under Alexander the Great, with a large horn symbolizing his power.
The Large Horn: Symbolizes Alexander the Great and his conquests that led to the expansion of the Greek empire.
The Four Horns: Represent the four smaller kingdoms that arose after Alexander's death and the division of his empire.
The Little Horn: A wicked and powerful horn that grows out from one of the four horns, representing a future tyrannical ruler.
2300 evenings and mornings: A time period mentioned in Daniel's vision, which can be interpreted as 2300 days or years, and has been subject to various interpretations.
The Sanctuary: A holy place, often associated with the temple in Jerusalem, that plays a central role in Daniel's vision and end-time events.
Gabriel: An angel who helps Daniel understand the meaning of his vision and explains the symbols and events in detail.

Joseph: One of Jacob's sons, who is sold into slavery in Egypt and later becomes governor because of his ability to interpret dreams.
Pharaoh's dream: A dream that Pharaoh has about seven fat and seven lean cows, as well as seven good and seven bad ears of grain, which no one can interpret until Joseph is called in.
Interpretation: The understanding or explanation of a dream's meaning, in this case carried out by Joseph for Pharaoh's dream.
Seven years: The time period that the dream symbolizes, where there are first seven years of abundant harvest followed by seven years of famine.
Storehouses: Storage of food and resources to be prepared for times of scarcity, something Joseph suggests that Pharaoh should do to handle the coming years of famine.
Governor: A high position in Egyptian society that Joseph receives after interpreting Pharaoh's dream, and he is given responsibility for organizing the building up of storehouses.

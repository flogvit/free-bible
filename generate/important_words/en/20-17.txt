Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The capacity to make good decisions and grasp complex situations or ideas.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and fairly, in accordance with morality, ethics, and the law.
Discipline: Correction or training used to improve a person's character or behavior.
Lord: A title often referring to God, the supreme power and creator of the universe.
Wise: People who possess wisdom, insight, and reason, and use this knowledge in a good way.
Fools: People who lack wisdom and reason, making poor decisions or acting unreasonably.
Friendship: A close relationship between two or more people based on love, trust, support, and shared interests.
Speech: The way we communicate with each other through words, both spoken and written.
Tongue: A symbol of speech and communication, and can also refer to the manner in which someone speaks or expresses themselves.
Heart: Often used metaphorically to represent a person's feelings, intentions, and inner being.
Life: The state of existing, growing, and developing as a living being.
Death: The end of life, when an organism ceases to exist and perform vital functions.

Light: The light source that God commanded Moses to make in the tabernacle to symbolize God's presence and guidance.
Levites: One of Israel's twelve tribes, chosen by God to serve as helpers for the priests and carry out special duties in the sanctuary.
Cleansing: The process of cleansing the Levites from sin and impurity before they could begin their service in the tabernacle.
Offerings: Gifts and sacrifices given to God as part of religious practice in ancient Israel.
Firstborn: The first child born in a family; in this context, it refers to the Levites replacing the firstborn Israelites in their role as holy to God.
Consecration: The ceremony where the Levites were set apart and dedicated to their service in the tabernacle.
Priests: Members of Aaron's lineage who had responsibility for performing ritual duties and making offerings in the tabernacle.

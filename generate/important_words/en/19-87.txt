Zion: A hill in Jerusalem that symbolizes God's presence and protection. It is also a central place of worship in Judaism.
The Lord: Another designation for God, expressing his sovereignty and power over all creation.
Loves: To have a deep feeling of devotion, care, and respect for someone or something. In this context, it refers to God's love for Zion and its inhabitants.
Gates: The entrances to the city, representing access and protection. In Psalm 87, it refers to the entrance to God's nearness and blessings.
Rahab: A poetic name for Egypt, often used in the Bible to represent enemies of Israel.
Babel: An ancient city in Mesopotamia, often used as a symbol of human pride and rebellion against God.
Philistines: A people group who were enemies of Israel and lived in the area that is now the Gaza Strip and parts of Israel.
Tyre: An ancient city in Phoenicia, known for its wealth and trade. In Psalm 87 it is mentioned along with other nations as an example that God loves all peoples.
Ethiopia: A country in Africa which in this context represents nations far from Israel. It emphasizes that God's love and grace apply to all nations and peoples.

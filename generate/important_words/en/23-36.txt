Sennacherib: The king of Assyria who attacked Judah and besieged Jerusalem during the reign of King Hezekiah.
Hezekiah: The king of Judah who sought God's help when Jerusalem was threatened by Sennacherib and his army.
Rabshakeh: A high officer in the Assyrian army, sent by Sennacherib to persuade the people of Jerusalem to surrender.
Jerusalem: The capital city of the kingdom of Judah, the center of Jewish faith and worship of God.
Judah: An ancient kingdom in southern Israel, ruled by King Hezekiah at the time of Isaiah chapter 36.
The angel of the Lord: A divine messenger from God who carries out God's will and protects His people.
God's house: Also known as the temple in Jerusalem, the place where the Jews worshiped God and performed religious rituals.
Deliver: To set free or rescue someone from a dangerous or difficult situation. In this context, it refers to God's act of saving Jerusalem from the Assyrians.

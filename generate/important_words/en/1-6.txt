Nephilim: A group of beings mentioned in Genesis 6, whose origin and nature are debated; some believe they were giants or angels.
Sons of God: The term used to describe divine beings, possibly angels, who according to Genesis 6 had children with the daughters of men.
Daughters of men: Women born of human parents who became involved in relationships with the sons of God, resulting in the Nephilim.
Noah: The main character in the flood story, a righteous man chosen by God to survive the great flood and save his family and the animals on earth.
The ark: A large ship built by Noah according to God's instructions to protect him, his family, and the animals from the flood.
The flood: A great deluge sent by God to destroy all living creatures on earth, except for Noah, his family, and the animals on the ark.
Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting in accordance with God's will and commands, something for which Noah is praised in Genesis 6.

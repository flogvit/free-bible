Winters: A metaphor for the people of Israel, representing their worthlessness and uselessness in their current state.
Fire: A symbol of God's judgment and punishment upon Israel for their sins and disobedience.
Burn: The process by which something is destroyed or consumed by fire, representing God's punishment and cleansing of Israel.
Fruitful: Describes the ability to produce a good harvest or results, here used to show Israel's lack of productivity and value.
The Lord: A title referring to God, the almighty Creator and ruler over the universe and all life.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and punishment of people based on their actions and faith.

Lord: A title used to refer to God, the almighty Creator.
Command: Instructions or orders given by God to His people.
Love: A deep devotion and care for God and His commands.
Heart: Symbolizes a person's inner being, including thoughts, feelings, and will.
Soul: The spiritual and immortal part of a human being, connected to their identity and relationship with God.
Strength: To show commitment and perseverance in faith and obedience to God's commands.
Teaching: Instruction and guidance about God's ways and commands.
Signs: Symbols that represent God's promises and presence.
Doorposts: Structural elements in a doorway, symbolizing the boundary between private and public space.
Land: The area that God promised to give His people, often referred to as "the Promised Land."

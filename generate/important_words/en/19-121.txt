Lord: The Almighty God, who is the source of help and protection for people.
Hill: To save or protect from danger or difficulties.
Keep: To hold safe and unharmed; to guard against harm or loss.
Sleep: A natural state of rest for body and mind, where consciousness temporarily ceases.
Sun: The star closest to the earth and the most important source of light and warmth.
Moon: Earth's natural satellite, which influences tides and reflects sunlight at night.
Harm: Physical or emotional pain, suffering, or loss.
Soul: The spiritual or immaterial part of a person, considered to be immortal.
Come: To approach or arrive at a place or situation.
Go: To move from one place to another by using the legs and feet.

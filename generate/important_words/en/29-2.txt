The Lord's Day: An event or point in the future when God will judge humanity and the world.
Imminent: An expression meaning that something is about to happen soon.
Locust swarm: A large gathering of locusts that destroys crops and vegetation, used here as a symbol of destruction and suffering.
Zion: The name of a hill in Jerusalem, often used to refer to the entire city or the Jewish people in general.
The Lord: A title for God that emphasizes his sovereignty and authority over all things.
Repentance: A feeling of remorse and sorrow over one's sins, along with a desire to change one's behavior and turn to God.
Blessing: God's favor and protection, often shown through material and spiritual abundance.
Outpouring: In this context, God's act of sending the Holy Spirit to people to give them power and guidance.
Prophesy: To deliver God's message or predict future events under divine inspiration.

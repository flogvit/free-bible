Sacrifice: Ritual actions where animals, food, or other gifts are given to God as a form of worship and gratitude.  
Blood: The fluid that circulates in the bodies of humans and animals, symbolizing life. In Leviticus 17, blood holds special significance in connection with sacrifices.  
Tabernacle: A temporary holy place where the Israelites worshipped God during their wilderness journey after leaving Egypt.  
Sin: An action or thought that breaks God's laws and commands, leading to spiritual separation from God.  
Atonement sacrifice: A sacrifice performed to make atonement for sins and cleanse the guilty person, so that he or she could restore a good relationship with God.  
Foreigners: People who were not part of Israelite society but lived among them. They also had to follow certain rules and regulations concerning sacrifices and blood.

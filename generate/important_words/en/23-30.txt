Here are some important words from Isaiah chapter 30 and their explanations:
Syria: A country in the Middle East that was a significant power in ancient times and is mentioned in Isaiah as part of Israel's political landscape.
Egypt: An ancient civilization in North Africa, known for its pyramids and pharaohs. In Isaiah chapter 30, Israel is warned against seeking help from Egypt.
The Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator who according to Genesis created heaven, earth, and all life.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment of sinners or nations that have done evil. In Isaiah chapter 30, Isaiah prophesies about God's judgment over Israel and their enemies.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and mercy shown to people. In Isaiah chapter 30 there is also a promise of grace and restoration for Israel after they turn away from their sins.
Repent: To change one's behavior and attitude, especially regarding one's relationship with God. Isaiah urges Israel to repent and trust in the Lord instead of seeking help from other nations.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem and a symbol of God's presence and protection of His people. In Isaiah chapter 30, God promises to be a protector and savior for Zion and its inhabitants.

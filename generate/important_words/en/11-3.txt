Solomon: King David's son who succeeded him as king over Israel and was known for his wisdom and wealth.
Wisdom: The ability to understand, judge, and act in a way that is wise and righteous, based on knowledge and experience.
Dream: An experience that occurs in the mind while sleeping, often consisting of thoughts, images, and feelings.
Fear of God: Deep respect and awe for God, which often leads to obedience to his commands and will.
Temple: A holy place dedicated to God, where people can worship and offer gifts to him.
Sacrifice: A gift given to God, often in the form of animals, food, or other valuable things, as a sign of gratitude, reverence, or atonement.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with God's will and commands, as well as treating others with justice and equality.

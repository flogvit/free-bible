1 Chronicles 27:
The children of Israel: A collective term for the twelve tribes of Israel, descended from Jacob's twelve sons.
Monthly watch: A period of one month during which a group of soldiers was responsible for protecting the king and the kingdom.
Commander: A military leader who had responsibility for leading the troops in war and ensuring their safety and discipline.
Twelve: The number of tribes in Israel, also representing the twelve months of the year, each under the care of one commander.
King David: The second king of the united kingdom of Israel and Judah, known for his wisdom and skill as a warrior and musician.
Zakarites: A clan or family group within the tribe of Levi, assigned special duties related to the temple and priestly service.
Scribes: Individuals responsible for keeping official records, documents, and laws in the kingdom.
Stewards: Persons entrusted with managing the king's property, resources, and finances.

Peace: A state of harmony and absence of conflict, often associated with God's protection and blessing.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and justly, in accordance with God's laws and will.
Lord: A title often used to refer to God in the Old Testament, especially in prophetic texts such as Isaiah.
Salvation: God's act of saving humanity from sin and its consequences, as well as granting eternal life and restored fellowship with Him.
Strength: The ability to face adversity and challenges with power and endurance, often given by God to believers.
Nation: A community of people who share a common culture, history, and territory, and are subject to a particular government.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and treatment of human actions and hearts, both individually and collectively.
Death: The end of physical life on earth, and a transition to either eternal life with God or eternal separation from Him.

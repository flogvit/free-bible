Life: The state that characterizes organisms with growth, reproduction, and the ability to respond to stimuli.
Death: The end of life for an organism, where biological functions cease permanently.
Righteousness: A moral principle concerning right and wrong, involving acting in accordance with ethical norms and rules.
Invocation: To pray or call upon a higher power, such as God, for help, guidance, or protection.
Hope: A feeling of optimism and expectation that something positive will happen in the future.
Despair: A feeling of deep sorrow, hopelessness, or loss of faith in oneself or one's situation.
Wisdom: The ability to think and act with insight, knowledge, experience, and understanding, using these to make good choices and decisions.

Sin offering: An offering sacrificed to God to atone for sin and obtain forgiveness.
Unintentional act: An accidental error or action, in this context a sin committed without intent.
High priest: The supreme religious leader of the Israelite priesthood, responsible for performing sacrifices and other ritual acts.
Communal offering: An offering given to God on behalf of the entire community to atone for their sins.
Sin: An act or thought that breaks God's laws and commands, leading to separation from God.
Atoning blood: The blood from a sacrificial animal used in ritual actions to cleanse and atone for sin.
Sacrificial animal: An animal sacrificed to God as part of a ritual, typically an innocent and unblemished creature.

Enemies: Persons or groups who stand in opposition to or are in conflict with God's people.
Covenant: An agreement or alliance between different parties to achieve a common goal.
Hiding places: Places where someone hides to avoid discovery or danger.
To put an end to: To completely remove or destroy something.
Name: The identification or mark that distinguishes a person, object, or concept from others.
Israel: The name of the ancient kingdom and people chosen by God who played a central role in the Old Testament.
Mountains: Large natural formations rising above the earth's surface, often used as symbols of strength and stability.
Sela: A mountain or rock formation mentioned in the book of Psalms, believed to be a place in present-day Jordan.

Jehovah: The name of God in the book of Job, representing the Almighty Creator.
Foundation Stone: The foundation or basis of the earth, used as a metaphor to describe the origin and stability of the earth.
Morning Stars: A poetic reference to angels, symbolizing their presence and joy during the creation of the earth.
Chaos of the Sea: A metaphor for chaos and disorder before God's intervention and the creation of the earth.
Doors of the Clouds: A poetic description of how rain comes from the clouds, as if they were doors that open and close.
Light and Darkness: Terms representing day and night, as well as the divine distinction between good and evil.
Source of Life: A reference to water, which is a necessary component for life and growth on earth.
Wild Animals: Animals that are not domesticated and live in nature, representing God's creation and the diversity of his creatures.

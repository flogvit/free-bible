Lord: The name often used to refer to God in the Old Testament, representing his authority and power.
Bless: To express gratitude or ask for God's favor and protection for someone or something.
Soul: The immaterial and eternal part of a human being, regarded as the center of personality and emotions.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks with God's will and moral law.
Grace: God's unconditional love and mercy given to people without them deserving it.
Merciful: The quality of showing compassion and grace, especially toward those who suffer or have done wrong.
Patient: Tolerant and willing to wait for changes or improvements, even when it takes a long time.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and in accordance with moral laws and principles.
Compassion: Sympathy and forgiveness shown toward someone over whom one has power, especially God's compassion toward people.
Remove: To take away or separate; here used of God removing sin and guilt from people.
Eagle: A symbol of strength and renewal, used here as a metaphor for how God renews human youthful vigor.

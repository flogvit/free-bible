Vow Offering: A gift or offering given voluntarily to God as an expression of gratitude, devotion, or fulfillment of a vow.  
Value: The monetary amount associated with a person, property, or animal in the context of vow offerings and redemption.  
Redeem: To buy back or regain something previously given away, sold, or sacrificed, often by paying a certain sum of money.  
Tithe: A tenth part of the harvest, livestock, or income given to the priests as a form of tax to God and his servants.  
Measurements of the Sanctuary: Specific units of measurement used in Leviticus to determine values and quantities related to vow offerings and sacrifices.  
Curse: A negative consequence or punishment resulting from breaking God's commands or failing to fulfill vows.

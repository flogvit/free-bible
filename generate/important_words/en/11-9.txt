Solomon: King of Israel and son of King David, known for his wisdom and wealth.
Temple: A sacred building dedicated to God, built by Solomon in Jerusalem as a place for worship and sacrifices.
Hiram: King of Tyre, an ally of Solomon who helped him obtain materials and labor to build the temple.
Covenant: An agreement between God and His people, where God promises protection and blessing in exchange for obedience and faithfulness.
Intercession: Praying to God on behalf of others, especially for their needs, protection, or blessing.
Obey: To follow God's commands and instructions as an expression of respect and faithfulness.
Bless: To grant divine favor, protection, or prosperity to someone or something.
Punish: God's act of bringing suffering, loss, or destruction as a result of sin or disobedience.

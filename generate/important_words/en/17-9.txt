Ester: The main character in the book, a Jewish woman who becomes queen of Persia and saves her people from destruction.
Ahasuerus: King of Persia, also known as Xerxes I, who marries Esther and later helps her save the Jews.
Mordecai: Esther's older cousin and guardian, who discovers a conspiracy against the king and later becomes the highest official in the kingdom.
Haman: The king's wicked advisor who plans to destroy the Jews, but is eventually hanged for his evil deeds.
Purim: A Jewish festival that celebrates victory over Haman and the deliverance of the Jews, established in Esther 9.
Lot: An object cast or drawn to decide a matter, often used in biblical texts. Haman used lots to determine the date for the destruction of the Jews.
Edict: An official order or proclamation issued by an authority. In Esther 9 there are two edicts: one allowing the destruction of the Jews and another allowing them to defend themselves.
Restoration: The process of bringing something back to its original state. In Esther 9 this refers to the restoration of the Jews after they were saved from Haman's evil plans.

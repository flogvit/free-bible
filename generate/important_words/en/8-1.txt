Naomi: A woman from Bethlehem who was married to Elimelek and mother to Mahlon and Kiljon. After her husband and sons died, she became a widow and returned to Bethlehem with her daughter-in-law Ruth.
Ruth: A Moabite woman who was married to Naomi's son Mahlon. After his death, she followed Naomi back to Bethlehem and became part of her family. She later married Boaz and became an ancestor of King David.
Boaz: A wealthy man from Bethlehem who was a relative of Naomi's late husband, Elimelek. He showed kindness to Ruth and eventually married her, securing a future for both her and Naomi.
Elimelek: Naomi's deceased husband who moved his family to Moab because of famine in Bethlehem.
Kiljon: Naomi's younger son who was married to Orpa. He died in Moab along with his brother Mahlon and father Elimelek.
Mahlon: Naomi's older son who was married to Ruth. He also died in Moab, leaving Ruth a widow.
Orpa: Another Moabite woman, married to Naomi's son Kiljon. After his death she chose to remain in Moab, while Ruth followed Naomi back to Bethlehem.
Gleaning: A practice where landowners allowed the poor and widows to gather leftover grain and other crops after harvest. Ruth used this practice to provide food for herself and Naomi.
Kinsman-Redeemer: A relative responsible for ensuring the welfare of a widow and the family of a deceased man, including the possibility of marrying the widow to continue the family line. Boaz took on this role for Ruth and Naomi's family.

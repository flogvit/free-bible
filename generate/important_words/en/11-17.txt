Elijah: A prophet in Israel who spoke God's words and conveyed His message to the people.
Drought: A long period without rain, leading to water shortages and poor harvests.
Zarephath: A city in Sidon where Elijah was sent by God to meet a widow and her son.
Widow: A woman who has lost her husband and often faces economic and social challenges.
Jar: A hollow ceramic or clay container used to store food, water, or other goods.
Oil: Liquid fat used for cooking, lighting, and anointing in biblical times.
Raven: A large black bird that God used to feed Elijah at the Brook Cherith during the drought.
Resurrection: The miraculous act of bringing someone back to life after death, as when Elijah revived the widow's son.
Brook Cherith: A small stream near the Jordan River where Elijah was hidden and received food and drink during the drought.

Amos: A prophet in the Old Testament who delivered God's message to the people of Israel.
Book 2: The second of nine chapters in the book of Amos, part of the twelve minor prophets in the Old Testament.
Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment or consequences for human actions, especially sin and injustice.
Sin: Violation of God's law, moral error, or actions that go against God's will.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people and the land they inhabited, often referred to as the promised land.
Judah: One of the twelve tribes of Israel and a kingdom south of Israel.
Enemies: Nations or groups that oppose God's people, often used as instruments of God's judgment.
Righteousness: Acting in accordance with God's will and morality, being just and good.
Punishment: Consequences or suffering resulting from sin and injustice, often given by God.

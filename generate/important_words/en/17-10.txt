Esther: The main character in the book, a Jewish woman who becomes queen of Persia.
Mordecai: A Jew and Esther's adoptive father, who exposes a conspiracy against the king.
Haman: A high-ranking Persian official who plans to destroy the Jews.
King Xerxes: The king of Persia who marries Esther and later saves the Jews.
Purim: A Jewish festival that celebrates the deliverance of the Jews from Haman's plot.
Fasting: To abstain from food and drink for a set time, often for spiritual purposes.
The lot: A method of random selection, used by Haman to choose the date for the destruction of the Jews.
Influence: The ability to affect others, especially in positions of power, to achieve a desired outcome.
Loyalty: Faithfulness and commitment toward a person, cause, or principle.

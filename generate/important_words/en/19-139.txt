Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his sovereignty and power.
Search: To look carefully into, explore, or examine something. In Psalm 139 it means that God knows us completely and sees into our innermost being.
Know: To have knowledge of or understanding about something. In this context it refers to God's all-knowing nature and his ability to know us fully.
Form: The process of shaping, making, or creating something. Here it refers to how God formed man in the mother's womb.
Darkness: Means absence of light, but can also symbolize evil or ignorance. In Psalm 139 it is used to show that God sees everything, even what is hidden or unknown to us.
Follow: To be with or accompany someone. In this context it means that God is with us and protects us wherever we go.

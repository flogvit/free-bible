Misery: A state of great distress, suffering, or discomfort, which Job experiences in chapter 30.
Contempt: A strong feeling of aversion or lack of respect toward someone or something. Job feels despised by younger men and the society around him.
Shame: A feeling of guilt, regret, or humiliation due to bad actions or mistakes. Job expresses his shame over his current situation and how others treat him.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life.
Hopelessness: A feeling of despair and lack of belief that the situation will improve. Job feels hopelessness in the face of his sufferings.
Pain: Physical or emotional suffering that Job experiences throughout the book.

Jehoiada: A priest in Jerusalem who played a key role in overthrowing Queen Athaliah and restoring King Joash to the throne.
Athaliah: An evil queen of Judah who killed all royal descendants to keep power, but failed to kill Joash.
Joash: The young king of Judah who was protected and raised by Jehoiada, and later restored as king with Jehoiada's help.
Temple: A holy place where people worshiped God and performed religious ceremonies. In this chapter, it refers to Solomon's temple in Jerusalem.
Ark of the Covenant: A sacred relic that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, which God gave to Moses on Mount Sinai. The ark symbolized God's presence among the people of Israel.
Levites: Members of the tribe of Levi, who were responsible for serving in the temple and carrying out religious duties.
Sabbath: The seventh day of the week, on which God rested after creating the world. The Israelites were also to rest and honor God on this day.

Jar: A clay container symbolizing the people of Jerusalem and their fragility.
Prophet: A person who speaks on behalf of God and conveys His message to humanity.
Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel, a central place for Jewish faith and culture.
The word of the Lord: An expression used to describe God's instructions and messages given through prophets.
Valley of Hinnom: A valley south of Jerusalem associated with pagan rituals and child sacrifice.
Topheth: A location in the Valley of Hinnom where idolatry and child sacrifice were practiced.
Thigh bones: Bones from the thigh or leg, here used as a symbol of destruction and death.
Destruction: The process of completely destroying something, often referring to cities and societies in the Bible.
Shame: The feeling of humiliation and degradation resulting from an action or situation.
Captivity: The state of being imprisoned or under the control of another power, often used regarding the people of Israel.

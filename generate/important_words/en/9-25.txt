Abigail: A wise and beautiful woman who was married to Nabal, a rich and hard-hearted man. She later becomes David's wife.
David: A young shepherd chosen by God to become Israel's king after Saul. He is known for his bravery and faithfulness to God.
Nabal: A rich and hard-hearted man who was married to Abigail. His unwillingness to help David leads to his death.
Samuel: A prophet and judge in Israel who anointed both Saul and David as kings over Israel.
The Lord: Another name for God, the almighty creator of heaven, earth, and all life.
Prayer: Communication between people and God, often to ask for help, guidance, or to express gratitude.
Blessing: A positive force or gift from God that brings protection, prosperity, or happiness.
Retribution: The act of punishing someone for their unjust actions, often seen as God's justice.
Reconciliation: The process of restoring a relationship between people and God or between two individuals after a conflict.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good decisions and act in a fair manner.

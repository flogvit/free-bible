Enemies: Persons who are opponents or have hostile intentions toward another person, often referred to in the Book of Psalms as the wicked or unrighteous.
Protection: The act of guarding or securing someone from harm, threats, or dangers. In Psalm 140, the psalmist asks for God's protection against enemies.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, especially in Jewish and Christian tradition, expressing respect and reverence.
Prayer: Communication with God, where a person asks for help, guidance, thanks, or forgiveness. The psalmist asks for God's intervention against his enemies in Psalm 140.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and morally, in accordance with God's will and commandments. The psalmist seeks justice against his enemies and their unjust actions.
Wicked: The opposite of good, representing everything that is harmful, immoral, and contrary to God's will. In Psalm 140, the wicked are seen as enemies who must be fought.
Salvation: The term used to describe God's deliverance of people from sin, suffering, and death. The psalmist asks for salvation from his enemies in Psalm 140.

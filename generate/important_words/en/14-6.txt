Solomon: King Solomon was the son of King David and Bathsheba, and he is known for his wisdom and for building the temple in Jerusalem.
Temple: A holy place for worship and offerings to God, built by King Solomon in Jerusalem as a permanent home for the Ark of the Covenant and God's presence.
Prayer: Communication between people and God, where one asks for help, gives thanks, or expresses thoughts and desires.
Ark of the Covenant: A sacred chest that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, Aaron's staff, and a golden jar of manna. It symbolized God's presence and covenant with the people of Israel.
Forgiveness: God's act of removing sin and guilt from those who repent and seek his grace.
Foreigner: A person who does not belong to the nation of Israel but may come to the temple to pray to God and seek his blessing.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks God's will and laws, leading to separation from God and his blessings.

Elisha: A prophet in Israel who performed miracles and spread God's message.
Syria: A country northeast of Israel, often in conflict with Israel during this period.
War: Armed conflict between nations or groups; here it refers to hostilities between Israel and Syria.
Army: An organized military force consisting of soldiers fighting for a nation or leader.
Spiritual vision: The ability to see the supernatural or divine, given by God to chosen individuals.
Miracle: An extraordinary event that cannot be explained by natural laws and is therefore regarded as an act of divine intervention.
Blindness: Loss of sight; here it refers to a temporary condition inflicted on Syrian soldiers by God through Elisha.
The prophet's servant: An assistant or disciple of the prophet Elisha, who helps him and learns from his teaching.
Dothan: A city in Israel where Elisha was located when the Syrian army came to capture him.
Samaria: The capital of the kingdom of Israel, where Elisha led the blind Syrian soldiers.

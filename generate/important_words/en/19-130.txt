Lord: A title used to show respect and reverence toward God, acknowledging His sovereignty and power.
Abyss: A symbol of distress, despair, or difficult situations that people may experience in life.
Prayer: Communication between humans and God, where people express their thoughts, feelings, concerns, and desires to Him.
Watchman: A person who keeps watch over something; in this context, a metaphor for being alert and waiting for God's answer or intervention.
Morning: An early part of the day, here used as a symbol of hope and expectation that God will intervene and bring help.
Redemption: Deliverance from sin, guilt, and punishment through God's grace and mercy.
Israel: The name of God's chosen people in the Old Testament, here used to represent believers who seek God's grace and help.

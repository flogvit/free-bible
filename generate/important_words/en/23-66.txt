The Lord's house: The temple in Jerusalem, a holy place where God was worshiped and offerings were given.
New heaven and new earth: A new creation, a renewed and perfect world that God will establish after the time of judgment.
Zion: Another name for Jerusalem, used symbolically to represent God's people and his kingdom on earth.
Birth: A metaphor describing the establishment of the new community and God's kingdom, born through God's intervention.
Shout of joy: An expression of gladness and gratitude toward God for his salvation and blessings.
Punishment: God's righteous judgment and consequences for those who have sinned and turned away from him.
Offering: Ritual acts performed to honor God and seek atonement for sins.
Cleansing: The process of being purified from sin and impurity in order to draw near to God.
Glory: God's majestic presence and power, which radiates out and is manifested in his creation.

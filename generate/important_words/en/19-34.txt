Lord: A title for God that expresses his sovereignty and power over all.
Fear: An awe or reverence for God, based on recognition of his greatness and holy nature.
Refuge: A place to seek protection and safety; in this context, it means trusting in God for security and comfort.
Praise: To express gratitude, honor, and respect to God for his goodness and grace.
Righteous: Those who live in accordance with God's will and moral standards, and are therefore regarded as righteous in his sight.
Delivered: To be set free from a difficult or dangerous situation, often used of God's intervention to rescue people from distress.
Angel: A divine messenger and servant of God, who carries out his will and helps people in need.
Brokenhearted: A person who is crushed or humbled, often due to suffering or adversity. In this context, it refers to God's care for those who suffer.

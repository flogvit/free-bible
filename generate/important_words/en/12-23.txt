Josiah: King of Judah who carried out a comprehensive religious reform and removed idolatry from the land.
Reform: A change or improvement, especially in a political, social, or economic structure.
Covenant: An agreement between two parties, often used to describe the covenant between God and the people of Israel.
Feasts: Religious celebrations and ceremonies that mark important events or beliefs.
Temple: A holy place where people gather to worship God and perform religious rituals.
Idols: False gods or images that people worship instead of the true God.
Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God to the people.
Sacrifice: A gift or act given to God as a sign of devotion, gratitude, or atonement for sins.

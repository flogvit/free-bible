Census: A process in which the number of people in a group or nation is counted and recorded. In Numbers, this is a counting of the children of Israel.
Levi: One of Jacob's twelve sons, and ancestor of the Levites, who had special religious duties and responsibilities in Israel.
Tabernacle: A portable sanctuary tent that served as a temporary place for worship and God's presence among the children of Israel during their wilderness journey.
High Priest: The supreme religious leader in Israel, responsible for performing sacrifices and ritual acts in the tabernacle and later in the temple.
Tribes: The twelve groups that made up the people of Israel, each with its own territory and descendants from one of Jacob's twelve sons.
Army: The military forces of the children of Israel, who protected the people and fought wars against hostile nations.

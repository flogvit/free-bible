Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to comprehend and evaluate situations and the consequences of actions; often linked to wisdom and insight.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with moral laws and ethical norms; to be fair and impartial in one's conduct.
Discipline: Correction or training used to teach and guide someone toward proper behavior and character.
Humility: The quality of being modest and not arrogant; recognizing one's own limitations and respecting others.
Pride: An excessive sense of self-worth and self-satisfaction that can lead to poor choices and destructive consequences.
Patience: The ability to endure difficulties or discomfort without complaining or losing heart; to be patient and persevering.
Fear of the Lord: A reverent awe and respect for God that leads to obedience and trust in His guidance and will.
Wrath: A strong feeling of displeasure or anger that can lead to conflict and harm if not controlled.
Upright: To be honest, sincere, and morally right in all aspects of life; to act in accordance with ethical principles.

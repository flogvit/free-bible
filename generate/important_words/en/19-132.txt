The Lord's dwelling: The place where God lives or is present, often associated with the temple in Jerusalem.
Temple: A sacred building dedicated to worship and religious ceremonies.
David: King of Israel and a central figure in the book of Psalms, known for his piety and musical talents.
Covenant: An agreement between two parties, here between God and His people, involving promises and obligations from both sides.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem and a symbol of God's presence and protection of His people.
Humility: An attitude of respect and submission toward God, acknowledging one's limitations and need for divine help.
Inheritance: That which is passed down from one generation to the next, including material goods, knowledge, and spiritual blessings.
Blessing: An expression of God's favor and goodwill, bringing protection, prosperity, and happiness.

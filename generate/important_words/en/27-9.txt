Prophecy: A prediction or revelation about future events given by God through a prophet.
Jerusalem: The historical and religious city in Israel, important to Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.
Gabriel: One of God's chief angels, known for bringing messages and revelations from God to people.
Seventy weeks: A prophetic time period mentioned in the book of Daniel 9, interpreted as 490 years, linked to Jewish history and the coming of Jesus.
Messiah: A title meaning "the anointed one" referring to a future leader or savior in Judaism. In Christianity, Jesus Christ is regarded as the Messiah.
Sacrifice for sin: An offering where an animal is killed and presented to God as a symbolic act to atone for human sins.
Transgression: An action that breaks God's law and is considered sinful.
Prayer: Communication with God through words or thoughts, often to ask for help, give thanks, or worship.
Repentance: The feeling of regret over past actions and the desire to change in order to follow God's way more closely.

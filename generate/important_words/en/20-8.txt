Wisdom: A virtue and a characteristic that represents understanding, insight, and sound judgment. Wisdom is personified in the book of Proverbs 8 and described as a helper and counselor to God.
Understanding: The ability to think, comprehend, and learn from experiences and situations. Understanding is closely linked to wisdom and is important for making good decisions.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and morally correct, and treating others with fairness and equality. Righteousness is an important value in the book of Proverbs.
Discipline: Training or upbringing that is necessary to correct and guide human behavior. Discipline is important for developing moral character and wisdom.
Knowledge: Information, facts, and skills acquired through experience, observation, or study. Knowledge is the foundation for wisdom and understanding.
Fear of God: Respect and reverence for God, which leads to obedience to His commands and a desire to live a righteous life.

Babylon: An ancient city and kingdom in Mesopotamia, known for its power and magnificent architecture.
The rivers: Waterways referring to the rivers of Babylon, where the people of Israel sat and wept during their exile.
Zion: A hill in Jerusalem and a symbol of God's dwelling place and Israel's spiritual home.
Forget: To lose memory of something or not remember it; here used as a prayer that Jerusalem should never be forgotten.
Right hand: Symbolizes strength and skill, especially in music and battle.
Daughter: Here refers to the people or nation coming from a specific city or land, such as "daughter Babylon."
Vengeance: Retribution or punishment for a previous action, often with a desire for justice or redress.
Edom: An ancient nation and enemy of Israel, who called for Jerusalem's destruction.

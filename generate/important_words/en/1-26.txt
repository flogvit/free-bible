Isaac: Son of Abraham and Sarah, an important patriarch in the Hebrew tradition and father to Jacob and Esau.
Abimelech: Philistine king with whom Isaac negotiated regarding wells and land rights.
Rebekah: Isaac's wife and mother of Jacob and Esau.
Jacob: Son of Isaac and Rebekah, twin brother to Esau and ancestor of the twelve tribes of Israel.
Esau: Son of Isaac and Rebekah, twin brother to Jacob and ancestor of the Edomites.
Covenant: A sacred agreement between God and people, often connected with promises and blessings.
Blessing: An expression of God's favor and protection over an individual or nation.
Well: A source of water that is vital for life and prosperity in desert environments.
Philistines: An ancient people who lived in Canaan and often came into conflict with the people of Israel.

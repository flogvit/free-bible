Abraham: Patriarch of the people of Israel and a central figure in Genesis, with whom God makes a covenant.
Isaac: Abraham's son, who is also an important figure in Genesis and is regarded as the heir to the covenant between God and Abraham.
Rebekah: A young woman from Mesopotamia, chosen as Isaac's wife and becomes the mother of Jacob and Esau.
Servant: An unnamed servant of Abraham, given the task of finding a wife for Isaac among Abraham's relatives.
Nahor: Abraham's brother, living in Mesopotamia, whose family the servant visits to find a wife for Isaac.
Laban: Rebekah's brother, who gives his blessing for her to marry Isaac.
Blessing: An expression of approval or a wish for happiness and prosperity, often given by an authority figure.
Covenant: An agreement or promise between two parties, in this case between God and Abraham, which includes promises of land and descendants.

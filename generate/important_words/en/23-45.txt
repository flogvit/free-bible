Cyrus: A Persian king who is referred to as God's anointed and used by God to free the Israelites from Babylonian captivity.
Lord: Another name for God, often used in the Old Testament to show respect and reverence.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from their sins and restoring them to a right relationship with Him.
Hidden places: Places that are unknown or inaccessible to humans but known to God.
Gold: A precious metal symbolizing wealth, power, and divine blessing.
Silver: Another valuable metal, also associated with wealth and divine blessing.
Darkness: Symbolizes evil, sin, and ignorance.
Light: Represents God's truth, righteousness, and wisdom.
Righteousness: The quality of being morally right and just in God's sight.

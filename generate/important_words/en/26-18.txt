Sin: An act, thought, or attitude that breaks with God's will and morality.
Righteousness: To act in accordance with God's will and ethical principles, the opposite of sin.
Soul: The spiritual and immortal part of a person, considered to be God's creation and existing beyond death.
Death: The end of life, when the body stops functioning and the soul separates from the physical world.
Repentance: A change of heart and mind that leads a person to turn away from sin and toward God.
Inheritance: That which is passed down from one generation to another, especially in relation to sin and righteousness.
Responsibility: The principle that each person is accountable for their own actions and the consequences thereof.
Life: The biological and spiritual existence that begins at conception and continues through death and eternal life.

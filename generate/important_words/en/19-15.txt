Lord: A title that shows respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his sovereignty and power.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with God's will and morality, behaving uprightly and ethically.
Blameless: A description of a person without moral faults or defects, living a life of purity and holiness.
Truth: That which is true and correct, in agreement with God's word and reality.
Slander: To speak negatively or maliciously about others behind their backs, which goes against God's desire for human interaction.
Harm: To cause physical, emotional, or spiritual pain to someone, either directly or indirectly.
Neighbor: Another word for fellow human being, used to describe how one should treat others with love and respect.
Lending money: Lending money to someone, often with interest. In Psalm 15, there is a call not to demand unreasonable interest from those in need.
Swear: To promise or confirm something with great seriousness and conviction, often with God as witness.

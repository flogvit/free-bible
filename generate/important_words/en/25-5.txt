1. Lamentations: A collection of five poetic laments in the Old Testament, expressing grief and suffering after the destruction of Jerusalem.
2. Jerusalem: The holy city for Jews, Christians, and Muslims; the center of the kingdom of Judah and the site of the first and second temples.
3. Lord: Another name for God, the almighty Creator who is responsible for maintaining order and justice in the world.
4. Discipline: Punishment or correction given by God to guide and correct people, often as a result of sin and disobedience.
5. Bondage: A state of being under another's control or power, often used metaphorically to describe the people of Israel under foreign rule.
6. Humiliation: The feeling of shame and loss of honor resulting from external circumstances or personal failings; a theme in Lamentations that shows the consequences of Israel's sins.
7. Prayer: Communication with God where people seek help, guidance, or forgiveness; an important aspect of faith and devotion in Lamentations.

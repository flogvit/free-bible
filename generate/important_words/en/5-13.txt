False prophets: People who claim to speak on behalf of God but spread lies and mislead the people.  
Signs and wonders: Miracles or supernatural events used to prove a prophet's authority.  
Detestable gods: False gods or idols that people worship instead of the true God.  
To follow: To obey and hold fast to God's commands and guidance.  
Death penalty: The punishment given for serious crimes, including leading others away from God, in this case by stoning.  
Family: Close relatives, such as parents, siblings, spouse, and children, who can be influenced by false prophets.  
Cities: Places where people live and gather, which may be destroyed by God's wrath if they turn away from Him.

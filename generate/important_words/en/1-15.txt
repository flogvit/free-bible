Abram: The main character in chapter 15, later known as Abraham, is a central figure in Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.
Lord: A title used to refer to God, the almighty Creator.
Covenant: An agreement or commitment between two parties, here between God and Abram.
Land: The area that God promises to give to Abram's descendants, later known as Canaan or the Promised Land.
Vision: A divine revelation or dream that provides insight or guidance.
Sacrificial animal: An animal offered to God as part of a covenant or religious ceremony.
Burnt offering: A ritual act in which smoke from burning offerings rises up to God as a symbolic gesture.
Future: What will happen later; here it refers to prophecies about Abram's descendants and their destiny.
Servants: People who perform work or services for others, especially in a subordinate or servile relationship.
River of Egypt: A reference to the Nile, the most important river in Egypt, symbolizing the future slavery of Abram's descendants in Egypt.
Four hundred years: The period during which Abram's descendants will be oppressed and serve as slaves in Egypt.
Judgment: God's just punishment or reward; here it refers to judgment upon the nations that oppress Abram's descendants.
Fourth generation: An expression referring to Abram's descendants four generations later, when they will return to the Promised Land.

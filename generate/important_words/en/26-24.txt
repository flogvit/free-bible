God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Ezekiel: The prophet who wrote the book of Ezekiel and received visions and messages from God.
Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel and a central theme in the book of Ezekiel, symbolizing God's people.
Cooking Pot: A metaphor for Jerusalem, representing the city's impending judgment and destruction.
Cleansing: The process by which God removes sin and impurity from His people to restore their relationship with Him.
Judgment: God's righteous punishment upon sin and evil, especially in connection with the destruction of Jerusalem.
Restoration: God's promise to restore and renew His people after judgment and exile.
Exile: The period when the Israelites were taken captive and held as prisoners in Babylon, an important context for the book of Ezekiel.
The Lord's Hand: An expression denoting God's power and presence in Ezekiel's life and prophetic ministry.

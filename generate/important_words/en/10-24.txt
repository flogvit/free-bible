Herod: King of Israel who ruled at the time of Jesus' birth and tried to kill him.
Prophecy: A divinely inspired statement or message about future events, often delivered through a prophet.
Plague: A deadly and contagious disease that affects a large number of people, in this case sent by God as punishment for David's sin.
David: King of Israel and the main character in 2 Samuel, known for his faith and courage, but also for his mistakes and sins.
Joab: David's army commander and nephew, who is loyal to David but also critical of some of his decisions.
Census: A counting of the population, in this context ordered by David, which leads to God's anger and punishment.
Angel: A supernatural being that serves as God's messenger and servant, in this context responsible for carrying out God's punishment.
Altar: A sacred structure where offerings and prayers are presented to God, in this case built by David to seek forgiveness.
Sin: An action or thought that breaks with God's will and laws, leading to behavior that harms oneself and others.

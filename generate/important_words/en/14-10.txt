Rehoboam: Son of King Solomon and successor as king over Israel.
Jeroboam: Former servant under Solomon, chosen by God to lead the ten northern tribes of Israel.
Yoke: Symbolizes a heavy burden or slavery, used here to describe the harsh labor imposed on the people by King Solomon.
Advisors: People who give counsel and guidance, especially to a leader or ruler.
Elders: Older individuals with experience and wisdom, often consulted for advice and direction.
Young men: The younger generation growing up under the leadership of elders, often lacking experience and wisdom.
Softer: To be more gentle, moderate, or flexible in one's treatment of others.
Harder: To be stricter, less willing to compromise, or more insensitive in one's treatment of others.
Ten tribes: The northern tribes of Israel that broke away from Rehoboam and formed their own kingdom under Jeroboam.

Isaiah: A prophet in the Old Testament who received revelations from God and conveyed them to the people of Israel.
The Spirit of the Lord: God's power and presence, which works in the world and gives wisdom, understanding, counsel, strength, knowledge, and fear of the Lord.
The Root of Jesse: A reference to King David's father, Jesse, symbolizing the Messiah's origin from David's royal line.
Messiah: The promised Savior and King whom the Jews awaited, and whom Christians identify as Jesus Christ.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and morally, in accordance with God's will and law.
Peace: A state of harmony and reconciliation between people and nations, as well as between people and God.
The wolf and the lamb: Symbols for former enemies living together in peace and harmony in the Messiah's kingdom.
Knowledge of the Lord: The understanding of God's nature, will, and plans, which will fill the earth when the Messiah reigns.

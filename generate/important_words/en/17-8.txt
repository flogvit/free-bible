Esther: The main character in the book, a Jewish woman who becomes queen of Persia and saves her people from destruction.
Xerxes: The king of Persia who is married to Esther and plays a central role in the story.
Mordecai: Esther's older cousin and guardian, who discovers a conspiracy against King Xerxes and helps Esther save the Jews.
Haman: A high official in the Persian empire who plans to destroy the Jews, but ultimately falls victim to his own evil schemes.
Purim: A Jewish festival that celebrates the deliverance of the Jews in Persia, established after the events in the book of Esther.
The Fast: A period during which Esther and the Jews fast and pray for God to intervene and save them from Haman's wicked plans.
Gallows: An instrument that Haman builds to hang Mordecai, but on which he himself ends up being hanged after his evil schemes are exposed.

Judgment: God's punishment and righteous consequences for human sins and godlessness.
Land: A geographical area, often used to refer to the land of Israel in the Old Testament.
Enemy: An adversary or malicious force working against God's people or God's will.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, also known as Jehovah or YHWH.
Sword: A weapon symbolizing war, destruction, and death; often used metaphorically in biblical texts.
Famine: Lack of food and nourishment, leading to suffering and death; can also be a consequence of God's judgment.
Plague: An infectious and deadly disease that can spread rapidly and cause great suffering and death; a sign of God's wrath.
Cry of lament: A loud and heart-wrenching sound expressing grief, suffering, and despair; often linked to God's punishment.
Trade: Economic activity involving the exchange of goods and services between people and nations.
Gold: A precious metal highly valued for its beauty and worth; often used as a symbol of wealth and power.

God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Psalm: A poetic religious text, often sung or recited as praise or prayer to God.
The Lord: Another name for God, used to show respect and reverence.
Earth: The planet we live on, created by God according to the Bible.
Heaven: The spiritual realm where God and angels dwell, also created by God.
Water: A life-giving liquid that covers much of the Earth's surface and is essential for all known forms of life.
Mountains: Large natural formations on the Earth's surface, often used as symbols of God's greatness and power.
Animals: Living creatures created by God, who inhabit the earth and play various roles in nature and human life.
Humanity: The being made in God's image, according to the Bible, and given responsibility to care for the earth and its creatures.
Praise: Expressing gratitude, honor, and worship to God through song, prayer, or other forms of expression.

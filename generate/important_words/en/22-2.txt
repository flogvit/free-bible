Solomon: King Solomon of Israel, who is believed to be the author of Song of Songs and regarded as a wise man in the Bible.
Bride: The woman mentioned in Song of Songs, loved by the bridegroom. In an allegorical interpretation, the bride often represents God's people or the church.
Bridegroom: The man who expresses his love for the bride in Song of Songs. In an allegorical interpretation, he often represents God or Jesus Christ.
Love: A deep devotion, passion, and tenderness between two people. The love between the bride and the bridegroom in Song of Songs often symbolizes God's love for His people.
Garden: A place where the bride and bridegroom meet and share their love. The garden can also symbolize paradise and harmony between God and humanity.
Spring: A time of year when nature awakens to life again after winter. Spring often symbolizes renewal, rebirth, and a new beginning.
Gazelle: A swift and graceful animal frequently mentioned in Song of Songs. The gazelle symbolizes beauty, grace, and freedom.

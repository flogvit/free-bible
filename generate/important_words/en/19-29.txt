Lord: Another name for God, meaning the almighty creator and ruler over the universe.
Voice: Here it refers to God's voice or word, expressing his will and power.
Thunder: A natural phenomenon associated with God's power and majesty, often used as a symbol of his presence and authority.
Water: Symbolizes life and cleansing in a biblical context, and can also represent God's grace and blessings.
Forest: Stands for the beauty and richness of nature, and is a reminder of God's creation and care for all life.
Temple: A holy place where people gather to worship and honor God, and seek his presence and guidance.
Strength: Refers to God's power and ability to protect, sustain, and give victory to his people.
Joy: A feeling of gladness and gratitude that comes from recognizing God's goodness and grace in our lives.

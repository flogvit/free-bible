Mercy: God's gracious and loving care for people, showing his willingness to forgive and help them in trouble.
Lord: A title used for God to show respect and acknowledge his authority and power.
Outstretched arm: An image of God's power and strength that he uses to protect and save his people.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and death, and giving them eternal life.
Enemies: Those who oppose God and his people, and try to harm or destroy them.
Thanksgiving: An expression of gratitude and reverence toward God for his goodness and mercy.
Eternity: Infinite time, symbolizing God's timelessness and unchangeability.

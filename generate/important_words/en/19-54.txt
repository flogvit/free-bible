God: The almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and giving them eternal life.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose or threaten the believer and their relationship with God.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence for God, acknowledging his authority and power over all things.
Name: God's character, attributes, and nature, representing who he is and what he stands for.
Prayer: Communication between people and God, where one asks for help, guidance, or gives thanks for what has been received.
Thanksgiving: An expression of gratitude and recognition of God's goodness, grace, and blessings.
Sacrifice: An act or gift given to God as a sign of devotion, thankfulness, or reconciliation.

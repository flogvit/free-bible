Heaven: The upper part of the atmosphere and outer space, often associated with God's dwelling place.
Law: God's instructions and commands to humanity, intended to guide them in righteous and holy living.
Sun: The star closest to Earth, the source of light and warmth, used as an image for God's light and truth.
Testimony: God's word and teaching, given through prophets and Scripture, serving as a source of guidance and wisdom.
Fear: A feeling of awe and reverence toward God and His power, leading to obedience and godliness.
Gold: A precious metal, used here as a metaphor for the value and beauty of God's words and commands.
Silver: Another precious metal, also used as a metaphor for the worth of God's teaching and insight.
Hidden faults: Unconscious sins or errors that people may commit without realizing it, requiring God's grace and forgiveness.
Redemption: God's act of saving and freeing people from sin and its consequences, through grace and love.

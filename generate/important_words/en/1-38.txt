Judah: One of Jacob's twelve sons and the ancestor of the tribe of Judah.
Tamar: Judah's daughter-in-law who pretends to be a prostitute in order to become pregnant by him.
Er: Judah's firstborn son, who dies because of his wickedness in God's sight.
Onan: Judah's second son, who also dies after refusing to fulfill his duty as a brother-in-law (levirate marriage) toward Tamar.
Shelah: Judah's third son, whom Tamar was supposed to marry, but whom Judah does not give her in marriage.
Perez: One of the twin sons born to Tamar and Judah, and an ancestor of King David and Jesus.
Zerah: The other twin son born to Tamar and Judah.

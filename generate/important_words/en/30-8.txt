Basket: A basket of summer fruit, a symbol of Israel's impending downfall and the end of God's patience.
Summer fruit: Used as a metaphor that the day of judgment is near, since summer fruits mark the end of the harvest season.
Temple: A holy place for worshiping God; here it refers to the destruction of the temple as part of God's judgment on Israel.
Lament: A mournful song or sad melody expressing grief and pain over the loss of the land and people.
Sea: An image of chaos and destruction; in this context, it represents the devastation that will strike Israel.
Measuring: An action performed by merchants to weigh and measure goods; in this context, it describes unjust and dishonest trading practices.
Sunset: A symbol of the end and darkness that will fall upon Israel as a result of God's judgment.
Famine: A lack of food and nourishment; in this context, it refers to a spiritual hunger for God's word and righteousness.

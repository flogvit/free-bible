Jerusalem: The capital city of Israel and a central place for Jewish faith and culture.
Lord: Another designation for God, representing his authority and power.
Lamentations: A poetic genre expressing grief, suffering, and repentance over an event or situation.
Widow: A woman who has lost her husband through death and has not remarried.
Tears: A symbol of sorrow, pain, and emotional distress.
Enemies: Persons or groups in conflict with or opposing Jerusalem and its inhabitants.
Punishment: The consequences of actions that violate God's will or laws.
Sin: Actions or thoughts that go against God's will or morality and lead to spiritual separation from God.
Comfort: The feeling of relief and support in times of distress and sorrow.
Humiliation: The feeling of shame and degradation resulting from external circumstances or one's own actions.

Amnon: Eldest son of King David, and half-brother to Absalom and Tamar. He commits a cruel act against his half-sister Tamar.
Tamar: Daughter of King David and Maaka, and sister to Absalom. She is subjected to sexual violence by her half-brother Amnon.
Absalom: Son of King David and Maaka, brother to Tamar. He avenges Tamar's honor by killing Amnon.
Jonadab: A relative and advisor of King David. He gives Amnon a malicious counsel that leads to the rape of Tamar.
Vengeance: The action Absalom takes to punish Amnon for his wicked deed against Tamar.
Conflict: The strife between Amnon, Absalom, and their father, King David, resulting from Amnon's actions and Absalom's vengeance.
Reconciliation: The attempt to restore peace and harmony after the conflict among family members.

The Lord's speech: God's communication to Moses, containing instructions and laws for the people of Israel.
High Priest: The supreme religious leader in Israel, responsible for performing sacrifices and other ceremonies in the tabernacle or temple.
Sacrifice: A gift given to God, often an animal or another form of material offering, as an act of worship, gratitude, or atonement for sin.
Holy Place: A place dedicated to God and used for worship and religious ceremonies.
Purification: The process of being cleansed from sin or impurity through ritual actions and prayers.
Impurity: A state of spiritual or physical contamination that can be transmitted by contact with certain objects, people, or situations.
Fellowship Offering: An offering in which both the priest and the offerer share in fellowship with God by eating part of the sacrificed animal.
Firstfruits: The first and best fruits or harvests that were gathered and offered to God as thanks for his blessings and care.

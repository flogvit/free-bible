Eternity: An infinite period of time in which God exists, without beginning or end.
Refuge: A place or person where one seeks safety and protection; here referring to God as humanity's protector and support.
Generation: A group of people born and living at approximately the same time, often used to describe a period characterized by a specific group of people.
Year: A unit of time representing one orbit of the Earth around the sun, used to measure time and the course of life.
Wrath: God's strong displeasure or anger, often directed toward human sin and disobedience.
Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and good judgment; something Psalm 90 asks God to teach humanity.
Work: Effort and activities carried out by people, either physically or mentally, to reach a goal or achieve something.
Glory: God's radiant and overwhelming presence, which people desire to see and experience in their lives and work.

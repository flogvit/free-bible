Ezra: A Jewish scribe and priest who helped lead the people of Israel after the exile in Babylon. He played a central role in restoring the temple and the law.
Book 10: The final chapter of Ezra's book, which focuses on the problem of mixed marriages between Israelites and other nations, as well as the need for repentance and separation.
Mixed marriages: Marriages between Israelites and people from other nations or cultures, which were seen as a violation of God's law and a threat to the religious and cultural identity of Israel.
Repentance: A change of mind and heart, where a person turns away from sin and turns toward God to seek forgiveness and new life.
Separation: In this context, it refers to the act of separating from those who had entered into mixed marriages, in order to preserve the purity and holiness of the people of Israel.

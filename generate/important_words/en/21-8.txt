Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience in a reasonable and prudent way in various situations.
Time: A continuous progression of events, where everything has its own time and season.
Life: The biological process that involves growth, reproduction, and death in living organisms.
Death: The end of life; an inevitable part of the natural cycle for all living beings.
Fate: A predetermined course of events or outcomes, often regarded as governed by a higher power.
Work: Physical or mental effort undertaken to achieve a goal or produce something of value.
Pleasure: A feeling of joy, satisfaction, or well-being experienced when enjoying something good.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and fairly, both toward oneself and others.
Injustice: Actions that contradict the principles of justice and morality.
Fear of God: A reverent awe and respect for God, leading to obedience and adherence to His commands.

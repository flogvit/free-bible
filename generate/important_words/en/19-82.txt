God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Assembly: A group of people gathered to worship God and listen to his word.
Son of God: A term referring to angels or heavenly beings who are close to God and serve him.
Judges: Persons with authority to judge and make decisions on behalf of God and the people.
Righteousness: The principle of acting in accordance with God's will and morality, including fair treatment of others.
Foundations of the world: The concept referring to the foundation upon which the world is built, both physically and spiritually.
Inherit: To receive something, especially property or status, from a parent or ancestor after their death.

Amaziah: A king of Judah who reigned for 29 years and succeeded his father Joash; known for his victory over the Edomites and conflict with Israel.
Joash: King of Israel, son of Jehoahaz, who fought against Amaziah and defeated him, expanded Israel's territory, and plundered the temple in Jerusalem.
Jeroboam II: Son of Joash and king of Israel; during his reign Israel experienced a period of prosperity and expansion.
Edom: An Old Testament kingdom southeast of the Dead Sea, inhabited by descendants of Esau, Jacob's brother; conquered by Amaziah but later revolted.
Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God; in 2 Kings 14 a prophet is sent to Amaziah to warn him against attacking Israel.
Gold: Precious metal symbolizing wealth and power; Amaziah took gold from the temple in Jerusalem after defeating the Edomites, but lost it to Joash.
Silver: Precious metal that also symbolizes wealth and power; Amaziah took silver from the temple along with the gold, but lost it to Joash.

Ephod: A part of the priestly garment consisting of a kind of vest, used by the high priest in ancient Israel.
Breastpiece: A part of the priestly garment that was worn on the chest and contained twelve precious stones, one for each of the tribes of Israel.
Urim and Thummim: Two objects placed in the breastpiece and used to seek divine guidance from God.
High Priest: The supreme religious leader in ancient Israel, who performed special rituals and sacrifices.
Sanctuary: The holy place where the Israelites worshiped God and carried out religious rituals.
Sacrifice: A gift or act given to God to honor him, ask for forgiveness, or give thanks for blessings.
Priestly Garments: Special clothes worn by the high priest and other priests during religious ceremonies.
Tribes of Israel: The twelve groups of descendants of Jacob's twelve sons, which made up ancient Israel.

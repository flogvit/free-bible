Saul: The first king of Israel, who fell in battle against the Philistines at Mount Gilboa.
Philistines: A people living in Canaan and enemies of Israel; they killed Saul and his sons in battle.
Gilboa: A mountain in Israel where the battle between the Israelites and the Philistines took place, and where Saul and his sons died.
Jonathan: One of Saul's sons, known for his friendship with David and his bravery in battle.
David: The future king of Israel and a central figure in the books of Samuel; he was not present at this battle.
Suicide: The act of taking one's own life; Saul committed suicide by falling on his own sword to avoid being captured by the Philistines.
Beth-Shan: A city where the Philistines hung the bodies of Saul and his sons on the city wall as a sign of victory and humiliation.
Jabesh-Gilead: A city in Israel where the men took down the bodies of Saul and his sons from Beth-Shan and buried them.

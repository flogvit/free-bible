Song of Songs: A poetic book in the Old Testament that expresses love between a man and a woman, often interpreted allegorically as the love between God and his people.
Bride: The woman who is loved by the man in the Song of Songs, often symbolizing God's people or the believing soul.
Groom: The man who loves the woman in the Song of Songs, can represent God or Christ who loves his people.
Love: A deep devotion and feeling of closeness between two persons, used in the Song of Songs to describe the relationship between God and humanity.
Vineyard: An image used in the Song of Songs to represent the place where love blooms and grows, can also symbolize Israel or the church.
Lebanon: A country in the Middle East mentioned in the Song of Songs, representing beauty and fertility in nature and in love.
Solomon: The king of Israel and assumed author of the Song of Songs, associated with wisdom and wealth.
Sister: An affectionate nickname used by the groom for his beloved in the Song of Songs, emphasizing the close relationship between the two.

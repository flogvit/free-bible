Fittings: Metal pieces used to reinforce and decorate structures, often used on the altar in the tabernacle.
Tabernacle: A temporary, portable sanctuary that the Israelites used to worship God during their wilderness journey.
Ark of the Covenant: A chest made of wood and gold that contained the stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, a jar of manna, and Aaron's staff.
Veil: A thick, woven curtain that separated the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place in the tabernacle.
Altar: A structure where offerings were burned to God as a symbol of worship, gratitude, and atonement for sins.
Basin: A container for water used for ritual cleansing before priests performed their duties in the tabernacle.
Grating: A network of metal rods or wires forming a pattern, often used as a decorative or structural component.

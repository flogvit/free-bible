Census: A count of the population of Israel, carried out to organize and administer the tribes.
Inheritance: The transfer of property, status, or rights from one generation to the next within a family or tribe.
Tribes: The twelve groups that made up the people of Israel, each with its own leader and territory, descended from Jacob's twelve sons.
Caleb: One of the twelve spies whom Moses sent to explore Canaan; he and Joshua were the only ones who believed that Israel could conquer the land.
Joshua: A key figure in Numbers, who succeeded Moses as leader of the Israelites and led them into the promised land of Canaan.
Warriors: Men who fought in the army and took part in military battles to protect and expand Israel's territory.
Daughter: A female offspring, mentioned especially in this book because the daughters of Zelophehad were granted the right to inherit.

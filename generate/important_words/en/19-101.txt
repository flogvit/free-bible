Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting in accordance with divine laws and ethical norms.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good choices and take well-considered decisions.
Protection: God's care and defense of his faithful followers against dangers and evil.
Grace: God's undeserved love and mercy toward people, despite their sins and faults.
Praise: An expression of gratitude, devotion, and honor to God through song, music, or prayer.
Discipline: God's correction and training of his children to guide them toward righteousness and good conduct.
Unrighteous: People who act in opposition to God's laws and ethical norms, thereby bringing harm upon themselves and others.

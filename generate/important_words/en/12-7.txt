Prophecy: A prediction or announcement of future events, given by God through a prophet.
Elisha: A prophet in Israel who received God's word and communicated it to the people.
Samarians: The inhabitants of Samaria, the capital of the northern kingdom of Israel, who were under siege by the Arameans.
Arameans: A people from the region of Aram (present-day Syria) who attacked and besieged Samaria.
Siege: A military tactic in which an enemy force surrounds and isolates a city or fortress to force its surrender.
Leprosy: An infectious skin disease that led to social exclusion and isolation in ancient times.
Miracle: A supernatural act performed by God that breaks the laws of nature and demonstrates His power.
Deliverance: The act of saving someone from danger or distress, often linked to God's intervention.
Famine: A severe shortage of food leading to hunger, weakness, and disease.
Abundance: A large amount of something, especially food or wealth, often seen as a sign of God's blessing.

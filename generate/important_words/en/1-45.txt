Joseph: One of Jacob's sons, sold as a slave by his brothers and later became governor in Egypt.
The Brothers: The sons of Jacob who betrayed Joseph and sold him into slavery.
Egypt: The land where Joseph became governor and where his family sought help during the famine.
Famine: A lack of food that strikes Egypt and Canaan, leading Joseph's brothers to travel to Egypt to buy grain.
Reunion: When Joseph is reunited with his brothers and forgives them for their actions against him.
Jacob: The patriarch and father of Joseph and his brothers, also known as Israel.
The Land of Goshen: A region in Egypt where Joseph invites his family to live and survive the famine.
Forgiveness: The act of accepting others' mistakes and letting go of the past, as Joseph does with his brothers.

Ark: Holy chest that contained Israel's holy relics, including the two stone tablets with the Ten Commandments.
David: King David was the second king of Israel and a central figure in the Old Testament. In 1 Chronicles 13 he wants to bring the ark to Jerusalem.
Uzza: A man from David's time who died when he touched the ark during transport, which was forbidden.
Jerusalem: The holy city and capital of ancient Israel, the place where David wanted to place the ark.
Philistines: A people who lived in Canaan and were enemies of Israel. They had previously taken the ark, but returned it after experiencing plagues.
Abinadab: A man who took care of the ark in his house after the Philistines returned it to Israel.
Eleazar: Abinadab's son, appointed by David to look after the ark in his father's house.

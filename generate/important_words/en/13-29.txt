David: King David, the second king of Israel and a central figure in the Old Testament.
Thank offering: An offering given to God as an expression of gratitude or acknowledgment of His blessings and goodness.
Temple: A holy place for worship and sacrifices, especially the one built in Jerusalem to house the Ark of the Covenant.
Solomon: David's son and successor as king of Israel, known for his wisdom and for building the first temple in Jerusalem.
Heart: Symbolizes a person's inner being, their thoughts, will, and feelings; often used to describe their character and attitude toward God.
Voluntary: To give or do something without compulsion, but out of one's own free will and desire.
Wealth: An abundance of material goods, money, and possessions, often seen as a blessing from God.
The Lord: A title often used for God in the Old Testament, referring to His sovereignty and authority over all creation.
Ark of the Covenant: A holy chest that contained the two stone tablets with the Ten Commandments, a jar of manna, and Aaron's staff that budded; it symbolized God's presence and covenant with the Israelites.

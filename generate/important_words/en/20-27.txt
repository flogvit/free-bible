Iron: Used metaphorically to describe how a person can shape and influence another through friendship and counsel.
Honey: Symbolizes something sweet and pleasant, often used as a metaphor for wisdom or good words.
Discipline: Admonition, correction, or training necessary to correct errors and teach righteousness and sound morality.
Outburst of anger: Intense rage or wrath that can lead to conflict and the destruction of relationships.
Wounds: Used both literally and metaphorically to describe pain and harm, whether physical or emotional.
Love: A deep devotion and care for others, including family, friends, and neighbors.
Neighborliness: The relationship between people who live near each other, and how they interact and help one another.
Self-praise: Praising oneself excessively, which is considered negative and can lead to arrogance and pride.
Patience: The ability to endure difficulties, adversity, or irritation without losing composure.
Loyalty: Faithfulness and support for a person, principle, or cause, even under difficult circumstances.

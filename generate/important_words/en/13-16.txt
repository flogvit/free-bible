Lord: A title for God that emphasizes his authority and power over all things.
Praise: A form of musical expression in which one praises, honors, and thanks God for his goodness and grace.
Covenant: An agreement between God and people, often connected with promises of blessing and protection.
Message: News or messages that are shared, especially concerning God's works and plans.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and bringing them into a close relationship with him.
People: A group of people; in this context it refers to the people of Israel, with whom God made a covenant.
Service: The act of serving or providing help, especially in connection with religious duties and tasks.
Sacrifice: A gift or action given to God as part of worship, often to seek his favor or forgiveness.

Hezekiah: King of Judah who prayed to God for healing and protection against the Assyrians.
Sickness: An illness or condition that negatively affects the body, in this case Hezekiah's fatal disease.
Prophet: A person who receives and delivers messages from God, in this case Isaiah.
Isaiah: The prophet who brought God's message and promise of healing to Hezekiah.
Healing: The process of becoming well or restoring health; here it refers to Hezekiah's recovery.
Sign: An event or action that confirms a divine message, in this case the shadow moving backward on the steps.
Assyrians: An ancient empire and people who threatened Judah but were defeated by God after Hezekiah's prayer.
Prayer: Communication with God where one asks for help, guidance, or gives thanks for blessings.

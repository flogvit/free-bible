Praise: A song or poem that expresses gratitude and praise to God for his goodness and power.
Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with God's will and morality, and is therefore upright and morally good.
Unrighteous: A person who does not follow God's will and morality, and is therefore considered evil or sinful.
Joy: An intense feeling of gladness or happiness, often linked to spiritual experience and the joy of God's presence.
Enemies: Persons or forces that oppose God's will and plan, and are therefore regarded as evil or harmful.
Lord: A title used for God, emphasizing his sovereignty and authority over all things and everyone.
Deeds: Actions or activities performed by humans or God, which have significance for their character and life.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and apply knowledge in a way that leads to good decisions and actions.

Saul: Israel's first king, chosen by God through the prophet Samuel. He later becomes jealous of David and tries to kill him.
David: A young shepherd who becomes an important warrior and leader in Israel. Later he becomes king after Saul and is the founder of the Davidic dynasty.
Jonathan: Saul's son and David's close friend. Jonathan helps David on several occasions and stands up to his father to protect him.
Samuel: The prophet who anoints both Saul and David as kings over Israel. He is a central figure in the early parts of 1 Samuel.
Michal: Saul's daughter and David's first wife. She helps David escape from her father Saul by deceiving him.
God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created heaven, earth, and all life. God guides and protects David through his trials.

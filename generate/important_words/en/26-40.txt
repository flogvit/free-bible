Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Chapter 40: The beginning of a series of chapters in Ezekiel that describe a vision of a new temple and its surroundings.
Temple: A holy place where people worship and offer sacrifices to God, usually central to Jewish faith and practice.
Measurements: Indications of length, width, and height, used in Ezekiel 40 to describe the temple structure and its rooms.
Vision: A spiritual experience in which God or angels give information, guidance, or warnings to a person.
Outer wall: The outermost boundary or barrier around the temple, separating the holy area from the secular world.
Gateway chambers: Entrance areas of the temple that lead into the inner parts of the building.
Altar: A structure where offerings and gifts are presented to God as part of worship.
Holy Place: The most sacred place in the temple, where God's presence is strongest and only the high priest may enter.

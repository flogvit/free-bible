David: The future king of Israel, who was a skilled warrior and musician, and who composed many of the Psalms.
Saul: The first king of Israel, who eventually fell out of favor with God and died in battle against the Philistines.
Jonathan: Saul's son and David's close friend, who also died fighting the Philistines.
Amalekite: A person from the Amalekites, who came to David with news of the deaths of Saul and Jonathan and claimed to have killed Saul himself.
Lamentation: A song or poem expressing grief and sorrow, often used in connection with death.
Ziklag: A city in Judah where David lived while hiding from King Saul.
Philistines: An Old Testament people who were enemies of Israel, against whom King Saul and his army fought.
Ziklag: Another city in Judah where David lived and ruled over his men before he became king.

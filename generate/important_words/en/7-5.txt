Deborah: A prophetess and judge in Israel who led the people together with Barak against the Canaanite army.
Barak: An Israelite military leader who, at Deborah's urging, gathered an army to fight the Canaanites.
Canaanites: A people who lived in Canaan, a region that includes parts of present-day Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and Syria. They were enemies of the Israelites in the book of Judges.
Sisera: A Canaanite military commander who was killed by Jael after fleeing from the battle against the Israelites.
Jael: A woman from the Kenite people who killed Sisera by driving a tent peg through his head while he slept.
Song of praise: A poetic song or poem often used to praise God or express gratitude for His actions.
The Lord: Another designation for God, the almighty Creator and deity whom the Israelites worshiped.
Victory: A triumph or success in a conflict, as in this case the victory of Deborah and Barak over the Canaanites.

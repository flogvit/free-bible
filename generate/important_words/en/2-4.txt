Moses: The prophet whom God chose to lead the Israelites out of slavery in Egypt and receive the Ten Commandments.
Staff: A long, thin rod that Moses used as a tool to perform miracles and demonstrate God's power.
Aaron: Moses' older brother, who served as his spokesperson and later became the high priest among the Israelites.
Signs: Miracles or supernatural events that God gave Moses to show His power and authority to the people.
Leprosy: A serious skin disease used in the Bible as a sign of God's judgment or purity.
Salvation: The term referring to God's act of delivering the Israelites from slavery and oppression in Egypt.
LORD: A name often used for God in the Old Testament, representing His divine authority and presence.

Righteous: A person who acts in accordance with moral principles and God's will, and is therefore good and upright.  
Peaceful: A state characterized by peace, harmony, and well-being, both spiritually and physically.  
Evil: The opposite of good; actions, thoughts, or attitudes that contradict God's will and morality.  
Heartless: Without compassion, love, or empathy for others; a person lacking moral qualities who does not care about the suffering of others.  
Comfort: To give support, help, and encouragement to someone who is suffering or in difficulty; a feeling of relief from pain, grief, or worry.  
Healing: The process of becoming well again after illness, injury, or suffering; restoration of health and well-being.  
Pride: Excessive self-esteem and self-satisfaction, especially regarding one's achievements, status, or wealth; a negative trait that can lead to destruction and rejection by God.

Babel: The name of a city and tower that people built in an attempt to reach heaven, but God confused their language and scattered them over the earth.
Language Confusion: The event where God confused human languages so they could not understand each other, leading to the scattering of the people and different language groups.
Nimrod: A mighty hunter and ruler mentioned in Genesis 11 who founded several cities, including Babel, and was a descendant of Noah's son Ham.
Shem: One of Noah's three sons who survived the flood together with his father. Shem is the ancestor of Semitic peoples and languages.
Arpachshad: Shem's son and a descendant of Noah; he is part of the lineage leading to Abraham.
Terah: Father of Abram (later called Abraham), Nahor, and Haran. He was part of the lineage from Noah to Abraham.
Haran: Terah's son and Abram's brother. He died in Ur of the Chaldeans before the family moved to Canaan.

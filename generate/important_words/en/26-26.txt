Tyre: An ancient Phoenician city located on an island off the coast of present-day Lebanon. The book prophesies its destruction.
Prophecy: A divinely inspired prediction or message delivered through a prophet.
Ezekiel: A Hebrew prophet and the author of the Book of Ezekiel in the Old Testament, who conveys God's messages and judgments.
Nebuchadnezzar: King of Babylon who reigned from 605 BC to 562 BC. He is mentioned in the book of Ezekiel as God's instrument for destroying Tyre.
Destruction: The act of completely tearing down or ruining something, which in this case refers to the city of Tyre.
Rebuilding: The process of constructing again what has been destroyed. In Ezekiel 26 it is prophesied that Tyre will never be rebuilt after its destruction.

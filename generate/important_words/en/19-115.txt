God: The Almighty Creator who, according to Genesis, created the heavens, the earth, and all life.
Created: The term used to describe God's act of bringing the universe and everything in it into existence.
Idols: False gods or idols that people worship instead of the true God.
Heaven: The highest place where God dwells and reigns, often associated with the spiritual realm and divine presence.
Earth: The physical world and planet we live on, created by God as a place for humans to dwell and exercise their stewardship.
Trust: A feeling of security and faith in God, that He will fulfill His promises and care for His people.
Bless: To ask for divine protection, prosperity, and happiness for someone or something; also an acknowledgment of God's grace and favor.
Lord: A title often used for God in the Bible, denoting His authority and sovereignty over all creation.

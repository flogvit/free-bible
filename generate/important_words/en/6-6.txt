Jericho: An ancient city in Canaan that the Israelites conquered under Joshua's leadership.
Joshua: Israel's leader after Moses, who led the Israelites into the promised land.
The angel of the Lord: A divine messenger who gives instructions and guidance from God.
Trumpets: Musical instruments used by the priests to signal God's presence and power.
Circuits: Circular movements around Jericho performed by the Israelites as part of the siege.
Walls: Structures surrounding and protecting the city of Jericho, which eventually collapsed during the attack.
Rahab: A woman from Jericho who helped the Israelites and was spared because of her faithfulness to God.

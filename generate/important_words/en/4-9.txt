Passover: A Jewish festival that celebrates the liberation of the Israelites from slavery in Egypt, as described in Exodus.
Tabernacle: A portable sanctuary tent where God was present among the Israelites and where sacrificial rituals were performed.
Cloud: A visible sign of God's presence that led the Israelites through the wilderness and rested over the tabernacle.
Sacrifice: A religious act in which animals, food, or other items are offered to God as a form of worship and gratitude.
Purification: A ritual process for removing impurities, both physical and spiritual, so that a person may take part in religious ceremonies.
Uncleanness: A state of being ritually unclean that prevents a person from participating in religious ceremonies and activities.
Commandment: A rule or instruction given by God to the Israelites through Moses about how they should live and worship God.

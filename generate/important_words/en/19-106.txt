The Lord's goodness: God's love and grace that he shows toward humanity.
Praise: To express admiration and gratitude to God through song, prayer, or other forms of worship.
Sin: An action, thought, or attitude that breaks with God's will and moral law.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love given to people, despite their sins and failures.
Faithfulness: God's ability to keep promises and remain consistent in his love and care for humanity.
Redemption: Deliverance from sin and its consequences, achieved through God's grace and the sacrifice of Jesus Christ.
Confession: Acknowledging one's own sins and faults, and humbly asking for God's forgiveness and grace.
Blessing: God's favor and protection over people, often linked to happiness, prosperity, and success.

Divorce: The process by which a marriage is dissolved and two people become legally separated.
Debt slave: A person who works to repay a debt, often in the form of service or labor.
Ransom: Payment required to free someone from captivity or slavery.
Clothing: Garments that people wear to cover their bodies and protect themselves from weather conditions.
Widow: A woman whose spouse has died and who has not remarried.
Poor: People who lack financial resources and material goods, often living under difficult circumstances.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and lawfully, and treating others with respect and honesty.

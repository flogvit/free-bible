Canaan: An area in the ancient Middle East that God promised to give to the children of Israel, later known as the land of Israel.
Spies: Persons sent out to explore and gather information about a specific region or people, in this case Canaan.
Report: A description or account of a situation or event, here the spies' feedback concerning Canaan.
Rebellion: An attempt to resist or defy authority, in this case against Moses and Aaron.
Punishment: A consequence or response to actions considered unacceptable, here God's punishment for the rebellion.
40 years: The time the children of Israel had to wander in the wilderness before they could enter Canaan.
Joshua: One of the two faithful spies who gave a positive report about Canaan and who later led the children of Israel into the land.
Caleb: The other faithful spy who gave a positive report about Canaan and who was also a central figure during the conquest of the land.

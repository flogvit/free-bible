Ezekiel: A prophet in the Old Testament who received visions and messages from God.
Jerusalem: The capital of ancient Israel, a central theme in the book of Ezekiel, and the location of Solomon's temple.
Siege: A military tactic where an enemy surrounds and isolates a city or fortress to force its surrender.
Symbolic actions: Actions performed by Ezekiel that represent God's message to the Israelites, such as lying on his side or making a model of Jerusalem.
Judgment: God's righteous consequences for Israel's disobedience and idolatry.
Repentance: To change one's behavior and attitude, especially in relation to God, and return to obedience and faithfulness.
The Day of the Lord: A future time of God's judgment and punishment upon the nations, including Israel, for their sins and disobedience.
Hope: The belief that God will restore His people after judgment and fulfill the promises given to Abraham and his descendants.

Praise: An expression of gratitude and homage to God for his goodness and greatness.
Lord: A title used for God, meaning the supreme authority and ruler over all.
Servants: Those dedicated to serving and honoring God, often referred to as believers or followers.
Host of heaven: A term referring to heavenly beings, such as angels, who serve God.
Jacob: The patriarch of Israel's twelve tribes and an important figure in the Old Testament.
Israel: The name of the biblical people whom God chose as his special people, also the name of the land they inhabited.
God's house: An expression referring to the temple in Jerusalem, a place for worship and sacrifice to God.
Power: God's ability to control and influence the world and human life.
Miracles: Supernatural events that break the laws of nature and are attributed to God's intervention.
Salvation: God's act of saving people from sin and its consequences, and giving them eternal life.

Ezra: A scribe and priest who led Jews back from exile and taught them God's law.
Nehemiah: A high official and leader who helped rebuild the walls of Jerusalem and reformed society based on God's law.
The Law: God's instructions and commandments given to Moses, including the Ten Commandments, which form the foundation of Jewish faith and practice.
Assembly: A group of people gathered to worship, pray, or study God's word.
Reading: The act of reading aloud from sacred scripture to convey knowledge and understanding of God's will and plans.
Understanding: The ability to interpret and appreciate the meaning of what is read or taught, especially concerning God's word.
Joy: A feeling of great happiness and gratitude, often linked to recognizing God's grace and blessings.
Sukkot: A Jewish festival that celebrates the harvest and recalls the Israelites' wandering in the wilderness after their deliverance from Egypt.

Manasse: An evil king of Judah who reigned for 55 years and led the people away from God by worshiping idols and practicing wicked deeds.
Amon: Son of Manasseh and king of Judah after his father, also known for his wickedness and idolatry.
Josiah: King of Judah who succeeded Amon and carried out a religious reform by removing idols and restoring the worship of God.
Hezekiah: Father of Manasseh and king of Judah who was faithful to God and ensured that the people followed God's laws and commands.
Prophet: A person chosen by God to convey God's message and instructions to the people.
Idols: False gods or statues that people worship instead of the true God.
Sin: Actions that go against God's will and laws, leading to separation between people and God.
Judgment: God's just punishment or consequences for human sin and disobedience.

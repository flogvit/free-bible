Darius: King of Persia who ruled during the rebuilding of the temple in Jerusalem.
Rebuilding: The process of restoring and constructing something that has been destroyed; here it refers to the temple in Jerusalem.
Temple: A holy place for worship and sacrifice in Jewish faith, especially Solomon's temple in Jerusalem.
Tattenai: Persian governor who oversaw the province where Jerusalem was located and raised questions about the rebuilding of the temple.
Column: An architectural element used to support a structure; symbolizes stability and strength.
Sacrifice: A gift or act showing devotion and gratitude to God, often in the form of food, drink, or animals.
Book of the Law: Reference to the writings containing God's laws and instructions for his people, especially the books of Moses.
Treasury: A place where wealth and valuable items were kept, often associated with royal or religious institutions.

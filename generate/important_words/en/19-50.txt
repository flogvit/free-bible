Sacrifice: Ritual gifts given to God, often in the form of animals or food, to show gratitude, reverence, and devotion.
Righteousness: Acting morally and ethically correct, in accordance with God's commands and principles.
Heaven: The spiritual realm where God dwells and reigns, often associated with reward and eternal life for righteous people.
Earth: The physical world we live in, created by God and given to humanity as a gift and a responsibility.
Praise: Religious songs and texts that express honor, glory, and gratitude to God.
Mercy: God's grace, love, and compassion toward people, especially regarding forgiveness of sins and salvation.
Forgiveness: God's act of removing the guilt and punishment of sin from a person who repents and seeks reconciliation.
Covenant: A holy agreement between God and people, where both parties have obligations and promises to each other.
Witness: Someone who gives testimony or confirmation of God's existence, power, and goodness, either directly or indirectly.
Justice: God's perfect morality and fairness, which he desires people to imitate and reflect.

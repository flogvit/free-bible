Praise: A tribute or thanks to God for his goodness and greatness.
Mercy: God's grace and compassion toward people, especially in their distress and suffering.
Righteousness: God's just and right actions according to his own standard and law.
Glory: God's majestic and radiant presence, reflected in his creation and works.
Kingdom: God's rule and control over the entire universe and everything in it.
Faithfulness: God's reliability and ability to keep his promises and fulfill his plans.
Grace: God's unmerited favor and love toward people, granting them salvation and blessings.
Salvation: God's rescue of people from sin and its consequences, through faith in Jesus Christ.

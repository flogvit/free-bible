Samson: One of Israel's judges, known for his immense strength and long hair, which was the source of his power.
Philistines: A people who lived in Canaan, often in conflict with the Israelites; they are Samson's and Israel's enemies in the book of Judges.
Ruins: A destroyed or collapsed building or structure, often used as a symbol of destruction and defeat.
Riddle: A task or question that requires thought and insight to solve, usually with a surprising or unexpected answer.
Lion: A large, powerful predator from the cat family, often symbolizing strength, courage, and royal authority.
Honey: A sweet, sticky substance produced by bees from flower nectar, associated with sweetness, abundance, and reward.
Bride price: Payment in the form of money, property, or services given from the groom's family to the bride's family as part of the marriage agreement.
Vengeance: The act of harming someone or something in response to a previous injury or injustice, often motivated by anger or bitterness.

Cities of Refuge: Safe cities established to provide protection for those who unintentionally killed someone, so they could seek asylum and avoid vengeance from the victim's family.
Joshua: The main character in the book of Joshua, who succeeded Moses as leader of the people of Israel and led them into the promised land of Canaan.
The Lord: Another designation for God, the almighty Creator, who plays a central role in the book of Joshua and gives guidance and instructions to Joshua and the people of Israel.
The Land of Canaan: The area that God promised to give to the people of Israel as their inheritance and homeland. This area corresponds roughly to present-day Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and parts of Jordan and Syria.
Avenger of Blood: A person who seeks to avenge the killing of a family member by killing the perpetrator. In Joshua 20, cities of refuge are established to protect killers from avengers of blood.
Moses: The prophet and leader of the people of Israel before Joshua, who received the Ten Commandments from God and led the people out of Egypt and through the wilderness.

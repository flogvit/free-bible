Isaac: Son of Abraham and Sarah, who is an important patriarch in the Jewish, Christian, and Islamic tradition.
Rebekah: Wife of Isaac and mother to the twins Esau and Jacob.
Esau: Eldest son of Isaac and Rebekah, known for selling his birthright to his brother Jacob for a bowl of lentil stew.
Jacob: Younger twin brother of Esau, who with the help of his mother Rebekah deceived his older brother and received the birthright and blessing from his father Isaac.
Birthright: A right given to the firstborn son in a family, which included inheritance of the family's property and status as head of the family.
Blessing: A divine favor or protection, often given by God through a patriarch such as Isaac.

Life: A state of growth, activity, and function that characterizes living organisms such as humans, animals, and plants.  
Choice: The decision to select between two or more alternatives; in this context, the choice between life and death, blessing and curse.  
Happiness: A feeling of joy, contentment, and well-being, often associated with a good life and blessings from God.  
Blessing: A favorable gift or act from God that brings happiness, prosperity, and protection.  
Curse: A negative force or effect resulting from disobedience to God, which can lead to suffering, distress, and destruction.  
Covenant: A solemn agreement between God and his people, in which both parties commit to fulfilling certain conditions.  
Repentance: The process of changing one’s mind, heart, and actions to turn back to God and follow his ways.  
Obedience: The willingness and ability to hear and obey God’s commands and instructions.

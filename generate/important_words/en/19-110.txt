Lord: A title that refers to God and his sovereign authority over all things and everyone.
Footstool: An object used to rest one's feet on; here it symbolizes the subjection of enemies under God's authority.
Priest: A religious leader who acts as a mediator between God and people, performing ceremonies and rituals.
Melchizedek: A mysterious king and priest mentioned in the Bible, representing an eternal priesthood without beginning or end.
Days: A unit of time; here it may also refer to a period or era.

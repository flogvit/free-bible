Oholah: A symbolic figure representing Samaria, the capital of the Northern Kingdom of Israel. She is described as an unfaithful sister.
Oholibah: Another symbolic figure representing Jerusalem, the capital of the Southern Kingdom of Judah. She is also described as an unfaithful sister and Oholah's sister.
Adultery: In this context, it refers to turning away from God and worshiping other gods, which is regarded as spiritual prostitution.
Assyria: An ancient empire in Mesopotamia that conquered and destroyed the Northern Kingdom of Israel. In Ezekiel 23, Assyria is associated with Oholah's adultery.
Babylon: An ancient empire in Mesopotamia that conquered and destroyed the Southern Kingdom of Judah. In Ezekiel 23, Babylon is associated with Oholibah's adultery.
Punishment: The consequences of Israel's and Judah's unfaithfulness toward God. The punishment includes destruction, captivity, and suffering.
Judgment: God's righteous evaluation and the consequences of human actions, especially those who have been unfaithful to him.
Salvation: God's promise to save his people from their sin and punishment, and to restore them to a right relationship with him.

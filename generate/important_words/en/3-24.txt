Menorah: A seven-branched lampstand symbolizing God's presence and light in the Tabernacle.
Oil: Used to keep the lamps on the Menorah burning, representing God's presence and light.
Bread: The twelve loaves placed before the Lord each week, symbolizing Israel's twelve tribes and their fellowship with God.
The Sanctuary: The holy space in the Tabernacle where God met his people, Israel.
Blasphemy Incident: An event where a man blasphemed God's name, resulting in his punishment.
Stones: Tools used for carrying out the death penalty by stoning, which was the punishment for blaspheming God's name.
Eye for an eye: A principle of just retribution meaning that the punishment should be proportional to the offense.
Blasphemy: Showing disrespect or irreverence toward God, including misusing his name.

Enemies: Persons who are against or wish to harm another person, in this case the psalmist.
Persecutor: A person who actively hunts down or tries to harm another person, often because of their faith or convictions.
Helpless: A person who lacks the ability or means to protect themselves or handle a situation.
Speak: To express thoughts, ideas, or feelings through words or sounds.
Bless: To ask God to grant favor, protection, or prosperity to someone.
Curses: Evil wishes or prayers intended to harm or bring misfortune upon someone.
Love: A deep feeling of devotion, care, and respect for another person.
Merciful: Showing compassion, grace, and forgiveness toward others, especially those who suffer or need help.
Justice: The principle of acting rightly and fairly, in accordance with moral and ethical norms.
The Lord: A title used to refer to God, especially in Jewish and Christian tradition.

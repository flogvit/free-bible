David's Mighty Men: Warriors who supported and fought alongside David, Israel's future king.
Benjamin: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, named after Jacob's youngest son.
Gad: Another of Israel's twelve tribes, named after one of Jacob's sons.
Saul: Israel's first king, who eventually became an enemy of David due to jealousy and distrust.
Ziklag: A city in Judah given to David by the Philistine king Achish, where David sought refuge from Saul.
Amasai: A leader of David's army and one of the thirty mighty warriors.
Spirit: God's invisible power or presence that provides guidance, strength, and inspiration.
Commander: A military leader responsible for organizing and leading soldiers in war.
Ephraim: One of Israel's twelve tribes, named after one of Joseph's sons.
Manasseh: Another of Israel's twelve tribes, also named after one of Joseph's sons.

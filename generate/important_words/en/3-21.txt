Lord: A title referring to God, often used to show respect and reverence.
Priests: Religious leaders in Israel who performed ritual duties and sacrifices in the tabernacle or temple.
Holy: Something set apart from the common and dedicated to God, especially in a religious context.
Uncleanness: A state of ritual impurity according to the laws in the books of Moses, which can arise due to contact with dead bodies, disease, or other factors.
Close relatives: Family members closely connected to a person, such as parents, siblings, spouse, and children.
Death: The end of life, a natural process that all living beings undergo.
Sacrifice: A gift or act given to God as a form of worship, thanksgiving, or atonement for sins.
Blind: A person who lacks sight, either partially or completely.
Disabled: A person with a physical disability or deformity.
Ritual: A ceremonial action performed according to religious customs and regulations.

Samson: A biblical judge and national hero in Israel, known for his supernatural strength and long hair.
Manoah: Samson's father, a faithful man who belonged to the tribe of Dan.
Tribe of Dan: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, originally led by Dan, Jacob's son.
Angel: A spiritual being and God's messenger, who conveys God's will and protects people.
Nazarite vow: A vow of dedication to God that includes special rules such as abstaining from alcohol, avoiding contact with dead bodies, and not cutting one's hair.
Philistines: A people who lived in Canaan, often in conflict with the Israelites. Samson fought against them.
Offering: A gift or act given to God as an expression of gratitude, reverence, or atonement for sins.
Burnt offering: A type of offering where something, usually an animal, is burned on an altar as a holy act to God.

The Lord's Day: A prophetic term for God's judgment and righteousness to be executed against wicked nations or people.
Babylon: An ancient city and empire in Mesopotamia, often used as a symbol of evil and idolatry in the Bible.
Assyrians: An ancient people and empire in the Middle East, known for their military power and cruelty; here used as God's instrument to punish Babylon.
Hosts of heaven: A reference to God's angels, who serve him and carry out his will in heaven and on earth.
Stars: Used symbolically to describe divine influence and rule over creation; may also refer to celestial bodies or rulers.
Terror: A strong feeling of fear and anxiety experienced by people when they face God's judgment.
Destruction: The total ruin of a city, land, or people as a result of God's judgment.
Refugees: People who flee from war, disasters, or oppression; here referring to those seeking to escape God's judgment on Babylon.
Sword: A weapon used in war and conflict; symbolizes God's punishment and destruction of wicked nations and people.
Gold: A precious metal representing wealth and prosperity; may also be a symbol of idolatry and materialism.

Levites: One of the twelve tribes of Israel, chosen by God to perform special religious duties and serve in the temple.
David: King David was the second king of Israel and a central figure in the Old Testament. He is known for his victory over Goliath and his strong relationship with God.
Service: Work or tasks carried out for God and His people, especially within the framework of religious life in Israel.
Kohathites: One of the four main groups of Levites, descended from Kohath, one of Levi's sons. They had specific responsibilities in the temple.
Gershon: The eldest son of Levi and ancestor of one of the four main groups of Levites. His descendants also had specific responsibilities in the temple.
Merari: Another son of Levi and ancestor of one of the four main groups of Levites. The Merarites also had specific responsibilities in the temple.
Elisaphan: A leader among the Levites and head of the Kohathites during David's time. He was responsible for transporting and caring for holy objects in the temple.

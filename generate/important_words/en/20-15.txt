Wisdom: The ability to think and act with knowledge, experience, understanding, and sound judgment.
Understanding: The ability to judge a situation or grasp a concept in a clear and rational way.
Discipline: Training or instruction that guides and corrects behavior, often used in the context of raising children.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and uprightly, both in moral and legal terms.
Humility: The quality of being modest and not arrogant, respectful toward others, and willing to learn.
Patience: The ability to endure difficulties, adversity, or discomfort without complaining or losing heart.
Fear of God: An attitude of awe, respect, and obedience toward God, leading to a desire to follow his commands and live according to his will.
Happiness: A feeling of well-being, contentment, and joy that comes from living a meaningful and good life.
Truth: That which is true, real, and in agreement with facts, reality, or the actual state of something.

Ahab: The king of Israel who reigned in Samaria and was married to Jezebel; he did evil in the Lord's sight and worshiped Baal.
Jehoshaphat: The king of Judah who allied with Ahab, even though he was a righteous king who sought God and followed His commands.
Micaiah: A prophet of God who warned Ahab and Jehoshaphat that they would suffer defeat in the war against Ramoth-Gilead if they did not listen to the Lord.
Ramoth-Gilead: A city in the Gilead region located near the border between Israel and Aram; the site of the battle between Ahab and the Arameans.
Zedekiah: A false prophet who advised Ahab and Jehoshaphat to go to war against Ramoth-Gilead, claiming that God would give them victory.
Arameans: A people from ancient Syria who fought against Israel and Judah under the leadership of their king Ben-Hadad II.
Ben-Hadad II: The king of Aram who attacked Ramoth-Gilead and fought against Ahab and Jehoshaphat.
The Spirit of God: The divine power and presence of God that speaks through prophets and provides guidance and wisdom.

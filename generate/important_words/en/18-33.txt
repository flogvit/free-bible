Elihu: A young man who criticizes Job's three friends and defends God's justice in Job's trials.
Righteousness: A moral principle that involves acting rightly and in accordance with ethical norms and laws.
Wisdom: The ability to think, understand, and act in a way that shows good judgment and insight.
Trials: Difficult situations or challenges that can test a person's faith, patience, and endurance.
Revelation: God's communication of truth and knowledge to people, often through prophets or divine intervention.
Redemption: Deliverance from sin and its consequences, achieved through faith in God and his grace.
Discipline: Correction or training, often from God, to teach and guide people toward righteousness and obedience.

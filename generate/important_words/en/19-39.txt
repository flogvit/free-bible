Life: A state of activity and growth that characterizes organisms, including humans, animals, and plants.
Heart: In a figurative sense, refers to a person's inner being, feelings, thoughts, and intentions.
Discipline: Correction or training given to guide and improve behavior or morality.
Days: A period of time representing the limits and transience of life. Also used as a unit of measurement for time.
Hope: A positive expectation about the future, based on faith and trust in God's promises and plans.
Lord: A title showing respect and reverence, often used as a designation for God in the Bible.
Vanity: Emptiness, meaninglessness, and transience; describes worldly pursuits and material goods that do not bring lasting satisfaction.
Wisdom: The ability to understand and apply knowledge and insight in a way that leads to sound judgment and right action.

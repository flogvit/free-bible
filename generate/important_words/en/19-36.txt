Mercy: God's loving and gracious care for people, especially in their distress and suffering.
Righteousness: The principle of acting rightly and in accordance with moral and ethical norms; in the book of Psalms it is often linked to God's character and actions.
Trust: A feeling of security and belief that someone will fulfill their promises and obligations; in the book of Psalms this often refers to trust in God.
Grace: Unmerited goodness and mercy shown by God to people, even though they do not deserve it because of their sins and failures.
The Lord: Another name for God, emphasizing his authority and sovereignty over all creation.
Fear: A reverent respect and submission toward God, acknowledging his almighty power and authority.
Protection: God's ability and willingness to guard and preserve his people from danger and evil.
Refuge: A place of safety and rest; in the book of Psalms it often refers to God's protective presence.
Source: A metaphor for God's life-giving and renewing power, which brings comfort and hope to believers.
Eternity: Time without end; in the book of Psalms this often refers to God's eternal existence and his promises of eternal life for believers.

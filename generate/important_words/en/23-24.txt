Earth: The planet we live on, which God created and controls according to the Bible.
The Lord's Day: A symbolic term for the time when God intervenes in human history and executes judgment.
Destruction: The process of destroying or causing great harm, often used in prophecies about divine punishment.
Nations: Groups of people who share a common culture, tradition, or nationality.
Punishment: The consequences or suffering resulting from breaking God's laws or doing evil deeds.
Predators: Wild animals that hunt and eat other animals, often used symbolically in the Bible to describe violence and evil.
Vineyards: Plants that produce grapes and thus wine, used as a picture of fruitfulness and blessing.
Joy: The feeling of happiness and contentment, often associated with God's presence and blessing.
Sorrow: The feeling of sadness and pain, which can result from sin, loss, or God's punishment.
Firmament: The apparent arch or dome of the sky above the earth, where God and his angels dwell.

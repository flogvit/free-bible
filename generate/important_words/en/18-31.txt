Righteousness: Acting in accordance with moral principles and ethics, and treating others fairly.
Covenant: An agreement or commitment between two parties, often used in a religious context to describe the relationship between God and humanity.
Purity of heart: An inner state of moral purity and sincerity, free from evil and sin.
Honesty: Being truthful and sincere in word and deed, without deceiving or hiding the truth.
Self-reflection: The process of thinking critically about oneself, one's actions and motives, to evaluate one's own character and behavior.
Wisdom: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good decisions and act wisely.
God's punishment: The consequences God gives people for their sins and unjust actions, often to teach them a lesson or guide them.

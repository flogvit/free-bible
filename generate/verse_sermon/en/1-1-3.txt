Here we see God's infinite power and authority in the work. With just words He created light, and with it, a decisive separation between light and darkness.

Light is more than mere physical illumination. It represents hope, wisdom, and God's presence. In the same way, God's word can bring light into our darkest moments. When we feel lost in darkness, we can trust God's word to bring us clarity and hope.

We can also reflect on this at a more personal level. God has created each of us with a purpose, and He desires that we shine our own light in the world. This light comes from Him, and we can use it to illuminate dark places and help others find their way.

So let us today remember the power of God's word, and the transformation it can bring. Let us pray that God's light will fill us and guide us in all our actions. For with God by our side, we can shine brightly in an often dark world.

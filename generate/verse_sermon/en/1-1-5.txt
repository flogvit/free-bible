At the beginning of creation, God introduces us to a concept that becomes a fundamental part of our existence: Time. He defines day and night, and calls the first one day. God arranges time in cycles, giving life rhythm and order.

This rhythm of day and night, light and darkness, work and rest, is a reminder of God's wisdom and care for us. He created not only the world, but also the structure we need to live in it.

God also shows us that both day and night have their place. It is in the daylight of the day that we carry out our work, find our way, and see clearly. But it is also in the darkness of the night that we rest, dream, and are renewed. Both are necessary for life's balance.

So, let us value each day and each night that God has given us. Let us use our days to work, serve, and shine His light into the world. And let us use our nights for rest and renewal, in gratitude for His love and care.

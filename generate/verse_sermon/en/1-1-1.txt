"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth." This simple yet powerful statement stands as the foundation of our faith and our understanding of the universe. Here in this one sentence we meet both the origin and the Creator—God.

It is easy to overlook the depth of these words. We live in a world where science continually seeks explanations, but this single sentence has carried truth through the centuries. "In the beginning God created." Here is not only the origin, but also the purpose. The world and everything in it are not products of chance, but part of a divine plan.

We were created by God, we are loved by God, and we have a purpose given by God. In a time when many seek meaning and identity, these words give us both. We are not random byproducts of the universe. We are the workmanship of a loving God.

In this simple sentence there is a reminder that in all beginnings of life—whether it be a new day, a new project, or a new phase in life—God is there. He is the beginning and the end, Alpha and Omega. And in His hands we are safe.

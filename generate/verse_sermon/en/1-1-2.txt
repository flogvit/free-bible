These words paint a picture of a world in chaos, without form, empty, and covered in darkness. But in the midst of this chaos, God's Spirit is there, hovering over the waters.

In our lives there will also be periods of chaos, disorder, and darkness. We may feel empty and desolate, as if we lack form and purpose. But, just as in creation, God's Spirit is with us, hovering over our lives.

God's Spirit is not merely a passive observer. As soon as God begins to speak, things begin to change. From chaos comes order. From emptiness comes life. From darkness comes light.

This gives us hope. No matter how dark or chaotic our lives may seem, God's Spirit is with us. He is ready to create something new in us, to bring light into our darkness, and to give us a new form and purpose. We can trust Him, because we know that He has the power to create beauty out of chaos.

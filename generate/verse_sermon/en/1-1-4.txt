Here we see God as the ultimate judge of good and evil, light and darkness. He acknowledges the quality of what He has created and establishes a clear separation between light and darkness.

This reveals to us that God is the one who defines what is good. It is not people, culture, or society that sets this standard – it is God Himself. Thus we can trust in His righteousness, His judgments, His guidance, because they come from the ultimate source of goodness.

In addition, this verse highlights the importance of distinguishing between the good (light) and the evil (darkness) in our lives. As God’s children, we are called to pursue the light, to live in truth, love, and goodness.

In a world that often blurs together good and evil, right and wrong, our task is to maintain the dividing line that God has drawn. We should always seek what is good and turn away from darkness. In this pursuit we can be confident of God’s leading, for He is Himself the source of light.

# Joel

**About the book:** The book of Joel is one of the twelve minor prophets in the Old Testament. The prophet Joel, son of Pethuel, delivers the Lord’s word to Judah in the face of a devastating locust plague and uses this catastrophe as a picture of the coming Day of the Lord.

**Main theme:** The Day of the Lord comes with judgment, but also with the promise of restoration and salvation for all who call on the name of the Lord.

**Outline:**
- The locust plague and a call to mourning and repentance (Chapter 1)
- The Day of the Lord, a call to repentance, and the promise of restoration (Chapter 2)
- The outpouring of the Spirit and salvation for those who call on the Lord (Chapter 3)

**Key themes:**
- **The Day of the Lord:** A day of darkness and judgment, but also hope for those who turn to the Lord.
- **Repentance:** A call to return to God with all your heart, with fasting, weeping, and mourning—to tear your hearts, not your garments.
- **Restoration:** God promises to replace what the locusts have eaten and to bless the land once again.
- **The outpouring of the Spirit:** God will pour out His Spirit on all people—sons and daughters, old and young, male servants and female servants.
- **Salvation:** All who call on the name of the Lord will be saved.

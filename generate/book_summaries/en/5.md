# 5. Moses (Deuteronomy)

**About the book:** The fifth book of Moses contains Moses' farewell speeches to the people of Israel on the plains of Moab, just before they are to enter the promised land. The book repeats and expands upon the law from Sinai, emphasizing the importance of loving and obeying God in order to receive his blessings.

**Main theme:** God's covenant with Israel is renewed, and the people are called to wholehearted obedience and love for the Lord as a condition for blessing in the promised land.

**Content overview:**
- Moses' first speech: Review of Israel's history (Chapters 1–4)
- Moses' second speech: The Ten Commandments and the great commandment (Chapters 5–11)
- Laws and regulations for life in the land (Chapters 12–26)
- Blessings and curses (Chapters 27–28)
- Covenant renewal and the choice between life and death (Chapters 29–30)
- Joshua appointed as leader and Moses' farewell song (Chapters 31–32)
- Moses' blessing over the tribes and his death (Chapters 33–34)

**Key themes:**
- **Love for God:** Israel is called to love the Lord with all their heart, soul, and strength (the Shema in chapter 6).
- **Covenant faithfulness:** God's covenant with Israel is confirmed, with promises of blessing for obedience and warnings of curse for disobedience.
- **The promise of the land:** God gives Israel the land of Canaan as an inheritance, just as he promised Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
- **Teaching the next generation:** Parents have the responsibility to teach their children God's word and his deeds.
- **One place of worship:** Israel is to worship God at the place he himself chooses.

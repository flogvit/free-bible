# 1 Peter

**About the book:** The First Letter of Peter was written by the apostle Peter to Christians living as foreigners scattered throughout Asia Minor (Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia). The letter was written to encourage believers who were suffering persecution and to exhort them to live holy lives in light of their living hope in Christ.

**Main theme:** Christians are called to live holy and patient lives in suffering, anchored in the hope of salvation through Jesus Christ’s resurrection.

**Outline of contents:**
- Greeting and the living hope (Chapter 1:1–12)
- The call to a holy life (Chapter 1:13–25)
- Spiritual growth and the royal priesthood (Chapter 2:1–10)
- Christian conduct in society (Chapter 2:11–3:12)
- Suffering for righteousness’ sake (Chapter 3:13–4:6)
- Service and suffering in the last days (Chapter 4:7–19)
- Exhortations to elders and the church (Chapter 5:1–11)
- Closing and greetings (Chapter 5:12–14)

**Key themes:**
- **Living hope:** Through Christ’s resurrection, believers are reborn into a living hope and an imperishable inheritance in heaven (1:3–4).
- **Holiness:** Believers are called to be holy in all their conduct, because the God who called them is holy (1:15–16).
- **Redemption:** Not with perishable things like silver or gold, but with the precious blood of Christ were believers redeemed (1:18–19).
- **Suffering with Christ:** Christians should not be surprised by suffering, but rejoice in sharing Christ’s sufferings and following his example (2:21; 4:12–13).
- **Humble service:** Both leaders and the church are called to humility, because God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble (5:5–6).

**About the Book:** The Gospel of Matthew is the first of the four Gospels in the New Testament and consists of 28 chapters. The book presents Jesus as the promised Messiah and Son of David, with a special focus on showing how Jesus fulfills the prophecies of the Old Testament.

**Main Theme:** Jesus is the promised Messiah who fulfills the Scriptures and establishes God's kingdom through his teaching, miracles, death, and resurrection.

**Outline of Contents:**
- Jesus' genealogy, birth, and childhood (Chapters 1–2)
- John the Baptist and Jesus' baptism (Chapter 3)
- Jesus' temptation and the beginning of his ministry (Chapter 4)
- The Sermon on the Mount: Jesus' teaching about God's kingdom (Chapters 5–7)
- Jesus' healings and miracles (Chapters 8–9)
- The sending out of the twelve disciples (Chapter 10)
- John's question and conflicts with the Pharisees (Chapters 11–12)
- Parables about God's kingdom (Chapter 13)
- John's death, the feeding of the 5,000, and Jesus walking on water (Chapter 14)
- Purity and the faith of the Canaanite woman (Chapter 15)
- Peter's confession and Jesus' first prediction of his suffering (Chapter 16)
- The transfiguration on the mountain (Chapter 17)
- Teaching about the church and forgiveness (Chapter 18)
- Teaching about marriage and wealth (Chapter 19)
- The parable of the workers in the vineyard (Chapter 20)
- The triumphal entry into Jerusalem and the cleansing of the temple (Chapter 21)
- Conflicts with religious leaders (Chapter 22)
- Woes against the Pharisees (Chapter 23)
- The Olivet Discourse on the Mount of Olives (Chapters 24–25)
- Jesus' suffering, crucifixion, and burial (Chapters 26–27)
- The resurrection and the Great Commission (Chapter 28)

**Key Themes:**
- **Fulfillment of Prophecy:** Matthew repeatedly shows how Jesus fulfills the Old Testament prophecies concerning the Messiah.
- **The Kingdom of God (Kingdom of Heaven):** Jesus proclaims that the kingdom of God is near and teaches about its character through parables and instruction.
- **Discipleship:** The book provides extensive teaching on what it means to follow Jesus, including the call to deny oneself.
- **Jesus' Authority:** Through healings, nature miracles, and teaching, Jesus demonstrates his divine authority over sickness, nature, demons, and the Scriptures.

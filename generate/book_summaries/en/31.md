# Obadiah

**About the book:** Obadiah is the shortest book in the Old Testament with only 21 verses. The prophet Obadiah proclaimed judgment against Edom, Israel’s neighboring people and kin through Esau, because of their hostile actions toward Judah during its time of distress.

**Main theme:** God’s judgment on Edom for their pride and betrayal of their brother nation Israel, and the promise of restoration for God’s people.

## Outline
- **Judgment against Edom** (verses 1–9): God declares that He will humble proud Edom, which dwells securely in mountain clefts, and destroy its wisdom and mighty men.
- **Edom’s sins against Jacob** (verses 10–14): Edom is accused of violence toward their brother nation Jacob, for standing by while Jerusalem was plundered, and even taking part in the destruction.
- **The day of the Lord and Israel’s restoration** (verses 15–21): The day of the Lord is near upon all nations; as Edom has done, so it will be done to them. On Mount Zion there will be a remnant, and the house of Jacob will possess their inheritance, while the kingdom will belong to the Lord.

## Key themes
- **God’s judgment on pride:** Edom’s arrogance and self-confidence in their mountain strongholds led to their downfall—no one can hide from God.
- **Brotherly betrayal:** Edom (descendants of Esau) betrayed their brother nation Israel (descendants of Jacob) during their time of distress.
- **God’s righteous retribution:** As Edom acted toward others, so it will be done to them—God’s justice restores balance.
- **Israel’s future restoration:** The promise that a remnant will survive and inherit the land, and that the Lord’s rule will be established.

# 1 Kings

**About the book:** 1 Kings tells the story of Israel’s kingdom from King David’s final days through the division of the realm and the subsequent kings in both Israel and Judah. The book covers Solomon’s golden age, including the building of the temple, his wisdom and wealth, but also his downfall through idolatry. It concludes with the prophet Elijah’s dramatic ministry under King Ahab.

**Main theme:** Faithfulness to God leads to blessing, while apostasy and idolatry lead to judgment and division.

**Content overview:**
- David’s final days and Solomon’s accession to the throne (Chapters 1–2)
- Solomon’s wisdom and wealth (Chapters 3–4)
- The building of the temple in Jerusalem (Chapters 5–8)
- God’s covenant with Solomon and his downfall (Chapters 9–11)
- Division of the kingdom: Rehoboam and Jeroboam (Chapters 12–14)
- Kings in Israel and Judah (Chapters 15–16)
- The prophet Elijah and his ministry (Chapters 17–19)
- Ahab, Naboth, and Ahaziah (Chapters 20–22)

**Key themes:**
- **God’s sovereignty:** God rules over kings and kingdoms, fulfilling His promises to David.
- **Faithfulness and apostasy:** The obedience or disobedience of the kings toward God determines their fate and the welfare of the people.
- **The temple as center:** The temple in Jerusalem becomes the central place for worship and God’s presence among the people.
- **Prophetic ministry:** The prophets, especially Elijah, confront idolatry and call the people back to God.
- **Consequences of sin:** Solomon’s idolatry and Jeroboam’s sin bring lasting consequences for the entire nation.

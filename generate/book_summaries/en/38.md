# Zechariah

**About the book:** The book of Zechariah is the second-to-last of the Minor Prophets and consists of 14 chapters containing prophecies and visions given to the prophet Zechariah, son of Berechiah. The book was written in the period following the return from the Babylonian exile, during the second and fourth years of King Darius’s reign (520–518 B.C.), and includes both night visions and messianic prophecies.

**Main theme:** God will rebuild Jerusalem and His people, and send His Messiah to save and rule over all nations.

**Outline:**
- Introduction and call to repentance (1:1–6)
- Eight night visions (1:7–6:8)
  - The riders among the myrtle trees (1:7–17)
  - Four horns and four craftsmen (2:1–4)
  - The man with the measuring line (2:5–17)
  - The high priest Joshua and Satan (3:1–10)
  - The golden lampstand and the two olive trees (4:1–14)
  - The flying scroll (5:1–4)
  - The woman in the ephah basket (5:5–11)
  - The four chariots (6:1–8)
- The crowning of Joshua and the prophecy about the Branch (6:9–15)
- Questions about fasting and exhortations (7–8)
- God’s judgment on the nations and the coming of the Messiah (9–11)
- Jerusalem’s distress and final deliverance (12–14)

**Key themes:**
- **God’s zeal for Zion:** God loves Jerusalem with great fervor and will rebuild both the city and His people (1:14; 8:2).
- **The coming of the Messiah:** Prophecies about the coming King who will arrive humbly, riding on a donkey, and about "the Branch" who will build the Lord’s temple (6:12; 9:9).
- **Cleansing from sin:** God will remove the land’s sin in one day and open a fountain for cleansing (3:9; 13:1).
- **The power of the Spirit:** The rebuilding will not happen by might or power, but by the Lord’s Spirit (4:6).
- **Universal worship:** All nations will come to Jerusalem to worship the Lord and celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles (14:16).

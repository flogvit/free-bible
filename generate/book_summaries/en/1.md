# 1. Genesis (Genesis)

**About the book:** The first book of Moses (Genesis) is the first book in the Bible and consists of 50 chapters. It tells about the creation of the world, the fall into sin, the early human generations, and the patriarchs Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. The book concludes with Joseph's story and how Jacob's family (the children of Israel) came to Egypt.

**Main theme:** God's creation of the world and his covenant relationship with humanity, especially through the calling of Abraham and his descendants.

**Content overview:**
- **The creation of the world and humanity** (Chapters 1–2)
- **The fall into sin and its consequences** (Chapter 3)
- **Cain and Abel** (Chapter 4)
- **From Adam to Noah: The early generations of humanity** (Chapter 5)
- **Noah and the flood** (Chapters 6–9)
- **The spread of humanity and the tower of Babel** (Chapters 10–11)
- **Abraham: God's call and covenant** (Chapters 12–25)
- **Isaac and his sons Esau and Jacob** (Chapters 26–27)
- **Jacob: Bethel, service with Laban, and reconciliation with Esau** (Chapters 28–36)
- **Joseph in Egypt** (Chapters 37–50)

**Key themes:**
- **Creation and origin:** God creates the heavens and the earth, and humankind in his image.
- **Sin and judgment:** From the fall into sin to the flood, God shows his holiness through judgment over evil.
- **Covenant and promise:** God establishes covenants with Noah and Abraham, with promises of land, descendants, and blessing.
- **Election and providence:** God chooses and guides the patriarchs through trials to fulfill his plans.
- **Faithfulness across generations:** God's promises continue from Abraham to Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph.

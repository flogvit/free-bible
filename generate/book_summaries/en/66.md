**About the Book:** The Revelation of John is the last book in the New Testament and consists of 22 chapters. It contains prophetic visions given to the apostle John while he was on the island of Patmos, filled with rich symbolism that conveys God's message about the end times, Christ's victory, and the eternal hope of believers.

**Main Theme:** God's sovereign control over history culminates in Christ's final victory over all powers of evil and the establishment of His eternal kingdom.

**Content Overview:**
- John's vision of Christ and the letters to the seven churches (Chapters 1–3)
- The throne room in heaven and the seven seals (Chapters 4–7)
- The seven trumpets (Chapters 8–9)
- The little scroll and the two witnesses (Chapters 10–11)
- The woman, the dragon, and the two beasts (Chapters 12–13)
- The Lamb on Mount Zion, the three angels' messages, and the harvest (Chapter 14)
- The seven bowls of wrath (Chapters 15–16)
- The fall of Babylon and judgment upon the prostitute (Chapters 17–18)
- Christ's return and the thousand-year reign (Chapters 19–20)
- The new heaven, the new earth, and the new Jerusalem (Chapters 21–22)

**Key Themes:**
- **Christ's Sovereignty:** Jesus is revealed as the risen Lord with the keys to death and Hades, worthy to open the scroll containing God's plan.
- **Judgment and Salvation:** God's righteous judgment falls upon evil, while salvation is offered to all who hold fast to the testimony of Jesus and keep God's commandments.
- **Perseverance in Trials:** The church is repeatedly called to endure, remain faithful, and overcome amid persecution.
- **God's Final Victory:** Satan, the beasts, and death itself are cast into the lake of fire, and God makes all things new.
- **Eternity with God:** The new Jerusalem comes down from heaven, and God will dwell with His people forever, where there is no more death, mourning, or pain.

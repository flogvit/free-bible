# Luke

**About the book:** The Gospel of Luke is the third gospel in the New Testament and was written by the physician Luke to Theophilus. Luke has carefully investigated everything from the beginning and provides an orderly account of Jesus’ life, death, and resurrection so that the reader may understand the reliability of what they have been taught.

**Main theme:** Jesus is the Savior of the world who brings salvation, healing, and hope to all people – including the outcasts, the poor, and non-Jews.

**Outline:**
- Preparation: The births of John and Jesus (Chapters 1–2)
- Jesus’ baptism and genealogy (Chapter 3)
- The temptation and the beginning of Jesus’ ministry in Galilee (Chapter 4)
- Calling of disciples, healings, and teaching (Chapters 5–6)
- Miracles and parables: Jesus’ power over sickness, nature, and death (Chapters 7–8)
- Peter’s confession, the transfiguration, and the journey toward Jerusalem (Chapter 9)
- Sending of the seventy-two, the good Samaritan, Mary and Martha (Chapter 10)
- Teaching on prayer and warnings against hypocrisy (Chapters 11–12)
- Repentance, healings on the Sabbath, and the growth of God’s kingdom (Chapters 13–14)
- Parables about what was lost: the sheep, the coin, and the prodigal son (Chapter 15)
- Parables about wealth and service, the ten lepers (Chapters 16–17)
- Prayer, children, the rich young ruler, and the blind man at Jericho (Chapter 18)
- Zacchaeus, the parable of the minas, and the triumphal entry into Jerusalem (Chapter 19)
- Confrontations in the temple and the Olivet Discourse (Chapters 20–21)
- The last supper, Gethsemane, and the arrest (Chapter 22)
- Trial, crucifixion, and burial (Chapter 23)
- Resurrection, the road to Emmaus, and the ascension (Chapter 24)

**Key themes:**
- **Salvation for all people:** Luke emphasizes that the gospel is for everyone – Jews, Gentiles, women, the poor, and outcasts.
- **The work of the Holy Spirit:** From the prophecies of Elizabeth and Zechariah to Jesus’ promise of power from on high, the Spirit is central.
- **Prayer:** Jesus prays at all important moments, and Luke includes unique teaching on prayer.
- **Joy and praise:** The gospel begins and ends with joy and praise in the temple.
- **Care for the poor and marginalized:** Parables and stories highlight God’s heart for those overlooked by society.

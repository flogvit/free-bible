# John

**About the book:** The Gospel of John is the fourth gospel in the New Testament and differs from the synoptic gospels by its deeper theological approach. The book focuses on Jesus' identity as God's Son and contains seven major signs (miracles) as well as the famous "I am" statements. The gospel was written so that readers would believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and through faith have life in his name.

**Main theme:** Jesus is the incarnate Word, God's only begotten Son, who gives eternal life to all who believe in him.

**Outline:**
- Prologue: The Word became flesh (1:1–18)
- John the Baptist's testimony and the first disciples (1:19–51)
- The wedding at Cana—the first sign (2:1–12)
- Cleansing of the temple (2:13–25)
- Conversation with Nicodemus about being born again (3:1–21)
- John the Baptist's final testimony (3:22–36)
- Jesus and the Samaritan woman (4:1–42)
- Healing of the official's son (4:43–54)
- Healing at the Pool of Bethesda and the Sabbath conflict (5)
- The feeding miracle and the discourse on the bread of life (6)
- Jesus at the Feast of Tabernacles (7)
- The woman caught in adultery and confrontation with the Jews (8)
- Healing of the man born blind (9)
- Jesus as the good shepherd (10:1–21)
- The Feast of Dedication and rejection (10:22–42)
- Raising of Lazarus (11)
- Anointing at Bethany and Palm Sunday (12)
- Washing of the feet and beginning of the farewell discourse (13)
- Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life—the promise of the Advocate (14)
- The vine and the branches—hatred from the world (15)
- The work of the Holy Spirit and joy at seeing him again (16)
- Jesus' high priestly prayer (17)
- Arrest and trials (18)
- Crucifixion and burial (19)
- Resurrection and encounters with the risen Lord (20)
- Epilogue: Jesus by the Sea of Tiberias and Peter's restoration (21)

**Key themes:**
- **Jesus' divinity:** Jesus is presented as the eternal Word who was with God and was God, and who became flesh to reveal the Father's glory.
- **Faith and eternal life:** The gospel repeatedly emphasizes that whoever believes in Jesus has eternal life and has passed from death to life.
- **"I am" statements:** Jesus reveals his identity through seven major "I am" declarations (the bread of life, the light of the world, the door, the good shepherd, the resurrection and the life, the way, the vine).
- **The Holy Spirit (the Advocate):** Jesus promises to send the Advocate who will guide the disciples into all truth and bear witness about him.
- **The commandment of love:** Jesus gives his disciples a new commandment to love one another as he has loved them, as a sign that they are his disciples.

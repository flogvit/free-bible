# Acts of the Apostles

**About the book:** The Acts of the Apostles was written by Luke as a continuation of his Gospel, addressed to Theophilus (1:1). The book documents the birth of the church on Pentecost and its growth from Jerusalem to Rome, driven by the power of the Holy Spirit through the apostles' witness.

**Main theme:** The power of the Holy Spirit drives the spread of the gospel from Jerusalem to Judea, Samaria, and to the ends of the earth through the apostles' witness (1:8).

**Content overview:**
- **Jesus’ ascension and the coming of the Holy Spirit** (Chapters 1–2)
- **The first Christians’ fellowship and miracles** (Chapters 3–5)
- **Selection of the seven, Stephen’s speech and martyrdom** (Chapters 6–7)
- **Philip and the spread of the gospel to Samaria and the Ethiopian eunuch** (Chapter 8)
- **Saul’s conversion and calling** (Chapter 9)
- **Peter and Cornelius: The Gentiles receive the gospel** (Chapters 10–11)
- **Herod’s persecution and Peter’s deliverance** (Chapter 12)
- **Paul’s first missionary journey** (Chapters 13–14)
- **The apostolic council in Jerusalem** (Chapter 15)
- **Paul’s second and third missionary journeys** (Chapters 16–21)
- **Paul’s arrest and trials** (Chapters 21–26)
- **Paul’s journey to Rome** (Chapters 27–28)

**Key themes:**
- **The power of the Holy Spirit:** The Spirit equips believers for witness and guides the church’s mission (2:4, 4:31, 13:2).
- **The universal reach of the gospel:** Salvation is offered to both Jews and Gentiles without distinction (10:34–35, 15:9).
- **Apostolic preaching:** Jesus is the Messiah, crucified and risen, offering repentance and forgiveness of sins (2:36–38, 13:38–39).
- **Persecution and faithfulness:** Believers suffer for the name of Jesus but continue to preach with boldness (5:41–42, 14:22).
- **Growth of the church:** Despite opposition, God’s word grows and the number of believers continually increases (6:7, 12:24, 19:20).

# Esther

**About the Book:** The book of Esther is one of the historical books in the Old Testament and consists of 10 chapters. It tells the dramatic story of the Jewish woman Esther, who becomes queen of Persia under King Ahasuerus, and how she risks her life to save the Jewish people from destruction.

**Main Theme:** God's hidden providence and care for His people through courageous individuals who act in faith, even when the situation seems hopeless.

**Content Overview:**
- Queen Vashti is deposed and Esther becomes queen (Chapters 1–2)
- Haman plots the extermination of the Jews (Chapter 3)
- Mordecai and Esther's grief and preparation (Chapter 4)
- Esther's courageous action and Haman's downfall (Chapters 5–7)
- The deliverance of the Jews and the origin of the festival of Purim (Chapters 8–10)

**Key Themes:**
- **God's Providence:** Although God is not mentioned directly, the story shows how events are guided toward the salvation of the Jewish people.
- **Courage and Self-Sacrifice:** Esther risks her life by going to the king uninvited to save her people.
- **Identity and Loyalty:** Esther hides her Jewish identity but ultimately stands up for her people.
- **Justice and Retribution:** Haman's evil plan turns back upon his own head.
- **Celebration and Remembrance:** The festival of Purim is established so that future generations will remember God's deliverance.

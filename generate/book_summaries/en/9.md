# 1 Samuel

**About the book:** The book of 1 Samuel tells the story of Israel’s transition from the period of the judges to the monarchy. It portrays the life and work of the prophet Samuel, Israel’s first king Saul and his downfall, as well as David’s journey from a shepherd boy to God’s chosen king.

**Main theme:** God’s sovereignty in raising up and removing leaders, and the importance of obedience to the Lord’s word.

**Outline:**
- The birth and calling of Samuel (Chapters 1–3)
- The ark is taken and returned (Chapters 4–6)
- Samuel as judge and the people’s demand for a king (Chapters 7–8)
- Saul is anointed and installed as king (Chapters 9–12)
- Saul’s disobedience and rejection (Chapters 13–15)
- David is anointed and serves at the court (Chapters 16–17)
- David’s friendship with Jonathan and conflict with Saul (Chapters 18–20)
- David flees from Saul (Chapters 21–26)
- David among the Philistines and the death of Saul (Chapters 27–31)

**Key themes:**
- **Obedience rather than sacrifice:** God values obedience to His word more highly than religious rituals (chap. 15:22).
- **God’s sovereignty:** The Lord raises up and removes leaders according to His will and plan.
- **The importance of the heart:** God looks at the heart, not outward appearances—David was chosen because he was a man after God’s own heart.
- **Faithfulness in trials:** David’s time on the run demonstrates patience and trust in God’s timing.
- **The dangers of envy:** Saul’s jealousy toward David led to his spiritual and moral decline.

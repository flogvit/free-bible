# 2 Samuel

**About the Book:** The book of 2 Samuel continues the story from 1 Samuel and portrays David’s rise to kingship and his reign over Israel. The book covers both his period of greatness with military victories and spiritual highs, as well as his fall through sin and the tragic consequences within his family.

**Main Theme:** God’s faithful covenant with David despite human weakness and sin.

**Content Overview:**
- David’s lament over Saul and Jonathan (Chapter 1)
- David becomes king over Judah and Israel (Chapters 2–5)
- The ark of the covenant brought to Jerusalem and God’s covenant with David (Chapters 6–7)
- David’s military victories (Chapters 8–10)
- David’s sin with Bathsheba and Uriah (Chapters 11–12)
- Amnon, Tamar, and Absalom’s revenge (Chapter 13)
- Absalom’s rebellion and David’s flight (Chapters 14–17)
- The death of Absalom and David’s grief (Chapters 18–19)
- Sheba’s rebellion (Chapter 20)
- Famine and the Gibeonites (2 Samuel 21:1–14)
- David’s mighty men and battles against the Philistines (2 Samuel 21:15–22)
- David’s song of praise and his last words (Chapters 22–23)
- The census and the plague (Chapter 24)

**Key Themes:**
- **God’s covenant with David:** God promises David an eternal dynasty, a covenant that points forward to the Messiah (chapter 7).
- **Sin and its consequences:** David’s sin with Bathsheba shows how even a man after God’s own heart can fall, and how sin brings serious repercussions.
- **God’s grace and forgiveness:** Despite David’s fall, God shows grace when David repents sincerely.
- **Leadership and responsibility:** The book illustrates both good and bad leadership, and the consequences of a leader’s choices for the entire nation.

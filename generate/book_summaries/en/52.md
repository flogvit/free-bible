# 1 Thessalonians

**About the book:** First Thessalonians is likely the oldest of Paul’s preserved letters, written to the church in Thessalonica that he had founded. The letter expresses Paul’s joy over the congregation’s faith and contains important teaching about Christ’s return and the resurrection of believers.

**Main theme:** Encouragement to a young church facing trouble, with instruction on holy living and hope in Christ’s return.

**Outline:**
- **Greeting and thanks for the exemplary faith of the Thessalonians** (Chapter 1)
- **Paul’s ministry and longing for the church** (Chapter 2)
- **Timothy’s mission and good report** (Chapter 3)
- **Exhortations to holiness and those who have died in Christ** (Chapter 4)
- **The Lord’s day and exhortations to watchfulness** (Chapter 5)

**Key themes:**
- **Exemplary faith:** The Thessalonians received the gospel amid trouble and became an example for all believers in Macedonia and Achaia (1:6–8).
- **Apostolic care:** Paul describes his ministry as that of a nursing mother and an exhorting father, marked by integrity and love (2:7–12).
- **Christ’s return (parousia):** Those who have died in Christ will rise first; afterward, the living will be caught up together with them to meet the Lord (4:13–18).
- **Watchfulness and holiness:** Believers are children of light and are called to sober living, holiness, and mutual encouragement while they wait for the Lord (5:4–11).

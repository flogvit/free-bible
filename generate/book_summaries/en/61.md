# 2 Peter

**About the book:** 2 Peter is Peter’s second and final letter, written shortly before his death (1:14). The letter is a heartfelt exhortation for believers to stand firm in the truth and grow in faith, while also warning against false teachers and confirming the promise of the Lord’s return.

**Main theme:** Believers must grow in knowledge of Christ and remain steadfast against false teaching as they await the day of the Lord.

**Outline:**
- Call to spiritual growth and confirmation of the truth (Chapter 1)
- Warning against false teachers and God’s judgment (Chapter 2)
- The day of the Lord and an appeal for holy living (Chapter 3)

**Key themes:**
- **Spiritual growth:** Believers are called to add virtue to their faith, knowledge, self-control, perseverance, godliness, brotherly kindness, and love (1:5–7).
- **Reliability of Scripture:** Prophecy did not come by human will, but men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit (1:20–21).
- **False teachers:** False teachers will arise who deny the Lord and lead many astray, but their judgment is certain (2:1–3).
- **The return of the Lord:** The Lord is not slow in keeping his promise, as some understand slowness. Instead, he is patient, wanting all to come to repentance. The day of the Lord will come like a thief (3:9–10).
- **New heaven and new earth:** Believers await new heavens and a new earth where righteousness dwells (3:13).

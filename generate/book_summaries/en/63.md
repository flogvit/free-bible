# 2 John

**About the book:** The second letter of John is the second shortest letter in the New Testament, written by "the elder" to "the chosen lady and her children"—likely a house church. The letter emphasizes the importance of walking in truth and love while warning against false teachers who deny that Christ came in the flesh.

**Main theme:** Remaining in Christ's teaching by walking in truth and love, and guarding against those who teach falsehood.

**Outline:**
- **Greeting and joy over faithfulness** (Chapter 1:1–4)
- **Exhortation to love and obedience** (Chapter 1:5–6)
- **Warning against deceivers and false teachers** (Chapter 1:7–11)
- **Closing and hope of meeting again** (Chapter 1:12–13)

**Key themes:**
- **The truth:** Truth is central to the letter and is repeatedly mentioned as the foundation for fellowship among believers (v. 1–4).
- **Love and obedience:** Loving one another is not a new command but the original command, demonstrated through obedience to God's commands (v. 5–6).
- **False teaching about Christ:** Deceivers who do not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh are called "the deceiver and the antichrist" (v. 7).
- **Remaining in Christ's teaching:** Whoever remains in Christ's teaching has both the Father and the Son, while whoever goes on without remaining in the teaching does not have God (v. 9).
- **Spiritual vigilance:** Believers are warned against receiving or supporting those who bring false doctrine (v. 10–11).

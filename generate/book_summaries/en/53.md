# 2 Thessalonians

**About the book:** Second Thessalonians is Paul’s follow-up letter to the church in Thessalonica, written together with Silvanus and Timothy. The letter addresses misunderstandings about the day of the Lord and gives practical exhortations to live an orderly life while waiting for Christ’s return.

**Main theme:** Clarification concerning the end times and encouragement to remain steadfast and diligent while awaiting the day of the Lord.

**Outline:**
- Greeting, thanksgiving, and God’s righteous judgment (Chapter 1)
- The day of the Lord: Apostasy and the man of lawlessness (2 Thessalonians 2:1–12)
- Encouragement to stand firm and remain faithful (2 Thessalonians 2:13–17)
- Prayer, exhortation to work, and final greeting (Chapter 3)

**Key themes:**
- **The day of the Lord and the end times:** Paul corrects misunderstandings that the day of the Lord has already come, explaining that apostasy and the man of lawlessness must appear first.
- **God’s righteous judgment:** God will repay those who persecute believers and grant rest to those who suffer when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven.
- **Steadfastness in faith:** The church is urged to stand firm and hold fast to the teachings they received, whether by word or letter.
- **Work and order:** Paul strongly warns against disorder and laziness, commanding that anyone unwilling to work should not eat.

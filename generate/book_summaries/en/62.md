# 1 John

**About the Book:** The First Letter of John was written by the apostle John to Christian congregations to strengthen their fellowship with God and one another, and to warn against false teaching. The letter emphasizes that genuine faith is demonstrated through love for God and others, obedience to God’s commands, and confession of Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh.

**Main Theme:** Fellowship with God through Jesus Christ is shown in a life characterized by light, love, and truth.

**Outline:**
- Introduction: The Word of Life and Fellowship (1:1–4)
- Walking in the Light: Confession and Forgiveness (1:5–2:2)
- Knowing God: Obedience and Love (2:3–11)
- To Children, Fathers, and Young Men: Spiritual Maturity (2:12–14)
- Warning Against the World and Antichrist (2:15–27)
- God’s Children and Righteousness (2:28–3:10)
- Brotherly Love in Action (3:11–24)
- Test the Spirits: Truth and Error (4:1–6)
- God’s Love and Our Love (4:7–21)
- Faith, Victory, and Witness (5:1–12)
- Conclusion: Assurance and Intercession (5:13–21)

**Key Themes:**
- **God is Light:** Fellowship with God requires that we walk in the light and confess our sins (1:5–9).
- **God is Love:** God’s nature is love, and this love has been revealed by sending his Son as an atoning sacrifice (4:8–10).
- **Jesus as Advocate:** Christ is our advocate and atoning sacrifice for the sins of the whole world (2:1–2).
- **Testing the Spirits:** Believers must distinguish between the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error by testing the confession about Christ (4:1–6).
- **Assurance of Salvation:** The letter is written so that believers may know they have eternal life (5:13).

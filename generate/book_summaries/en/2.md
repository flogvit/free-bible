# 2. Exodus (Exodus)

**About the book:** The second book of Moses, also known as Exodus, is the second book in the Bible and consists of 40 chapters. It tells the story of the Israelites' deliverance from slavery in Egypt, the revelation at Mount Sinai, and the establishment of the covenant between God and the people of Israel, with the tabernacle serving as God's dwelling place among them.

**Main theme:** God's mighty deliverance of His people from bondage and the establishment of a covenant that makes Israel a holy nation of priests with God's presence in their midst.

**Content overview:**
- The Israelites in Egyptian slavery (Chapters 1–2)
- Moses' call and mission (Chapters 3–4)
- Confrontation with Pharaoh and the plagues (Chapters 5–11)
- The departure from Egypt and the Passover celebration (Chapters 12–13)
- Crossing the Red Sea and wandering in the wilderness (Chapters 14–18)
- God's lawgiving at Mount Sinai (Chapters 19–24)
- Instructions for the tabernacle (Chapters 25–31)
- The golden calf and renewal of the covenant (Chapters 32–34)
- Construction and completion of the tabernacle (Chapters 35–40)

**Key themes:**
- **Deliverance:** God hears His people's cry and saves them from slavery with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm.
- **Covenant:** At Sinai, God makes a covenant with Israel and gives them the law as guidance for life as His people.
- **God's presence:** The tabernacle is built as God's dwelling place, where He can live among His people.
- **God's holiness:** Through the laws and the service of the tabernacle, Israel learns about God's holiness and how to approach Him.
- **Faithfulness and forgiveness:** Even when the people sin with the golden calf, God renews the covenant and shows His grace and mercy.

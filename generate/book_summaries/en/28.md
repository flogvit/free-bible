# Hosea

**About the book:** The book of Hosea is the first of the twelve minor prophets and consists of 14 chapters. The prophet Hosea ministered in the northern kingdom of Israel during the 700s B.C., under the kings Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah of Judah, as well as Jeroboam II of Israel. The book uses Hosea’s own marriage to the unfaithful Gomer as a powerful picture of the relationship between God and Israel.

**Main theme:** God’s faithful love for His people despite their spiritual unfaithfulness, and the call to repentance and restoration.

**Content overview:**
- **Hosea’s marriage to Gomer and the symbolic names of his children** (Chapters 1–3)
- **God’s accusation against Israel: Lack of faithfulness and knowledge of God** (Chapters 4–7)
- **Israel’s disobedience and the consequences of judgment** (Chapters 8–10)
- **God’s unchanging love for Israel** (Chapter 11)
- **Lessons from the history of the fathers and warnings** (Chapters 12–13)
- **Call to repentance and promise of restoration** (Chapter 14)

**Key themes:**
- **Spiritual unfaithfulness:** Israel is compared to an unfaithful wife who has left her husband for other lovers (idols).
- **God’s faithful love (hesed):** Despite Israel’s betrayal, God’s love and desire for reconciliation remain unchanged.
- **The covenant relationship:** God reminds Israel of the covenant from Egypt and their obligations as His people.
- **Repentance and hope:** The book concludes with a heartfelt call to repentance and a promise of healing and blessing.

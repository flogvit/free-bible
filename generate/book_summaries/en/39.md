# Malachi – Summary

## About the Book
Malachi is the last book of the Minor Prophets and concludes the Old Testament. The name "Malachi" means "my messenger" in Hebrew, and the book was likely written around 450–430 BC, during the Persian period after the temple had been rebuilt. The prophet speaks to a people who have become indifferent in their worship and their relationship with God.

## Main Theme
God calls His people back to faithful worship and covenant loyalty, preparing them for the coming day of the Lord.

## Content Overview

**God’s Love and Israel’s Ungratefulness** (ch. 1:1–5)  
God affirms His love for Jacob/Israel by contrasting it with Esau/Edom.

**Accusation Against the Priests for Profane Offerings** (ch. 1:6–14)  
The priests are criticized for offering sick and defective animals, which dishonors God’s name.

**Warning to the Priests and the Broken Levitical Covenant** (ch. 2:1–9)  
The priests have abandoned their calling and led the people astray.

**Unfaithfulness in Marriage and Toward God** (ch. 2:10–17)  
Criticism of divorce and marriage to foreign women; the people have wearied God with their words.

**The Messenger and the Day of the Lord** (ch. 3:1–6)  
The promise of a messenger who will prepare the way, and the Lord coming to His temple for purification and judgment.

**Call to Repentance and Tithes** (ch. 3:7–12)  
An appeal to return to God through faithful giving, with a promise of blessing.

**The Righteous and the Wicked on the Day of Judgment** (ch. 3:13–24)  
Contrast between those who fear the Lord and the wicked; the promise of Elijah’s coming before the great day of the Lord.

## Key Themes

- **God’s Unchanging Love:** Despite the people’s unfaithfulness, God remains faithful to His covenant with Israel.
- **Holy and Worthy Worship:** God requires that offerings and worship reflect His holiness and dignity.
- **Covenant Faithfulness:** Both the covenant with Levi (the priests) and the marriage covenant must be kept holy.
- **The Day of the Lord:** A coming day involving judgment on the wicked and salvation for the righteous.
- **Preparation and Repentance:** The promise of the prophet Elijah, who will come to turn hearts back before the judgment.

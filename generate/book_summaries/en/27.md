# Daniel

**About the book:** The book of Daniel tells the story of the Jewish prophet Daniel and his friends living in exile in Babylon. The book combines narratives about faithfulness to God under persecution with apocalyptic visions concerning future world empires and God’s ultimate victory.

**Main theme:** God is sovereign over all kings and kingdoms, and He delivers those who remain faithful to Him.

## Outline of Contents

- **Daniel and his friends at the Babylonian court** (Chapter 1)
- **Nebuchadnezzar’s dream about the statue** (Chapter 2)
- **The fiery furnace** (Chapter 3)
- **Nebuchadnezzar’s dream about the tree and his humiliation** (Chapter 4)
- **The writing on the wall at Belshazzar’s feast** (Chapter 5)
- **Daniel in the lions’ den** (Chapter 6)
- **The vision of the four beasts** (Chapter 7)
- **The vision of the ram and the goat** (Chapter 8)
- **Daniel’s prayer and the seventy weeks** (Chapter 9)
- **The vision concerning the end times** (Chapters 10–12)

## Key Themes

- **God’s sovereignty:** God rules over all the kings of the earth and determines the destiny of kingdoms.
- **Faithfulness under trial:** Daniel and his friends remain faithful to God even when threatened with death.
- **God’s revelation:** God reveals secrets and the future through dreams and visions.
- **Judgment and deliverance:** God judges the proud and delivers His people in the end times.
- **Resurrection:** The dead will rise, some to eternal life and some to everlasting shame (12:2).

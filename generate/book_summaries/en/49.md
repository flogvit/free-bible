# Ephesians

**About the book:** The letter to the Ephesians was written by the apostle Paul to the church in Ephesus, likely while he was in prison. The letter explores God’s eternal plan of salvation in Christ and how this creates unity between Jews and Gentiles in one church. It combines deep theology with practical guidance for Christian living.

**Main theme:** God’s plan to unite all things in Christ, and how believers are to live as one body in love and holiness.

**Outline:**
- **Introduction and God’s plan of salvation** (Chapter 1)
- **From death to life – unity in Christ** (Chapter 2)
- **Paul’s ministry and the mystery of Gentile inclusion** (Chapter 3)
- **Unity, spiritual gifts, and growth in the body of Christ** (Chapter 4)
- **Living in the light – a new life in love** (5:1–21)
- **Family relationships, work, and spiritual armor** (5:22–6:24)

**Key themes:**
- **Election and grace:** God chose believers before the foundation of the world, and salvation is entirely by grace through faith (1:4–5; 2:8–9).
- **Unity in Christ:** Christ has broken down the dividing wall between Jews and Gentiles and created one new humanity in himself (2:14–16).
- **The church as the body of Christ:** The church is Christ’s body, where each part has its function, and Christ is the head over all things (1:22–23; 4:15–16).
- **The new life:** Believers are called to put off the old self and put on the new self, created after God’s likeness (4:22–24).
- **Spiritual warfare:** Believers are to put on the full armor of God to stand against the devil’s cunning attacks (6:10–18).

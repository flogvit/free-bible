# Hebrews

**About the book:** The Letter to the Hebrews was written to Christians of Jewish background who were in danger of returning to Judaism. The letter argues systematically for the superiority of Christ and the new covenant over the old covenant, and urges readers to hold fast to their faith.

**Main theme:** Jesus Christ is God’s perfect revelation and the eternal high priest who has established a new and better covenant through his sacrifice.

**Outline:**
- **Christ, God’s Son – greater than angels** (Chapters 1–2)
- **Christ greater than Moses – warning against unbelief and the promise of God’s rest** (Chapters 3–4)
- **Christ as high priest in the order of Melchizedek** (Chapters 5–7)
- **The superiority of the new covenant and Christ’s perfect sacrifice** (Chapters 8–10)
- **Witnesses of faith throughout history** (Chapter 11)
- **Encouragement to endure under God’s discipline** (Chapter 12)
- **Final exhortations and greetings** (Chapter 13)

**Key themes:**
- **Christ’s superiority:** Jesus is greater than angels, Moses, and the Levitical priesthood, and is God’s perfect revelation.
- **The eternal high priesthood:** Christ is a high priest in the order of Melchizedek with an indestructible priesthood that saves completely.
- **The new covenant:** The new covenant in Christ’s blood is better than the old because it provides true forgiveness and a new heart.
- **Faith and endurance:** Faith is "the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen" (11:1), and believers are called to persevere through trials.
- **Warning against falling away:** Serious warnings against turning away from faith and treating the blood of God’s Son with contempt.

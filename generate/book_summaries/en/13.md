# 1 Chronicles

**About the book:** 1 Chronicles is a historical book that covers Israel’s history from Adam to the death of King David. The book places special emphasis on David’s kingdom, the preparations for the temple, and the organization of worship. It was likely written after the exile to give the rebuilt community an understanding of its heritage and identity.

**Main theme:** God’s faithful leadership of His people through genealogies and the monarchy, with a focus on proper worship and preparation for the temple.

**Content overview:**
- Genealogies from Adam to Israel (Chapters 1–9)
- The death of Saul and David’s anointing as king (Chapters 10–12)
- Bringing the ark of the covenant to Jerusalem (Chapters 13–16)
- God’s covenant with David (Chapter 17)
- David’s military victories (Chapters 18–20)
- The census and the plague (Chapter 21)
- Preparations for the temple (Chapter 22)
- Organization of the Levites and priests (Chapters 23–26)
- David’s administration and farewell address (Chapters 27–29)

**Key themes:**
- **Genealogies and God’s plan:** The extensive genealogies demonstrate God’s faithful leadership through generations from Adam to David.
- **Proper worship:** The placement of the ark of the covenant and the organization of the Levites highlight the importance of correct worship.
- **The Davidic covenant:** God’s promise of an eternal kingdom through David’s line points forward to the Messiah.
- **Temple preparations:** David prepares materials and organization for the temple that Solomon will build.
- **Obedience and blessing:** The contrast between Saul’s disobedience and David’s devotion shows the connection between obedience to God and blessing.

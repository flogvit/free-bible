# 1 Timothy

**About the book:** The First Letter to Timothy is a letter from the apostle Paul to his young coworker Timothy, who was serving in the church at Ephesus. The letter gives practical guidance on organizing and leading the congregation and maintaining sound teaching, and it is one of the so-called "pastoral epistles".

**Main theme:** Guidance on how to lead and organize God’s church with sound doctrine, godliness, and good morals.

**Outline:**
- Warning against false teaching and Paul’s testimony about grace (Chapter 1)
- Prayer and order in worship (Chapter 2)
- Qualifications for overseers and deacons (Chapter 3)
- Warnings against apostasy and encouragement to pursue godliness (Chapter 4)
- Care for widows, elders, and slaves (Chapters 5–6:2)
- Warnings about wealth and the good fight of faith (Chapter 6:3–21)

**Key themes:**
- **Sound doctrine:** The letter repeatedly warns against false teaching and urges believers to hold fast to the sound teaching that agrees with the gospel.
- **Church leadership:** Paul gives detailed qualifications for overseers and deacons, emphasizing character and how one manages their household.
- **Godliness:** Godliness (εὐσέβεια) is highlighted as valuable for both this life and the coming one, and as true wealth when accompanied by contentment.
- **Prayer for all people:** The church is encouraged to pray for everyone, including those in authority, because God wants all people to be saved.
- **The good fight of faith:** Timothy is urged to fight the good fight of faith and keep safe what has been entrusted to him.

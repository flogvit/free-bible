# Ezra

**About the book:** The book of Ezra is a historical book in the Old Testament consisting of 10 chapters. It tells of the Jews' return from exile in Babylon, the rebuilding of the temple in Jerusalem, and Ezra's reforms to restore true worship.

**Main theme:** God's faithfulness in restoring His people and their worship after the exile, and the importance of obedience to God's law.

**Outline:**
- **Cyrus's decree and the return begins** (Chapter 1)
- **List of those who returned from exile** (Chapter 2)
- **The altar is rebuilt and the temple foundation is laid** (Chapter 3)
- **Opposition and a halt in temple construction** (Chapter 4)
- **Prophets encourage and building resumes** (Chapter 5)
- **The temple is completed and dedicated** (Chapter 6)
- **Ezra's journey to Jerusalem with royal authority** (Chapter 7)
- **List of those who traveled with Ezra** (Chapter 8)
- **Ezra's prayer for forgiveness regarding mixed marriages** (Chapter 9)
- **The people repent and dissolve mixed marriages** (Chapter 10)

**Key themes:**
- **God's sovereignty:** God moves the hearts of pagan kings to fulfill His promises of restoration (Ezra 1:1).
- **Restoration of worship:** The rebuilding of the temple and the reinstatement of sacrifices and festivals are central.
- **Purity and holiness:** Separation from foreign peoples and the dissolution of mixed marriages highlight the need for spiritual purity.
- **God's Word and law:** Ezra as a scribe demonstrates the importance of studying, practicing, and teaching God's law (Ezra 7:10).

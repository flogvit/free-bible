# 2 Timothy

**About the book:** 2 Timothy is Paul’s second letter to his young coworker Timothy, written while Paul was imprisoned in Rome. This is likely Paul’s final letter, in which he prepares Timothy to carry on the ministry and gives personal exhortations before his impending death.

**Main theme:** An appeal for faithful service, endurance in suffering, and preservation of sound teaching amid apostasy.

**Outline:**
- **Greeting, thanksgiving, and encouragement to serve with boldness** (Chapter 1)
- **Exhortations to faithful service and right handling of the word of truth** (Chapter 2)
- **Difficulties in the last days and the significance of Scripture** (Chapter 3)
- **Paul’s farewell, call to preach the word, and personal greetings** (Chapter 4)

**Key themes:**
- **Endurance in suffering:** Paul urges Timothy to suffer for the gospel and endure as a good soldier of Christ Jesus (1:8; 2:3).
- **Preservation of sound teaching:** Timothy is exhorted to hold fast to the healthy words and guard the good deposit entrusted to him (1:13–14; 2:15).
- **Authority of Scripture:** All Scripture is God-breathed and useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting, and training in righteousness (3:16–17).
- **Faithful service:** Timothy must preach the word in season and out of season, and complete his ministry despite opposition and apostasy (4:1–5).
- **God’s faithfulness:** Even though people fail, God remains faithful and will preserve those who belong to him (2:13, 19; 4:17–18).

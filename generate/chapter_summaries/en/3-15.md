**Main Theme:** Laws concerning ritual impurity related to bodily discharges in men and women, and how purity is restored.

**Content:** The chapter provides detailed instructions for handling impurity from abnormal discharge in men, seminal emission, menstruation, and abnormal bleeding in women. Both persons and objects that come into contact with the unclean become unclean themselves and must be washed. Cleansing requires a seven-day waiting period, ritual washing (for men with discharge specifically in running water), and for prolonged discharge also an offering of two turtledoves or two young pigeons. The purpose is to keep the Israelites separated from impurity so that they do not defile God's tabernacle and die.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Zav (זוב):** Abnormal bodily fluid that causes impurity and requires extensive cleansing rituals.
- **Unclean until evening:** The most common form of temporary impurity, which is removed by washing after sunset.
- **Seven days:** The standard period for more serious impurity such as menstruation and abnormal discharge.
- **Sin offering and burnt offering:** Offerings required on the eighth day to complete cleansing from prolonged discharge.
- **God's dwelling (mishkan):** The tabernacle that must be protected from impurity—the rationale behind the entire purity system.

**Main Theme:** Chapter 38 describes the completion of the tabernacle's outer furnishings and provides a detailed account of the materials used.

**Content:** Bezalel and Oholiab build the bronze altar from acacia wood overlaid with bronze, and the basin is made from bronze mirrors brought by the women at the entrance to the tent of meeting. The courtyard is surrounded by hangings of finely twisted linen, supported by pillars and bases of bronze. The chapter concludes with an exact inventory of all the gold, silver, and bronze used—the silver coming from the census tax of half a shekel per man.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bronze Altar** – The outer altar made of bronze where sacrificial animals were burned, serving as the center of worship in the courtyard.
- **Basin** – A bronze laver for the priests' ritual washing, crafted from the women's mirrors as a symbol of surrender.
- **Courtyard** – The enclosed area around the tabernacle where the people could approach God.
- **Material Inventory** – A detailed record of gold (29 talents), silver (100 talents), and bronze (70 talents), demonstrating accountability and precision in stewardship.

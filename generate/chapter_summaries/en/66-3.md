**Main Theme:** Christ speaks to the churches in Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea with warnings, encouragement, and promises to the one who overcomes.

**Content:** To Sardis, Jesus warns that they have a reputation of being alive but are dead, calling them to repentance—only a few have kept themselves unstained. Philadelphia is commended for faithfulness despite little strength; they receive an open door and the promise of protection in the hour of trial. Laodicea is criticized for lukewarmness and self-deception regarding their wealth; Jesus calls them to buy true riches from Him and to open the door when He stands knocking.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **White garments:** A symbol of purity and victory, promised to the faithful in Sardis and Laodicea.
- **The key of David:** Christ has authority to open and shut—no one can hinder His will (Philadelphia).
- **Lukewarm:** The spiritual condition of Laodicea, neither hot nor cold, which Christ says He will spit out.
- **"I stand at the door and knock":** Christ's invitation to fellowship with anyone who opens for Him.

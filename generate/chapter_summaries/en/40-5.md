**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about life in God's kingdom through the Beatitudes and a deeper understanding of God's will.

**Content:** In the opening of the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus declares blessings upon the humble, those who mourn, the merciful, and the peacemakers. He calls his disciples to be salt and light in the world. Jesus affirms the validity of the law but expands it by showing that God's will applies not only to outward actions such as murder and adultery, but also to attitudes of the heart like anger and lust. The chapter culminates with the command to love one's enemies and to be perfect as the heavenly Father is perfect.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Beatitudes (μακάριοι):** Eight declarations about who is blessed in God's kingdom – the poor in spirit, those who mourn, the meek, and the persecuted.
- **Salt and Light:** Images of the disciples' calling to preserve and illuminate the world through good deeds.
- **Fulfilling the Law (πληρῶσαι):** Jesus came not to abolish the law, but to fulfill it and reveal its true meaning more fully.
- **"But I tell you" (ἐγὼ δὲ λέγω ὑμῖν):** Jesus' authoritative statements that expand the law to include attitudes of the heart.
- **Perfection (τέλειοι):** The call to reflect God's perfect love, which embraces both the evil and the good.

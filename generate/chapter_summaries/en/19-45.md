**Main Theme:** A royal wedding psalm that extols the king's majesty and beauty, with messianic overtones.

**Content:** The psalmist praises the king for his beauty, grace, and righteousness, addressing him with divine language: "Your throne, O God, endures forever" (v.7). The bride is urged to leave her family and devote herself completely to the king as her lord. The psalm concludes with promises of descendants who will become princes, and eternal honor for the king's name.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Oil of gladness (v.8):** The king is anointed with joy above his companions, a picture of divine election.
- **Gold from Ophir (v.10):** The queen stands at the king's right hand in costly gold, symbolizing honor and dignity.
- **The eternal throne (v.7):** The king's reign is described as everlasting and righteous—quoted in Hebrews 1:8–9 concerning Christ.
- **Scepter of righteousness (v.7):** The king's rule is characterized by uprightness and truth.

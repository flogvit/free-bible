**Main Theme:** A liturgical hymn of praise that extols God for his eternal love through creation and the history of Israel.

**Content:** The psalm opens with an invitation to give thanks to the Lord, the God of gods and the Lord of lords. It praises God as the Creator of heaven, earth, and the heavenly bodies, then recounts his mighty acts in delivering Israel from Egypt, leading them through the wilderness, and conquering the land. Each verse concludes with the refrain "for his love endures forever," which is repeated 26 times.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Chesed (חֶסֶד):** God's faithful, covenantal love that lasts forever—the central theme of the psalm.
- **Creation:** God formed the heavens with wisdom, spread out the earth over the waters, and created the great lights (vv. 5–9).
- **Deliverance (Exodus):** God struck down Egypt's firstborn, brought Israel out with a mighty hand, and parted the Red Sea (vv. 10–15).
- **The Promised Land:** God gave the land of Sihon and Og as an inheritance to Israel, his servant (vv. 17–22).

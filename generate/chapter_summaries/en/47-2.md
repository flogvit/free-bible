**Main Theme:** Paul explains his travel plans and urges forgiveness and restoration of the one who has sinned.

**Content:** Paul decided not to come to the Corinthians in sorrow, but wrote a letter with tears to show his love. He encourages the church to forgive and comfort the one who was disciplined, so that Satan will not gain an advantage. Paul describes his anxiety in Troas when he did not find Titus, but thanks God, who always leads them in triumph and spreads the knowledge of Christ everywhere.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Forgiveness (χαρίζεσθαι):** The church is urged to confirm their love and forgive the repentant sinner.
- **The fragrance of Christ (Χριστοῦ εὐωδία):** Believers are a fragrance from Christ – for some, it leads to life; for others, to death.
- **Satan's schemes (τὰ νοήματα):** Without forgiveness, Satan can exploit the situation and harm the church.
- **Triumphal procession (θριαμβεύοντι):** God always leads believers in victory through Christ.

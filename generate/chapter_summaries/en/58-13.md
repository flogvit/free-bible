**Main Theme:** Practical exhortations about the Christian life in light of Jesus' perfect sacrifice and his unchangeable nature.

**Content:** The chapter opens with exhortations concerning brotherly love, hospitality, care for prisoners and the suffering, as well as the sanctity of marriage and freedom from greed. The author points to Jesus Christ as "the same yesterday, today, and forever," and warns against strange teachings. Jesus suffered outside the city gate in order to sanctify the people, and believers are called to go out to him and offer a sacrifice of praise. The chapter concludes with exhortations to obey leaders, a prayer that the God of peace may equip readers to do his will, and greetings.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Brotherly Love (φιλαδελφία):** The fundamental attitude that should characterize fellowship among Christians.
- **Jesus Christ – the same:** Expresses Christ's eternal unchangeableness as the firm foundation of faith (v.8).
- **Outside the camp/gate:** A picture of Jesus' suffering in disgrace, which believers are called to share.
- **Sacrifice of Praise:** The Christian offering is not animals, but the fruit of lips that confess God's name.
- **The Great Shepherd:** Jesus who was brought up from the dead by the blood of the eternal covenant.

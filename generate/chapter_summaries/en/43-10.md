**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself as the good shepherd and the gate to salvation, declaring his unity with the Father.

**Content:** Jesus uses the image of the shepherd and the sheepfold to describe his relationship with believers: He is both the gate that provides access to salvation and the good shepherd who lays down his life for the sheep. He emphasizes the mutual knowledge between himself and his own, stating that he also has "other sheep" whom he must gather into one flock. During the Feast of Dedication in Jerusalem, Jesus is confronted by Jewish leaders, and his statement "I and the Father are one" leads to accusations of blasphemy and attempts to stone him. Jesus withdraws to the region beyond the Jordan, where many come to believe.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Good Shepherd:** Jesus as the one who lays down his life for the sheep, in contrast to the hired hand who flees.
- **The Gate:** Jesus as the only way to salvation and abundant life.
- **One Flock, One Shepherd:** The vision of gathering all believers—including "other sheep"—into one unity.
- **"I and the Father are one":** Jesus' declaration of unity with God, which triggers an accusation of blasphemy.

**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals his coming suffering and glory as he encounters both worship and unbelief.

**Content:** In Bethany, Mary anoints Jesus' feet with expensive nard oil, an act Jesus connects to his burial. During his entry into Jerusalem, he is hailed as king, yet he predicts that he must die like a grain of wheat to bear fruit. A voice from heaven confirms God's glory, and Jesus declares that the ruler of this world will be cast out when he himself is lifted up. Despite many signs, the people remain in unbelief—fulfilling Isaiah's prophecy—while Jesus concludes with an appeal to believe in the light.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Nard Oil:** Mary's anointing symbolizes devotion and points forward to Jesus' burial.
- **The Grain of Wheat:** This image illustrates that Jesus' death will bring salvation to many.
- **Lifted Up (ὑψωθῶ):** A double image referring both to the crucifixion and Jesus' exaltation.
- **Light and Darkness:** Jesus is the light who came into the world; those who believe become children of the light.
- **Unbelief:** Despite the signs, Isaiah's prophecy about hardened hearts is fulfilled.

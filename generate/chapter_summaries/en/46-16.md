**Main Theme:** Paul concludes the letter with practical instructions regarding the collection, travel plans, fellow workers, and exhortations to faithfulness and love.

**Content:** Paul instructs the congregation about a weekly collection for the saints in Jerusalem. He shares his travel plans—he intends to remain in Ephesus until Pentecost before traveling through Macedonia to Corinth. He mentions his fellow workers Timothy and Apollos, urging the congregation to receive them well. The chapter ends with strong exhortations to stand firm in the faith and to do everything in love, followed by greetings from the churches in Asia and the Aramaic cry "Maranata" (Our Lord, come!).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Collection (λογεία):** An organized monetary collection for the poor Christians in Jerusalem, expressing Christian solidarity.
- **Open door (θύρα ἀνέῳγεν):** A metaphor for opportunities for evangelism in Ephesus, despite opposition.
- **Maranata (Μαράνα θά):** An Aramaic cry meaning "Our Lord, come!"—expressing expectation of Christ's return.
- **Stand firm (στήκετε):** An exhortation to hold fast to the faith with courage and strength.
- **Love (ἀγάπη):** Everything in congregational life should be marked by love—the fundamental Christian virtue.

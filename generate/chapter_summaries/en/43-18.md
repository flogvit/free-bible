**Main Theme:** Jesus is arrested, interrogated, and brought before Pilate, while revealing his divine identity and the nature of his kingdom.

**Content:** Jesus goes with his disciples to a garden by the Kidron Valley, where Judas arrives with soldiers and temple guards to arrest him. At Jesus' words "I am," the soldiers fall to the ground. Peter cuts off the ear of Malchus, the servant, but Jesus tells him to put away his sword. Jesus is first taken to Annas, then to Caiaphas, while Peter denies him three times. Before Pilate, Jesus declares that his kingdom is not of this world, and Pilate finds no guilt in him, yet the people choose to release Barabbas instead.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"I am" (Ἐγώ εἰμι):** Jesus' divine self-revelation that causes the soldiers to fall to the ground.
- **The cup of suffering:** Symbolizes God's will, which Jesus willingly accepts (v.11).
- **Peter's denial:** Three denials fulfilled at the crowing of a rooster, showing human weakness.
- **"My kingdom is not of this world":** Jesus explains to Pilate that his kingship is of a different nature than earthly kingdoms.
- **Barabbas:** The robber whom the people choose over Jesus, illustrating humanity's rejection of the innocent one.

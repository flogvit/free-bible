**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts the Corinthians to resolve conflicts internally and honor God with their bodies by fleeing sexual immorality.

**Content:** Paul criticizes the church for taking lawsuits before unbelieving judges instead of resolving them within the community—those who are to judge the world and angels should be able to handle everyday disputes. He warns that those who practice unrighteousness will not inherit God’s kingdom, yet reminds the Corinthians that they have been washed, sanctified, and justified. Furthermore, he emphasizes that the body belongs to the Lord and is a temple of the Holy Spirit; therefore, they must flee sexual immorality and honor God with their bodies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Judge the world and angels** – The saints will one day judge alongside Christ, which qualifies them to settle minor disputes.
- **Washed, sanctified, justified** – Describes the radical transformation that occurs through faith, in Jesus’ name and by God’s Spirit.
- **Temple of the body** – The body is the dwelling place of the Holy Spirit and does not belong to the believer themselves, but to God.
- **Bought with a price** – Believers have been purchased at a cost (Christ’s blood) and should therefore honor God with their bodies.

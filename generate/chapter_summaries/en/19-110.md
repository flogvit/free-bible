**Main Theme:** A prophetic psalm about the Messiah as king and priest, exalted at God's right hand.

**Content:** The Lord declares that David's Lord shall sit at his right hand until his enemies are made a footstool for his feet. From Zion God sends forth his scepter of power, and the people offer themselves willingly on the day of battle. The Lord has sworn an unchangeable oath: this king is also an eternal priest in the order of Melchizedek. He will judge among the nations and shatter kings on the day of wrath.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's right hand:** The place of honor symbolizing authority and exaltation.
- **Melchizedek:** The mysterious king-priest from Genesis 14, who unites kingship and priesthood.
- **Footstool:** A picture of total victory where enemies are completely subdued.
- **The brook by the way:** Refreshment in the midst of battle, giving strength to lift the head in triumph.

**Main Theme:** God humbles the mighty King Nebuchadnezzar to show that the Most High has power over all kingdoms.

**Content:** King Nebuchadnezzar himself recounts a dream in which a great tree is cut down, but its stump is preserved. When the Babylonian wise men cannot interpret the dream, Daniel explains that the tree represents the king himself, who will lose his sanity and live like an animal for seven periods of time until he acknowledges God's sovereignty. The prophecy is fulfilled, and after the appointed time Nebuchadnezzar is restored—he praises and honors the King of heaven.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Great Tree:** Symbolizes Nebuchadnezzar's worldwide power and influence.
- **The Watcher from Heaven:** A heavenly messenger who announces God's judgment against the king.
- **Seven Periods of Time:** The duration of the king's humiliation until he learns humility.
- **The Stump with Roots:** A sign of hope—the kingdom will be preserved and restored.
- **The Most High:** God as the sovereign ruler over all human kingdoms.

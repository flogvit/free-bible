**Main Theme:** David calls on God for righteous judgment and deliverance from enemies who repay his kindness with evil.

**Content:** David asks God to intervene as a warrior and judge against enemies who pursue him without cause. He contrasts his own care for them—fasting and mourning when they were sick—with their betrayal and false accusations. The psalm alternates between prayers for the defeat of the enemy and promises of public praise when God acts.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Chaff before the wind (v.5):** A picture of the enemies' fate—they will be scattered and vanish by the Lord's angelic intervention.
- **Net and trap (vv.7–8):** The enemies dig pits for David, but they themselves will fall into their own snares.
- **Lions (כְּפִירִים, v.17):** The enemies are compared to young lions threatening David's life.
- **Witnesses of violence (v.11):** False witnesses arise and accuse David of things he knows nothing about.

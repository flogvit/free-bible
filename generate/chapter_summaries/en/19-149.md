**Main Theme:** An exhortation to God's people to praise the Lord with joy and carry out his righteous judgment.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a call to sing a new song to the Lord in the assembly of the faithful. Israel and the children of Zion are invited to rejoice in their Creator and King with dancing, tambourine, and lyre. The Lord takes delight in his people and adorns the humble with salvation. Believers are to praise God while carrying out his judgment against the nations—this is an honor for all his faithful ones.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **New song (שִׁיר חָדָשׁ):** Expresses fresh and renewed praise to God.
- **Dance and music (מָחוֹל, תֹּף, כִנּוֹר):** Holy worship involving the whole body and instruments.
- **Two-edged sword (חֶרֶב פִּיפִיּוֹת):** Symbol of the power of God's word and the execution of divine judgment.
- **The faithful/humble (חֲסִידִים, עֲנָוִים):** God's people who receive salvation and are honored to carry out his will.

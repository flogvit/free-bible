**Main Theme:** God’s final judgment on Babylon, the symbol of worldly evil, luxury, and idolatry.

**Content:** A mighty angel announces that Babylon has fallen and become a dwelling place for demons and unclean spirits. God’s people are urged to leave the city so they will not share in her sins or be struck by her plagues, which have reached all the way to heaven. While the kings of the earth, merchants, and sailors mourn the loss of wealth and trade, heaven, the holy ones, the apostles, and the prophets are called to rejoice over God’s righteous judgment. An angel throws a large millstone into the sea as a sign of Babylon’s final and total destruction.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Babylon the Great:** A symbol of the world’s god-opposing center of power, characterized by luxury, immorality, and persecution of the holy ones.
- **The Millstone:** An angel casts a large stone into the sea as a dramatic picture of Babylon’s sudden and irreversible fall.
- **The Merchants’ Lament:** The earth’s traders mourn their lost wealth—a list of luxury goods, including "human souls"—reveals the system’s inhumate greed.
- **The Cup of Double Retribution:** Babylon receives double for her deeds, an expression of God’s righteous retribution.
- **Heaven’s Rejoicing:** In contrast to earthly sorrow, heaven and the holy ones are called to rejoice that God has executed a just judgment.

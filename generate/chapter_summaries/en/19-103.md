**Main Theme:** A psalm of praise in which David encourages his soul and all creation to praise the Lord for His boundless grace and mercy.

**Content:** The psalm opens and closes with the call "Praise the Lord, my soul" and lists God's benefits: forgiveness, healing, redemption, and being crowned with grace. It contrasts human frailty with God's eternal faithfulness toward those who fear Him. Finally, the praise is expanded to include angels, heavenly hosts, and all creation.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **חֶסֶד (grace/faithfulness):** God's covenant loyalty that is higher than the heavens above the earth (v.11) and endures from everlasting to everlasting (v.17).
- **Eagle (v.5):** A symbol of renewal and vitality—youth is renewed like the eagle's.
- **East and west (v.12):** The infinite distance illustrates how completely God removes our sins.
- **Father and children (v.13):** God's tender mercy compared to a father's love for his children.
- **Grass and flower (v.15-16):** Human frailty contrasted with God's eternal love.

**Main Theme:** Moses exhorts Israel to wholehearted love and obedience toward God as the foundation for life in the promised land.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the Shema—Israel's confession of faith that the Lord is one—followed by the command to love God with all your heart, soul, and strength. These words are to be taught to children, bound as signs on the body (tefillin), and written on the doorposts (mezuzah). Moses warns against forgetting God when enjoying prosperity in Canaan, against idolatry, and against putting God to the test. When children ask about the meaning of the commands, parents are to recount the story of the exodus from Egypt.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shema ("Hear, O Israel"):** The central confession of faith affirming God's unity and calling for undivided loyalty (v. 4).
- **Tefillin and mezuzah:** Physical reminders of God's words—bound on the hand and forehead, written on doorposts—that are to shape every aspect of life (vv. 8–9).
- **"A land flowing with milk and honey":** A picture of the promised land's abundance and the fulfillment of God's promise (v. 3).
- **Faith instruction:** The command to teach the commands to children and answer their questions with the story of salvation (vv. 7, 20–25).

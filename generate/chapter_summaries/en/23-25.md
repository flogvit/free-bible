**Main Theme:** A song of praise to God for his mighty deeds and a prophetic vision of the final victory over death.

**Content:** Isaiah praises God for destroying hostile cities and being a refuge for the weak. He prophesies about a feast on Mount Zion for all peoples, where God will swallow up death, wipe away tears, and remove the disgrace of his people. The chapter concludes with judgment against Moab, who will be completely humbled.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Feast (v.6):** A rich meal with choice foods and clear wine symbolizing God's final salvation for all peoples.
- **The Veil Removed (v.7):** The covering over the nations represents ignorance or sorrow that God will take away.
- **Death Swallowed Up (v.8):** A vision of death's ultimate destruction, quoted by Paul in 1 Cor 15:54.
- **Moab in the Dung Pit (vv.10–12):** An image of total humiliation for the proud, contrasted with God's care for the humble.

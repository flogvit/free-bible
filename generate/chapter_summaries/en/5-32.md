**Main Theme:** Moses' song praises God's faithfulness and justice, warns Israel against apostasy, and proclaims God's judgment upon their enemies.

**Content:** Moses delivers a song as a witness against Israel before his death. The song contrasts God's perfect care – He found Israel in the wilderness and guarded them as the apple of His eye – with the people's ingratitude when they became full and turned to foreign gods. God will punish His people, but ultimately He will avenge their blood and judge their enemies. The chapter concludes with God commanding Moses to go up Mount Nebo to see the land before he dies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Rock (הַצּוּר):** A recurring image of God as unchanging and reliable, in contrast to false gods.
- **Eagle:** An image of God's care – like an eagle that stirs its nest and carries its young on its wings (v.11).
- **Jeshurun (יְשֻׁרוּן):** A poetic name for Israel; "the upright one" who became fat and kicked against God (v.15).
- **Vengeance and Atonement:** God will ultimately avenge the blood of His servants and cleanse the land (v.43).

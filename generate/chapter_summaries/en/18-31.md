**Main Theme:** Job makes a solemn declaration of innocence in the form of conditional curses to prove his righteousness before God.

**Content:** Job begins with his covenant with his eyes regarding sexual purity and then reviews various areas of life where he asserts his innocence: he has treated servants fairly, helped the poor, widows, and orphans, avoided idolatry and greed, and shown hospitality to strangers. Throughout the chapter, he uses the oath formula "if I have done this, let a curse come upon me." The chapter concludes with Job's challenge to God to answer him and his desire for a written indictment.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant with my eyes (v.1):** Job committed himself not to look at a virgin with lust – symbolizing inner moral discipline.
- **The scales of righteousness (v.6):** Job asks to be weighed on the scale of righteousness – an image of God's righteous judgment.
- **Conditional curses (אִם-formula):** A recurring structure in which Job calls down punishment upon himself if he has sinned – a legal oath form.
- **Indictment (v.35):** Job desires a written document from his accuser – expressing a longing for a fair trial with God.

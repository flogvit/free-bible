**Main Theme:** Habakkuk's prayer is a praise of God's mighty acts of salvation and a confession of faith in the midst of fear and distress.

**Content:** The chapter is shaped as a prayer (tefillah) where Habakkuk first expresses awe at God's works and asks for the renewal of his deeds. This is followed by a powerful theophany describing God coming in majesty from the south, where nature trembles and nations are shaken before his presence. Habakkuk reacts with physical trembling to this vision but concludes with a radical confession of faith: even if everything fails—fig trees, vines, olive groves, fields, and livestock—he will rejoice in the Lord, his saving God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Theophany (vv. 3–15):** God's mighty appearance is described with cosmic imagery—his glory covers the heavens, pestilence and plague follow him, mountains quake, and the sun and moon stand still.
- **Salvation/yeshua (vv. 8, 13):** God rides out for salvation for his people and his anointed—one central theme that reveals God's saving purpose.
- **Hooves on the heights (v. 19):** The image of having feet like a deer and walking on the heights expresses victory and security given by God.
- **The confession of faith (vv. 17–18):** In the midst of total material distress, the prophet chooses to rejoice in the Lord—a timeless expression of faith independent of circumstances.

**Main Theme:** God’s providence saves David by having Hushai’s advice preferred over Ahithophel’s.

**Content:** Ahithophel advises Absalom to attack David immediately while he is weak, but Hushai persuades Absalom to wait and gather a larger army—a counsel that gives David time to flee. Hushai warns David through the priests Zadok and Abiathar, and the messengers Jonathan and Ahimaaz escape by hiding in a well. David and his people cross the Jordan River and arrive at Mahanaim, where they receive supplies from local supporters. When Ahithophel sees that his advice is not followed, he takes his own life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ahithophel’s counsel:** A wise but brutal strategy to kill only David in order to end the conflict quickly.
- **Hushai’s counter-counsel:** An apparently better strategy that actually serves David by giving him time to escape.
- **The well at Bahurim:** A symbol of how ordinary people risked their lives to help David.
- **Ahithophel’s suicide:** Shows the consequence of trusting in one’s own wisdom rather than God.

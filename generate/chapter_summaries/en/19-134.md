**Main Theme:** A call to nighttime praise in the temple, with an answer in the form of the Lord's blessing.

**Content:** Psalm 134 is the last of the Songs of Ascents and addresses the servants of the Lord who stand in the temple at night. They are encouraged to lift their hands toward the sanctuary and bless the Lord. The psalm concludes with a blessing: May the Lord, Creator of heaven and earth, bless you from Zion.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Servants of the Lord:** Likely Levites or priests who had nighttime service in the temple.
- **Lifted hands:** A gesture of worship expressing prayer and surrender to God.
- **Zion:** The temple hill in Jerusalem, the place where God's presence dwelt and from which blessing flowed.
- **Creator of heaven and earth:** A title that emphasizes God's omnipotence and sovereignty.

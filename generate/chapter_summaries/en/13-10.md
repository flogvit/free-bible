**Main Theme:** Saul’s death as a result of unfaithfulness to the Lord, and the transfer of the kingdom to David.

**Content:** The Philistines defeat Israel on Mount Gilboa, and Saul’s three sons are killed. Severely wounded by archers, Saul takes his own life to avoid disgrace at the hands of the enemy. The Philistines find the bodies, place Saul’s armor in the temple of their gods, and display his head in the temple of Dagon. The men of Jabesh-Gilead retrieve the bodies and bury them with a seven-day fast. The Chronicler explains that Saul died because of his unfaithfulness to the Lord—he did not keep God’s word and sought counsel from a medium.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Gilboa:** The site of the decisive defeat where Saul and his sons fell.
- **Unfaithfulness (מַעַל):** Saul’s sin against the Lord that led to his downfall.
- **Temple of Dagon:** The Philistine sanctuary where Saul’s head was displayed as a trophy of victory.
- **Jabesh-Gilead:** The city whose inhabitants showed loyalty by burying Saul with honor.
- **The Kingdom Given to David:** God’s sovereign act that ends Saul’s dynasty and begins David’s.

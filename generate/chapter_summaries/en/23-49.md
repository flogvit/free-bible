**Main Theme:** The Lord’s Servant is to bring salvation to Israel and be a light for the nations, and God assures Zion of His unfailing love.

**Content:** The Lord’s Servant was called from his mother’s womb with a mission extending beyond Israel—he is to be a light to the nations and bring salvation to the ends of the earth. Zion complains of being forsaken, but God responds with a powerful promise: even if a mother could forget her child, He will never forget His people. The chapter concludes with promises that the exiles will return, kings will serve Israel, and God Himself will fight against the enemies of His people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord’s Servant:** Called from his mother’s womb, with a mouth like a sharp sword—chosen to restore Israel and be a light for the nations.
- **A Light to the Nations:** The Servant’s mission expands from Israel to the whole world—God’s salvation will reach the ends of the earth.
- **Engraved in God’s Hands:** Zion’s walls are drawn on the palms of God’s hands—an image of His uneraseable love.
- **The Mother Image:** God compares His faithfulness to a mother’s love, yet surpasses it—he will never forget His people.

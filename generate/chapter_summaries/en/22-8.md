**Main Theme:** The unconquerable power of love and the longing for perfect union.

**Content:** The woman longs to be able to show her love openly and asks her beloved to set her as a seal upon his heart, because love is strong as death and cannot be quenched by anything. Her brothers discuss how they should protect their little sister when she becomes ready for marriage, and the woman declares her own maturity. The chapter concludes with a contrast between Solomon's vineyard and the woman's own, along with an invitation to the beloved to come.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Seal upon the heart:** A symbol of unbreakable belonging and ownership in love.
- **Love strong as death:** Describes the unconquerable and intense nature of love.
- **Wall and door:** Images representing the woman's virtue and readiness for marriage.
- **Vineyard:** A metaphor for the woman's own body and worth, contrasted with Solomon's wealth.

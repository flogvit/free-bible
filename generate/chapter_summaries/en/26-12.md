**Main Theme:** God commands Ezekiel to perform symbolic actions that foretell Jerusalem’s fall and the people’s exile.

**Content:** Ezekiel is instructed to pack like a fugitive, dig through a wall, and go out with his face covered—a sign of how the prince in Jerusalem will be taken away to Babylon without seeing the land. He must also eat and drink with trembling as a sign of the people’s fear. God refutes the proverb "The days drag on, and every vision comes to nothing," assuring that His word will be fulfilled soon, not in some distant future.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rebellious House (בֵּית מְרִי):** A description of Israel as having eyes but not seeing, ears but not hearing.
- **Exile Gear (כְּלֵי גוֹלָה):** Symbolic packing that foreshadows the coming deportation.
- **Covered Face:** A sign of the prince’s fate—he will not see the land to which he is taken (fulfilled in Zedekiah’s blinding).
- **The Proverb about Delayed Visions:** The people’s rejection of the prophecies, which God firmly refutes.

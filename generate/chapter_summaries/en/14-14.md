**Main Theme:** King Asa’s godly reforms and God’s victory over the Cushite army.

**Content:** King Asa did what was right in the Lord’s eyes by removing foreign altars, high places, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles, and he urged Judah to seek the Lord. During a time of peace, he built fortified cities and maintained a powerful army. When Zerah the Cushite attacked with a million men, Asa cried out to the Lord for help, and God struck down the enemy so that they fled. Judah pursued them as far as Gerar, plundered their towns, and took enormous spoils of war.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Reforms:** Asa removed idolatry and restored pure worship of the Lord in Judah.
- **Fortified Cities:** During a time of peace, Asa built defensive works because the Lord gave him rest.
- **Prayer in Distress:** Asa’s prayer expresses total dependence on God: "There is no difference for You whether You help the strong or the powerless."
- **God’s Victory:** The Lord Himself struck down the Cushites, and the fear of the Lord fell upon the enemy cities.

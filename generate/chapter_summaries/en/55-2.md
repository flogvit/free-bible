**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts Timothy to faithful service, endurance in suffering, and right handling of the word of truth.

**Content:** Timothy is urged to be strong in Christ's grace and entrust the gospel to reliable people who can teach others. Through images of a soldier, an athlete, and a farmer, the need for endurance and discipline is emphasized. Paul quotes a trustworthy confession: those who die with Christ will live; those who endure will reign—but Christ remains faithful even when we are unfaithful. Timothy must avoid wordy disputes and false teaching, cleanse himself to be a vessel for honor, and gently guide opponents toward repentance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Soldier, athlete, farmer (vv. 3–6):** Three images illustrating focused service, disciplined struggle, and hard work with reward.
- **Vessels for honor and dishonor (vv. 20–21):** In a large house there are various vessels; by cleansing oneself, one becomes a sanctified instrument fit for the Master's use.
- **The word of truth (v. 15):** Timothy is to rightly handle (ὀρθοτομοῦντα) the gospel like a worker who does not shame himself.
- **God's firm foundation (v. 19):** God's foundation stands firm with a double seal: The Lord knows those who are his, and everyone who names the name of the Lord must turn away from unrighteousness.

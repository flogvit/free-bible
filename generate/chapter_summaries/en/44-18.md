**Main Theme:** Paul establishes the church in Corinth and is strengthened by the Lord's promise, while Apollos receives deeper instruction in Ephesus.

**Content:** Paul arrives in Corinth and works as a tentmaker together with the married couple Aquila and Priscilla. He preaches in the synagogue but meets resistance from the Jews and turns to the Gentiles. The Lord encourages him through a vision and promises him protection, so Paul stays in the city for a year and a half. After appearing before the Roman proconsul Gallio without being condemned, Paul travels on via Ephesus to Antioch. In Ephesus, Apollos, a learned Jew from Alexandria, teaches, and Priscilla and Aquila give him a deeper understanding of God's way.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tentmakers (σκηνοποιοί):** Paul, Aquila, and Priscilla shared the same trade, which created a foundation for fellowship and cooperation in the mission.
- **Night Vision:** The Lord spoke to Paul in a vision and promised: "Do not be afraid! I have many people in this city" – this gave him courage to stay.
- **Gallio:** The Roman proconsul rejected the Jews' accusations as an internal religious dispute, which provided Paul with legal protection.
- **Apollos:** An eloquent and knowledgeable Jew from Alexandria who was instructed by Priscilla and Aquila and later strengthened the church in Achaia.

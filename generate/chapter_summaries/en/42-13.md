**Main Theme:** Jesus calls for repentance and warns of God’s judgment, while at the same time revealing the growth of God’s kingdom and his own care for the people.

**Content:** Starting from tragedies (the death of the Galileans and the fall of the tower in Siloam), Jesus urges everyone to repent in order to avoid destruction. The parable of the barren fig tree illustrates God’s patience, but also that time is limited. Jesus heals a woman whom Satan had bound for eighteen years and defends this act against criticism for breaking the Sabbath. He teaches about the hidden growth of God’s kingdom through the parables of the mustard seed and the yeast, and warns that many will be excluded from the kingdom. The chapter concludes with Jesus’ moving lament over Jerusalem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Narrow Door:** Symbolizes that salvation requires serious effort and repentance, not merely superficial acquaintance with Jesus.
- **The Fig Tree:** Represents Israel/humanity given time to bear fruit, but which must ultimately answer for its life.
- **Mustard Seed and Yeast:** Images of the invisible beginning of God’s kingdom that grows into something great and all-pervading.
- **The Hen and Her Chicks:** Expresses Jesus’ deep care and longing to gather his people, as well as his sorrow over their rejection.

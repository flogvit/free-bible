**Main Theme:** Practical wisdom about righteousness, humility, and trust in the Lord.

**Content:** The chapter opens by stating that a good name is more valuable than riches, and that the Lord has created both the poor and the rich. It offers exhortations on child-rearing, warnings against exploiting the poor, and cautions against associating with angry people. From verse 17 begins "The Words of the Wise," which encourages listening and placing one's trust in the Lord. The chapter concludes with practical advice concerning finances and work.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **A Good Name** (v. 1): Reputation and integrity are more valuable than material wealth.
- **Child-Rearing** (vv. 6, 15): Train up a child in the right way; discipline drives foolishness away.
- **The Words of the Wise** (v. 17): Introduces a collection of wise sayings to be kept in the heart.
- **Boundary Stone** (v. 28): A symbol of respect for tradition and others' property.
- **The Diligent One** (v. 29): The skilled worker will stand before kings, not before obscure people.

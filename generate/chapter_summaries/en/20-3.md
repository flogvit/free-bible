**Main Theme:** The chapter exhorts us to seek wisdom and trust in the Lord, with promises of blessing, security, and a good life.

**Content:** The chapter opens with exhortations to hold fast to God's instruction and to trust in the Lord with all one's heart rather than relying on one's own understanding (vv. 1-12). Wisdom is praised as more valuable than silver and gold, described as a tree of life for those who grasp it; by wisdom God founded the earth (vv. 13-20). Further practical advice is given to live securely, do good to one's neighbor, and avoid violence and injustice, with the promise that the Lord blesses the righteous (vv. 21-35).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Trust in the Lord (בְּטַח אֶל־יְהוָה):** Trusting God with all one's heart and not leaning on one's own understanding is the foundation of a right life.
- **Tree of Life (עֵץ־חַיִּים):** Wisdom is compared to the tree of life—it gives life and happiness to those who hold fast to it.
- **God's Discipline (מוּסַר יְהוָה):** The Lord's correction is an expression of love, like a father's care for his son.
- **Wisdom in Creation:** God founded the earth by wisdom and established the heavens by understanding, demonstrating the cosmic significance of wisdom.

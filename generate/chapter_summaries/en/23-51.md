**Main Theme:** God comforts his people with promises of eternal salvation and the restoration of Zion, urging them to trust in him rather than fear humans.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a reminder of Abraham and Sarah as the origin of the people, promising that Zion will be rebuilt into a garden like Eden. God declares that his righteousness and salvation endure forever, while heaven and earth shall pass away. The prophet calls upon the arm of the Lord and recalls his mighty deeds at the Exodus from Egypt. Jerusalem, which has drunk the cup of God's wrath, is promised that this cup will be taken from her and given to her oppressors.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Rock (צוּר):** Abraham as the root and origin of the people, a reminder of God's faithfulness.
- **The Arm of the Lord (זְרוֹעַ יְהוָה):** A symbol of God's creative power and saving strength, which parted the sea.
- **The Cup of Wrath (כּוֹס חֲמָתוֹ):** The image of God's judgment that Jerusalem has drained, but which will now be transferred to her enemies.
- **Zion as Eden:** The promise that devastated areas will become like the Lord's garden, filled with joy and praise.

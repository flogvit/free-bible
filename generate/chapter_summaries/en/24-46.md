**Main Theme:** God's judgment against Egypt and comfort for Israel.

**Content:** The chapter contains prophecies against Egypt, focusing on the defeat at Carchemish where Nebuchadnezzar defeated Pharaoh Neco's army (vv.1-12). God further warns of an invasion of Egypt and judgment upon the land's gods and Pharaoh (vv.13-26). The chapter concludes with a message of comfort to Jacob/Israel: God will deliver them from captivity and discipline them with measure, while bringing an end to the nations among whom they are scattered (vv.27-28).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Carchemish:** The place on the Euphrates where Babylon defeated Egypt in 605 B.C., a turning point in Middle Eastern history.
- **The Nile rising:** A picture of Egypt's ambition to flood and conquer the world, but which is stopped by God (vv.7-8).
- **The beautiful heifer:** Egypt is compared to a heifer that is attacked by a gadfly from the north—Babylon (v.20).
- **The Lord's day of vengeance:** The defeat is described as an offering to the Lord where the sword is filled with blood (v.10).
- **Jacob, do not fear:** God's promise of deliverance and safety for Israel, in contrast to the judgment upon the nations (vv.27-28).

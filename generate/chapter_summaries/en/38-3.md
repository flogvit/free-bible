**Main Theme:** The high priest Joshua’s cleansing and reinstatement as a sign of God’s forgiveness and coming salvation.

**Content:** Zechariah sees the high priest Joshua standing before the angel of the Lord while Satan accuses him. The Lord rebukes Satan and calls Joshua "a brand plucked from the fire." Joshua’s filthy garments are replaced with clean clothes and a pure turban, symbolizing that his guilt has been removed. The angel of the Lord gives Joshua a conditional promise to judge God’s house if he walks in the ways of the Lord. The chapter concludes with promises concerning "my servant the Branch" and that the land’s guilt will be removed in one day.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Filthy/Clean Garments:** Symbolize sin and forgiveness—Joshua’s cleansing represents the people’s spiritual restoration.
- **The Branch (צֶמַח):** A messianic title pointing to the coming Savior.
- **The Stone with Seven Eyes:** A mysterious symbol connected to God’s omniscience and His watchfulness over the rebuilding.
- **Under the Vine and Fig Tree:** An image of peace and prosperity in the messianic age.

**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself as the Savior who seeks the lost and as the rightful King coming to Jerusalem.

**Content:** The chapter begins with Jesus meeting the tax collector Zacchaeus in Jericho, who repents and hears that salvation has come to his house. Jesus tells the parable of the ten minas to teach about faithfulness and responsibility while waiting for God's kingdom. Then Jesus rides into Jerusalem on a young donkey as the crowd hails him as King. He weeps over Jerusalem and predicts the city's destruction, before cleansing the temple and beginning to teach there daily.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Zacchaeus in the tree:** A despised tax collector who seeks Jesus and experiences salvation – illustrating that the Son of Man came to seek and save the lost.
- **The ten minas:** A parable about managing what one has been entrusted with faithfully while waiting for the Lord's return.
- **The young donkey:** Jesus rides into Jerusalem as the peaceful King, fulfilling prophecies.
- **The cleansing of the temple:** Jesus asserts his authority over the temple and condemns it for having become a "den of robbers" instead of a house of prayer.

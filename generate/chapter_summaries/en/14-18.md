**Main Theme:** God's truth versus false prophecy, and the consequences of rejecting the Lord's word.

**Content:** King Jehoshaphat of Judah allies with King Ahab of Israel to attack Ramoth-Gilead. While 400 prophets promise victory, the prophet Micaiah warns of defeat and reveals a vision in which God permits a lying spirit to deceive Ahab's prophets. Ahab disguises himself in battle but is still struck by a random arrow and dies, while Jehoshaphat is saved when he cries out to the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ramoth-Gilead:** A disputed city east of the Jordan that triggers the fateful military alliance.
- **The 400 Prophets:** Represent false prophecy that tells the king what he wants to hear.
- **The Lying Spirit:** God's sovereign permission for Ahab to be deceived as judgment on his disobedience.
- **The Disguise:** Ahab's attempt to avoid fate fails, demonstrating the inevitability of God's word.

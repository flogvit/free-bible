**Main Theme:** Ezekiel sees the holy chambers of the temple and the measurement of the outer wall of the temple area, which separates the holy from the common.

**Content:** The prophet is led to chambers north and south of the temple building and receives detailed measurements for them. These chambers are sacred rooms where priests eat the most holy offerings (grain offering, sin offering, guilt offering) and change from their service garments into ordinary clothes before going out to the people. Then the entire outer wall of the temple area is measured: 500 measuring reeds on each side. The function of the wall is to separate the holy from the common.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Holy Chambers (lishkot hakodesh):** Special rooms reserved for the priests' meals and storage of sacred items.
- **Service Garments:** Priests must lay aside their holy garments in the chambers and put on other clothes before meeting with the people.
- **Measuring Reed (kaneh hamiddah):** The measuring instrument used to measure the outer boundaries of the temple area.
- **The Division Between Holy and Common:** The wall around the temple area marks the boundary between what is dedicated to God and the ordinary.

**Main Theme:** God shows Amos three visions of judgment against Israel, and the prophet is confronted by the priest Amaziah in Bethel.

**Content:** God shows Amos three visions: locusts, fire, and a plumb line. After the first two visions, Amos pleads for mercy, and God relents and spares the people. In the third vision, God declares that He will no longer spare Israel. The priest Amaziah orders Amos out of Bethel, but Amos defends his divine calling and pronounces judgment against Amaziah and his family.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Plumb line (אֲנָךְ):** A symbol of God's standard of justice, showing that Israel falls short.
- **God's relenting (נִחַם):** God changes His mind after Amos' intercession in the first two visions.
- **"Not a prophet, but a shepherd":** Amos' defense of his calling—he was not a professional prophet but was called directly by God.
- **Bethel:** The royal sanctuary where the conflict between the prophet and the religious establishment takes place.

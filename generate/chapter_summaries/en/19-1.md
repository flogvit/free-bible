# Psalm 1 – Summary

**Main Theme:** The psalm describes the contrast between the way and destiny of the righteous and that of the wicked.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a blessing on the one who keeps away from the influence of ungodly people—their counsel, their path, and their gathering places (v. 1). Instead, the righteous person finds delight in the law of the Lord and meditates on it day and night (v. 2). Such a person is compared to a fruitful tree planted by flowing water, which thrives and prospers in all they do (v. 3). The wicked, however, are like chaff that the wind blows away (v. 4). They will not stand in the judgment nor have a place among the righteous (v. 5). The psalm concludes with the statement that the Lord knows the way of the righteous, while the way of the wicked leads to destruction (v. 6).

**Key Words/Images:**

- **The tree by streams of water (v. 3):** A symbol of the stability, growth, and fruitfulness of the righteous through nourishment from God's word.

- **Chaff/dust (v. 4):** A symbol of the worthlessness and transience of the wicked—lacking substance or deep roots.

- **The two ways (vv. 1, 6):** A fundamental image of life's choice between righteousness and wickedness, with different outcomes.

- **Torah/law (v. 2):** The Lord's instruction as a source of life and blessing for the one who meditates on it.

**Main Theme:** God's laws for justice, rest, and worship—with the promise of guidance to the promised land.

**Content:** The chapter gives commands for fair judgment: do not spread false reports, do not follow a crowd to do wrong, do not accept bribes, and protect both the poor and the foreigner. It establishes the Sabbath year (every seventh year) and the Sabbath day for rest, as well as three annual pilgrimage festivals: the Festival of Unleavened Bread, the Harvest Festival, and the Festival of Ingathering. God promises to send an angel to lead Israel into Canaan and warns against making covenants with the people there or worshiping their gods.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fair Judgment (vv. 1–8):** Prohibitions against false testimony, bribes, and perverting justice—for both the poor and the rich.
- **Sabbath Year and Sabbath (vv. 10–12):** Every seventh year the land must rest, and every seventh day people and animals are to have rest.
- **The Three Festivals (vv. 14–17):** The Festival of Unleavened Bread, the Harvest Festival, and the Festival of Ingathering—all men must appear before the Lord.
- **The Angel Who Leads (vv. 20–23):** God sends His angel to protect and guide the people into the promised land; His name is in the angel.
- **Warning Against Idolatry (vv. 24, 32–33):** Israel must not worship the gods of Canaan or make covenants with the peoples there.

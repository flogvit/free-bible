**Main Theme:** Israel crosses the Jordan River miraculously under Joshua's leadership, with the ark of the covenant at the forefront.

**Content:** Joshua leads the people from Shittim to the Jordan, where they wait for three days. The people are instructed to consecrate themselves and follow the ark of the covenant at a distance of 2,000 cubits. God promises to exalt Joshua in the eyes of the people, just as He was with Moses. When the priests carrying the ark step into the overflowing Jordan, the waters stop and stand up like a heap, allowing the entire nation to cross on dry ground toward Jericho.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant (aron habberit):** God's visible presence going before the people and opening the way into the promised land.
- **Lord of all the earth (adon kol-ha'arets):** A title used for the God of the ark, demonstrating His universal sovereignty over all nations and lands.
- **Consecration:** The people must cleanse themselves before God performs miraculous acts among them.
- **The waters that stop:** A creation miracle showing God's authority over nature, recalling the crossing of the Red Sea.
- **Dry ground (charavah):** Symbolizing God's salvation and a safe path through impossible obstacles.

**Main Theme:** God’s judgment on King Ahaziah for idolatry and the Lord’s protection of His prophet Elijah.

**Content:** King Ahaziah of Israel injures himself in a fall and sends messengers to Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, to ask whether he will recover. God sends Elijah to meet the messengers with a message of judgment: because the king sought a foreign god instead of the God of Israel, he will die. The king sends two groups of soldiers to bring Elijah, but both are consumed by fire from heaven. The third captain humbles himself, and Elijah goes with him to the king and repeats the judgment. Ahaziah dies, and his brother Joram becomes king in his place.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Baal-zebub:** A Philistine god in Ekron whom Ahaziah consulted—an expression of Israel’s turning away from the Lord.
- **Fire from heaven:** God’s powerful protection of His prophet and judgment on those who despise His word.
- **The third captain:** His humility and plea for mercy show the way to avoid God’s judgment.
- **"Is there no God in Israel?":** The Lord’s rhetorical question that reveals the heart of Ahaziah’s sin—rejecting the living God.

**Main Theme:** Jesus encounters opposition from religious leaders and family, but establishes a new community through the selection of the twelve and the definition of his true kinship.

**Content:** Jesus heals a man with a withered hand on the Sabbath, and the Pharisees begin to plot with the Herodians how they might take his life. Great crowds from all over the region flock to him, and unclean spirits confess him as the Son of God. Jesus chooses twelve apostles and gives them authority to preach and drive out demons. He warns against blasphemy against the Holy Spirit and declares that his true family are those who do God's will.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Withered Hand:** Symbolizes the Sabbath conflict and Jesus' authority over religious traditions.
- **The Twelve:** The selection of the apostles marks the establishment of a new people of God.
- **The House of the Strong Man:** This image shows that Jesus binds Satan to free people from evil.
- **The Unforgivable Sin:** A warning against attributing the works of the Holy Spirit to Satan.
- **Jesus' True Family:** Those who do God's will are Jesus' brother, sister, and mother—an expanded spiritual kinship.

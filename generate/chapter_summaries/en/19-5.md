**Main Theme:** A morning prayer for God's guidance and protection against deceitful enemies.

**Content:** David calls upon God as his King and presents his case every morning. He contrasts the fate of the wicked—those who speak lies and practice treachery—with his own access to God's house through the Lord's mercy. The psalm concludes with a declaration of trust: God blesses the righteous and surrounds him with favor like a shield.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Morning (בֹּקֶר):** David arranges his prayer for God in the morning and waits for an answer.
- **Open Grave (קֶבֶר־פָּתוּחַ):** The enemies' throat is compared to an open grave—their words bring death.
- **Shield of Favor (כַּצִּנָּה רָצוֹן):** God crowns the righteous with His favor as a protective shield.

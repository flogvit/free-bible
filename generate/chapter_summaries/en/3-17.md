**Main Theme:** Sanctification of blood – regulations for sacrifices and the prohibition against eating blood.

**Content:** All slaughter offerings must be brought to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting to be sacrificed to the Lord, so that the Israelites no longer sacrifice to goat demons. The prohibition against eating blood is grounded in the fact that blood carries life and has been given by God for atonement on the altar. When hunting, the blood must be covered with earth. These laws apply to both Israelites and foreigners, and violation results in being cut off from the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Blood (הַדָּם):** Represents life and is designated for atonement on the altar; therefore, it is forbidden to eat.
- **Entrance of the Tent of Meeting:** All sacrifices must be brought here to ensure proper worship and prevent idolatry.
- **Atonement (לְכַפֵּר):** Blood covers sin and restores the relationship between humanity and God.
- **Goat demons (שְׂעִירִם):** Idols that the Israelites previously sacrificed to; centralized sacrifice prevents this practice.

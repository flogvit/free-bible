**Main Theme:** Paul defends his apostolic office and the divine origin of the gospel against false teachers who are disturbing the churches in Galatia.

**Content:** Paul opens by emphasizing that his call as an apostle comes directly from Jesus Christ and God the Father, not from men. He expresses astonishment that the Galatians are turning so quickly away from the gospel to a different message, and warns that anyone who preaches a different gospel is cursed. Paul recounts his past as a persecutor of the church, his conversion through divine revelation, and how he did not visit Jerusalem until three years later, meeting only Peter and James—all to demonstrate that his gospel does not originate from human sources.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Apostle (ἀπόστολος):** Paul's authority as a messenger comes directly from Christ and God, not from human appointment.
- **Gospel (εὐαγγέλιον):** The true message about Christ that Paul received through revelation, which cannot be altered.
- **Anathema (ἀνάθεμα):** A curse—the severe warning against preaching any other gospel.
- **Revelation (ἀποκάλυψις):** Paul received the gospel directly from Jesus Christ, not through human teaching.

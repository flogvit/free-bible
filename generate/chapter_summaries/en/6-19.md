**Main Theme:** The distribution of the land is completed as the last six tribes receive their inheritance through casting lots.

**Content:** The chapter describes the allocation of territories to Simeon (within Judah's territory, since their lot was too large), Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Dan. The tribe of Dan had to expand its area by conquering the city of Leshem, which they renamed Dan. Joshua himself received Timnath-Serah in the hill country of Ephraim as his inheritance. The distribution was completed at Shiloh at the entrance of the tent of meeting, led by the priest Eleazar and Joshua.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Casting lots (גּוֹרָל):** The method God used to distribute the land fairly among the tribes.
- **Simeon's inheritance within Judah:** Shows God's flexible plan where the tribes shared land according to need.
- **Dan's conquest of Leshem:** Illustrates that some tribes had to fight to secure their territory.
- **Timnath-Serah:** Joshua's own inheritance, given after the people were finished – an example of a leader's humility.
- **Shiloh:** The place where the casting of lots took place before the Lord, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

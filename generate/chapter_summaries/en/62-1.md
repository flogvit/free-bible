**Main Theme:** John testifies to the word of life and calls believers into fellowship with God by walking in the light and confessing their sins.

**Content:** John begins by testifying about what he himself has seen, heard, and touched—the word of life that was from the beginning and was made manifest. He proclaims this so that readers may have fellowship with him, with the Father, and with the Son, and so that their joy may be complete. The core message is that God is light, and in him there is no darkness at all; therefore, believers must walk in the light to have genuine fellowship. Those who confess their sins will experience forgiveness and cleansing through Jesus' blood.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Word of life (λόγος τῆς ζωῆς):** Jesus Christ as the eternal, revealed reality that the apostles witnessed.
- **Fellowship (κοινωνία):** The deep communion among believers, the Father, and the Son that arises from walking in the light.
- **Light and darkness:** God's nature is light; walking in darkness is incompatible with fellowship with him.
- **Confession and cleansing:** Acknowledging sin leads to forgiveness and cleansing through Jesus' blood, because God is faithful and just.

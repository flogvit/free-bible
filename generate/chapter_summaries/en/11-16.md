**Main Theme:** A series of kings in Israel turn away from God, and evil culminates with Ahab introducing the worship of Baal.

**Content:** The prophet Jehu son of Hanani proclaims judgment against Baasha and his house for following the sins of Jeroboam. After Baasha's death, a turbulent period of coups and power struggles follows: Zimri kills Elah, but takes his own life after seven days when Omri besieges Tirzah. Omri prevails and establishes Samaria as the new capital. His son Ahab becomes the worst of all kings—he marries Jezebel and introduces Baal worship in Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Way of Jeroboam:** The recurring sin of idolatry that all the kings of Israel follow.
- **Samaria:** The new capital built by Omri, which becomes the center of idol worship.
- **Baal and Asherah:** Canaanite gods whom Ahab officially establishes for worship in Israel.
- **Jezebel:** A Sidonian princess who brings Baal worship to Israel through her marriage to Ahab.

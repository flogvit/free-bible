**Main Theme:** The ministry and death of the two witnesses, their resurrection, and the seventh trumpet's proclamation of God’s eternal kingdom.

**Content:** John is commissioned to measure God’s temple, but the outer court is left to the nations who will trample the holy city for 42 months. Two witnesses prophesy for 1,260 days with power to perform signs, but the beast from the abyss kills them after they have completed their testimony. After three and a half days, God raises them up, and they ascend to heaven while an earthquake strikes the city. The seventh angel blows his trumpet, and it is proclaimed that the kingdom of the world belongs to the Lord and His Anointed One. God’s heavenly temple is opened, and the ark of the covenant becomes visible.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Two Witnesses:** God’s prophetic witnesses, described as two olive trees and two lampstands, who preach in sackcloth and ashes before being killed and raised to life.
- **The Beast from the Abyss:** The evil power that overcomes and kills the witnesses once their mission is completed.
- **The Seventh Trumpet:** Marks the proclamation of God’s eternal kingdom and initiates the worship of the 24 elders.
- **The Ark of the Covenant:** Revealed in the heavenly temple as a sign of God’s faithfulness and the fulfillment of His covenant.

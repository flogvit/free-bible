**Main Theme:** God's judgment against the nations surrounding Israel—Ammon, Edom, Damascus, Kedar, Hazor, and Elam—with promises of future restoration for some.

**Content:** The chapter contains six oracles of judgment against nations that have sinned against God and His people. Ammon is accused of seizing the land of Gad, while Edom is criticized for its pride and false sense of security. Damascus, Kedar, and Hazor will be struck by Nebuchadnezzar's army, and Elam will have its bow—their strength—shattered. Amidst the judgment, hope is given: God promises to restore the fortunes of both Ammon (v.6) and Elam (v.39).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cup of Wrath** (v.12): A picture of God's judgment that the nations must drink—none can escape.
- **Eagle** (vv.16, 22): Symbolizes both Edom's pride (nesting on high) and the enemy who attacks.
- **Lion from the Jordan** (v.19): A picture of God driving out Edom's inhabitants from their land.
- **Restoration** (vv.6, 39): Hebrew "שְׁבוּת" – a promise that God will turn their fortunes in the future.

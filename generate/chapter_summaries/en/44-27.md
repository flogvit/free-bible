**Main Theme:** Paul’s perilous sea voyage to Rome demonstrates God’s faithfulness and protection amid disaster.

**Content:** Paul is sent as a prisoner to Rome under the command of the officer Julius. Despite Paul’s warnings, they continue their journey late in the autumn, and the ship is caught by the violent storm Euroclydon. After two weeks without hope, Paul receives an angelic message: all 276 on board will survive. The ship runs aground and breaks apart, but everyone reaches land safely—exactly as God had promised.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Storm Euroclydon:** The fierce northeasterly wind that threatened the ship and symbolizes life’s unpredictable dangers.
- **The Angel’s Message:** God’s promise to Paul that no one would perish, serving as a foundation for hope in the midst of crisis.
- **The Bread and Thanksgiving:** Paul breaks bread and gives thanks before everyone, a testimony of faith that encourages others.
- **God’s Saving Power:** All 276 people survive the shipwreck, a concrete fulfillment of God’s promise.

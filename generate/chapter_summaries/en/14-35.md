**Main Theme:** King Josiah's great Passover celebration and his tragic death in battle against the pharaoh of Egypt.

**Content:** In his eighteenth year as king, Josiah organized a comprehensive Passover celebration in Jerusalem, where priests and Levites served in their divisions according to the regulations established by David and Solomon. The king and the leaders provided abundant sacrificial animals, and the Passover was celebrated in a manner unmatched since the days of the prophet Samuel. After this, Pharaoh Neco of Egypt marched toward Carchemish. Although Neco warned Josiah with words from God not to interfere, Josiah disguised himself and went into battle at Megiddo, where he was mortally wounded.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Passover Celebration:** The greatest Passover since the time of Samuel, a high point in Josiah's religious reforms.
- **Priests and Levites:** Organized service according to the regulations of David and Solomon demonstrates the restored temple order.
- **Megiddo:** The place where Josiah was mortally wounded, a turning point in Judah's history.
- **Jeremiah's Lamentations:** The prophet Jeremiah sang laments over Josiah, which became a lasting tradition in Israel.

**Main Theme:** Joseph reveals his identity to his brothers and invites the family to settle in Egypt.

**Content:** Joseph can no longer hold back his emotions and tearfully reveals his identity to his brothers, who are stunned. He forgives them and explains that God sent him to Egypt to preserve life during the famine. Pharaoh is pleased with the news and invites the entire family to settle in Goshen. The brothers return to Canaan with gifts and wagons, and although Jacob initially does not believe them, his spirit is revived when he sees the evidence.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Joseph's weeping:** Expresses deep emotional release after years of separation and secrecy.
- **God's providence:** Joseph interprets his suffering as part of God's plan to save many lives (vv. 5–8).
- **Goshen:** The region in Egypt where the family will live, near Joseph and with good grazing conditions.
- **Jacob's spirit revived:** Symbolizes the transition from grief and unbelief to hope and joy.

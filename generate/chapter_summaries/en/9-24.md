**Main Theme:** David Spares Saul’s Life at En-Gedi and Shows Reverence for the Lord’s Anointed.

**Content:** While Saul hunts David with 3,000 soldiers, David seeks refuge in a cave where he and his men are hiding. David cuts off a corner of Saul’s robe but immediately regrets it because Saul is the Lord’s anointed. Later, David confronts Saul and shows him the piece of robe as proof of his innocence. Saul acknowledges that David is more righteous than himself and prophesies that David will become king. David swears not to destroy Saul’s descendants.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord’s Anointed (מְשִׁיחַ יְהוָה):** David’s theological reason for not harming Saul—the king is God’s chosen one.
- **The Piece of Robe:** Physical evidence of David’s opportunity and self-control, as well as a symbol of the kingdom.
- **The Cave at En-Gedi:** The place where roles are reversed—the hunter becomes vulnerable, and the prey shows mercy.
- **Oath:** David swears to preserve Saul’s lineage—a covenant that later proves significant during David’s reign.

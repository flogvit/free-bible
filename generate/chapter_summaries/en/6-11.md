**Main Theme:** Joshua completes the conquest of northern Canaan with God's help and divides the land among the tribes.

**Content:** King Jabin of Hazor gathers a large coalition of northern kings against Israel, but the Lord promises Joshua victory. Joshua surprises the enemy at the waters of Merom and defeats them completely. He captures Hazor and all the other cities, devotes them to destruction as the Lord had commanded Moses, and wipes out the Anakim from the mountains. The land has rest from war, and Joshua distributes it as an inheritance to the tribes of Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hazor:** The most powerful city in the north, the center of the coalition, which Joshua burns down.
- **Waters of Merom:** The place where Joshua launches a surprise attack and defeats the great enemy army.
- **Devotion to destruction (herem):** Total destruction of the enemy as a holy act of obedience to God's command.
- **Anakim:** The giant people who were wiped out from the mountains, with only a few remaining in Philistine cities.
- **Rest from war:** The result of the conquest—the land gains peace and is distributed as an inheritance.

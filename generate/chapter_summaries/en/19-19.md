**Main Theme:** God's glory is revealed both through creation and His perfect law.

**Content:** The psalm begins with how the heavens and the sky testify to God's honor without words, comparing the sun to a bridegroom and a mighty warrior. Then God's law is praised as perfect, reliable, more valuable than gold, and sweeter than honey. The psalm concludes with a prayer for cleansing from hidden sins and protection against arrogant actions, along with a commitment to the Lord as rock and redeemer.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The heavens declare** – Creation testifies wordlessly of God's greatness to all the world.
- **Torah (God's law)** – Described as perfect; it gives wisdom and gladdens the heart.
- **The sun as bridegroom** – Symbolizes strength and beauty; nothing is hidden from its warmth.
- **Gold and honey** – God's word is more valuable and sweeter than earthly riches.
- **Rock and redeemer** – Final declaration of trust in God as a firm foundation and Savior.

**Main Theme:** The beginning of the gospel about Jesus Christ, the Son of God, and the start of his public ministry.

**Content:** John the Baptist prepares the way in the wilderness and baptizes Jesus in the Jordan, where God confirms Jesus as his Son through the descent of the Spirit. After 40 days in the wilderness, Jesus begins his ministry in Galilee with the message that the kingdom of God is near, and he calls the first disciples. He demonstrates his authority through teaching and by healing the sick and casting out evil spirits, including a man with leprosy. Jesus prioritizes prayer and emphasizes that his mission is to preach in many towns.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gospel (εὐαγγέλιον):** The good news about Jesus Christ that inaugurates a new era.
- **Repentance (μετάνοια):** The call to a change of mind and faith as a response to the nearness of God's kingdom.
- **Kingdom of God (βασιλεία τοῦ θεοῦ):** God's reign that has drawn near through Jesus.
- **Authority (ἐξουσία):** Jesus' unique authority in teaching and over evil spirits.
- **Immediately (εὐθύς):** Mark's characteristic word that underscores the urgency and immediacy of Jesus' ministry.

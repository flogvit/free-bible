**Main Theme:** Job defends his own wisdom and describes God's sovereign power over all creation.

**Content:** Job answers his friends with irony, claiming that he does not fall short of them in insight. He complains about being made a laughingstock, even though he is righteous. Job urges his friends to ask the animals, the birds, and the fish – all know that the Lord's hand has created these things. Then he describes God's absolute power: He tears down and builds up again, humbles counselors, kings, and priests, and leads nations into darkness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Animals as Witnesses (vv.7–9):** All creation testifies that God's hand is behind everything.
- **God's Sovereign Power (vv.14–16):** God tears down what cannot be rebuilt again, and holds back the waters or releases them.
- **Leaders in Darkness (vv.17–21):** God leads counselors, judges, kings, and priests away like captives and takes understanding from the elders.
- **Nations Rise and Fall (v.23):** God makes nations great and destroys them again according to His will.

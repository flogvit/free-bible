**Main Theme:** The Danites conquer Laish and establish their own cult with stolen religious objects.

**Content:** In a time when there was no king in Israel, the tribe of Dan seeks new territory. Five scouts discover the peaceful city of Laish and meet a Levitical priest at Micah's house. Later, 600 armed Danites set out for Laish, steal Micah's religious objects, and persuade the priest to join them. They conquer and burn Laish, rebuild the city as Dan, and establish their own sanctuary there, where Jonathan, son of Gershom, becomes the priest.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Laish:** A peaceful, isolated city that falls victim to the Danites' conquest because it lacked allies and protection.
- **Pesel and ephod:** Religious objects stolen from Micah, which the Danites use to establish their own unauthorized worship.
- **No king in Israel:** The framing statement that explains the moral and religious disorder of this period.
- **Jonathan, son of Gershom:** The priest who leaves Micah for better career opportunities and becomes the ancestor of a priestly line in Dan.

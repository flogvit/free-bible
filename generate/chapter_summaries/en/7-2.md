**Main Theme:** Israel's repeated apostasy from God and the introduction of the cycle of judges as a pattern for the rest of the book.

**Content:** The angel of the Lord rebukes Israel at Bochim for breaking the covenant by failing to tear down the altars of the Canaanites. After Joshua's death and the passing of the generation that had witnessed God's mighty works, a new generation arose who did not know the Lord and worshiped Baal and Ashtoreth. So God allowed enemies to plunder them, but raised up judges to deliver them—yet each time a judge died, the people returned to idolatry. God decided to leave the remaining nations in the land to test Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bochim ("the weeping ones"):** The place where the angel of the Lord spoke and the people wept over their disobedience.
- **The Cycle of Judges:** The repeated pattern of sin, punishment, crying out for help, and deliverance through a judge.
- **Covenant Breaking:** Israel broke the covenant by making agreements with the Canaanites and failing to destroy their altars.
- **Baal and Ashtoreth:** Canaanite gods whom Israel turned to instead of the Lord.

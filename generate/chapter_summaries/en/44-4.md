**Main Theme:** Peter and John’s bold witness before the Council, and the first congregation’s prayer and fellowship.

**Content:** After healing the lame man, Peter and John are arrested by the temple leaders. Filled with the Holy Spirit, Peter testifies to the Council that salvation is found in Jesus’ name alone. The Council forbids them to speak in Jesus’ name, but the apostles reply that they must obey God rather than men. The congregation gathers for prayer; the place where they are meeting is shaken, and all are filled with the Spirit. Believers live in deep unity, sharing everything they own, so that no one is in need.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Cornerstone (v.11):** Jesus is the stone rejected by the builders who has become the chief cornerstone – rejected by the leaders, yet the foundation of God’s salvation.
- **Salvation in Jesus’ Name (v.12):** There is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved – a central Christian teaching.
- **Boldness/παρρησία (vv.13, 29, 31):** The apostles speak with courage despite threats, empowered by the Holy Spirit.
- **One Heart and One Soul (v.32):** The deep fellowship of the congregation, where all shared everything and no one was in need.
- **Barnabas (vv.36–37):** Introduced as "son of encouragement," an example of generosity who sold his field.

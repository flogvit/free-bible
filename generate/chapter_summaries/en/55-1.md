**Main Theme:** Paul encourages Timothy to hold fast to the faith and not be ashamed of the gospel or of Paul's imprisonment.

**Content:** Paul writes to his beloved spiritual child, Timothy, reminding him of the sincere faith he inherited from his grandmother Lois and his mother Eunice. He urges Timothy to fan into flame the gift of grace he received through the laying on of hands, for God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control. Paul testifies to his own unwavering trust in Christ amid suffering and asks Timothy to guard the good deposit entrusted to him by the Holy Spirit. He also mentions Onesiphorus's faithful service as a contrast to those who deserted him in Asia.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gift of grace (χάρισμα):** The spiritual gift Timothy received through Paul's laying on of hands, which must be fanned into flame.
- **Power, love, and self-control:** The three characteristics of God's Spirit that replace fear in ministry.
- **The good deposit (παραθήκη):** The true teaching entrusted to Timothy, which must be guarded by the Holy Spirit.
- **Be ashamed (ἐπαισχύνομαι):** A key concept in this chapter—Timothy must not be ashamed of the Lord or of Paul as a prisoner.

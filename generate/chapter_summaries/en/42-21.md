**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about the signs of the end times, the fall of Jerusalem, and the need for vigilance while waiting for the coming of the Son of Man.

**Content:** Jesus commends a poor widow who gives all she has to the temple, in contrast to the rich who give from their abundance. He prophesies the destruction of the temple and describes signs that will come: false messiahs, wars, natural disasters, persecution, and the siege of Jerusalem. The chapter culminates with the promise of the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory, and Jesus urges his disciples to be watchful and pray so that they may stand on that day.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Widow's Offering (vv. 1–4):** The poor widow's two small coins illustrate that God values the devotion of the heart over the size of the gift.
- **Destruction of the Temple (vv. 5–6):** Jesus' prophecy that not one stone will be left upon another, fulfilled in AD 70.
- **Times of the Gentiles (v. 24):** The period during which Jerusalem will be trampled by Gentile nations until their time is completed.
- **The Son of Man (v. 27):** Jesus describes his own return in a cloud with power and great glory.
- **The Fig Tree (vv. 29–31):** A parable teaching disciples to recognize the signs of the times, just as budding trees signal the coming of summer.

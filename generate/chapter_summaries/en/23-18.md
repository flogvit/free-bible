**Main Theme:** A prophecy about Cush (Nubia) in which God calmly observes before intervening, ending with the people bringing gifts to Zion.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a call to the land of whirring wings beyond the rivers of Cush, a nation known for its height and strength. God declares that He will remain silent and observe from His dwelling place, like the heat of the sun or a cloud of dew in the harvest season. Before the harvest is completed, God will cut off the vines—a picture of judgment against the plans of a great power. Finally, this mighty nation will bring gifts to the Lord, the God of hosts, on Mount Zion.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The land of whirring wings:** A description of Cush, possibly referring to insects or boats along the Nile.
- **God's silence:** God waits patiently and observes from His dwelling place before acting (v.4).
- **Harvest metaphor:** God will cut off the vines before the grapes ripen—a picture of judgment coming before the enemy reaches its goal (v.5).
- **Gifts to Zion:** A future time when the people of Cush acknowledge the Lord and bring tribute to His holy mountain (v.7).

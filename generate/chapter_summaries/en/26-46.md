**Main Theme:** Regulations for worship, offerings, and property in the new temple.

**Content:** The chapter provides rules for the prince's worship on the Sabbath and at the New Moon, including which offerings he must bring and how he is to enter and leave the temple. A daily morning offering of a one-year-old lamb is established. The people are to go out through the gate opposite the one they entered by. The prince may give an inheritance to his sons permanently, but gifts given to servants return in the Year of Jubilee. The chapter concludes with a description of the kitchens where priests and temple servants will prepare sacrificial meat.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **East Gate:** The east gate of the inner court is to be closed on workdays, but open on the Sabbath and at the New Moon.
- **The Prince (נָשִׂיא):** A leader with special privileges and responsibilities in temple worship.
- **The Daily Offering (עוֹלַת תָּמִיד):** A one-year-old lamb is to be offered every morning as an eternal ritual.
- **The Year of Jubilee (שְׁנַת הַדְּרוֹר):** The year when property given to servants returns to the prince.
- **The Kitchens:** Places in the court where priests prepare guilt offerings, sin offerings, and grain offerings.

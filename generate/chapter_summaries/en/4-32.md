**Main Theme:** Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh receive land east of the Jordan on condition that they fight alongside Israel.

**Content:** The tribes of Reuben and Gad ask to settle in the fertile land east of the Jordan because of their large herds. Moses sternly warns them, comparing them to the spies who led Israel into forty years of wandering in the wilderness. The tribes promise to go armed at the front of Israel's army during the conquest of Canaan, while their families remain in fortified cities. Moses accepts the agreement and gives them – together with the half-tribe of Manasseh – the kingdoms of Sihon and Og.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Miqneh (מִקְנֶה, livestock):** The tribes' great wealth in cattle is the reason for their request for grazing land.
- **Kadesh-Barnea (קָדֵשׁ בַּרְנֵעַ):** Moses refers to the spies' rebellion as a warning against disobedience.
- **Chaluts (חָלוּץ, armed vanguard):** The tribes promise to lead Israel's army until the conquest is complete.
- **Fortified cities (עָרֵי הַמִּבְצָר):** The tribes will build secure cities for women and children while the men fight.

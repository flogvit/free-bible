**Main Theme:** The Fall of Jerusalem and God’s Protection of His Faithful Servants.

**Content:** After a long siege, the Babylonians break through Jerusalem's walls. King Zedekiah flees but is captured; he is forced to watch his sons be killed, then blinded and taken to Babylon. The city is burned, and the people are deported, while the poorest are allowed to remain with vineyards and fields. Jeremiah is protected by Nebuchadnezzar’s order and placed under Gedaliah’s care. God also sends a promise of deliverance to Ebed-Melech because he trusted in the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Fall of the City (vv.1–3):** After a year-and-a-half-long siege, the walls are breached, and Babylonian leaders enter through the city gate—symbolizing that Judah has lost its independence.
- **Zedekiah’s Fate (vv.4–7):** The king’s flight, capture, and blinding fulfill prophecies and demonstrate the consequences of rejecting God’s word.
- **Nebuzaradan (vv.9–14):** The Babylonian commander carries out both judgment (deportation) and mercy (toward the poor and Jeremiah).
- **Ebed-Melech (vv.15–18):** God’s promise of deliverance to the Ethiopian who trusted in Him shows that faith leads to salvation even amid judgment.

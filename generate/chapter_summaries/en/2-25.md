**Main Theme:** God gives instructions regarding voluntary offerings and the construction of the sanctuary where He will dwell among His people.

**Content:** God commands Moses to collect gifts from everyone who has a willing heart—gold, silver, textiles, skins, and other materials. The central instruction is: "Make a sanctuary for me, so that I may dwell in their midst." This is followed by detailed specifications for the Ark of the Covenant with its mercy seat and cherubim, the table for the bread of the Presence, and the seven-branched lampstand—all made of pure gold.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Terumah (תְּרוּמָה):** A voluntary offering given from a heart motivated by love for God.
- **Ark of the Covenant:** An acacia wood chest overlaid with gold, intended to hold the testimony (the tablets of the law).
- **Mercy Seat (kapporet):** A lid made of pure gold with two cherubim, the place where God would meet and speak with Moses.
- **Menorah:** A seven-branched lampstand hammered from a single piece of pure gold, symbolizing God's light.

**Main Theme:** Daniel’s confession of sin and Gabriel’s revelation concerning the seventy weeks.

**Content:** In the first year of Darius’ reign, Daniel understands from Jeremiah’s prophecy that Jerusalem’s desolation will last for seventy years. He turns to God in prayer with fasting, confesses Israel’s sins, and asks for forgiveness and the restoration of Jerusalem and the temple. While he is praying, the angel Gabriel comes and reveals the prophecy of the seventy weeks, which are decreed to put an end to sin, make atonement for guilt, and bring in everlasting righteousness. The prophecy also describes that a Messiah will be cut off and that the city and the sanctuary will be destroyed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Seventy Weeks (שָׁבֻעִים שִׁבְעִים):** A period of 490 years decreed for God’s plan of salvation and Jerusalem’s future.
- **Confession of Sin:** Daniel’s humble prayer in which he acknowledges the people’s rebellion and asks for God’s mercy.
- **Messiah/Anointed One (מָשִׁיחַ):** A future ruler who will come, but who will also be cut off.
- **Gabriel:** The angel who gives Daniel insight and understanding of God’s prophetic plan.

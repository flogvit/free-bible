**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts the lives of the righteous and the wicked through a collection of independent proverbs.

**Content:** Proverbs 10 marks the beginning of Solomon's collection of proverbs and consists of antithetic parallelisms that set wisdom against folly, righteousness against godlessness. The power of speech is a recurring theme—the mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life, while the mouth of the wicked conceals violence. Diligence is praised over laziness, and the Lord's blessing is presented as the source of lasting wealth. Love covers transgressions, while hatred stirs up strife.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Righteous/Wicked (צַדִּיק/רָשָׁע):** The fundamental contrast that structures the entire chapter—the righteous stand as an eternal foundation, while the wicked perish like a storm.
- **Mouth/Lips/Tongue:** The speech of the righteous is like refined silver and a fountain of life; the lips of the fool lead to destruction.
- **The Lord's Blessing (בִּרְכַּת יְהוָה):** The source of wealth without toil accompanying it; reverence for God prolongs life.
- **Diligence and Laziness:** The diligent hand brings wealth; the lazy person is like vinegar to the teeth and smoke to the eyes.

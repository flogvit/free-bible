**Main Theme:** The chapter provides regulations for temple service, stating that only the sons of Zadok may serve at the altar because they remained faithful.

**Content:** Ezekiel is brought to the east gate, which must remain closed because the Lord has entered through it—only the prince may sit there to eat bread before the Lord. The Levites who turned away and followed idols are demoted to gatekeepers and slaughterers, while the sons of Zadok are permitted to approach God as priests. Detailed rules are given concerning the priests' garments, marriage, purity, and teaching duties. The priests shall have no earthly inheritance, for the Lord himself is their inheritance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Closed East Gate:** Symbolizes the Lord's holy entrance and his permanent presence in the temple.
- **The Sons of Zadok:** Represent faithful priestly service and are granted exclusive rights to the altar as a reward for their loyalty.
- **Linen Garments:** Priests must wear linen, not wool, to avoid sweating—symbolizing purity in service.
- **"I am your inheritance":** God himself is the priests' allotted portion; they shall own no land in Israel.

**Main Theme:** God will protect Jerusalem against the attacks of the nations and pour out a spirit of grace that leads to deep mourning and repentance.

**Content:** The Lord declares that he will make Jerusalem a cup that causes staggering and a heavy stone for all the nations who attack the city. On that day, God will strike the horses of the enemies with confusion and blindness, while protecting Judah and Jerusalem—even the weakest among them will be like David. God will seek to destroy all the nations that come against Jerusalem. Finally, the Lord will pour out a spirit of grace and prayer upon the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and they will look on him whom they have pierced and mourn deeply, with mourning like that at Hadad-Rimmon.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cup of staggering and heavy stone:** Jerusalem becomes a trap that causes enemies to stumble and harm themselves.
- **House of David:** The royal dynasty that will share in God's protection and the spirit of grace.
- **The one pierced:** A mysterious figure whom the people look upon and mourn with deep repentance.
- **Spirit of grace and prayer:** God's Spirit that creates repentance and intense sorrow among the people.

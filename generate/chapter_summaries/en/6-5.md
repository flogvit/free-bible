**Main Theme:** Israel prepares spiritually and physically to enter Canaan through circumcision, the celebration of Passover, and a divine encounter.

**Content:** The kings of Canaan lose courage when they hear about Israel's miraculous crossing of the Jordan. Joshua circumcises the new generation born in the wilderness, and God declares that He has "rolled away" the shame of Egypt from the people—therefore the place is called Gilgal. The Israelites celebrate Passover and eat from the produce of the land, and the manna ceases. Joshua meets the commander of the Lord's army, who commands him to remove his sandals because the place is holy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Circumcision:** The covenant sign marking the consecration of the new generation before entering the Promised Land.
- **Gilgal:** The name means "to roll away"—God removed the shame of Egypt from the people.
- **Passover:** The first Passover celebration in the Promised Land, marking a new era.
- **Commander of the Lord's army:** A heavenly figure confirming God's presence in the coming battle.

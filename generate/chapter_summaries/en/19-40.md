**Main Theme:** A psalm of thanksgiving that combines praise for God’s salvation with a plea for new deliverance.

**Content:** The psalmist testifies to how the Lord heard his cry, lifted him out of the pit of destruction, set his feet on solid ground, and gave him a new song of praise. He proclaims that God does not desire sacrifices, but obedience and a heart that has God’s law within it. The psalmist has proclaimed God’s righteousness and faithfulness in the great assembly. Now he is once again surrounded by troubles and sins, and he earnestly prays that God will hasten to his aid.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The pit of destruction and the mire (v.3):** Symbolize life-threatening distress and hopelessness from which God rescues.
- **A new song (v.4):** God’s salvation creates a fresh song of praise that also becomes a testimony to others.
- **Ears opened (v.7):** God desires attentiveness and obedience rather than external sacrifices.
- **The scroll (vv.8–9):** The psalmist is called to do God’s will as written, with the law within his heart.
- **Poor and afflicted (v.18):** The psalmist’s self-description expressing dependence on God.

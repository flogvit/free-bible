**Main Theme:** A lament expressing deep despair and the experience of being abandoned by God, without resolution or hope.

**Content:** The psalm was written by Heman the Ezrahite and belongs to the collection of the sons of Korah. The psalmist cries out to God day and night from a state near death, yet experiences that God hides His face. He describes how God's wrath rests upon him and how friends and acquaintances have withdrawn. The psalm ends in darkness—without the deliverance that otherwise characterizes laments.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sheol/the pit (שְׁאוֹל, בּוֹר):** The realm of the dead as a picture of the psalmist's desperate condition and nearness to death.
- **Darkness (מַחֲשַׁכִּים):** Symbolizes both physical and spiritual isolation from God and people.
- **God's wrath (חֲמָתֶךָ):** The psalmist experiences his suffering as an expression of God's anger toward him.
- **Isolation:** Friends and acquaintances have been removed; the psalmist is alone with his pain.

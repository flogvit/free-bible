**Main Theme:** The Lord's suffering servant bears the people's sin and guilt, and through his substitutionary suffering comes healing and justification.

**Content:** The chapter describes a servant who grows up unattractive and is despised and rejected by men. He bears our sicknesses and pains, is pierced for our transgressions, and crushed for our iniquity. Like a lamb he is led to slaughter without opening his mouth. Despite innocent suffering and death, he will see offspring, prolong his days, and justify many.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lamb Led to Slaughter** – The servant suffers silently and willingly, like a lamb being led away (v.7)
- **Wounds That Heal** – By his wounds we receive healing; the suffering brings restoration to others (v.5)
- **Straying Sheep** – The people have gone astray like sheep, but the Lord laid on him the punishment for us all (v.6)
- **Guilt Offering (asham)** – The servant's life is given as a guilt offering for sin (v.10)

**Main Theme:** Paul concludes the letter with guidance on Christian relationships, spiritual warfare, and prayer.

**Content:** The chapter begins with exhortations to children and parents, as well as slaves and masters, regarding mutual respect in the Lord. Then believers are urged to put on the full armor of God to stand against the devil's cunning attacks and the spiritual forces of evil. Paul emphasizes the importance of persistent prayer in the Spirit and asks for intercession for his own preaching. Tychicus is sent as a messenger to bring news and encourage the church, before the letter closes with a blessing of peace, love, and grace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The full armor of God (πανοπλία τοῦ θεοῦ):** A military metaphor for spiritual equipment that protects the believer in the struggle against evil powers.
- **The devil's cunning attacks (μεθοδείαι τοῦ διαβόλου):** Refers to the enemy's strategic and deceptive methods.
- **The sword of the Spirit (μάχαιρα τοῦ πνεύματος):** God's word as the only offensive weapon in the spiritual armor.
- **Prayer in the Spirit:** The continuous spiritual communication that supports all spiritual warfare.

**Main Theme:** A thanksgiving psalm in which Israel praises the Lord for deliverance from overwhelming enemies.

**Content:** David urges Israel to confess that without the Lord's help, they would have been swallowed up by their enemies when people rose against them. Floodwaters and raging streams would have drowned them. But the Lord did not give them as prey, and they escaped like a bird from the fowler's snare. Israel's help is in the name of the Lord, the Maker of heaven and earth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Swallowed alive (v.3):** The enemies are compared to predators that would have devoured the people in their wrath.
- **The floodwaters (vv.4–5):** The wild, proud waters symbolize overwhelming danger and chaos that threatened to drown them.
- **The bird and the snare (v.7):** The people are like a bird that has escaped from the fowler's trap—the snare is broken, and they are free.
- **Maker of heaven and earth (v.8):** A confession that help comes from the Almighty God who created all things.

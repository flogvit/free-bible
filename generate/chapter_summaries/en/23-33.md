**Main Theme:** God will judge the oppressors and grant salvation and security to the righteous who dwell in Zion.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a woe oracle against the destroyer (Assyria), who himself shall be struck by destruction. In the midst of crisis, the people cry out to the Lord for mercy, and God responds by rising up to execute judgment. Those who walk righteously, reject injustice, and refuse bribes will dwell securely on the heights. The chapter culminates in a vision of Jerusalem as a peaceful home where the Lord himself is judge, lawgiver, and king—and where the people's sin is forgiven.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Destroyer (v.1):** The Assyrians who have plundered others but will themselves be plundered when their time comes to an end.
- **The Lord as Judge, Lawgiver, and King (v.22):** God's threefold sovereign role that guarantees salvation for his people.
- **The Righteous One (vv.15–16):** The one who walks uprightly, speaks truthfully, and rejects injustice—he will dwell securely.
- **Zion as a Peaceful Dwelling (v.20):** Jerusalem is portrayed as an unshakable tent, a place of lasting security.
- **Devouring Fire (v.14):** God's holiness that instills fear in sinners but provides safety for the righteous.

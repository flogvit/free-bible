**Main Theme:** The Lamb who was slain is alone worthy to open the sealed scroll and receives worship from all creation.

**Content:** John sees a scroll with seven seals in God's right hand, and no one in all creation is found worthy to open it. One of the elders reveals that the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has triumphed. John sees a Lamb looking as if it had been slain, which takes the scroll and receives worship. The four living creatures and the twenty-four elders sing a new song about the worthiness of the Lamb, because with its blood it purchased people from every nation and made them a kingdom and priests. Finally, countless angels and all creation unite in praising the One seated on the throne and the Lamb.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The scroll with seven seals:** Represents God's hidden plan and salvation history that only the Lamb can reveal.
- **The Lamb who was slain:** Christ as the sacrificed Passover lamb, with seven horns (perfect power) and seven eyes (the seven spirits of God/perfect insight).
- **The Lion of Judah/Root of David:** Messianic titles connecting Jesus to the promises given to the tribe of Judah and the royal line of David.
- **The new song:** Celebration of the Lamb's work of salvation that has created a new people from all nations.
- **Universal worship:** All creation—in heaven, on earth, and under the earth—unites in praising God and the Lamb.

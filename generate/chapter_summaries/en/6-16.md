**Main Theme:** The chapter describes the land allocation to Joseph's descendants, with a focus on the territory of the tribe of Ephraim.

**Content:** The inheritance for Joseph's sons extended from the Jordan River near Jericho in the east to the Mediterranean Sea in the west. The borders of Ephraim are described in detail, including cities such as Ataroth-Addar, Upper and Lower Beth-Horon, and Gezer. Ephraim also received cities within Manasseh's territory. The Canaanites in Gezer were not driven out but remained there as forced laborers.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Inheritance (גּוֹרָל):** The land distribution was determined by casting lots, demonstrating God's will in the allocation.
- **Joseph's sons:** Ephraim and Manasseh inherited as two separate tribes, according to Jacob's blessing.
- **Forced labor (מַס־עֹבֵד):** The Canaanites were not destroyed but subjugated as a labor force—a sign of incomplete obedience.

**Main Theme:** John receives a revelation from Jesus Christ on the island of Patmos, with a message for the seven churches in Asia Minor.

**Content:** The book opens with a blessing to those who read and hold fast to the words of prophecy. John, who is on Patmos because of his faith, has an overwhelming vision of the risen Christ walking among seven golden lampstands. Christ identifies himself as the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, and commissions John to write what he sees and send it to the seven churches.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Seven Lampstands:** Represent the seven churches in Asia Minor that are to receive the message.
- **The Seven Stars:** Symbolize the angels (or messengers) of the seven churches.
- **Alpha and Omega:** Christ as the beginning and the end, the eternal God.
- **Son of Man:** The glorified Christ with white hair, eyes like blazing fire, and a voice like the sound of many waters.

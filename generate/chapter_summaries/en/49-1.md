**Main Theme:** Paul praises God for His eternal plan of salvation in Christ and prays that the church may receive spiritual insight.

**Content:** Paul greets the church in Ephesus and praises God for every spiritual blessing in Christ. He explains how, before the foundation of the world, God chose and predestined us to be adopted as His children, and how we have redemption and forgiveness of sins through Christ's blood. God's plan is to bring everything together in heaven and on earth into one unity in Christ. Believers are sealed with the Holy Spirit as a guarantee of their inheritance. Paul prays that the church may receive a spirit of wisdom and revelation to know God's power—the same power that raised Christ from the dead and seated Him at His right hand, far above all rule and authority, for the sake of the church, which is His body.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Chosen (ἐξελέξατο):** God chose us in Christ before the creation of the world to be holy and blameless.
- **Redemption (ἀπολύτρωσις):** Liberation and forgiveness of sins obtained through Christ's blood.
- **Seal of the Spirit (ἐσφραγίσθητε):** The Holy Spirit as a guarantee and down payment on the coming inheritance.
- **Head and Body (κεφαλή/σῶμα):** Christ is the head over all things for the church, which is His body and fullness.

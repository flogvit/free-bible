**Main Theme:** God commands the prophet Hosea to marry an unfaithful woman as a prophetic sign of Israel's spiritual adultery against the Lord.

**Content:** Hosea ministered during the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah in Judah, and Jeroboam II in Israel. God commanded him to take a "harlot" named Gomer, because the land had turned away from the Lord. They had three children with symbolic names that expressed God's judgment against Israel: Jezreel (judgment on the house of Jehu), Lo-Ruhama ("not shown mercy"), and Lo-Ammi ("not my people"). However, God promised to show mercy toward Judah.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Adultery/Spiritual Unfaithfulness** – Symbolizes Israel's spiritual adultery and idolatry against God.
- **Jezreel** – The name means "God sows" and announces God's judgment on the dynasty of Jehu for the bloodshed in the valley of Jezreel.
- **Lo-Ruhama** – Means "not shown mercy" and signals that God will no longer spare Israel.
- **Lo-Ammi** – Means "not my people" and marks the breaking of the covenant relationship between God and Israel.

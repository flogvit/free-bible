**Main Theme:** God announces judgment over Judah because of idolatry, but promises a future restoration that surpasses the exodus from Egypt.

**Content:** God forbids Jeremiah to marry or attend houses of mourning and feasting as a sign of the coming disaster. The people will die by disease, sword, and famine without burial. The reason is that they have abandoned God and served foreign gods—worse than their ancestors did. God will send "fishers" and "hunters" to catch them, for no one can hide from His eyes. Nevertheless, God promises a future return from the north, and nations will recognize the emptiness of idols.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fishers and Hunters (v.16):** A picture of God's judgment reaching everyone; no one can flee or hide.
- **Prohibition on Mourning (vv.5–7):** Jeremiah is not allowed to attend funerals—symbolizing that God has removed His peace, grace, and mercy.
- **Hardness of Heart (v.12):** The people follow the stubbornness of their evil hearts and refuse to listen to God.
- **New Exodus (vv.14–15):** The future return will overshadow the exodus from Egypt as God's great act of salvation.

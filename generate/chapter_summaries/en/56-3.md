**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts believers to a life of goodness and obedience, rooted in God's saving grace.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an exhortation to submit to governing authorities and show gentleness toward all people. Paul grounds this instruction in the contrast between our former life in sin and God's salvation—not by our works, but through his mercy, via the washing of regeneration and renewal by the Holy Spirit. Believers are urged to devote themselves to good works and to avoid useless controversies and divisive people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Washing of regeneration (παλιγγενεσία):** Baptism as a sign of the radical renewal God brings about in the believer.
- **God's goodness and love (χρηστότης, φιλανθρωπία):** God's character revealed in the work of salvation, not as a response to human merit.
- **Heirs (κληρονόμοι):** Believers have become God's heirs with the hope of eternal life.
- **Good works (καλὰ ἔργα):** Not the basis for salvation, but the fruit of salvation that believers are to pursue diligently.

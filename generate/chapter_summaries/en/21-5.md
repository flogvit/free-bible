**Main Theme:** The chapter warns against hasty vows to God and against trusting in wealth, pointing instead to godliness and simple joy as the good.

**Content:** The Preacher urges caution in worship—few words and fulfilled vows—because God is in heaven and man on earth. He exposes the emptiness of riches: whoever loves money will never be satisfied, abundance brings sleeplessness, and wealth can be lost, leaving nothing behind. Yet it is good to enjoy food, drink, and labor in the days that God gives, for this is God's gift to humanity.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Vow (נֶדֶר):** A vow made to God must be kept; it is better not to promise than to break one's word.
- **Vanity (הֶבֶל):** The pursuit of wealth is meaningless because it never satisfies.
- **The Worker's Sleep:** The worker sleeps well, while the rich person's abundance keeps them awake.
- **God's Gift:** The ability to rejoice in life's simple blessings is a gift from God.

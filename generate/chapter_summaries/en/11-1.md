**Main Theme:** The transfer of the throne from David to Solomon and the confirmation of God’s chosen heir.

**Content:** The aging King David is ill, and Abishag from Shunem is brought in to care for him. Adonijah attempts to seize power with support from the priest Abiathar and the army commander Joab, but the prophet Nathan and Bathsheba inform David. David reaffirms his oath that Solomon shall inherit the throne, and Solomon is anointed king at the Gihon spring. Adonijah’s supporters flee, and Adonijah himself seeks refuge at the altar, but he is allowed to live after showing submission.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Anointing at Gihon** – Zadok anoints Solomon with oil from the tent, legitimizing him as God’s chosen king.
- **The King’s Mule** – Solomon rides on David’s own mule, a visible sign that he is the rightful heir.
- **The Altar Horns** – Adonijah grasps the horns of the altar to seek protection, an act demonstrating his fear and submission.
- **The Oath** – David’s oath to Bathsheba regarding Solomon’s right to inherit the throne is decisive for the outcome of events and shows the binding power of the word.

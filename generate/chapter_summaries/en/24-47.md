**Main Theme:** A prophecy of God's judgment against the Philistines, conveyed through the image of a flood from the north and the Lord's unstoppable sword.

**Content:** Jeremiah receives this message before Pharaoh attacked Gaza. The Lord warns that a devastating flood will rise up from the north and overwhelm the land of the Philistines, so that all its inhabitants cry out in despair. The judgment will strike Gaza and Ashkelon and cut off any helper for Tyre and Sidon. The chapter concludes with a poetic dialogue in which the sword cannot rest, because the Lord Himself has commanded it against Ashkelon and the coast.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The flood from the north:** Symbolizes the hostile army (likely Babylon) that will overwhelm the land with destruction.
- **The Lord's sword:** Personified as God's instrument of judgment that cannot rest until its mission is completed.
- **Caphtor:** The place of origin for the Philistines (likely Crete), emphasizing that the entire people will be struck.
- **Gaza and Ashkelon:** The two main cities representing all Philistia under God's judgment.

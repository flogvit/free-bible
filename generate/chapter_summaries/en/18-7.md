**Main Theme:** Job bitterly complains to God about the brevity of life and meaningless suffering, challenging Him to explain why He torments humanity.

**Content:** Job compares human life to the toil of a hired worker—short, hard, and without rest. He describes his own nights filled with unrest and his body covered in sores. Job turns directly to God with accusatory questions: Why do You watch over humans so closely? Why have You made me Your target? He asks God to look away from him before he dies and vanishes forever.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hired Worker (שָׂכִיר):** Human life is compared to the weary existence of a day laborer waiting for his wages.
- **Weaver's Shuttle (אָרֶג):** Job's days fly by faster than the shuttle in a loom—a picture of life's brevity.
- **Cloud That Vanishes (כָּלָה עָנָן):** Just as a cloud dissolves and is gone, so the dead descend to Sheol without returning.
- **Guardian of Humanity (נֹצֵר הָאָדָם):** An ironic title for God as the One who constantly watches and tests humanity.

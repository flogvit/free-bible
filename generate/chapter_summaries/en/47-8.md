**Main Theme:** Paul urges the Corinthians to generous giving, grounded in Christ’s example and God’s grace.

**Content:** Paul highlights the exemplary generosity of the Macedonians—they gave abundantly out of their poverty and affliction. He reminds them of Christ’s grace: though he was rich, yet for our sake he became poor (v.9). The Corinthians are encouraged to complete the collection they began, according to their ability and with a willing heart. The goal is equality (ἰσότης)—so that abundance meets need among the churches. Titus and two other trustworthy brothers are sent to administer the gift with integrity.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Grace (χάρις):** Generosity is not an obligation, but a gift of grace flowing from God’s grace in Christ.
- **Christ’s Poverty:** Jesus became poor so that we might become rich—the christological model for all Christian giving.
- **Equality (ἰσότης):** The principle of mutual sharing among churches, illustrated by the manna quotation from Exodus 16:18.
- **Willingness (αὐθαίρετοι):** Gifts are to be given freely and according to one’s ability, not under compulsion.

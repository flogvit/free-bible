**Main Theme:** The judgments of the fifth and sixth trumpets bring demonic plagues and massive destruction upon humanity.

**Content:** At the sounding of the fifth trumpet, the abyss is opened, and locust-like creatures are released to torment people who do not have God's seal for five months—without killing them. They are led by Abaddon (Apollyon), the angel of destruction. At the sixth trumpet, four angels bound at the Euphrates are released, and a vast army kills one-third of humanity. Despite these judgments, the survivors refuse to repent from idolatry and sin.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Abyss (ἄβυσσος):** The demonic prison from which evil is unleashed during God's judgment.
- **The Locusts:** Demonic creatures with scorpion-like power that torment those without God's seal, but cannot harm nature or kill.
- **Abaddon/Apollyon:** The angel of the abyss and king over the locusts; the name means "Destroyer."
- **The Four Angels at the Euphrates:** Bound angels who are released to lead a deadly army at a predetermined time.
- **Lack of Repentance:** Humanity's stubbornness toward God despite His clear acts of judgment.

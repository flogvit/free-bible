**Main Theme:** God sends the plague of locusts and darkness upon Egypt, but Pharaoh continues to refuse to let the Israelites go.

**Content:** God sends the eighth plague—an enormous swarm of locusts that covers the land and devours everything that survived the hail. After God removes the locusts, He hardens Pharaoh’s heart again. Then God sends the ninth plague—a thick darkness that lasts for three days and paralyzes the Egyptians, while the Israelites have light. Throughout the chapter, Pharaoh tries to negotiate who may go, but Moses insists that everyone—with all they own—must be allowed to leave. The confrontation ends with Pharaoh threatening Moses’ life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Locusts (אַרְבֶּה):** A swarm without equal, symbolizing God’s power to completely destroy Egypt’s resources.
- **Darkness (חֹשֶׁךְ):** A darkness so thick it could be felt, which struck the Egyptians for three days while Israel had light—a picture of the separation between God’s people and their oppressors.
- **Hardened Heart:** God’s hardening of Pharaoh’s heart serves a dual purpose: to demonstrate God’s signs and to create a story for future generations (v.1–2).
- **Negotiations:** Pharaoh’s repeated attempts at compromise (only the men, let the livestock stay) show resistance to complete obedience to God.

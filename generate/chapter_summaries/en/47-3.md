**Main Theme:** The ministry of the new covenant in the Spirit surpasses the old covenant ministry in glory and brings freedom and transformation.

**Content:** Paul defends his apostolic ministry by pointing to the Corinthians themselves as his letter of recommendation, written by Christ with the living Spirit of God on tablets of human hearts. He contrasts the old covenant ministry—the letter that kills, engraved on stone tablets—with the new covenant ministry in the Spirit that gives life. While Moses covered his face so that the Israelites would not see the end of the fading glory, believers now, with unveiled faces, reflect the Lord's glory and are transformed from one degree of glory to another.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tablets of human hearts:** The Corinthians are Christ's letter, written not on stone tablets but on the tablets of human hearts—the inner reality of the new covenant.
- **Letter and Spirit:** The letter (the law) kills, but the Spirit gives life—contrasting the working of the old and new covenants.
- **Veil/κάλυμμα:** Moses' veil symbolizes the veiled understanding that still lies over Israel when reading the old covenant, but is removed in Christ.
- **Glory (δόξα):** The glory of the new covenant surpasses that of the old, and believers are transformed from one degree of glory to another by the Lord's Spirit.
- **Freedom:** Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom—the hallmark of the new covenant in contrast to the slavery of the law.

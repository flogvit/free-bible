**Main Theme:** The Power of the Tongue and the Difference Between Earthly and Heavenly Wisdom.

**Content:** James warns against becoming teachers without understanding the great responsibility this entails (v.1). The tongue is described as a small but powerful member that can be both a blessing and a curse—an unruly evil full of deadly poison. The chapter distinguishes between earthly wisdom, characterized by jealousy and self-assertion, and heavenly wisdom, which is pure, peaceable, gentle, open to reason, full of mercy and good fruits.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bit and Rudder:** Small tools that steer large bodies—illustrating the tongue's power to direct the whole person.
- **Fire:** The tongue is compared to a spark that sets an entire forest ablaze—showing its destructive potential.
- **Spring:** A spring cannot give both sweet and bitter water—highlighting the unnatural nature of the same mouth blessing and cursing.
- **Wisdom from Above:** True wisdom from God is pure, peaceable, gentle, open to reason, and full of mercy, in contrast to earthly wisdom.

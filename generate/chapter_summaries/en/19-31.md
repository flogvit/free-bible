**Main Theme:** A trusting prayer for deliverance from enemies and distress, in which the psalmist completely entrusts himself into God's hands.

**Content:** David cries out to the Lord for protection and confesses that God is his rock and fortress. In the midst of intense suffering—despised by enemies, abandoned by friends, forgotten as one dead—he holds fast to his trust in God. He praises God's wonderful goodness toward those who fear Him, and concludes by urging all the righteous to love the Lord and be strong.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rock and fortress (סַלְעִי וּמְצוּדָתִי):** God as an impregnable refuge and sure protection against enemies.
- **"Into your hands I commit my spirit" (בְּיָדְךָ אַפְקִיד רוּחִי):** Total surrender to God, quoted by Jesus on the cross (Luke 23:46).
- **Broken vessel (כִּכְלִי אֹבֵד):** A picture of feeling worthless and forgotten by people.
- **The light of God's face (הָאִירָה פָנֶיךָ):** A prayer for God's gracious presence and salvation.
- **Hide in the shelter of God's face (בְּסֵתֶר פָּנֶיךָ):** God's protection against human evil and slander.

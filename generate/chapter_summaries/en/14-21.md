**Main Theme:** King Joram's evil reign and God's judgment upon him through illness, enemy attacks, and disgrace.

**Content:** Joram becomes king after Jehoshaphat and kills all his brothers to secure power. He follows the evil ways of Ahab's house through his marriage to Ahab's daughter and leads Judah into idol worship. Nevertheless, God preserves David's dynasty because of His covenant with David. The prophet Elisha sends a letter announcing judgment: Philistines and Arabs plunder the kingdom and take Joram's family; only his youngest son Jehoahaz remains. Joram dies from a painful intestinal disease after two years and is buried without honor—not in the royal tombs.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Letter from Elijah (מִכְתָּב):** A written prophetic judgment—unique to Elijah—that proclaims punishment over Joram.
- **David's Covenant (בְּרִית):** God does not destroy Judah despite Joram's wickedness, because He promised David an eternal "lamp" (נִיר).
- **Intestinal Disease:** God's direct judgment, fulfilled exactly as Elijah prophesied.
- **Burial Without Honor:** Joram is buried in the City of David but not in the royal tombs—a sign of the people's contempt.

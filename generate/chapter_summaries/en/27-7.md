**Main Theme:** Daniel's vision of four world kingdoms that are ultimately overcome by God's eternal kingdom.

**Content:** In the first year of King Belshazzar, Daniel sees four great beasts rising from the sea, representing four kingdoms. The fourth beast has ten horns, and a little horn that speaks boastful words arises among them. The Ancient of Days holds court; the fourth beast is slain, and one like a son of man receives eternal dominion. The kingdom of the holy ones shall endure forever.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Four Beasts:** Represent four world kingdoms that will arise on earth.
- **The Little Horn:** An arrogant horn that speaks against the Most High and persecutes the holy ones.
- **The Ancient of Days:** God as Judge, depicted on a throne of fire with white garments.
- **Son of Man:** A heavenly figure who receives eternal dominion over all peoples.
- **The Kingdom of the Holy Ones:** The eternal kingdom given to the Most High's holy ones after judgment.

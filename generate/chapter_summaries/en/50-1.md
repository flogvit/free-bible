**Main Theme:** Paul expresses gratitude and joy over the Philippians’ fellowship in the gospel, and encourages them to live worthily for Christ amid opposition.

**Content:** Paul and Timothy greet the church in Philippi and thank God for their faithful partnership in the gospel from the very first day. Paul explains that his imprisonment has advanced the cause of the gospel, and reflects on life and death—"to live is Christ, to die is gain." He urges the Philippians to stand united in one spirit, to contend for the faith of the gospel, and not to be intimidated by opponents.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Koinonia (fellowship):** The Philippians’ active participation and partnership in the work of the gospel from the beginning.
- **Joy:** A recurring theme—Paul prays with joy and rejoices that Christ is proclaimed, regardless of motives.
- **To live is Christ:** Paul’s life motto expressing total devotion to Christ, where even death becomes gain.
- **Advance of the gospel:** Paul’s chains have led to the spread of the gospel in the praetorium and given other believers boldness.

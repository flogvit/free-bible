**Main Theme:** A hymn that honors God alone for his grace and faithfulness, in contrast to powerless idols.

**Content:** The psalm begins with a prayer that God should receive the glory, not humans, and defends God's honor against the mockery of the nations. It contrasts the living God in heaven with lifeless idols of silver and gold that can neither speak, see, hear, nor act. Israel, the house of Aaron, and all who fear God are urged to trust in the Lord, who is their help and shield. The psalm concludes with blessings and a commitment to eternal praise, since the dead cannot praise God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Idols (עֲצַבִּים):** Lifeless statues of silver and gold contrasted with the living God who acts freely.
- **Help and shield (עֶזְרָם וּמָגִנָּם):** Expressions of God's protection and support for those who trust in him.
- **Hallelujah (הַלְלוּ־יָהּ):** A call to praise that concludes the psalm and marks the living people's commitment to eternal worship.

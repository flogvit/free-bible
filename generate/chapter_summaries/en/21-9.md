**Main Theme:** Death strikes everyone alike; therefore, people should enjoy life and act with wisdom while there is still time.

**Content:** The Preacher observes that the same fate awaits all—the righteous and the wicked, the pure and the impure. Since the living know they will die, they are encouraged to enjoy food, wine, and companionship with joy. Time and chance happen to everyone, regardless of ability. A story about a poor wise man who saved a city but was forgotten illustrates that wisdom is valuable yet often overlooked.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Same fate (מִקְרֶה אֶחָד):** Death makes no distinction between the righteous and the wicked.
- **A living dog is better than a dead lion:** The living have hope, but the dead know nothing.
- **Time and chance:** Success does not depend on skill, but on circumstances.
- **The poor wise man:** Wisdom can save, yet it is often forgotten and despised.

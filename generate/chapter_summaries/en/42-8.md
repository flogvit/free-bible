**Main Theme:** Jesus demonstrates his authority through teaching and mighty works, showing his power over nature, evil spirits, sickness, and death.

**Content:** Jesus travels around proclaiming the kingdom of God, supported by women who serve him with their resources. He teaches through the parable of the sower about how people receive God's word in different ways, and he instructs on openness and true family. Jesus displays his power by calming the storm on the sea, freeing a man from a legion of demons in the region of the Gerasenes, healing a woman who had suffered from bleeding for twelve years, and raising Jairus' twelve-year-old daughter from the dead.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Sower:** God's word is received differently—some reject it, others fall away, but the good soil bears fruit with perseverance.
- **The Storm:** Jesus has power over the forces of nature; the disciples' fear reveals their lack of faith.
- **Legion:** The deliverance of the possessed man demonstrates Jesus' authority over demonic powers and his care for the individual.
- **Faith That Saves:** Both the woman with bleeding and Jairus approach Jesus in faith and experience his healing power.

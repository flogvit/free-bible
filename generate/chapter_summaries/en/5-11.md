**Main Theme:** Moses exhorts Israel to love God and keep His commandments, with a promise of blessing for obedience and a warning of curse for disobedience.

**Content:** Moses reminds the people of God's mighty acts in Egypt, at the Red Sea, and in the wilderness—including the judgment against Dathan and Abiram. He describes Canaan as a land unlike Egypt, dependent on God's rain from heaven. Obedience will bring rain at the proper time and abundance, while disobedience leads to drought and destruction. God's words are to be bound on the body, taught to children, and written on the doorposts. The blessing is to be proclaimed from Mount Gerizim and the curse from Mount Ebal.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's mighty hand:** Refers to God's visible intervention in history, especially the exodus from Egypt and judgment over enemies.
- **A land that drinks rain from heaven:** Canaan depends on God's care, unlike Egypt's artificial irrigation.
- **Gerizim and Ebal:** The two mountains where blessing and curse were to be proclaimed—symbolizing the choice Israel faces.
- **Bind on the hand and between the eyes:** The command to let God's words shape all of life, both action and thought.

**Main Theme:** Jehu is anointed king and executes God’s judgment on the house of Ahab by killing King Joram, King Ahaziah, and Queen Jezebel.

**Content:** The prophet Elisha sends one of the sons of the prophets to anoint Jehu as king over Israel, with the mission to wipe out the house of Ahab in vengeance for the blood of the prophets. Jehu goes to Jezreel, where he kills King Joram of Israel on Naboth’s vineyard—a direct fulfillment of the Lord’s judgment. King Ahaziah of Judah is also killed while fleeing. Queen Jezebel is thrown from a window by her own courtiers, and dogs devour her body, just as the prophet Elijah had foretold.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Anointing:** The young prophet anoints Jehu in secret, marking God’s choice of him as an instrument of judgment.
- **Naboth’s Vineyard:** The place where Joram is killed, fulfilling the judgment against Ahab for murdering Naboth.
- **Jezebel’s Fall:** The queen adorns herself before her death, yet ends up as food for dogs—an image of how pride leads to downfall.
- **"Is it peace?":** The repeated question that reveals there can be no peace while idolatry and injustice prevail.

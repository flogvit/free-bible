**Main Theme:** Jesus' high priestly prayer before the crucifixion, in which he prays for his own glorification, the preservation of his disciples, and the unity of all believers.

**Content:** Jesus lifts his eyes to heaven and asks the Father to glorify him so that he may glorify the Father. He defines eternal life as knowing the only true God and Jesus Christ. Jesus prays for his disciples, asking for protection from the evil one, sanctification in the truth, and unity like that between the Father and the Son. He extends this prayer to all who will come to believe, expressing his desire that they be with him and see his glory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Glorification (δόξα):** Jesus asks to receive back the glory he had with the Father before the world was created.
- **Unity (ἕν):** The deep unity between the Father and the Son is to be reflected in the fellowship of believers.
- **Preservation (τηρέω):** Jesus prays that the Father will keep the believers in his name and from the evil one.
- **Sanctification (ἁγιάζω):** The disciples are to be sanctified in the truth, which is God's word.

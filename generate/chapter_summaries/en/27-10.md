**Main Theme:** Daniel receives a vision by the Tigris River where a heavenly messenger reveals the unseen battle between spiritual powers.

**Content:** In the third year of King Cyrus, Daniel mourned and fasted for three weeks. By the Tigris River, he had a vision of a man dressed in linen, with a body like beryl and a face like lightning. The man explained that Daniel's prayers had been heard from the first day, but the prince of Persia had resisted him for 21 days until Michael came to help. The messenger came to reveal what will happen to Daniel's people in the last days and told him that the struggle against the princes of Persia and Greece continues.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The man in linen:** A heavenly figure with a supernatural appearance who brings revelation to Daniel.
- **The prince of Persia (שַׂר מַלְכוּת פָּרַס):** A spiritual power behind the Persian empire that opposes God's messenger.
- **Michael:** One of the chief princes who fights for Israel in the spiritual realm.
- **The last days (אַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים):** The future time when prophecy will be fulfilled for Daniel's people.

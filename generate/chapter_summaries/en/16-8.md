**Main Theme:** The people gather to hear God’s law, which leads to both weeping and joy, culminating in the celebration of the Feast of Tabernacles.

**Content:** Ezra read the Law of Moses aloud to the people at the Water Gate, while the Levites explained its meaning. When the people heard the words of the law, they wept, but Nehemiah and Ezra urged them to rejoice because the day was holy and "the joy of the Lord is your strength." The people then celebrated the Feast of Tabernacles for seven days with daily Scripture reading, concluding with a solemn assembly on the eighth day.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Water Gate:** The gathering place where the people assembled to hear the law read.
- **The joy of the Lord:** The source of strength for God’s people—sorrow should give way to joy on holy days.
- **Feast of Tabernacles:** The festival rediscovered in the law and celebrated for the first time since the days of Joshua.
- **Levites:** Helpers who explained and translated the law so that the people could understand it.

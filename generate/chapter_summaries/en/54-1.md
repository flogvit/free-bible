**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts Timothy to preserve sound doctrine and fight the good fight of faith.

**Content:** Paul writes to Timothy, his true child in the faith, urging him to remain in Ephesus to oppose false teachings and endless myths. He emphasizes that the goal of this exhortation is love from a pure heart, a good conscience, and sincere faith. Paul testifies to his own transformation from a mocker and persecutor into an apostle, as proof of Christ's boundless patience and grace. He concludes with a doxology and encourages Timothy to hold fast to faith and a good conscience.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sound doctrine (ὑγιαινούσῃ διδασκαλίᾳ):** The correct teaching that aligns with the gospel, in contrast to false teachings and endless speculations.
- **The good fight (καλὴν στρατείαν):** The image of the spiritual battle Timothy is to wage, armed with faith and a good conscience.
- **Shipwreck of the faith (ἐναυάγησαν):** A metaphor for those who have rejected a good conscience and departed from the faith, such as Hymenaeus and Alexander.
- **The foremost of sinners (πρῶτός εἰμι ἐγώ):** Paul's self-description as an example of God's boundless grace and mercy.

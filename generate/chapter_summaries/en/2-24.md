**Main Theme:** The covenant between God and Israel at Mount Sinai.

**Content:** Moses conveys God’s words and laws to the people, who unanimously promise to obey. He builds an altar with twelve stone pillars and sprinkles the blood of sacrifices on the people as a sign of the covenant. Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy elders see the God of Israel and eat and drink in his presence. Then Moses goes up onto God’s mountain together with his servant Joshua, while the elders remain with Aaron and Hur. Moses stays on the mountain for forty days to receive the stone tablets containing the law.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Covenant Blood:** The blood sprinkled on the people confirms the covenant between God and Israel.
- **The Sapphirine Pavement:** Under God’s feet was something like sapphire stone, clear as the sky itself—a sign of heavenly purity.
- **Devouring Fire:** God’s glory appears as fire on the mountain—his holiness and majesty.
- **The Stone Tablets:** God promises to give Moses the tablets containing the law as guidance for the people.

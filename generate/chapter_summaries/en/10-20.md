**Main Theme:** Sheba's rebellion against David is crushed through Joab's pursuit and the intervention of a wise woman.

**Content:** The Benjamite Sheba leads a rebellion against David, gaining followers from Israel. After first killing Amasa at Gibeon, Joab pursues him. At the city of Abel Beth Maacah, a wise woman persuades the inhabitants to kill Sheba to save their city; his head is then thrown over the wall to Joab. The chapter concludes with an overview of David's officials.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sheba son of Bichri:** A Benjamite who leads a rebellion against David with the cry, "We have no portion in David."
- **The Wise Woman:** An unnamed woman who saves the city of Abel Beth Maacah through negotiation and action.
- **Joab's Treachery:** Joab kills Amasa at Gibeon under the guise of a greeting, repeating his pattern of treachery.
- **"A mother in Israel":** The woman's description of the city underscores its significance and the value of preserving it.

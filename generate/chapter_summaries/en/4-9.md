**Main Theme:** The Passover celebration and God's guidance through the pillar of cloud.

**Content:** God commands Israel to celebrate the Passover in the wilderness of Sinai one year after leaving Egypt. Those who are unclean or on a long journey may observe it in the second month instead, while anyone who deliberately fails to keep it shall be cut off from the people. The cloud over the tabernacle directs the Israelites' journeys—when it lifts up, they break camp; when it settles over the tabernacle, they make camp.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Passover (pesach):** The annual celebration commemorating deliverance from Egypt, with strict requirements for purity.
- **Second Passover:** An additional provision for those who were unclean or traveling, demonstrating God's grace and flexibility.
- **Pillar of Cloud:** God's visible presence above the tabernacle that guided the people—cloud by day and appearing as fire by night.
- **At the Lord's Command:** This expression is repeated to emphasize that the people's movements were entirely directed by God.

**Main Theme:** Samson’s marriage to a Philistine woman and the first conflict with the Philistines, guided by God’s hidden plan.

**Content:** Samson wants to marry a Philistine woman from Timnah, against his parents’ wishes. They did not know that this was from the Lord, who sought an opportunity against the Philistines who ruled over Israel. On the way, the Spirit of the Lord comes upon Samson, and he kills a lion. At the wedding, he gives the guests a riddle based on honey he found in the lion’s carcass. The bride is pressured to reveal the answer, and Samson must kill 30 Philistines in Ashkelon to pay the wager. He leaves the bride, who is then given to his best man.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Spirit of the Lord (רוּחַ יְהוָה):** Came upon Samson and gave him supernatural strength to kill the lion and the 30 Philistines.
- **The Riddle:** "Out of the eater came something to eat, out of the strong came sweetness" – based on the lion and the honey, symbolizing Samson’s secrets.
- **An opportunity against the Philistines (תֹאֲנָה):** God’s hidden purpose behind Samson’s seemingly disobedient choice of marriage.
- **Philistine domination:** The context for the conflict – the Philistines ruled over Israel at this time.

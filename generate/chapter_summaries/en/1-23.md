**Main Theme:** Sarah's death and Abraham's purchase of a burial site in Canaan.

**Content:** Sarah dies at the age of 127 in Kiriath-Arba (Hebron) in Canaan, and Abraham mourns her. As "a stranger and a sojourner," he negotiates with the Hittites to buy a burial plot. The Hittites, who call him "a prince of God," offer him a grave for free, but Abraham insists on paying the full price. He buys the cave of Machpelah along with the surrounding field from the Hittite Ephron for 400 shekels of silver and buries Sarah there.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cave of Machpelah:** The first property owned by the Israelites in the promised land, purchased as a burial place for the patriarchal family.
- **Stranger and sojourner (ger v'toshav):** Abraham's self-description showing that he has not yet inherited the land God promised him.
- **400 shekels of silver:** The full market price ensuring undisputed ownership rights – Abraham did not want to receive the land as a gift from people.
- **Prince of God (nesi elohim):** The Hittites' honorific title for Abraham, showing his high standing among the peoples of Canaan.

**Main Theme:** The chapter describes the setting up of the lampstand and the ritual consecration of the Levites for service in the Tabernacle.

**Content:** Aaron receives instructions to set up the seven lamps of the lampstand so that they shine forward. This is followed by detailed instructions for the cleansing of the Levites through sprinkling with water of purification, shaving, and washing their clothes. The Israelites lay their hands on the Levites, who are then waved as a wave offering to the Lord. The Levites are set apart as a substitute for all the firstborn in Israel and given to Aaron and his sons for service. The age for service is established from 25 to 50 years; after that, they may assist but not perform the heavy work.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lampstand (menorah):** The seven-branched lamp fixture that Aaron was to light so the lamps would shine forward, made of pure gold.
- **Cleansing:** Ritual preparation of the Levites through sprinkling with water of purification, shaving, and washing their garments.
- **Wave offering (tenufa):** The Levites were symbolically waved as an offering, marking their dedication to the Lord's service.
- **Substitute for the firstborn:** The Levites were taken in place of all the firstborn in Israel, who belonged to God since the exodus from Egypt.

**Main Theme:** The prophet Nathan confronts David with his sin, and the chapter reveals both God's judgment and mercy.

**Content:** Nathan tells David a parable about a rich man who steals a poor man's only lamb, then reveals that David himself is the guilty one. David confesses his sin, and Nathan assures him of forgiveness but warns that the sword will never depart from his house and that the child will die. After the child's death, David comforts Bathsheba, and she gives birth to Solomon, whom God loves and calls Jedidiah. The chapter concludes with David leading the conquest of Rabbah and returning to Jerusalem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Parable of the Lamb:** Nathan's story exposes David's sin through an image of abuse of power and injustice.
- **"You are the man":** Nathan's direct confrontation that reveals David as the sinner.
- **"I have sinned against the Lord":** David's confession that leads to forgiveness.
- **Jedidiah ("beloved of the Lord"):** God's name for Solomon, showing grace amid judgment.
- **The sword in David's house:** The prophecy of ongoing conflict as a consequence of sin.

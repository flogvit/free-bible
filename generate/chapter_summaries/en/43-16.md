**Main Theme:** Jesus prepares his disciples for his departure by promising the coming of the Holy Spirit and victory over the world’s opposition.

**Content:** Jesus warns the disciples about coming persecution and expulsion from the synagogue, but promises to send the Advocate (παράκλητος) who will convince the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment. He uses the image of a woman in labor to illustrate that their sorrow will be turned into lasting joy. Jesus encourages them to pray in his name and assures them of the Father’s love. The chapter concludes with Jesus’ declaration of victory: "I have overcome the world".

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Advocate (παράκλητος):** The Holy Spirit whom Jesus sends to guide the disciples into all truth and to glorify Christ.
- **Birth Pains:** A picture of the disciples’ temporary sorrow that will be transformed into lasting joy when they see Jesus again.
- **Pray in Jesus’ Name:** New access to the Father through Christ, with the promise of answered prayer and complete joy.
- **Victory over the World:** Jesus’ triumph gives his disciples peace and boldness amid the world’s trouble.

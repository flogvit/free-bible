**Main Theme:** The Lamb opens the first six seals, which unleash judgment and catastrophe upon the earth.

**Content:** When the Lamb breaks the first four seals, four horses are called forth by the living creatures: white (conquest), red (war), black (famine), and pale yellow (death and Hades with authority over a fourth of the earth). The fifth seal reveals the souls of martyrs under the altar, who cry out for justice and receive white robes while they wait. The sixth seal triggers cosmic chaos—earthquakes, a darkened sun, a blood-red moon, and falling stars—while all people, from kings to slaves, hide in fear of the Lamb's wrath.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Four Horsemen:** Symbolize conquest, war, famine, and death that strike the earth.
- **Souls of the Martyrs:** Those killed for God's word wait under the altar for God's righteous judgment.
- **Cosmic Upheaval:** Earthquakes and heavenly signs herald "the great day of the Lamb's wrath."
- **The Lamb's Wrath:** Judgment comes from the crucified Savior, now revealed as Judge.

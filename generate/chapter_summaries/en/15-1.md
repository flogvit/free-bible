**Main Theme:** God fulfills His word through Jeremiah by stirring King Cyrus to send the Jews back to rebuild the temple in Jerusalem.

**Content:** In the first year of Cyrus's reign, the Lord stirs the spirit of the Persian king to issue a decree: The Jews are permitted to return to Jerusalem to build the house of the Lord. Leaders from Judah and Benjamin, along with priests and Levites, rise up to go, and their neighbors support them with gifts. Cyrus also returns the temple vessels that Nebuchadnezzar had taken away, and they are handed over to the governor Sheshbazzar.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cyrus's Decree:** The Persian king acknowledges the God of Israel and grants permission for temple construction.
- **The Spirit of the Lord:** God stirs both the king's spirit and the people's spirit to act.
- **Temple Vessels:** The sacred objects taken by Nebuchadnezzar are returned – a sign of restoration.

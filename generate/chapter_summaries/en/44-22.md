**Main Theme:** Paul defends himself to the crowd in Jerusalem by recounting his conversion and his calling to the Gentiles.

**Content:** Paul delivers his defense speech in Hebrew, sharing about his Jewish background and training under Gamaliel, as well as his former persecution of Christians. He describes his encounter with the risen Jesus on the road to Damascus and his healing through Ananias. When he mentions his call to the Gentiles, the crowd erupts in fury and demands his death. The Roman commander intends to flog him to extract an answer, but Paul reveals that he is a Roman citizen, thereby avoiding mistreatment.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Light from heaven** – The bright light that surrounded Paul at Damascus symbolizes God's intervention and Jesus' glory.
- **Gentiles** – Paul's call to preach to the Gentiles provoked the Jewish crowd and triggered violent anger.
- **Roman citizenship** – Paul's citizen rights protected him from unlawful flogging and secured his legal protection.
- **Ananias** – A devout Jew whom God used to heal Paul and convey his calling to service.

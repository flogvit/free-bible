**Main Theme:** God condemns Israel’s irresponsible shepherds and promises to become the good shepherd Himself, gathering and protecting His people.

**Content:** God accuses Israel’s leaders of exploiting the flock instead of caring for it—they have not strengthened the weak, healed the sick, or sought out the lost. Therefore, God will take charge of His sheep Himself, gather them from among the nations, and bring them back to the land of Israel. He will also judge between the strong and the weak within the flock and raise up His servant David as one shepherd over them. God promises a covenant of peace with blessing, security, and abundance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shepherd and Flock:** A picture of the relationship between leaders and people—the wicked shepherds neglect the flock, but God Himself will be the good shepherd.
- **My Servant David:** A future ruler from David’s line who will shepherd the people with justice (a messianic promise).
- **Covenant of Peace (בְּרִית שָׁלוֹם):** God’s promise of lasting peace, security, and blessing for His people.
- **Judgment Between Sheep and Sheep:** God will distinguish between the strong who oppress and the weak who are oppressed within the nation.

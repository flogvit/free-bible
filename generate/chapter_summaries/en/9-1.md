**Main Theme:** Hannah's faith and prayer lead to Samuel's birth and dedication to the Lord's service.

**Content:** Elkanah had two wives: Hannah, who was barren, and Peninnah, who had children and provoked Hannah. The family traveled annually to the sanctuary at Shiloh to worship the Lord. There, Hannah prayed earnestly for a child and vowed to dedicate him to the Lord as a Nazirite for his entire life. The priest Eli initially misunderstood her silent prayer but later blessed her. God heard Hannah's prayer, and she gave birth to Samuel. After weaning him, she brought him to the sanctuary to fulfill her vow.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Barrenness:** In ancient times, barrenness was seen as a great shame and sorrow, which explains Hannah's deep despair.
- **Nazirite Vow:** Hannah promised that no razor would ever touch Samuel's head—a sign of dedication to God.
- **Shiloh:** The place where the tabernacle/tent of meeting was located before the temple was built; Israel's central sanctuary.
- **Prayer from the Heart:** Hannah's silent, earnest prayer shows that God sees the attitude of the heart, not merely outward actions.

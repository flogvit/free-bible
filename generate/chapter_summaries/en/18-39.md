**Main Theme:** God displays his sovereign wisdom through questions about the mysteries of animal life that Job cannot explain.

**Content:** God continues his speech to Job with rhetorical questions concerning wild animals: the birth of mountain goats, the freedom of the wild donkey, the untamable nature of the wild ox, the lack of wisdom in the ostrich, the fearless courage of the war horse, and the hunting instinct of birds of prey. Through these examples, God shows that he alone understands and governs creation in ways humans cannot comprehend.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Wild Donkey (פֶּרֶא):** A symbol of freedom – God has set it free from human control and given the wilderness as its home.
- **Wild Ox (רֵּים):** Represents untamable strength that humans cannot break or use for labor.
- **War Horse (סוּס):** Described with fearless courage and joy in battle – its strength comes from God, not from man.
- **Eagle (נֶשֶׁר):** Builds its nest on inaccessible cliffs and spots prey from afar – an illustration of God’s wisdom in creation.

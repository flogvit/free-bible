**Main Theme:** Yom Kippur – the annual Day of Atonement on which the high priest makes atonement for the sins of Israel.

**Content:** God instructs Moses on how Aaron is to enter the Most Holy Place to make atonement. Aaron must put on white linen garments, offer a bull as a sin offering for himself and his family, and cast lots over two male goats – one for the Lord and one for Azazel. The blood from the sin offerings is to be sprinkled on the mercy seat and the altar to cleanse the sanctuary from the people’s uncleanness. Aaron lays his hands on the live goat, confesses the sins of the people over it, and sends it away into the wilderness with a designated man. This day is to be an everlasting ordinance involving fasting and complete rest.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mercy Seat (kapporet):** The cover of the ark of the covenant where God reveals Himself in the cloud and atonement is made through the sprinkling of blood.
- **Azazel:** The live goat that carries the people’s sins out into the wilderness after the laying on of hands and confession.
- **Casting Lots:** The divine selection determining which goat is to be sacrificed and which is to be sent away.
- **Linen Garments:** The simple white garments worn by the high priest when he enters the Most Holy Place.
- **Sabbath of Sabbaths (shabbat shabbaton):** The command for complete rest and fasting on this most holy day.

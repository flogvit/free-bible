**Main Theme:** Personified Wisdom cries out to humanity and reveals her eternal origin and life-giving value.

**Content:** Wisdom is portrayed as a woman who calls out in public places, inviting everyone to listen. She declares her origin from eternity, before creation, and describes how she was with God as His delight during the work of creation. Wisdom gives kings the ability to rule justly and offers wealth, honor, and life to those who love her. Those who find wisdom find life and the Lord's favor, while those who despise her harm themselves.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Personified Wisdom:** Wisdom is depicted as a woman who actively seeks out people to offer them guidance and life.
- **Before Creation:** Wisdom existed from eternity, before mountains, seas, and earth were formed, and was present as God's delight during creation (vv. 22–31).
- **The Lord's Favor:** Whoever finds wisdom finds life and obtains the Lord's favor (v. 35).
- **Righteousness and Truth:** The words of Wisdom are true, righteous, and without deceit—the foundation for good governance and a right way of living.

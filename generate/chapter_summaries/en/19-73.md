**Main Theme:** The resolution of Asaph's crisis of faith through encountering God in the sanctuary.

**Content:** The psalm depicts Asaph's inner struggle with envy over the prosperity and apparent impunity of the wicked. The turning point comes when he enters God's sanctuary and understands their ultimate destiny. He acknowledges his own ignorance and confesses that God is his only refuge and portion—both in life and in death.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Sanctuary (מִקְדְּשֵׁי־אֵל):** The place where Asaph gains insight into God's righteous plan and the fate of the wicked.
- **Slippery places (חֲלָקוֹת):** A picture of the unstable ground upon which God places the wicked before their fall.
- **The Rock of my heart (צוּר־לְבָבִי):** An expression of God as the psalmist's firm foundation and eternal inheritance.
- **God's nearness (קִרֲבַת אֱלֹהִים):** The conclusion of the psalm: the good life is found in fellowship with God.

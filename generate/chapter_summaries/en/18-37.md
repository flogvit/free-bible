**Main Theme:** Elihu describes God's incomprehensible greatness revealed through the mighty forces of nature.

**Content:** Elihu trembles with awe as he describes how God governs thunder, lightning, snow, rain, and ice. He challenges Job with rhetorical questions about the mysteries of nature that humans cannot understand or control. The chapter culminates in a proclamation of the Almighty's awe-inspiring majesty – He is exalted in power and righteousness, and therefore people should fear Him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's thundering voice:** The sound of God's thunder and lightning symbolizes His majestic power that fills the whole earth (v.2-5).
- **Snow and rain:** God commands the snow to fall and the rain to pour down, as a sign of His sovereign control over creation (v.6).
- **God's breath:** The cold and ice come from God's breath, showing His power over all elements (v.10).
- **The Majesty of the Almighty:** God comes with awe-inspiring splendor from the north, exalted in power and righteousness (v.22-23).

**Main Theme:** A mighty angel proclaims that God’s mystery will soon be fulfilled, and John is commissioned to prophesy again.

**Content:** John sees a mighty angel clothed in a cloud, with a rainbow over his head, a face like the sun, and feet like pillars of fire. The angel stands with one foot on the sea and the other on land. He holds a little open scroll and cries out, which triggers seven peals of thunder that John is forbidden to write down. The angel swears by the eternal God that there will be no more delay—God’s mystery will be fulfilled when the seventh angel blows his trumpet. John must eat the little scroll; it tastes sweet in his mouth but makes his stomach bitter. He is then told to prophesy again about many peoples, nations, languages, and kings.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Mighty Angel:** A heavenly messenger with a majestic appearance, symbolizing God’s authority over all creation (sea and land).
- **The Little Scroll (βιβλαρίδιον):** God’s word/revelation that is sweet to receive but bitter when the message of judgment must be proclaimed.
- **The Seven Peals of Thunder:** Divine messages that remain sealed—certain mysteries are not revealed.
- **God’s Mystery (μυστήριον):** God’s plan of salvation, which will be fulfilled at the seventh trumpet, as the prophets have declared.

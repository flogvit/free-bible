**Main Theme:** God's sovereign installation of his anointed king on Zion, despite the rebellion of the nations.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a rhetorical question asking why the nations and their rulers conspire against the Lord and his Anointed. God in heaven laughs at their futile plans and declares that he has installed his king on Zion. Through a divine decree, the king is proclaimed as God's Son, with the promise of the nations as his inheritance. The psalm concludes with an exhortation to the kings of the earth to serve the Lord with fear and take refuge in him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's Anointed (מָשִׁיחַ):** The installed king against whom the nations rebel—a title with deep messianic significance.
- **"You are my Son":** God's decree proclaiming the king's unique relationship to God and his legitimate rule.
- **Scepter of iron:** A symbol of the king's invincible power to crush opposition and rule over the nations.
- **Kiss the Son:** An appeal for submission and reverence to avoid God's wrath.
- **Blessed are all who take refuge in him:** The psalm's concluding blessing for those who seek protection in God.

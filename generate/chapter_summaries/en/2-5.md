**Main Theme:** Pharaoh rejects God’s demands and increases the oppression of the Israelites.

**Content:** Moses and Aaron ask Pharaoh to let the people of Israel go so they may worship God in the wilderness, but Pharaoh defiantly replies: "Who is the Lord?" He refuses and punishes the people by requiring them to gather their own straw while still producing the same amount of bricks. The Israelite overseers are beaten and complain to Moses and Aaron. Moses turns to God in frustration and asks why he was sent when it has only made things worse for the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Who is the Lord?"** – Pharaoh’s arrogant rejection of the God of Israel, setting the stage for the coming conflict.
- **Bricks without straw** – A symbol of the impossible oppression Pharaoh imposes on the Israelites.
- **Overseers (שֹׁטְרִים)** – Israelite leaders caught between Pharaoh’s demands and the people’s suffering.
- **Moses’ complaint** – Expresses doubt and frustration over his mission as the situation worsens.

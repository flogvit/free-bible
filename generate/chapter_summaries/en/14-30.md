**Main Theme:** King Hezekiah’s Passover celebration gathers Judah and Israel for renewed worship in Jerusalem.

**Content:** Hezekiah invites all Israel and Judah to celebrate the Passover in Jerusalem in the second month, since the priests had not been consecrated in time. Messengers are sent out with a call to repentance, but many in the north mock them—only some from Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun come. The people remove idolatrous altars from Jerusalem, and the Passover is celebrated with great joy for fourteen days. Hezekiah prays for those who ate without proper cleansing, and God hears and heals the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Passover in the second month:** The celebration is postponed because the priests were not consecrated—showing flexibility within faithfulness to God’s will.
- **Letter of repentance:** Hezekiah’s call to the northern kingdom to return to the Lord, the God of their fathers.
- **Heart over ritual:** God forgives those who seek Him with all their heart, even without complete ritual purity (vv. 18–20).
- **Joy as in Solomon’s days:** The celebration is described as unmatched since the time of Solomon (v. 26).

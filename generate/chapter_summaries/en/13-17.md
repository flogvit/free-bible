**Main Theme:** God declines David's request to build a temple, but instead gives him the promise of an eternal dynasty.

**Content:** David expresses to the prophet Nathan his desire to build a house for the Lord’s ark of the covenant, but God answers through Nathan that it is David’s son who will build the temple. God promises to raise up David’s offspring and establish an everlasting kingdom, and He will be like a father to David’s descendant. David responds with a humble prayer of thanksgiving in which he praises God’s greatness and faithfulness toward Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The cedar house vs. the tent curtains:** The contrast between David’s palace and the simple dwelling of the ark shows David’s devout desire to honor God.
- **The Davidic Covenant:** God’s unconditional promise of an eternal dynasty through David’s offspring, with messianic overtones.
- **"I will be his father":** Expresses the unique relationship between God and David’s royal descendant.
- **The wordplay on "house":** David wants to build God a house (temple), but God will build David a house (dynasty).

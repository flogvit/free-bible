**Main Theme:** God gives Moses detailed instructions for the priestly garments that will consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve.

**Content:** Aaron and his sons are chosen for the priesthood, and holy garments are to be made for honor and beauty. The ephod and breastpiece shall bear the names of Israel's twelve tribes as a memorial before the Lord. Urim and Thummim are placed in the breastpiece, and Aaron carries the judgment of the people of Israel on his heart. A gold plate inscribed with "Holy to the Lord" is fastened to the turban, and Aaron bears the guilt associated with the holy offerings. The priests must wear linen undergarments and follow the regulations to avoid death.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ephod:** An apron of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet thread, with two onyx stones engraved with the names of the tribes.
- **Breastpiece (choshen mishpat):** The "breastpiece of judgment" with twelve precious stones representing the tribes of Israel, worn over the heart.
- **Urim and Thummim:** Sacred objects placed in the breastpiece; Aaron carries the judgment of Israel on his heart before the Lord.
- **Gold plate (tsits):** A plate inscribed "Holy to the Lord" on Aaron's forehead, bearing the guilt of the holy offerings.
- **Bells and pomegranates:** Attached to the hem of the robe, so that the sound is heard when Aaron enters the sanctuary.

**Main Theme:** Job acknowledges God's overwhelming power, yet expresses despair over his own helplessness in the face of suffering.

**Content:** Job responds to his friends by acknowledging that no human can be righteous before God. He describes God's incomprehensible power over creation—the mountains, the earth, the sun, and the stars. Yet Job feels crushed without cause and unable to defend himself. He longs for a mediator who could stand between him and God, so that he might speak without fear.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's omnipotence (vv. 4–10):** Job describes God as the One who moves mountains, shakes the earth, and rules over the heavenly bodies.
- **Helplessness (vv. 14–20):** Even though Job is innocent, he cannot argue against God—his own mouth would condemn him.
- **The transience of life (vv. 25–26):** Days fly by like runners, light boats, and an eagle swooping down on its prey.
- **The Mediator (v. 33):** Job longs for someone who could lay a hand on both of them and create a fair dialogue between humanity and God.

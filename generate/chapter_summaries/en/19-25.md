**Main Theme:** An acrostic psalm of prayer in which David seeks God's guidance, forgiveness, and protection.

**Content:** David lifts his soul to the Lord and expresses trust in Him amid threats from enemies. He asks to be taught God's ways and led in truth, calling upon God's mercy and steadfast love that have existed from eternity. The psalm confesses sin and seeks forgiveness, while emphasizing that God is good and upright and guides the humble. It concludes with a prayer for Israel's deliverance from all troubles.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's ways and paths (דְּרָכֶיךָ/אֹרְחוֹתֶיךָ):** A picture of God's will and way of life that David asks to be taught.
- **Mercy and steadfast love (רַחֲמִים/חֶסֶד):** God's eternal attributes that form the basis for hope in forgiveness.
- **Fear of the Lord (יְרֵא יְהוָה):** Those who fear the Lord receive guidance and share in His intimate counsel (סוֹד).
- **Covenant and testimony (בְּרִית/עֵדֹת):** God's faithful relationship with those who keep His commands.

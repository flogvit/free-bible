**Main Theme:** Luke 24 describes Jesus' resurrection, his appearances to the disciples, and his ascension.

**Content:** Women find the tomb empty and receive a message from two men in radiant clothing that Jesus has risen. Jesus reveals himself to two disciples on the road to Emmaus and is recognized when he breaks bread. He then appears to the eleven, proves his bodily resurrection, and opens their minds to understand the Scriptures. Jesus gives them the mission commission and promises the power of the Holy Spirit before being taken up into heaven.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Empty Tomb:** The testimony of the resurrection begins with the women finding the tomb empty and the stone rolled away.
- **The Breaking of Bread at Emmaus:** Jesus is recognized by the disciples as he breaks bread, a moment of revelation and fellowship.
- **"Flesh and Bones":** Jesus shows that he is not a spirit but has a real, physical resurrected body that can be touched.
- **Fulfillment of Scripture:** Jesus explains how the Law, the Prophets, and the Psalms testify to his suffering and resurrection.
- **The Ascension:** Jesus blesses the disciples and is taken up into heaven, marking the transition to the church's mission.

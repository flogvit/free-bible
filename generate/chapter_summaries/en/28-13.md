**Main Theme:** God’s judgment on Ephraim/Israel for their idolatry and ingratitude.

**Content:** Ephraim once had honor, but died spiritually by sinning against Baal. The people continued to make idols and worship calves. God reminds them that He is their only Savior from Egypt, yet they forgot Him when they became satisfied. Therefore, He will attack them like a wild beast. Their sin is "bound up" and hidden, and judgment comes like birth pains—but Ephraim is an unwise son who does not come forth in due time.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Morning mist and dew:** Israel will vanish quickly, without lasting endurance (v. 3)
- **Wild beasts (lion, leopard, bear):** God Himself becomes like a wild animal against His people in judgment (vv. 7–8)
- **Birth pains:** Judgment comes as unavoidable suffering, but Ephraim lacks the wisdom to be "born" (v. 13)
- **East wind:** The Lord’s wind from the wilderness that dries up all springs and destroys the treasure house (v. 15)

**Main Theme:** God's gracious healing of Hezekiah and the warning about Babylon's future dominance.

**Content:** King Hezekiah becomes mortally ill, but after earnest prayer, God hears him and grants him 15 additional years of life. As a sign of his healing, God causes the shadow to move backward ten steps on Ahaz's stairway. Later, Hezekiah foolishly shows all his treasures to Babylonian envoys, and Isaiah prophesies that everything will be carried off to Babylon and that descendants will become eunuchs there. Hezekiah accepts the judgment, grateful for peace during his lifetime.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fig cake on the boil:** Isaiah prescribes a simple medical treatment as an instrument of God's healing.
- **The shadow moving backward:** A miraculous sign where the shadow moves ten steps backward confirms God's promise of healing.
- **The treasury:** Hezekiah's proud display of wealth becomes the occasion for prophecy about future exile.
- **"It is good that the Lord has spoken":** Hezekiah's acceptance of judgment shows submission, but also relief that punishment is delayed.

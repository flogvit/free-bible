**Main Theme:** Paul argues that justification comes through faith in Christ, not by works of the law.

**Content:** Paul rebukes the Galatians for turning back to the law after having received the Spirit by faith. He demonstrates from Scripture that Abraham was justified by faith, and that all who believe are Abraham's children and receive the blessing. Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us. The law served as a guardian until Christ came, but now all who have been baptized into Christ are God's children and heirs—without distinction between Jew or Greek, slave or free, male or female.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Guardian (παιδαγωγός):** The law acted as a disciplinarian that led us to Christ, but we are no longer under it now that faith has come.
- **Abraham's offspring (σπέρμα):** The promises were given to Abraham and his offspring, who is Christ—and all who belong to Christ are heirs according to the promise.
- **Clothed with Christ:** Those baptized into Christ have put on Christ and are therefore all one in him.
- **The curse of the law:** All under the law are under a curse, but Christ redeemed us by becoming a curse for us on the cross.

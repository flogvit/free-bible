**Main Theme:** God warns the remnant of Judah against fleeing to Egypt and promises blessing if they remain in the land.

**Content:** The commanders and the people ask Jeremiah to seek God’s will regarding their future, solemnly promising to obey the answer. After ten days, God’s response comes: If they stay in Judah, God will build them up and protect them from Babylon. But if they go to Egypt, sword, famine, and plague will strike them there. Jeremiah accuses them of deceiving themselves, because they have already decided not to obey God’s word.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ten days:** The waiting period before God answers – showing that prophetic guidance requires patience.
- **Build and plant:** God’s promise of restoration for those who remain in the land in obedience.
- **Sword, famine, and plague:** The threefold judgment awaiting those who flee to Egypt in disobedience.
- **Curse and mockery:** The remnant of Judah will become an example of God’s judgment if they go to Egypt.

**Main Theme:** God equips Moses and Aaron to confront Pharaoh, and the first plague (blood) strikes Egypt.

**Content:** God appoints Moses as "god for Pharaoh" and Aaron as his prophet. Before Pharaoh, Aaron transforms his staff into a snake (tannin), and when the Egyptian magicians do the same, Aaron's staff swallows theirs. God sends the first plague: the Nile and all water in Egypt turn to blood, the fish die, and the water becomes undrinkable. Pharaoh's heart is hardened, and he refuses to let the people go. The chapter concludes with a warning of the plague of frogs.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"God for Pharaoh"** – Moses receives divine authority before the Egyptian king.
- **Staff turned into a snake (tannin)** – A sign of God's superior power over Egyptian magic.
- **Hardened heart** – Pharaoh's recurring resistance to God's will.
- **Plague of blood** – The first plague that strikes Egypt's life-giving Nile.

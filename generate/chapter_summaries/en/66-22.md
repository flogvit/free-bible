**Main Theme:** The Bible's final vision of eternal life with God and Jesus' promise to come soon.

**Content:** John sees the river of life flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb, with the tree of life bearing fruit for the healing of the nations. There will no longer be any curse, night, or need for light, for God himself will shine upon his servants. Jesus repeatedly confirms that he is coming soon to reward each person according to what they have done, and invites all who are thirsty to take the water of life without cost. The book concludes with a warning against altering the words of prophecy, John's prayer "Come, Lord Jesus!", and a blessing of grace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **River and Tree of Life:** Symbolize eternal life and healing flowing from God's presence—a restoration of Eden.
- **Alpha and Omega:** Jesus as the beginning and the end, the first and the last—an expression of his divine eternity.
- **Morning Star:** Jesus as the root and offspring of David, the fulfillment of messianic promises and the light that heralds a new day.
- **"Come!":** The invitation and longing of the Spirit, the bride, and believers for Jesus' return.

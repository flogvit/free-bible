**Main Theme:** Abuse of power and God’s judgment against injustice, but also His grace toward those who humble themselves.

**Content:** King Ahab wants to buy Naboth’s vineyard, but Naboth refuses to sell his ancestral inheritance. Queen Jezebel arranges a judicial murder by having false witnesses accuse Naboth of cursing God and the king, and he is stoned to death. The prophet Elijah proclaims God’s judgment: Ahab’s blood will be licked up by dogs where Naboth’s blood was shed, and Jezebel will be eaten by dogs in Jezreel. When Ahab humbles himself, God postpones the judgment until the time of his son.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Inheritance (נַחֲלָה):** The reason Naboth refused to sell—the land was God’s gift to the family and could not be sold permanently.
- **False witnesses (בְּנֵי־בְלִיַּעַל):** "Men of worthlessness" who were used to condemn an innocent man to death.
- **Dogs (הַכְּלָבִים):** A symbol of shameful judgment—they will lick up Ahab’s blood and eat Jezebel.
- **Humiliation (נִכְנַע):** Ahab’s repentance, which led God to delay the judgment, testifying to His grace.

**Main Theme:** A short, intense prayer for God's swift help against enemies.

**Content:** The psalmist cries out to God to hurry and save him. He prays that those who seek his life may be put to shame and turn back, while those who seek God should rejoice and praise Him. The psalm concludes with the psalmist's confession of his own poverty and helplessness, and a heartfelt plea that God must not delay.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Hurry" (חוּשָׁה):** Repeated twice, emphasizing the desperate urgency of the prayer.
- **Shame and disgrace:** The enemies will experience the opposite of what they intended for the psalmist.
- **"Poor and needy" (עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן):** The psalmist's self-description expressing total dependence on God.
- **"Great is God":** The praise that believers will proclaim when God intervenes.

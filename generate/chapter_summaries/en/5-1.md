**Main Theme:** Moses begins his farewell speech by reminding Israel of their history from Horeb to Kadesh-Barnea, focusing on the spies' rebellion and its consequences.

**Content:** At the Jordan in the land of Moab, Moses recounts how God commanded the people to depart from Horeb toward the promised land. He describes the appointment of leaders and judges, and how twelve spies confirmed that the land was good—but the people grumbled in their tents and accused God of hating them. Because of this rebellion, the entire generation was condemned to die in the wilderness; only Caleb and the children's generation would enter the land, with Joshua as leader. When the people repented and attempted to attack on their own, they were driven back by the Amorites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Horeb:** The mountain where God spoke to Israel and gave them command to break camp toward the promised land.
- **Kadesh-Barnea:** The place where Israel sent spies and where the rebellion against God's command took place.
- **The Spies:** Twelve men who confirmed that the land was good, but the people's fear of the Anakites overcame their trust in God's promise.
- **God's Judgment:** The rebellious generation would die in the wilderness—only Caleb and the children would inherit the land, led by Joshua.

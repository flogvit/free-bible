**Main Theme:** Ritual purity laws for women after childbirth.

**Content:** When a woman gives birth to a boy, she is unclean for seven days, and the boy shall be circumcised on the eighth day. This is followed by a 33-day period of purification. In the case of giving birth to a girl, the period of uncleanness lasts 14 days, followed by 66 days. After the purification period, the woman must bring a one-year-old lamb as a burnt offering and a young pigeon or turtledove as a sin offering to the priest at the tent of meeting; if she cannot afford a lamb, she may offer two turtledoves or two young pigeons.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Uncleanness (טָמְאָה):** A ritual state requiring separation and time before restoration.
- **Purification Period (דְּמֵי טָהֳרָה):** The period during which the woman may not touch holy things or enter the sanctuary.
- **Circumcision (יִמּוֹל):** The covenant sign performed on male infants on the eighth day.
- **Sin Offering and Burnt Offering:** Sacrifices that bring atonement and restore full ritual purity.

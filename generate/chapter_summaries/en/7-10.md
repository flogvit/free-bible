**Main Theme:** Israel's apostasy, God's discipline, and the people's repentance preparing the way for a new judge.

**Content:** After the judges Tola and Jair, Israel again falls into idolatry and serves the gods of seven surrounding nations. God hands them over to the Philistines and Ammonites for 18 years. When Israel cries out to God, He reminds them of past deliverance and initially rejects their plea. But when the people remove their idols and turn back to the Lord, He is moved by their suffering, and the leaders of Gilead seek a commander against the Ammonites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Minor Judges (Tola and Jair):** Two judges who led Israel for a total of 45 years during a relatively peaceful period.
- **Idolatry:** Israel served gods from seven nations—the most extensive apostasy described in Judges.
- **"Go and cry out to the gods you have chosen":** God's sharp rejection shows that apostasy has consequences.
- **"His soul was grieved over the misery of Israel":** God's compassion prevails when the people show genuine repentance.

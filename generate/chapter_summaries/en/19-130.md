**Main Theme:** A prayer from deep distress that moves from personal confession of sin to hope for the redemption of all Israel.

**Content:** The psalmist cries out to the Lord from the depths and asks God to hear his prayer. He acknowledges that no one can stand if God keeps a record of sins, but with God there is forgiveness. With intense longing, the psalmist waits for the Lord—more than watchmen wait for the morning—and urges Israel to hope in the Lord, who will redeem his people from all their sins.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Depths (מַעֲמַקִּים):** Symbolizes extreme distress, despair, or the abyss of sin from which the psalmist cries out.
- **Forgiveness (סְלִיחָה):** God's willingness to forgive is the basis for reverence and worship.
- **Wait/Hope (קוה):** Intense, patient expectation toward God and his word, repeated to emphasize the endurance of faith.
- **Faithful Love (חֶסֶד):** God's covenantal, faithful grace that provides a foundation for hope.
- **Redemption (פְדוּת):** God's act of freeing Israel from all sin.

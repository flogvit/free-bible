**Main Theme:** The prophecy of Gog from Magog and God's sovereign judgment against the enemies who attack restored Israel in the last days.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to prophesy against Gog, the prince of Meshech and Tubal, declaring that He Himself will bring Gog and a great coalition of nations against Israel. The attack comes when Israel dwells securely in their rebuilt land. God will judge Gog with earthquakes, pestilence, bloodshed, torrential rain, hail, fire, and sulfur. Through this judgment, God will reveal His holiness and power so that all nations may know that He is the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gog from Magog:** A future hostile ruler from the north who leads a coalition against Israel.
- **Hooks in his jaws (v.4):** An image of God's sovereign control—He directs even the enemy toward judgment.
- **A cloud covering the land (vv.9, 16):** Describes the overwhelming invading force.
- **Fire and sulfur (v.22):** Instruments of God's judgment that recall Sodom, demonstrating His power over the forces of nature.
- **God's self-revelation (v.23):** The primary purpose of these events—that the nations may know the Lord.

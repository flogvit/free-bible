**Main Theme:** Israel's apostasy with the golden calf and Moses' intercession.

**Content:** While Moses is on the mountain, the people pressure Aaron to make a golden calf that they worship with celebration and sacrifice. God intends to destroy the people, but Moses intercedes and reminds God of the promises made to the fathers, and God relents. Moses breaks the tablets of the law, destroys the calf, and the Levites kill about 3,000 men. Moses prays again for the people, but God warns of future punishment.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Golden Calf:** A symbol of Israel's unfaithfulness and idolatry while Moses was on the mountain.
- **Broken Tablets:** Moses' response to covenant breaking—the divinely inscribed tablets are shattered at the foot of the mountain.
- **Moses' Intercession:** Moses appeals to God's honor and the promises made to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
- **Stiff-necked People:** God's description of Israel as a stubborn people (עַם־קְשֵׁה־עֹרֶף).

**Main Theme:** God prepares salvation through miraculous births and prophetic promises.

**Content:** Luke begins his gospel by explaining his purpose: to provide Theophilus with an orderly and reliable account. The angel Gabriel first visits the priest Zechariah and announces that his barren wife Elizabeth will give birth to John, who will prepare the way for the Lord. Gabriel then visits the virgin Mary in Nazareth and proclaims that she will conceive by the Holy Spirit and bear Jesus, the Son of God, who will reign on David's throne forever. Mary visits Elizabeth, and both women praise God—Mary through the Magnificat. At John's birth, Zechariah regains his speech and prophesies about God's salvation through the Benedictus.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gabriel:** God's messenger who announces both John's and Jesus' births, connecting heaven and earth.
- **Magnificat (Mary's Song of Praise):** Mary's hymn celebrating God's mighty works and his care for the humble.
- **Benedictus (Zechariah's Song of Praise):** Zechariah's prophetic hymn about God's salvation and John's role as the forerunner.
- **The Holy Spirit:** Active throughout the chapter—filling John from birth, overshadowing Mary, and inspiring Elizabeth and Zechariah.
- **Fulfillment of Promises:** The chapter shows how God fulfills his promises to Abraham and David through these events.

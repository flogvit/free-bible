**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches that true purity comes from the heart, not external traditions, and shows his grace to both Jews and Gentiles.

**Content:** Jesus confronts the Pharisees for prioritizing human traditions over God's commands, teaching that what comes out of the heart makes a person unclean, not what enters the mouth. In the region of Tyre and Sidon, he heals the daughter of a Canaanite woman who demonstrates great faith. By the Sea of Galilee, he heals many sick people and feeds four thousand men (plus women and children) with seven loaves and a few small fish.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Uncleanness of the Heart:** The evil that comes from the heart (evil thoughts, murder, adultery, sexual immorality, theft, false testimony, slander) makes a person unclean, not the failure to wash hands.
- **Blind Guides:** Jesus describes the Pharisees as blind leading the blind—both will fall into a pit.
- **Children's Bread and Dogs:** Jesus uses the image of children and dogs to illustrate the relationship between Israel and the Gentiles, yet he commends the Canaanite woman's faith.
- **Feeding of the Four Thousand:** Jesus demonstrates his care and power by feeding a large crowd with minimal resources.

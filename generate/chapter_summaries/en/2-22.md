**Main Theme:** Laws concerning restitution, property protection, and social justice in the covenant community.

**Content:** The chapter provides rules for compensation in cases of theft and property damage, including the right to self-defense against burglars, double restitution for stolen items, and liability for damage caused by grazing animals or fire. It contains laws regarding entrusted goods and lending, with an oath before the Lord serving as the final decision in disputes. The chapter forbids sorcery, bestiality, and idolatry, while commanding protection for vulnerable groups such as foreigners, widows, and fatherless children. Finally, it gives instructions concerning firstfruits, firstborn sons and animals, along with the call to be a holy people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Double Restitution:** The foundational principle for making amends in cases of theft and fraud (v.3, 6, 8).
- **Oath before the Lord:** A sacred oath used to resolve disputes when there are no witnesses (v.10).
- **The Foreigner (ger):** Israel is to protect foreigners because they themselves were foreigners in Egypt (v.20).
- **The Cloak as Pledge:** A poor person's cloak must be returned before sunset, for God is compassionate (v.25-26).
- **Holy People:** Israel is called to be holy to God, expressed through concrete obedience (v.30).

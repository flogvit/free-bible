**Main Theme:** With Rebekah's help, Jacob obtains the blessing that Isaac intended to give Esau.

**Content:** The old and blind Isaac asks Esau to hunt game and prepare food so that he may bless him before his death. Rebekah overhears this and helps Jacob deceive Isaac by dressing him in his brother's clothes and covering his hands with goat skins. Isaac blesses Jacob with the dew of heaven, the abundance of the earth, and dominion over his brothers. When Esau discovers the deception, he cries out in despair, but Isaac confirms that the blessing stands firm and gives Esau a prophecy that he will serve his brother, yet one day break free. Esau plans to kill Jacob, so Rebekah sends him to her brother Laban in Haran.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Blessing:** Isaac's transfer of God's promises regarding material prosperity and dominion to the next generation.
- **The Deception:** The planned trickery by Jacob and Rebekah using clothes and goat skins to hide Jacob's identity.
- **"The voice is Jacob's, but the hands are Esau's":** Isaac's doubt that nevertheless does not stop him from blessing the wrong son.
- **"Let him also be blessed":** Isaac's confirmation that the blessing is irrevocable, even after the deception is revealed.
- **Esau's Cry:** The loud and bitter outcry expressing the loss of the blessing.

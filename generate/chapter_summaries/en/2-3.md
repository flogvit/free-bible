**Main Theme:** God reveals Himself to Moses in the burning bush and calls him to lead Israel out of Egypt.

**Content:** While Moses was tending the flocks of his father-in-law Jethro, the priest of Midian, God revealed Himself in a bush that burned without being consumed at Mount Horeb. God commanded Moses to remove his sandals because he was standing on holy ground and introduced Himself as the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He had seen the suffering of the Israelites and intended to lead them to a land flowing with milk and honey. When Moses asked for God's name, God answered: "I am who I am" – and sent him to Pharaoh with a promise of miraculous signs that would compel Egypt to release His people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Burning Bush:** God's presence manifested in fire that does not consume – a sign of His holiness and transcendence.
- **Holy Ground:** The place where God meets humanity becomes holy and demands reverence (removing the sandals).
- **"I am who I am":** God's self-revelation expressing His eternal being and faithfulness to the covenant.
- **A Land Flowing with Milk and Honey:** A picture of the promised land – God's concrete promise of deliverance and blessing.

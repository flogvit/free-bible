**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself as the light of the world and the eternal "I am," leading to a confrontation with the Jewish leaders.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the story of the woman caught in adultery, where Jesus challenges her accusers with the words, "Let him who is without sin among you be the first to throw a stone at her" (v.7). Jesus proclaims himself as the light of the world and teaches that the truth will set you free (v.32). In an intense debate with the Jewish leaders, Jesus reveals his divine identity with the words, "Before Abraham was born, I am" (v.58). This leads them to seek to stone him, but Jesus hides himself and leaves the temple.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Light of the world (τὸ φῶς τοῦ κόσμου):** Jesus declares himself as the light that gives life and guides people away from darkness (v.12).
- **The truth will set you free:** Whoever remains in Jesus' word will know the truth that frees them from slavery to sin (v.31-36).
- **"I am" (ἐγὼ εἰμί):** Jesus' use of the divine name demonstrates his eternal existence and unity with the Father (v.24, 28, 58).
- **Children of Abraham:** Jesus challenges the claim of spiritual kinship with Abraham and points out that true belonging is shown through faith and deeds (v.39-44).

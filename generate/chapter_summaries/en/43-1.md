**Main Theme:** John 1 reveals the eternal existence and incarnation of the Word (Logos), as well as the testimony about Jesus as God's Lamb.

**Content:** The prologue (vv. 1–18) declares that the Word was with God from the beginning, is himself God, and that all things were created through him. The Word became flesh and dwelt among us, full of grace and truth. John the Baptist testifies about Jesus as "the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world" and reports that he saw the Spirit descend upon him like a dove. Jesus calls his first disciples—Andrew, Peter, Philip, and Nathanael—and Nathanael confesses him as the Son of God and King of Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Logos (the Word):** The eternal Word who was with God, is God, and became human in Jesus Christ.
- **Light and darkness:** The Word is the light of life that shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it.
- **God's Lamb:** John the Baptist's title for Jesus as the one who takes away the sin of the world.
- **Incarnation:** The Word became flesh (σὰρξ ἐγένετο) and dwelt among people.
- **Testimony (μαρτυρία):** John the Baptist's role as a witness pointing to Christ.

**Main Theme:** A prayer for protection and praise of God’s faithfulness amid mortal danger.

**Content:** David, hiding from Saul in a cave, seeks refuge under God’s wings until the danger passes. He describes his enemies as lions with teeth like spears and tongues like sharp swords, yet trusts that God will send His mercy and faithfulness from heaven. The enemies themselves fall into the grave they dug for David. With a steadfast heart, he praises God among the nations and declares that God’s love reaches to the heavens.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shadow of wings:** A picture of God’s protection and refuge in times of danger.
- **Lions and weapons:** The enemies are described as predators with teeth like spears and tongues like swords—expressions of deadly peril.
- **Net and grave:** The trap set by the enemy into which they themselves fall—demonstrating God’s righteous intervention.
- **Steadfast heart:** David’s unshakable trust in God as the foundation for praise.
- **God’s mercy to the heavens:** God’s love and faithfulness are boundless, extending over all creation.

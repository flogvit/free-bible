**Main Theme:** Paul gives guidance on order in worship, focusing on head coverings and the proper observance of the Lord's Supper.

**Content:** Paul urges the Corinthians to imitate him as he imitates Christ, presenting an order where Christ is the head of man, man is the head of woman, and God is the head of Christ. Women should wear a head covering when they pray or prophesy, while men should not cover their heads—yet both genders are mutually dependent in the Lord. Paul then criticizes their practice at the Lord's Supper, where some eat ahead of time and become full or drunk, while others go hungry. He reminds them of the words of institution and warns that unworthy participation brings judgment—as a result, many are weak and sick, and some have died.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Head (κεφαλή):** Used both literally (head covering) and figuratively (authority/source) to show relationships between God, Christ, man, and woman.
- **Lord's Supper (κυριακὸν δεῖπνον):** The communal meal in remembrance of Jesus' death, which should be observed worthily and in unity.
- **The new covenant (καινὴ διαθήκη):** The cup represents the new covenant in Jesus' blood, proclaiming his death until he comes.
- **Self-examination (δοκιμαζέτω):** Each person should examine themselves before participating in the Lord's Supper to avoid judgment.

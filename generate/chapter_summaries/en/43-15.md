**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches the disciples to remain in him as branches in the vine, to love one another, and to prepare themselves for the world's hatred.

**Content:** Jesus presents himself as the true vine with the Father as the vinedresser, who prunes the branches so they bear more fruit. He urges the disciples to remain in him, for without him they can do nothing. The commandment to love is central: they are to love one another just as Jesus has loved them—the greatest love is to give one's life for friends. Jesus warns that the world will hate and persecute them because they do not belong to the world, but the Advocate (the Spirit of Truth) will testify about him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Vine and the Branches:** A picture of the life-giving connection between Jesus and his disciples—without him they can bear no fruit.
- **Fruit:** The result of remaining in Jesus; the Father is glorified when the disciples bear much fruit.
- **The Commandment to Love:** Jesus' command to love one another as he has loved them, including giving one's life for friends.
- **Friends, Not Servants:** Jesus calls his disciples friends because he has made known to them everything he heard from the Father.
- **The Advocate (παράκλητος):** The Spirit of Truth whom the Father sends to testify about Jesus.

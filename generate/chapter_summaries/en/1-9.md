**Main Theme:** God establishes a covenant with Noah and all creation, using the rainbow as a sign of His promise never again to destroy the earth with a flood.

**Content:** God blesses Noah and his sons, giving them authority over animals and permission to eat meat, but forbids eating blood. Human life is strictly protected because humans are created in God's image. God establishes His covenant with all living things and gives the rainbow as an eternal sign of the covenant. Noah plants a vineyard, becomes drunk, and Ham sees his father's nakedness and tells his brothers—this leads to a curse on Canaan and blessings on Shem and Japheth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rainbow (קֶשֶׁת):** God's visible sign of the covenant that a flood will never again destroy the earth.
- **Blood (דָּם):** Represents life; it is forbidden to eat, and human blood demands retribution.
- **Image of God (צֶלֶם אֱלֹהִים):** The foundation for the sanctity of human life and the prohibition against murder.
- **Curse on Canaan:** The consequence of Ham's action toward his father; Canaan shall be "a servant of servants" to his brothers.

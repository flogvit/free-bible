**Main Theme:** David is anointed king over all Israel and establishes Jerusalem as the capital, supported by his mighty warriors.

**Content:** All Israel gathers in Hebron and recognizes David as their leader, and he is anointed king according to the Lord's word through Samuel. David then conquers Jerusalem (Zion) from the Jebusites, and Joab becomes commander of the army after leading the attack. The chapter lists David's heroes and their deeds, including the episode where three men risk their lives to fetch water from Bethlehem—water that David pours out before the Lord because it represents their blood.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Fortress of Zion (מְצֻדַת צִיּוֹן):** Jerusalem is conquered and becomes "the City of David," the future center of Israel's kingdom.
- **Anointing according to the Lord's word:** David's kingship is not merely political but fulfills God's promise given through the prophet Samuel.
- **The Three Heroes:** Symbolize extraordinary loyalty; David's response shows his reverence for God and appreciation for the lives of his men.
- **The Lord of Hosts was with him:** Verse 9 emphasizes that David's growth was due to God's presence and support.

**Main Theme:** All human striving is futile without the Lord's blessing and cooperation.

**Content:** This pilgrimage psalm, attributed to Solomon, teaches that building a house and guarding a city are useless without God. Restless labor from early morning until late is meaningless, for the Lord gives His beloved sleep. Children are highlighted as God's gift and inheritance, and many sons bring strength and honor to the family.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **House and City:** Symbols of human endeavor and security that require God's cooperation.
- **Sleep/Rest:** A gift from God to His beloved, in contrast to anxious striving.
- **Children as Inheritance:** Children are a blessing and reward from the Lord.
- **Arrows in a Warrior's Hand:** An image representing sons who provide strength and protection.
- **City Gate:** The place of public affairs where a man with many sons stands secure.

**Main Theme:** Moses repeats the Ten Commandments and confirms the covenant between God and the new generation of Israel at Horeb.

**Content:** Moses gathers the people and reminds them that God spoke to them face to face from the fire on the mountain, and that the covenant applies to those living now, not only to their ancestors. He recounts the Ten Commandments, from having no other gods to not coveting. Because the people feared the great fire, they asked Moses to serve as a mediator and receive God's words on their behalf. God approved this and expressed the desire that the people would have a heart that fears Him and keeps His commandments all days.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Horeb Covenant:** The covenant God made with Israel, which Moses emphasizes applies to the current generation, not only to the forefathers.
- **The Fire:** God's presence manifested in fire on the mountain, which both revealed His glory and created fear among the people.
- **Mediator:** Moses' role as intermediary between God and the people, established because the people feared hearing God's voice directly.
- **The Ten Commandments:** God's foundational laws for the covenant people, including the Sabbath commandment, which is here grounded in deliverance from Egypt.

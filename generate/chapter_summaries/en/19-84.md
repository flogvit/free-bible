**Main Theme:** A longing praise of God's holy dwelling and the blessedness of being in His presence.

**Content:** The psalmist expresses a deep yearning for the Lord's courts, where even the sparrow and the swallow find shelter. Blessed are those who dwell in God's house and those who have their strength in Him as they journey on pilgrimage through the Valley of Baca toward Zion. The psalm declares that one day in God's courts is better than a thousand elsewhere, and that the Lord grants grace and glory to those who walk blamelessly.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's dwellings (מִשְׁכְּנוֹת):** The temple as the beloved place of God's presence and worship.
- **Valley of Baca (עֵמֶק הַבָּכָא):** The pilgrims' path through difficult places that is transformed into a blessing.
- **Sun and shield (שֶׁמֶשׁ וּמָגֵן):** God as the life-giver and protector of His people.
- **From strength to strength (מֵחַיִל אֶל־חָיִל):** The pilgrims' growth in strength on the way toward God.

**Main Theme:** A prophecy concerning the fall of Tyre and its future restoration under the Lord's sovereignty.

**Content:** The prophecy declares destruction over Tyre and Sidon, the great trading cities by the sea. The ships of Tarshish are called to lament, for their harbor is destroyed. The Lord of Hosts has planned this to humble all human pride and glory. After 70 years of being forgotten, Tyre will rise again as a trading city, but its income will ultimately be dedicated to the Lord and serve those who worship Him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ships of Tarshish:** Symbolize Tyre's maritime trade power, now in ruins.
- **70 years:** The period during which Tyre will lie forgotten, like a king's lifespan.
- **The harlot's song:** A picture of how Tyre, after 70 years, will seek to regain its position among the nations.
- **Holy to the Lord:** Tyre's future trade will not be hoarded but will benefit God's people.

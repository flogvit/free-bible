**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about forgiveness, faith, service, and the coming of God's kingdom.

**Content:** Jesus warns against causing others to stumble and encourages repeated forgiveness when someone repents. He heals ten lepers, but only one—a Samaritan—returns to give thanks, and Jesus commends his faith. When asked about God's kingdom, Jesus answers that it does not come visibly, but is "among you." He compares the day of the Son of Man to the days of Noah and Lot: people live carefree until judgment suddenly comes, and whoever tries to save their life will lose it.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mustard Seed:** Even minimal faith can make the impossible possible.
- **The Ten Lepers:** Illustrates the importance of gratitude—only one out of ten returned.
- **The Days of Noah and Lot:** Images of unexpected judgment in the midst of daily routines.
- **Lot's Wife:** A warning against looking back and clinging to the old life.

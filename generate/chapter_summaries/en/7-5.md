**Main Theme:** A song of praise that honors the Lord for victory over the Canaanites and celebrates those who fought for Israel.

**Content:** Deborah and Barak sing this song after their victory over Sisera. The song opens with a powerful description of God's appearance from Seir, where the earth shook and the heavens poured down rain. It praises the tribes that willingly came to battle (Zebulun, Naphtali, Ephraim, Benjamin, Machir, Issachar), but sharply criticizes those who stayed away (Reuben, Dan, Asher, Gilead). Jael is praised as the most blessed among women because she killed Sisera, while Meroz is cursed for not coming to help. The song concludes with a prayer that all God's enemies may perish, and the land had rest for forty years.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Voluntary Service (התנדב):** The people are praised for willingly offering themselves in battle against the enemy (vv. 2, 9).
- **Theophany from Seir:** God's mighty appearance is described with earthquakes and heavenly signs (vv. 4–5).
- **Sisera's Mother at the Window:** A moving picture of the enemies' expectation of victory that never comes (vv. 28–30).
- **Jael – Blessed Among Women:** She is praised for killing Sisera with a tent peg and hammer (vv. 24–27).
- **The Sun in Its Strength:** Those who love the Lord are compared to the sun when it rises in its power (v. 31).

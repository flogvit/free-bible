**Main Theme:** The Holy Spirit sends Barnabas and Paul on the first missionary journey, where the gospel is proclaimed to both Jews and Gentiles.

**Content:** The church in Antioch sends out Barnabas and Saul (Paul) under the guidance of the Holy Spirit. On Cyprus, Paul confronts the sorcerer Elymas, and the proconsul Sergius Paulus comes to faith. In Antioch of Pisidia, Paul delivers a speech in the synagogue, proclaiming Jesus as the fulfillment of God’s promises and offering forgiveness of sins through him. When Jewish leaders reject the message, Paul and Barnabas turn to the Gentiles, and many come to believe.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sending out (ἀφορίσατε):** The Holy Spirit calls, and the church sends out—the mission has a divine origin.
- **Descendant of David:** Paul shows how Jesus is the promised Savior from David’s line.
- **Forgiveness of sins (ἄφεσις ἁμαρτιῶν):** Central to the gospel—what the law could not give, Jesus gives to all who believe.
- **Light for the nations:** The prophecy from Isaiah is fulfilled as the gospel goes to the Gentiles after Jewish rejection.

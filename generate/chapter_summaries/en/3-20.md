**Main Theme:** The chapter establishes the death penalty and other severe consequences for idolatry and sexual sins, grounding this in Israel's calling to be a holy people.

**Content:** The chapter begins with a prohibition against offering children as sacrifices to the idol Molech, and continues with penalties for spiritism and various sexual sins such as adultery, incest, and homosexuality. The punishments include stoning, burning, and being cut off from the people. God calls Israel to holiness and separation from other nations, warning that disobedience will cause the land to "spew them out."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Molech:** A Canaanite idol to whom children were sacrificed; such worship profaned God's sanctuary.
- **"Their blood is upon them":** An expression meaning that the guilty person bears responsibility for their own death.
- **Holiness:** Israel's calling to be set apart for God, grounded in the fact that God Himself is holy.
- **The land spews out:** A figurative warning that disobedience leads to exile from the promised land.

**Main Theme:** The Levites receive 48 cities with pasturelands from the other tribes, in accordance with the Lord’s command to Moses.

**Content:** The heads of the Levitical families came to Eleazar, Joshua, and the heads of the tribes at Shiloh and requested cities to live in, just as the Lord had commanded. By casting lots, the three Levitical clans—Kehath (including Aaron’s descendants), Gershon, and Merari—received a total of 48 cities with pasturelands from all the tribes. Several of these cities were cities of refuge. The chapter concludes by affirming that the Lord fulfilled all His promises: Israel received the land, rest from their enemies, and not one word of God’s good promises failed to come true.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Levitical Cities (48 cities):** Unlike the other tribes, the Levites did not receive a territorial inheritance but were given cities scattered throughout Israel to serve the people.
- **Casting Lots:** The distribution was determined by lot, demonstrating that God directed the allocation.
- **Cities of Refuge:** Several of the Levitical cities served as places of asylum for those who had killed someone unintentionally.
- **Fulfillment of Promises:** This chapter confirms that God kept all His promises to the fathers—the land, victory, and rest.

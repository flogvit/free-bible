**Main Theme:** A festival psalm that calls Israel to praise, recalls God's deliverance, and exhorts obedience to Him alone.

**Content:** The psalm opens with an invitation to festive worship using music and the shofar during the appointed feast. God speaks, reminding them how He delivered Israel from Egypt and answered them at Meriba. He commands that they have no foreign gods, but Israel did not listen and followed their own ways. The psalm concludes with God's longing: if His people would only listen, He would crush their enemies and satisfy them with the finest wheat and honey from the rock.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shofar at the new moon:** Symbolizes festival celebration and the call to gather before God (v.4).
- **Burden lifted from the shoulder:** A picture of deliverance from slavery in Egypt (v.7).
- **Meriba:** The place where God tested Israel and answered them in their distress (v.8).
- **No foreign god:** The heart of the covenant relationship—faithfulness to the Lord alone (v.10).
- **Honey from the rock:** A picture of God's abundant blessings through obedience (v.17).

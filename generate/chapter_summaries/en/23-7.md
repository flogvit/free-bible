**Main Theme:** God offers King Ahaz a sign of deliverance in the midst of crisis, but the king’s unbelief leads to both promise and judgment.

**Content:** King Ahaz of Judah fears the attack from Syria and Israel, but Isaiah conveys God’s message that the enemies will not succeed. When Ahaz refuses to ask for a sign from God, the Lord still gives the Immanuel sign: a young woman shall bear a son, and before he grows up, the lands of the enemies will lie desolate. At the same time, the prophet warns that Assyria will become an instrument of judgment against Judah itself.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Immanuel ("God with us"):** The sign of God’s presence and promise of protection in the midst of crisis.
- **Shear-Jashub ("a remnant shall return"):** Isaiah’s son’s name points to hope for a faithful remnant that will survive judgment.
- **Two smoking firebrands:** A picture of Syria and Israel as burned-out and powerless threats.
- **Assyria’s razor:** A symbol of the coming judgment that will strike the land when God uses Assyria as His instrument.

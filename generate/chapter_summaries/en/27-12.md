**Main Theme:** Prophecies about the end times with promises of resurrection, judgment, and salvation for God’s people.

**Content:** The archangel Michael will arise to protect God’s people during an unprecedented time of distress. Many will rise from the dead—some to eternal life, others to everlasting shame. Those who are wise and those who lead many to righteousness will shine like the stars. Daniel is instructed to seal the words of the book until the end times and receives the promise that he will rest and stand up for his allotted portion at the end of days.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Michael:** The archangel who protects Israel during the great tribulation.
- **Resurrection:** The dead will awaken—to eternal life or everlasting contempt.
- **The wise (maskilim):** Those with insight will shine like the sky and the stars.
- **Sealed book:** The prophecies are to remain closed until the end times.
- **End of days:** Daniel is promised rest and resurrection to his allotted portion.

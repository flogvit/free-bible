**Main Theme:** Jeremiah laments over Judah’s lies and betrayal, and God announces judgment against the people who have abandoned His law.

**Content:** The prophet longs to flee from a people marked by deceit, fraud, and faithlessness (vv. 1–5). God will refine them like metal in a furnace and make Jerusalem into heaps of ruins (vv. 6–11). The reason is that they abandoned God’s law and followed the Baal gods (vv. 12–13). A lamenting woman is called to mourn over the coming disaster, through which death will enter even through the windows (vv. 16–21). The chapter concludes with the truth that genuine boasting lies in knowing God, and that both Israel and the nations are uncircumcised in heart (vv. 22–25).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The tongue as a drawn bow (v. 2):** The people’s words are like deadly weapons—they shoot lies and deceit at one another.
- **The furnace (v. 6):** God will test and purify the people through suffering, just as metal is refined in fire.
- **Death entering through the windows (v. 20):** A powerful image of how judgment will strike everyone, even those who think themselves safe.
- **Circumcision of the heart (v. 25):** External religious signs are worthless without inner devotion—Israel is as uncircumcised as the nations.

**Main Theme:** God's sovereign election and Israel's situation in salvation history.

**Content:** Paul expresses deep sorrow for his Jewish fellow countrymen who have not accepted Christ. He explains that true Israel is not defined by ethnic descent, but by God's promise and election—illustrated through Isaac, Jacob, and Esau. God has the sovereign right to show mercy to whom he wills, and his plan includes both Jews and Gentiles. Israel stumbled because they sought righteousness through works rather than through faith.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Election (ἐκλογή):** God's free choice, not based on human works but on his own will.
- **The Potter:** A picture of God's sovereign right to shape his creatures for honor or dishonor.
- **Children of the Promise:** Those who belong to God through the promise, not through physical descent.
- **The Stone of Stumbling:** Christ, whom Israel stumbled over because they sought righteousness through works instead of faith.

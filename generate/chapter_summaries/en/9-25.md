**Main Theme:** Abigail’s wisdom averts David’s vengeance, and God judges the foolish Nabal.

**Content:** After Samuel’s death, David sends men to the wealthy Nabal to request provisions as thanks for protecting his shepherds, but Nabal contemptuously rejects them. David swears revenge, but Nabal’s wise wife Abigail meets him with gifts and a humble speech that persuades him to refrain from bloodshed. God strikes Nabal ten days later, and David takes Abigail as his wife, along with Ahinoam from Jezreel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Nabal ("fool"):** His name reflects his character—hard and evil in his deeds, one who repays good with evil.
- **Abigail:** A wise and beautiful woman who acts quickly and humbly to prevent bloodshed and protect her household.
- **"A wall around us":** The shepherds’ description of David’s men as their protection—the basis for David’s rightful expectation.
- **God’s judgment:** The Lord Himself strikes Nabal—David need not take vengeance with his own hand.

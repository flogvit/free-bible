**Main Theme:** Ahab's death in war against Aram after ignoring the true prophet Micaiah's warning.

**Content:** King Ahab of Israel and King Jehoshaphat of Judah plan a war against Aram to retake Ramoth-Gilead. While 400 prophets promise victory, the prophet Micaiah warns of defeat and reveals that God has sent a lying spirit into the other prophets. Ahab disguises himself in battle but is still struck by a random arrow and dies – dogs lick up his blood, just as the Lord's word had said. The chapter concludes with notes on Jehoshaphat's godly rule in Judah and Ahaziah's wicked reign in Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lying Spirit:** God permits a spirit to deceive Ahab's prophets in order to bring him to judgment.
- **Disguise:** Ahab tries to avoid his fate by disguising himself, but God's judgment cannot be escaped.
- **Dogs Licking the Blood:** The fulfillment of prophecy demonstrates God's faithfulness to His word.
- **Ramoth-Gilead:** A strategic city that becomes the site of Ahab's final defeat.

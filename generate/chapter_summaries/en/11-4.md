**Main Theme:** Solomon's administrative organization of Israel through officials and district governors.

**Content:** The chapter lists Solomon's highest officials, including priests, scribes, commanders of the army, and other court positions. Solomon divided the kingdom into twelve districts, each with a governor responsible for supplying the king and his household one month per year. The text concludes by describing the prosperity of the people: Judah and Israel were numerous and lived in joy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Twelve District Governors (נִצָּבִים):** Administrative leaders who ensured a steady supply to the royal house throughout the year.
- **Officials (שָׂרִים):** High-ranking servants who carried out various functions in the kingdom.
- **As numerous as the sand by the sea:** A picture of Israel's large population and the fulfillment of God's promise to Abraham.
- **They ate, drank, and were glad:** An expression of peace, prosperity, and blessing under Solomon's rule.

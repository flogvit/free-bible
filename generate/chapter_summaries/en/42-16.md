**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about the proper use of wealth and warns against greed through two parables.

**Content:** The chapter begins with the parable of the dishonest manager, who uses his master's resources wisely to secure his future—Jesus encourages disciples to use worldly resources for eternal purposes. He emphasizes that one cannot serve both God and Mammon. The Pharisees, who were lovers of money, mocked him. Jesus concludes with the story of the rich man and Lazarus, showing that earthly wealth does not guarantee eternal salvation, and that God's word (Moses and the prophets) is sufficient guidance for salvation.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mammon (μαμωνᾶς):** An Aramaic term for wealth or money—Jesus warns against letting material values become one's master.
- **Abraham's bosom (κόλπον Ἀβραάμ):** A picture of the blessed rest and fellowship with the faithful fathers after death.
- **The great chasm (χάσμα μέγα):** Symbolizes the insurmountable divide between salvation and destruction—the choice must be made in this life.
- **Moses and the prophets:** God's revealed word, which is sufficient to lead people to repentance.

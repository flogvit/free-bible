**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts the Corinthians to live in a manner worthy of God’s grace and to remain separate from unbelievers.

**Content:** Paul urges the Corinthians not to receive God’s grace in vain, but to seize the day of salvation. He describes the characteristics of apostolic ministry through a series of paradoxes: poor yet making many rich; sorrowful yet always rejoicing. With an open heart, he appeals to the Corinthians for mutual openness. The chapter concludes with a strong warning against being yoked together with unbelievers, and promises that God will be their Father if they keep themselves separate from uncleanness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Day of salvation (ἡμέρα σωτηρίας):** Quoted from Isaiah – the present time is the decisive moment to respond to God’s grace.
- **Unequal yoke (ἑτεροζυγοῦντες):** Image from Deuteronomy 22:10 – believers should not bind themselves together with unbelievers in incompatible partnerships.
- **Temple of God (ναὸς θεοῦ):** Believers are God’s dwelling place, which requires holiness and separation from idolatry.
- **Beliar:** Jewish designation for Satan or evil – highlights the absolute opposition between Christ and the powers of darkness.

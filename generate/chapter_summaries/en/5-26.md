**Main Theme:** The chapter describes the rituals for the firstfruits offering and tithes, as well as the mutual covenant declaration between God and Israel.

**Content:** When the Israelites enter the land, they are to bring the firstfruits to the sanctuary and recite a historical confession that begins with "a wandering Aramean was my father" – a summary of Israel's history from the time of the patriarchs through Egypt to deliverance. Every third year, the tithe is to be given to Levites, foreigners, orphans, and widows. The chapter concludes with a mutual covenant declaration: Israel acknowledges the Lord as their God, and the Lord declares Israel to be His treasured possession (עַם סְגֻלָּה).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Firstfruits (רֵאשִׁית):** The first and best portion of the harvest, given to God in recognition that the land is His gift.
- **"A wandering Aramean":** The opening of the historical confession, recalling Jacob's uncertain life and God's faithfulness.
- **Treasured possession (עַם סְגֻלָּה):** Israel as God's chosen people, called to holiness and obedience.
- **A land flowing with milk and honey:** A description of the promised land's fertility and abundance.

**Main Theme:** Nehemiah receives news of Jerusalem's destruction and turns to God in prayer and fasting.

**Content:** Nehemiah, who serves as cupbearer to the Persian king in Susa, hears from his brother Hanani that the returned Jews are living in distress and disgrace, and that Jerusalem's walls and gates lie in ruins. Overcome with grief, Nehemiah fasts and prays to the God of heaven. He confesses Israel's sins, reminds God of His promise to gather His scattered people, and asks for favor from the king so he can help.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Walls and Gates:** A symbol of Jerusalem's vulnerability and disgrace—the city lies unprotected and humiliated.
- **Fasting and Prayer:** Nehemiah's response shows deep dependence on God before taking action.
- **Confession:** Nehemiah identifies with the people's sin ("we have sinned") and takes collective responsibility.
- **God of Heaven:** An expression of God's sovereignty over all kingdoms, including Persia.

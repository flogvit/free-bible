**Main Theme:** A lament in which David, in deep distress, cries out to God as his only refuge and asks for deliverance.

**Content:** The psalm was written when David was in a cave, surrounded by enemies and completely isolated. He pours out his complaint to the Lord and acknowledges that no one else cares about him. David confesses God as his refuge and his portion in the land of the living, and prays to be led out of his "prison" so that he may praise God's name together with the righteous.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cave (מְעָרָה):** The concrete situation of hiding and distress that forms the backdrop for the prayer.
- **Refuge (מַחְסִי):** God as David's only safe place of shelter when everyone else has failed him.
- **Prison (מַסְגֵּר):** A picture of the desperate situation from which David seeks deliverance.
- **My portion (חֶלְקִי):** An expression that God is David's inheritance and highest good in the land of the living.

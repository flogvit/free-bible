**Main Theme:** A pilgrimage psalm in which David expresses deep humility and childlike trust in God.

**Content:** David declares that he has put aside pride and ambition, and does not strive for things too great or beyond his understanding. He has brought his soul to rest, like a weaned child who rests contentedly with its mother—no longer demanding, but secure. The psalm concludes with an exhortation to Israel to hope in the Lord from now on and forevermore.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Humility (לֹא־גָבַהּ לִבִּי):** Not having a proud heart or haughty eyes, but accepting one's limitations.
- **Weaned child (גָּמֻל):** The image of a child who no longer demands, but finds rest in the mother's closeness—symbolizing mature, contented trust.
- **Hope (יַחֵל):** Israel's enduring expectation in the Lord, from now on and into eternity.

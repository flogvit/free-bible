**Main Theme:** The Lord’s message of judgment to Jerusalem through the prophet Jeremiah during the Babylonian siege.

**Content:** King Zedekiah sends messengers to Jeremiah seeking the Lord’s help against Nebuchadnezzar. Jeremiah replies that the Lord Himself will fight against Jerusalem in anger and hand the city over to Babylon. The people are given a choice between the way of life and the way of death: those who remain in the city will die, while those who surrender to the Chaldeans will live. The royal house is urged to practice justice and rescue the oppressed; otherwise, God’s wrath will burn like fire.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Way of Life and the Way of Death** – The choice set before the people: surrender means life, resistance means death (vv. 8–9).
- **Outstretched Hand and Mighty Arm** – Expressions usually describing God’s salvation, here used to depict His judgment against His own people (v. 5).
- **Fire** – A symbol of God’s wrath that will consume Jerusalem and its surroundings (vv. 10, 12, 14).
- **Justice in the Morning** – The royal house’s duty to administer just judgments daily (v. 12).

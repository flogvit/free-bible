**Main Theme:** Israel's journey is marked by both rebellion and victory – God punishes complaining, but also grants healing and military success.

**Content:** Israel defeats the Canaanite king of Arad after making a vow to God. The people's complaining leads God to send poisonous snakes, but Moses' bronze snake on a pole brings healing to those who look at it. At the well in Beer, the people sing a song of praise. After peaceful passage is denied, Israel defeats the Amorite kings Sihon and Og and takes their land east of the Jordan.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Bronze Snake:** A symbol of God's salvation through faith – those who looked at it were healed. It points forward to Christ (John 3:14-15).
- **The Poisonous Snakes:** God's judgment on the people's ingratitude and rebellion against Him and Moses.
- **The Song at the Well:** An expression of joy and gratitude for God's care and provision of water in the wilderness.
- **Sihon and Og:** Amorite kings whose defeat marks the beginning of Israel's settlement east of the Jordan.

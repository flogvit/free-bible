**Main Theme:** God uses Assyria as His instrument for judgment, but will also punish their arrogance.

**Content:** The chapter begins with a woe oracle against Israel's unjust leaders who oppress the poor and vulnerable. God calls Assyria "the rod of My anger" and sends them against Israel, but the king of Assyria does not understand his role and boasts in his own power. Therefore, when His work in Jerusalem is completed, God will punish the arrogance of Assyria. A remnant of Israel shall return and rely on the Lord in truth, and the yoke of Assyria shall be broken.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The rod of My anger"** – Assyria is God's instrument for discipline, not an independent power.
- **The axe that boasts** – Illustrates the arrogance of Assyria: can a tool take credit from the craftsman?
- **The remnant that returns (שְׁאָר יָשׁוּב)** – A faithful minority shall turn back to God.
- **Israel's light as a consuming fire** – God Himself becomes a fire that consumes the power of Assyria.

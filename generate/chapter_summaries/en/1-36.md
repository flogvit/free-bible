**Main Theme:** The genealogy of Esau and the establishment of the Edomites as a people in the region of Seir.

**Content:** This chapter lists Esau's three wives and his sons born in Canaan. Esau moved to the mountains of Seir because the land could not support both him and Jacob due to their great wealth. It then follows with detailed genealogies of Esau's descendants, the original clans of the Horites in Seir, and the eight kings who reigned in Edom before Israel had kings.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Edom:** Esau's other name, meaning "red," which became the name of his descendants' people and land.
- **Chiefs (alufim):** Clan leaders who ruled over the various family branches in Edom, an early form of political organization.
- **Seir:** The mountainous region southeast of the Dead Sea where Esau settled, which became the homeland of the Edomites.
- **Horites:** The original inhabitants of the land of Seir before the Edomites took over the area.

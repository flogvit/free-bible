**Main Theme:** Isaiah warns against seeking help from Egypt instead of trusting in the Lord, and promises God's protection of Jerusalem and judgment upon Assyria.

**Content:** The prophet criticizes Israel's reliance on Egypt's horses and chariots, reminding them that Egypt is merely human and not God. The Lord promises to protect Jerusalem as a bird guards its young. The people are urged to repent and reject idols. Assyria will fall by a sword that is not of human origin.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Horses and Chariots:** A symbol of human military power on which Israel mistakenly relies instead of God.
- **Flesh, Not Spirit:** A contrast between Egypt's limited human strength and God's infinite spiritual power.
- **Birds That Guard:** An image of the Lord's tender protection over Jerusalem.
- **A Sword That Is Not Human:** God's direct intervention against Assyria, without the use of earthly weapons.

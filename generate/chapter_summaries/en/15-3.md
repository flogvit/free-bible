**Main Theme:** The restoration of worship in Jerusalem through the building of the altar, offerings, and laying the foundation of the new temple.

**Content:** When the seventh month arrived, the people gathered in Jerusalem, where Joshua and Zerubbabel built the altar and resumed sacrifices, including the Feast of Tabernacles. In the second year, the foundation of the temple was laid, and priests and Levites led worship with trumpets and cymbals. While many shouted for joy, older people who remembered the first temple wept, so that the sound of rejoicing and weeping mingled and could be heard far away.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Altar:** The first thing to be rebuilt, symbolizing the restoration of the relationship between God and His people through sacrifice.
- **The Seventh Month:** The time of gathering, connected to the Feast of Tabernacles and other holy festivals.
- **The Temple Foundation:** Its laying marks the beginning of the new temple and stirs strong emotions.
- **Weeping and Rejoicing:** The weeping of the elders mixed with the shouts of joy from the younger generation—an image of the tension between loss and hope.

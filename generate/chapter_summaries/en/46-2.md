**Main Theme:** Paul shows that God's wisdom, revealed through the Spirit, stands in contrast to human wisdom and is understood only by those who have the mind of Christ.

**Content:** Paul came to the Corinthians with humility and did not preach with persuasive words of wisdom, but with the power of the Spirit, so that their faith would rest on God's power. He preached only Jesus Christ, and him crucified. God's hidden wisdom, predetermined before the beginning of time, is inaccessible to the rulers of this world, but revealed to believers through the Holy Spirit. The natural person does not accept what belongs to the Spirit, but the spiritual person judges all things—for believers have the mind of Christ.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's wisdom in mystery (σοφίαν ἐν μυστηρίῳ):** The hidden plan of salvation that God predetermined before the ages for the glory of believers.
- **Revelation by the Spirit:** The Holy Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God, and makes God's gifts known to believers.
- **The natural person (ψυχικὸς ἄνθρωπος):** A person without the Spirit who cannot understand spiritual truths because they are judged spiritually.
- **The mind of Christ (νοῦν Χριστοῦ):** Believers have shared in Christ's way of thinking and understanding through the Spirit.

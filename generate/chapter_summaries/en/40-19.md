**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about radical discipleship through God's will for marriage, the value of children, and the dangers of wealth.

**Content:** Jesus travels to Judea and teaches on the inviolability of marriage from creation—divorce is permitted only in cases of sexual immorality. He blesses the children and declares that the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as them. A rich young man asks about eternal life, but goes away sorrowful when Jesus tells him to sell everything and follow Him. Jesus warns that it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich person to enter God's kingdom, but adds: "For with God all things are possible." To the disciples He promises thrones and a hundredfold reward for those who leave everything for His sake.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **One flesh (σὰρξ μία):** Marriage unites man and woman into one unity that people should not separate.
- **The kingdom of heaven belongs to children:** Children represent the humble trust required to receive God's kingdom.
- **Camel through the eye of a needle:** A hyperbolic image illustrating how difficult it is for the rich to enter God's kingdom due to the grip of wealth.
- **Regeneration (παλιγγενεσία):** The coming renewal when the Son of Man will sit on His glorious throne.

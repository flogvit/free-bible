**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts the Thessalonians to holy living and comforts them with the hope of Christ's return.

**Content:** The chapter begins with an exhortation to live a holy life, abstain from sexual immorality, and treat one another justly, for God has called us to purity. Paul commends their brotherly love but encourages them to grow even more, live quiet lives, and work with their own hands. He then addresses the question concerning those who have died in Christ: at the Lord's return, the dead will rise first, after which we who are alive will be caught up together with them to meet the Lord in the air. They are encouraged to comfort one another with this hope.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sanctification (ἁγιασμός):** God's will for believers, expressed through sexual purity and righteous living.
- **Brotherly love (φιλαδελφία):** The Thessalonians have been taught by God to love one another and demonstrate this throughout Macedonia.
- **Those who have fallen asleep (κοιμωμένων):** A picture of the dead in Christ who will be raised at the Lord's return.
- **The Lord's coming (παρουσία):** The Lord will descend with a command, the voice of an archangel, and the trumpet of God.
- **Being caught up (ἁρπαγησόμεθα):** Believers will be caught up in clouds to meet the Lord in the air.

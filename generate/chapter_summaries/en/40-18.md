**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about humility, care for the little ones, conflict resolution in the church, and limitless forgiveness.

**Content:** The chapter opens with Jesus placing a child in their midst and teaching that whoever humbles themselves like this child is greatest in the kingdom of heaven. He strongly warns against causing any of "these little ones" to stumble. Jesus gives guidelines for resolving conflicts: first a private conversation between two people, then involving witnesses, and finally bringing it before the church. The chapter concludes with the parable of the unforgiving servant who had an enormous debt forgiven but refused to forgive his fellow servant—and thus lost his own forgiveness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Child:** A symbol of humility and trust; the attitude required to enter God's kingdom.
- **The Lost Sheep:** Illustrates God's desire to seek out the one who has wandered away; none of these little ones should be lost.
- **Binding and Loosing:** The church's authority to act on God's behalf in matters concerning forgiveness and discipline.
- **Seventy Times Seven:** An expression of limitless forgiveness, not a specific number, but an attitude of boundless grace.
- **The Unforgiving Servant:** Shows that those who have received God's forgiveness must forgive their neighbor from the heart.

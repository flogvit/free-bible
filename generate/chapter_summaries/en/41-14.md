**Main Theme:** Jesus' final hours before the crucifixion—from the anointing in Bethany to the trial before the Council.

**Content:** Religious leaders plot to kill Jesus, while a woman in Bethany anoints him with costly nard ointment as preparation for his burial. Jesus celebrates the Passover meal with the twelve and institutes the Lord's Supper with bread and wine as symbols of his body and blood. In Gethsemane, Jesus prays in deep distress but submits to the Father's will. Judas betrays Jesus with a kiss, and he is taken to the high priest where he confesses to being the Messiah and is condemned for blasphemy, while Peter denies knowing him three times.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Nard Ointment:** The costly ointment symbolizes devotion and prepares Jesus' body for burial.
- **The Bread and the Cup:** Jesus institutes the Lord's Supper as a sign of the new covenant in his blood, poured out for many.
- **Gethsemane:** The place where Jesus struggles in prayer, revealing both his humanity and obedience to the Father's will.
- **The Rooster's Crow:** Peter's three denials before the rooster crows twice fulfill Jesus' prophecy and reveal human weakness.

**Main Theme:** David is sent away from the Philistine army before the war against Israel.

**Content:** The Philistines gather at Aphek to wage war against Israel. David's presence among the Philistines arouses distrust among the commanders, who fear he will turn against them in battle. Despite King Achish's defense of David, he must yield to the demands of the commanders and sends David back in peace. David and his men return to Philistine territory while the army marches toward Jezreel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Philistine commanders (seranim):** The five rulers who governed the Philistine cities and had the authority to override even King Achish.
- **Satan/adversary:** The commanders' fear that David would become an enemy from within during the battle.
- **"Saul has slain his thousands, and David his ten thousands":** The famous song recalling David's earlier victories over the Philistines.
- **God's angel:** Achish's description of David as an expression of highest trust and recognition.

**Main Theme:** God's unfailing commitment to Jerusalem's restoration and glory.

**Content:** The prophet promises not to remain silent until Jerusalem's righteousness shines forth and its salvation burns like a torch. The city will receive new names—"Hephzibah" (my delight is in her) and "Beulah" (married)—as signs of God's love. Watchmen are placed on the walls to cry out continually to the Lord until Jerusalem becomes the praise of the earth. God swears to protect the people's harvest from enemies, and it is proclaimed that a highway must be cleared for the people returning home.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Crown and diadem (v.3):** Jerusalem as a splendid crown in the Lord's hand—expressing the city's dignity before God.
- **Watchmen on the walls (vv.6–7):** Those who remind the Lord of His promises and do not remain silent—an image of persistent intercession.
- **New name (vv.2,4):** Hephzibah and Beulah replace "forsaken" and "desolate"—symbolizing a total transformation of Israel's status.
- **The banner/sign (v.10):** A sign lifted for the nations—a call to gather and return home.

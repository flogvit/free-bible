**Main Theme:** God confirms His eternal covenant with Abraham, establishing circumcision as the sign of the covenant and promising land and numerous descendants.

**Content:** God reveals Himself to the 99-year-old Abram as El Shaddai and commands him to walk before His face and be blameless. Abram receives the name Abraham ("father of a multitude of nations"), and Sarai becomes Sarah. God promises that Sarah will bear Isaac within a year, and that the covenant will continue through him, while Ishmael is blessed to become the father of twelve princes and a great nation. Circumcision is instituted as an eternal sign of the covenant for all males on the eighth day, and any uncircumcised male shall be cut off from his people. Abraham obeys immediately and circumcises his entire household that same day.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **El Shaddai (אֵל שַׁדַּי):** God's name emphasizing His almighty power to fulfill the promises.
- **Name Change:** Abram becomes Abraham and Sarai becomes Sarah—new names mark a new identity and calling within the covenant.
- **Circumcision (בְּרִית מִילָה):** A physical sign of the covenant in the flesh, performed on the eighth day, setting God's people apart.
- **Eternal Covenant and Land Promise:** The covenant includes the promise of the land of Canaan as an eternal possession for Abraham and his descendants.

**Main Theme:** The murder of Gedaliah and the chaos that followed Jerusalem's fall.

**Content:** Ishmael, a member of the royal family, murders Gedaliah, whom the Babylonians had appointed as governor, along with Jews and Babylonians who were with him. He also kills 70 out of 80 pilgrims who had come with offerings for the temple and takes the rest of the people captive toward Ammon. Johanan and his men pursue Ishmael, rescue the captives, but Ishmael escapes. The people settle near Bethlehem with plans to flee to Egypt out of fear of Babylonian revenge.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ishmael son of Nethaniah:** A royal descendant who carried out a treacherous coup against the Babylonian-supported leadership.
- **Gedaliah:** The governor who was killed despite warnings, symbolizing the last hope for stability in Judah.
- **The Pilgrims:** 80 mourning men lured into an ambush, illustrating the depth of betrayal and desecration.
- **Johanan son of Kareah:** The military commander who sought to restore order and save the people.

**Main Theme:** God's laws regarding sexual purity and holiness that set Israel apart from the Egyptians and Canaanites.

**Content:** The Lord commands Moses to speak to the Israelites, instructing them not to follow the customs of Egypt or Canaan. The chapter lists prohibited sexual relationships with close relatives, as well as prohibitions against sexual intercourse during menstruation, adultery, homosexual practices, and bestiality. It also forbids offering children to Molech. God warns that the land will "vomit out" those who do these things, just as it did with the nations before them.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **To uncover someone's nakedness:** A Hebrew expression for sexual intercourse, used consistently throughout to describe forbidden relationships.
- **The land vomits out:** A powerful image indicating that sin defiles the land itself, causing it to reject its inhabitants (vv. 25, 28).
- **Molech:** A Canaanite deity who received child sacrifices; this prohibition shows that the chapter addresses more than just sexual ethics.
- **Toevah (abomination):** A Hebrew term for actions that are detestable to God and violate the covenant relationship.
- **"I am the Lord":** A repeated formula emphasizing that God's identity and authority form the basis of all these commands.

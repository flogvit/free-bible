**Main Theme:** The Appointment of the Seven Servants and the Beginning of Opposition Against Stephen.

**Content:** As the church grew, a conflict arose between the Greek-speaking and Hebrew-speaking Jewish Christians because the widows of the Greek-speaking group were being overlooked in the daily distribution. The apostles asked the congregation to select seven men, full of the Spirit and wisdom, to lead this ministry, so that the apostles themselves could devote themselves to prayer and the ministry of the word. Stephen, one of the seven, performed great signs and wonders, but was accused of blasphemy by opponents from several synagogues and brought before the Council.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hellenists and Hebrews:** The two groups in the early church – Greek-speaking and Hebrew-speaking Jewish Christians – reflect the cultural diversity and challenges within the congregation.
- **The Seven:** Men chosen for practical service, freeing the apostles to pray and preach – an early form of ministry division.
- **Stephen's Face Like an Angel:** When he stood before the Council, his face shone like that of an angel – a sign of God's presence and affirmation.
- **False Witnesses:** Opponents used lies to accuse Stephen of speaking against the temple and the law.

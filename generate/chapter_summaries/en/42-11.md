**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about prayer, reveals the power of God's kingdom over evil, and warns against religious hypocrisy.

**Content:** Jesus teaches his disciples the Lord's Prayer and encourages persistent prayer with confidence in God's generosity—he gives the Holy Spirit to those who ask. When Jesus casts out a demon, he is accused of colluding with Beelzebul, but he rejects this charge and shows that the kingdom of God has come with power. He warns against spiritual emptiness and demands that people take a stand: whoever is not with him is against him. In a Pharisee's house, Jesus pronounces six woes against the Pharisees and experts in the law for their hypocrisy—they focus on outward appearances while neglecting justice, love, and true knowledge of God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's Prayer:** The model prayer that expresses trust in God as Father and petitions for his kingdom, daily bread, and forgiveness.
- **God's finger:** An expression of God's direct power in casting out demons, showing that the kingdom of God has come (v. 20).
- **The Stronger One:** Jesus is the one who overcomes Satan ("the strong man") and frees people from his grip (vv. 21–22).
- **The Woes:** Six serious warnings against religious hypocrisy—outward piety without inner transformation.
- **The Key of Knowledge:** The experts in the law have removed access to true knowledge of God; they neither enter themselves nor allow others to enter (v. 52).

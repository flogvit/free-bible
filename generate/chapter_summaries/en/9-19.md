**Main Theme:** God protects David from Saul's repeated attempts to murder him through human help and divine intervention.

**Content:** Saul orders his men to kill David, but Jonathan defends David and gets his father to swear that David shall live. Soon Saul's hatred awakens again, and he tries to spear David with his own spear. Michal helps David escape through the window and deceives the messengers by placing a teraphim figure in the bed. David flees to Samuel at Ramah, and when Saul sends men to seize him, the Spirit of God comes upon them so that they prophesy. Finally, Saul himself goes there and also prophesies—naked and helpless all night long.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Spear:** Saul's murder weapon that he throws at David, a symbol of his uncontrolled rage.
- **Teraphim:** An idol figure that Michal uses to deceive Saul's men, showing her desperate cunning to save David.
- **Prophetic Spirit:** God's supernatural protection that renders Saul's men and Saul himself unable to harm David.
- **"Is Saul also among the prophets?":** A proverb that arises when Saul lies naked and prophesies, showing his powerlessness before God.

**Main Theme:** Rules concerning the holiness and purity of priests in their service to the Lord.

**Content:** The priests, descendants of Aaron, must avoid ritual impurity from contact with the dead, except for close family members. They are not to defile themselves by cutting their bodies or marry prostitutes, women who have been defiled, or divorced women. The high priest has even stricter requirements: he must avoid all contact with the dead, even his parents, and may only marry a virgin. Priests with physical defects cannot perform sacrificial service at the altar but are permitted to eat from the holy offerings.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Holiness (קֹדֶשׁ):** The priests must be holy to God because they present His offerings.
- **Ritual Purity:** Contact with the dead causes impurity; priests must guard their purity in order to serve in the sanctuary.
- **Defect (מוּם):** Physical blemishes disqualify one from serving at the altar, but not from fellowship or from eating the holy meals.
- **High Priest:** As the supreme representative of the people, he is held to the strictest standards regarding purity and marriage.

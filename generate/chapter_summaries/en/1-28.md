**Main Theme:** God confirms the covenant with Abraham through a dream to Jacob at Bethel.

**Content:** Isaac blesses Jacob and sends him to Paddan-Aram to find a wife from Rebekah's family, not among the Canaanites. Seeing this, Esau takes a wife from Ishmael's line. On his journey, Jacob spends the night and dreams of a ladder between earth and heaven with angels, and the Lord promises him the land, numerous descendants, and His protection. Jacob sets up the stone as a memorial pillar, names the place Bethel, and makes a vow to give God a tenth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Jacob's Ladder (סֻלָּם):** A ladder between earth and heaven with angels symbolizes the connection between God and humanity.
- **Bethel (בֵּית־אֵל):** Means "House of God" – the place where Jacob met God and which became an important sanctuary.
- **Memorial Pillar (מַצֵּבָה):** The stone that Jacob anointed with oil as a witness to God's revelation.
- **Abraham's Blessing:** The covenant promises concerning land and descendants are passed from Abraham through Isaac to Jacob.

**Main Theme:** The organization of priests and Levites into service groups under King David.

**Content:** The chapter describes how Aaron's descendants through Eleazar and Ithamar (Nadab and Abihu died without children) were divided into 24 priestly courses by casting lots. Eleazar received 16 courses and Ithamar 8, corresponding to the number of family heads. Then the other Levitical families are listed, and these also cast lots for their service turns just like the priests.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **24 Priestly Courses:** A systematic division for rotating temple service, where each group served in turn.
- **Casting Lots:** A method of fair distribution showing that the arrangement was from God, not human favoritism.
- **Eleazar and Ithamar:** The two surviving sons of Aaron from whom the priestly lines descended.
- **Organization of the Levites:** The other Levites were also organized by families using the same principle of casting lots.

**Main Theme:** A meditation on the value, beauty, and life-giving power of God's word.

**Content:** Psalm 119 is the longest psalm in the Bible with 176 verses, structured as an acrostic poem with 22 sections—one for each letter of the Hebrew alphabet. The psalmist expresses deep love for God's law (torah) and longs to live in harmony with his commands. Through prayer, praise, and reflection, he describes how God's word provides guidance, comfort in suffering, and wisdom that surpasses that of enemies. The psalm acknowledges human weakness and the need for God's grace and help to remain faithful.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Torah (law/teaching):** God's guidance for life, used synonymously with commands, statutes, testimonies, and decrees throughout the psalm.
- **Lamp for my feet:** God's word gives light and direction on life's path (v.105).
- **Truth:** God's word is reliable and eternal—"the sum of your word is truth" (v.160).
- **Longing and joy:** The psalmist describes an intense thirst for God's word and finds deep delight in it (vv.20, 47, 97).
- **Suffering and comfort:** In trials, the psalmist finds comfort and life through God's promises (vv.50, 71, 92).

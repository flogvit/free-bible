**Main Theme:** After the fall of Jerusalem, Jeremiah is granted freedom, and a remnant of the people reorganizes under Gedaliah’s leadership in Mizpah.

**Content:** Nebuzaradan releases Jeremiah from his chains at Ramah and gives him the choice between going to Babylon or staying in the land. Jeremiah chooses to join Gedaliah, whom the Babylonians have appointed governor over those remaining in Judah. Gedaliah encourages the people to settle in the land, serve the king of Babylon, and gather the harvest. Jews who had fled to neighboring countries return, but Johanan warns Gedaliah that Ishmael plans to kill him—a warning Gedaliah dismisses as a lie.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ramah:** The place where Jeremiah was freed from his chains among the captives destined for deportation to Babylon.
- **Gedaliah:** The governor appointed by the Babylonians to lead the remnant of the people, who urged cooperation with the occupiers.
- **Mizpah:** The administrative center where Gedaliah settled and gathered the remaining population.
- **Remnant (שְׁאֵרִית):** The poor and those left in the land who were not deported, now given a new beginning.

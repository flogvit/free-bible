**Main Theme:** A pilgrimage song that alternates between a grateful look back at God’s restoration and a prayer for new deliverance.

**Content:** When the Lord restored Zion’s destiny, the people were like dreamers—filled with laughter and joy. Even the nations acknowledged: "Great things has the Lord done for these." Now the people pray for fresh restoration, "like streams in the Negev," with the promise that those who sow in tears will reap with joy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Restoration of Zion (שִׁיבַת צִיּוֹן):** God’s mighty intervention that changes the people’s destiny, likely a reference to return from exile.
- **Like dreamers (כְּחֹלְמִים):** Expresses that the restoration was so overwhelming it hardly felt real.
- **Streams in the Negev (אֲפִיקִים בַּנֶּגֶב):** Dry riverbeds suddenly filled with water—a picture of sudden and surprising blessing.
- **Sow in tears, reap with joy:** A promise that present suffering will be turned into future joy through God’s faithfulness.

**Main Theme:** Everything has its time, and humanity must find joy in life despite limited understanding of God's works.

**Content:** The Preacher presents a poetic list of life's contrasts—birth and death, weeping and laughter, war and peace. God has made everything beautiful in its time and placed eternity in the human heart, yet humans cannot grasp God's work from beginning to end. Humans and animals share the same fate: both die and return to dust. The conclusion is that people should rejoice in their labor, for it is their portion.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Time (עֵת):** Everything in life has its appointed time under heaven—both joy and sorrow, building and tearing down.
- **Eternity in the heart:** God has placed a longing for eternity within humanity, but full understanding of God's plan remains hidden.
- **Dust:** Both humans and animals come from dust and return to it—a reminder of the inevitability of death.
- **Joy as a gift:** Enjoying food, drink, and work is a gift from God amid life's transience.

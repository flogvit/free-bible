**Main Theme:** God intervenes to end the famine in Samaria and confirms His word through the prophet Elisha.

**Content:** During the Aramean siege of Samaria, Elisha prophesies that food prices will drop drastically the next day. Four leprous men at the city gate go to the enemy camp and find it abandoned—the Lord had caused the Arameans to hear the sound of a great army, so they fled in panic. The people plunder the camp, and prices fall as prophesied. An officer who doubted Elisha’s word is trampled to death at the gate—seeing the abundance but unable to eat, just as predicted.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Four Lepers:** Outcasts who become instruments of God’s salvation—they bring the message of deliverance to the city.
- **The Sound of the Lord:** God uses a supernatural sound to drive the enemy into flight, without Israel having to fight.
- **The Officer at the Gate:** A symbol of the consequences of unbelief—he sees the fulfillment but does not share in the blessing.
- **The Fall of Food Prices:** Concrete evidence that God’s word through the prophet is being fulfilled.

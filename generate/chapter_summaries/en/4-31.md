**Main Theme:** The war against Midian as God’s vengeance and detailed rules for distributing war spoils and ritual purification.

**Content:** God commands Moses to take vengeance on the Midianites on behalf of Israel before he dies. Twelve thousand soldiers, led by Phinehas, go into battle where all the men, five kings, and Balaam are killed. Moses becomes angry because the women—who had led Israel into sin at Peor—were spared, and orders that only the virgin girls may live. The warriors must be purified for seven days outside the camp, and all spoils are cleansed by fire or water. The spoils are divided equally between the soldiers and the people, with portions given to the priests and Levites. The commanders voluntarily give gold to the Lord because no Israelite fell in battle.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Vengeance (נקם):** The war is the Lord’s vengeance against Midian for their role in the Peor incident.
- **Phinehas:** The priest who leads the army with holy objects and trumpets, connected to his zeal from chapter 25.
- **Purification:** Detailed rules for the ritual cleansing of soldiers and spoils highlight the uncleanness associated with war.
- **Distribution of Spoils:** Systematic division among soldiers, people, priests, and Levites demonstrates fair sharing and God’s portion.
- **Voluntary Offering:** The officers’ gift of gold in gratitude that no Israelite died shows thankfulness to God.

**Main Theme:** An apocalyptic vision concerning the fate of world empires and the persecution of God's people.

**Content:** Daniel sees a ram with two horns (Media-Persia) that charges in every direction but is overcome by a male goat with one large horn (Greece). When the goat reaches the height of its power, the horn breaks and is replaced by four horns. From one of them grows a little horn that attacks the host of heaven, removes the daily sacrifice, and desecrates the sanctuary for 2,300 evenings and mornings. The angel Gabriel explains that this concerns the end times, and that the wicked king will ultimately be broken without human hand.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ram (אַיִל):** Symbolizes the Medo-Persian empire with its two horns (Media and Persia).
- **Male Goat (צְפִיר הָעִזִּים):** Represents the Greek empire; the large horn is the first king (Alexander).
- **The Little Horn:** A future wicked king who persecutes God's people and desecrates the sanctuary.
- **The Daily Sacrifice (התמיד):** The temple service that is removed by the wicked king.
- **2,300 Evenings and Mornings:** The time period until the sanctuary is restored.

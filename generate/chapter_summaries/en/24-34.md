**Main Theme:** God judges Judah for breaking the covenant after the people reenslaved those they had freed.

**Content:** The prophet Jeremiah delivers God’s message to King Zedekiah during Babylon’s siege: Jerusalem will fall, but Zedekiah himself will die in peace. The people had made a covenant to free their Hebrew slaves, yet they took them back again. God condemns this as profaning His name and declares judgment: sword, pestilence, and famine will strike those who broke the covenant.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dror (freedom):** The freeing of slaves that the law required after six years of service—the people violated this obligation.
- **Covenant ritual with the calf:** Those who broke the covenant had walked between the pieces of a split calf—a ritual signifying commitment under the threat of death.
- **Lachish and Azekah:** The last fortified cities in Judah still resisting the Babylonians, showing how desperate the situation had become.
- **Profaning God’s name:** The people’s broken oath dishonored God, since the covenant had been made in the temple.

**Main Theme:** God confirms his covenant with Solomon and Israel at the temple dedication, with promises of forgiveness upon repentance and a warning against apostasy.

**Content:** When Solomon finished his prayer, God sent fire from heaven that consumed the sacrifices, and the glory of the Lord filled the temple so that the priests could not enter. The people worshiped and celebrated for two weeks—seven days for the temple dedication and seven days for the Feast of Tabernacles. God appeared to Solomon during the night and gave the famous promise of forgiveness and healing if the people humble themselves, pray, seek his face, and turn from their wicked ways. At the same time, God warned that apostasy would lead to the people being uprooted from the land and the temple rejected.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fire from heaven:** God's visible approval of the temple and sacrifices as a sign of his acceptance.
- **The glory of the Lord:** God's presence filling the temple so intensely that the priests could not serve.
- **Verse 14 promise:** Four conditions for restoration—humility, prayer, seeking God's face, and turning from evil ways.
- **Two paths of the covenant:** Blessing through obedience to the Davidic line; curse and exile through apostasy to other gods.

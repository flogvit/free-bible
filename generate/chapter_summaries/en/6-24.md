**Main Theme:** Joshua’s final speech challenges Israel to renew their covenant with the Lord and choose whom they will serve.

**Content:** Joshua gathers all Israel at Shechem and recounts God’s saving acts from Abraham up to the conquest of the land. He challenges the people to choose between the Lord and other gods, declaring: "But as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord." The people affirm three times that they will serve the Lord alone. Joshua makes a covenant, writes it down, and sets up a memorial stone as a witness. The chapter concludes with Joshua’s death (at 110 years old), the burial of Joseph’s bones at Shechem, and the death of Eleazar.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shechem:** The gathering place where the covenant is renewed—a location of deep patriarchal significance where Jacob purchased land.
- **"Choose for yourselves this day":** Joshua’s challenge to the people to make a conscious decision about whom they will serve—neutrality is not an option.
- **The Witness Stone:** A large stone erected under the oak tree at the sanctuary as a physical witness to the people’s covenant pledge.
- **Covenant Renewal:** Israel reaffirms their loyalty to the Lord and commits themselves to rejecting foreign gods.

**Main Theme:** A song about Israel's endurance under persecution and trust that the Lord will punish those who hate Zion.

**Content:** Since their youth, Israel has been attacked repeatedly, but the enemies have never overcome them. The oppressors are described as farmers who plow deep furrows on Israel's back, yet the Lord is righteous and has cut the cords of the wicked. The psalm prays that Zion's enemies will be put to shame and wither away like grass on the rooftops—receiving no blessing from anyone.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Plowing on the back (v.3):** A picture of brutal oppression and suffering inflicted by enemies.
- **Grass on the rooftops (v.6):** Symbolizes the fate of the enemies—they wither before they can be harvested, without fruit or value.
- **The Lord's righteousness (v.4):** God intervenes and cuts the bonds that bind His people.
- **Lack of blessing (v.8):** The enemies will not receive the traditional harvest blessing, a sign of their curse.

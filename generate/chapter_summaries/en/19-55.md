**Main Theme:** A lament in which David cries out to God for help against enemies and a treacherous friend, finding rest in trust in the Lord.

**Content:** The psalmist is filled with anxiety and fear due to enemy persecution and wickedness in the city. What hurts most is the betrayal by a close friend with whom he shared fellowship in God's house. He longs to flee like a dove and find peace in the wilderness, but instead chooses to cry out to God morning, evening, and at noon. The psalm culminates in the exhortation to cast your burden on the Lord, who will sustain the righteous.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dove's wings (v. 7):** Expresses a deep longing to escape pain and find peace away from enemies.
- **Cast your burden on the Lord (v. 23):** A central exhortation to entrust one's worries to God in trust of His care.
- **Friend's betrayal (vv. 13–14):** The pain that a confidant, who shared sweet conversation in God's house, has become a traitor.
- **Smooth words/butter (v. 22):** Describes the false friend's deceitful speech—soft as butter, yet war in his heart.

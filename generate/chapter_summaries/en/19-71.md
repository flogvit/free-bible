**Main Theme:** An aging believer asks for God's continued protection and promises to proclaim His greatness to future generations.

**Content:** The psalmist seeks refuge in God and prays for deliverance from enemies who claim that God has abandoned him. He recalls his lifelong trust in God—from the womb into old age—and pleads not to be forsaken when strength fails. The psalm culminates in praise and a promise to declare God's righteousness and mighty deeds to coming generations.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rock and Fortress (צוּר, מְצוּדָה):** God as an unassailable refuge and sure protection.
- **From the womb into old age:** Expresses lifelong dependence on and trust in God.
- **Proclaim to the generations:** The desire to pass on God's mighty deeds to future generations.
- **Righteousness (צִדְקָה):** God's faithful acts as the foundation for salvation and praise.

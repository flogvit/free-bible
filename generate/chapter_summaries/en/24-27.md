**Main Theme:** God commands the nations to submit to Nebuchadnezzar’s yoke and warns against false prophets.

**Content:** God instructs Jeremiah to make a yoke and send messages to the kings of Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre, and Sidon, telling them to submit to Nebuchadnezzar, whom God has made His servant. Those who refuse will be punished with sword, famine, and pestilence. Jeremiah warns King Zedekiah, the priests, and the people against false prophets who promise quick deliverance and the return of the temple vessels. God promises that the remaining vessels will be taken to Babylon, but He will one day bring them back.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Yoke:** A symbol of submission to Babylonian rule—those who bow their necks under it will survive.
- **Nebuchadnezzar as God’s Servant:** God uses the pagan king as an instrument of His judgment upon the nations.
- **False Prophets:** Those who preach lies in God’s name and promise swift liberation from Babylon.
- **The Temple Vessels:** The sacred objects taken to Babylon, with a promise of future restoration.

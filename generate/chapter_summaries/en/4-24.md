**Main Theme:** Balaam blesses Israel and prophesies about a coming ruler and the fate of neighboring nations.

**Content:** Balaam stops seeking omens and receives God's Spirit when he sees Israel camped by tribe. He pronounces two great blessing oracles over Israel that describe the people's beauty, strength, and future victory. In a messianic prophecy, he sees a star and a scepter that will arise from Israel and crush Moab. Balak becomes furious over the blessings, but Balaam cannot go against the Lord's word. Finally, Balaam prophesies judgment against Amalek, the Kenites, and other peoples before they each go their separate ways.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Star of Jacob** (v.17): A messianic image of a coming king/ruler who will defeat Israel's enemies.
- **The Lion** (v.9): A symbol of Israel's invincible strength—no one dares to rouse it.
- **God's Spirit** (v.2): Balaam prophesies under divine inspiration, not through pagan omens.
- **Tents/Dwellings** (v.5): An image of Israel's blessed condition, like valleys and gardens beside water.

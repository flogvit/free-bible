**Main Theme:** Psalm 15 describes who is worthy to dwell in God's presence.

**Content:** David asks who may be a guest in the Lord's tent and live on his holy mountain. The answer is the one who lives blamelessly, acts righteously, and speaks truth in their heart. Such a person does not slander, does no harm to their neighbor, honors those who fear God, keeps their promises even at personal cost, and does not exploit others financially. Those who live this way will never be shaken.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's tent/holy mountain:** Images of God's presence and the fellowship of worship with him.
- **Blameless conduct (תָמִים):** Living with integrity in action, speech, and heart.
- **Never be shaken (לֹא יִמּוֹט):** The promise of stability and security for those who live righteously.

**Main Theme:** God will defeat the forces of chaos, cleanse his people from sin, and gather Israel to worship in Jerusalem.

**Content:** The chapter opens with God's judgment against Leviathan, the sea monster that symbolizes chaos and hostile powers. It then portrays Israel as a vineyard that God himself tends and waters, protected from enemies. Jacob’s sin will be atoned for by removing idolatry—the altars are smashed and the Asherah poles destroyed. The chapter concludes with a promise that the exiled Israelites will be gathered from Assyria and Egypt when the great trumpet sounds, and they will worship the Lord on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Leviathan:** A mythical sea monster symbolizing chaotic forces and God’s enemies, whom the Lord will slay with his sword.
- **The Vineyard:** A picture of Israel that God protects and waters; he holds no anger against it but will defend it from thorns and thistles.
- **Atonement for Sin (v.9):** Jacob’s sin is atoned for by breaking the altar stones and removing idolatrous symbols (the Asherah poles and sun pillars).
- **The Great Trumpet:** The signal for Israel’s gathering from exile in Assyria and Egypt to worship in Jerusalem.

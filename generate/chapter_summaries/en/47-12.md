**Main Theme:** Paul defends his apostleship by showing that God's power is made perfect in human weakness.

**Content:** Paul recounts a heavenly revelation from fourteen years ago, when he was caught up to the third heaven and heard words that cannot be spoken. To prevent pride, he was given a "thorn in the flesh," and when he pleaded with the Lord for relief, Christ answered: "My grace is sufficient for you, for power is perfected in weakness." Paul then defends his apostleship before the Corinthians, reminds them of the apostolic signs he performed among them, and expresses deep concern that during his third visit he may find strife, jealousy, and impurity in the congregation.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Third heaven/Paradise:** Paul's mystical experience where he received divine revelation.
- **Thorn in the flesh:** A affliction given to keep Paul humble—described as "a messenger of Satan."
- **"My grace is sufficient":** Christ's response showing that divine power works best through human weakness.
- **Apostolic signs:** Signs, wonders, and mighty deeds that confirm Paul's apostolic ministry.

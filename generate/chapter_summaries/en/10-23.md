**Main Theme:** David's last prophetic words and the list of his mighty warriors (gibborim).

**Content:** David speaks prophetically about the righteous ruler who is like morning light and rain that causes the grass to sprout, confirming God's eternal covenant with his house. The wicked are compared to thorns thrown into the fire. This is followed by a list of David's heroes: the three foremost (Josheb-Basshebeth, Eleazar, and Shammah), as well as Abishai and Benaiah who distinguished themselves. The list concludes with the thirty, totaling 37 names, including Uriah the Hittite.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Morning light and rain (v.4):** A picture of the righteous ruler who brings blessing and growth to the people.
- **Eternal covenant (בְּרִית עוֹלָם, v.5):** God's unbreakable promise to David and his descendants regarding a lasting dynasty.
- **Gibborim (הַגִּבֹּרִים):** David's mighty men, an elite group of warriors known for extraordinary bravery.
- **The water from Bethlehem (vv.15–17):** Three warriors risked their lives to fetch water for David, who poured it out before the Lord—a picture of loyalty and willingness to sacrifice.

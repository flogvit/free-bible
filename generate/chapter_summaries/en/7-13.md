**Main Theme:** God prepares a deliverer for Israel through the miraculous birth of Samson as a Nazirite.

**Content:** Once again, Israel did what was evil, and the Lord gave them into the hands of the Philistines for forty years. The angel of the Lord appeared to Manoah's barren wife and promised her a son who would be a Nazirite from birth and begin to deliver Israel from the Philistines. After the angel disappeared in the flames of the offering, Manoah realized they had met the angel of the Lord. Samson was born, the Lord blessed him, and the Spirit of the Lord began to stir within him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Nazirite:** A person consecrated to God with special vows – here referring to Samson from conception, marked by the rule that no razor shall touch his head.
- **Angel of the Lord:** A divine messenger who brings the promise of Samson's birth and disappears supernaturally in the altar fire.
- **Barrenness and Promise:** Manoah's wife was barren, but God intervened with the promise of a son – a pattern known from other biblical stories.
- **Spirit of the Lord:** God's power that begins to work in Samson even at a young age, preparing him for his calling.

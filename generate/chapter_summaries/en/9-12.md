**Main Theme:** Samuel's farewell speech in which he affirms his integrity, recalls God's faithfulness, and urges the people to remain obedient under the new monarchy.

**Content:** Samuel begins his farewell address by calling the people to testify regarding his blameless service. He reviews Israel's history, showing how God faithfully delivered them from their enemies whenever they repented. Thunder and rain during the wheat harvest confirm that the people's demand for a king was sinful, yet Samuel assures them of God's continued grace if they remain obedient. He promises to continue praying for them and guiding them in the right path.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Witness (עֵד):** Samuel asks God and the anointed one to be witnesses to his integrity, establishing legal confirmation of his service.
- **Thunder and rain (קֹלוֹת וּמָטָר):** A miraculous sign during the dry wheat harvest that demonstrates God's power and disapproval.
- **God's great name (שְׁמוֹ הַגָּדוֹל):** The reason God will not abandon His people—His own name's honor is at stake.
- **Void/emptiness (תֹהוּ):** Idols are described as empty and helpless, in contrast to the living God.

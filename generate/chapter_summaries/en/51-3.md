**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts believers to live a new life in Christ by putting off the old self and putting on the new.

**Content:** Since believers have been raised with Christ, they are to seek what is above, not earthly things—for their life is hidden with Christ in God. They are urged to put to death sinful behavior and clothe themselves with virtues such as compassion, kindness, and love, which bind everything together in perfect unity. In Christ there is no distinction between Greek and Jew, slave and free. The chapter also provides guidance for the household—husbands and wives, children and parents, slaves and masters—and all things are to be done in the Lord's name with thankfulness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Put off/Put on (ἀπεκδυσάμενοι/ἐνδυσάμενοι):** The image of taking off the old self and putting on the new describes the Christian transformation.
- **The peace of Christ (εἰρήνη τοῦ Χριστοῦ):** Is to rule in the hearts of believers and unite them as one body.
- **Love (ἀγάπη):** Called the bond of perfection that binds all virtues together.
- **Household Code:** Exhortations to various members of the household regarding mutual respect and obedience in the Lord.

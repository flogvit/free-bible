**Main Theme:** God, like a potter, has full right to shape and reshape His people, and His plans can change based on the people's response.

**Content:** God sends Jeremiah to the house of the potter, where he sees how the potter reshapes the clay when the vessel fails. This illustrates that God can alter His plans: He will spare a nation that turns from evil but withdraw blessing from one that does evil. Israel rejects God's call to repentance and says "it is hopeless". The people plot to harm Jeremiah, who prays for God's righteous judgment against his persecutors.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Potter and the Clay:** Illustrates God's sovereign right to shape the destiny of nations.
- **Conditional Prophecy:** God's judgments and blessings can change based on the people's response.
- **"It is hopeless" (נוֹאָשׁ):** The people's rejection and decision to follow the evil of their own hearts.
- **Jeremiah's Intercessory Lament:** The prophet's prayer for protection and justice against those who seek to kill him.

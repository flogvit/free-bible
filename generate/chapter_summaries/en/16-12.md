**Main Theme:** Overview of the priestly and Levitical families after the exile, and the solemn dedication of Jerusalem's walls.

**Content:** The chapter lists the priests and Levites who returned from Babylon with Zerubbabel and Jeshua, as well as the genealogy of the high priests from Jeshua to Jaddua. At the dedication of the wall, the priests and Levites purified themselves, along with the people, the gates, and the wall. Nehemiah organized two large choirs that marched in opposite directions around the wall and met at the temple, where they offered sacrifices and celebrated with great joy that could be heard from far away. Men were also appointed to manage the storerooms to ensure that the priests' and Levites' rightful portions were provided.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Genealogies:** Documentation of legitimate priestly and Levitical families, ensuring continuity in temple service after the exile.
- **Dedication (chanukka):** The solemn consecration of the rebuilt wall, marking the completion of Nehemiah's project.
- **Choirs of Praise:** Two large processions featuring singing, music, and thanksgiving, symbolizing the people's joy and gratitude to God.
- **Purification:** Ritual preparation of the priests, Levites, people, gates, and wall, emphasizing the holiness of the dedication ceremony.
- **David's Command:** The organization of praise and service followed the instructions given by King David, the man of God.

**Main Theme:** God reveals himself at Mount Sinai and makes a covenant with Israel as a holy people.

**Content:** Three months after leaving Egypt, Israel arrives in the wilderness of Sinai and camps before the mountain. God offers a covenant: if the people obey his voice, they will be his treasured possession, a kingdom of priests and a holy nation. The people respond unanimously that they will do everything the Lord has said. After three days of consecration, God comes down on the mountain in fire and smoke, while the sound of the shofar grows louder. Moses is called up onto the mountain but warned to keep the people away from it under penalty of death.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Eagles' wings** (v.4): God describes how he carried Israel out of Egypt as an eagle carries its young – a picture of care and protection.
- **Treasured possession (סְגֻלָּה)** (v.5): Israel is to be God's special treasure among all peoples, chosen for a special purpose.
- **Kingdom of priests** (v.6): The entire people are to have a priestly function – set apart for service to God.
- **Shofar** (v.16,19): The sound of the ram's horn marks God's presence and grows stronger throughout the theophany.
- **Consecration** (v.10,14): The people must cleanse themselves and wash their clothes as preparation for meeting the holy God.

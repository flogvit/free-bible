**Main Theme:** Absalom's rebellion against King David and David's flight from Jerusalem in humility and trust in God.

**Content:** Absalom wins the people's favor by standing at the city gate, flattering them, and undermining their confidence in David as judge. After four years, he goes to Hebron and has himself proclaimed king. David flees Jerusalem with his loyal men, including the Philistine Ittai, but sends the ark back with the priests Zadok and Abiathar. He asks them and Hushai to remain in the city as spies, while he himself goes up the Mount of Olives weeping and barefoot.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Stealing hearts (v.6):** Absalom manipulates the people through flattery and false promises of justice.
- **The Mount of Olives (v.30):** David goes up weeping with his head covered—a picture of humiliation and sorrow.
- **The ark sent back (vv.25–26):** David entrusts his destiny to God instead of using the ark as a talisman.
- **Hushai as a spy (vv.32–37):** David acts strategically by placing a loyal counselor among the enemy.

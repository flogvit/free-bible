**Main Theme:** God gives Solomon exceptional wisdom in response to his humble prayer.

**Content:** Solomon forms an alliance with Egypt through marriage to Pharaoh's daughter, while working on major construction projects. At the high place in Gibeon, God appears to him in a dream and offers Solomon whatever he asks for. Solomon humbly prays for a listening heart to judge the people, and God grants him unmatched wisdom as well as riches and honor that he did not ask for. Solomon's wisdom is demonstrated in the judgment between two women who both claim to be the mother of a living child.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **High Places (במות):** Temporary places of worship used before the temple was built, where both the people and Solomon offered sacrifices.
- **Listening Heart (לֵב שֹׁמֵעַ):** Solomon's prayer for the ability to listen and understand in order to judge fairly.
- **The Sword Judgment:** The wise test that revealed the true mother through her willingness to give up the child to save its life.
- **God's Wisdom (חָכְמַת אֱלֹהִים):** The people's recognition that Solomon's judgment was given by God.

**Main Theme:** Joseph interprets the dreams of Pharaoh’s imprisoned servants but is himself forgotten in prison.

**Content:** Pharaoh’s cupbearer and baker are imprisoned and each receives a dream that confuses them. Joseph, who serves them in prison, offers to interpret the dreams and declares that interpretations belong to God. He predicts that within three days the cupbearer will be restored to his position, while the baker will be executed. On Pharaoh’s birthday both interpretations come true, but the cupbearer forgets to help Joseph.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dreams:** Divine communication that reveals the future and requires interpretation.
- **The Vine and the Baskets:** Symbols in the dreams representing restoration and judgment respectively.
- **"Lift up your head":** An expression used with a double meaning—exaltation for the cupbearer, execution for the baker.
- **Forgetfulness:** The cupbearer’s failure to remember Joseph points to God’s timing in salvation history.

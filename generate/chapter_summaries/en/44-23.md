**Main Theme:** Paul is rescued from Jewish persecution through God’s providence and Roman protection, and receives the promise to testify in Rome.

**Content:** Paul stands before the Jewish council and divides the assembly by focusing on belief in the resurrection, creating conflict between Pharisees and Sadducees. The Lord appears to Paul during the night and assures him that he will bear witness in Rome. When more than forty Jews swear an oath to kill Paul, his nephew reveals the plot, and the Roman commander sends Paul under heavy guard to Governor Felix in Caesarea.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Whitewashed wall:** Paul’s words to the high priest, symbolizing outward purity that hides inner impurity.
- **The Resurrection:** The theological point of contention that splits the council between Pharisees (who believe) and Sadducees (who reject it).
- **The Lord’s nighttime visit:** God’s confirmation to Paul that his missionary mission will continue all the way to Rome.
- **The murder plot:** The oath taken by more than forty men to kill Paul, demonstrating the hostility he faces.

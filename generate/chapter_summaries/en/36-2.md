**Main Theme:** Prophecies concerning God’s judgment on Israel’s neighboring nations and an exhortation to the humble to seek the Lord.

**Content:** The chapter begins with a call for Judah to gather together and seek the Lord before the day of wrath arrives (vv. 1–3). This is followed by oracles of judgment against the Philistines (Gaza, Ashkelon, Ashdod, Ekron), Moab and Ammon (who mocked God’s people), Cush, and Assyria with its capital Nineveh. The remnant of Judah will inherit the coastal land of the Philistines, and all the coasts of the earth will worship the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Day of the Lord’s Wrath:** The coming judgment that the people must prepare for through repentance.
- **Seek the Lord:** An exhortation to the humble to pursue righteousness and humility in order to be protected.
- **Sodom and Gomorrah:** Moab and Ammon will become like these destroyed cities—an everlasting desolation.
- **Nineveh as a Desert:** The proud city will become a wasteland where wild animals dwell.

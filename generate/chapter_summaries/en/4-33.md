**Main Theme:** A complete travel log of Israel's journey from Egypt to the border of Canaan, with instructions regarding the conquest of the land.

**Content:** Moses recorded all of Israel's stages of travel from Rameses to the plains of Moab by the Jordan, at the Lord's command. The chapter marks Aaron's death on Mount Hor in the 40th year, when he was 123 years old. God commands the Israelites to drive out all the inhabitants of Canaan, destroy their idols and high places, and distribute the land by casting lots according to the size of each tribe. If they do not drive out the inhabitants, these will become like thorns in their eyes and pricks in their sides, and God will do to Israel what He had intended to do to the Canaanites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Stages of Travel (מַסְעֵי):** The systematic record of the journey demonstrates God's faithful guidance throughout the 40 years in the wilderness.
- **Mount Hor:** The place where Aaron died and the priesthood passed on, a milestone in the transition to a new generation.
- **Casting Lots (גוֹרָל):** The method for fairly distributing the land, whereby larger tribes receive larger portions.
- **Thorns and Pricks:** A picture of the consequences of disobedience—the remaining peoples will continually trouble Israel.

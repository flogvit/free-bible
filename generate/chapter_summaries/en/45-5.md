**Main Theme:** Justification by faith brings peace with God, and Christ's grace far surpasses Adam's sin.

**Content:** Those who are justified by faith have peace with God and access to his grace through Jesus Christ. Suffering produces endurance, a tested character, and hope—a hope that does not disappoint because God's love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit. Christ died for us while we were still sinners, and through his death we were reconciled to God. Paul contrasts Adam and Christ: through one man's disobedience, sin and death came to all, but through one man's obedience, righteousness and life come—and where sin increased, grace abounded even more.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Justification (δικαίωσις):** Being declared righteous before God through faith, not by works.
- **Reconciliation (καταλλαγή):** Restoration of the relationship between God and humanity through Christ's death.
- **Adam and Christ:** Two representative heads—Adam's fall brought death; Christ's obedience brings life.
- **Grace (χάρις):** God's unmerited gift that overflows where sin was great.

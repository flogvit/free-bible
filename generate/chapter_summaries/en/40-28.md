**Main Theme:** Jesus' resurrection is confirmed, and he gives the disciples the Great Commission before promising to be with them always.

**Content:** At dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary come to the tomb, where an angel announces that Jesus has risen. Jesus meets the women and sends them to the disciples. The guards report the event to the chief priests, who bribe them to spread the lie that the disciples stole the body. In Galilee, the eleven disciples meet the risen Jesus, who gives them the Great Commission: to make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything he has commanded – with the promise of his presence until the end of the age.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Empty Tomb:** The proof that Jesus has conquered death, confirmed by the angel and the rolled-away stone.
- **The Angel:** God's messenger who announces the resurrection and sends the women with the message to the disciples.
- **The Great Commission:** Jesus' command to make disciples of all nations through baptism and teaching.
- **All Authority:** Jesus declares his universal authority in heaven and on earth as the basis for the mission.
- **"I am with you":** Jesus' promise of enduring presence with his disciples until the end of the world.

**Main Theme:** Saul's first military victory confirms his kingship over Israel.

**Content:** Nahash, the king of the Ammonites, besieged Jabesh-Gilead and demanded that all the inhabitants have their right eyes gouged out as a condition for peace. When the message reached Saul, the Spirit of God came upon him, and he mobilized all Israel by sending out pieces of cut-up oxen as a warning. With a large army, he completely defeated the Ammonites. After the victory, the people renewed the kingdom at Gilgal, where Saul was officially confirmed as king in the presence of the Lord with peace offerings and great joy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Right eye:** Nahash's demand symbolized total humiliation and would have rendered the men useless as warriors.
- **Spirit of God:** The Spirit came upon Saul and equipped him for leadership and military action.
- **Cut-up oxen:** Saul's dramatic method of mobilization, which spread "the fear of the Lord" and gathered the people together as one man.
- **Gilgal:** The place where the kingdom was renewed and Saul was officially confirmed as king before the Lord.

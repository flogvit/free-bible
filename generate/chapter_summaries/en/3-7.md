**Main Theme:** The chapter provides detailed regulations for the guilt offering and the peace offering, as well as the priests' portions and dietary restrictions for the people.

**Content:** The chapter begins with rules for the guilt offering, which is most holy and must be slaughtered in the same place as the burnt offering. This is followed by extensive instructions for the peace offering, including the thank offering with its types of bread and time limits for eating the meat. The prohibition against eating fat and blood is emphasized, and anyone who eats sacrificial meat while unclean shall be cut off from the people. The priests are to receive the breast (wave offering) and the right thigh (heave offering) as their perpetual portion.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Guilt Offering (אָשָׁם):** A most holy offering for atonement, where the fat is burned and the priests eat the meat in a holy place.
- **Peace Offering (שְׁלָמִים):** A communal meal offering that includes the thank offering, the vow offering, and the freewill offering, with different time limits for consumption.
- **Fat and Blood:** Strictly forbidden to eat – the fat belongs to the Lord, and the blood is holy; violation results in being cut off from the people.
- **Wave Offering (תְּנוּפָה):** The breast that is waved before the Lord and then given to Aaron's sons as the priests' perpetual right.

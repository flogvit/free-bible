**Main Theme:** God's judgment on bad shepherds and false prophets, and the promise of a righteous Davidic king.

**Content:** God condemns Israel's leaders (shepherds) who have scattered and neglected His people, promising to gather the remnant of His people and raise up new, good shepherds. He prophesies about a righteous Branch from David's line who will reign with wisdom and justice, and will be called "The Lord Our Righteousness." The chapter contains sharp criticism of the false prophets in Jerusalem who prophesy lies from their own hearts, strengthen the hands of evildoers, and lead the people astray—they are compared to Sodom and Gomorrah. God emphasizes His omnipresence and warns against misusing the expression "the burden of the Lord."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shepherds (רֹעִים):** A designation for Israel's leaders who have failed in their duty to lead and protect the people.
- **Righteous Branch (צֶמַח צַדִּיק):** A messianic title for the coming Davidic king who will restore justice.
- **The Lord Our Righteousness (יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ):** The name of the coming king, pointing to salvation and justification from God.
- **Fire and Hammer (אֵשׁ / פַטִּישׁ):** Images of the power of God's word that consumes and shatters, in contrast to the empty dreams of the false prophets.

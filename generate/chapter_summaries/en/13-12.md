**Main Theme:** Warriors from all the tribes of Israel join David to make him king over all Israel.

**Content:** The chapter describes how brave warriors from various tribes came to David, first in Ziklag while he was fleeing from Saul, and later in Hebron to transfer the kingdom to him. The Spirit came upon Amasai, who declared his loyalty to David. Detailed numbers show that more than 300,000 fighting men gathered with undivided hearts, and the gathering was celebrated with a great meal lasting three days.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ziklag and Hebron:** The two places where warriors joined David—first in exile, then at his coronation.
- **The Gadites with lion-like faces:** Warriors from Gad are described as swift as gazelles and having faces like lions (v.9).
- **The sons of Issachar:** Men who "understood the times" and knew what Israel ought to do (v.33).
- **A camp like God's camp:** David's growing army is compared to a divine host (v.23).
- **Undivided heart:** All came with "a whole heart" to make David king (v.39).

**Main Theme:** Jesus’ crucifixion, death, and burial – God’s Son dies to accomplish salvation.

**Content:** Jesus is handed over to Pilate, who yields to the crowd’s demand that he be crucified, while Barabbas is released. Judas regrets his betrayal and takes his own life. Jesus is mocked, flogged, and crucified at Golgotha, where he dies with the cry, "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" At his death, the temple curtain tears in two, the earth shakes, and many holy ones rise from their graves. Joseph of Arimathea buries Jesus in a new tomb, which the Jewish leaders arrange to have guarded.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The temple curtain tears:** Symbolizes that the way to God is now open to all through Jesus’ death.
- **"My God, why have you forsaken me?":** Jesus’ cry from Psalm 22 expresses his deep suffering as the sin-bearer.
- **Pilate washes his hands:** Shows an attempt to disclaim responsibility, but also affirms that Jesus was innocent.
- **The centurion’s confession:** A Gentile acknowledges that Jesus was God’s Son – the gospel is for all peoples.
- **The field of blood:** The thirty silver pieces become a testimony to Judas’ betrayal and the fulfillment of prophecy.

**Main Theme:** The greatness and fall of Uzziah – from blessing to pride and leprosy.

**Content:** Uzziah became king over Judah at the age of sixteen and reigned for 52 years. As long as he sought the Lord, God gave him success: he defeated the Philistines and other nations, built fortifications, and strengthened the army. But when he became powerful, his heart grew proud, and he entered the temple to burn incense – a service reserved for the priests. The Lord struck him with leprosy, and he lived in isolation until his death while his son Jotham ruled as regent.

**Key words/images:**
- **Seeking God:** Uzziah's success was directly linked to his seeking the Lord under the guidance of Zechariah (v.5).
- **Pride (גָּבַהּ לִבּוֹ):** Uzziah's heart "became high" – success led to self-confidence that displaced dependence on God.
- **The incense altar:** The symbol of Uzziah's overstepping boundaries – he sought to take the priests' role in the holy place.
- **Leprosy:** God's judgment on Uzziah's rebellion, which made him ritually unclean and excluded from the temple for the rest of his life.

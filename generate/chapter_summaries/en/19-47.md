**Main Theme:** A psalm that proclaims God as the universal King over all peoples and nations of the earth.

**Content:** The psalm (by the sons of Korah) opens with a call for all peoples to clap their hands and shout for joy to God, the Most High and awesome King. God has subdued the peoples under Israel and chosen their inheritance—the pride of Jacob. The climax comes when God "ascends" amid shouts of joy and the sound of the shofar, and the psalm culminates with Him seated on His holy throne while the princes of the nations gather as the people of the God of Abraham.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Clapping and Shouts (תִּקְעוּ־כָף / תְרוּעָה):** Expressions of worship and homage to God as King.
- **Shofar (שׁוֹפָר):** The horn that marks God's majestic ascent and royal presence.
- **God's Throne (כִּסֵּא קָדְשׁוֹ):** A symbol of God's eternal and holy dominion over the nations.
- **The God of Abraham (אֱלֹהֵי אַבְרָהָם):** Connects God's universal kingship to His covenant with Abraham and his people.

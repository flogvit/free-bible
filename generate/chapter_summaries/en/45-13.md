**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts Christians to submit to authorities, love their neighbor, and live in light of Christ’s return.

**Content:** All people must submit to governing authorities, for they are established by God and serve as His agents to promote good and punish evil. Christians should pay taxes and show respect. Love fulfills the entire law, because whoever loves their neighbor does no harm to them. Since salvation is near, believers must wake from spiritual sleep, put aside deeds of darkness, and clothe themselves with the Lord Jesus Christ.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God’s servant (διάκονος):** Authorities are God’s instruments for maintaining order and punishing evil.
- **Love fulfills the law:** All commandments are summed up in love for one’s neighbor—it does no harm to anyone.
- **Weapons of light:** Believers must lay aside the darkness of sin and put on Christ as spiritual armor.
- **Wake from sleep:** A picture of spiritual alertness in view of the nearness of salvation.

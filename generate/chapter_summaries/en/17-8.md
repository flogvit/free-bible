**Main Theme:** The Jews are granted the right to defend themselves through a new royal decree.

**Content:** After Haman's execution, King Ahasuerus gives Haman's estate to Esther and his signet ring to Mordecai. Esther pleads with the king to revoke Haman's decree against the Jews, but since a royal decree cannot be recalled, the king authorizes Mordecai to write a new decree. This new decree grants the Jews the right to gather and defend themselves against their enemies on the 13th of Adar. Mordecai goes out in royal garments, and there is great joy among the Jews and throughout the city of Susa.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Signet Ring:** A symbol of royal authority transferred from Haman to Mordecai, marking a shift in power.
- **The New Decree:** Shows that God's people can be saved even when human laws seem unchangeable.
- **Mordecai's Royal Garments:** Symbolize vindication and honor after humiliation—a picture of God's justice.
- **Joy and Light (אוֹרָה וְשִׂמְחָה):** Expresses the Jews' transition from despair to hope and celebration.

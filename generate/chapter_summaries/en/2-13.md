**Main Theme:** Consecration of the firstborn and God's guidance of Israel out of Egypt.

**Content:** God commands Moses to consecrate all the firstborn among the Israelites, both human and animal, as belonging to the Lord. Moses instructs the people to remember their departure from Egypt by eating unleavened bread for seven days, teaching their children about God's mighty acts, and wearing these reminders on their hands and between their eyes. The firstborn donkey is to be redeemed with a lamb, and firstborn sons are to be redeemed. God leads the people on a detour through the wilderness toward the Red Sea—not by way of the Philistine land—so that they would not regret seeing war. Moses takes Joseph's bones with him, and the Lord goes before them in a pillar of cloud by day and a pillar of fire by night.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Firstborn (בְּכוֹר):** All firstborn belong to God as a reminder that He struck down Egypt's firstborn but spared Israel's.
- **Unleavened bread (מַצּוֹת):** A symbol of the hurried departure from Egypt, eaten for seven days as an annual remembrance.
- **Pillar of cloud and pillar of fire:** God's visible presence that guides and protects His people day and night.
- **Joseph's bones:** Fulfillment of Joseph's oath and a sign of faith in God's promises regarding the land.

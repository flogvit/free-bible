**Main Theme:** Proverbs about wisdom in speech, self-control, and interpersonal relationships, collected by the men of King Hezekiah.

**Content:** This chapter contains proverbs attributed to Solomon that were gathered by the men of King Hezekiah. The proverbs offer guidance on humility before kings and powerful people, caution in conflicts and speech, as well as the value of moderation in all things. The chapter also emphasizes how to treat enemies with kindness and warns against a lack of self-control.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Apples of gold in settings of silver (v. 11):** A word spoken at the right time is valuable and beautiful.
- **Snow in harvest time (v. 13):** A faithful messenger refreshes his master, just as cool snow brings relief in the heat.
- **Burning coals on the head (v. 21–22):** Showing kindness to an enemy by giving food and drink brings God's blessing.
- **A city without walls (v. 28):** A person without self-control is as vulnerable as a city left unprotected.

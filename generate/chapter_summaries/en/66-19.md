**Main Theme:** The chapter celebrates God's victory over evil through heavenly praise and Christ's triumphant return.

**Content:** A great multitude in heaven cries out "Hallelujah!" for God's righteous judgment against the great prostitute, while the 24 elders and the four living creatures worship. The marriage of the Lamb is announced, where the bride is clothed in bright linen symbolizing the righteous acts of the saints. Heaven opens, and Christ comes on a white horse as "Faithful and True," "the Word of God," and "King of kings." The beast and the false prophet are thrown into the lake of fire, and their armies are slain.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hallelujah:** A cry of praise that appears four times in this chapter—the only place in the New Testament where this word is used.
- **The Marriage of the Lamb:** The image of the final union between Christ and his people, where the bride has made herself ready.
- **White Horse and Rider:** Christ as the victorious warrior who judges with righteousness and is called the Word of God.
- **Lake of Fire:** The final judgment where the beast and the false prophet are cast alive.

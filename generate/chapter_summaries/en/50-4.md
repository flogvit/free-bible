**Main Theme:** Paul concludes the letter with exhortations concerning joy, prayer, and right thinking, as well as thanks for the Philippians' financial gift.

**Content:** Paul urges unity between Euodia and Syntyke, and exhorts the congregation to persistent joy and prayer accompanied by thanksgiving, which will bring God's peace. He teaches them to focus their thoughts on what is true, noble, and good. Paul shares his testimony of having learned contentment in all circumstances and that he can do all things through Christ who strengthens him. He thanks them for the gift they sent with Epaphroditus and assures them that God will supply all their needs according to His riches in glory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's peace (ἡ εἰρήνη τοῦ θεοῦ):** The peace that surpasses all understanding and guards hearts and minds in Christ.
- **Contentment (αὐτάρκης):** Paul has learned to be content regardless of external circumstances—whether in abundance or need.
- **Strength in Christ:** "I can do all things through him who strengthens me" – the power comes from Christ, not from one's own ability.
- **Fragrant offering (ὀσμὴν εὐωδίας):** The Philippians' gift is described as an offering that pleases God.

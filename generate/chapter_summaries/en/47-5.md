**Main Theme:** Paul conveys the hope of resurrection and calls the church to reconciliation with God through Christ.

**Content:** Paul compares our earthly bodies to tents and points toward the eternal, heavenly dwelling we will receive. He emphasizes that we walk by faith, not by sight, and that all must stand before Christ's judgment seat. Christ's love drives the apostles in their ministry of reconciliation: God reconciled the world to himself through Christ and gave us the message of reconciliation. Whoever is in Christ is a new creation – Christ was made sin for us so that we might become the righteousness of God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tent/Dwelling (σκῆνος/οἰκία):** The earthly body is temporary like a tent, while the heavenly dwelling is eternal.
- **New Creation (καινὴ κτίσις):** Whoever is in Christ receives a radically new existence – the old has passed away.
- **Reconciliation (καταλλαγή):** God reconciled the world to himself through Christ and does not count people's sins against them.
- **Ambassador (πρεσβεύω):** Believers are Christ's ambassadors who convey God's appeal for reconciliation.

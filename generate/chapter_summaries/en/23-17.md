**Main Theme:** A prophecy of judgment against Damascus and Israel that foretells destruction but also points toward repentance.

**Content:** Damascus will become a ruin, and the cities of Aroer will be abandoned. The fortress of Ephraim and the kingdom of Damascus will fall, and the glory of Jacob will be diminished—"as when the reaper gathers the standing grain." This is because Israel forgot their God who saves them and worshiped foreign gods. Yet on that day, people will turn to their Maker. The chapter concludes with judgment against the nations that attack God’s people—they will be swept away like chaff before the wind.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ruins (מַפָּלָה):** The total destruction of Damascus as a picture of God’s judgment on the nations.
- **The Reaper:** The remnant of Israel is compared to the few stalks left after harvesting—only a small remnant survives.
- **Olive Tree:** Just as shaking an olive tree leaves only a few olives behind, this illustrates the small remnant that will be spared.
- **Chaff before the wind (מֹץ):** The nations that attack Israel will be driven away and vanish before morning comes.

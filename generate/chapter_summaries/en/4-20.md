**Main Theme:** The chapter deals with rebellion, disobedience, and consequences – both for the people and their leaders.

**Content:** Miriam dies in Kadesh, and the people complain about a lack of water. God commands Moses to speak to the rock, but instead Moses strikes it twice. Because Moses and Aaron did not trust God and failed to honor him as holy, they are not allowed to lead the people into the land. Edom refuses Israel passage through their territory, and Aaron dies on Mount Hor, where Eleazar takes over the priestly service.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Meribah (Water of Strife):** The place where the people contended with God about water, and where Moses' disobedience had serious consequences.
- **The Rock:** A symbol of God's care – he provides water in the wilderness but requires faith and obedience.
- **Mount Hor:** The place where Aaron dies and the priesthood is transferred to the next generation through Eleazar.
- **Edom:** Israel's "brother" (descendants of Esau) who refuses them passage, a sign of future conflict.

**Main Theme:** A lament over falsehood and lying among people, with trust in God’s pure word and his promise to protect the weak.

**Content:** David cries out to the Lord because the godly and faithful seem to have disappeared. People speak falsehood with flattery and a double heart, and the proud ask, "Who is lord over us?" The Lord answers that he will now rise up to protect the poor and those in distress. The psalm concludes with confidence that God will preserve his people against this evil generation.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Double heart (בְּלֵב וָלֵב):** Describes the hypocrisy of those who say one thing but mean another.
- **Slippery lips:** The deceitful use flattery and deception in their speech.
- **Silver refined seven times:** God’s word is perfectly pure and reliable, in contrast to human lies.
- **The poor and those in distress:** The vulnerable whom God promises to protect against oppressors.

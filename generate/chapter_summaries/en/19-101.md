**Main Theme:** David commits to living and ruling with integrity, remaining faithful to the Lord.

**Content:** The psalm is David's pledge to sing of God's grace and justice, and to walk blamelessly in his house. He will reject all that is evil and deceitful, and keep himself away from the proud and slanderers. His eyes are upon the faithful in the land, and every morning he will destroy the wicked from the Lord's city.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Grace and Justice (חֶסֶד וּמִשְׁפָּט):** The two cornerstones of David's rule and praise.
- **Blameless Way (דֶּרֶךְ תָּמִים):** David's commitment to moral integrity, both in private and public life.
- **The Lord's City (עִיר־יְהוָה):** Jerusalem as God's holy city, where evil will not be tolerated.
- **The Faithful in the Land (נֶאֶמְנֵי־אֶרֶץ):** The righteous whom David will surround himself with as advisors and servants.

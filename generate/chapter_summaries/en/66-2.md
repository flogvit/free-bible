**Main Theme:** Jesus Christ sends letters to four churches in Asia Minor with praise, criticism, and promises to those who overcome.

**Content:** The chapter contains letters to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, and Thyatira. Jesus knows each church's deeds and situation—he praises perseverance and faithfulness but criticizes the loss of first love, tolerance for false teaching (the teachings of Balaam, the Nicolaitans), and the false prophetess "Jezebel." Each church is called to repentance, and the overcomer is promised specific rewards: the tree of life, the crown of life, hidden manna, and the morning star.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lampstand:** Represents the church; Ephesus is warned that it may be removed if they do not repent.
- **Crown of Life (στέφανος τῆς ζωῆς):** A promise to Smyrna of eternal reward for faithfulness until death.
- **Two-edged sword:** An image of Christ's word that judges, especially directed at the false teachers in Pergamum.
- **Morning Star:** A promise to the overcomers in Thyatira, symbolizing Christ himself and a share in his rule.

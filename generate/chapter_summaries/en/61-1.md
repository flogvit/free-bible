**Main Theme:** Peter exhorts believers to spiritual growth and the confirmation of their calling, founded on God's promises and apostolic eyewitness testimony.

**Content:** God has given believers everything they need for life and godliness, including precious promises that make them partakers of the divine nature. Peter urges them to add to their faith a series of virtues—from knowledge and self-control to brotherly love and love—to make their calling secure. Aware of his imminent death, Peter wishes to leave behind this reminder. He confirms his eyewitness testimony from the Mount of Transfiguration and emphasizes the reliability of the prophetic word, which came through the Holy Spirit.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Divine nature (θεία φύσις):** The promise that believers become partakers of God's own nature by fleeing from the corrupt desires of the world.
- **The list of virtues (v. 5–7):** A step-by-step development of Christian qualities, beginning with faith and culminating in love (ἀγάπη).
- **The Mount of Transfiguration:** Peter's eyewitness account of Jesus' glory and the Father's voice affirming the Son.
- **The prophetic word:** The reliability of Scripture, given by the Holy Spirit, as a light in the darkness until the day dawns.

**Main Theme:** A call to praise and a warning against disobedience to God.

**Content:** The psalm opens with an invitation to sing and shout for joy to the Lord, our Savior's rock, and to worship him as the great God and Creator of all. The people are described as his flock under his care. The psalm concludes with a warning not to harden hearts as their ancestors did at Meribah and Massah in the wilderness, which led to them being excluded from God's rest.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Savior's rock (צוּר יִשְׁעֵנוּ):** God as a secure and unshakable refuge and Savior.
- **Shepherd and flock (עַם מַרְעִיתוֹ וְצֹאן יָדוֹ):** The image of God as the caring shepherd for his people.
- **Meribah and Massah:** Historical places where Israel tested God in the wilderness.
- **God's rest (מְנוּחָה):** What God promised to his obedient people, which their ancestors missed out on.

**Main Theme:** God’s cleansing and restoration of Zion after judgment.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an image of seven women seizing one man to bear his name and remove their shame—a sign of great distress. But "on that day" the Branch of the Lord and the fruit of the land will become a source of beauty and honor for those who remain in Israel. Those left in Jerusalem will be called holy. The Lord will cleanse the daughters of Zion by means of the spirit of judgment and the spirit of burning, and He will create a cloud by day and a glowing fire by night over Zion—a shelter and protection against heat and storm.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Branch of the Lord (צֶמַח יְהוָה):** A symbol of God’s blessing and restoration for those who remain.
- **Holy (קָדוֹשׁ):** The remnant in Jerusalem will be called holy, set apart for God.
- **Spirit of judgment and burning (רוּחַ מִשְׁפָּט וּרוּחַ בָּעֵר):** God purifies His people through judgment and the burning away of impurity.
- **Cloud and fire:** God’s presence as protector and guide, echoing the wilderness journey.
- **Shelter/Canopy (סֻכָּה):** God’s protection providing shade from heat and shelter from storms.

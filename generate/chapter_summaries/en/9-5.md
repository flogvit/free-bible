**Main Theme:** The Lord's supremacy over the Philistine god Dagon is demonstrated through the devastating power of the ark of the covenant.

**Content:** The Philistines place the captured ark of the covenant in the temple of Dagon at Ashdod, but the statue of Dagon falls before it—the second time with its head and hands cut off. The Lord strikes the people with tumors, and these plagues follow the ark from Ashdod to Gath and then on to Ekron. Finally, the entire city cries out to heaven, and the Philistine rulers decide that the ark of the covenant must be sent back.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant (אֲרוֹן הָאֱלֹהִים):** The symbol of God's presence, demonstrating His power even in enemy territory.
- **Dagon:** The Philistine god who falls powerless before the Lord's ark of the covenant.
- **Tumors (טְחֹרִים):** The physical plague that strikes the Philistines as a sign of God's judgment.
- **The Lord's Hand (יַד־יְהוָה):** An expression of God's active intervention and punishment upon the Philistines.

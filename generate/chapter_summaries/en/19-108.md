**Main Theme:** A psalm of praise in which David expresses unwavering trust in God and asks for His help to achieve victory over enemies.

**Content:** The psalmist commits to praising God with song and music among the nations, highlighting God's boundless love and faithfulness. God has spoken in His holiness and promised dominion over both the tribes of Israel and hostile nations such as Moab, Edom, and Philistia. The psalmist acknowledges that human help is futile and that victory comes only from God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Harp and lyre (v.3):** Instruments of worship that awaken the dawn – expressing wholehearted praise.
- **God's love above the heavens (v.5):** A poetic image for God's boundless grace (chesed) and faithfulness.
- **Moab as a washbasin (v.10):** An image of God's dominion over hostile nations – they are reduced to servants.
- **Fortified city (v.11):** A symbol of the enemies' strength that only God can overcome.

**Main Theme:** God calls Judah to repentance and warns of a coming destruction from the north as a result of the people's apostasy.

**Content:** The chapter opens with God’s call to Israel to turn back and remove their idols (vv. 1–4). Then a threatening judgment is proclaimed: an enemy from the north will lay the land waste (vv. 5–18). Jeremiah expresses deep personal anguish over the fate of his people (vv. 19–21). An apocalyptic vision shows the earth returning to chaos—"formless and void"—as at the beginning of creation (vv. 23–26). Nevertheless, God promises that He will not make a complete end of the land (v. 27).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Circumcision of the heart (v. 4):** God demands inner transformation, not merely outward rituals—the heart must be cleansed from evil.
- **The lion from the thicket (v. 7):** A picture of the enemy from the north coming to destroy the land.
- **Tohu va-vohu (v. 23):** The prophet sees the earth as "formless and void"—the same expression found in Genesis 1:2, showing the cosmic scale of the destruction.
- **Daughter Zion in labor pains (v. 31):** Jerusalem is compared to a woman in childbirth, crying out in distress over her murderers.

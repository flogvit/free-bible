**Main Theme:** God urges Jerusalem to wake up and clothe herself in glory, for He will redeem His people and reveal His salvation to all nations.

**Content:** Zion is called to rise from the dust and cast off the chains of captivity, for the unclean shall no longer enter the holy city. God declares that His people were sold for nothing and will be redeemed without payment. The messenger who proclaims peace and salvation over the mountains is praised, and the watchmen shall rejoice when they see the Lord return to Zion. The chapter concludes with the introduction of the Lord's servant, who will be highly exalted, yet whose appearance was marred more than any other human being.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Zion's Awakening** – Jerusalem is called to wake up, clothe herself in strength and glory, and shake off the dust of captivity (v.1-2)
- **The Messenger's Feet** – The beautiful feet on the mountains belong to the one who brings good news of peace and salvation (v.7)
- **The Lord's Arm** – God uncovers His holy arm before all nations as a sign of His saving power (v.10)
- **The Lord's Suffering Servant** – Introduced as one who will be exalted, yet also dishonored, causing kings to stand silent in awe (v.13-15)

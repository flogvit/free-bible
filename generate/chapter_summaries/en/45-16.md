**Main Theme:** Paul concludes the letter to the Romans with personal greetings, recommendations, and warnings to the church in Rome.

**Content:** Paul recommends Phoebe, a servant of the church at Cenchreae, and greets many fellow workers, including Priscilla and Aquila, who risked their lives for him. He warns against those who create divisions and lead others astray with flattery, promising that the God of peace will soon crush Satan. The chapter includes greetings from Paul's coworkers, among them Tertius, who wrote down the letter, and ends with a greeting of grace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Διάκονος (servant/deacon):** Phoebe is described as a servant in the church, an early testimony to women's service in the apostolic church.
- **Συνεργοί (fellow workers):** Paul mentions many fellow workers, showing how the gospel spread through teamwork.
- **Διχοστασίαι (divisions):** A warning against those who create strife and stumbling blocks contrary to the teaching received.
- **Φίλημα ἅγιον (holy kiss):** A greeting expressing Christian love and fellowship among believers.

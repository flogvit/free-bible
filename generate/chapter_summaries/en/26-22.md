**Main Theme:** God accuses Jerusalem ("the city of bloodshed") of its many sins and announces judgment through purifying fire.

**Content:** The chapter contains three oracles: First, Jerusalem is accused of bloodguilt, idolatry, dishonoring parents, oppressing foreigners, widows, and fatherless children, as well as sexual sins. Then the image of a smelting furnace is used—Israel has become like dross and will be melted in God's fiery wrath. Finally, all social classes are criticized: prophets, priests, rulers, and the people for corruption and lawbreaking. God sought someone who could "stand in the gap," but found none—therefore judgment comes.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The City of Bloodshed:** Jerusalem is called this because of violence, murder, and unjust judgments.
- **The Smelting Furnace:** Israel is compared to base metal that must be melted to separate out impurities.
- **Stand in the Gap:** An expression for intercession and the role of a mediator—none were found who could turn away judgment.
- **Dross (סִיג):** The people have become worthless like waste metal, without the precious silver that should be there.

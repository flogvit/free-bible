**Main Theme:** A hymn of praise that exalts the Lord's greatness and his gracious care for the lowly and helpless.

**Content:** The psalm opens and closes with "Hallelujah" and calls upon the servants of the Lord to praise his name from now on forever, from sunrise to sunset. The Lord is exalted above all nations, with his glory over the heavens, yet he stoops down to look at heaven and earth. He lifts the poor from the dust and the needy from the ash heap to set them among princes, and gives the childless woman a place as a joyful mother.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hallelujah:** The framework of the psalm (vv. 1, 9), an exhortation to praise the Lord (יָהּ).
- **The Name of the Lord:** God's name is to be blessed and praised universally—in time (forever) and space (all over the earth).
- **Exalted yet near:** God reigns on high, but stoops down to lift up the lowly and poor.
- **From dust to princes:** A picture of God's radical restoration of the needy to honor and dignity.

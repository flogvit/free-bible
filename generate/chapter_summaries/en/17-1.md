**Main Theme:** King Ahasuerus' magnificent banquet and Queen Vashti's downfall following her disobedience.

**Content:** King Ahasuerus of Persia, who rules over 127 provinces from India to Cush, holds a 180-day feast for his nobles, followed by a seven-day feast for everyone in the citadel of Susa. On the seventh day, the king commands Queen Vashti to display her beauty before the guests, but she refuses to come. Acting on the advice of Memukan, the king issues an irrevocable decree that Vashti shall never again appear before him and that her royal position be given to another—so that all women will honor their husbands.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Feast (מִשְׁתֶּה):** Symbolizes the king's immense wealth and power, used to showcase the glory of the empire.
- **Disobedience:** Vashti's refusal represents a challenge to royal authority with far-reaching consequences.
- **Persian Law:** The principle that royal decrees cannot be revoked is established here as a major theme in the book.
- **Memukan:** The advisor who fears that Vashti's example will spread contempt among women throughout the empire.

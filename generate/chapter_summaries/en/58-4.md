**Main Theme:** The chapter deals with God's rest that remains open to believers, and Jesus as the compassionate high priest.

**Content:** The author warns against missing out on God's rest due to unbelief, just as the Israelites did in the wilderness. There is still a Sabbath rest for God's people, and we are encouraged to strive to enter it. God's word is living and sharper than any two-edged sword, exposing the thoughts of the heart. Jesus, our great high priest, has passed through the heavens and can sympathize with our weaknesses because he was tempted in every way. Therefore, we may approach the throne of grace with confidence.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's rest (κατάπαυσις/σαββατισμός):** A spiritual rest that remains for God's people, connected to God's rest on the seventh day.
- **The living word of God:** Described as sharper than a two-edged sword, penetrating to the deepest parts and judging the thoughts of the heart.
- **High priest (ἀρχιερεύς):** Jesus who has passed through the heavens and can have compassion on us because he was tempted, yet without sin.
- **The throne of grace:** The place where believers may approach with confidence to receive mercy and grace to help in time of need.

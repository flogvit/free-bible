**Main Theme:** David's final instructions and Solomon's consolidation of the kingdom through righteous judgment.

**Content:** Before his death, David exhorts Solomon to keep the Lord's commands and gives him instructions to punish Joab and Shimei, as well as to show kindness to the sons of Barzillai. After David dies, Solomon strengthens his power by executing Adonijah (who had requested Abishag), banishing the priest Abiathar, and killing Joab and Shimei when they violated their conditions. David reigned for 40 years, and Solomon's kingdom was firmly established.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **David's Testament** – David's final words combine a spiritual exhortation to remain faithful to the Lord with political instructions for the stability of the realm.
- **The Altar Horns** – Joab sought refuge at the horns of the altar but found no protection from his past crimes.
- **The Brook Kidron** – The boundary Shimei was not allowed to cross; his disobedience led to his death and illustrates the consequences of breaking an oath.
- **Benaiah's Role** – Solomon's trusted servant who carried out the judgments and was appointed commander in Joab's place.

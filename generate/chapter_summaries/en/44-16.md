**Main Theme:** The Gospel reaches Europe through the guidance of the Holy Spirit, despite opposition and persecution.

**Content:** Paul takes Timothy with him and is led by the Spirit to Macedonia after a vision of a man pleading for help. In Philippi, Lydia, a dealer in purple cloth, comes to faith and is baptized along with her entire household. After Paul drives out a spirit of divination from a slave girl, he and Silas are beaten and imprisoned. An earthquake opens the prison doors, and the jailer comes to faith along with his family. Paul appeals to his Roman citizenship and leaves the city with an apology.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Macedonia Vision:** God's call to bring the Gospel to Europe, conveyed through a night vision.
- **Lydia:** The first European Christian, whose heart the Lord opened to the message.
- **Spirit of Divination (πνεῦμα πύθωνα):** An evil spirit that Paul casts out in Jesus' name, demonstrating Christ's power over spiritual forces.
- **The Jailer's Conversion:** Illustrates salvation coming through faith in the Lord Jesus – "you and your household."
- **Roman Citizenship:** Paul uses his legal status to protect believers and advance the cause of the Gospel.

**Main Theme:** God encourages the people to complete the temple construction with promises of future glory, blessing, and Zerubbabel’s selection.

**Content:** Haggai delivers three prophetic messages to Zerubbabel, the high priest Joshua, and the people. God assures them that the new temple will surpass the former one in glory and promises peace on this place. Through questions posed to the priests concerning purity and impurity, Haggai shows that the people’s previous disobedience had rendered their offerings unclean—but from now on, God will bless them. The chapter concludes with a messianic promise: God will shake heaven and earth, overthrow the kingdoms of the nations, and make Zerubbabel His chosen signet ring.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Temple:** The rebuilt temple will be filled with God’s glory and surpass Solomon’s temple in honor.
- **Purity and Impurity:** The priests’ teaching illustrates that impurity spreads more easily than holiness—such was the people’s condition before repentance.
- **Signet Ring (חוֹתָם):** Zerubbabel is chosen as God’s signet ring, a symbol of royal authority and God’s special selection within the Davidic line.
- **Shaking:** God will shake heaven, earth, and the nations—a cosmic upheaval that inaugurates God’s final dominion.

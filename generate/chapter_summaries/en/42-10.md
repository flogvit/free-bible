**Main Theme:** Jesus sends out 72 disciples to proclaim the kingdom of God and teaches about true love for one’s neighbor and what is most important.

**Content:** Jesus sends 72 disciples two by two to proclaim that the kingdom of God is near and to heal the sick. When they return with joy over their spiritual authority, Jesus reminds them that the greatest joy is that their names are written in heaven. Through the parable of the good Samaritan, Jesus answers the question of who our neighbor is: the one who shows mercy. In the account of Martha and Mary, Jesus teaches that listening to his word is what matters most.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few** – Illustrates the urgent need for proclaimers of the gospel.
- **The good Samaritan** – Shows that true love for one’s neighbor transcends cultural and religious boundaries.
- **The better portion** – Mary chose what was necessary: to listen to Jesus’ words rather than be distracted by many tasks.
- **Names written in heaven** – The deepest reason for joy is fellowship with God, not spiritual power.

**Main Theme:** God allows Satan to test Job with physical suffering, but Job holds fast to his integrity.

**Content:** Satan is given permission to strike Job's body, but must spare his life. Job is afflicted with painful boils all over his body and sits in the ashes. His wife urges him to curse God and die, but Job rejects this, saying they must accept both good and bad from God—and he does not sin with his lips. Three friends come to comfort him, and when they see his great pain, they sit with him in silence for seven days and nights.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Boils (שְׁחִין רָע):** The physical affliction that strikes Job from the sole of his foot to the crown of his head, symbolizing total bodily destruction.
- **Potsherd and ashes:** Job scrapes himself with a potsherd and sits in the ashes—signs of deep grief and humiliation.
- **"Skin for skin":** Satan's proverb suggesting that people will give up everything to save their own lives.
- **Seven days of silence:** The friends' compassionate silence shows the depth of the mourning ritual and respect for Job's suffering.

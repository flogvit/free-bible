**Main Theme:** True wisdom cannot be found through human effort, but is defined as the fear of God.

**Content:** The chapter begins by describing humanity's impressive ability to extract metals and precious stones from the depths of the earth. Then the question is posed: where can wisdom be found? Neither humans, the deep, the sea, nor the realm of the dead know it. Only God understands the way of wisdom and knows its origin, because He sees all things and created the order of nature. The conclusion is that true wisdom is to fear the Lord and turn away from evil.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mining** – A picture of human ingenuity and ability to find hidden treasures in the earth.
- **The Hidden Nature of Wisdom** – Wisdom cannot be bought with gold or precious stones; it is inaccessible to all created beings.
- **Fear of God (יִרְאַת אֲדֹנָי)** – The final definition: true wisdom is reverence for the Lord and turning away from evil.

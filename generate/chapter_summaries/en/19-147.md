**Main Theme:** A hymn of praise that extols God for His greatness in creation, His care for the humble, and His unique revelation to Israel.

**Content:** The psalm begins with a call to praise God, who builds Jerusalem and gathers the exiles of Israel. The Lord heals those with broken hearts and counts the stars—His understanding is beyond measure. God provides rain and food for all living things, yet He takes no delight in the strength of the horse; He values those who fear Him and hope in His unfailing love. The psalm concludes by stating that God has revealed His words and laws to Israel in a way no other nation has experienced.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Building Jerusalem** (v.2): God's restoration of His people after destruction and exile.
- **Healing broken hearts** (v.3): God's care for the grieving and suffering.
- **Counting the stars** (v.4): God's infinite wisdom and power over all creation.
- **God's word** (vv.15–18): God's sovereign command that governs nature—snow, frost, and wind.
- **Laws and commands to Israel** (vv.19–20): Israel's unique privilege as the recipient of God's revelation.

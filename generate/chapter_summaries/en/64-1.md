**Main Theme:** A personal letter encouraging hospitality toward traveling preachers and walking in the truth.

**Content:** The elder writes to his dear friend Gaius, expressing joy that he is walking in the truth. He commends Gaius for his hospitality toward traveling Christian brothers and urges him to continue supporting them. In contrast stands Diotrephes, who rejects John's authority and refuses to welcome the brothers. John recommends Demetrius, who has a good testimony, and exhorts: "Do not imitate what is evil, but what is good."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Walking in the truth** – Living in harmony with the truth of the gospel; John's greatest joy.
- **Hospitality** – Welcoming and supporting traveling preachers who go out for the sake of the Name.
- **Diotrephes** – An example of destructive leadership that seeks the foremost place and rejects apostolic authority.
- **Co-workers for the truth** – Believers become co-workers with the truth by supporting the preachers.

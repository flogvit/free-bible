**Main Theme:** God's judgment against four neighboring nations that showed hostility toward Judah at the fall of Jerusalem.

**Content:** Ezekiel prophesies against Ammon, Moab, Edom, and the Philistines. The Ammonites are punished because they rejoiced over the destruction of the temple and the exile of Judah—their land will be given to the people of the East, and Rabbah will become pasture for camels. Moab (along with Seir) is judged for saying that Judah is like all other nations. Edom is punished for acts of vengeance against Judah, and the Philistines for their long-standing hostility. All will know that the Lord is God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **People of the East (בְנֵי־קֶדֶם):** Nomadic tribes who will take over the lands of Ammon and Moab as instruments of God's judgment.
- **Rabbah as camel pasture:** The capital of Ammon will become desolate—a picture of total destruction.
- **"They shall know that I am the Lord":** A repeated formula emphasizing the purpose of judgment: the revelation of God's power.
- **Vengeance (נָקָם):** A key concept for Edom and the Philistines—their vengeance against Judah brings about God's retribution.

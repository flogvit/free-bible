**Main Theme:** Jesus' suffering, crucifixion, and death as the fulfillment of his saving work.

**Content:** Jesus is handed over to Pilate, who finds him innocent but yields to the crowd's demands and releases Barabbas. After being mocked and scourged, Jesus is crucified at Golgotha between two thieves. At his death, the temple curtain tears in two, and a Roman officer confesses him as God's Son. Joseph of Arimathea buries Jesus in a rock tomb, observed by the women who had followed him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Barabbas:** A rebel released instead of Jesus – illustrates the concept of substitution.
- **Temple Curtain:** Torn in two at Jesus' death – symbolizes free access to God through Christ.
- **"My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?":** Jesus' cry from Psalm 22 – expresses the depth of suffering and the fulfillment of Scripture.
- **The Officer's Confession:** A Gentile acknowledges Jesus as God's Son – points to the universal scope of the gospel.

**Main Theme:** Paul defends his faith before King Agrippa by testifying about his conversion and his calling to preach the gospel.

**Content:** Paul recounts his former life as a Pharisee and persecutor of Christians, then describes his encounter with the risen Jesus on the road to Damascus. He explains that his mission is to proclaim repentance to both Jews and Gentiles, in agreement with the prophets. Festus interrupts and calls him mad, but Agrippa admits he is almost convinced. Finally, it is concluded that Paul could have been released if he had not appealed to Caesar.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The heavenly revelation** – The light from heaven that surpassed the brilliance of the sun, where Jesus called Paul to service (v.13-16).
- **From darkness to light** – The core of Paul's mission: turning people from darkness to light and from Satan's power to God (v.18).
- **The hope of the twelve tribes** – Paul asserts that his message about the resurrection fulfills Israel's ancient hope (v.6-7).
- **Not done in a corner** – Paul's argument that Jesus' life, death, and resurrection were public events, not hidden myths (v.26).

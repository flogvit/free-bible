**Main Theme:** God leads Abraham’s servant to find Rebekah as a wife for Isaac, thus continuing the covenant promise to the next generation.

**Content:** Abraham sends his trusted servant to his relatives in Mesopotamia to find a wife for Isaac—not from the Canaanites. The servant prays for God’s guidance and receives a clear sign at the well: Rebekah gives him and his camels water. Her family agrees to the marriage and sees the Lord’s hand in what has happened. Rebekah travels to Canaan, meets Isaac, and becomes his wife.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Well:** A meeting place where God reveals His will through Rebekah’s hospitality.
- **Oath (Promise):** The servant swears to follow Abraham’s instructions to find a wife from his relatives, ensuring the continuity of the covenant promise.
- **God’s hesed (faithfulness):** The servant praises God for not abandoning His grace and truth toward Abraham (v. 27).
- **Veiling:** Rebekah covers herself with a veil when she sees Isaac—a sign of honor and transition into marriage.

**Main Theme:** God comforts His people with promises of salvation and reveals His incomparable greatness and care.

**Content:** The chapter opens with God's command to comfort Jerusalem—their time of punishment is over. A voice cries out to prepare the way for the Lord in the wilderness. God’s glory will be revealed to all. The prophet contrasts human transience with God’s eternal word and incomparable power—He sits enthroned above the earth, and the nations are like a drop in a bucket. The chapter concludes with the promise that those who wait on the Lord will receive renewed strength.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Comfort (נַחֲמוּ):** God’s fundamental message to His suffering people—their guilt has been paid.
- **The Voice in the Wilderness:** One crying out to clear a way for the Lord’s coming, an image of spiritual preparation.
- **Grass and Flower:** Human transience contrasted with God’s word that stands forever.
- **Shepherd:** God tenderly gathering the lambs and carrying them in His bosom.
- **Eagle’s Wings:** Those who hope in the Lord will renew their strength and soar on wings like eagles.

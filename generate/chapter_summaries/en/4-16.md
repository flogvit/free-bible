**Main Theme:** Korah’s rebellion against the leadership of Moses and Aaron, and God’s judgment on the rebels.

**Content:** Korah (a Levite), Dathan, Abiram, and On (from the tribe of Reuben) gather 250 leaders and challenge the authority of Moses and Aaron, claiming that the entire congregation is holy. Moses falls on his face and proposes a test: the rebels are to offer incense before the Lord, so that God will show whom He has chosen. God reveals His glory and intends to destroy the whole congregation, but Moses and Aaron intercede. The earth opens up and swallows Korah, Dathan, Abiram, and their families, while fire from the Lord consumes the 250 who offered incense.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Incense Shovels (מַחְתּוֹת):** The instrument used in the test to determine whom God had chosen for priestly service.
- **The Earth Opens Up:** God’s supernatural judgment, showing that the rebellion was against the Lord Himself, not merely against Moses.
- **"The whole congregation is holy" (רַב־לָכֶם):** The rebels’ argument rejecting the divinely appointed order of chosen priests and Levites.
- **Fire from the Lord:** God’s direct intervention that consumes the 250 who unlawfully performed priestly duties.

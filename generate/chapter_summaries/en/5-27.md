**Main Theme:** Moses gives instructions about covenant renewal with stones, an altar, and curses when Israel crosses the Jordan.

**Content:** Moses and the elders of Israel command the people to set up large stones on Mount Ebal, cover them with plaster, and write the entire law on them. There they are also to build an altar of uncut stones and offer sacrifices to the Lord. Six tribes are to stand on Mount Gerizim to pronounce blessings, and six on Mount Ebal for the curses. The Levites proclaim twelve curses against those who break the covenant—including idolatry, disrespect for parents, injustice, and sexual sins—and the people respond "Amen" to each one.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Stones with the law:** A visible witness of the covenant that will remind the people of their obligation to God.
- **Altar of uncut stones:** Symbolizes worship without human manipulation, a pure surrender to God.
- **Gerizim and Ebal:** Two mountains representing the choice between blessing and curse.
- **The Curses (ארור):** Twelve declarations marking the seriousness of breaking God's commands, especially in secret.
- **Amen:** The people's solemn affirmation and acceptance of the covenant terms.

**Main Theme:** A pilgrim song expressing joy at going to Jerusalem and praying for peace in the holy city.

**Content:** David expresses his joy when invited to go to the house of the Lord. Jerusalem is described as a city built closely together, where the tribes ascend to give thanks to the name of the Lord. The city is also the seat of justice and the thrones of David. The psalm encourages praying for peace in Jerusalem and ends with a promise to seek the good of the city for the sake of one's brothers and the house of the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **House of the Lord (בֵּית יְהוָה):** The temple in Jerusalem as the destination of the pilgrimage and the center of worship.
- **Shalom/peace (שָׁלוֹם):** Repeated several times as a prayer for Jerusalem's welfare, security, and wholeness.
- **Tribes (שְׁבָטִים):** The tribes of Israel gathering in Jerusalem as a testimony and to give thanks to God.
- **Thrones for judgment (כִסְאוֹת לְמִשְׁפָּט):** Jerusalem as the seat of royal and judicial authority under the house of David.

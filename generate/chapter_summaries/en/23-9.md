**Main Theme:** Isaiah 9 contrasts messianic hope with judgment upon an unrepentant Israel.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a promise of great light for the people in darkness and deliverance from oppression, compared to the day of Midian. A child will be born with divine titles and will reign on David's throne with righteousness and peace without end. The rest of the chapter describes God's judgment upon the Northern Kingdom (Ephraim/Samaria) for their pride and wickedness—with repeated warnings that "his anger has not turned away, his hand is still stretched out."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Light in darkness:** A symbol of salvation and hope for an oppressed people.
- **Prince of Peace:** A messianic title pointing to a ruler with divine authority and an eternal kingdom of peace.
- **The day of Midian:** An allusion to Gideon's victory—God will again grant unexpected deliverance.
- **Stretched-out hand:** A recurring image of God's continuing wrath upon a people who refuse to turn back.

**Main Theme:** A lament that holds fast to God's eternal covenant promises to David amid apparent breach and suffering.

**Content:** The psalm opens with praise of God's faithfulness (חֶסֶד) and His eternal covenant with David concerning an enduring dynasty. It extols God's incomparable power over creation and heavenly beings. Midway through, the tone shifts dramatically to lament that the covenant seems broken—the throne is dishonored and enemies triumph. The psalmist concludes by crying out to God to remember His promises and the shame of His servants.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant (בְּרִית):** God's sworn promise to David of an eternal dynasty and throne.
- **Faithfulness (אֱמוּנָה):** God's unfailing reliability as the foundation for hope even in crisis.
- **The Anointed One (מָשִׁיחַ):** The Davidic king who bears God's promises, now seemingly rejected.
- **Throne cast to the ground:** A picture of the dynasty's fall and the painful contrast with the promises.
- **Rahab:** The sea monster symbolizing chaotic forces that God has defeated, a testimony to His power.

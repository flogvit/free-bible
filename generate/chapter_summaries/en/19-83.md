**Main Theme:** A prayer for God's intervention against a coalition of enemies threatening Israel's existence.

**Content:** Asaph cries out to God not to remain silent while enemies conspire against his people. Ten nations have allied with the aim of completely wiping out Israel. The psalmist asks God to act as in the past against Midian, Sisera, and Jabin, and to humble the enemies so that they acknowledge that the Lord alone is the Most High over all the earth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Enemy Alliance** (vv. 7–9): Ten nations, including Edom, the Ishmaelites, Moab, Ammon, and Assyria, gathered against Israel.
- **Historical Victories** (vv. 10–12): References to God's past interventions against Midian and Canaanite kings.
- **Chaff Before the Wind** (vv. 14–15): A picture of how God will scatter and destroy the enemies.
- **"Acknowledge that you alone are the Lord" (v. 19):** The ultimate goal of the psalm is for all to recognize God's sovereignty.

**Main Theme:** Jacob's reconciliation with Esau and settlement in Canaan.

**Content:** Jacob meets Esau with deep reverence—he bows seven times and arranges the family with the servant women first, followed by Rachel and Joseph at the rear. Against all expectations, Esau runs to meet him, embraces him, and weeps. Jacob insists that Esau accept the gift as "my blessing." The brothers part in peace; Jacob travels to Succoth, then to Shechem, where he buys land and erects an altar called "El, the God of Israel."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Seven bows** – Jacob's profound submission shows respect and humility toward his brother.
- **Embrace and tears** – The emotional encounter marks forgiveness and reconciliation.
- **"To see your face is like seeing God's face"** – Jacob's words express relief at meeting grace instead of vengeance.
- **The altar El-Elohe-Israel** – Jacob confesses that God is the God of Israel, marking his new identity.

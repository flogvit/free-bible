**Main Theme:** Paul gives guidance on prayer for all people and on orderly conduct in the congregation.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an exhortation to pray and give thanks for all people, including kings and those in authority, so that believers may live peaceful lives in godliness. The theological basis is that God desires all to be saved, and that Christ Jesus is the one mediator who gave himself as a ransom. Practical instructions follow: men are to pray with holy hands, without anger or quarreling; women are to adorn themselves with good deeds rather than outward display, and to learn in quietness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mediator (μεσίτης):** Christ Jesus is the sole mediator between God and humanity.
- **Ransom (ἀντίλυτρον):** Christ gave himself as a payment to set all people free.
- **Quietness (ἡσυχία):** Women are encouraged to learn in quietness and peace during the congregation's gathering.
- **Godliness (εὐσέβεια/θεοσέβεια):** The goal of a peaceful life and good deeds is a life of true worship.

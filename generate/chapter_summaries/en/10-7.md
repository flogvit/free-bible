**Main Theme:** God rejects David's plan to build a temple, but instead promises to build David an eternal royal house (dynasty).

**Content:** When David expresses his desire to build a house (temple) for God, God responds through the prophet Nathan with a surprising promise: It is not David who will build a house for God, but God who will build a house (dynasty) for David. God promises that David's descendant will build the temple and that his royal throne will stand firm forever. David answers with a humble prayer in which he praises God's greatness, marvels at his own unworthiness, and asks that the promise be fulfilled.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **House (bayit):** A play on words that ties the chapter together—David wants to build God a house (temple), but God will build David a house (dynasty).
- **Eternal throne:** God's promise that David's royal line will endure forever, a promise pointing toward the Messiah.
- **Father–son relationship:** God promises to be like a father to David's descendant, expressing a close covenantal bond.
- **David's prayer:** The king's humble response shows gratitude and awe at God's undeserved grace.

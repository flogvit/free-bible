**Main Theme:** A psalm of Asaph that praises God for his righteous judgment against the proud and his exaltation of the righteous.

**Content:** The psalm opens with thanksgiving to God because his name is near and his wonders are proclaimed. God himself speaks and declares that at the appointed time he will judge righteously and keep the pillars of the earth firm. The proud are warned against boasting and lifting up their horn, for it is God alone who humbles and exalts. The psalm concludes with the psalmist's promise to sing praises to the God of Jacob forever, with the certainty that the horns of the wicked will be cut off while the horns of the righteous will be lifted up.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Horn (קרן):** A symbol of power and pride; the horns of the wicked will be cut off, while those of the righteous will be exalted (vv. 5–6, 11).
- **God's Cup (כוס):** A cup of mixed wine in the Lord's hand that the wicked must drink to the dregs—a picture of God's judgment (v. 9).
- **Pillars of the Earth (עמודים):** God holds the world steady amid chaos; an expression of his sovereign control (v. 4).
- **Appointed Time (מועד):** God has set the time for his judgment; humans cannot hasten or hinder it (v. 3).

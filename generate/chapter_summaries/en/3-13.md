**Main Theme:** The chapter provides rules for how the priest should evaluate skin diseases and fabric damage to determine ritual purity or impurity.

**Content:** When a person has suspicious skin changes, they must be brought to the priest for examination. The priest assesses the symptoms and may declare the person clean or unclean, or isolate them for seven days for further observation. Those who are unclean must live outside the camp with torn garments and a covered mouth, crying out "unclean, unclean!" The chapter also addresses *tsara'at* in clothing and leather, which must be washed or burned.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Tsara'at (צָרַעַת):** A collective term for various skin diseases and fabric damage that result in ritual impurity.
- **Clean/Unclean (טָהוֹר/טָמֵא):** The central distinction the priest must determine, deciding a person's status within the community.
- **The Priest as Judge:** Aaron and his sons have exclusive authority to evaluate and declare purity.
- **Isolation Outside the Camp:** The unclean must live separately to preserve the holiness of the camp.

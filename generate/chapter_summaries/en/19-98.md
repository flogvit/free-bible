**Main Theme:** A call to all creation to praise the Lord for his salvation and coming righteous judgment.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a call to sing a new song because the Lord has done wonderful deeds and won victory with his right hand. He has revealed his salvation to the nations and remembered his grace and faithfulness toward the house of Israel. All the earth is called to rejoice with song and instruments, and nature itself—sea, rivers, and mountains—is invited to shout for joy before the Lord who comes to judge the earth with righteousness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **New song (שִׁיר חָדָשׁ):** Expresses a response to God's new acts of salvation and wonderful deeds.
- **God's right hand (יְמִינוֹ):** A symbol of God's power and victory that brings salvation.
- **Ends of the earth (אַפְסֵי־אָרֶץ):** Shows the universal scope of salvation—all peoples are witnesses.
- **Nature rejoices:** The sea roars, rivers clap their hands, mountains sing—the whole creation praises.
- **Righteous judgment (בְּצֶדֶק):** The Lord comes to judge the earth with righteousness and justice.

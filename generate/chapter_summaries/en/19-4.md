**Main Theme:** An evening psalm about trust in God, who gives peace and security amid opposition.

**Content:** David addresses God as his God of righteousness and asks to be heard. He confronts opponents who chase after emptiness and lies, reminding them that the Lord has chosen the godly. The psalm calls for silence, self-examination, and righteous offerings. David concludes with deep joy and peace—he can lie down and sleep safely because the Lord alone gives him security.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God of righteousness (אֱלֹהֵי צִדְקִי):** God who defends and justifies the psalmist in distress.
- **Light of your face (אוֹר פָּנֶיךָ):** God's gracious presence and blessing that the psalmist seeks.
- **Joy in my heart (שִׂמְחָה בְלִבִּי):** An inner joy from God that surpasses material wealth.
- **Peace/shalom (שָׁלוֹם):** The deep security that enables the psalmist to rest in God alone.

**Main Theme:** Paul defends himself against accusations from Jewish leaders before Governor Felix and remains in custody for two years.

**Content:** The high priest Ananias and the orator Tertullus accuse Paul of stirring up unrest among Jews and attempting to desecrate the temple. Paul defends himself by stating that his accusers cannot prove their claims, and he confesses that he worships the God of our ancestors according to "the Way," with faith in the resurrection. Felix postpones the case but keeps Paul under mild custody for two years, partly because he hopes for a bribe. When Felix is succeeded by Festus, he leaves Paul in prison to win favor with the Jews.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Way (ὁδός):** A designation for the Christian movement that Paul belongs to, called a "sect" by his opponents.
- **Resurrection:** The core of Paul's faith and defense—the belief that both the righteous and the unrighteous will be raised.
- **Clear Conscience:** Paul strives to maintain an irreproachable conscience before God and people.
- **Felix's Fear:** The governor becomes afraid when Paul speaks about righteousness, self-control, and the coming judgment.

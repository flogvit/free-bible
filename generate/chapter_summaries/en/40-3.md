**Main Theme:** John the Baptist prepares the way for the Messiah through a baptism of repentance, and Jesus is baptized and confirmed as God’s Son.

**Content:** John the Baptist preaches in the wilderness of Judea, calling the people to repent because the kingdom of heaven is near. He warns the Pharisees and Sadducees against false security based on their ancestry and demands fruit that matches true repentance. John points to a mightier one who is coming, who will baptize with the Holy Spirit and fire. Jesus comes from Galilee to be baptized; although John hesitates, Jesus insists in order to "fulfill all righteousness." At his baptism, the heavens open, the Spirit descends like a dove, and the Father’s voice confirms Jesus as His beloved Son.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Repentance (metanoia):** A radical change of mind and complete turning around that John requires as preparation for God’s kingdom.
- **Voice in the wilderness:** John fulfills Isaiah’s prophecy as the one crying out in the wilderness, preparing the way for the Lord.
- **Baptism with Spirit and fire:** The contrast between John’s water baptism and Jesus’ coming baptism, which brings both cleansing and judgment.
- **The opened heaven:** At Jesus’ baptism, the heavens open as a sign of restored connection between God and humanity.
- **God’s Son:** The Father’s declaration confirms Jesus’ divine identity and mission.

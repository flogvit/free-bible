**Main Theme:** God warns of the tenth and decisive plague—the death of the firstborn—which will force Pharaoh to release Israel.

**Content:** The Lord tells Moses that He will send one final plague upon Egypt: At midnight, all the firstborn shall die, from the son of Pharaoh on the throne to the daughter of the slave girl behind the mill, and also the firstborn of the livestock. There will be great crying in Egypt, but against the Israelites not even a dog shall bark—thus showing the Lord that He makes a distinction between Egypt and Israel. The Israelites are to ask the Egyptians for articles of silver and gold, and God grants them favor. Moses leaves Pharaoh in burning anger after prophesying that Pharaoh’s servants will come and bow down before him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Firstborn (בְּכוֹר):** All the firstborn in Egypt shall die, from the highest to the lowest—a blow against Egypt’s future and idolatry.
- **Midnight (חֲצֹת הַלַּיְלָה):** The timing of God’s judgment underscores His sovereign intervention in history.
- **The Distinction Between Egypt and Israel:** God makes a clear separation, shown by the fact that not even a dog shall bark against the Israelites.
- **Silver and Gold:** The Israelites receive favor and take valuables from the Egyptians—fulfilling the promise to Abraham.
- **Hardened Heart:** The Lord hardened Pharaoh’s heart in order to multiply His signs in Egypt (vv. 9–10).

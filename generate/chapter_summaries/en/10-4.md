**Main Theme:** The murder of Ish-bosheth and David’s righteous judgment over the murderers.

**Content:** After Abner’s death, Ish-bosheth loses heart, and all Israel is dismayed. Two army commanders, Rechab and Baana, kill Ish-bosheth while he rests in the middle of the day and bring his head to David in Hebron. They expect a reward, but David condemns them as wicked men who have slain an innocent man and orders their execution. The head of Ish-bosheth is buried in Abner’s tomb.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Midday Rest:** Ish-bosheth was killed at his most vulnerable moment, highlighting the cowardice of the act.
- **Righteous Man (צַדִּיק):** David calls Ish-bosheth righteous and shows respect for Saul’s house despite their enmity.
- **Mephibosheth:** Jonathan’s lame son is mentioned, showing that Saul’s lineage continues.
- **God’s Vengeance:** The murderers claim to act according to God’s will, but David rejects this and carries out just punishment.

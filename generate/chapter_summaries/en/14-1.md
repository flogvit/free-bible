**Main Theme:** God gives Solomon wisdom and wealth in response to his humble prayer at Gibeon.

**Content:** Solomon strengthened his kingdom and gathered the leaders of Israel to go to the high place at Gibeon, where the Tent of Meeting stood. He offered a thousand burnt offerings on the bronze altar, and that night God appeared to him and offered to grant whatever he asked. Solomon requested wisdom and knowledge to lead the people, and God gave him this, along with unmatched wealth and honor. The chapter concludes with Solomon's riches and his decision to build the Lord's temple.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gibeon:** The place where the Tent of Meeting and the bronze altar stood, where Solomon sought God through sacrifice.
- **Wisdom and Knowledge (חָכְמָה וּמַדָּע):** What Solomon asked for in order to judge and lead God's great people justly.
- **The Bronze Altar:** The altar made by Bezalel, on which Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings.
- **God's Promise:** Because Solomon sought wisdom rather than wealth or honor, God gave him all these things as well.

**Main Theme:** God announces judgment against Israel for covenant breaking, idolatry, and false political alliances.

**Content:** Israel has broken God's covenant and rebelled against his law. They have set up kings without God's approval and made idols from their silver and gold, especially the calf in Samaria. Instead of seeking help from God, they turn to Assyria. Even though they offer sacrifices, God does not accept them. As punishment, they will return to an Egypt-like captivity, and fire will consume the cities of both Israel and Judah.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sow the wind, reap the whirlwind** (v.7): This image shows that Israel's empty actions will lead to catastrophic consequences.
- **The calf of Samaria** (vv.5–6): An idol made by humans that will be shattered into pieces—it is no god at all.
- **A vessel no one wants** (v.8): Because of their unfaithfulness, Israel has become worthless among the nations.
- **Altars for sinning** (v.11): Ephraim's many altars, intended for worship, have become occasions for sin.

**Main Theme:** God appoints Joshua as leader after Moses and commands him to lead Israel into the promised land.

**Content:** After Moses' death, God speaks to Joshua and gives him the task of leading the people across the Jordan into Canaan. God promises to be with Joshua just as He was with Moses, and commands him three times to be strong and courageous. Joshua must hold fast to the Torah day and night in order to prosper. Joshua orders the people to prepare themselves and reminds the Transjordan tribes (Reuben, Gad, and half of Manasseh) to help their brothers before returning home. The people promise to obey Joshua just as they had obeyed Moses.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Be strong and courageous" (חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ):** God's repeated exhortation to Joshua (vv. 6, 7, 9, 18) – courage comes from God's presence.
- **The Torah (סֵפֶר הַתּוֹרָה):** The Book of the Law must not depart from Joshua's mouth; meditation and obedience are the keys to success.
- **Jordan:** The river marking the boundary of the promised land; crossing it symbolizes the transition into a new era.
- **The Promised Land:** The land God swore to give the fathers, with boundaries from the wilderness to Lebanon and from the Euphrates to the Mediterranean Sea.

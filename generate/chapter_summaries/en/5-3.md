**Main Theme:** Moses recounts the victory over King Og of Bashan and the distribution of the territories east of the Jordan.

**Content:** Israel defeated King Og of Bashan and captured all sixty of his cities. Moses allocated the lands to Reuben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh, but required the armed men to assist the other tribes in conquering the territory west of the Jordan. Moses earnestly pleaded to enter the Promised Land, but God refused and instead commanded him to strengthen Joshua as his successor.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Og's iron bedstead:** A symbol of the giant king's size – the bed was nine cubits long (v. 11).
- **Sixty cities:** The extent of the conquest in the region of Argob demonstrates God's mighty help.
- **The top of Pisgah:** The place where Moses was only allowed to see, but not enter, the Promised Land.
- **"Do not fear":** God's encouragement to Israel before battle, a guarantee of victory.

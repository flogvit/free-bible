**Main Theme:** Elihu explains how God speaks to people through dreams and suffering to save them from destruction.

**Content:** Elihu addresses Job directly, claiming to speak sincerely by the Spirit of God, while emphasizing that he is Job's equal, formed from the same clay. He quotes Job's claim of being innocent and unjustly accused by God, correcting this by stating that God is greater than humans. Elihu describes how God speaks through nighttime dreams and through sickness and suffering to turn people away from sin. If an angel acts as an intercessor and finds a ransom, the person may be healed, restored, and confess their sins with thanksgiving.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Spirit of God (רוּחַ־אֵל):** Elihu claims that the Spirit of God has created him and gives him authority to speak.
- **Dream and vision (חֲלוֹם חֶזְיוֹן לַיְלָה):** A way God reveals Himself and warns people.
- **The angel as mediator (מַלְאָךְ מֵלִיץ):** A heavenly intermediary who can intercede for the suffering person.
- **Ransom (כֹּפֶר):** The price paid to rescue a person from the grave.
- **Light of life (אוֹר הַחַיִּים):** The goal of God's intervention—that people may live in the light.

**Main Theme:** The division of the kingdom as a result of Rehoboam’s unwise rejection of the people’s plea.

**Content:** After Solomon’s death, Israel gathers at Shechem to make Rehoboam king. The people, led by Jeroboam, ask him to lighten the burdens from Solomon’s time. Rehoboam rejects the wise counsel of the elders and instead follows the advice of the young men to respond harshly. The text emphasizes that this was a turning from God to fulfill the prophecy given through Ahijah of Shiloh. The northern kingdom breaks away from the house of David, and Rehoboam must flee to Jerusalem after the overseer Hadoram is stoned.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Yoke** – A symbol of the tax burdens and forced labor that the people asked to have lightened.
- **The elders’ counsel vs. the young men’s counsel** – The contrast between wisdom and immaturity in leadership.
- **Scorpions** – Rehoboam’s threat of even harsher treatment than his father’s "whips."
- **"What portion do we have in David?"** – The cry of rebellion that marks the final split of the kingdom.

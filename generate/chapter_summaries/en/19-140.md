**Main Theme:** A prayer from David for protection against violent and wicked enemies, with trust in God's righteous judgment.

**Content:** David asks God to rescue him from evil people who plot harm and set traps for him. He confesses that the Lord is his God and the strength of his salvation, who protects him on the day of battle. The psalm concludes with the certainty that God will plead the cause of the poor and needy, and that the righteous shall praise His name.

**Key Words/Images:**
- *Tongues like serpents* (v.4): The enemies' speech is compared to serpent venom—a picture of deadly, malicious words.
- *Traps and snares* (v.6): The proud have laid nets and traps—images of hidden schemes against the righteous.
- *Shield in the day of battle* (v.8): God covers David's head in combat—an image of divine protection.
- *The cause of the poor* (v.13): The Lord pleads the case for the helpless—an expression of God's justice and care.

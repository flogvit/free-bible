**Main Theme:** Paul encourages the Corinthians to complete their collection for the saints in Jerusalem with a generous and joyful heart.

**Content:** Paul sends coworkers ahead to ensure that the gift promised by the Corinthians is ready when he arrives with the Macedonians. He emphasizes the principle of sowing generously to reap generously, stating that God loves a cheerful giver. Generous giving meets not only the needs of the saints but also leads to thanksgiving and praise to God from many. The chapter concludes with thanks to God for his indescribable gift.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sow and Reap (v. 6):** Whoever sows sparingly will reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will reap generously – a picture of the blessings of generosity.
- **Cheerful Giver (v. 7):** God loves the one who gives willingly from the heart, not out of compulsion or reluctance.
- **God’s Grace and Abundance (v. 8):** God can supply all grace in abundance so that believers always have enough for every good work.
- **Indescribable Gift (v. 15):** Paul concludes with thanks for God’s indescribable gift – likely a reference to Christ or God’s saving grace.

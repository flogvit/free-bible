**Main Theme:** Christian freedom must be limited by love and consideration for weaker believers.

**Content:** Paul addresses the issue of food sacrificed to idols. He acknowledges that there is only one God (the Father) and one Lord (Jesus Christ), and that idols have no real existence. Nevertheless, he warns against allowing this knowledge to become a stumbling block for weaker believers, because wounding their conscience is to sin against Christ himself. Paul declares that he would rather give up eating meat forever than cause a brother to fall.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Knowledge (γνῶσις):** Insight that can lead to pride if not guided by love.
- **Love (ἀγάπη):** Builds up the community and should carry more weight than personal freedom.
- **Weak conscience (συνείδησις ἀσθενής):** Describes believers who do not yet have full freedom regarding food sacrificed to idols.
- **Stumbling block (πρόσκομμα):** Something that may cause a fellow believer to fall in their faith.

**Main Theme:** Solomon's dedicatory prayer at the temple, in which he asks God to hear the people's prayers in various situations of distress.

**Content:** Solomon blesses the assembly and praises God for fulfilling His promise to David that his son would build the temple. From a bronze platform, he kneels and offers an extensive prayer, acknowledging that even the highest heavens cannot contain God. He asks that God will hear prayers directed toward the temple in seven situations: disputes involving oaths, military defeat, drought, famine and plague, individual cries for help, prayers from foreigners, war and captivity. The prayer concludes with a plea for God to come to His resting place with the ark of the covenant.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bronze Platform** – A 5x5x3 cubit platform where Solomon kneels before the entire assembly in humble prayer.
- **The Heaven of Heavens** – An expression indicating that God is too great to be contained by any earthly structure, even the temple itself.
- **Forgiveness and Repentance** – A recurring theme: when the people sin and turn back, God will hear and forgive.
- **Ark of Strength** – The ark of the covenant as a symbol of God's presence and power among His people.

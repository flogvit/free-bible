**Main Theme:** A hymn of praise that exalts God as Creator and Ruler, and encourages trust in his faithful love.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a call to the righteous to sing praises to the Lord with harp and lyre, singing a new song. It proclaims that God created heaven and earth by his word, and that his counsel stands firm forever while the plans of nations are brought to nothing. The psalm warns against trusting in military might, for the eye of the Lord is upon those who fear him and hope in his mercy. In conclusion, the people declare their trust: "Our soul waits for the Lord – he is our help and our shield."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **New song (שִׁיר חָדָשׁ):** An expression of continually renewed praise in response to God's greatness.
- **The word of the Lord (דְבַר יְהוָה):** God's creative and sustaining power – by his word the heavens were made.
- **The eye of the Lord (עֵין יְהוָה):** God's attentive care for those who fear him and hope in his mercy.
- **Chesed (חֶסֶד):** God's faithful love that fills the earth, and in whom believers place their hope.

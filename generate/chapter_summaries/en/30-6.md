**Main Theme:** Amos warns against self-satisfied luxury and indifference, which will lead to exile and destruction.

**Content:** The prophet cries woe over the leaders in Zion and Samaria who live in extravagant luxury with ivory beds, feasts, and wine, while they do not grieve over the impending fall of Joseph (Israel). God swears by Himself that He abhors Jacob's pride and will hand the city over. A hostile nation shall oppress Israel from Hamat to the Arabah valley.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ivory beds (מִטּוֹת שֵׁן):** A symbol of extravagant luxury and self-satisfaction among the elite.
- **The ruin of Joseph (שֵׁבֶר יוֹסֵף):** The nation's coming destruction that the leaders ignore.
- **Justice turned to poison (מִשְׁפָּט לְרֹאשׁ):** A picture of how justice has been perverted into something poisonous.
- **God's oath (נִשְׁבַּע אֲדֹנָי בְּנַפְשׁוֹ):** The Lord's solemn oath by Himself underscores the inevitability of judgment.

**Main Theme:** God promises restoration, healing, and an everlasting covenant with the house of David and the Levites, despite Jerusalem's destruction.

**Content:** While Jeremiah is imprisoned in the guard court, God reveals promises of healing and restoration for Jerusalem and Judah. He will raise up a righteous Branch from David who will execute justice and righteousness. God confirms His everlasting covenant with the royal house of David and the Levitical priests, comparing its unchangeable nature to the order of creation—day and night. The descendants of the people shall become as countless as the host of heaven and the sand on the seashore.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Righteous Branch (צֶמַח צְדָקָה):** A messianic title for the coming King from David's line who will bring righteousness.
- **The Lord Our Righteousness (יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ):** The new name for Jerusalem, expressing that the city's salvation comes from God alone.
- **Covenant with Day and Night:** God's natural order is used as a guarantee that His promises to David and the Levites will stand firm.
- **Healing and Peace (אֲרֻכָה וּמַרְפֵּא):** God's promise to heal the people's wounds and give them lasting peace and truth.

**Main Theme:** Prophecy concerning the destruction of Moab and an exhortation to humility before Zion and its coming righteous ruler.

**Content:** Moab is urged to send tribute to the ruler of Zion and provide shelter for refugees from Judah. Amidst the prophecy of judgment, a vision emerges of a righteous king on David's throne. Moab's pride is condemned, and the chapter describes mourning over ruined vineyards and fertile fields. The Lord announces that Moab's glory will be disgraced within three years.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **David's throne (v. 5):** A messianic vision of a righteous ruler who judges with truth and promotes justice.
- **Moab's pride (v. 6):** Moab's arrogance and empty boasting are highlighted as the cause of judgment.
- **The vineyard's destruction (vv. 8–10):** An image of total economic ruin—the vineyards in Heshbon and Sibma lie desolate.
- **Three years (v. 14):** A timed prophecy indicating when Moab's glory will come to an end.

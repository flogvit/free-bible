**Main Theme:** God's judgment against Tyre for their schadenfreude and greed at the fall of Jerusalem.

**Content:** God declares that He will send many nations against Tyre, led by King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon, because the city rejoiced over Jerusalem's destruction and hoped to profit from it. The city will be besieged, its walls torn down, and it will become a bare rock where fishermen spread their nets to dry. The princes of the coastal lands will tremble and sing a lament over the fall of the mighty sea city, and Tyre will be brought down to the realm of the dead, never to be rebuilt again.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bare mountain/rock foundation:** Tyre will be stripped down to bare rock – an image of total destruction and humiliation.
- **The sea and waves:** God will raise up nations against Tyre like the waves of the sea – symbolizing irresistible judgment.
- **Lament (qinah):** The coastal princes sing a funeral song – showing Tyre's significance and the height from which it fell.
- **The grave/the realm of the dead:** Tyre is brought down to those who go into the grave – expressing final and irreversible ruin.

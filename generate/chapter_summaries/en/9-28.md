**Main Theme:** Saul's desperate search for guidance through forbidden necromancy leads to the judgment of his kingdom.

**Content:** King Saul, afraid of the Philistine army, receives no answer from the Lord through dreams, Urim, or prophets. Despite having himself banned mediums in the land, he seeks out a medium at En-Dor to summon Samuel. The deceased prophet rebukes Saul and announces that the Lord has torn the kingdom from him because of his disobedience regarding Amalek, and that Saul and his sons will die in battle against the Philistines the next day.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Medium (בַּעֲלַת־אוֹב):** One who calls up the dead—a practice forbidden by Israel's law, which Saul himself had eradicated.
- **God's Silence:** The Lord did not answer Saul through any legitimate channel, showing the broken relationship between God and the king.
- **Samuel's Mantle (מְעִיל):** The identifying garment that points to Samuel, reminding of when he announced that the kingdom was torn from Saul.
- **Amalek:** The reference to Saul's disobedience explains why judgment is now being carried out.

**Main Theme:** God's final revelation through the Son, who is exalted above all angels.

**Content:** In the past, God spoke through the prophets, but now He has spoken through His Son, who is the heir of all things and the one through whom the world was created. The Son is the radiance of God's glory and the exact imprint of His nature, and after making purification for sins, He sat down at the right hand of Majesty. Through a series of Old Testament quotations, the author shows that the Son has a far more exalted name and position than angels, who are ministering spirits sent to serve those who will inherit salvation.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Radiance of God's glory (ἀπαύγασμα):** The Son reflects and communicates God's glory in a unique way.
- **Sitting at the right hand:** Expresses the exalted position of the Son and His completed work of salvation.
- **Heir (κληρονόμος):** The Son is appointed as heir of all things, underscoring His sovereign rule.
- **Ministering spirits:** Angels are servants sent for the sake of those who will be saved, in contrast to the royal throne of the Son.

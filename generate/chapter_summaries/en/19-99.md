**Main Theme:** Psalm 99 is a hymn of praise celebrating the Lord's holiness and his righteous rule as king over all nations.

**Content:** The Lord reigns above the cherubim in Zion, exalted above all peoples, and the earth trembles before him. He loves justice and has established right and righteousness in Jacob. Moses, Aaron, and Samuel called upon him and were answered; he spoke to them in the pillar of cloud. God was forgiving toward them, yet he also punished their sins. The psalm calls us to exalt the Lord and worship at his holy mountain, for he is holy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cherubim:** God reigns above the cherubim, a picture of his majestic presence above the ark of the covenant.
- **Holiness:** God's holiness is repeated three times (vv. 3, 5, 9) as the central theme of the psalm.
- **Pillar of Cloud:** God spoke to his servants through the pillar of cloud, a sign of his guidance.
- **The Holy Mountain:** Zion/the temple hill as the place for worshiping the holy God.

**Main Theme:** Wisdom in leadership, self-control, and reverence for God create stability, while folly and godlessness lead to destruction.

**Content:** The chapter contrasts righteous and wicked leaders—a righteous king brings stability to the land, while one who accepts bribes tears it down. It warns against uncontrolled anger, pride, and flattery. A central verse teaches that without prophetic vision, the people run wild. Fear of man is a trap, but whoever trusts in the Lord is secure.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Discipline and Rebuke (v.1, 15, 17):** Discipline brings wisdom; whoever rejects correction meets sudden destruction.
- **Prophetic Vision/חָזוֹן (v.18):** Without divine revelation and guidance, the people lose their direction and moral compass.
- **Fear of Man vs. Trust in the Lord (v.25):** Fear of people ensnares, but trust in God brings security.
- **Righteous Rule (v.4, 14):** A king who judges righteously establishes his kingdom.

**Main Theme:** The completion of the priestly garments and the tabernacle in exact obedience to God’s commands.

**Content:** The chapter describes the detailed making of the priestly garments for Aaron and his sons: the ephod, the breastpiece with its twelve precious stones, the robe with golden bells and pomegranates, and the gold plate inscribed "Holy to the Lord". All the furnishings of the tabernacle were then brought to Moses for inspection. Moses saw that everything had been done exactly as the Lord had commanded, and he blessed the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ephod and breastpiece:** The high priest’s central garments featuring twelve precious stones representing the tribes of Israel.
- **"As the Lord had commanded Moses":** A repeated refrain emphasizing the people’s complete obedience to God’s instructions.
- **Gold plate (צִיץ):** The forehead plate inscribed "Holy to the Lord" – a symbol of the high priest’s consecration to God.
- **Moses’ blessing:** God’s approval through Moses as he inspected the completed work.

**Main Theme:** God’s judgment on Sodom and Gomorrah and Lot’s deliverance by God’s grace.

**Content:** Two angels arrive in Sodom, where Lot welcomes them, but the men of the city—young and old alike—surround his house and demand to "know" the guests. The angels pull Lot inside, strike the men with blindness, and lead Lot, his wife, and his daughters out of the city. God causes sulfur and fire to rain down on Sodom and Gomorrah; Lot’s wife becomes a pillar of salt when she looks back in disobedience. Lot and his daughters settle in a mountain cave, where the daughters bear children by their father and become the matriarchs of the Moabites and Ammonites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sulfur and fire:** God’s total judgment on sin, symbolizing divine wrath and justice.
- **Pillar of salt:** Lot’s wife as a warning against disobedience and turning back toward what is condemned.
- **Zoar ("the little"):** The city to which Lot fled, demonstrating God’s mercy amid judgment.
- **Moab and Ben-Ammi:** The sons born to Lot’s daughters, ancestors of the Moabites and Ammonites.

**Main Theme:** Elijah's confrontation with the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel shows that the Lord alone is God.

**Content:** After three years of drought, God sends Elijah to King Ahab. On Mount Carmel, Elijah challenges the 450 prophets of Baal to a test: the god who answers with fire is the true God. Baal does not answer, but the Lord's fire consumes Elijah's offering, even though it has been soaked with water. The people confess that the Lord is God, the prophets of Baal are killed, and the rain returns.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Carmel:** The place of the decisive confrontation between the Lord and Baal.
- **Fire from heaven:** God's dramatic answer proving his power and sovereignty.
- **The twelve stones:** Elijah rebuilds the altar with twelve stones as a symbol of united Israel.
- **Rain:** A sign that the curse is broken and God restores the land.

**Main Theme:** A prayer for deliverance from deceitful people and a longing for peace.

**Content:** Psalm 120 is the first of the Songs of Ascents. The psalmist cries out to the Lord in distress and testifies that God answered him. He asks to be saved from lying lips and a deceitful tongue, describing God's judgment on liars as sharp arrows and burning coals. The psalmist laments having to live among peace-hating people—figuratively described as Meshek and Kedar—and expresses his own longing for peace in contrast to the warlike disposition of those around him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Song of Ascents (שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת):** The first of 15 psalms (120–134) that were likely sung on the way up to Jerusalem.
- **Deceitful tongue:** Represents falsehood and deception that threaten the psalmist's life and honor.
- **Meshek and Kedar:** Distant peoples symbolizing alienation and life among hostile neighbors.
- **Arrows and burning coals:** Images of God's judgment on liars, corresponding to the harm their words cause.

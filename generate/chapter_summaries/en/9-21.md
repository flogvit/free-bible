**Main Theme:** David’s flight from Saul leads him to both the sanctuary at Nob and enemy territory.

**Content:** David flees to the priest Ahimelech at Nob, where he receives the bread of the Presence and Goliath’s sword. Saul’s servant Doeg witnesses this meeting. David continues to Achish, king of the Philistines in Gath, but is recognized and must pretend to be insane to escape.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bread of the Presence** – The holy bread normally reserved for priests, given to David in his time of need.
- **Goliath’s sword** – Symbolizes David’s earlier victory and God’s help, now used during his flight.
- **Doeg the Edomite** – Saul’s servant who observes the event, a threatening presence.
- **Feigned madness** – David’s cunning to survive among enemies shows his resourcefulness in a desperate situation.

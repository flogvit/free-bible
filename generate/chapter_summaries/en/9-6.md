**Main Theme:** The Philistines return the Ark of the Covenant to Israel after experiencing God's punishing power.

**Content:** After seven months of plagues, the Philistines consult priests and diviners on how to return the ark. They send it back on a new cart pulled by two cows, along with gold objects as a guilt offering. The cart arrives at Beth Shemesh, where the people sacrifice the cows to the Lord, but 70 men are struck down because they looked into the ark. Fearing God's holiness, Beth Shemesh sends messengers to Kiriath Jearim to retrieve the ark.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Guilt Offering (אָשָׁם):** Gold tumors and gold mice that the Philistines sent to appease Israel's God.
- **The New Cart:** A symbol of reverence; the cows walking straight to Beth Shemesh confirmed that the plagues were from God.
- **God's Holiness:** The judgment upon Beth Shemesh shows that even Israel cannot handle the ark without reverence and remain unpunished.
- **Beth Shemesh:** The Israelite border town where the ark first arrived, and where the people experienced both joy and judgment.

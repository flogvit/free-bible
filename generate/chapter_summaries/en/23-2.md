**Main Theme:** The Exaltation of the Lord in the Last Days – Both as a Hope of Salvation for the Nations and Judgment over All Human Pride.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a vision that the house of the Lord shall stand firm on the mountain, to which all nations shall stream to learn God’s ways. The Lord will judge between the nations, and swords shall be beaten into plowshares. Then there is a warning against Judah’s idolatry and wealth. On the day of the Lord, everything proud and lofty will be humbled – from the cedars of Lebanon to high towers – and the Lord alone will be exalted.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Mountain of the Lord:** A symbol of God’s sovereignty where all peoples gather to learn His ways.
- **Swords into Plowshares:** An image of universal peace when God judges between the nations.
- **The Day of the Lord:** A day of judgment in which all human pride and idolatry will be humbled.
- **Hiding in the Rocks:** Humanity’s flight from the majesty of the Lord as He rises to terrify the earth.

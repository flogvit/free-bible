**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts unity between strong and weak believers, describes his ministry to the Gentiles, and shares his travel plans.

**Content:** Those who are strong in faith should bear the weaknesses of the weak, following Christ's example, so that the church may glorify God with one mind and one mouth. Paul describes his calling as apostle to the Gentiles and his goal to preach the gospel where Christ has not yet been named. He plans to visit Rome on his way to Spain, but must first bring a collection to the poor saints in Jerusalem. He asks for prayer for protection and that his ministry may be well received.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bear weaknesses (v.1):** The strong have a duty to support the weak in faith, not merely seek their own satisfaction.
- **God of hope (v.13):** God gives joy, peace, and overflowing hope through the power of the Holy Spirit.
- **Gentile offering (v.16):** Paul views his ministry as priestly—the Gentiles become a holy offering to God.
- **The collection (vv.26–27):** The gift from Macedonia and Achaia for Jerusalem demonstrates mutual fellowship between Jewish and Gentile believers.
- **God of peace (v.33):** The chapter concludes with a blessing of God's peace over the church.

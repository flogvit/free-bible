**Main Theme:** Life in the Spirit brings freedom from condemnation and inseparable union with God’s love in Christ.

**Content:** Those who are in Christ Jesus are free from condemnation, because the law of the Spirit has set them free from the law of sin and death. The Spirit makes believers children of God and co-heirs with Christ, and helps us in our weakness by interceding for us. All creation groans and longs together with believers for final redemption. Paul assures us that nothing—not even death or life, angels or rulers, things present or to come, powers, height or depth, nor anything else in all creation—can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **No Condemnation (v.1):** A foundational promise to those who are in Christ—complete acquittal.
- **Abba, Father (v.15):** The Spirit testifies to our adoption as children and gives us bold access to God as Father.
- **The Groaning of Creation (v.22):** All creation is subjected to futility and longs for liberation together with the children of God.
- **The Golden Chain (vv.29–30):** God’s plan of salvation from foreknowledge to glorification—an unbreakable chain.
- **More Than Conquerors (v.37):** No opposition can separate believers from Christ’s love.

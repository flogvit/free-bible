**Main Theme:** The chapter exhorts us to remember our Creator in youth and concludes with life's great conclusion: fear God and keep his commandments.

**Content:** The Preacher urges us to remember our Creator before old age comes, using poetic imagery to describe the weakening of the body and the approach of death. He repeats that everything is fleeting (havel havalim). As a wise teacher, the Preacher has arranged many proverbs to guide the people. The conclusion is clear: fear God and keep his commandments, for God will bring every deed into judgment, including what is hidden, whether good or evil.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The house of the Creator (the body):** Allegorical images describing the frailties of old age—the guardians who tremble, those who grind ceaselessly stopping their work, and the windows growing dark.
- **Havel havalim:** "Vanity of vanities"—the recurring theme of the book, repeated in its conclusion.
- **The silver cord and the golden bowl:** Poetic images representing the fragility of life and the inevitability of death.
- **Fear God:** The conclusion of both the chapter and the book—the whole duty of humanity is to fear God and obey his commandments.
- **Judgment:** God will bring every deed into judgment, including what is hidden.

**Main Theme:** The chapter celebrates the love between the bride and the groom through rich natural imagery and mutual longing.

**Content:** The bride and groom praise each other with poetic comparisons—she is a lily among thorns, he is like an apple tree among trees. The groom invites the bride to come out because winter has passed and spring has arrived with flowers and birdsong. The bride expresses her devotion: "My beloved is mine, and I am his," and warns the daughters of Jerusalem not to stir up love until it so desires.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lily and Apple Tree (vv. 1–3):** Images of the lovers' beauty and value to one another.
- **Spring (vv. 11–13):** Symbolizes the blossoming and renewal of love.
- **Gazelle/Hart (vv. 8–9, 17):** The groom's swiftness and grace as he comes to his beloved.
- **The Little Foxes (v. 15):** A warning to protect love from destructive threats.

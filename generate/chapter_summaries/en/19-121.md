**Main Theme:** A song of ascent expressing trust in the Lord's unceasing protection.

**Content:** The psalmist lifts his eyes to the mountains and asks where help comes from, before affirming that it comes from the Lord, the Maker of heaven and earth. God neither slumbers nor sleeps but watches over Israel continually. The Lord is a shade at your right hand; He protects you from all evil and preserves your going out and your coming in from this time forth and forevermore.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Keeper/Preserver (שֹׁמֵר):** Repeated six times, emphasizing God's active and enduring protection.
- **Shade at your right hand:** A picture of God's close presence and safeguarding from life's dangers.
- **Sun and moon:** Represent round-the-clock protection, both day and night.
- **Going out and coming in:** Encompasses all of life—everything you do is under God's care.

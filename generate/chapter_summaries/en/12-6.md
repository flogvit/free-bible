**Main Theme:** God's protection and power are revealed through the prophet Elisha in the face of Aram's threats and Samaria's desperation.

**Content:** The chapter presents three episodes: Elisha makes a borrowed iron axe head float on the Jordan. Then he repeatedly reveals the king of Aram's war plans, and when the Arameans surround Dothan to capture him, God strikes them with blindness so that Elisha can lead them to Samaria—where they are given food and sent home instead of being killed. Finally, a brutal famine during Ben-Hadad's siege is described, in which the king, in despair, threatens to kill Elisha.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The floating iron axe head:** Demonstrates God's power over natural laws and His care for everyday needs.
- **Chariots of fire and horses:** The invisible heavenly army surrounding Elisha—"those who are with us are more than those who are with them".
- **Blindness and opened eyes:** God controls what people see; both the servant and the enemy have their eyes opened or closed in response to Elisha's prayer.
- **A meal for the enemy:** Grace instead of vengeance—the enemy is sent home with food, and the raids cease.

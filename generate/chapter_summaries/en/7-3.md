**Main Theme:** God tests Israel through enemies and raises up judges as deliverers when the people repent.

**Content:** God allowed Canaanite peoples to remain in the land to test Israel and teach them warfare. The Israelites married these peoples and worshiped their gods, which led to God's anger and oppression. The chapter describes three judges: Othniel, who freed Israel from Cushan-Rishathaim; Ehud, who killed Eglon, king of Moab, with a hidden sword and secured 80 years of peace; and Shamgar, who struck down 600 Philistines with an oxgoad.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Testing (נַסּוֹת):** God leaves enemies in the land to test whether Israel will keep His commands.
- **Cycle of Apostasy:** Israel sins, becomes oppressed, cries out to God, and He raises up a deliverer—a pattern that repeats.
- **Judge/Deliverer (מוֹשִׁיעַ):** God chooses and equips leaders with His Spirit to free the people.
- **God's Anger and Grace:** God punishes sin but hears the people's cry and grants deliverance.

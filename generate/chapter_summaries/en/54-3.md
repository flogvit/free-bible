**Main Theme:** Paul sets out the qualifications for overseers and deacons in the church, and describes the church's identity as the pillar and foundation of the truth.

**Content:** Overseers must be blameless, faithful in marriage, sober-minded, hospitable, and able to teach, with well-ordered family life and not recent converts. Deacons are to be worthy, honest, and tested first before serving. The chapter concludes by stating that the church is "the church of the living God, the pillar and foundation of the truth," along with a hymn about the mystery of godliness: Christ revealed in the flesh, vindicated in the Spirit, seen by angels, proclaimed among the nations, believed on in the world, and taken up in glory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Overseer (episkopos):** A leader who watches over the church and must meet strict moral and spiritual requirements.
- **Deacon:** A servant in the church who must be tested and prove worthy before serving.
- **Pillar and foundation of the truth:** A picture of the church as bearing up and safeguarding Christian truth.
- **Mystery of godliness:** The heart of the Christian faith expressed in an early confession concerning Christ's person and work.

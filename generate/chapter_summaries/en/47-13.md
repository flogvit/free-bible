**Main Theme:** Paul concludes the letter with a warning about his upcoming visit, an exhortation to self-examination, and a Trinitarian blessing.

**Content:** Paul announces his third visit and warns that he will not spare those who have sinned. He urges the Corinthians to examine themselves to see whether they are in the faith, reminding them that Christ was crucified in weakness but lives by God's power. The letter ends with exhortations concerning unity, peace, and love, along with the well-known Trinitarian blessing.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Self-examination (δοκιμάζετε):** The Corinthians are urged to examine their own faith and confirm that Christ is in them.
- **Weakness and power:** Christ was crucified in weakness but lives by God's power—a pattern that also applies to believers.
- **Building up (οἰκοδομή):** Paul's authority is given to build up the church, not to tear it down.
- **The Trinitarian blessing:** The conclusion mentions the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit.

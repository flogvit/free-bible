**Main Theme:** Paul appeals to Philemon to receive the returning slave Onesimus as a beloved brother in Christ.

**Content:** Paul, imprisoned for the sake of Christ, writes together with Timothy to Philemon and asks him to welcome Onesimus, who has become a believer and is now useful to both of them. Rather than commanding, Paul appeals through love and offers personally to repay any wrong or debt. He urges Philemon to receive Onesimus no longer as a slave but as a beloved brother, both in human terms and in the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Brother (ἀδελφός):** Onesimus' new status as an equal within the fellowship of faith.
- **Appeal (παρακαλῶ):** Paul chooses persuasion through love rather than apostolic authority.
- **Useful/Useless (εὔχρηστος/ἄχρηστος):** A play on words with the name Onesimus ("useful") – he was useless, but now he is useful.
- **Heart (σπλάγχνα):** Expresses deep love – Onesimus is Paul's own heart, and Philemon has refreshed the hearts of the saints.
- **Willingness (ἑκούσιον):** Paul desires that Philemon's kindness should spring from free will, not compulsion.

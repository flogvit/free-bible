**Main Theme:** Job expresses deep despair over his situation and sees only the grave as his future.

**Content:** Job describes how his spirit is crushed and his life is ebbing away. He asks God to be his guarantor, since no one else will stand in his place, and he accuses his friends of lacking wisdom and understanding. Job now regards Sheol (the realm of the dead) as his home and refers to the grave as his father, the worm as his mother and sister. He asks where his hope is, concluding that it descends into Sheol along with him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Guarantor (עָרְבֵנִי):** Job asks God to act as his guarantor in a legal image, since no human being will take his side.
- **Sheol as home:** Job describes the realm of the dead as his dwelling and the grave as his bed—death has become his only expectation.
- **The grave as family:** In a poignant image, Job calls the grave his father and the worm his mother and sister—death is now his closest companion.
- **Hopelessness (תִקְוָה):** Job asks rhetorically where his hope is, answering that it sinks into the dust along with him.

**Main Theme:** Israel's complaints in the wilderness lead to God's judgment, while Moses receives help to carry the responsibility of leadership.

**Content:** The people complain, and God's fire burns at the edge of the camp until Moses prays (the place is called Taberah). The mixed crowd and the Israelites weep for meat and long for the food of Egypt, which exhausts Moses from the burden of leadership. God responds by giving His Spirit to 70 elders who are to help Moses; even Eldad and Medad, who remained in the camp, prophesied. God sends quail, but strikes the people with a plague while the meat is still between their teeth, and the place is called Kibroth-hattaavah ("graves of craving").

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Taberah ("burning"):** The place where God's fire burned as judgment over the people's complaints.
- **The Manna:** God's daily provision that the people despised in their longing for Egyptian food.
- **The 70 Elders:** Received God's Spirit from Moses to share the burden of leadership.
- **The Quail:** God's answer to the people's craving for meat, which was followed by judgment.
- **Kibroth-hattaavah ("graves of craving"):** The place where those who craved were buried.

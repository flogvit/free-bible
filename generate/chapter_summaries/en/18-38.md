**Main Theme:** God confronts Job from the storm with rhetorical questions that reveal his sovereign power over all creation.

**Content:** God speaks to Job from the storm and challenges him with questions about creation: Where was Job when the earth was founded? God describes his control over the sea, light and darkness, weather phenomena such as snow, hail, and lightning, as well as the paths of the stars. The chapter concludes with questions about who provides food for the lion and the raven—all to show that Job cannot comprehend God's wisdom and therefore cannot judge his actions.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Storm (הַסְּעָרָה):** God's form of revelation, emphasizing his majesty and power.
- **The Foundations of the Earth:** A picture of the order of creation and God's role as the architect of the universe.
- **The Boundaries of the Sea:** God set boundaries for the sea like a nursing child—demonstrating his control over chaotic forces.
- **The Morning Stars and the Sons of God:** Heavenly joy at creation testifies to the greatness of the created world.
- **The Lion and the Raven:** God provides for all creatures, even the wild animals.

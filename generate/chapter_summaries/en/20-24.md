**Main Theme:** Practical wisdom concerning righteousness, diligence, and reverence for God in the face of evil and adversity.

**Content:** The chapter warns against envying wicked people, for they have no future. Wisdom builds a house and provides strength, while laziness leads to poverty. The righteous fall seven times but rise again. We are urged to rescue the innocent, judge righteously, avoid vengeance, and fear both the Lord and the king.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The House Built with Wisdom (vv. 3–4):** Wisdom, understanding, and knowledge create a solid and valuable home.
- **Honey (vv. 13–14):** Just as honey is sweet, wisdom is good for the soul and gives hope for the future.
- **The Sluggard's Field (vv. 30–34):** An object lesson showing how neglect leads to ruin and poverty.
- **Seven Falls (v. 16):** The righteous rise again after adversity, while the wicked stumble and remain fallen.

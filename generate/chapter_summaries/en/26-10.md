**Main Theme:** God's glory leaves the temple in Jerusalem, carried by the cherubim.

**Content:** Ezekiel sees God's throne above the cherubim, and a man clothed in linen is instructed to take glowing coals from between the cherubim and scatter them over the city. The Lord's glory lifts itself from the cherub and moves to the threshold of the temple, while the cloud fills the courtyard. The cherubim lift their wings and move toward the eastern gate, with the wheels beside them and the glory of the God of Israel above them. Ezekiel recognizes that these are the same creatures he saw by the Kebar River.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cherubim:** Four heavenly beings with four faces (cherub, human, lion, eagle) and four wings, who carry God's throne.
- **Wheels (galgal/ofannim):** Wheels beside the cherubim, full of eyes, that move in unity with the cherubim because the spirit of life is in them.
- **Glowing Coals:** Fire from between the cherubim to be cast over Jerusalem, a sign of judgment.
- **God's Glory (kavod):** The Lord's visible presence as it leaves the temple and moves eastward.

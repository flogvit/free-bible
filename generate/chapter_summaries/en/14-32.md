**Main Theme:** God's deliverance of Jerusalem from Assyria and Hezekiah's fluctuating faith and pride.

**Content:** King Sennacherib of Assyria invades Judah, and Hezekiah prepares Jerusalem's defense by strengthening the walls and blocking water sources. He encourages the people to trust in God rather than the Assyrian army. God hears the prayers of Hezekiah and the prophet Isaiah, sends an angel who destroys the enemy, and Sennacherib is killed by his own sons. Later, Hezekiah falls ill and is healed, but his heart becomes proud; he humbles himself, and God's wrath is averted. During a visit from Babylonian envoys, God tests his heart.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Water Sources:** Hezekiah blocks the springs outside the city to deny the enemy access to water.
- **"The Lord Is With Us":** Hezekiah's encouragement that God's arm is stronger than the enemy's fleshly arm.
- **The Angel of the Lord:** God's supernatural intervention that annihilates the Assyrian army.
- **Pride and Humility:** Hezekiah's heart became proud after receiving blessings, but he humbled himself and turned away God's wrath.

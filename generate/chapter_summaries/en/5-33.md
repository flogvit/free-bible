**Main Theme:** Moses' farewell blessings over the tribes of Israel and praise of God's incomparable greatness as Israel's protector.

**Content:** Before his death, Moses blesses the tribes of Israel with prophetic words concerning their future and character (Simeon is the only one not mentioned). The chapter opens with a hymn about the Lord coming from Sinai and the giving of the law (vv. 2-5), and concludes with praise of God as "the God of Jeshurun" – He who rides on the heavens to help His people and supports them with eternal arms (vv. 26-29). Levi is given a central role as guardians of the covenant and teachers of God's law, while Joseph receives the richest blessing with promises of the land's abundance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Jeshurun** – A poetic honorific title for Israel meaning "the upright," used in the framework surrounding the blessings (vv. 5, 26).
- **Urim and Thummim** – The sacred objects given to the tribe of Levi to seek God's will (v. 8).
- **"Fire-law" (eshdat)** – A picture of the Torah that was given from God's right hand at Sinai (v. 2).
- **Eternal arms** – A description of God who carries and protects His people with arms that are beneath them (v. 27).

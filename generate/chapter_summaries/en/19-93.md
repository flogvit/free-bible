**Main Theme:** The psalm proclaims the Lord's eternal kingdom and sovereign power over all forces.

**Content:** The Lord reigns as king, clothed in majesty and strength, and has established the earth so that it stands firm. His throne is established from of old. Even though the floods roar and the mighty waves of the sea surge, the Lord on high is far more powerful. The psalm concludes by affirming that God's testimonies are trustworthy, and that holiness adorns his house forever.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord reigns (יְהוָה מָלָךְ):** God's sovereign rule over creation.
- **Clothed in majesty and strength:** A figurative description of God's royal dignity and power.
- **Floods (נְהָרוֹת):** Represent chaotic forces that are nevertheless subject to God's power.
- **God's testimonies (עֵדֹתֶיךָ):** His reliable words and revelation that stand firm.

**Main Theme:** The Death and Legacy of Moses – The End of an Era and the Transition to New Leadership.

**Content:** Moses ascends Mount Nebo, where the Lord shows him the entire Promised Land, but he is not allowed to enter it himself. Moses dies at the age of 120 in the land of Moab, with his sight and vitality undiminished, and God buries him in an unknown place. Israel mourns for thirty days, and Joshua, filled with the spirit of wisdom after Moses laid hands on him, takes over leadership. The chapter concludes by stating that no prophet like Moses has ever arisen in Israel – one who knew the Lord face to face.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Nebo:** The vantage point where Moses sees the fulfillment of God's promise to the fathers without entering the land himself.
- **Face to face:** An expression describing Moses' unique, direct relationship with God – unlike any other prophet.
- **Laying on of hands:** The ritual in which Moses transfers authority and the spirit of wisdom to Joshua as his successor.
- **Unknown grave:** God's burial of Moses in a hidden place emphasizes that Moses was not to become an object of cultic worship.

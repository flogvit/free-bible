**Main Theme:** Saul's growing paranoia leads to the massacre of the priests at Nob, while David gathers his first loyal followers.

**Content:** David flees to the cave of Adullam, where about 400 men in distress, debt, and bitterness gather under his leadership. He sends his parents to Moab for protection, and the prophet Gad guides him to the forest of Hereth in Judah. Doeg the Edomite accuses priest Ahimelech of having helped David, and Saul orders the execution of all the priests at Nob. Doeg kills 85 priests and destroys the entire city. Only Abiatar escapes and seeks refuge with David, who takes responsibility for the tragedy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cave of Adullam:** A place of refuge where David becomes leader of marginalized people – a picture of God's kingdom gathering the outcasts.
- **Doeg the Edomite:** A foreigner who obeys Saul's ungodly command when Israelites refuse – showing the dangers of blind loyalty to power.
- **85 priests killed:** Saul's massacre of the Lord's priests marks his complete moral decay and break with God.
- **Abiatar:** The only surviving priest who brings the ephod to David – the priesthood now follows the true anointed king.

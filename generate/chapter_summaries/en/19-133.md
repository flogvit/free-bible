**Main Theme:** The Blessing of Fellowship – Brotherly Life as a Source of God’s Blessing.

**Content:** This short pilgrimage psalm by David praises how good and pleasant it is when brothers live together in unity. Unity is compared to the precious anointing oil that ran down over Aaron's head and beard, and to the dew of Hermon that falls on the mountains of Zion. Where fellowship exists, the Lord has commanded His blessing: life forevermore.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Anointing Oil (שֶׁמֶן):** The precious oil used in priestly consecration symbolizes holiness and abundance.
- **Aaron's Beard:** The reference to the high priest's anointing emphasizes the holy character of the blessing.
- **Dew of Hermon (טַל־חֶרְמוֹן):** Dew from the high Mount Hermon symbolizes refreshing, life-giving blessing.
- **Mount Zion:** God's holy dwelling place where the blessing is ordained.

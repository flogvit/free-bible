**Main Theme:** Paul warns against false teaching and encourages believers to live rooted in Christ, in whom the fullness of deity dwells.

**Content:** Paul expresses his struggle for the churches in Colossae and Laodicea, wishing that they may be firmly grounded in love and know God’s mystery: Christ. He warns them not to be captured by empty philosophy and human traditions. In Christ, believers were buried with him in baptism and raised with him; the record of debt that stood against them has been nailed to the cross. Paul urges them not to let anyone judge them regarding food, drink, or festivals, which are only shadows of what was to come.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fullness of deity (v. 9):** In Christ dwells all the fullness of deity bodily – he is fully God.
- **The record of debt nailed to the cross (v. 14):** The image that the indictment against us has been erased and removed through Christ’s death.
- **Triumphal procession (v. 15):** Christ disarmed the powers and made a public spectacle of them – an image drawn from Roman victory celebrations.
- **Shadow and substance (v. 17):** The Old Testament regulations were shadows; the reality (the body) belongs to Christ.

**Main Theme:** Job defends his integrity before his friends and demands the opportunity to present his case directly to God.

**Content:** Job rejects his friends' advice and calls them "comforters of lies" and "worthless physicians" (v.4). He expresses a desire to speak directly with God and assert his innocence, even if God were to kill him (v.15). Job asks God for two things: to remove His heavy hand and not terrify him, so that they may have a fair conversation. He also asks God to reveal what sins he has committed and why God hides His face and treats him as an enemy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Comforters of lies/Worthless physicians** (v.4): Job's harsh judgment of his friends' empty comfort and false theology.
- **"Though He slay me, yet will I hope in Him"** (v.15): An expression of Job's steadfast trust in God amid suffering.
- **Withered leaf and dry straw** (v.25): A metaphor for human frailty, which Job believes God is needlessly pursuing.
- **Feet in the stocks** (v.27): A metaphor for Job's feeling of being trapped and watched by God.

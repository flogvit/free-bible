**Main Theme:** The Ark of the Covenant is brought into Solomon's temple, and God's glory fills the sanctuary.

**Content:** After the temple work is completed, Solomon gathers Israel's leaders to bring the Ark of the Covenant from the City of David to the temple during the Feast of Tabernacles. The priests place the ark in the Most Holy Place under the wings of the cherubim. The ark contains only the two stone tablets from Horeb. As the Levites sing praises with music and song, a cloud fills the temple, and the Lord's glory is so powerful that the priests cannot carry out their service.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant:** The symbol of God's covenant with Israel, now placed in its permanent resting place in the temple.
- **Cherubim:** Angelic figures that cover the ark and represent God's throne and protection.
- **The Cloud (God's Glory):** God's visible presence that fills the temple and confirms his acceptance of the sanctuary.
- **The Song of Praise:** The Levites' unified praise with "for his steadfast love endures forever" triggers the manifestation of God's glory.

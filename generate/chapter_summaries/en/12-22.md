**Main Theme:** The discovery of the Book of the Law in the temple leads to Josiah’s repentance and a prophetic message from Huldah.

**Content:** King Josiah, who became king at eight years old, ruled righteously according to the Lord’s will. In his eighteenth year as king, the high priest Hilkia found the Book of the Law during temple repairs. When Josiah heard the words of the law, he tore his clothes in despair and sent messengers to the prophetess Huldah. She delivered a double message: The Lord would bring disaster upon Jerusalem because of the people’s rebellion, but Josiah himself would die in peace because his heart was tender and he humbled himself before God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Book of the Law (סֵפֶר הַתּוֹרָה):** The scroll found in the temple that revealed the people’s disobedience to God.
- **Tearing clothes:** Josiah’s response to the words of the law—an expression of deep grief and repentance.
- **Huldah:** The female prophetess who conveyed God’s judgment and grace to the king.
- **Tender heart:** Josiah’s humble attitude, which led to his being spared from witnessing the coming disaster.

**Main Theme:** The chapter provides a detailed overview of the genealogies of the tribe of Levi, their duties, and the cities they were allotted in Israel.

**Content:** The chapter begins with Levi's three sons—Gershon, Kohath, and Merari—and their descendants. Special emphasis is placed on Aaron's priestly line and the priests' duties at the altar of burnt offering and the altar of incense. David appointed singers from the three Levitical branches—Heman (a Kohathite), Asaph (a Gershonite), and Ethan (a Merarite)—to lead the praise. Finally, the cities allotted to the Levites by lot from the various tribes are listed, including the cities of refuge.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Levi's three branches:** Gershon, Kohath, and Merari form the foundation for the organization and service of the Levites.
- **Aaron's priestly line:** Aaron and his sons were responsible for the offerings and atonement for Israel according to Moses' command.
- **The singers' service:** David organized the temple singing with three chief singers who each represented a different Levitical branch.
- **Levitical cities:** Cities with pasturelands were allotted to the Levites by lot from the tribes of Israel as their dwelling places.

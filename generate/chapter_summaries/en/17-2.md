**Main Theme:** Esther becomes queen of Persia following a kingdom-wide beauty contest, while keeping her Jewish identity hidden.

**Content:** After Queen Vashti was deposed, King Ahasuerus gathered young women from throughout the empire to find a new queen. The orphaned Jewish girl Esther, raised by her cousin Mordecai, was taken to the palace and underwent twelve months of beautification treatments. She won the king's favor and was crowned queen, but did not reveal her Jewish background as instructed by Mordecai. Mordecai uncovered a conspiracy against the king, and this event was recorded in the king's chronicles.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hidden identity:** Esther keeps her Jewish heritage secret at Mordecai's command—a motif that becomes crucial later on.
- **God's providence:** Although God is not mentioned directly, Esther's elevation to queen demonstrates His guidance behind the scenes.
- **Hegai (the eunuch):** The king's official in charge of the women who showed special favor toward Esther.
- **The king's chronicles:** The record of Mordecai's exposure of the conspiracy—a foreshadowing of future reward.

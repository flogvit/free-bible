**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals his divine authority through healings, nature miracles, and exorcisms.

**Content:** After the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus heals a leper, an officer's servant, and Peter's mother-in-law, and drives out many evil spirits—fulfilling Isaiah's prophecy. The faith of the Gentile officer is highlighted as greater than any in Israel, and Jesus prophesies that many from all nations will share in God's kingdom. Jesus calms the storm on the Sea of Galilee and frees two demon-possessed men in Gadara, but the people ask him to leave their region.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Officer's Faith:** A Gentile officer shows humble trust in Jesus' authority and becomes a model of faith that transcends ethnic boundaries.
- **The Calming Storm:** Jesus demonstrates mastery over natural forces, causing the disciples to ask who he really is.
- **The Son of Man:** Jesus uses this title for himself and emphasizes that following him requires giving up security and comfort.
- **Exorcism:** The demons recognize Jesus as "the Son of God" and obey his word, confirming his divine power.

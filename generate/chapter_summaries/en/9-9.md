**Main Theme:** God prepares Saul to become Israel's first king through an apparently chance encounter with the prophet Samuel.

**Content:** Saul, a tall young man from Benjamin, is sent by his father Kish to look for lost donkeys. The search leads him to Samuel's town, where God had revealed to Samuel the day before that he would meet Israel's future leader. Samuel invites Saul to the sacrificial meal, gives him the place of honor, and hints that all Israel's longing rests upon him and his house. The chapter ends with Samuel promising to declare God's word to Saul.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Donkeys (אֲתֹנוֹת):** The lost animals serve as the outward pretext for God's hidden plan to bring Saul to Samuel.
- **The Seer (הָרֹאֶה):** An older term for prophet; Samuel is the one who sees and communicates God's will.
- **High Place (בָּמָה):** The location of the communal meal and worship service where Samuel honors Saul before the people.
- **Prince/נָגִיד (nagid):** God calls Saul "prince" over Israel—a leader who will deliver the people from the hand of the Philistines.

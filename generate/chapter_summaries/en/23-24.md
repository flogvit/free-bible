**Main Theme:** God's all-encompassing judgment upon the earth and its inhabitants due to covenant breaking, followed by his glorious reign on Zion.

**Content:** Isaiah prophesies that the Lord will lay the earth waste and scatter its inhabitants—all social classes are struck alike. The reason is that humanity has broken the laws and the everlasting covenant; therefore, the curse devours the earth, and only a few remain. Amidst judgment, praise is heard from the ends of the earth, from a remnant that honors the Lord. The chapter culminates with the Lord punishing the host of heaven and the kings of the earth, after which he reigns in glory on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Everlasting covenant (בְּרִית עוֹלָם):** The covenant that humanity has broken, triggering God's judgment.
- **Curse (אָלָה):** The curse that devours the earth because of its guilt.
- **Chaotic city (קִרְיַת־תֹּהוּ):** A picture of civilization's collapse and destruction.
- **The earth staggers like a drunkard (כַּשִּׁכּוֹר):** A dramatic image of the earth's instability under judgment.
- **The Lord reigns on Zion:** The climax of hope—God's glorious kingdom in Jerusalem.

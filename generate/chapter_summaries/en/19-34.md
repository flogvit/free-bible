**Main Theme:** An acrostic psalm of thanksgiving in which David praises God for deliverance and teaches the way of reverence for God.

**Content:** The psalm is linked to the time when David pretended to be insane before Abimelech. David encourages people to praise and exalt the Lord together, and testifies that God hears the afflicted and saves them from all distress. Central wisdom instruction is given: guard your tongue from evil, turn away from what is wrong, and do what is good. The psalm assures us that the Lord is near to those with broken hearts and that He rescues His servants.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The angel of the Lord (v. 8):** God's protective presence encamping around those who fear Him.
- **Taste and see (v. 9):** An invitation to personally experience God's goodness.
- **Broken hearts (v. 19):** God is especially near to the humble and contrite in spirit.
- **Reverence for God (vv. 10, 12):** Awe of the Lord as the foundation for blessing and wisdom.

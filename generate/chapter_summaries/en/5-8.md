**Main Theme:** Moses exhorts Israel to remember the Lord's care in the wilderness and warns against forgetting Him in times of prosperity.

**Content:** The chapter reminds the Israelites that the Lord led them for 40 years in the wilderness to humble and test them. He gave them manna to teach them that man does not live by bread alone, but by everything that comes from the mouth of the Lord. Now God is bringing them into a good land with an abundance of water, fruit, and minerals. Moses warns against becoming proud and forgetting that it is God who gives the power to gain wealth—otherwise they will perish like the nations before them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Manna:** Unknown food God gave in the wilderness to teach the people dependence on His word, not just bread.
- **The Good Land:** A land with water sources, wheat, barley, grapevines, fig trees, pomegranates, olives, and honey—an image of God's blessing.
- **Fatherly Discipline:** God trains Israel as a father trains his son, through trials with a good purpose.
- **Pride:** The danger of saying "my own power has gained me this wealth" and forgetting God as the source of all things.

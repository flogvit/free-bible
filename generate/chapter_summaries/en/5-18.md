**Main Theme:** The chapter establishes provisions for the Levites' support, forbids pagan practices, and promises a coming prophet like Moses.

**Content:** The Levites shall have no earthly inheritance because the Lord is their inheritance; they are to live from offerings and firstfruits. Israel must not imitate the detestable practices of the nations, such as divination, sorcery, and necromancy, but be wholehearted toward God. The Lord promises to raise up a prophet like Moses from among the people, whom the people must listen to. False prophets who speak in the name of other gods or deliver messages that God has not commanded shall die.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The Lord is his inheritance"** (v.2): The Levites do not own land but have God himself and his gifts as their foundation for life.
- **Detestable thing (תּוֹעֵבָה)** (v.12): Pagan practices such as divination and necromancy are abhorrent to God.
- **"A prophet like me"** (vv.15, 18): God promises a future prophet with the same authority as Moses, who will speak God's words.
- **Wholehearted (תָּמִים)** (v.13): Israel is called to undivided loyalty toward the Lord, in contrast to the religious practices of the nations.

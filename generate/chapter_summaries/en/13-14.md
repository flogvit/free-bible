**Main Theme:** God establishes David's kingdom through blessing and victory over the Philistines.

**Content:** King Hiram of Tyre sends craftsmen and cedar trees to David to build a palace, and David understands that the Lord has established him as king for Israel's sake. He acquires more wives and children in Jerusalem. When the Philistines attack, David seeks God's guidance twice and receives different strategies: first a direct assault, then a flanking maneuver where the sound of marching feet in the balsam trees signals that God is going before them. David wins both times, burns the Philistine idols, and his fame spreads to all nations.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Baal-perazim** – "Lord of Breakthrough"; the place where God broke through the enemies like a flood.
- **Balsam trees** – The trees where David was to listen for God's footsteps as a sign to attack.
- **Philistine gods** – The idols that were left behind and burned, showing their powerlessness against Israel's God.
- **David's fame** – The Lord put fear of David over all nations, as a sign of God's blessing.

**Main Theme:** Saul’s disobedience to God’s command leads to his rejection as king over Israel.

**Content:** God commands Saul through Samuel to completely destroy the Amalekites as punishment for their attack on Israel during the wilderness journey. Saul wins the war but spares King Agag and keeps the best of the livestock. Samuel confronts Saul about his disobedience and declares that obedience is better than sacrifice. God rejects Saul as king, and Samuel himself kills Agag before leaving Saul permanently.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Herem (ban):** God’s command for total destruction of the Amalekites and everything they owned as a holy judgment.
- **Obedience rather than sacrifice:** Samuel’s message that God values obedience more highly than religious rituals (v.22).
- **The torn robe:** A symbol that God is tearing the kingdom from Saul and giving it to another (vv.27–28).
- **God’s regret:** The Lord’s sorrow over having made Saul king, showing the seriousness of Saul’s betrayal (vv.11, 35).

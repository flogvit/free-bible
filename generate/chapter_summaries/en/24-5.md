**Main Theme:** God accuses Jerusalem and Judah of apostasy and injustice, and warns of judgment by a foreign nation.

**Content:** God looks for one righteous person in Jerusalem but finds none—neither among the poor nor among the leaders. Both prophets and priests have led the people astray, and the rich oppress the weak. God will send a mighty nation from afar as punishment, yet promises not to make a complete end of His people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Predators (lion, wolf, leopard):** Symbolize the coming judgment that will strike all who leave the city.
- **Well-fed stallions:** A picture of the people's uncontrolled desires and spiritual adultery.
- **Birdcatcher:** The wicked trap people in their snares and fill their houses with unjust wealth.
- **The sand as the boundary of the sea:** God's sovereign power over creation, contrasted with the people's rebellion.

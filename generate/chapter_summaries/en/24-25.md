**Main Theme:** God announces judgment over Judah and all nations through the image of a cup of wrath that everyone must drink.

**Content:** After 23 years of faithful preaching without the people listening, Jeremiah declares that God will send Nebuchadnezzar against Judah and neighboring peoples. They are to serve Babylon for 70 years, after which God will punish Babylon. Through the image of a cup of wrath, Jeremiah shows that all nations, from Jerusalem to the ends of the earth, must drink God's judgment—and eventually Babylon itself. The chapter concludes with judgment on the shepherds (leaders) who have not protected the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Cup of Wrath** – A cup of wine symbolizing God's judgment; all nations must drink and stagger.
- **70 Years** – The period Judah is to serve Babylon before God punishes the oppressor.
- **Nebuchadnezzar, My Servant** – The king of Babylon whom God uses as an instrument of judgment, but who will himself be judged.
- **The Shepherds** – The leaders who must lament because their time has come; they lose all chance of escape.
- **Sheshak** – A code word for Babylon, which will drink the cup last, yet cannot escape.

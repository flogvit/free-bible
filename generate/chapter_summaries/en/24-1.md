**Main Theme:** God calls Jeremiah to be a prophet for the nations and promises to be with him.

**Content:** Jeremiah, son of the priest Hilkiah from Anathoth, is called as a prophet in the 13th year of King Josiah's reign. God tells him that He knew and set him apart even before his birth. When Jeremiah protests that he is too young, God touches his mouth and gives him authority over the nations—to uproot and tear down, to destroy and overthrow, but also to build and to plant. Through visions of an almond branch and a boiling pot tilting from the north, God reveals that He watches over His word to fulfill it, and that disaster will come from the north upon Judah because of their idolatry.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Called from the womb:** God knew and set Jeremiah apart before his birth, showing that the prophetic mission was God's sovereign will.
- **Almond branch (שָׁקֵד):** A wordplay with "watch" (שֹׁקֵד)—symbolizing that God watches over His word to fulfill it.
- **Boiling pot from the north:** Symbolizes the coming judgment upon Judah from northern enemies.
- **Fortified city, pillar of iron, wall of bronze:** Images of God's protection of Jeremiah against opposition from kings, leaders, and people.

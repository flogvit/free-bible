**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about discipleship and servant leadership on the way to Jerusalem.

**Content:** Jesus teaches about the indissolubility of marriage and welcomes children as examples of how to receive God's kingdom. A rich man goes away sorrowful when Jesus calls him to sell everything and follow Him, and Jesus warns how difficult it is for the wealthy to enter God's kingdom. On the way to Jerusalem, Jesus predicts His suffering, death, and resurrection, and teaches the disciples that true greatness is found in serving—just as the Son of Man came to serve and give His life as a ransom. The chapter concludes with blind Bartimaeus being healed through faith and following Jesus on the way.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **One flesh (σὰρξ μία):** Marriage unites man and woman into one unity that should not be separated.
- **Like a child:** God's kingdom must be received with a child's openness and trust.
- **The camel and the eye of a needle:** A dramatic illustration of how impossible it is for the rich to save themselves—but all things are possible with God.
- **Ransom (λύτρον):** The Son of Man gives His life as a ransom for many.
- **Your faith has saved you:** Bartimaeus' healing shows that faith opens the way for God's saving action.

**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about vigilance, trust in God, and right priorities in light of God's kingdom and the coming judgment.

**Content:** Jesus warns the disciples against the yeast of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy, and encourages bold confession. Through the parable of the rich fool, he teaches that life does not consist in an abundance of possessions and urges seeking God's kingdom rather than material worries. He calls for vigilance with parables about servants waiting for their master and warns that his coming will bring division. Finally, he challenges the crowds to interpret the signs of the times and settle with their accuser while there is still time.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Yeast (ζύμη):** Symbol of the Pharisees' hypocrisy that spreads and destroys – disciples must guard against insincerity.
- **The rich fool (ἄφρων):** Example of foolishness – storing up treasures for oneself without being rich toward God.
- **Ravens and lilies:** Illustrate God's care for creation and how much more he will provide for his children.
- **The faithful steward (οἰκονόμος):** Picture of responsible service while waiting for the Lord's coming.
- **Fire and baptism:** Jesus describes his suffering and how his coming brings both judgment and division.

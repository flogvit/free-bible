**Main Theme:** The War Against Moab and God’s Miraculous Help Through the Prophet Elisha.

**Content:** King Joram of Israel revolted against Baal but held fast to the sins of Jeroboam. When Moab revolted, Joram allied himself with Jehoshaphat of Judah and the king of Edom. When the armies ran out of water, they sought the prophet Elisha, who prophesied that God would provide both water and victory. The water came miraculously, and the Moabites were deceived by its red color into thinking the kings had killed each other. Israel prevailed, but when the king of Moab sacrificed his firstborn son, great wrath came upon Israel, and they withdrew.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Sins of Jeroboam:** The persistent idolatry in the Northern Kingdom that even reform-minded kings did not abandon.
- **Water That Looked Like Blood:** The Moabites’ misinterpretation led to their defeat—a picture of how God uses circumstances to confuse the enemy.
- **Human Sacrifice:** The desperate act of Moab’s king reveals the cruelty of pagan idolatry and stands in contrast to Israel’s God.
- **Elisha:** The prophet who conveys God’s word and miraculous intervention, responding only because of Jehoshaphat’s faith.

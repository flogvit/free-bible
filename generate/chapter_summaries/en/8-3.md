**Main Theme:** Ruth goes to the threshing floor and asks Boaz to be her kinsman-redeemer.

**Content:** Naomi instructs Ruth to prepare herself and go to the threshing floor at night to find Boaz. Ruth lies down at his feet and asks him to "spread his cloak" over her as a kinsman-redeemer. Boaz praises her hesed and promises to settle the matter, but notes that there is a closer kinsman-redeemer. He sends her home before dawn with six measures of barley, and Naomi assures her that Boaz will resolve everything that same day.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Threshing Floor (גֹּרֶן):** The place for threshing grain, which becomes the setting for the decisive meeting between Ruth and Boaz.
- **Spread the Cloak (פָרַשׂ כָּנָף):** Ruth's request for marital protection, symbolizing taking someone under one's care.
- **Kinsman-Redeemer (גֹּאֵל):** A relative with the right and duty to redeem property and continue the name of a deceased family member.
- **Hesed (חֶסֶד):** Boaz commends Ruth's faithful love as greater than that shown by others, because she sought him rather than younger men.

**Main Theme:** Psalm 24 is a liturgical hymn that celebrates God's sovereignty over creation and his majestic entrance as the King of glory.

**Content:** The psalm begins by declaring that the earth and everything in it belongs to the Lord, because he founded it upon the seas. It then asks who may ascend the mountain of the Lord—the answer is the one with clean hands and a pure heart. The psalm culminates in a dramatic call to open the ancient gates for the King of glory, who is identified as YHWH Sebaot—the Lord, strong and mighty, mighty in battle.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The mountain of the Lord / holy place:** God's dwelling where only the worthy may draw near.
- **Clean hands and a pure heart:** Ethical and inner requirements for standing in God's presence.
- **King of glory (מֶלֶךְ הַכָּבוֹד):** God's majestic title as warrior and victor.
- **Ancient gates (פִּתְחֵי עוֹלָם):** Symbolic gates opened for God's entrance, likely connected to the temple.

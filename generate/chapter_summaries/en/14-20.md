**Main Theme:** God grants Judah a miraculous victory over an overwhelming enemy army when they seek Him in prayer and praise.

**Content:** King Jehoshaphat faces a massive invasion from Moab, Ammon, and the hill country of Seir. He proclaims a fast and gathers the people for prayer, declaring: "We do not know what to do, but our eyes are turned toward you." The Levite Jahaziel prophesies that the battle belongs to God. The next day, Jehoshaphat sends singers ahead of the army, and the enemies destroy one another. After three days of gathering spoils, Judah returns home with joy, and the fear of God falls upon surrounding kingdoms. The chapter concludes with Jehoshaphat's alliance with the wicked king Ahaziah resulting in their ships being shattered at Ezion-Geber.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The battle belongs to God"** – The core message of the prophecy: God's people need not fight when the Lord fights for them.
- **Praise before victory** – Singers went ahead of the army singing, "Praise the Lord, for his mercy endures forever," and victory came through worship.
- **Valley of Beracah** – The "Valley of Blessing," named after the people's thanksgiving; reminds us that God's intervention leads to blessing.
- **Enemy self-destruction** – Ammon, Moab, and Seir turned against each other—an illustration of how evil ultimately destroys itself.

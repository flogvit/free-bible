**Main Theme:** A vision of life-giving water flowing from the temple and the distribution of the promised land.

**Content:** Ezekiel sees water flowing out from under the threshold of the temple, eastward toward the Dead Sea. The water gradually grows deeper—from ankle-deep to a river that cannot be crossed—and heals everything it touches, so that the Dead Sea is filled with life and fish. Along the riverbank grow trees with healing leaves and fruit that never fails. The chapter then describes the boundaries of Israel's land and how it is to be divided among the twelve tribes, where even foreigners are to receive an inheritance on equal terms with the Israelites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Temple Spring:** Water flowing from the sanctuary symbolizes God's blessing and life that proceeds from his presence.
- **The Growing Waters:** The four measurements (ankles, knees, hips, swimable) illustrate how God's blessing increases and cannot be contained.
- **Healing of the Dead Sea:** The salty, lifeless sea is healed—a picture of God's power to restore what is dead.
- **Trees by the River:** Fruit trees with ever-lasting fruit and healing leaves symbolize enduring life and healing from God.
- **The Division of the Land:** The inclusion of foreigners in the inheritance shows God's grace extending beyond Israel.

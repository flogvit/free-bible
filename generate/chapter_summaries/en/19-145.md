**Main Theme:** An acrostic psalm of praise celebrating God's eternal kingdom, majesty, and faithful care for all his creatures.

**Content:** David exalts God as the eternal King and commits to praising his name forever. The psalm proclaims God's incomprehensible greatness, his grace and mercy toward all, and his righteousness in everything he does. It describes how the Lord upholds those who fall, provides food for all living things, and is near to all who call on him in truth. Finally, all flesh is urged to bless God's holy name forever and ever.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's kingdom (malkut):** God's kingdom is eternal and extends over all generations (v.13).
- **Gracious and merciful (channun ve-rachum):** The classic description of God's character—slow to anger and rich in compassion (v.8).
- **Opens his hand (poteach et-yadecha):** A picture of God's generous provision that satisfies every living thing (v.16).
- **Near (karov):** The Lord is near all who call on him in truth and hears their cry (vv.18–19).

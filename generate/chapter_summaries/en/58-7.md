**Main Theme:** Jesus is an eternal high priest in the order of Melchizedek, superior to the Levitical priesthood.

**Content:** Melchizedek was king of Salem and priest of God Most High, and Abraham gave him a tenth—demonstrating his greatness. The author argues that the Levitical priesthood could not bring anything to perfection, and that a change in the priesthood entails a change in the law. Jesus, from the tribe of Judah, has been appointed as priest by God’s oath and lives forever to intercede for those who come to God through him. He is holy, innocent, and exalted above the heavens, having offered himself once for all.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Melchizedek:** A mysterious priest-king to whom Abraham gave a tenth; a foreshadowing of Christ as eternal priest.
- **In the order of Melchizedek:** Jesus’ priesthood is not based on physical descent, but on the power of an indestructible life.
- **A better hope:** The new arrangement brings us near to God, something the law could not achieve.
- **Eternal intercession:** Jesus always lives to intercede for believers and therefore can save completely.

**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts the congregation to prayer, hard work, and proper handling of those who live disorderly lives.

**Content:** Paul asks for intercession so that the Lord's word may spread widely and be honored, and for protection from evil people. He warns against associating with those who walk disorderly and refuse to work, reminding them of his own example: "If anyone will not work, neither shall he eat." The disobedient are to be admonished as brothers, not treated as enemies. The letter concludes with a blessing of peace and Paul's personal greeting as a mark of authenticity.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Disorderly conduct (ἀτάκτως):** Living without discipline, especially by avoiding work and engaging in useless activity.
- **The Lord's faithfulness:** The Lord will strengthen and preserve the congregation from evil (v. 3).
- **Example (τύπος):** Paul points to his own labor as a pattern for the congregation to imitate.
- **Brotherly admonition:** The disobedient are to be corrected in love, not cast out as enemies.

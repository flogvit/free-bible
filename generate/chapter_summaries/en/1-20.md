**Main Theme:** God’s protection of Sarah and the sanctity of marriage despite Abraham’s fear and misleading half-truths.

**Content:** Abraham travels to Gerar and presents Sarah as his sister, whereupon King Abimelek takes her. God warns Abimelek in a dream and reveals that Sarah is married. Abimelek returns Sarah with gifts and land rights, while Abraham – whom God calls a prophet – prays for the king’s household so that God heals them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Prophet (נָבִיא):** The first time this title is used in the Bible, referring to Abraham as an intercessor.
- **Dream:** God communicates with Abimelek through a nighttime dream to prevent sin.
- **Innocent Heart (בְּתָם־לְבָב):** Abimelek acted in good faith, and God acknowledges his innocence.
- **Healing:** God had closed every womb in Abimelek’s household but healed them after Abraham’s prayer.

**Main Theme:** A song of praise that extols God's greatness and his protection of Zion, the holy city.

**Content:** The psalm begins with praise to the Lord in his holy city, Zion, which is described as beautiful and a joy for all the earth. Hostile kings who gathered against the city were seized with fear and fled in panic. The people are encouraged to walk around Zion, count its towers, and consider its walls, so that they may tell future generations about God, who is their God forever and will guide them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Zion:** God's holy dwelling place and the city of the great King, the center of his presence on earth.
- **The Flight of the Kings:** Hostile rulers who attacked the city were struck with terror, a testimony to God's invincible protection.
- **Ships of Tarshish:** A symbol of human power and pride that is shattered by God's east wind.
- **The Towers and Walls:** Concrete signs of the city's strength that testify to God's faithfulness through generations.

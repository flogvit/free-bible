**Main Theme:** Samson's revenge campaign against the Philistines culminates in a mighty victory through God's power.

**Content:** When Samson's father-in-law gives his wife to another man, Samson takes revenge by burning the Philistines' crops with torches tied to 300 jackals. The Philistines kill his former wife and her father, and Samson strikes back with a great defeat of them. The men of Judah hand him over bound to the Philistines, but the Spirit of the Lord comes upon him—he breaks the bonds and kills a thousand men with an ass's jawbone. God answers his prayer for water by opening a spring, and Samson judges Israel for twenty years.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ass's Jawbone (לְחִי חֲמוֹר):** Symbolizes how God uses simple tools for mighty victories.
- **The Spirit of the Lord (רוּחַ יְהוָה):** God's power that comes upon Samson and gives him supernatural strength.
- **En-Hakkore (עֵין הַקּוֹרֵא):** "The Spring of the Caller" – reminds us of God's answer to prayer in times of distress.
- **Twenty Years as Judge:** Marks Samson's role as Israel's leader during Philistine domination.

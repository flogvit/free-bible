**Main Theme:** God accuses Israel of deceit and false alliances, reminding them of their history with Jacob and the prophets to call them to repentance.

**Content:** Ephraim surrounds God with lies and deception, making covenants with Assyria and Egypt instead of trusting in the Lord. The prophet recalls how Jacob deceived his brother and wrestled with God, urging the people to turn back, hold fast to faithfulness and justice, and always wait upon God. Ephraim is compared to a deceitful merchant who boasts of wealth, but God will hold them accountable. The Lord reminds them that He brought Israel out of Egypt through a prophet, warning that Ephraim's blood will come upon themselves.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Wind and east wind (v.2):** Ephraim chases after emptiness and destruction instead of seeking God.
- **Jacob (vv.4–5):** The patriarch who both deceived and wrestled with God—a picture of Israel's relationship with the Lord.
- **False scales (v.8):** Ephraim is called "Canaan" (merchant) with treacherous scales, symbolizing economic and spiritual dishonesty.
- **The prophet (vv.11, 14):** God has always spoken through prophets—through a prophet He brought Israel out of Egypt.

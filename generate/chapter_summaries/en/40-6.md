**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about genuine righteousness practiced for God alone, not for human recognition.

**Content:** The chapter begins with a warning against practicing righteousness to be seen by others (v. 1). Jesus then provides guidance on three acts of piety—giving to the needy, prayer, and fasting—all of which are to be done in secret for the Father. The Lord's Prayer is presented as the model prayer (vv. 9-13). The chapter concludes with teaching about storing up treasures in heaven rather than on earth, and an exhortation not to worry but to seek God's kingdom first.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hypocrites (ὑποκριταί):** Those who practice their righteousness to be honored by people, having already received their reward.
- **In secret (ἐν τῷ κρυπτῷ):** God's perspective—the Father sees what is done in secret and will reward openly.
- **The Lord's Prayer:** The model prayer that teaches disciples to pray with focus on God's name, kingdom, and will, as well as daily needs and forgiveness.
- **Mammon (μαμωνᾷ):** Wealth as a competing master—one cannot serve both God and money.
- **Worry (μεριμνάω):** Jesus warns against anxious worry and points to God's care for birds and flowers as evidence of His provision.

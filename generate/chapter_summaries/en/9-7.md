**Main Theme:** Samuel leads Israel's spiritual renewal and achieves victory over the Philistines through prayer and faithfulness to God.

**Content:** After the ark of the Lord had been in Kiriath-Jearim for twenty years, Samuel called Israel to repentance by removing foreign gods. He gathered the people at Mizpah for fasting and prayer, and when the Philistines attacked, the Lord thundered against them so that Israel was victorious. Samuel set up the Ebenezer stone as a memorial and judged Israel throughout his life, making annual journeys between Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ebenezer ("Stone of Help"):** A memorial stone Samuel erected to mark God's help in the victory over the Philistines.
- **Repentance:** Israel's call to remove Baal and Ashtoreth and return to the Lord alone.
- **Samuel's Judgeship:** His lifelong service as judge and intercessor for Israel, with an annual circuit through the land.
- **God's Thunder:** The Lord's supernatural intervention that caused confusion among the Philistines and gave Israel victory.

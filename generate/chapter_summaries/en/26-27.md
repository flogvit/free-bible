**Main Theme:** A lament over the splendor of the trading city of Tyre and its coming destruction.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to deliver a lament over Tyre, which is compared to a magnificent ship built from the finest materials. The city boasts of its perfect beauty and trades with numerous nations from Tarshish to Arabia. But an east wind shatters the ship in the middle of the sea, and all its wealth, crew, and warriors sink. All who traded with Tyre mourn bitterly, for the mighty city has become nothing forever.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Ship:** Tyre is described as a magnificent ship with materials from many lands—a picture of the city's greatness and vulnerability.
- **The Trade Network:** Verses 12–24 mention trading partners from all over the known world, showing Tyre's enormous wealth and influence.
- **The East Wind:** Symbolizes God's judgment that shatters Tyre in the midst of its glory (v. 26).
- **The Lament (קִינָה):** The genre emphasizes that this is a dirge over something already considered lost.
- **"In the middle of the sea":** Repeated several times, marking both Tyre's strength and the place of its downfall.

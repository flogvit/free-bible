**Main Theme:** Faithfulness to God in Exile – Daniel and his friends hold fast to their faith amid pressure for cultural assimilation in Babylon.

**Content:** King Nebuchadnezzar besieges Jerusalem and takes young men from the royal family to Babylon for training in Babylonian wisdom. Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah are given new Babylonian names and are to eat the king's food. Daniel decides not to defile himself and asks for vegetables and water instead. After ten days, they appear healthier than the others, and God grants them extraordinary wisdom – Daniel also receives the ability to understand visions and dreams. At the end of their training, they surpass all the wise men of the kingdom by ten times.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Defilement (יִתְגָּאַל):** Daniel refuses to profane himself with the king's food – faithfulness to God's commands takes precedence over career advancement.
- **Name Change:** The four are given Babylonian names in an attempt to replace their Israelite identity.
- **Ten-Day Test:** A trial that demonstrates how God blesses faithfulness with physical and spiritual strength.
- **Wisdom from God:** All their knowledge and insight are gifts from God, not merely the result of their training.

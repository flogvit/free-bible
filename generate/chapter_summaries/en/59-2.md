**Main Theme:** True faith is demonstrated through deeds and impartial love for all people.

**Content:** James warns against discrimination based on social status, as this violates the royal law of loving one's neighbor. He argues strongly that faith without works is dead and worthless—like a body without spirit. Abraham and Rahab are highlighted as examples showing that justification comes through faith that expresses itself in action. Mercy triumphs over judgment.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Partiality (προσωπολημψία):** Favoring the rich over the poor in the assembly contradicts faith in Christ.
- **The Royal Law:** The command to love your neighbor as yourself is the standard for right conduct.
- **Faith and Works:** Faith without works is dead; genuine faith cooperates with and is perfected through deeds.
- **The Law of Freedom:** Believers are to live and be judged by this law, in which mercy triumphs.

**Main Theme:** Israel praises God for salvation at the Red Sea and experiences his care in the wilderness.

**Content:** Moses and the Israelites sing a song of praise that honors God as warrior and deliverer after the victory over Egypt at the Red Sea. The song extols God's majesty and power, describing how nations will tremble with fear as Israel journeys toward the promised land. Miriam leads the women in dance and song with tambourines. At Marah, God makes bitter water drinkable through a tree, promising to be Israel's healer if they keep his commands. The people camp at Elim, where there are twelve springs and seventy palm trees.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's right hand** (v. 6, 12): A symbol of God's power and victory over enemies.
- **Who is like you among the gods?** (v. 11): A proclamation of God's incomparable holiness and majesty.
- **The Lord will reign forever** (v. 18): The climax of the song, declaring God's eternal kingdom.
- **The tree at Marah** (v. 25): God's means of making bitter water sweet—a sign of his care and testing.

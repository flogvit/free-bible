**Main Theme:** God's unfailing fatherly love for Israel despite the people's persistent unfaithfulness.

**Content:** God reminds how He called Israel as His son out of Egypt and tenderly taught Ephraim to walk, yet the people turned to Baal and idols. Although judgment must come through Assyria, God reveals His inner struggle: He cannot completely abandon Israel because He is God and not a human being. The chapter concludes with a promise that the people will return from exile like birds and dwell in their homes once more.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **My Son out of Egypt (v.1):** Expresses God's intimate father-son relationship with Israel and the Exodus as the foundational story.
- **Taught to walk / carried on arms (v.3):** A picture of God's tender care, like a father teaching his child to walk.
- **Bonds of love (v.4):** God drew His people with human bonds and cords of love, not by force.
- **God, not man (v.9):** God's holiness and divine nature are the reason He shows mercy rather than acting in wrath.
- **Birds from Egypt and Assyria (v.11):** A symbol of the people's return from exile—trembling yet secure.

**Main Theme:** A psalm of trust in which David confesses the Lord as his only refuge, inheritance, and source of lasting joy.

**Content:** David seeks protection from God and declares Him to be his Lord and supreme good. He distances himself from idolatry and confesses that the Lord is his portion and cup—his lot has fallen in a glorious place. Because the Lord is always at his side, he can live securely with the certainty that God will not abandon him to the realm of the dead but will show him the path of life and eternal joy in His presence.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Inheritance and cup (v.5):** The Lord Himself is David’s portion and lot, not earthly goods or other gods.
- **The realm of the dead/the grave (v.10):** Confidence that God will not leave His faithful ones to death and decay.
- **The path of life (v.11):** God reveals the way to perfect life and eternal joy in His presence.
- **The Lord at my right hand (v.8):** God’s constant presence provides security and stability.

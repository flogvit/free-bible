**Main Theme:** The consecration of Aaron and his sons to the priestly service through anointing and sacrifice.

**Content:** Moses performs the consecration ceremony according to the Lord's command in front of the entire congregation. He washes Aaron and his sons, clothes them in the priestly garments, and anoints the tabernacle, the altar, and Aaron with holy oil. Then a sin offering bull, a burnt offering ram, and a consecration ram are sacrificed, where the blood from the latter is applied to the priests' right earlobe, thumb, and big toe. Aaron and his sons must remain at the entrance of the tabernacle for seven days to complete the consecration.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Anointing (משׁח):** The holy oil that sanctifies the tabernacle and Aaron for service to the Lord.
- **Consecration Ram (אֵיל הַמִּלֻּאִים):** A special sacrifice marking the priests' installation into office.
- **Blood on the right side:** The blood on the ear, hand, and foot symbolizes that the whole priest is consecrated to hear, act, and walk in God's service.
- **Seven days:** The number of completeness marking the complete period of consecration.

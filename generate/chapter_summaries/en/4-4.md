**Main Theme:** Counting and service assignments for the three Levitical clans: Kohath, Gershon, and Merari.

**Content:** God commands Moses and Aaron to count all Levites between 30 and 50 years of age who are fit for service at the tabernacle. The Kohathites are to carry the most holy objects (the ark, the table, the lampstand, and the altars), but only after Aaron and his sons have covered them; touching or looking at them means death. The Gershonites are given responsibility for the curtains and hangings, while the Merarites carry the framework. In total, 8,580 Levites fit for service are counted.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covering of the holy objects:** Aaron and the priests must cover all the holy things before the Levites can carry them, demonstrating God's holiness and humanity's limited access.
- **Age 30–50 years:** The age range for service shows that sanctuary work required maturity and physical strength.
- **Death by touching:** The warning of death for touching or seeing the holy things emphasizes the absolute reverence required toward God's presence.
- **Division of labor among the clans:** Each clan has specific tasks, illustrating God's ordered structure for service.

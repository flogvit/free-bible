**Main Theme:** Joshua's farewell address to the leaders of Israel, urging faithfulness to the Lord and warning against apostasy.

**Content:** The elderly Joshua gathers the elders, heads, judges, and officials of Israel to give his final exhortation. He reminds them that the Lord has fought for them and driven out mighty nations, and that not one word of all God's good promises has failed. Joshua exhorts the people to hold fast to the Law of Moses and not to intermarry with the remaining nations or worship their gods. He warns that if they break the covenant, God will bring disaster upon them, and they will perish from the good land.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **One man shall chase a thousand** – Illustrates God's power fighting for Israel.
- **The Book of the Law of Moses** – The standard for Israel's obedience, which they must not depart from.
- **A snare and a trap** – The remaining nations will become a snare if Israel mixes with them.
- **Not one word has failed** – God's absolute faithfulness to all His promises.

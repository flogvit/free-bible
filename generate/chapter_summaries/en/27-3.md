**Main Theme:** God’s power to deliver his faithful servants from the decree of the most powerful king.

**Content:** King Nebuchadnezzar erected a golden statue on the plain of Dura and commanded all people to worship it at the sound of music. Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refused to bow down and were thrown into a furnace heated seven times hotter than usual. The king saw four men walking freely in the fire, the fourth "looking like a son of the gods." The three emerged unharmed, and the king praised their God and promoted them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Golden Statue:** A symbol of Nebuchadnezzar’s power and his demand for absolute loyalty from all peoples.
- **The Fiery Furnace:** Represents the ultimate threat and punishment, yet becomes the place of divine revelation.
- **The Fourth Figure:** A heavenly being who testifies to God’s presence in the midst of trial.
- **Unshakable Faith:** The three men’s willingness to die rather than compromise their faith in God.

**Main Theme:** God gives Israel victory over Jericho through faith and obedience, not military strength.

**Content:** God instructs Joshua to march around the closed city of Jericho for six days, with priests carrying the ark of the covenant and blowing trumpets. On the seventh day, they circle seven times, and when the people shout, the walls fall down. The city is placed under the ban (cherem) – all life is destroyed and metal objects are consecrated to the Lord’s treasury. Only Rahab and her family are spared because she hid the spies. Joshua pronounces a curse on anyone who rebuilds the city.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant:** God’s presence goes before the people in battle – the victory is His, not theirs.
- **Shofars (trumpets):** Symbolize God’s intervention and proclamation of victory.
- **Cherem (the ban):** The city and everything in it belong to God as the firstfruits of the conquest.
- **Rahab:** The Gentile who by faith is incorporated into Israel and saved from judgment.
- **The falling walls:** God’s supernatural power brings down what is impregnable to humans.

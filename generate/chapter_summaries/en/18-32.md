**Main Theme:** Elihu, a young man, steps forward to speak after Job's three friends have failed to give him a satisfactory answer.

**Content:** Elihu has waited patiently while the elders spoke, but becomes frustrated that his friends cannot answer Job and yet still condemn him as guilty. He is also disturbed that Job defends himself rather than God. Elihu claims that it is not age that brings wisdom, but God's Spirit that gives people understanding. He feels compelled to speak and promises to be impartial.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's Spirit (נִשְׁמַת שַׁדַּי):** It is the breath of the Almighty, not age, that gives humans insight and understanding.
- **New wineskins:** Elihu compares his inner being to wine in new wineskins that are about to burst—he is full of words that must come out.
- **Impartiality:** Elihu promises not to show favoritism to anyone, for then his Creator would take him away.

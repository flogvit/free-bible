**Main Theme:** Longing for the beloved and celebration of royal wedding splendor.

**Content:** The woman seeks her beloved during the night, first on her bed, then through the city streets and squares. After asking the city guards, she finds him and brings him to her mother's house. The chapter concludes with a magnificent description of King Solomon's wedding procession, featuring his litter surrounded by sixty of Israel's mighty men.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Nighttime Search:** The woman's intense longing drives her out into the city to find her beloved.
- **City Guards:** Represent authorities whom the woman approaches in her search.
- **Solomon's Litter (appiryon):** A magnificent canopy made of cedar from Lebanon, with silver pillars and a golden seat, symbolizing royal splendor.
- **Wedding Day:** The daughters of Zion are urged to look upon the king wearing the crown his mother placed on him on his wedding day.

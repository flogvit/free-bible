**Main Theme:** God's protection of the infant Jesus against Herod's persecution, fulfilling Old Testament prophecies.

**Content:** Wise men from the East follow a star to Jerusalem to worship the newborn King of the Jews. King Herod, disturbed by this news, tries to locate the child through them, but they are warned in a dream and return home by another route. Joseph flees with his family to Egypt after an angelic warning, while Herod massacres the male children in Bethlehem. After Herod's death, the family returns and settles in Nazareth in Galilee.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Star:** A divine sign leading the Gentiles (the wise men) to the Messiah.
- **The Gifts (gold, frankincense, myrrh):** Symbolize Jesus' kingship, divinity, and coming suffering.
- **Flight to Egypt:** Presents Jesus as the new Moses and fulfills the prophecy "Out of Egypt I called my son."
- **Herod's Massacre of Infants:** Fulfills Jeremiah's prophecy about Rachel weeping for her children.

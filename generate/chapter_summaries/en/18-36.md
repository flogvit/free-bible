**Main Theme:** Elihu defends God's righteous rule and points to His incomprehensible greatness in creation.

**Content:** Elihu asserts that God is just – He punishes the wicked and grants justice to the poor. Suffering is used by God to reveal human sins and call people to repentance; those who listen will live in blessing, while those who reject Him will perish. Elihu warns Job against turning to bitterness and concludes by lifting his gaze toward God's unfathomable greatness displayed through nature's forces – rain, clouds, and lightning.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Suffering as discipline** – God uses distress to open people's ears to correction and call them back from sin (vv. 8–10, 15).
- **The consequences of choice** – Those who obey God complete their days in prosperity; those who do not listen perish in ignorance (vv. 11–12).
- **Rain and clouds** – God controls the water cycle and demonstrates His power through natural phenomena (vv. 27–28).
- **God's unfathomability** – God is exalted, and the number of His years cannot be explored (v. 26).

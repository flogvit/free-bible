**Main Theme:** Paul arrives in Jerusalem despite prophetic warnings and is arrested after a riot in the temple.

**Content:** Paul travels through Tyre and Caesarea to Jerusalem, where believers and the prophet Agabus warn him of impending imprisonment. Nevertheless, Paul is prepared to die for the Lord's name. In Jerusalem, he meets James and the elders, who advise him to participate in a purification ritual to show that he honors the law. Jews from Asia falsely accuse him of bringing Gentiles into the temple, sparking a riot. Roman soldiers intervene and arrest Paul, granting him permission to address the crowd.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Agabus's prophecy:** The prophet binds himself with Paul's belt as a heavy symbolic sign that Paul will be bound by the Jews and handed over to the Gentiles.
- **"The Lord's will be done":** The believers' response when Paul cannot be persuaded—an expression of surrender to God's plan.
- **Purification ritual:** Paul participates in Jewish purification with four men to show that he does not teach Jews to reject the Mosaic law.
- **Trophimus from Ephesus:** A Gentile Christian seen with Paul in the city, mistakenly assumed to have been brought into the temple.

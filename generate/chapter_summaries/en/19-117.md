**Main Theme:** A universal call to all nations to praise the Lord for his enduring love and faithfulness.

**Content:** Psalm 117 is the shortest chapter in the Bible, containing only two verses. It issues a powerful appeal to all nations and peoples to praise Yahweh. The basis for this call is God's overwhelming faithful love (chesed) toward Israel and his eternal faithfulness (emet). The psalm concludes with the liturgical exclamation "Halleluja".

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Chesed (faithful love):** God's covenant loyalty and gracious devotion that "overcomes" or is mighty over us.
- **Emet (truth/faithfulness):** God's reliability that endures forever.
- **All nations (kol-gojim):** A universal vision in which the Gentile peoples are called to join in praising the God of Israel.
- **Halleluja:** A liturgical conclusion meaning "Praise the Lord" – framing the purpose of the psalm.

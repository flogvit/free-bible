**Main Theme:** God judges rebellious Jerusalem, but promises to cleanse the people and restore a humble remnant in whom He delights.

**Content:** The chapter begins with judgment against Jerusalem for its rebellion—its leaders are like roaring lions and wolves, its prophets are untrustworthy, and its priests profane what is holy. God announces that He will gather the nations for judgment, but afterward give the peoples a pure language so they may serve Him together. He promises to remove the proud and leave behind a humble people who take refuge in the name of the Lord. The chapter ends with rejoicing: the Lord Himself is in their midst as a mighty Savior who delights in His people with singing.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Roaring lions and wolves** (v.3): This image describes Jerusalem’s corrupt leaders and judges as predators who exploit the people.
- **A pure language** (v.9): God will transform the peoples so they may call upon His name and serve Him in unity.
- **A humble and lowly remnant** (v.12): The purified group that remains will take refuge in the Lord.
- **God rejoices with singing** (v.17): This surprising image of God singing for joy over His people expresses His deep love.

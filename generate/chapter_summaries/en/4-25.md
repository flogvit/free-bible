**Main Theme:** Israel's sin with Baal-Peor leads to God's judgment, but Phinehas' zealous action stops the plague and secures for him an eternal covenant priesthood.

**Content:** At Shittim, the Israelites begin to engage in sexual immorality with Moabite women and worship Baal-Peor, which arouses God's anger. A plague kills 24,000 people. When Zimri, a leader from the tribe of Simeon, openly brings the Midianite woman Cozbi into the camp, the priest Phinehas intervenes and kills them both with a spear, and the plague stops. God rewards Phinehas with a covenant of peace and an eternal priesthood for his zeal, and commands Israel to treat the Midianites as enemies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Baal-Peor:** A local idol that Israel joined through idolatry and sexual immorality.
- **Phinehas' Zeal:** His decisive action demonstrated godly fervor that turned away God's wrath from the people.
- **Covenant of Peace:** God's promise to Phinehas of an eternal priesthood as a reward for his faithfulness.
- **The Plague:** God's judgment on Israel's sin, which took 24,000 lives before it was stopped.

**Main Theme:** Solomon’s wisdom and wealth reach their peak, confirmed by the visit of the Queen of Sheba and God’s blessing.

**Content:** The Queen of Sheba comes to test Solomon’s wisdom with riddles and is overwhelmed by his knowledge and splendor. She praises the God of Israel who has placed Solomon on the throne. The chapter then describes Solomon’s immense wealth: 666 talents of gold annually, a magnificent ivory throne, and merchant fleets that bring exotic goods. Solomon reigned for 40 years before his son Rehoboam succeeded him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Queen of Sheba:** A pagan queen who acknowledges the God of Israel and testifies to Solomon’s God-given wisdom.
- **666 talents of gold:** Symbolizes the extreme wealth that flowed into Israel during Solomon’s reign.
- **Ivory throne:** A magnificent throne with twelve lions, illustrating the greatness and splendor of royal authority.
- **Ships of Tarshish:** The merchant fleet that every three years brought gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks.

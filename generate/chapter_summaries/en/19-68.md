**Main Theme:** A triumphant song of praise celebrating God's power, his care for the weak, and his victorious march with his people.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a prayer that God should arise and scatter his enemies. It praises God as a father to the fatherless and defender of widows, and as the one who gives the lonely a home. The psalm describes God's mighty march from Sinai through the wilderness, his victory over kings, and his entry into the sanctuary. It culminates in an invitation for all the kingdoms of the earth to sing to God and acknowledge his sovereignty.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Rider on the clouds" (v.5):** An image of God's majesty and sovereignty over creation.
- **"Father of the fatherless" (v.6):** Expresses God's care for the vulnerable and marginalized in society.
- **"Ascended on high, led captives" (v.19):** A prophetic picture of God's (and the Messiah's) triumph, quoted in Ephesians 4:8.
- **"God's chariots are ten thousands" (v.18):** Symbolizes God's invincible army and heavenly power.

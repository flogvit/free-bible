**Main Theme:** God's judgment on Israel's idolatry and the promise of a surviving remnant.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to prophesy against the mountains of Israel concerning the coming destruction. The high places, altars, and idols will be destroyed, and the people will be struck by sword, famine, and pestilence. A remnant will survive in exile; these will remember God and feel disgust for their abominations. Through judgment, they will know that the Lord is God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The mountains of Israel:** Symbolize the high places where idolatry took place.
- **Bamot (high places):** The cult sites to be destroyed along with the altars and sun pillars.
- **Sword, famine, and pestilence:** The three instruments of judgment that will strike the people wherever they are.
- **"You shall know that I am the Lord":** The repeated purpose of judgment—to reveal God's identity and power.

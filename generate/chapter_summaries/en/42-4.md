**Main Theme:** Jesus begins his public ministry after overcoming the devil's temptations, demonstrating his authority through teaching and healings.

**Content:** Filled with the Holy Spirit, Jesus is led into the wilderness where he fasts for 40 days and rejects the devil's three temptations using Scripture. In the synagogue at Nazareth, he reads from Isaiah and declares that the prophecy is fulfilled, but the people become enraged and attempt to kill him. In Capernaum, Jesus teaches with authority, casts out an unclean spirit, heals Simon's mother-in-law and many sick people. He continues to proclaim the kingdom of God in the synagogues.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Temptation in the Wilderness:** Jesus fasts for 40 days and overcomes the devil by quoting Scripture, demonstrating his obedience to God.
- **The Lord's Favorable Year (Isaiah 61):** Jesus declares himself as the anointed one who brings freedom to captives and sight to the blind—the Messianic prophecy is fulfilled.
- **No Prophet Is Accepted in His Hometown:** Jesus explains the rejection in Nazareth by referring to Elijah and Elisha, who were sent to Gentiles.
- **Authority (ἐξουσία):** The people are amazed that Jesus commands unclean spirits with authority and power.

**Main Theme:** A lament over the destruction of the sanctuary, with a plea for God to intervene against enemies who mock His name.

**Content:** Asaph laments that God appears to have rejected His people and allowed enemies to destroy the sanctuary by fire. The people experience that there are no prophets left, and no one knows how long the suffering will last. The psalmist reminds God of His mighty works in creation and salvation history, and urges Him to rise up to defend His covenant and His people against those who mock His name.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Sanctuary (מִקְדָּשׁ):** The temple that enemies have burned and desecrated, symbolizing God's presence.
- **Leviathan:** The sea monster that God crushed, representing His sovereign power over the forces of chaos.
- **Covenant (בְּרִית):** The basis for the people's appeal to God to remember His obligations.
- **God's Hand:** The psalmist asks why God holds back His right hand instead of intervening.

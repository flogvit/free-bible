**Main Theme:** A thanksgiving psalm in which the psalmist praises God for deliverance from death and vows to serve him faithfully.

**Content:** The psalmist loves the Lord because he heard his prayer when the cords of death surrounded him. He confesses that the Lord is gracious, righteous, and merciful, and that he protects the simple. In response, the psalmist will lift up the cup of salvation, fulfill his vows, and bring thank offerings to the temple in Jerusalem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- *Cords of death* (חֶבְלֵי־מָוֶת): A figurative description of life-threatening distress from which God rescued the psalmist.
- *Cup of salvation* (כּוֹס־יְשׁוּעוֹת): A liturgical symbol of gratitude and celebration of God's deliverance.
- *Precious death* (v.15): The death of the faithful is precious in the Lord's sight—an expression of God's care for his people.
- *Lord's servant* (עַבְדְּךָ בֶן־אֲמָתֶךָ): The psalmist acknowledges himself as God's servant, freed from his bonds.

**Main Theme:** God judges the wicked and promises restoration, comfort, and eternal worship for the humble who tremble at his word.

**Content:** God declares that heaven is his throne and the earth his footstool—he seeks not temples, but humble and repentant hearts. False worship is equated with idolatry and will be punished. Zion suddenly gives birth to her children, and God comforts his people as a mother comforts her child. All nations will gather to see God’s glory, and survivors will be sent out to proclaim it to distant peoples. In the new heaven and new earth, all will worship the Lord, while the rebellious will suffer eternal shame.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Heaven as throne:** God is not confined to human-built temples but rules over all creation (vv. 1–2).
- **Zion’s birth:** Zion gives birth to her children suddenly and without pain, a picture of God’s miraculous restoration (vv. 7–9).
- **Mother imagery:** God promises to comfort his people as a mother comforts her child (v. 13).
- **New heaven and new earth:** God creates a new world where his people will endure forever (v. 22).

**Main Theme:** A prophecy of God's judgment against Mount Seir (Edom) because of their perpetual enmity toward Israel.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to prophesy against Mount Seir and announces total destruction. The accusations are Edom's eternal hatred, that they handed over the Israelites to the sword in their time of distress, and that they claimed ownership over Israel and Judah—even though the Lord was there. As punishment, the mountain will become an everlasting desolation, filled with the slain, and its cities will lie in ruins. The goal is that all will know that the Lord is God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Seir:** A geographical term for Edom, the descendants of Esau and Israel's neighboring people to the south.
- **Perpetual enmity (אֵיבַת עוֹלָם):** Edom's long-standing hatred toward Israel, stretching back to Jacob and Esau.
- **Blood (דָּם):** Edom's guilt in shedding blood; those who do not hate blood will be pursued by blood.
- **"The two peoples and the two lands":** Edom's claim over both Israel and Judah as their territory.
- **Recognition formula:** "They/you shall know that I am the Lord" – repeated four times as the purpose of judgment.

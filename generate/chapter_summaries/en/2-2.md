**Main Theme:** Moses’ birth, flight, and God’s preparation to intervene on behalf of His people.

**Content:** Moses is born into a Levite family, hidden for three months, and placed in a basket made of reeds on the Nile, where Pharaoh’s daughter finds and adopts him. As an adult, Moses kills an Egyptian who is mistreating a Hebrew and must flee to Midian, where he marries Zipporah and has a son named Gershom. The chapter concludes with God hearing Israel’s cry under slavery and remembering His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The basket (תֵּבָה):** The same word used for Noah’s ark—a means of rescue through water.
- **"Drawn out of the water":** Moses’ name (מֹשֶׁה) is explained by his deliverance from the Nile.
- **Gershom:** The name means "a stranger there" and expresses Moses’ experience as a refugee in a foreign land.
- **God remembered His covenant:** God’s faithfulness to the promises made to the patriarchs forms the foundation for the coming deliverance.

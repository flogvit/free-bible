**Main Theme:** Rules concerning vows (נֶדֶר) and oaths, with special focus on women's vows under male authority.

**Content:** A man who makes a vow to the Lord must fulfill it without exception. For unmarried women in their father's house and for married women, the vow stands if the father or husband remains silent on the day he hears about it, but it may be annulled if he objects on that same day. Widows and divorced women are fully responsible for their own vows. If a husband annuls a vow after having waited, he himself bears her guilt.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Vow (נֶדֶר):** A binding commitment to God that must be fulfilled.
- **To remain silent (הֶחֱרִישׁ):** Tacit consent that confirms the validity of the vow.
- **To annul (הֵפֵר):** The right of male authority to cancel a woman's vow.
- **The same day:** The deadline by which an objection must be made in order to be valid.

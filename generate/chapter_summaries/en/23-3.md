**Main Theme:** God's judgment on Jerusalem and Judah for their rebellion, unjust leadership, and pride.

**Content:** The Lord will remove all support from Jerusalem—both material (food and water) and human (competent leaders such as judges, prophets, and counselors). Instead, immature and arbitrary rulers will take over, leading to social breakdown where people oppress one another. God accuses the leaders of plundering the vineyard and crushing the poor. The daughters of Zion are condemned for their pride and vanity; their luxury will be replaced with shame and destruction.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Support (מַשְׁעֵן):** God removes all support—material (bread, water) and human (leaders)—as punishment for rebellion.
- **Vineyard:** A symbol of Israel, which God has cultivated but whose leaders have destroyed through exploitation.
- **Daughters of Zion:** Represent the upper-class women of Jerusalem, whose pride and luxury will be transformed into shame and sorrow.
- **Righteous vs. Wicked:** The contrast between the righteous who will enjoy the fruit of their deeds and the wicked who will receive their due reward.

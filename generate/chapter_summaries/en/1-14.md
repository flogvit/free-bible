**Main Theme:** Abram rescues Lot from hostile kings and is blessed by Melchizedek, the priest of God Most High.

**Content:** Four kings under the leadership of Kedorlaomer attack five kings near the Dead Sea and take Lot captive. Abram pursues the enemies with 318 men, defeats them, and frees Lot along with all his possessions. Upon returning, Abram meets Melchizedek, king of Salem, who blesses him, and Abram gives him a tenth. Abram refuses the offer from the king of Sodom regarding war spoils so that he owes nothing to him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Melchizedek:** A mysterious king-priest from Salem who blesses Abram and receives a tenth, serving as a foreshadowing of Christ.
- **Tithing:** Abram's voluntary gift to Melchizedek shows recognition of God as the giver of victory.
- **El Elyon (God Most High):** God's title as Creator and Owner of heaven and earth.
- **Abram's Refusal:** By refusing war spoils, Abram demonstrates that his wealth comes from God alone.

**Main Theme:** Jesus pronounces seven woes upon the Pharisees and scribes for their hypocrisy, concluding with a lament over Jerusalem.

**Content:** Jesus warns his disciples and the crowds against the hypocrisy of religious leaders—they impose heavy burdens on others without lifting a finger to help, seeking honor rather than humble service. In seven woes, he accuses them of shutting the kingdom of heaven in people's faces, neglecting the most important demands of the law (justice, mercy, faithfulness), and being inwardly full of hypocrisy and wickedness. Jesus declares that the blood of all the righteous will be required from this generation, ending with a sorrowful lament over Jerusalem for rejecting his care.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hypocrites (ὑποκριταί):** The Pharisees appear righteous outwardly but are inwardly full of evil—like whitewashed tombs.
- **Blind guides (ὁδηγοὶ τυφλοί):** They lead the people astray by focusing on minor details while overlooking what truly matters.
- **Woes (οὐαί):** Seven prophetic words of judgment exposing the sin of religious leaders.
- **Hen and chicks:** A picture of Jesus' desire to gather and protect Jerusalem, but they were unwilling.
- **Humility and service:** The greatest among you must be a servant—those who exalt themselves will be humbled.

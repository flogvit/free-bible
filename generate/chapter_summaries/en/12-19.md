**Main Theme:** God protects Jerusalem against Assyria in response to King Hezekiah's prayer and faith.

**Content:** King Hezekiah receives threats from the Assyrian king Sennacherib, first through Rabshakeh, then via a letter. Hezekiah seeks God in the temple and prays for deliverance. The prophet Isaiah conveys God's promise to protect Jerusalem and humble Assyria. An angel of the Lord strikes down 185,000 Assyrian soldiers in one night, and Sennacherib returns to Nineveh, where he is later murdered by his own sons.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sackcloth and Temple:** Hezekiah puts on sackcloth and goes to the temple—expressing humility and dependence on God in crisis.
- **The Letter Spread Out Before the Lord:** Hezekiah lays the threatening letter before God—symbolizing surrender of the problem to the Lord.
- **The Angel of the Lord:** God's supernatural intervention that kills 185,000 soldiers in a single night.
- **The Virgin Daughter of Zion:** A poetic image of Jerusalem mocking the defeated enemy (v.21).

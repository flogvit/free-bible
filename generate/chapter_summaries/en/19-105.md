**Main Theme:** A historical psalm of praise that extols God for his faithfulness to the covenant with Abraham and his mighty deeds on behalf of Israel.

**Content:** The psalm begins with an exhortation to praise the Lord and proclaim his great works among the nations. It recounts Israel's salvation history: the covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; Joseph's journey from slave to ruler in Egypt; the sending of Moses and Aaron and the plagues upon Egypt; the Exodus accompanied by silver and gold; and God's care in the wilderness through the pillar of cloud and fire, manna, and water from the rock. The psalm culminates with the fulfillment of the promise of the land, stating its purpose: that Israel should keep God's laws.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant (בְּרִית):** God's eternal promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob regarding the land of Canaan, which remains valid for a thousand generations.
- **God's Wonders (נִפְלְאוֹת):** The mighty signs in Egypt and the wilderness that demonstrate God's sovereign power over nature.
- **Chosen People:** Israel as God's chosen people (בְּחִירָיו), Abraham as his servant, and Aaron as the chosen one.
- **Promise of the Land:** Canaan as the inheritance given by God to his people, fulfilling the covenant promise.

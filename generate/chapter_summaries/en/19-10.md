**Main Theme:** A lament over the conduct of the wicked and a prayer that God will intervene on behalf of the oppressed.

**Content:** The psalmist asks why God remains distant in times of trouble, while the godless pursue the weak and arrogantly deny that God will act. The wicked are described as one who lies in wait like a lion, ready to catch the helpless in his net. The psalmist urges God to arise, break the power of the ungodly, and defend the fatherless. The psalm ends with an affirmation of God's eternal kingdom and confidence that He hears the prayer of the humble.

**Key words/images:**
- **The lion lying in wait (v.9):** The wicked are compared to a lion lying in ambush to snatch its prey.
- **The snare/net (v.9):** A picture of the cunning plans of the wicked to trap the weak.
- **"God has forgotten" (v.11):** The arrogant assumption of the ungodly that God neither sees nor intervenes.
- **God's eternal kingdom (v.16):** The affirmation that the Lord is king forever, in contrast to the false security of the wicked.

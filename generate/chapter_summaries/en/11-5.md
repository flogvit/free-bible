**Main Theme:** Solomon's wisdom, wealth, and preparations for temple construction through an alliance with King Hiram of Tyre.

**Content:** God gave Solomon exceptional wisdom and understanding, and he ruled over a mighty kingdom in peace—from the Euphrates to Egypt. Judah and Israel lived securely under his reign. Since the Lord had now given him rest from enemies, Solomon could fulfill the promise to build a temple, something his father David was unable to do because of wars. He made an agreement with King Hiram of Tyre for the delivery of cedar and cypress trees in exchange for wheat and oil, and organized a workforce of 30,000 men for forced labor.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Wisdom (חָכְמָה):** God gave Solomon wisdom greater than all the wise men of the East and Egypt, and his fame reached all nations.
- **Peace (שָׁלוֹם):** The peace in the kingdom was the prerequisite for temple construction—no enemies or disaster threatened it.
- **Cedar from Lebanon:** Symbolizes the precious materials for God's house, delivered through an international alliance.
- **Forced Labor (מַס):** 30,000 Israelites were organized in shifts to work on the temple project.

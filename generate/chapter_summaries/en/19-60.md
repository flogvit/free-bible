**Main Theme:** A lament psalm in which David seeks God’s help after a military defeat, yet expresses confidence in God’s promises of victory.

**Content:** The psalm is linked to David’s wars against Aram and Edom. David laments that God has rejected and shaken His people, and he pleads for restoration. Amidst the lament, a divine oracle sounds forth, declaring God’s sovereignty over both the land of Israel and the lands of the enemies. The psalm concludes with an affirmation of trust: only through God can they achieve victory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The wine of staggering (v. 5):** A picture of God’s judgment that leaves His people disoriented and helpless.
- **Banner/standard (v. 6):** A rallying point given by God to His faithful ones, symbolizing hope and deliverance.
- **"Moab is my washbasin" (v. 10):** God’s sovereign rule over enemy nations, expressed through humiliating imagery.
- **Fortified city (v. 11):** A reference to Edom’s capital; it represents the seemingly insurmountable enemy whom only God can defeat.

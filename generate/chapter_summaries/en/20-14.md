**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts wisdom and folly through practical life advice concerning speech, work, emotions, and the fear of God.

**Content:** The wise woman builds her house, while the foolish one tears it down with her own hands (v.1). The chapter warns that a way may seem right to a person, but its end is the ways of death (v.12). The fear of the Lord provides secure confidence and is a fountain of life that protects from the snares of death. A central verse declares that righteousness exalts a nation, while sin is a disgrace to peoples (v.34).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Wise Woman (v.1):** Wisdom personified as a woman who builds and preserves her house, in contrast to folly which destroys it.
- **The Way to Death (v.12):** A path that appears right but leads to ruin—a warning against self-deception and false security.
- **Fear of the Lord as a Fountain of Life (vv.26–27):** Reverence for the Lord offers protection and is a source of life that keeps one away from the snares of death.
- **Righteousness and Nations (v.34):** The well-being of nations depends on righteousness; sin brings shame upon peoples.

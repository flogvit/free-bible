**Main Theme:** God sends his messenger to prepare the way, promising judgment on the wicked and blessing upon the godly.

**Content:** The Lord will send his messenger to prepare the way, then the Lord himself—also called the angel of the covenant—will suddenly come to his temple to purify the sons of Levi as silver and gold are refined. Because God is unchanging, the sons of Jacob will not be consumed; instead, he calls them to repentance and accuses them of robbing him in tithes and offerings. The Lord promises abundance and protection from the destroyer to those who bring the full tithe, and he writes the names of the godly in his book of remembrance. The chapter concludes with the promise that the prophet Elijah will be sent before the great and awesome day of the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Angel of the Covenant:** The Lord himself coming suddenly to his temple—he whom the people say they seek and long for.
- **The Refiner's Fire:** God sits as a silversmith, purifying the sons of Levi like silver and gold.
- **Tithes:** The people are accused of robbing God, yet promised heavenly blessings through faithful tithing.
- **The Sun of Righteousness:** Will rise with healing in its wings for those who revere God's name.
- **The Prophet Elijah:** Will be sent before the great and awesome day of the Lord to turn the hearts of parents to their children and the hearts of children to their parents.

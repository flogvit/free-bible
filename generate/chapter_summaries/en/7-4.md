**Main Theme:** God delivers Israel from Canaanite oppression through the prophetess Deborah, the military commander Barak, and the courageous woman Jael.

**Content:** After Ehud's death, Israel does what is evil, and the Lord hands them over to King Jabin of Canaan, whose commander Sisera oppresses them for twenty years with 900 iron chariots. The prophetess Deborah conveys the Lord's command to Barak to gather 10,000 men and meet the enemy at the Kishon River. However, because Barak refuses to go without her, she prophesies that the honor of victory will belong to a woman. The Lord throws Sisera's army into confusion, and his entire force falls by the sword. Sisera flees on foot and seeks refuge with Jael, who welcomes him kindly but then kills him with a tent peg while he sleeps. In this way, God humbles King Jabin before Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Deborah's Palm:** The place where Deborah sat and judged Israel, symbolizing her authority as prophetess and judge.
- **900 Iron Chariots:** Represent the superior military power of the Canaanites, which God overcomes.
- **The Tent Peg:** Jael's weapon against Sisera—a common household tool that becomes God's instrument for deliverance.
- **A Woman's Hand:** The fulfillment of Deborah's prophecy—the honor of victory goes to Jael, not Barak.

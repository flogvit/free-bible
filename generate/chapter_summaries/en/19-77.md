**Main Theme:** A lament in which the psalmist moves from deep despair to renewed trust by remembering God’s mighty acts of salvation.

**Content:** The psalmist cries out to God in distress and finds no comfort, troubled by sleeplessness and heavy questions about whether God’s faithfulness has ceased. The turning point comes when he resolves to remember God’s miraculous deeds from the past. The psalm culminates in a poetic description of God’s power at the Red Sea, where He led His people out of Egypt through the hands of Moses and Aaron.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God’s right hand (v.11):** A symbol of God’s saving power and intervention in history.
- **The waters trembled (vv.17–19):** A poetic theophany describing nature’s response to God’s presence at the Red Sea.
- **The Shepherd (v.21):** God leads His people as a shepherd leads his flock, through Moses and Aaron.

**Main Theme:** Detailed regulations for offerings during the three major festivals in the seventh month.

**Content:** The chapter describes the offerings for the Day of Trumpets (1st day), the Day of Atonement (10th day), and the Feast of Tabernacles (15th–22nd days). During the Feast of Tabernacles, the number of bulls is gradually reduced from 13 to 7 over seven days, while rams (2) and lambs (14) remain constant. The eighth day is a separate solemn assembly with reduced offerings. All these are in addition to the daily offerings and voluntary gifts.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Yom teruah (Day of Trumpets):** The first day of the seventh month, marked by trumpet blasts and a holy convocation.
- **The Day of Atonement:** The tenth day, characterized by self-denial and special sin offerings for atonement.
- **Feast of Tabernacles:** A seven-day festival with extensive offerings, concluding with an aseret (solemn assembly) on the eighth day.
- **Burnt offering (olah):** An offering completely burned, producing a "pleasing aroma" to the Lord.
- **Sin offering (chattat):** A male goat sacrificed daily to make atonement for the people.

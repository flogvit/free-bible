**Main Theme:** The renewal of the covenant and God's demand for wholehearted loyalty and righteousness.

**Content:** Moses recounts how he carved new stone tablets after the first ones were broken, and how God wrote the Ten Commandments on them. The tribe of Levi was set apart to carry the ark of the covenant, serve the Lord, and bless in his name—therefore they have no inherited portion, for the Lord is their inheritance. Moses exhorts the people regarding what God requires: to fear him, walk in his ways, love him, and serve him with all their heart. God is the God of gods and the Lord of lords, who shows no partiality but secures justice for the fatherless, the widow, and the foreigner.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Stone Tablets:** The new tablets bearing the Ten Commandments symbolize the renewal of the covenant following the people's sin.
- **The Ark of the Covenant:** The acacia wood chest that held the tablets, a sign of God's presence among his people.
- **Circumcise the foreskin of your heart:** A metaphor for inner transformation and openness to God, not merely outward religiosity.
- **Love for the foreigner:** God's care for vulnerable groups, which Israel itself is called to imitate.

**Main Theme:** God calls Samuel to be a prophet and reveals His judgment against the house of Eli.

**Content:** In a time when the word of the Lord was rare, the young Samuel serves in the temple under the priest Eli. One night, God calls out to Samuel three times, but the boy thinks it is Eli who is calling. When Eli realizes that it is God speaking, he instructs Samuel to listen. God reveals that judgment will come upon the house of Eli because his sons cursed God and Eli failed to stop them. Samuel shares this prophecy with Eli, who accepts God's will, and Samuel becomes recognized as a prophet throughout Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The word of the Lord was rare"** – Describes the spiritual condition in Israel before Samuel was called.
- **"Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening"** – Samuel’s obedient response, serving as a model for hearing God's voice.
- **God’s lamp (נֵר אֱלֹהִים)** – The lamp in the temple that had not yet gone out, symbolizing God’s presence.
- **The house of Eli under judgment** – God’s irreversible judgment due to the sins of Eli’s sons and Eli’s failure to correct them.

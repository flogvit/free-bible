**Main Theme:** Jacob prepares for his meeting with Esau through strategic planning, humble prayer, and a nighttime struggle that transforms his identity.

**Content:** After parting from Laban, Jacob meets God's angels and names the place Mahanaim. Fearing Esau, he sends gifts and divides his camp into two groups while praying to God for protection. At the Jabbok River, Jacob wrestles all night with a mysterious figure who touches his hip, dislocating it, yet blesses him and gives him the name Israel. Jacob names the place Peniel because he has "seen God face to face."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mahanaim ("two camps"):** The place where Jacob meets God's angels, foreshadowing his own division of the camp.
- **Mincha (gift):** The extensive gifts Jacob sends to appease Esau demonstrate his attempt at reconciliation.
- **Israel ("he who struggles with God"):** Jacob's new name symbolizes his transformation and perseverance in struggle with both God and people.
- **Peniel/Penuel ("face of God"):** The place where Jacob wrestled, named because he saw God face to face and survived.
- **Gid ha-nashe (hip tendon):** Israelites do not eat this tendon as a memorial of Jacob's struggle and injury.

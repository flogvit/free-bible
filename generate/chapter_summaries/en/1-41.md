**Main Theme:** God exalts Joseph from prison to ruler over Egypt through the interpretation of Pharaoh's dreams.

**Content:** Pharaoh dreams of seven fat cows being swallowed by seven lean ones, and seven good heads of grain being swallowed by seven thin ones. When no one can interpret the dreams, the cupbearer remembers Joseph and recommends him. Joseph gives glory to God and explains that the dreams foretell seven years of abundance followed by seven years of famine. He proposes storing grain during the good years, and Pharaoh puts him in charge of all Egypt. Joseph gathers enormous stores of grain, has two sons, and when the famine comes, people from all lands stream to Egypt to buy grain.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Dreams (seven cows and seven heads of grain):** God reveals the future through parallel images showing abundance and lack.
- **"It is not me, but God" (בִּלְעָדָי):** Joseph's humble testimony shows that all wisdom comes from God.
- **God's Spirit (רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים):** Pharaoh recognizes that the Spirit of God is in Joseph.
- **Exaltation:** Joseph receives a ring, linen garments, and a gold chain—symbols of authority and honor.

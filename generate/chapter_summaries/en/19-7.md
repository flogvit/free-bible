**Main Theme:** A prayer for righteous judgment in which David calls upon God as judge and defender against false accusations.

**Content:** David seeks refuge in God and declares his innocence before the charges of his enemies. He asks God to rise up as a righteous judge over the nations and punish the wicked. The psalm expresses confidence that evil will recoil upon those who plan it, and concludes with praise for the Lord's righteousness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lion (v. 3):** The enemy is compared to a lion tearing apart—a picture of brutal persecution.
- **The Judge (vv. 9, 12):** God as the righteous judge who tests hearts and kidneys.
- **The Pit (v. 16):** The wicked dig a pit but fall into it themselves—the motif of retribution.
- **The Shield (v. 11):** God as protector for those who are upright in heart.

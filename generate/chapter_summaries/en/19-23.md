**Main Theme:** Psalm 23 expresses deep trust in God as shepherd and host, who provides care, protection, and hope throughout life.

**Content:** David confesses that the Lord is his shepherd who meets all his needs. God leads him to rest, restores his soul, and guides him on right paths. Even in the valley of the shadow of death, God's presence brings security and comfort. The psalm culminates with the image of God as a gracious host and the certainty of eternal belonging in the Lord's house.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shepherd (רֹעִי):** God as a caring leader who provides for all needs and gives security.
- **Green pastures and still waters:** Symbols of rest, refreshment, and God's abundant care.
- **Valley of the shadow of death (גֵּיא צַלְמָוֶת):** The darkest trials of life where God's presence overcomes fear.
- **Rod and staff:** The shepherd's tools representing God's guidance, protection, and comfort.
- **Cup overflowing:** An expression of God's abundance of blessings and hospitality.

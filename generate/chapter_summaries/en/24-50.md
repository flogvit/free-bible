**Main Theme:** God's judgment against Babylon and the promise of Israel's restoration and forgiveness.

**Content:** Jeremiah proclaims that Babylon, which has oppressed Israel, will fall to a coalition of nations from the north. The Babylonian gods Bel and Merodak will be put to shame. Israel and Judah, who have been like scattered sheep misled by bad shepherds, will return to their land under an everlasting covenant with the Lord. God promises to forgive His people's sin and punish Babylon just as they punished others.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bel and Merodak:** The chief gods of Babylon who will be disgraced when the city falls (v.2).
- **Scattered sheep:** A picture of Israel, misled by bad leaders and devoured by lions (Assyria and Babylon) (vv.6, 17).
- **Hammer:** Babylon is called "the hammer of the whole earth" – a tool for destruction that will now itself be broken (v.23).
- **Sword:** A recurring theme of God's judgment striking every part of Babylonian society (vv.35–38).

**Main Theme:** Jesus demonstrates his divine authority through miracles, healings, and calls to discipleship.

**Content:** By the Sea of Galilee, Jesus calls his first disciples—Simon Peter, James, and John—after a miraculous catch of fish. He heals a man with leprosy and a paralyzed man who was lowered through the roof, simultaneously demonstrating his authority to forgive sins. Jesus calls Levi the tax collector and eats with tax collectors and sinners, provoking the Pharisees. When questioned about fasting, Jesus responds with parables about new wine in new wineskins.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fishers of Men** – Jesus’ call to his disciples to win people for God’s kingdom.
- **Forgiveness of Sins** – Jesus displays divine authority by forgiving the sins of the paralyzed man.
- **Physician for the Sick** – Jesus came not for the righteous, but to call sinners to repentance.
- **New Wine in New Wineskins** – Jesus’ teaching requires new forms and cannot be forced into old religious structures.

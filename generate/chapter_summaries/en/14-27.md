**Main Theme:** King Jotham’s righteous rule over Judah and his military and building successes.

**Content:** Jotham became king at the age of 25 and reigned for 16 years in Jerusalem. He did what was right in the Lord’s eyes, just as his father Uzziah had done, but he did not enter the temple. He built the upper gate of the house of the Lord and fortified works on the Ophel wall, as well as cities and towers throughout Judah. He defeated the Ammonites, who paid him a large tribute for three years. Jotham became powerful because he kept his ways firm before the Lord, yet the people continued to act corruptly.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Upper Gate:** Jotham’s building project at the temple shows his care for God’s house.
- **Ammonite Tribute:** 100 talents of silver and 20,000 cors of grain annually testify to Jotham’s military strength.
- **"Kept his ways firm":** The key to Jotham’s success was his faithfulness to the Lord.
- **The People’s Corruption:** Despite the king’s piety, the people continued in sin.

**Main Theme:** God gives instructions regarding holy objects and mixtures for use in the tabernacle.

**Content:** God commands Moses to make an altar of acacia wood overlaid with gold, where Aaron is to burn incense morning and evening and perform an annual atonement. At a census, every Israelite over 20 years old must pay half a shekel as a ransom. A bronze basin is to be made for the priests' ritual cleansing. God provides recipes for holy anointing oil and incense mixture reserved for the sanctuary—misuse results in being cut off from the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Altar of Incense:** A gold-overlaid altar placed before the veil, where incense is burned as a daily service to the Lord.
- **Half a Shekel:** A ransom payment that all Israelites must give equally, regardless of wealth, for atonement and the maintenance of the sanctuary.
- **The Bronze Basin:** A vessel for washing hands and feet before priestly service, required on pain of death.
- **Anointing Oil:** Holy oil used to consecrate the tabernacle, its furnishings, and the priests.

**Main Theme:** The dedication of the altar through the offerings of the twelve tribal leaders over twelve days.

**Content:** After Moses had set up and anointed the tabernacle, the twelve leaders of Israel brought gifts: six carts and twelve oxen for the service of the Levites. Then each leader presented identical offerings on his designated day: silver bowls, a gold dish with incense, and animals for burnt offerings, sin offerings, and peace offerings. The chapter concludes with Moses hearing God's voice speaking from the mercy seat between the cherubim.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dedication (חֲנֻכָּה):** The altar was dedicated through systematic offerings from all the tribes.
- **Mercy Seat (כַּפֹּרֶת):** The place above the ark of the covenant where God spoke to Moses between the cherubim.
- **Tribal Leaders (נְשִׂיאִים):** The twelve leaders represented the entire people in this solemn dedication.
- **Service of the Levites:** The carts and oxen were distributed to Gershon and Merari according to their duties, while the Kohathites carried the holy objects on their shoulders.

**Main Theme:** A psalm of thanksgiving in which David praises God for healing and deliverance from death.

**Content:** The psalm is linked to the dedication of the house (the temple) and expresses gratitude that God has lifted the psalmist up from Sheol and the grave. David reflects on his former self-satisfaction and how God's hidden face led to despair. The central truth is that God's anger lasts only a moment, while life is found in His favor—weeping may stay for the night, but joy comes in the morning. The psalm culminates in praise: God has turned my mourning into dancing.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sheol and the grave (שְׁאוֹל, בוֹר):** Symbols of the realm of death from which God has rescued the psalmist.
- **Weeping and joy (בֶּכִי, רִנָּה):** The contrast between night's sorrow and morning's gladness shows God's faithful care.
- **Mourning into dancing (מִסְפְּדִי לְמָחוֹל):** The image of total transformation from lamentation to celebration.
- **Sackcloth and joy (שַׂק, שִׂמְחָה):** God removes the garment of mourning and clothes the psalmist in gladness.

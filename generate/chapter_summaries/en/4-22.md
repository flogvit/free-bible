**Main Theme:** God protects His blessed people Israel by controlling the pagan prophet Balaam.

**Content:** King Balak of Moab fears Israel after seeing what they did to the Amorites, and sends two delegations to bring the diviner Balaam to curse the people. At first, God forbids Balaam to go, but later permits it on the condition that he speaks only God's words. On the way, the angel of the Lord stands with a drawn sword; the donkey sees the angel and turns aside three times before God opens its mouth. When his eyes are opened, Balaam acknowledges his sin, and the chapter ends with Balak taking him up to the heights of Baal.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The talking donkey:** A miracle in which God opens the animal's mouth to rebuke the prophet who cannot see spiritually.
- **The angel of the Lord:** God's messenger standing with a drawn sword to stop Balaam on his journey.
- **Blessing versus cursing:** Israel cannot be cursed because they are blessed by God (v. 12).
- **God's sovereignty:** Even a pagan prophet can speak only what God puts in his mouth.

**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts Christians to mutual acceptance and not to judge one another in matters of food and holy days.

**Content:** The chapter addresses how Christians with differing practices regarding food and special days should live together in the church. Paul emphasizes that each person is accountable to God alone, and that Christ died and rose again to be Lord over both the living and the dead. Instead of judging one another, Christians should avoid becoming a stumbling block to others and instead pursue peace and mutual upbuilding. The kingdom of God is not about food and drink, but righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Weak in faith:** One who has scruples concerning food or days, whom the church should welcome without criticism.
- **God's judgment seat (βῆμα):** All will stand before God's judgment seat and give an account for themselves.
- **Stumbling block (πρόσκομμα/σκάνδαλον):** Something that causes a believing brother or sister to fall—this must be avoided.
- **The kingdom of God:** Not food and drink, but righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit.
- **Faith (πίστις):** Whatever does not proceed from faith is sin—the believer's conviction before God.

**Main Theme:** God calls the people to rebuild the temple and warns of the consequences of neglecting his house.

**Content:** In the second year of King Darius's reign, God speaks through the prophet Haggai to Governor Zerubbabel and the high priest Joshua. The people have prioritized their own houses while the Lord's house lies in ruins, and God shows that their economic difficulties are connected to this neglect. After Haggai's exhortation, the leaders and the people heed God's word; the Lord stirs their spirit, and they begin work on the temple on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Pay attention to how you have fared"** (שִׂימוּ לְבַבְכֶם): God's repeated call for self-examination and to see the connection between disobedience and adversity.
- **The bag with a hole** (צְרוֹר נָקוּב): A picture of labor without blessing—wages slip away because God has not been prioritized.
- **"I am with you"** (אֲנִי אִתְּכֶם): God's promise of presence when the people turn and obey.
- **The house of the Lord** (בֵּית יְהוָה): The temple as a symbol of God's presence and the people's relationship with him.

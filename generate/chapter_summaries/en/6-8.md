**Main Theme:** Israel's victory over Ai through God's strategy, followed by a covenant reading at Mount Ebal.

**Content:** After the first defeat at Ai, God gives Joshua a new strategy involving an ambush. The Israelites lure out Ai's warriors with a feigned retreat, while the ambush force enters and burns the city. All 12,000 people are killed, and the king is hanged on a tree until evening before his body is thrown at the city gate under a pile of stones—but the spoil and livestock are allowed to the people, unlike at Jericho. Joshua then builds an altar on Mount Ebal and reads the entire law with blessings and curses for the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ambush (אֹרֵב):** God's strategy for victory involved military cunning, in contrast to the frontal assault at Jericho.
- **Joshua's outstretched javelin (כִּידוֹן):** Joshua held his javelin outstretched toward Ai as a signal and kept it raised until the victory was complete—a sign of God's guidance.
- **Mount Ebal:** The place where Joshua built an altar and read the law, in obedience to Moses' command (Deut 27).
- **Blessing and curse:** The people heard the words of the law between Mount Gerizim and Mount Ebal, marking their commitment to the covenant.

**Main Theme:** Absalom's death in the war against his father David and the king's deep grief over the loss of his son.

**Content:** David organizes his forces into three divisions under Joab, Abishai, and Ittai, but the people refuse to let the king himself take part in the battle. David commands the commanders to spare Absalom. In the Forest of Ephraim, Absalom's forces suffer a great defeat, and Absalom himself becomes caught by his hair in an oak tree. Despite the king's order, Joab kills Absalom with three spears through his heart, and his ten armor-bearers finish him off. Two messengers bring the news to David, who breaks down in grief over his son.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Forest of Ephraim:** The battlefield where 20,000 fell, and where the forest took more lives than the sword.
- **The Oak (הָאֵלָה):** The tree where Absalom became caught in its branches, a picture of his downfall.
- **"Spare the young man":** David's plea for mercy toward Absalom which Joab ignored.
- **The Two Messengers:** Ahimaaz and the Cushite who brought news of the victory and Absalom's death.

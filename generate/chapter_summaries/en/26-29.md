**Main Theme:** God's judgment on Egypt for their pride and unreliability as a support for Israel.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to prophesy against Pharaoh and Egypt. Pharaoh is compared to a large crocodile in the Nile who arrogantly claims to have created his own power. God will punish Egypt with destruction and 40 years of desolation through Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon, as recompense for his siege of Tyre. Egypt will be restored but remain a weak nation. The chapter concludes with a promise that God will cause a horn to sprout for Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Crocodile in the Nile:** Pharaoh is portrayed as a great sea creature who arrogantly declares, "The Nile is mine; I have made it" – an image of Egypt's arrogance.
- **The Reed Support:** Egypt is described as a reed staff that breaks and injures anyone who leans on it – an image of Egypt's unreliability as an ally.
- **40 Years of Desolation:** The period during which Egypt will lie desolate, from Migdol to Syene, before being restored as a weak nation.
- **The Sprouting Horn:** A promise of renewed strength and hope for the house of Israel on the day Egypt is judged.

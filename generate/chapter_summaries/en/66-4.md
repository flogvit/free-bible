**Main Theme:** John receives a vision of God's heavenly throne surrounded by worship.

**Content:** John is called up to heaven through an open door and sees God's throne, shining like precious stones and encircled by an emerald-green rainbow. Around the throne sit 24 elders in white garments with crowns of gold, and before the throne lies a sea of crystal and seven burning torches that are God's seven spirits. Four living creatures full of eyes praise God day and night as "holy, holy, holy," and the elders fall down, cast their crowns, and proclaim God worthy as the Creator of all things.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Throne:** God's majestic seat in heaven, the center of all worship and power.
- **The 24 Elders:** Likely represent the redeemed or angelic beings who continually worship God.
- **The Four Living Creatures:** Heavenly creatures (lion, ox, man, eagle) that proclaim God's holiness without ceasing.
- **Seven Fiery Torches:** Symbolize God's seven spirits—the fullness of the Holy Spirit.
- **"Worthy are you":** The basis of worship: God created all things according to His will.

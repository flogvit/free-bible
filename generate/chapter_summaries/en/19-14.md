**Main Theme:** A wisdom psalm that contrasts the fool's godlessness and humanity's universal sin with the hope of God's salvation from Zion.

**Content:** The psalm begins with the fool's claim that there is no God, followed by a description of humanity's moral decay—all have turned away; none do good. God looks down from heaven and finds no one seeking Him. Evildoers oppress God's people but will be struck with fear because God is with the righteous. The psalm concludes with a prayer for salvation to come from Zion.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Fool (נָבָל):** One who denies God in his heart and lives accordingly—not intellectual doubt, but moral rebellion.
- **All have turned away:** Universal sin—none do good, not even one.
- **They devour my people as bread:** A picture of ruthless oppression of the vulnerable.
- **The Lord is their refuge:** God protects the poor from their oppressors.
- **Salvation from Zion:** Eschatological hope for God's intervention that will bring joy to Israel.

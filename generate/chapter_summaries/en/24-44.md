**Main Theme:** Jeremiah warns the Jews in Egypt that their continued idolatry will lead to God's judgment and destruction.

**Content:** God reminds the Jews in Egypt that the destruction of Jerusalem was caused by idolatry, and warns them against repeating the same sin. The people refuse to listen and defend their worship of "the queen of heaven," claiming they were better off when they offered sacrifices to her. God responds by declaring that nearly all the Jews in Egypt will perish by sword and famine, with only a few returning. As a sign of the certainty of judgment, Jeremiah prophesies that Pharaoh Hophra will be handed over to his enemies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Queen of Heaven (מְלֶכֶת הַשָּׁמַיִם):** A pagan deity, likely Ishtar/Astarte, whom the people insisted on worshipping with incense and drink offerings.
- **Incense to foreign gods:** A symbol of idolatry and spiritual unfaithfulness that provoked God's wrath.
- **Remnant (שְׁאֵרִית):** Only a small remnant of the Jews in Egypt will survive as witnesses that God's word is fulfilled.
- **Pharaoh Hophra:** The king of Egypt, whose downfall would serve as a confirming sign of God's judgment on the Jews in Egypt.

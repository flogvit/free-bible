**Main Theme:** God sees and hears the outcasts – Hagar’s flight and God’s promise concerning Ishmael.

**Content:** Sarai, who is childless, gives her Egyptian slave woman Hagar to Abram so that she may have a child through her. When Hagar becomes pregnant, conflict arises between the women, and Hagar flees into the wilderness. There, the angel of the Lord meets her at a spring, commands her to return, and promises that her son Ishmael will become the ancestor of a numerous people. Hagar gives God the name "El Roi" (God who sees me), and she bears Ishmael when Abram is 86 years old.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hagar** – Sarai’s Egyptian slave woman, who becomes the mother of Abram’s first son and the first person in the Bible to give God a name.
- **Ishmael** – Means "God hears"; fulfillment of the promise that God heard Hagar’s distress.
- **El Roi** – "The God who sees me"; Hagar’s confession that God sees the outcasts and suffering.
- **Be’er Lahai Roi** – "The Well of the Living One who sees me"; the place where God met Hagar in the wilderness.

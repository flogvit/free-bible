**Main Theme:** Solomon's wisdom and wealth reach their peak, confirmed through the visit of the Queen of Sheba and his immense prosperity.

**Content:** The Queen of Sheba comes to Jerusalem to test Solomon's wisdom with riddles and is overwhelmed by his knowledge, magnificent court, and temple. She praises the God of Israel who placed Solomon on the throne to exercise justice and righteousness. The chapter describes Solomon's enormous wealth: 666 talents of gold annually, a magnificent ivory throne with lions, golden shields, and ships from Tarshish that brought gold, silver, ivory, and exotic animals. Solomon surpassed all the kings of the earth in wealth and wisdom, and people from all over the world sought his presence.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Queen of Sheba:** Represents the world's recognition of Solomon's God-given wisdom and Israel's greatness.
- **Ivory Throne:** A magnificent throne adorned with gold and twelve lions, a symbol of royal power and unmatched splendor.
- **666 Talents of Gold:** The annual income in gold illustrating Solomon's extraordinary material wealth.
- **Ships of Tarshish:** The merchant fleet that brought exotic goods every three years, symbolizing international trade and influence.

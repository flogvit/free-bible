**Main Theme:** Elijah's ascension and the transfer of the prophetic office to Elisha.

**Content:** Elijah and Elisha travel from Gilgal through Bethel and Jericho to the Jordan, where Elijah parts the water with his cloak. Elisha asks for a double portion of Elijah's spirit, and this is granted when he sees Elijah taken up to heaven in a whirlwind accompanied by a chariot of fire and horses of fire. Elisha picks up Elijah's cloak, parts the Jordan, and is recognized by the sons of the prophets as Elijah's successor. He performs his first miracles: healing the water source at Jericho and cursing a group of mocking youths.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Elijah's cloak (אַדֶּרֶת):** Symbolizes the prophetic office and God's power transferred from Elijah to Elisha.
- **Chariot of fire and horses of fire:** The heavenly host of God that separates the two prophets and takes Elijah away.
- **Double portion of the spirit (פִּי־שְׁנַיִם):** Elisha's prayer for the birthright—to be Elijah's spiritual heir.
- **Parting of the Jordan:** Confirms God's power through the prophets, paralleling Moses and Joshua.

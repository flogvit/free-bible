**Main Theme:** David transfers responsibility for building the temple to Solomon and gives him the divinely revealed plans.

**Content:** David gathers Israel's leaders and explains that God did not permit him to build the temple because he was a man of war, but chose Solomon for the task. He gives Solomon detailed plans for the temple which he received from the Lord's hand, along with specifications for all furnishings and service. David exhorts the people to obedience and encourages Solomon: "Be strong and courageous," assuring him that God will be with him until the work is completed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Resting place for the ark** – The temple was to be a permanent resting place for the ark of the covenant, God's footstool on earth.
- **Conditional promise** – Solomon's kingdom would endure forever if he kept God's commands (v. 7).
- **Plans from God's hand** – David emphasizes that all the temple plans came by divine revelation, not human wisdom.
- **"Be strong and courageous"** – David's encouragement to Solomon, with the promise of God's presence throughout the entire project.

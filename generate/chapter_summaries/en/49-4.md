**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts believers to live in unity and holiness as one body in Christ.

**Content:** Paul, a prisoner for the Lord, urges the church to walk worthy of their calling with humility, gentleness, and patience, preserving the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace. He describes how Christ has given ministry gifts—apostles, prophets, evangelists, shepherds, and teachers—to equip the saints for works of service, building up the body of Christ until we all reach maturity. Believers are urged to put off the old self and clothe themselves with the new self, created after God in righteousness and holiness. The chapter concludes with practical exhortations regarding truthful speech, righteous anger, honest work, and edifying words—and not to grieve the Holy Spirit of God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Unity (ἑνότης):** The unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace, expressed through "one body, one Spirit, one Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God."
- **The Body of Christ (σῶμα τοῦ Χριστοῦ):** The church as a living organism where each part contributes to growth and building up in love.
- **The Old and New Self:** Putting off the corrupt life and putting on the new self, created in God's image.
- **Ministry Gifts (δόματα):** Christ gives various ministries to the church to equip the saints for maturity and unity in faith.

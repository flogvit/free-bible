**Main Theme:** God challenges Job’s criticism and demonstrates His sovereign power through the wonders of creation.

**Content:** God answers Job from the storm and asks whether Job will set aside His judgment in order to appear righteous himself. Job responds humbly, placing his hand over his mouth—he acknowledges that he has spoken once, yes, twice, but will not continue. God then challenges Job: Can he clothe himself with majesty and humble the proud? As proof of His power, God describes Behemoth and Leviathan—mighty creatures that no human can tame or control.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Behemoth** (בְּהֵמוֹת): A huge land animal with strength in its loins and sinews like bronze tubes, described as "the first of God’s works" (v. 19).
- **Leviathan** (לִוְיָתָן): A fearsome sea creature that cannot be caught with a hook or tamed like a domestic animal.
- **Hand over the mouth** (יָדִי שַׂמְתִּי לְמוֹ־פִי): Job’s gesture of humility and silence, showing his recognition of his own limitations.
- **The Storm** (סְעָרָה): The setting for the theophany—God reveals Himself in nature’s overwhelming power.

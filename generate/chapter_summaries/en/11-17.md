**Main Theme:** God provides for His prophet Elijah and demonstrates His power over life and death through miracles.

**Content:** Elijah announces to King Ahab that there will be no dew or rain except by his word. God leads Elijah first to the brook Cherith, where ravens bring him food, and then to a widow in Zarephath. Despite the widow's poverty, the flour and oil miraculously last throughout the entire drought. When the widow's son dies, Elijah prays to the Lord, and the boy is brought back to life, confirming to the widow that Elijah is a man of God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Ravens at Cherith:** God uses creation to supernaturally provide for His prophet.
- **The Flour and Oil:** Symbols of God's enduring care and faithfulness toward those who trust in Him.
- **The Raising of the Boy:** The first resurrection miracle in the Bible, pointing to God's power over death.
- **The Widow at Zarephath:** A Gentile woman who witnesses the power of the God of Israel.

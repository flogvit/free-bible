**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts faithful worship (Hannah and Samuel) with priestly corruption (Eli's sons), and announces God's judgment upon Eli's house.

**Content:** Hannah praises God for His sovereign power to humble the proud and exalt the lowly. While the young Samuel serves faithfully in the sanctuary, Eli's sons Hophni and Phinehas dishonor the Lord's offering through greed and immorality. A man of God comes to Eli with words of judgment: his lineage will lose the priesthood, and both sons will die on the same day. God promises to raise up a faithful priest instead.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Horn (קֶרֶן):** A symbol of strength and honor; Hannah proclaims that her horn is exalted in the Lord (v. 1, 10).
- **Sons of Belial (בְּנֵי בְלִיָּעַל):** A designation for Eli's sons who did not know the Lord and acted wickedly (v. 12).
- **Ephod (אֵפוֹד):** A priestly garment worn by the young Samuel, symbolizing his service to the Lord (v. 18).
- **Faithful Priest (כֹּהֵן נֶאֱמָן):** God's promise to raise up a priest who will do His will, in contrast to Eli's house (v. 35).

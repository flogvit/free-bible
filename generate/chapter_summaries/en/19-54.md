**Main Theme:** A prayer for deliverance from enemies, with trust in God's help and a promise of a thank offering.

**Content:** David asks God to save him by His name and defend him with His power, in a situation where the Ziphites have betrayed him to Saul. He describes his enemies as foreigners and violent men who do not regard God. In the midst of distress, he expresses confidence that God is his helper, and promises to bring voluntary thank offerings when God turns the evil back against his persecutors.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's name (בְּשִׁמְךָ):** God's name represents His character and power to save.
- **Foreigners/Violent men (זָרִים/עָרִיצִים):** Describes the enemies as godless and brutal people.
- **Voluntary offering (בִּנְדָבָה):** A spontaneous thank offering expressing genuine gratitude.
- **God's faithfulness (אֲמִתְּךָ):** God's truth and reliability as the basis for judgment against the enemies.

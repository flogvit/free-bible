**Main Theme:** The kingdom splits in two due to Rehoboam's harshness and Jeroboam's rebellion, leading to idolatry in the north.

**Content:** After Solomon's death, the people ask Rehoboam for relief, but he follows the advice of the young men and threatens even harsher conditions. Ten tribes break away and make Jeroboam king over Israel, while Rehoboam retains Judah and Benjamin. The prophet Shemaiah prevents civil war by delivering God's word. Jeroboam establishes idolatry with golden calves in Bethel and Dan to prevent the people from going to Jerusalem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Whips and scorpions (v.11):** Rehoboam's threat of harsher rule than his father's, which triggers the split.
- **The golden calves (vv.28–29):** Jeroboam's idols in Bethel and Dan that lead Israel away from the Lord.
- **"What portion do we have in David?" (v.16):** The northern tribes' cry of rebellion marking the break with David's dynasty.
- **The word of the Lord through Ahijah (v.15):** The division was foretold and occurred according to God's plan.

**Main Theme:** Paul urges the Galatians to stand firm in the freedom Christ has given and to walk by the Spirit, not by the flesh.

**Content:** Paul warns against allowing themselves to be circumcised, because that means committing oneself to the entire law and falling away from grace. In Christ, neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts for anything, but faith working through love. This freedom must not be misused as an opportunity for the desires of the flesh, but rather used to serve one another in love. Paul describes the contrast between the works of the flesh and the fruit of the Spirit, and exhorts believers to walk by the Spirit.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Freedom (ἐλευθερία):** Christ has set believers free from slavery to the law; this freedom must be preserved but not misused.
- **Fruit of the Spirit (καρπὸς τοῦ πνεύματος):** The nine qualities that grow in those led by the Spirit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.
- **Flesh versus Spirit (σὰρξ/πνεῦμα):** The fundamental conflict in the Christian life between the sinful nature and God's Spirit.
- **Leaven (ζύμη):** A picture of how a little false teaching can corrupt the whole congregation.

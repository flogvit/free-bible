**Main Theme:** After the city wall is completed, Nehemiah organizes the city's security and registers the people who have returned from exile.

**Content:** When the wall was finished, Nehemiah appointed gatekeepers, singers, and Levites, and gave Hanani and Hananiah responsibility for Jerusalem because of their faithfulness and fear of God. God put it on Nehemiah’s heart to register the people by family lines, and he found the book containing the genealogy of those who first returned from Babylon. The list includes Israelites, priests, Levites, temple servants, and descendants of Solomon’s servants—totaling 42,360 persons. Some priests who could not prove their ancestry were excluded from priestly service until a decision could be made using Urim and Tummim.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Genealogy:** Documentation of lineage was essential to preserve Israel's identity and rights after the exile.
- **Urim and Tummim:** Sacred objects used by the high priest to seek God’s will in difficult decisions.
- **Gatekeepers:** Levites responsible for guarding the city gates and regulating access, vital for Jerusalem’s security.
- **Temple Servants (the Nethinim):** Non-Israelites who served in the temple with practical tasks.

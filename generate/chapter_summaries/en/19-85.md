**Main Theme:** A Korah psalm that is a prayer for national restoration and a vision of God's salvation where grace and truth meet.

**Content:** The psalm begins with thanksgiving for God's past forgiveness and restoration of Jacob (vv. 2-4). This is followed by a heartfelt plea for God to turn again toward His people and show His mercy (vv. 5-8). The psalmist listens for God's answer and receives a promise of peace for the faithful (vv. 9-10). The psalm culminates in a poetic vision where righteousness and peace kiss each other, and the land yields its harvest (vv. 11-14).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Grace and truth meet (חֶסֶד־וֶאֱמֶת נִפְגָּשׁוּ):** The central image where God's attributes unite in perfect harmony.
- **Righteousness and peace kiss each other:** A poetic description of the messianic future where opposites are reconciled.
- **Truth springs from the earth:** Expresses the hope that faithfulness will grow among people while righteousness looks down from heaven.
- **God's anger and forgiveness:** The contrast between God's past grace and the current prayer for renewed forgiveness.

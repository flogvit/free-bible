**Main Theme:** Mordecai and Esther prepare to confront the threat against the Jewish people through fasting and courageous action.

**Content:** When Mordecai learns of the decree to destroy the Jews, he publicly mourns in sackcloth and ashes. He sends a message to Queen Esther, urging her to go to the king to plead for her people. Esther hesitates because approaching the king uninvited risks death, but Mordecai reminds her that she may have become queen precisely "for such a time as this." Esther asks the Jews in Susa to fast for three days and declares: "If I perish, I perish."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sackcloth and ashes (שַׂק וָאֵפֶר):** Traditional expressions of deep mourning and humility used by Mordecai and the Jews.
- **The golden scepter:** A symbol of the king's favor—only those to whom the king extends the scepter are allowed to live.
- **"For such a time as this":** Mordecai’s words suggesting that Esther may have been placed in her position by God for this very moment.
- **Three days of fasting:** The community’s preparation before Esther’s dangerous act, expressing dependence on God’s help.

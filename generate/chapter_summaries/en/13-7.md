**Main Theme:** Genealogies of six of Israel's tribes, emphasizing military strength and territories.

**Content:** The chapter lists the descendants of Issachar, Benjamin, Naphtali, Manasseh, Ephraim, and Asher, including numbers of fighting men in each tribe. Ephraim's lineage includes a tragic account of sons killed by men from Gath, Ephraim's grief, and the birth of his son Beriah. The genealogy leads forward to Joshua, son of Nun. The settlement areas of the tribes are mentioned, and several women are highlighted, including Sheerah, who built cities.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fighting men (גִּבּוֹרֵי חַיִל):** A repeated expression that underscores the military capacity of the tribes and God's blessing through numerous descendants.
- **Genealogies (הִתְיַחְשָׂם):** Documentation of family lines that confirm the identity and inheritance rights of the tribes in Israel.
- **Joshua (יְהוֹשֻׁעַ):** Ephraim's famous descendant who led Israel into the Promised Land.
- **Beriah (בְּרִיעָה):** A name meaning "in trouble," given because Ephraim's household was struck by tragedy.

**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts wisdom and folly, emphasizing integrity, reverence for God, and humility before the Lord's will.

**Content:** Proverbs 19 teaches that integrity is more valuable than wealth and strongly warns against false witnesses, who will not go unpunished. The chapter describes how wealth attracts friends while the poor are abandoned, and encourages showing mercy to the needy—for this is like lending to the Lord. Furthermore, it stresses the importance of accepting discipline and counsel, raising children with discipline, and that ultimately only the Lord's purpose stands firm.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Integrity (תֻּמָּה):** Walking uprightly is better than deceit, regardless of social status.
- **False witness (עֵד שְׁקָרִים):** Repeated twice (vv. 5, 9)—the liar will not go unpunished but will perish.
- **The Lord's purpose (עֲצַת יְהוָה):** People make many plans, but the Lord's will is what stands firm.
- **Reverence for God (יִרְאַת יְהוָה):** Leads to life and security, protected from disaster.

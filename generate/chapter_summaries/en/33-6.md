**Main Theme:** God brings a legal case against Israel for their unfaithfulness and calls them to genuine obedience rather than empty sacrifices.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a cosmic courtroom scene where the mountains are summoned as witnesses while God accuses His people. God reminds Israel of His faithful deliverance from Egypt and asks what He has done to deserve their infidelity. In response to the question of what God requires, comes the famous answer: to do justice, to love steadfast love (חֶסֶד), and to walk humbly with your God. Nevertheless, the people practice deceit and violence, following in the sinful ways of Omri and Ahab, which will lead to destruction and disgrace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Legal Case (רִיב):** God acts as prosecutor in a cosmic courtroom with the mountains serving as witnesses against Israel.
- **The Exodus from Egypt:** God's act of salvation is used as evidence of His faithfulness and as the basis for the accusation.
- **Micah 6:8:** The classic expression of true worship: justice, steadfast love (חֶסֶד), and walking humbly with God.
- **False Weights:** A symbol of the economic oppression and deceit that permeates society.

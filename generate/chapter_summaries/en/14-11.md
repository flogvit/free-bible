**Main Theme:** Rehoboam strengthens the kingdom of Judah after the division of the realm, and God confirms that the division was His will.

**Content:** When Rehoboam gathered 180,000 soldiers to reconquer Israel, God forbade him through the prophet Shemaiah from going to war, because the division was from the Lord. Rehoboam obeyed and instead built up fifteen fortified cities in Judah and Benjamin. The Levites and priests left the northern kingdom because Jeroboam rejected them and appointed his own priests for idol worship, and many godly Israelites followed them to Judah. This strengthened the kingdom for three years while the people walked in the ways of David and Solomon.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fortified Cities:** Rehoboam built fifteen fortified cities with supplies and weapons to secure the borders of the kingdom.
- **The Prophet Shemaiah:** God's messenger who prevented civil war by revealing that the division of the realm was God's will.
- **The Migration of the Levites:** The priests left their possessions in the north because Jeroboam replaced them with his own priests for the calves and goats.
- **Three Years of Faithfulness:** The period when Judah followed the Lord's ways, which gave the kingdom strength and stability.

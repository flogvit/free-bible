**Main Theme:** Jeremiah laments the prosperity of the wicked, and God responds with a challenge and His own grief over having to abandon His people.

**Content:** Jeremiah asks God why the wicked prosper and pleads for judgment against them. God answers by challenging Jeremiah to prepare himself for even greater trials. Then God expresses His deep sorrow over having abandoned His house and handed over His beloved inheritance to enemies. The chapter concludes with a promise of restoration for both Israel and neighboring nations if they turn back.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The wilderness of the Jordan** (גְאוֹן הַיַּרְדֵּן): A symbol of the coming, greater trials Jeremiah must prepare for.
- **God's inheritance/vineyard** (נַחֲלָה/כֶּרֶם): Israel as God's precious possession, now destroyed and forsaken.
- **The lion in the forest** (אַרְיֵה בַיָּעַר): A picture of how the people have turned against God with hostility.
- **To sow wheat and harvest thorns** (זָרְעוּ חִטִּים וְקֹצִים קָצָרוּ): An expression of futile effort under God's wrath.

**Main Theme:** A declaration of trust in the Lord as a righteous judge amid threats from the wicked.

**Content:** David rejects advice to flee like a bird to the mountains and instead declares his refuge in the Lord. Although the wicked bend their bow to shoot the upright in darkness, the Lord reigns in his holy temple and tests both the righteous and the wicked. Those who love violence hate his soul, and over them he lets fire, sulfur, and scorching wind rain down as their cup. The Lord is righteous and loves righteousness—the upright shall behold his face.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Refuge (חָסִיתִי):** Seeking protection in the Lord rather than fleeing from danger.
- **God's throne in heaven:** The Lord sees and tests all people from his exalted position.
- **Bow and arrows in darkness:** A picture of the hidden attacks of the wicked against the upright.
- **Fire and sulfur:** God's judgment on the wicked, a cup filled with destruction.
- **Behold God's face:** The privilege of the righteous—close fellowship with God.

**Main Theme:** James exhorts believers to humility before God and warns against worldly desires and self-reliance.

**Content:** Conflicts and quarrels arise from the passions that wage war within a person. Friendship with the world is enmity toward God, but God gives grace to the humble. James calls for repentance: draw near to God, cleanse your hands, purify your hearts, and humble yourselves. He warns against slander and judging others, as well as boasting about future plans without considering God's will. Whoever knows how to do good but does not do it sins.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Friendship with the world (φιλία τοῦ κόσμου):** Loving the world and its values makes a person an enemy of God.
- **Humility (ταπεινόω):** God opposes the proud but exalts those who humble themselves before Him.
- **Life as vapor (ἀτμίς):** Human life is fleeting like mist that vanishes—therefore we must live dependent on God's will.
- **Double-mindedness (δίψυχοι):** Those who waver between God and the world must cleanse and sanctify their hearts.

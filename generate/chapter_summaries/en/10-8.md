**Main Theme:** David expands the kingdom through military victories and establishes a righteous rule with the Lord's help.

**Content:** David defeats the Philistines, Moabites, Hadadezer of Zobah, the Arameans from Damascus, and the Edomites, making them tributary vassals. He dedicates the plunder from all these nations—gold, silver, and bronze—to the Lord. The text emphasizes that "the Lord gave David victory wherever he went." The chapter concludes with a list of David's officials and states that he ruled with justice for all the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Vassals (נֹשְׂאֵי מִנְחָה):** The defeated peoples became tributary subjects who paid tribute to David.
- **Dedicated Plunder:** David dedicated the war spoils to the Lord, demonstrating his reverence for God and acknowledgment of Him as the giver of victory.
- **Justice and Righteousness (מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה):** David ruled all the people with justice, a hallmark of an ideal king.
- **The Lord Gave Victory:** A repeated statement (vv. 6, 14) emphasizing that David's military success was God's work.

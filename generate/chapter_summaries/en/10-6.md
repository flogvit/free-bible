**Main Theme:** David brings the ark of the covenant to Jerusalem after a failed first attempt.

**Content:** David gathers 30,000 men to bring God's ark from Baale-Judah. The first attempt ends tragically when Uzzah dies after touching the ark, and David leaves the ark at Obed-Edom's house for three months. When God blesses Obed-Edom's household, David brings the ark to Jerusalem with dancing, sacrifices, and celebration. David's wife Michal despises his devoted dancing and remains childless for the rest of her life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant:** God's holy throne, symbolizing His presence among the people.
- **Uzzah's Death:** A warning about God's holiness and the importance of approaching Him in the right way.
- **David's Dance:** An expression of wholehearted worship and joy over God's presence.
- **Michal's Contempt:** The contrast between genuine worship and pride, with serious consequences.

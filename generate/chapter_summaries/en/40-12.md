**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals his authority as Lord of the Sabbath and warns against rejecting the work of the Holy Spirit.

**Content:** The chapter shows the conflict between Jesus and the Pharisees through Sabbath disputes, where Jesus heals a man with a withered hand and declares that something greater than the temple is here. When the Pharisees accuse him of casting out demons by Beelzebul, Jesus warns against sinning against the Holy Spirit. He concludes by redefining his true family as those who do God's will, pointing to the sign of Jonah as a prophecy of his death and resurrection.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Son of Man's lordship over the Sabbath:** Jesus declares his divine authority over the Jewish holy day and its regulations.
- **Sin against the Holy Spirit:** The unforgivable sin of attributing God's work to the devil, a persistent rejection of the truth.
- **The sign of Jonah:** A prophetic picture of Jesus' death and resurrection—three days in the heart of the earth.
- **The bruised reed and the smoking flax:** From Isaiah—a picture of the Messiah's gentle care for the weak and wounded.

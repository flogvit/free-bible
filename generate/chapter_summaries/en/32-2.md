**Main Theme:** Jonah's prayer from inside the fish, where he praises God for deliverance from death.

**Content:** God sends a great fish to swallow Jonah, and he is in the belly of the fish for three days and three nights. From the depths, Jonah cries out to the Lord in his distress, describing how he sank down into the deep sea with waves and breakers surging over him. Even when he felt cut off from God's presence, he turned in prayer toward the holy temple. Jonah praises God who brought his life up from the grave and vows to fulfill his promises—for salvation belongs to the Lord. God commands the fish to vomit Jonah onto dry land.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Belly of the fish (מְעֵי הַדָּג):** Symbolizes the realm of death and total helplessness, yet also the place where God hears prayer.
- **Sheol/the realm of the dead (שְׁאוֹל):** Jonah describes his situation as crying out from the depths of Sheol, underscoring the severity of his distress.
- **The holy temple (הֵיכַל קָדְשֶׁךָ):** The direction of Jonah's prayer and hope—God's dwelling place and presence.
- **Salvation belongs to the Lord (יְשׁוּעָתָה לַיהוָה):** The climax of the chapter and its theological core—only God can save.

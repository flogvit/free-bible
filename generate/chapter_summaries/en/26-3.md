**Main Theme:** Ezekiel is appointed as a watchman for Israel with the responsibility to warn the people.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to eat the scroll, which tastes as sweet as honey, and then speak to the people of Israel. Although Israel is hard-hearted and rebellious, God makes Ezekiel’s forehead as hard as diamond. The Spirit brings Ezekiel to the exiles at Tel Abib, where he sits stunned for seven days. God appoints him as a watchman with personal responsibility to warn both the wicked and the righteous. Finally, Ezekiel sees the glory of the Lord in the valley and receives command to isolate himself and remain silent until God opens his mouth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Scroll:** God’s word that Ezekiel must take in and make his own before he can preach.
- **Watchman (tsofeh):** Ezekiel’s role with responsibility to warn of danger and judgment.
- **Hard Forehead:** God equips Ezekiel to withstand the people’s resistance.
- **The Glory of the Lord:** God’s majestic presence that confirms Ezekiel’s calling.
- **Silence:** A sign that Ezekiel is to speak only when God gives him words.

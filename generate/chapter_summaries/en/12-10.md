**Main Theme:** Jehu carries out God’s judgment against the house of Ahab and the Baal cult in Israel, but fails to remain wholeheartedly devoted to the Lord.

**Content:** In Samaria, Jehu exterminates Ahab’s seventy sons and kills forty-two relatives of Ahaziah, king of Judah. By deception, he gathers all the Baal worshipers for a sacrifice and puts them to death, destroys the temple of Baal, and turns it into a latrine. The Lord promises that Jehu’s descendants will hold the throne of Israel for four generations, but because Jehu did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam, Hazael begins to cut down Israel’s territory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Seventy Heads:** The heads of Ahab’s sons in baskets symbolize the complete judgment against his house.
- **Temple of Baal Destroyed:** Jehu’s eradication of the Baal cult demonstrates God’s judgment on idolatry.
- **Golden Calves at Bethel and Dan:** The sins of Jeroboam that Jehu did not abandon reveal his incomplete obedience.
- **Jonadab son of Rechab:** A witness to Jehu’s zeal, linked to the Rechabites.

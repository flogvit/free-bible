**Main Theme:** A prayer for God's judgment against unjust leaders who pervert justice.

**Content:** David accuses the powerful of speaking falsehood and practicing violence instead of rendering righteous judgment (vv. 2–3). The wicked are described as going astray from birth, compared to a deaf serpent that cannot be tamed (vv. 4–6). The psalmist asks God to break their power and make them vanish like flowing water, a melting snail, or a stillborn child (vv. 7–10). The psalm concludes with the assurance that the righteous will see God's vengeance and that there is a God who judges on earth (vv. 11–12).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Deaf serpent (vv. 5–6):** The stubbornness of the wicked—they refuse to hear the truth, like a cobra closing its ear to the snake charmer.
- **Lion's teeth (v. 7):** A symbol of the destructive power of the evil ones that God will shatter.
- **Melting snail (v. 9):** An image of how the wicked will waste away and come to nothing.
- **God as Judge (v. 12):** The climax of the psalm—the affirmation that there is a righteous God who judges on earth.

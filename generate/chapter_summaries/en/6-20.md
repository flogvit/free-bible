**Main Theme:** The Lord commands the establishment of six cities of refuge as protection for those who kill unintentionally.

**Content:** God commands Joshua to appoint cities of refuge, just as He had promised through Moses. A person who kills unintentionally may flee there and receive protection from the avenger of blood. The elders are to accept the fugitive, and he must remain in the city until the death of the high priest. Three cities were appointed west of the Jordan (Kedesh, Shechem, and Hebron) and three east of the Jordan (Bezer, Ramoth, and Golan), available to both Israelites and foreigners.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **City of Refuge (עָרֵי הַמִּקְלָט):** Cities that provided legal protection for someone who killed without intent.
- **Avenger of Blood (גֹּאֵל הַדָּם):** A relative with the right and duty to avenge the death of a family member.
- **Unintentional Killing (בִּשְׁגָגָה):** Killing without malicious intent or prior enmity, as opposed to premeditated murder.
- **Death of the High Priest:** Marked the time when the fugitive could safely return home.

**Main Theme:** Paul thanks God for the Thessalonians' faith and endurance, pointing to Christ's righteous judgment at his return.

**Content:** Paul, Silas, and Timothy thank God for the church's growing faith and love, as well as their perseverance amid persecution. They comfort the believers with the assurance that God will justly repay: trouble to those who persecute them, and rest to the faithful when Jesus is revealed from heaven. The authors pray that God would count them worthy of his calling, so that the name of Jesus may be glorified in them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ὑπεραυξάνει ἡ πίστις (the faith grows exceedingly):** Describes the church's exceptional spiritual growth amid trials.
- **Ἀποκάλυψις τοῦ κυρίου (the revelation of the Lord):** The visible return of Jesus from heaven with his mighty angels in flaming fire.
- **Ὄλεθρον αἰώνιον (eternal destruction):** The consequence of judgment for those who do not know God—separated from the presence of the Lord.
- **Ἐνδοξασθῆναι (to be glorified):** The goal: that Jesus may be honored in the saints and in all who believe.

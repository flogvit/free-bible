**Main Theme:** Jesus' divine identity is confirmed through the Transfiguration, while he prepares his disciples for his coming suffering.

**Content:** Jesus takes Peter, James, and John up a high mountain, where he is transfigured before them with a radiant face and white garments, and Moses and Elijah appear. God's voice from the cloud confirms: "This is my Son, the beloved – listen to him!" After descending, Jesus heals a demon-possessed boy and rebukes the disciples for their lack of faith. He predicts his death and resurrection, and pays the temple tax with a coin found in a fish's mouth so as not to cause offense.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Transfiguration (μετεμορφώθη):** Jesus reveals his divine glory to the three disciples on the mountain.
- **"Listen to him":** God's command emphasizes Jesus' authority over Moses and Elijah (the Law and the Prophets).
- **Faith like a mustard seed:** Even the smallest faith can accomplish the impossible when directed toward God.
- **The Son of Man's suffering:** Jesus prepares his disciples that he will be handed over, killed, and raised on the third day.

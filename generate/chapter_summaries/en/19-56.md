**Main Theme:** A psalm of trust in which David, surrounded by enemies, overcomes fear through confidence in God and His word.

**Content:** The psalm was written when the Philistines seized David in Gath. David describes how his enemies pursue and watch him all day long, yet in the midst of fear he chooses to trust in God. He asks God to take notice of his suffering and to gather up his tears. The psalm culminates in praise and a promise to walk before God in the light of life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"In God I will praise His word"** (v.5, 11): A repeated refrain expressing that trust in God's word overcomes fear of people.
- **Tears in Your skin bottle** (v.9): An image showing that God sees and preserves every single tear David sheds in his distress.
- **"The light of life"** (v.14): An expression for salvation and living in God's presence, as opposed to the darkness of death.

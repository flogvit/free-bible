**Main Theme:** Pentecost marks the outpouring of the Holy Spirit and the birth of the Christian church.

**Content:** On Pentecost, the Holy Spirit came upon the disciples with a sound like a mighty wind and tongues as of fire, and they began to speak in other languages. Peter proclaimed that this fulfilled Joel's prophecy and testified about Jesus as crucified, raised from the dead, and exalted to God's right hand as Lord and Messiah. About 3,000 people repented, were baptized, and formed the first Christian community, characterized by the apostles' teaching, fellowship, breaking of bread, and prayer.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tongues as of fire:** A visible sign of the Spirit's coming upon each believer.
- **Other languages (glossolalia):** The miraculous ability to proclaim God's mighty works so that all nations could understand.
- **Repentance and baptism:** Peter's call to respond—repent and be baptized for the forgiveness of sins.
- **Fellowship (koinonia):** The life of believers together, marked by shared possessions, meals, and prayer.

**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches that true purity comes from the heart, not from external traditions, and demonstrates his power over evil spirits even in Gentile regions.

**Content:** Pharisees and scribes from Jerusalem criticize the disciples for eating with unwashed hands. Jesus responds by exposing their hypocrisy: they place human traditions above God's commands, as seen in the korban practice where people evade helping their parents. He teaches that defilement comes from the heart, not from what one eats. In the region of Tyre, Jesus casts an unclean spirit out of a Syrophoenician woman’s daughter, and in the Decapolis he heals a deaf man who could hardly speak.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tradition (παράδοσις):** Human-made rules that the Pharisees placed above God's commands.
- **Heart (καρδία):** The source of both defilement and true worship—the inner person.
- **Korban:** A gift practice that was misused to circumvent the command to honor parents.
- **Ephphatha (Εφφαθα):** An Aramaic word meaning "be opened"—Jesus opens both ears and hearts.
- **Crumbs (ψιχία):** A picture showing that God's grace also reaches the Gentiles.

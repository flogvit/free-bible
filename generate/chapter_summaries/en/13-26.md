**Main Theme:** The organization of the Levites' duties as gatekeepers, treasurers, and civil officials.

**Content:** This chapter describes how the gatekeepers were organized into divisions under leaders named Meshelemiah, Obed-Edom, and Hosah, with guards assigned to the four sides of the temple. Other Levites were given responsibility for the storehouses containing the holy offerings and war spoils that King David and the leaders had dedicated. Some Levites were also appointed as judges and overseers over Israel, both west and east of the Jordan.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gatekeepers:** Levites who guarded the temple gates and maintained order and holiness.
- **Storehouses:** Places for storing holy gifts, tithes, and war spoils dedicated to the house of the Lord.
- **Casting Lots:** A method used to assign service positions fairly, regardless of rank.
- **Judges and Overseers:** Levites with civil duties to handle God's and the king's affairs throughout Israel.

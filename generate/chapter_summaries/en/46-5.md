**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts the congregation to exercise church discipline and keep themselves pure from sin.

**Content:** Paul criticizes the congregation for tolerating a man who is living in a sexual relationship with his father's wife—a sin so serious that even pagans distance themselves from it. He commands them to hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved. Using the image of leaven permeating the whole batch of dough, he emphasizes that sin must be removed from the community. The congregation is to judge its own members, but leave judgment of those outside to God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Leaven (ζύμη):** A little leaven permeates the whole batch of dough—sin in the congregation affects the entire community.
- **Passover Lamb (πάσχα):** Christ is our Passover lamb who has been sacrificed; the congregation must live in purity.
- **Hand over to Satan:** Exclusion from the congregation with the purpose of repentance and ultimate salvation.
- **Judge those within:** The congregation has responsibility to judge its own members, not those outside.

**Main Theme:** Jerusalem is like grapevine wood—useless and destined for the fire because of the people's unfaithfulness.

**Content:** God asks the prophet what grapevine wood is worth compared to other types of timber. The answer is nothing—it is fit neither for craftsmanship nor even for a peg, whether it is whole or already scorched by fire. In the same way, God has handed over the inhabitants of Jerusalem to the fire. They have been unfaithful, and the land will be laid waste.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Grapevine wood:** Symbolizes Jerusalem—valuable only for its fruit (obedience), but the wood itself is worthless.
- **The fire:** God's judgment that consumes what has no useful value.
- **Unfaithfulness (ma'al):** The specific term for Jerusalem's sin—a breach of the covenant relationship with God.
- **Recognition formula:** "You shall know that I am the Lord"—the judgment will reveal God's identity and power.

**Main Theme:** Temple construction resumes under prophetic encouragement and meets administrative challenges from Persian authorities.

**Content:** The prophets Haggai and Zechariah inspire Zerubbabel and Joshua to resume temple building in Jerusalem. Tattenai, the governor of the province west of the Euphrates, comes to investigate and asks who gave permission for the construction. God's eye rests upon the Jewish elders so that the work is not stopped while the matter is sent to King Darius. In the letter, the Jews' response is quoted: they are God's servants rebuilding the temple that Cyrus had given them permission to build, and they ask the king to examine the archives to confirm this.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's eye (v. 5):** God's protective care over His people, preventing opponents from stopping the work.
- **The prophets Haggai and Zechariah (vv. 1–2):** God's spokespeople who encourage the people to faithfulness and action.
- **Cyrus's decree (vv. 13–15):** The historical authorization that legitimizes temple construction under Persian rule.
- **Sheshbazzar (vv. 14–16):** The first leader who laid the foundation of the temple after returning from Babylon.

**Main Theme:** A song of praise expressing trust in God as the eternal rock, and hope in resurrection and judgment.

**Content:** The chapter is a song sung in Judah about the strong city that God has given his people, contrasted with the lofty city that will be humbled to dust. The people are urged to trust in the Lord forever, for he is the eternal rock. The prophet expresses longing for God and his righteous judgments. The chapter culminates in a promise that the dead shall live and rise again, while the people are encouraged to seek refuge until God's wrath has passed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Strong City (v.1):** God's city of salvation with walls and ramparts, symbolizing security for the righteous people.
- **Eternal Rock (v.4):** The Lord as an unchanging foundation for eternal trust.
- **Perfect Peace (v.3):** The deep peace (shalom shalom) given to those who trust in God.
- **Dew from the Light (v.19):** God's life-giving power that awakens the dead to life.
- **Hide Yourself a Little While (v.20):** An encouragement to seek refuge while God's judgment is carried out over the earth.

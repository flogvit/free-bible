**Main Theme:** Jonathan and David renew their covenant and say farewell when Saul’s murder plans are confirmed.

**Content:** David flees to Jonathan and asks what he has done wrong, since Saul seeks his life. They devise a plan: David will stay away from the New Moon festival, and Jonathan will test his father’s intentions. When Saul reacts with rage and even throws a spear at Jonathan, it becomes clear that David must flee. Jonathan signals David through an agreed-upon arrow signal, and the two share a tearful farewell after renewing their covenant.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant (בְּרִית):** Jonathan and David renew their covenant, in which David promises to show loyal love toward Jonathan’s family forever.
- **The Arrow Signal:** A secret communication system where the placement of the arrows reveals whether David is safe or must flee.
- **New Moon Festival (חֹדֶשׁ):** The royal meal where David’s absence exposes Saul’s murder plans.
- **Loyal Love (חֶסֶד):** The covenant faithfulness Jonathan asks David to maintain toward his family, even after Jonathan’s death.

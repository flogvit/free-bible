**Main Theme:** A prophecy of judgment against Babylon proclaiming the city's total destruction on the day of the Lord.

**Content:** God gathers an army from distant lands to execute His judgment upon Babylon. The day of the Lord is described as terrible and merciless, with cosmic signs where the sun, moon, and stars are darkened. God raises up the Medes as His instrument—a people who cannot be bribed with gold or silver. Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, shall become like Sodom and Gomorrah and will never again be inhabited by humans, but only by wild animals and desert creatures.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Day of the Lord (יוֹם יְהוָה):** A day of divine judgment characterized by wrath, destruction, and cosmic upheaval.
- **The Medes:** The people God raises up as His instrument to destroy Babylon—they are merciless and cannot be bribed.
- **Cosmic Darkness:** The stars, sun, and moon cease to shine—a sign of God's intervention in the natural order.
- **Sodom and Gomorrah:** Babylon's fate is compared to the total destruction of these cities as an expression of God's judgment.
- **Desolate City with Wild Animals:** Babylon shall remain uninhabited and house only desert creatures, owls, and jackals—an image of complete desolation.

**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts the invitations of Wisdom and Folly, showing which path leads to life or death.

**Content:** Wisdom, personified as a woman, has built her house with seven pillars and invites the inexperienced to her meal so that they may live and walk in the way of understanding. Lady Folly, on the other hand, lures with "stolen water" and "secret bread"—symbols of forbidden pleasure—but her guests end up in the realm of the dead. In the middle of the chapter it is emphasized that wisdom begins with fear of the Lord, and that the wise accept guidance, while the scoffer rejects it.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Wisdom's house with seven pillars:** Symbolizes perfect wisdom and a safe place for learning.
- **The meal:** Bread and wine represent receiving wisdom and instruction.
- **Stolen water:** A picture of sin's alluring yet destructive nature.
- **Fear of the Lord:** The foundation and beginning of all true wisdom (v.10).
- **The realm of the dead (Sheol):** The path of folly leads to the dead and the abyss.

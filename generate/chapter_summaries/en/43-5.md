**Main Theme:** Jesus heals on the Sabbath and reveals his divine authority as the Son who has power to give life and to judge.

**Content:** At the Pool of Bethesda in Jerusalem, Jesus heals a man who had been sick for 38 years and commands him to take up his bed and walk—on the Sabbath. This triggers conflict with the Jewish leaders, who accuse Jesus of breaking the Sabbath and blasphemy when he calls God his Father. Jesus defends himself with a long discourse in which he explains his unique unity with the Father: the Son does what the Father does, gives life to whom he wills, and has been given all judgment. He points to four witnesses concerning his identity: John the Baptist, his own works, the Father himself, and the Scriptures—but accuses his listeners of rejecting this testimony.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Pool of Bethesda:** The place where the sick man waited for healing; Jesus shows that he is the source of healing, not the water.
- **The Sabbath:** Jesus' healing on the Sabbath reveals his divine authority and provokes the conflict that drives the chapter.
- **Father and Son:** Jesus reveals his unique relationship with the Father—they work together, and the Son has been given power to give life and to execute judgment.
- **Eternal Life:** Whoever hears Jesus' word and believes in the Father has already passed from death to life.
- **Testimony:** Four witnesses confirm Jesus' identity: John the Baptist, Jesus' works, the Father, and the Scriptures (including Moses).

**Main Theme:** The Ark of the Covenant is taken by the Philistines, and God's glory departs from Israel.

**Content:** Israel suffers two major defeats against the Philistines, with 4,000 and then 30,000 killed respectively. In an attempt to secure victory, they bring the Ark of the Covenant from Shiloh, but the Philistines still win and take the ark as war booty. Eli's two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, die in battle. When the 98-year-old Eli hears that the ark has been taken, he falls backward and dies. Phinehas' wife gives birth to a son she names Ichabod – "the glory has departed from Israel".

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant (אֲרוֹן בְּרִית־יְהוָה):** The symbol of God's presence among the people, which the Israelites mistakenly believed would guarantee victory.
- **Ichabod (אִי־כָבוֹד):** The name means "no glory" and marks that God's glory has left Israel with the loss of the ark.
- **Eli's fall:** The old priest's death upon hearing about the ark symbolizes the end of an era and the consequence of his sons' sin.
- **The Philistines' fear:** Even the Gentiles recognized Israel's God as the one who struck Egypt, but God's people trusted in the ark rather than in God Himself.

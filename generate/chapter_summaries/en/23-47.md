**Main Theme:** A prophetic judgment against Babylon announcing the empire's fall from power to humiliation.

**Content:** Babylon, called "virgin daughter of Babylon," will be toppled from her throne and become like a slave woman. God gave Israel into Babylon's hand as punishment, but Babylon showed no mercy and placed a heavy yoke on the aged. Babylon trusted in its wisdom and magic and proudly declared: "I am, and there is no other." Therefore, disaster will come suddenly upon them, and neither sorcerers, astrologers nor their trading partners can save them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Virgin daughter of Babylon** – A title that ironically contrasts Babylon's proud self-image with the coming humiliation.
- **"I am, and there is no other"** – Babylon's arrogant self-exaltation that mimics divine language.
- **Our Redeemer (go'el)** – The Lord of hosts who defends Israel and avenges their cause against the oppressor.
- **Sorcery and astrology** – Babylon's occult practices that will prove powerless against God's judgment.

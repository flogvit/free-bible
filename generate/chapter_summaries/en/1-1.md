# Summary of Genesis 1

**Main Theme:** God creates the heavens, the earth, and all life in six days through His word.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the earth being formless and empty, darkness over the deep, and the Spirit of God hovering over the waters. Through six days, God creates by His word: first light and darkness, then the expanse of the sky, dry land with vegetation, the heavenly bodies, sea creatures and birds, and finally land animals and humankind. Humankind is created in God’s image, male and female, and given dominion over creation. God sees that all He has made is exceedingly good.

**Key Words/Images:**

- **בָּרָא (bara)** – "created": This Hebrew verb is used specifically for God’s creative act, something only God does.
- **תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ (tohu vavohu)** – "formless and empty": Describes the chaotic state of the earth before God ordered creation.
- **צֶלֶם (tselem)** – "image": Humankind is created in God’s image, expressing human unique dignity and relationship to God.
- **טוֹב (tov)** – "good": Repeated as a refrain throughout the chapter; God sees that creation is good.

**Main Theme:** Paul’s longing and care for the Thessalonians, confirmed by Timothy’s good report.

**Content:** Paul sends Timothy to Thessalonica to strengthen and encourage the church amid trials, and to ensure that the tempter has not led them astray. Timothy returns with good news about their faith, love, and longing for Paul, bringing great comfort and joy. Paul concludes with a prayer that God would cause their love to increase and keep them blameless in holiness until the coming of Jesus.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Strengthen (στηρίξαι):** Timothy’s mission was to establish the faith of the church so they could stand firm under trials.
- **Trials (θλίψεσιν):** Sufferings that Paul had predicted would come, and which Christians are destined to endure.
- **The Tempter (ὁ πειράζων):** Paul’s concern that Satan might have tempted them away from the faith.
- **Holiness (ἁγιωσύνη):** The goal for believers—to be blameless before God at the coming of Jesus.

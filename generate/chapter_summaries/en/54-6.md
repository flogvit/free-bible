**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts Timothy to godliness with contentment, warns against greed and false teaching, and charges him to guard the gospel.

**Content:** The chapter opens with exhortations to slaves to honor their masters. Paul warns against false teachers and emphasizes that godliness with contentment is great gain, while the love of money is the root of all kinds of evil. Timothy is called to fight the good fight of faith and keep the commandment blameless until Christ's return. The rich are exhorted to be generous, and the chapter concludes with an appeal to guard what has been entrusted to him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Godliness with contentment (εὐσέβεια μετὰ αὐταρκείας):** True wealth is not material but spiritual satisfaction in God.
- **The root of the love of money (ῥίζα ... ἡ φιλαργυρία):** The love of money is the source of all evil and leads people away from the faith.
- **The good fight of faith (τὸν καλὸν ἀγῶνα):** The Christian life as a struggle in which one lays hold of eternal life.
- **King of kings (βασιλεὺς τῶν βασιλευόντων):** God's sovereign majesty over all earthly powers.

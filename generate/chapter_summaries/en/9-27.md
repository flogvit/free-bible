**Main Theme:** David seeks refuge with the Philistines to escape Saul’s persecution.

**Content:** David flees with his 600 men to King Achish of Gath and is given the city of Ziklag as a base. Over the course of one year and four months, David attacks the Geshurites, Girzites, and Amalekites, but lies to Achish, claiming he has been raiding areas belonging to Judah. He kills all witnesses to conceal the truth, leading Achish to believe that David has made himself hated throughout Israel and will remain his servant forever.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Ziklag:** The city given to David by Achish, which later remained under the control of the kings of Judah.
- **Deception:** David misleads Achish by hiding whom he is truly attacking.
- **Double Dealing:** David lives as an ally of the Philistines while secretly protecting Israel’s interests.
- **Survival:** David’s strategy to avoid Saul by living outside the borders of Israel.

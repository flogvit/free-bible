**Main Theme:** An appeal to repentance and God’s promise of healing and renewal for Israel.

**Content:** Hosea calls Israel to turn back to the Lord, confess their sins, and offer words (praise) instead of sacrifices. The people are urged to abandon false trust in Assyria, military power, and idols. God responds with a promise to heal their unfaithfulness and love them freely, comparing His blessing to dew that causes Israel to flourish. The chapter concludes with a wisdom exhortation: the righteous will walk in the Lord’s ways, while transgressors will stumble.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dew (טַל):** God will be like life-giving dew for Israel, an image of renewal and blessing.
- **Lily and Lebanon:** Israel shall blossom like a lily and take root like the trees of Lebanon—symbols of beauty and stability.
- **Green Cypress:** God compares Himself to an evergreen cypress, as Israel’s source of fruit and life.
- **Fruit of Lips:** Praise and confession replace animal sacrifices as what Israel brings to God.

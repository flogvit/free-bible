**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts the church not to be confused about the day of the Lord and explains what must happen before Jesus' return.

**Content:** Paul warns against being troubled by rumors that the day of the Lord has already come. First, the rebellion must occur and the man of lawlessness must be revealed – he who exalts himself above everything called God. Something holds him back until his time comes, but when he is revealed, Jesus will destroy him. Paul thanks God for the church and encourages them to stand firm and hold fast to the traditions they have received.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The man of lawlessness (ὁ ἄνθρωπος τῆς ἀνομίας):** A future figure who sets himself up in God's temple, claiming to be God.
- **What holds him back (τὸ κατέχον):** A force or person that prevents lawlessness from breaking out fully until the proper time.
- **The mystery of lawlessness (τὸ μυστήριον τῆς ἀνομίας):** The evil already at work in secret, but not yet fully revealed.
- **The traditions (παραδόσεις):** The apostolic teaching that the church must hold fast to, given through word and letter.

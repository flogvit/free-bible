**Main Theme:** A prayer to the God of vengeance to intervene against the wicked and defend the righteous.

**Content:** The psalmist cries out to God to rise up as judge of the world and repay the proud who oppress his people, killing widows, foreigners, and orphans. The wicked claim that God does not see, but the psalmist argues: He who planted the ear and formed the eye—should he not hear and see? The psalm expresses confidence that the Lord will not abandon his people, but is a refuge who will ultimately destroy the wicked.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God of Vengeance (אֵל נְקָמוֹת):** God who restores justice by punishing oppressors.
- **Refuge/rock (מִשְׂגָּב/צוּר):** God as protection and safety for the righteous in times of trouble.
- **Discipline and instruction (יסר/למד):** God's correction through adversity brings peace and wisdom from his law.
- **Human thoughts are vanity (הֶבֶל):** Human plans are fleeting, but God's plan endures.

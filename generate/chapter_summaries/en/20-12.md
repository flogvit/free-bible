**Main Theme:** The contrast between the words, actions, and destiny of the righteous and the wicked.

**Content:** This chapter presents a series of contrasts between wisdom and folly, where whoever loves discipline loves knowledge (v.1). The power of speech is central: lying lips are an abomination to the Lord, while truthful words endure forever (vv. 19,22). The diligent hand shall rule, but the lazy person will be put to forced labor (v.24). The righteous know the needs of their own animals, but the mercy of the wicked is cruel (v.10).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Eshet chayil (the capable wife):** She is a crown for her husband, while a shameful wife is like rot in his bones (v.4).
- **Root that stands firm:** The root of the righteous will not be shaken, an image of stability and security (vv. 3,12).
- **The power of the tongue:** Words can be like sword thrusts, but gentle words bring healing (v.18).
- **The path of righteousness:** On the path of righteousness there is life, and its way leads not to death (v.28).

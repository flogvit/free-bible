**Main Theme:** Job maintains his innocence and describes the inevitable downfall of the wicked.

**Content:** Job swears by the living God—who has denied him justice and made his soul bitter—that he will never speak falsehood or abandon his integrity. He refuses to concede to his friends' accusations and declares that his conscience is clear. He then describes the fate of the wicked: their children will be struck by the sword and famine, their accumulated wealth will pass to the righteous, and they themselves will be swept away by a storm and an east wind.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Oath by God (v.2-4):** Job solemnly swears by God's life that he will hold fast to the truth.
- **Integrity/Innocence (תֻּמָּה, v.5-6):** Job's unwavering insistence on his moral purity despite the accusations.
- **The Hypocrite's Hopelessness (v.8-10):** The godless have no real access to God when trouble comes.
- **The Storm (v.20-22):** A picture of God's judgment that irrevocably sweeps the wicked away from their place.

**Main Theme:** A hymn that encourages trusting in God alone, not in humans, because the Lord's kingdom endures forever.

**Content:** The psalmist urges praise of the Lord throughout life and warns against placing trust in people, who are mortal. Blessed is the one whose help is the God of Jacob—the One who made heaven and earth and remains faithful forever. God helps the oppressed, feeds the hungry, sets prisoners free, opens the eyes of the blind, lifts up those who are bowed down, protects foreigners, and supports orphans and widows. He leads the way of the wicked astray.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hallelujah:** Frames the psalm (vv. 1 and 10) as a call to praise.
- **Human Mortality:** People lose their breath, return to dust, and their plans come to nothing (vv. 3–4).
- **The God of Jacob:** The personal God who remains faithful and helps the vulnerable (vv. 5–6).
- **God's Care for the Weak:** The oppressed, hungry, prisoners, blind, those bowed down, foreigners, orphans, and widows (vv. 7–9).

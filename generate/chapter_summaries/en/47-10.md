**Main Theme:** Paul defends his apostolic authority and ministry against critics in Corinth.

**Content:** Paul appeals to the gentleness of Christ and responds to criticism that he is humble when present but bold in his letters. He emphasizes that his battle is fought with spiritual weapons that have divine power to tear down strongholds and take every thought captive to obey Christ. Paul stays within the sphere of service God has assigned him and does not boast about the work of others. He concludes that whoever boasts should boast in the Lord, for it is the one whom the Lord commends who stands approved.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Spiritual weapons (ὅπλα):** Paul's ministry does not rely on human means but uses weapons with divine power to tear down fortresses.
- **Thought structures (λογισμούς):** Human reasoning and everything that sets itself up against the knowledge of God, which must be torn down.
- **The measure/area (κανών):** The sphere of service God has assigned to Paul, which includes Corinth.
- **Boast in the Lord:** True recognition comes not from self-praise but from the Lord's commendation.

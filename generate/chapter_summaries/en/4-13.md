**Main Theme:** Twelve spies explore Canaan and return with a divided report about the land.

**Content:** The Lord commands Moses to send one leader from each tribe to explore Canaan. Moses gives Hoshea, son of Nun, the name Joshua. The spies explore the land for 40 days and bring back grapes, pomegranates, and figs as proof of the land's fertility. They confirm that the land flows with milk and honey, but ten of the spies spread fear by reporting about powerful peoples, fortified cities, and giants. Caleb tries to calm the people and urges them to take possession of the land, but the other spies insist it is impossible.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Cluster of Grapes from the Valley of Eshcol:** Symbolizes the land's abundance and the reliability of God's promise—so large that two men had to carry it.
- **Descendants of Anak (Nephilim):** The giant people whom the spies feared, causing them to feel like grasshoppers.
- **"A land that devours its inhabitants":** The spies' exaggerated negative report that undermined the people's faith.
- **Caleb:** The one who spoke words of faith in this chapter and encouraged the people to trust in God's promise.

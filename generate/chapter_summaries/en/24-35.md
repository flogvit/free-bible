**Main Theme:** The obedience of the Rechabites to their ancestor stands in sharp contrast to Judah’s disobedience toward God.

**Content:** God commands Jeremiah to invite the Rechabites into the temple and offer them wine. They refuse to drink because their forefather Jonadab had commanded them to abstain from wine, not build houses, and live in tents. God uses their faithful obedience to a human father as a rebuke of Judah, who has refused to listen to God even though He repeatedly sent His prophets. While Judah is threatened with disaster, the Rechabites receive the promise that there will always be someone from their line standing before the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Rechabites:** Descendants of Jonadab son of Rekab who faithfully kept his commands regarding abstinence and a nomadic lifestyle for generations.
- **The Wine:** A symbol of the test that reveals the Rechabites’ obedience in contrast to Judah’s disobedience.
- **Obedience to Father vs. God:** The Rechabites obeyed their human ancestor, while Judah refused to obey the living God.
- **Standing Before the Lord:** The promise given to the Rechabites of enduring service and presence before God as a reward for their faithfulness.

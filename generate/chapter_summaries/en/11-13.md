**Main Theme:** Obedience to God’s word is crucial, even when one is misled by others who claim to speak on God’s behalf.

**Content:** A man of God from Judah is sent to Bethel to prophesy against Jeroboam’s altar and predict that a king named Josiah will destroy it. When Jeroboam stretches out his hand to seize him, the hand withers but is healed after the man of God prays for him. The man of God had received strict orders not to eat or drink in Bethel, yet he is deceived by an older prophet who lies about a revelation from an angel. As punishment for his disobedience, the man of God is killed by a lion on his way home. The older prophet buries him and confirms that his prophecy will be fulfilled.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Altar at Bethel** – A symbol of Jeroboam’s idolatry, which will be judged and destroyed.
- **The Withered Hand** – A sign that God protects His messengers and confirms their message.
- **The Lion** – God’s instrument of judgment against disobedience, yet strangely it does not harm the donkey or the body.
- **The False Prophet** – A warning that even believers can be misled by those who claim to speak on God’s behalf.

**Main Theme:** God’s faithful servant Job is tested through suffering but holds fast to his faith.

**Content:** Job was a righteous and God-fearing man in the land of Uz, blessed with great wealth and ten children. In the heavenly assembly, Satan challenged Job’s motives, claiming that his piety depended on the blessings he received. God allowed Satan to strike everything Job owned, but not Job himself. In one day, Job lost all his livestock, servants, and all ten of his children. Though deeply grieved, Job did not sin; instead, he worshiped God and said: "The Lord gave, and the Lord has taken away; may the name of the Lord be praised."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Uz:** The land where Job lived, likely east of Israel in what may have been the region of Edom.
- **Sons of God (בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים):** Heavenly beings who came before the Lord, including Satan.
- **Satan (הַשָּׂטָן):** "The Accuser" who questions the sincerity of human devotion to God.
- **"The Lord gave, and the Lord has taken away":** Job’s confession of faith amid disaster—an expression of trust in God’s sovereignty.

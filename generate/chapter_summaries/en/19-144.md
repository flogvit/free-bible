**Main Theme:** A Davidic psalm that praises God as rock and protector, and asks for deliverance and blessing.

**Content:** David praises God who trains his hands for war and is his refuge. He reflects on human frailty compared to God's greatness, and asks God to intervene with power against deceitful enemies. The psalm concludes with a vision of a blessed people enjoying prosperity and peace, declaring happy the nation whose God is the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rock (צוּר):** God as a firm and secure refuge in battle and danger.
- **Man as a breath (הֶבֶל):** Human life's brevity contrasted with God's eternal greatness.
- **Lightning and arrows:** God's mighty intervention against enemies through theophany imagery.
- **Sons like plants, daughters like corner pillars:** Images of a blessed home with a flourishing family and prosperity.

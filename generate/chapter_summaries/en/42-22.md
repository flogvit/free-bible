**Main Theme:** Jesus' last Passover meal with his disciples, his arrest, and the beginning of his trial mark the turning point toward the cross.

**Content:** Satan enters Judas, who arranges to betray Jesus to the chief priests. During the Passover meal, Jesus institutes the Lord's Supper and teaches the disciples that true greatness lies in serving others. He predicts Peter's denial and prepares the disciples for the coming trial. In Gethsemane, Jesus prays earnestly while the disciples sleep, before Judas betrays him with a kiss. Peter denies Jesus three times, and Jesus is brought to the Jewish council where he affirms his identity as God's Son.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's Supper (vv. 19–20):** The bread and wine represent Jesus' body and blood in the new covenant, given for the disciples.
- **The Cup in Gethsemane (v. 42):** A symbol of Jesus' impending suffering, which he submits to the Father's will to bear.
- **The Rooster's Crow (vv. 34, 60–61):** The sign that fulfills Jesus' prophecy about Peter's denial and leads to Peter's bitter remorse.
- **The Hour of Darkness (v. 53):** Jesus describes the arrest as the power of darkness, highlighting the spiritual battle behind these events.

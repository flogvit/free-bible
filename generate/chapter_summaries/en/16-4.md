**Main Theme:** Rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem despite hostile opposition, with trust in God and practical readiness.

**Content:** When Sanballat, Tobiah, and their allies heard that the wall-building work was progressing, they planned to attack Jerusalem. Nehemiah responded with prayer and by posting guards. He encouraged the people not to fear but to remember the Lord and fight for their families. The workers were organized so that half stood guard with weapons while the others built, and all were armed. God frustrated the enemies' plans, and the work continued from dawn until the stars appeared.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Conspiracy** – The enemies (Sanballat, Tobiah, Arabs, Ammonites, Ashdodites) unite against Jerusalem.
- **Prayer and Watchfulness** – Nehemiah's strategy combines spiritual dependence with practical action.
- **Sword and Trowel** – The workers build with one hand while holding a weapon in the other, an image of vigilance.
- **Shophar (horn)** – A signal horn used to gather the people at the sound of attack; a sign of unity and readiness.

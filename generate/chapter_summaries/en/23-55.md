**Main Theme:** God's gracious invitation to salvation, repentance, and the everlasting covenant.

**Content:** God invites all who are thirsty to come and receive water, wine, and milk without payment—a picture of salvation as a gift. He offers an everlasting covenant tied to the promises given to David. The chapter calls for repentance: seeking the Lord, forsaking evil ways, and turning back to God who forgives abundantly. God's thoughts and ways are higher than human ones, and His word will always accomplish its purpose. The chapter concludes with a promise of joyful restoration where creation rejoices.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Water, wine, and milk:** Symbols of God's salvation and blessings offered freely to all who thirst.
- **Everlasting covenant (בְּרִית עוֹלָם):** God's unshakable promise tied to David, now extended to the people.
- **God's word as rain:** Just as rain makes the earth fruitful, God's word will always achieve its purpose.
- **Trees clapping their hands:** Nature rejoices at God's salvation—thorn bushes are replaced by noble trees as a lasting sign.

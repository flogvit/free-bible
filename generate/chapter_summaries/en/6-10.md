**Main Theme:** The Lord fights for Israel and grants them a miraculous victory over the five Amorite kings and their cities.

**Content:** When five Amorite kings, led by Adoni-Zedek of Jerusalem, attack Gibeon because it has made peace with Israel, Joshua comes to its aid in fulfillment of the Lord's promise of victory. The Lord intervenes by throwing panic among the enemies and sending large hailstones from heaven that kill more than the sword does. Joshua prays for the sun and moon to stand still, and the Lord answers his prayer—an unprecedented event. The five kings are found hiding in a cave, killed, and hanged. Then Joshua systematically conquers the cities of Makkedah, Libnah, Lachish, Eglon, Hebron, and Debir.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hailstones from heaven** – God's direct intervention in battle shows that victory is His work, not merely Israel's military strength.
- **The sun stands still** – A unique miracle where God answers a human prayer and extends the day to complete the victory.
- **The five kings in the cave** – Symbolizes how God's enemies cannot hide and that judgment is inevitable.
- **Herem (the ban)** – The total destruction of the cities as an expression of God's judgment and obedience to His command.

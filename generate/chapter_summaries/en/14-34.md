**Main Theme:** King Josiah’s religious reforms and the rediscovery of the Book of the Law lead to a renewal of the covenant in Judah.

**Content:** Josiah became king at eight years old and did what was right in the Lord’s eyes. In his eighth year as ruler, he began to seek God, and in his twelfth year, he started cleansing Judah, Jerusalem, and the northern regions from idolatry. In his eighteenth year, during temple renovations, the priest Hilkiah found the Book of the Law, which was read aloud to the king. Josiah tore his clothes in grief, sought counsel from the prophetess Huldah, and she confirmed God’s judgment upon the people—but assured that Josiah himself would be spared. The king gathered the people for a renewal of the covenant, and they committed themselves to follow the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Book of the Law (סֵפֶר הַתּוֹרָה):** The rediscovery of Moses’ law book in the temple triggered the reforms and the covenant renewal.
- **Tearing one’s clothes (וַיִּקְרַע אֶת־בְּגָדָיו):** An expression of deep sorrow and repentance when the king heard the words of the Law.
- **The Prophetess Huldah:** A female prophet who delivered God’s judgment and grace to the king.
- **Covenant Renewal (בְּרִית):** The king and the people renewed their commitment to follow the Lord’s commands and laws.

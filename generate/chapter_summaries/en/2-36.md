**Main Theme:** The construction of the tabernacle begins with God’s blessed craftsmen and the people’s abundance of gifts.

**Content:** Bezalel, Oholiab, and other God-given craftsmen begin work on the sanctuary. The people give so generously that Moses must ask them to stop—the materials are more than enough. The craftsmen make the tabernacle’s tent coverings from fine linen and goat hair, cover them with red-dyed ram skins and finer skins, fashion the boards from acacia wood with bases of silver, and create the veil as well as the screen for the entrance to the tent.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Wisdom from God (חָכְמָה):** The craftsmen’s skill is a gift from the Lord, not merely natural talent.
- **Abundance of gifts:** The people’s generosity is so great that they must be stopped—a testimony to the devotion of their hearts.
- **The Veil (פָּרֹכֶת):** The woven veil with cherubim marks the boundary between the Holy Place and the Most Holy Place.

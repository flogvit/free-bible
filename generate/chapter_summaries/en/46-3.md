**Main Theme:** Paul warns against division and human wisdom, pointing out that the church is God’s building with Christ as its only foundation.

**Content:** Paul rebukes the Corinthians for their immaturity and divisions—they are still fleshly and can only tolerate "milk." He explains that he and Apollos are merely servants; it is God who gives growth. The church is God’s temple where the Spirit dwells, and Christ is the only foundation. The wisdom of this world is foolishness to God, and all things belong to believers—but they belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Milk and solid food:** A picture of spiritual immaturity—the Corinthians were not ready for deep teaching.
- **God’s field and building:** The church is God’s property where servants plant and water, but God gives the growth.
- **The Foundation (Christ):** Jesus Christ is the only basis on which the church can be built.
- **God’s temple:** Believers are God’s holy temple where the Spirit dwells—to destroy it has serious consequences.

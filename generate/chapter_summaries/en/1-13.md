**Main Theme:** Abram and Lot separate, and God renews His promise of the land to Abram.

**Content:** Abram and Lot return from Egypt to Canaan with great wealth in livestock, silver, and gold. Conflicts between their herdsmen lead Abram to suggest that they part ways. Lot chooses the fertile Jordan Valley near Sodom, while Abram remains in Canaan. God promises Abram all the land he can see and declares that his offspring will be as countless as the dust of the earth. Abram settles by the oaks of Mamre in Hebron and builds an altar to the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Jordan Valley:** The fertile region Lot chooses, compared to the garden of the Lord and Egypt, yet near the sinful cities of Sodom and Gomorrah.
- **Dust of the earth:** A picture of Abram's future descendants—so numerous that they cannot be counted.
- **The altar in Hebron:** Abram's worship and gratitude to God after receiving the promise of the land anew.

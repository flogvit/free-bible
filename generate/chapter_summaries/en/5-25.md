**Main Theme:** Laws that promote justice, dignity, and care in the life of Israel’s community.

**Content:** The chapter contains laws concerning fair punishment (a maximum of 40 lashes), a prohibition against muzzling an ox while it is threshing, and levirate marriage to preserve the name and inheritance of a deceased brother. It commands honest weights and measures in trade, since dishonesty is an abomination to the Lord. The chapter concludes with the command to remember Amalek’s evil attack and to blot out their memory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Forty lashes:** The maximum limit for corporal punishment—protecting the dignity of the guilty as "your brother."
- **The threshing ox:** A picture that those who work deserve to enjoy the fruits of their labor.
- **The shoe ceremony (chalitza):** The ritual in which a widow removes the sandal from the foot of her brother-in-law who refuses levirate marriage—a public testimony of his failure.
- **Just weight and measure:** A symbol of honesty and integrity as the foundation for a blessed life in the land.
- **Remember Amalek:** A reminder to blot out the people who, without fear of God, attacked the weakest among Israel.

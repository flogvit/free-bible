**Main Theme:** Matthew 26 depicts the events leading up to Jesus' suffering through betrayal, the Last Supper, prayer in anguish, and arrest.

**Content:** The chapter begins with the chief priests plotting to seize Jesus, while a woman anoints him with costly oil in Bethany. During the Passover meal, Jesus institutes the Lord's Supper with bread and wine as signs of the new covenant in his blood. In Gethsemane, Jesus struggles in prayer and submits to the Father's will before Judas betrays him with a kiss. Jesus is arrested, questioned by the Sanhedrin, and condemned for blasphemy, while Peter denies him three times.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Anointing at Bethany:** A woman's act of love that prepares Jesus for burial and becomes an enduring testimony.
- **The Lord's Supper:** Jesus institutes bread and wine as signs of his body and blood, poured out for the forgiveness of sins.
- **The Prayer in Gethsemane:** Jesus asks that the cup of suffering might pass from him, yet submits to the Father's will.
- **Judas' Kiss:** The betrayal is carried out with a sign of friendship that turns into treachery.
- **Peter's Denial:** The disciple who promised loyalty fails three times before the rooster crows.

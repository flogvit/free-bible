**Main Theme:** David's final preparations for the temple, his praise of God, and the transition of the kingdom to Solomon.

**Content:** David presents his gifts for the temple and encourages the people to give willingly, which they do with joy. He praises God in a powerful prayer where he acknowledges that everything belongs to the Lord and that humans are like foreigners and guests on earth. David prays for Solomon to have a whole heart to keep God's commands and complete the building of the temple. Solomon is anointed as king a second time, and the chapter concludes with David's death after 40 years of reign.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Voluntary gifts (נְדָבָה):** Both David and the people give generously from a whole heart for the building of the temple.
- **"Everything comes from you" (מִמְּךָ הַכֹּל):** David's recognition that all gifts actually belong to God and are merely given back to Him.
- **Foreigners and guests (גֵּרִים וְתוֹשָׁבִים):** The transience of human life – "our days on earth are like a shadow".
- **The Lord's throne (כִּסֵּא יְהוָה):** Solomon sits on the Lord's throne as king, emphasizing the theocracy in Israel.

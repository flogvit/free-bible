**Main Theme:** Stephen delivers a defense speech about Israel's history and becomes the first Christian martyr.

**Content:** Stephen, accused of blasphemy, defends himself before the Sanhedrin by reviewing Israel's salvation history from Abraham through Joseph and Moses to Solomon and the temple. He shows how the people repeatedly rejected God's chosen leaders, culminating in a sharp accusation against the council for having killed the Righteous One and resisted the Holy Spirit. Filled with the Spirit, Stephen sees Jesus standing at God's right hand, and is stoned while praying for forgiveness for his enemies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Righteous One (ὁ δίκαιος):** A messianic title for Jesus that Stephen uses in his accusation against the council.
- **Uncircumcised in heart and ears:** Stephen's characterization of the leaders as spiritually stubborn, like their forefathers.
- **Jesus standing at God's right hand:** Stephen's vision confirming Jesus' exaltation and his role as intercessor.
- **Tabernacle vs. temple:** Stephen argues that God does not dwell in buildings made by human hands.

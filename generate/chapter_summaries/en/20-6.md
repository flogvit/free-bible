**Main Theme:** Proverbs 6 warns against financial foolishness, laziness, wickedness, and sexual immorality, pointing to wisdom as protection from life's pitfalls.

**Content:** The chapter begins with warnings against putting up security for another person's debt and encourages learning from the diligence of the ant. It lists seven things the Lord hates, including pride, lying, and sowing discord. Furthermore, it emphasizes the value of parental instruction as a light on the path. The chapter concludes with strong warnings against adultery and its serious consequences: disgrace and vengeance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Ant:** A model of diligence and self-discipline—it works without supervision and gathers food at the right time.
- **Seven Abominations:** A list of what the Lord hates, revealing His character and requirements for righteousness.
- **Fire in the Lap:** An image of the inevitable consequences of unfaithfulness—one cannot play with fire without getting burned.
- **Light and Lamp:** Parental commands and instruction are compared to a light that guides and protects.

**Main Theme:** God preserves Daniel in the lions’ den and reveals His power over earthly kingdoms.

**Content:** Darius the Mede organizes the empire with 120 satraps and three chief administrators, among whom Daniel distinguishes himself. Jealous leaders manipulate the king into issuing a decree forbidding prayer to anyone but him for thirty days. Daniel continues faithfully to pray, is thrown into the lions’ den, but God sends an angel who shuts the lions’ mouths. The king rescues Daniel and issues a decree honoring Daniel’s God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lions’ Den:** A symbol of mortal danger from which God has power to deliver.
- **The Unchangeable Laws:** The laws of the Medes and Persians, which cannot be altered, reveal the limitations of human systems.
- **Faithfulness in Prayer:** Daniel prays three times a day toward Jerusalem, despite the prohibition.
- **The Living God:** Darius confesses that Daniel’s God is living and His kingdom endures forever.

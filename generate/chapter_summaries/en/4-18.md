**Main Theme:** The chapter establishes the responsibilities, rights, and provision for priests and Levites in their service at the sanctuary.

**Content:** The Lord gives Aaron and his sons the responsibility to bear the sin associated with the sanctuary and the priesthood, while the Levites are to serve as their helpers. The priests receive the holy offerings as their portion, and the Levites receive the tithe from Israel as wages for their service. Neither priests nor Levites shall have an earthly inheritance—the Lord Himself is their inheritance. The Levites must give a tenth of the tithe to the priests, and this arrangement is confirmed as an everlasting covenant of salt.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bear sin (עֲוֺן):** The priests are responsible for protecting the sanctuary from desecration and bearing the consequences of error.
- **The Lord as inheritance:** Priests and Levites receive no earthly property; God Himself is their portion and inheritance.
- **Covenant of salt (בְּרִית מֶלַח):** An eternal, unbreakable covenant that guarantees the priests' right to the offerings.
- **Tithe of the tithe:** The Levites receive the people's tithe but must themselves give a tenth onward to the priests.

**Main Theme:** God confirms the Abrahamic covenant with Isaac and blesses him amid conflicts with the Philistines.

**Content:** During a famine, God instructs Isaac to remain in Gerar rather than go to Egypt, renewing the promises given to Abraham concerning land and numerous descendants. Like his father, Isaac, out of fear, passes off Rebekah as his sister, but Abimelech discovers the truth and rebukes him. Isaac experiences great material blessing, yet the Philistines' jealousy leads to well conflicts. After repeated disputes, Isaac finally finds peace in Rehoboth and Beersheba, where God reveals Himself again and Abimelech seeks a covenant with him. The chapter concludes with Esau marrying Hittite women at age forty, causing bitter grief to his parents.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant Renewal:** God confirms the promises made to Abraham for Isaac—land, descendants as numerous as the stars, and blessing for all nations.
- **The Sister Deception:** Isaac repeats Abraham's sin by calling Rebekah his sister, yet God protects them nonetheless.
- **The Wells (Esek, Sitnah, Rehoboth):** Symbolize the conflict with the Philistines and God's final gift of space and peace.
- **Beersheba:** The place where God reveals Himself, Isaac builds an altar, and the covenant with Abimelech is made—a center for worship and peace.

**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself as the Messiah in Jerusalem and confronts the religious leaders with their lack of faith and fruitfulness.

**Content:** Jesus rides into Jerusalem on a donkey, hailed as David's son, and cleanses the temple of money changers. He curses a fig tree as a sign of judgment and teaches his disciples about the power of faith in prayer. Through two parables—the two sons and the wicked tenants—Jesus warns the religious leaders that God's kingdom will be taken from them and given to those who bear fruit.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Palm Sunday/The Entry:** Jesus fulfills Zechariah's prophecy by riding humbly in as a king of peace, not as a military conqueror.
- **The Cleansing of the Temple:** Jesus asserts his authority over the temple and condemns that the house of prayer has become a "den of robbers."
- **The Fig Tree:** A symbol of Israel/the religious leaders who have foliage (outward piety) but no fruit (obedience).
- **The Parable of the Vineyard:** Illustrates Israel's history of rejecting the prophets and finally God's Son, with a warning of judgment.
- **The Cornerstone:** Jesus quotes Psalm 118 about the stone that was rejected—a prophecy concerning his rejection and exaltation.

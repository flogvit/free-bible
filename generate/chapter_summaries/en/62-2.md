**Main Theme:** John exhorts believers to live in the light by keeping God’s commands, loving one another, and resisting worldly temptations and false teachings.

**Content:** Jesus Christ is our advocate and atoning sacrifice for the sins of the whole world. Those who know God keep His commands and walk as Jesus walked. John warns against loving the world and its desires, and against antichrists who deny that Jesus is the Christ. Believers have an anointing from the Holy One that teaches them the truth, and they are urged to remain in Christ so that they may have confidence at His coming.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Advocate (παράκλητος):** Jesus Christ intercedes for us with the Father when we sin.
- **Light and darkness:** Whoever loves their brother remains in the light; whoever hates walks in darkness and is blinded.
- **Antichrist:** Those who deny the Father and the Son, and that Jesus is the Christ—many have already appeared.
- **Anointing (χρῖσμα):** The gift of the Holy Spirit that teaches believers the truth and protects them from deception.

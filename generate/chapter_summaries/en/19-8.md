**Main Theme:** A hymn of praise that extols God's majesty in creation and humanity's exalted position within God's creation.

**Content:** The psalm is framed by praise to the Lord's majestic name over all the earth (vv. 2, 10). The psalmist wonders how God, who created the heavens with the moon and stars, still remembers the humble human being. Paradoxically, God has made humanity only slightly lower than Himself, crowned them with glory and honor, and given them dominion over all creatures.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Slightly lower than God"** (v. 6): Humanity's exalted dignity – created almost on a divine level.
- **Glory and Honor** (v. 6): Humanity is crowned like a king with responsibility for creation.
- **Dominion** (vv. 7–9): Humanity is appointed to rule over animals, birds, and fish – an echo of the creation account.
- **Infants and nursing children** (v. 3): God establishes strength through the weakest to silence the enemy.

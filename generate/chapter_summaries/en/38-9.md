**Main Theme:** God judges Israel's enemies and promises a Prince of Peace who will bring salvation and universal dominion.

**Content:** The chapter opens with judgment against neighboring nations—Damascus, Tyre, Sidon, and the Philistine cities—which God will destroy. Then the messianic King is proclaimed: He comes humble, riding on a donkey, righteous and saving. He will remove weapons of war and reign from sea to sea. Through the blood of the covenant, prisoners are freed; the Lord Himself will fight for His people and bless them with abundance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Peaceful King on a Donkey** (v. 9): The coming Messiah who is righteous, humble, and brings salvation—fulfilled in Jesus.
- **Blood of the Covenant** (v. 11): The basis for freeing prisoners from "the waterless pit"—pointing to atonement.
- **Dominion from Sea to Sea** (v. 10): The King's universal kingdom of peace extending over all the earth.
- **Prisoners of Hope** (v. 12): God's people waiting for the fulfillment of His promises and double restoration.

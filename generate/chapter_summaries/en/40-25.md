**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about vigilance, faithfulness, and mercy in light of the Son of Man's return and judgment.

**Content:** The chapter contains three parables. In the parable of the ten virgins, five wise and five foolish wait for the bridegroom—only the prepared are allowed into the celebration. In the parable of the talents, a master entrusts his servants with different sums; the faithful stewards are rewarded, while the fearful one who buried his talent is cast out. Finally, the final judgment is depicted where the Son of Man separates nations as a shepherd separates sheep from goats—those who showed mercy to "the least of these my brothers" inherit the kingdom, while those who neglected them go away into eternal punishment.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Bridegroom:** Christ returning—an appeal for vigilance and readiness.
- **Talents:** Resources and abilities given by God to be managed faithfully, not in fear.
- **Sheep and Goats:** A picture of the final judgment where people are separated according to their deeds.
- **"The least of these my brothers":** The vulnerable and needy—service to them is service to Christ himself.

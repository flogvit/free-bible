**Main Theme:** The organization of the temple musicians into 24 divisions under the leadership of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun.

**Content:** David and the military commanders set apart sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun for prophetic musical service with lyres, harps, and cymbals. In total, there were 288 trained singers, divided into 24 groups of 12 each. The order of service was determined by casting lots, ensuring that all were equal—both master and apprentice, young and old.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Prophetic Music:** The musicians' service is described as to "prophesy" – an expression of inspired praise.
- **Casting Lots:** Ensured a fair distribution of service shifts without favoritism.
- **288 Musicians:** This number illustrates the scope of the organized temple service that David prepared.

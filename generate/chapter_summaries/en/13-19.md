**Main Theme:** The Ammonites' insult to David's envoys leads to war and Israel's victory over the Ammonites and their Aramean allies.

**Content:** When Nahash, king of the Ammonites, dies, David sends envoys to console his son Hanun. Hanun humiliates them by shaving off half their beards and cutting their garments in two. The Ammonites hire Aramean forces, but Joab and Abishai defeat them. The Arameans gather new troops under Shophak, the commander of Hadadezer's army, but David himself leads Israel to victory, and the Arameans submit and cease helping the Ammonites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Humiliation of Envoys:** Hanun shaved David's men and cut their garments—a gross insult that triggered the war.
- **Joab's Strategy:** He divided the army to face threats from two fronts and showed trust in God's will for the outcome.
- **Aramean Submission:** After their defeat, Hadadezer's vassals made peace with David and refused to help the Ammonites again.

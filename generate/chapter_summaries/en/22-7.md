**Main Theme:** The bridegroom's admiration of the Shulammite woman's beauty and her reciprocal declaration of love.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a call for the Shulammite woman to return so she may be admired. The bridegroom describes her beauty from head to foot using rich poetic imagery. The woman responds with the declaration of love, "I belong to my beloved, and his desire is for me" (v. 11), inviting him to go out into the fields and spend the night together in the vineyards.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shulammite** – The woman's name or title, possibly derived from "Solomon" or "peace/perfection."
- **Palm Tree** – The woman's figure is compared to a palm tree; the man wishes to "climb up" and grasp its fruit.
- **Dudaim (Love Apples)** – A fragrant plant associated with fertility and love, which the woman has set aside for her beloved.
- **Vine** – A symbol of love and fertility; the couple will together see if it blooms.

**Main Theme:** Chapter 8 explores humanity's limited understanding when faced with power, injustice, and God's unfathomable works.

**Content:** The Preacher advises obeying the king's command and keeping an oath to God, for the king's word holds absolute authority. He acknowledges life's injustice: the righteous are treated as if they were wicked, and the wicked as if they were righteous. No one has power over death or can comprehend everything God does. Nevertheless, he recommends fearing God and enjoying life's simple pleasures—eating, drinking, and being glad.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The King's Command:** Symbolizes authority that must be respected; no one can challenge the one who holds power.
- **Hevel (vanity/futility):** The injustice where the righteous and the wicked receive fates opposite to what they deserve.
- **Time and Judgment:** The wise person knows the right time and way, but humans do not know what the future brings.
- **Fear of God:** It will go well with those who fear God, in contrast to the godless.

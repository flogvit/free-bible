**Main Theme:** Israel’s repentance from mixed marriages and restoration of covenant faithfulness.

**Content:** After Ezra’s prayer and confession, Shekaniah proposes that the people make a covenant to send away their foreign wives and their children. Ezra fasts, mourns, and takes the lead in the process. All men from Judah and Benjamin gather in Jerusalem, and a commission is appointed to handle the cases. Within three months, more than one hundred men from among the priests, Levites, and the people are identified and commit themselves to separating from their foreign wives.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant (berit):** A solemn agreement with God to correct sin and follow the Torah.
- **Foreign women (nasjim nokrijot):** Women from the nations surrounding Israel, who represented a threat to religious purity.
- **Unfaithfulness (ma'al):** The word is used repeatedly to describe the people’s betrayal of God through mixed marriages.
- **Trembling/fear (mara'idim):** The people trembled with awe before God and the matter—a sign of genuine repentance.

**Main Theme:** A prophecy about the kingdom of the righteous king, warnings to the self-complacent, and the promise of the outpouring of the Spirit that brings righteousness and peace.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a vision of a righteous king and leaders who rule with justice, depicting the contrast between the fool and the noble. Then the self-satisfied women are warned of coming destruction—the harvest will fail, and the land will be laid waste. But hope lies in the outpouring of the Spirit from on high, which will transform the wilderness into fertile ground and bring lasting righteousness, peace, and security.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Righteous King (vv.1–2):** A ruler who governs with justice and provides protection like a shelter from the storm and shade in a dry land.
- **The Fool and the Noble (vv.5–8):** The contrast between one who speaks folly and oppresses the poor, and one who plans noble deeds.
- **The Self-Satisfied Women (vv.9–14):** A symbol of false security that will be shaken when destruction comes.
- **The Spirit from on High (v.15):** God's Spirit poured out, transforming desolation into fertility.
- **The Fruit of Righteousness (vv.17–18):** Righteousness leads to peace, quietness, and lasting security.

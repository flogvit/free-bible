**Main Theme:** Jacob gathers his twelve sons to give them prophetic words about their future in the last days.

**Content:** On his deathbed, Jacob calls his sons together and pronounces prophetic blessings over each of them. Reuben, Simeon, and Levi receive rebuke for past sins, while Judah is promised leadership and the messianic Shiloh prophecy. Joseph receives the richest blessing with promises of fruitfulness and victory. After the blessings, Jacob gives instructions to be buried in the cave of Machpelah together with Abraham, Sarah, Isaac, Rebekah, and Leah, before he dies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lion of Judah (v.9):** A picture of Judah's royal power and strength as the ancestor of the kingly line.
- **Shiloh (v.10):** A prophecy about a coming ruler from Judah whom the peoples will obey—interpreted messianically.
- **Cave of Machpelah (vv.29–32):** The family burial place that confirms the covenant promise concerning the land.
- **The last days (v.1):** An expression pointing toward prophetic fulfillment in the future.

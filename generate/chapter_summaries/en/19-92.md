**Main Theme:** A Sabbath psalm that praises God for his greatness and righteousness, contrasting the fate of the wicked with the blessing of the righteous.

**Content:** The psalm opens with an invitation to give thanks and praise the Lord with music, proclaiming his kindness and faithfulness morning and evening. It reflects on God's great works and deep thoughts, which the fool does not understand. While the wicked flourish like grass only to be destroyed forever, the righteous are exalted and flourish like a palm tree and a cedar—planted in the house of the Lord and fruitful even in old age.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sabbath Song:** The psalm is specifically linked to the Sabbath day as a time for praise and reflection.
- **Palm and Cedar (v. 13):** Symbols of the righteous one's growth, strength, and lasting fruitfulness.
- **Grass (v. 8):** A picture of the temporary prosperity of the wicked before their final destruction.
- **Planted in the house of the Lord (v. 14):** Expresses that the righteous are rooted in fellowship with God.

**Main Theme:** Paul’s final exhortation to Timothy to remain faithful in ministry, with the assurance of reward for all who endure.

**Content:** Paul solemnly urges Timothy to preach the Word in season and out of season, for many will turn away from the truth to myths. Paul himself regards his life as a drink offering that is soon to be poured out, yet he has finished the race and kept the faith. Despite loneliness and opposition—only Luke is with him, and Demas has left him—he trusts that the Lord will rescue him into His heavenly kingdom.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Crown of righteousness (v.8):** The reward awaiting not only Paul but all who have loved the Lord’s appearing.
- **Drink offering (v.6):** Paul describes his impending death as an offering poured out for God.
- **Itching ears (vv.3–4):** A picture of people who gather teachers to tell them what they want to hear.
- **Finished the race (v.7):** Paul uses athletic imagery to describe his faithful service throughout life.

**Main Theme:** All people are guilty before God, but are justified by faith in Jesus Christ, not by works of the law.

**Content:** Paul states that the Jews have an advantage because they were entrusted with God's words, yet both Jews and Gentiles stand equally guilty under sin. No one is justified by the law, for the law reveals sin. God's righteousness has now been revealed through faith in Jesus Christ—all who believe are justified by grace through the redemption found in him. God presented Christ as a sacrifice of atonement, thereby excluding all human boasting.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Justification (δικαιοσύνη):** To be declared righteous before God, not by one's own works, but by faith in Christ.
- **Sacrifice of Atonement (ἱλαστήριον):** Christ as the one who atones for sin and turns away God's wrath through his blood.
- **All have sinned (πάντες ἥμαρτον):** The universal condition of sin that makes all people—Jews and Gentiles alike—equally dependent on God's grace.
- **Faith apart from works of the law:** Salvation comes by faith alone, which excludes human boasting and confirms the true purpose of the law.

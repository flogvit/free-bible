**Main Theme:** God saves Noah and his family through the flood that destroys all life on earth.

**Content:** God commands 600-year-old Noah to enter the ark with his family, seven pairs of clean animals and birds, and one pair of unclean animals. After seven days, the flood begins as the springs of the deep burst forth and the windows of heaven are opened. The rain lasts for 40 days and nights, and the waters cover even the highest mountains. All life on dry land perishes, but the Lord shuts the ark and preserves Noah and those with him. The waters prevail over the earth for 150 days.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Ark (תֵּבָה):** The vessel of salvation that God uses to preserve life through judgment.
- **Clean/Unclean (טְהוֹרָה/לֹא טְהֹרָה):** The distinction between animal types points forward to the sacrificial system and God's holiness.
- **40 days and nights:** A period of judgment and cleansing, a number that recurs throughout the Bible.
- **The Lord shut the ark:** God's sovereign act that sealed salvation for the chosen ones.

**Main Theme:** The chapter shows how God's prophetic word is fulfilled through political upheavals in both Aram and Judah.

**Content:** Elisha warns the Shunammite woman of a seven-year famine, and upon her return, she regains her property with the king's help. In Damascus, Elisha prophesies that Hazael will become king over Aram and cause great harm to Israel; Hazael kills Ben-Hadad and takes the throne. In Judah, Joram reigns, followed by his son Ahaziah—both wicked kings influenced by the house of Ahab—but the Lord spares Judah for David's sake.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Famine (רָעָב):** God's warning of seven years of distress demonstrates His sovereignty over nature and care for believers.
- **Hazael's cruelty:** Elisha's weeping over future suffering reveals prophetic insight into Israel's coming trials.
- **David's lamp (נִיר):** God's promise to preserve an heir for David explains why Judah was not destroyed despite wicked kings.
- **The house of Ahab:** Both Joram and Ahaziah were connected to Ahab's family through marriage, which led them into idolatry.

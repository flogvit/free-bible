**Main Theme:** Paul explains how believers are freed from slavery to the law through Christ and have received adoption as children of God.

**Content:** Paul compares believers to heirs who were under guardians until the set time, when God sent His Son to redeem those under the law. God has sent the Spirit of His Son into the hearts of believers, crying out "Abba, Father". Paul warns the Galatians against returning to weak and powerless elemental forces, using an allegory about Hagar and Sarah to illustrate the difference between slavery under the law and the freedom of the promise.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Adoption (υἱοθεσία):** Believers are no longer slaves but children of God and heirs through Christ.
- **The Fullness of Time:** The right moment when God sent His Son, born of a woman, born under the law.
- **Abba, Father:** The Spirit's cry in the hearts of believers confirming their new relationship with God.
- **Hagar and Sarah:** An allegory illustrating the difference between the covenant from Sinai (slavery) and the covenant of promise (freedom).
- **The Heavenly Jerusalem:** The city of the free woman, who is the mother of all believers—in contrast to the earthly Jerusalem.

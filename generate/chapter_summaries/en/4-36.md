**Main Theme:** Rules regarding inheritance through daughters to preserve the tribal territories.

**Content:** The leaders of the tribe of Manasseh expressed concern that land could be lost if inheriting daughters married outside their tribe. Through Moses, the Lord gave a command that daughters who inherit land must marry within their father's tribe, so that the inheritance is not transferred between tribes. Zelophehad's daughters—Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah—obeyed and married their cousins. The book of Numbers concludes with a summary of the commands given on the plains of Moab by the Jordan.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Inheritance (נַחֲלָה):** The territory belonging to each tribe and family, given by God.
- **Tribal Affiliation:** The principle that property should remain within tribal boundaries through generations.
- **Zelophehad's Daughters:** The five sisters who set a precedent for women's inheritance rights in Israel.
- **The Year of Jubilee:** Mentioned as a factor that would not return land if it had been transferred by marriage to another tribe.

**Main Theme:** Laws that protect vulnerable groups and promote justice in society.

**Content:** The chapter provides regulations concerning divorce and forbids remarrying a former wife after she has married someone else. A newlywed man is exempt from military service for one year. Several laws protect the poor, foreigners, widows, and fatherless children—including prohibitions against oppressing day laborers and commands to leave portions of the harvest for those in need. The principle of individual responsibility is established: no one shall die for another's sin.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Bill of Divorce (סֵפֶר כְּרִיתֻת):** Formal documentation that regulates divorce and protects a woman's rights.
- **Leaving the Harvest:** Fields, olive trees, and vineyards are not to be harvested completely—the remainder belongs to the poor.
- **Remember that you were slaves in Egypt:** The basis for showing care toward vulnerable groups—Israel's own experience as an oppressed people.
- **Individual Responsibility:** Each person must bear the consequences of their own sin, not that of family members.

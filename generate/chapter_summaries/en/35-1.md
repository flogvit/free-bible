**Main Theme:** The prophet Habakkuk’s complaint about injustice and God’s surprising answer concerning judgment through the Chaldeans.

**Content:** Habakkuk complains to God about violence, wrongdoing, and the paralysis of the law in Judah (vv. 2–4). God answers that He is raising up the Chaldeans as His instrument of judgment—a terrifying warrior nation (vv. 5–11). The prophet responds with a new complaint: How can the holy God use a people who are even more unjust, and who capture humans like fish in a net (vv. 12–17)?

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Massa (משא):** A burden or message—a designation for the prophet’s solemn words of judgment.
- **Chaldeans (כַּשְׂדִּים):** The Babylonian people whom God uses as an instrument of judgment against Judah.
- **The fish metaphor (vv. 14–17):** The Chaldeans gather people like fish in a net and offer sacrifices to their tools—an image of brutal conquest.
- **Eagle/raptor (נֶשֶׁר):** An image of the Chaldeans’ speed and ruthlessness in attack.

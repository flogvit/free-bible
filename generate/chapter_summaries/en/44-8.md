**Main Theme:** The gospel spreads to Samaria and to the ends of the earth through persecution and God's guidance.

**Content:** After Stephen's death, a great persecution breaks out in Jerusalem, and believers are scattered into Judea and Samaria. Philip proclaims Christ in Samaria with signs and healings, and many come to faith—including the magician Simon. When Peter and John arrive from Jerusalem, the new believers receive the Holy Spirit, but Simon tries to buy this gift and is sharply rebuked. Then God leads Philip to an Ethiopian court official who is reading Isaiah; Philip proclaims Jesus to him, and the official is baptized and continues on his way with joy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Persecution and Scattering:** The persecution in Jerusalem paradoxically leads to the gospel spreading into new regions.
- **Simon the Magician:** Warns against trying to buy God's gifts with money (simony).
- **The Ethiopian Court Official:** Represents the spread of the gospel to the ends of the earth and to all nations.
- **Isaiah 53:** The prophecy about the suffering servant is interpreted as fulfilled in Jesus Christ.

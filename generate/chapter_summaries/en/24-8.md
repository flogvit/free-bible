**Main Theme:** God announces judgment against Judah for their stubborn apostasy, while the prophet mourns the people's fate.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the shocking description of how the graves of Judah’s kings, leaders, and common people will be desecrated—their bones scattered under the sky they worshipped. God accuses the people of clinging to deceit and refusing to turn back, in contrast to migratory birds that know their seasons. The wise and the scribes have rejected the Lord’s word, while prophets and priests proclaim false peace. The chapter culminates with Jeremiah’s intense grief: "Is there no balm in Gilead?"—a desperate question about why the people cannot find healing.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Migratory Birds (v.7):** The stork, the turtledove, and the swallow know their seasons, but God’s people do not know the Lord’s justice—an image of unnatural disobedience.
- **The Scribes’ False Pen (v.8):** The scribes have distorted God’s law, so their wisdom is worthless.
- **"Peace, Peace" – But No Peace (v.11):** The false prophets trivialize the people’s wounds with empty assurances.
- **Balm in Gilead (v.22):** A rhetorical question about healing—medicine exists, but the people refuse to be healed.
- **Eyes as a Fountain of Tears (v.23):** The prophet’s deep sorrow over the fallen is expressed in the wish to weep day and night.

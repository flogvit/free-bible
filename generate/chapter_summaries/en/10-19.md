**Main Theme:** David's return to Jerusalem as king after Absalom's death, marked by reconciliation and emerging division.

**Content:** The chapter opens with David's intense grief over Absalom until Joab rebukes him and reminds him of his responsibility toward the people. David sends messengers to Judah and appoints Amasa as commander in place of Joab to win their loyalty. On the way back, he meets Shimei who asks for forgiveness, Mephibosheth who explains that Ziba deceived him, and the elderly Barzillai whom he thanks for his help. The chapter concludes with a bitter dispute between Judah and the other tribes over who has the greater right to claim the king.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **David's grief:** The king's overwhelming weeping over his rebellious son reveals the deep conflict between his role as father and his role as king.
- **Joab's rebuke:** The sharp confrontation exposes the tension between David and his military commander.
- **Shimei's pardon:** David's forgiveness of the man who cursed him demonstrates royal grace on a day of victory.
- **The dispute between Israel and Judah:** The conflict at the Jordan foreshadows the coming division of the kingdom.

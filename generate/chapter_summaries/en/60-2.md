**Main Theme:** The identity of Christians as God's chosen people and their calling to live holy lives in the world.

**Content:** Peter urges believers to put away evil and seek God's word like newborn babies. As living stones, they are built up into a spiritual house with Christ as the cornerstone—chosen by God but rejected by humans. Christians are a royal priesthood and a holy nation, called out of darkness into light. As strangers in the world, they are to live exemplary lives, submit to authorities, and endure unjust suffering following Christ's example—he who bore our sins on the cross and is the shepherd of souls.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Living stones (λίθοι ζῶντες):** Believers are built together into a spiritual temple with Christ as the cornerstone.
- **Royal priesthood (βασίλειον ἱεράτευμα):** All Christians have direct access to God and offer spiritual sacrifices.
- **Strangers and exiles (πάροικοι καὶ παρεπίδημοι):** The temporary status of Christians in the world calls them to holy living.
- **Christ's suffering as an example (ὑπογραμμός):** Christ left an example of innocent suffering that believers are to follow.

**Main Theme:** The many gifts of the Spirit serve unity in Christ's body.

**Content:** Paul teaches that no one can confess "Jesus is Lord" except by the Holy Spirit. There are different kinds of spiritual gifts, services, and workings, but the same God gives them all for the common good. By one Spirit we were all baptized into one body—whether Jew or Greek, slave or free. Just as the parts of a body are different yet mutually dependent, so it is in the church: the seemingly weaker parts are indispensable. Paul concludes by pointing to an even more excellent way.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Spiritual gifts (χαρίσματα):** Various gifts of the Spirit such as wisdom, knowledge, healing, prophecy, and speaking in tongues, given to each person for the benefit of the community.
- **Christ's body:** The church as one body with many members, where every part is necessary and no one can say to another "I do not need you."
- **One Spirit, one baptism:** The Holy Spirit unites all believers into one body across ethnic and social divides.
- **Mutual care:** The members should have the same concern for one another—if one member suffers, all suffer together; if one is honored, all rejoice with it.

**Main Theme:** God confronts His people's hypocrisy, affirms His sovereignty, and calls them out of Babylon.

**Content:** God accuses Israel of invoking His name without truth and righteousness, despite their stubborn nature. He has foretold events to prevent them from attributing these things to idols. For the sake of His name, God withholds His wrath and has purified the people in the furnace of affliction. He calls them to leave Babylon with rejoicing, reminding them of His care when He caused water to flow from the rock. The chapter concludes with the warning: "There is no peace for the wicked".

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Iron neck** (v.4): A picture of Israel's stubbornness and disobedience toward God.
- **Furnace of affliction** (v.10): God's purification of His people through trials, not to destroy them but to refine them.
- **The First and the Last** (v.12): God's sovereign identity as ruler over history from beginning to end.
- **Peace like a river** (v.18): The blessing Israel missed due to their disobedience.
- **Go out from Babylon** (v.20): A call to deliverance and new life, marked by rejoicing and testimony of God's salvation.

**Main Theme:** Jesus demonstrates his power over demons, sickness, and death through three miracles.

**Content:** In the region of the Gerasenes, Jesus frees a man possessed by demons called "Legion" and sends them into a herd of pigs that drowns. The healed man is sent out to preach in the Decapolis. Back at the Sea of Galilee, Jesus heals a woman who had been bleeding for twelve years and declares that her faith has saved her. Then he goes to the house of Jairus, a synagogue leader, and raises his dead daughter to life with the words "Talita kum."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Legion:** The name given to the demons possessing the man, showing both the extent of evil's power and Jesus' superiority.
- **Touching the cloak:** The woman's act of faith that led to her healing shows that faith opens the way for God's power.
- **"Talita kum":** Jesus' Aramaic words to the dead girl, meaning "Little girl, get up," demonstrating his authority over death.
- **Faith:** A recurring theme – Jesus highlights the importance of faith both for the woman and for Jairus.

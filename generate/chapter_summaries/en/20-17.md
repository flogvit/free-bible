**Main Theme:** The chapter offers wisdom on speech, relationships, and the significance of the heart, with the Lord as the one who tests the human inner being.

**Content:** Proverbs 17 contrasts wisdom and folly through practical life situations. Peace is valued higher than wealth accompanied by strife, and true friendship proves itself in difficult times. The chapter warns against lying, injustice, and lack of self-control, while highlighting the value of silence and a joyful heart. The Lord tests hearts, and to acquit the guilty or condemn the innocent is an abomination to Him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The crucible and the furnace (v.3):** Just as silver and gold are refined in fire, the Lord tests the human heart.
- **Water let loose (v.14):** The beginning of strife is compared to water breaking through—therefore one should stop before conflict escalates.
- **A bear robbed of her cubs (v.12):** Better to meet a raging bear than a fool in his folly—a powerful image of the danger of foolishness.
- **A joyful heart (v.22):** A joyful heart is good medicine, while a crushed spirit dries up the bones.

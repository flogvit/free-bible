**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself to the disciples after his resurrection and reinstates Peter in ministry.

**Content:** After a fruitless night of fishing, Jesus meets the disciples by the Sea of Tiberias and gives them a miraculous catch of 153 fish. During a meal that Jesus has prepared, he asks Peter three times if he loves him and entrusts him with the task of being a shepherd to his sheep. Jesus hints at how Peter will die and corrects a misunderstanding concerning the disciple whom he loved. The chapter confirms this disciple’s testimony as true.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Catch of Fish (153 fish):** Symbolizes abundance and the disciples’ mission as fishers of men under Jesus’ leadership.
- **The Meal:** As host, Jesus shows care and fellowship with the disciples after his resurrection.
- **"Do you love me?" (three times):** Peter’s threefold affirmation corresponds to his threefold denial and marks his restoration.
- **The Shepherd Commission:** Peter is given responsibility to tend Jesus’ lambs and sheep—a picture of spiritual leadership in the church.
- **"Follow me":** Jesus’ call to discipleship, regardless of another person’s fate.

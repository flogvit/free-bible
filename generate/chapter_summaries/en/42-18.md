**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about persistent prayer, humility, and what it takes to follow him into the kingdom of God.

**Content:** Jesus tells the parable of the widow and the unjust judge to encourage persistent prayer, and the parable of the Pharisee and the tax collector to warn against self-righteousness. He blesses children and shows that the kingdom of God belongs to those who receive it with childlike trust. A rich ruler asks about eternal life but goes away sorrowful when Jesus tells him to sell everything and follow him. Jesus predicts his suffering and resurrection, and heals a blind man at Jericho who calls out to him in faith.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Unjust Judge:** Illustrates how much more God will answer his chosen ones who cry out to him day and night.
- **The Pharisee and the Tax Collector:** Contrast between self-righteousness and humble repentance – whoever humbles themselves will be exalted.
- **Children:** Symbol of how one must receive the kingdom of God – with trust and dependence.
- **The Eye of a Needle:** Image of how difficult it is for the rich to enter the kingdom of God, yet what is impossible for humans is possible for God.
- **The Blind Man:** Example of persistent faith that leads to salvation and discipleship.

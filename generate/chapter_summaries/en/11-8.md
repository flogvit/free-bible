**Main Theme:** Solomon dedicates the temple with a solemn ceremony in which God's glory fills the house, followed by his great dedicatory prayer.

**Content:** King Solomon gathers Israel's leaders to bring the ark of the covenant into the Most Holy Place of the temple. When the priests leave the sanctuary, a cloud fills the temple as a sign of God's presence, so that the priests cannot serve. Solomon offers a great dedicatory prayer in which he praises God, acknowledges that even heaven cannot contain Him, and asks that God will hear prayers directed toward this place in various times of distress. The celebration lasts fourteen days with extensive sacrifices, after which the people are sent home rejoicing.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant:** Contained the two stone tablets from Sinai and was placed in the Most Holy Place under the wings of the cherubim.
- **The Cloud/The Glory of the Lord:** God's visible presence that filled the temple and confirmed that He had taken up residence there.
- **The Dedicatory Prayer:** Solomon's comprehensive prayer covering seven situations in which the people may turn toward the temple in prayer.
- **The Temple as a House of Prayer:** A place where both Israelites and foreigners can pray and be heard by God.

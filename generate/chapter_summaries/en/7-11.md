**Main Theme:** Jephthah becomes Israel's leader and defeats the Ammonites, but his unwise vow brings tragic consequences for his daughter.

**Content:** Jephthah, a skilled warrior and son of a prostitute, was rejected by his brothers but later brought back to lead Gilead against the Ammonites. He first attempted diplomacy, arguing from Israel's history and God's right to give them the land, but the king of the Ammonites would not listen. Before the battle, Jephthah made a vow to sacrifice the first person who came out of his house if he returned victorious. Israel won, but it was his only daughter who greeted him at home. After two months in the mountains, he fulfilled his vow, and it became an annual custom in Israel to remember her.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rejected Leader:** Jephthah was first rejected by his family, yet God used him to deliver Israel.
- **The Spirit of the Lord:** The Spirit came upon Jephthah and equipped him for battle (v.29).
- **The Unwise Vow:** Jephthah's vow illustrates the dangers of making hasty promises to God.
- **Daughter's Acceptance:** She accepted her father's vow and asked only for time to mourn her virginity.

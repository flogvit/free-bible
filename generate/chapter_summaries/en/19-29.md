**Main Theme:** Psalm 29 is a powerful tribute to the Lord's majesty and power, manifested through nature's violent phenomena.

**Content:** The psalm opens with an invitation to the heavenly beings to give the Lord glory and worship him in holy splendor. Then the voice of the Lord (קוֹל יְהוָה) is described seven times as it thunders over the waters, breaks the cedars of Lebanon, sends forth flames of fire, makes the wilderness of Kadesh tremble, and strips bare the forests. The psalm culminates with the Lord enthroned as eternal king over the flood, giving his people strength and blessing them with peace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The voice of the Lord (קוֹל יְהוָה):** Repeated seven times as a symbol of God's perfect power and majesty in creation.
- **Sons of God (בְּנֵי אֵלִים):** The heavenly beings invited to worship the Lord and give him glory.
- **The cedars of Lebanon:** A symbol of nature's mightiest forces, yet still broken by the voice of God.
- **The flood (מַבּוּל):** Refers to the primeval flood; God reigns as eternal king over the powers of chaos.
- **Peace (שָׁלוֹם):** The psalm's concluding blessing—God gives his people strength and peace.

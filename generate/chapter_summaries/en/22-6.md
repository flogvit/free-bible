**Main Theme:** The chapter praises the unique beauty of the beloved and the mutual belonging between the two lovers.

**Content:** The daughters of Jerusalem ask where the beloved has gone, and the woman answers that he is in his garden to gather lilies. She affirms their mutual belonging: "I am my beloved's and my beloved is mine." The man praises her beauty and compares her to Tirzah and Jerusalem. Among sixty queens and eighty concubines, she is the only one, unique and perfect, praised by all who see her.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Garden:** A symbol of intimacy and love; the beloved goes down to his garden to gather lilies and see if the vines have blossomed.
- **Tirzah and Jerusalem:** Cities representing beauty and majesty; the woman is compared to these.
- **"She is unique":** The woman's uniqueness among many women underscores her incomparable value to the beloved.
- **Dawn, moon, and sun:** Images of the woman's radiant beauty that surpasses everything else.

**Main Theme:** Saul's conversion and Peter's miracles demonstrate the power of the gospel to transform persecutors and confirm faith through signs.

**Content:** Saul, who is persecuting Christians, meets the risen Jesus on the road to Damascus and becomes blind. Ananias heals him, and Saul is baptized and immediately begins preaching that Jesus is God's Son. After plots against his life, he flees from Damascus, and Barnabas vouches for him before the apostles in Jerusalem. The church in Judea, Galilee, and Samaria enjoys peace and grows. Peter heals the paralyzed Aeneas in Lydda and raises Tabitha (Dorcas) from the dead in Joppa, leading many to faith.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The light from heaven:** God's intervention that stops Saul and reveals Jesus as Lord.
- **Chosen instrument (σκεῦος ἐκλογῆς):** God calls Saul to carry Jesus' name before Gentiles, kings, and the people of Israel.
- **Ananias laying hands on him:** Symbolizes healing, the gift of the Holy Spirit, and inclusion in the community.
- **Tabitha's resurrection:** Demonstrates the apostles' authority and that the gospel gives life even over death.

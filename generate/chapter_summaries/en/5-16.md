**Main Theme:** The chapter gives instructions for the three annual pilgrimage festivals and for righteous administration of justice in Israel.

**Content:** Israel is to celebrate three festivals at the place the Lord chooses: Passover/Unleavened Bread in the month of Abib, commemorating the exodus from Egypt; the Festival of Weeks seven weeks later with voluntary offerings; and the Festival of Tabernacles for seven days at the harvest. All men must appear before the Lord three times a year, and no one is to come empty-handed. Judges and officials are to be appointed in all towns to judge fairly without taking bribes. Asherah poles and sacred stones beside altars are forbidden.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The place the Lord chooses:** A repeated expression pointing to centralized worship at one holy site.
- **Justice, justice you shall pursue:** Double emphasis on righteousness as the foundation for life in the land.
- **Unleavened bread (matsot):** Symbol of the hasty departure from Egypt and a reminder of deliverance.
- **Rejoicing before the Lord:** The festivals include the entire household, Levites, foreigners, fatherless children, and widows in the celebration.

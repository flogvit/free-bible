**Main Theme:** The chapter exhorts believers to persevere in faith by looking to Jesus as our example, receiving God's disciplining love, and drawing near to him with reverence under the new covenant.

**Content:** Surrounded by a great cloud of witnesses, we are called to lay aside every sin and run the race marked out for us with our eyes fixed on Jesus, the founder and perfecter of faith. God's discipline is presented as evidence of his fatherly love, leading to holiness and the fruit of righteousness. The chapter contrasts the terrifying revelation at Sinai with the glory of the new covenant on Mount Zion, the heavenly Jerusalem. We are urged to seek peace and sanctification, not to reject him who speaks from heaven, for we receive a kingdom that cannot be shaken.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cloud of witnesses:** The faithful witnesses from chapter 11 who surround us and inspire perseverance.
- **Founder and perfecter of faith (ἀρχηγὸν καὶ τελειωτήν):** Jesus, who both begins and completes our faith, our ultimate example.
- **God's discipline (παιδεία):** God's loving correction as proof of his fatherly love and means to sanctification.
- **Sinai vs. Zion:** The contrast between the fear-inducing revelation of the old covenant and the gracious access to God under the new covenant.
- **A kingdom that cannot be shaken (βασιλείαν ἀσάλευτον):** The eternal kingdom of God that remains when everything temporary is removed.

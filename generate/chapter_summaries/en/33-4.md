**Main Theme:** Prophecy concerning Zion’s future exaltation, universal peace under the Lord’s rule, and Israel’s restoration after suffering.

**Content:** In the last days, the mountain of the Lord’s temple will be raised high above all hills, and nations will stream to it to learn God’s ways. The Lord will judge between the peoples, and war will cease—swords will become plowshares. Before this glory arrives, Israel must endure suffering and exile to Babylon, but the Lord will gather the lame and the exiles and reign as king from Zion forever.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Mountain of the Lord (הַר בֵּית־יְהוָה):** The temple mountain that will be exalted above all mountains as the center for worship and teaching.
- **Swords into plowshares:** A symbol of total transformation from war to peace when God judges between nations.
- **Vineyards and fig trees:** Symbols of security and prosperity—each person shall sit in peace without fear.
- **The lame remnant (הַצֹּלֵעָה):** God’s promise to gather the weak and exiled and make them into a mighty people.

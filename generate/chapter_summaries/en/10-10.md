**Main Theme:** David’s goodwill is rejected and leads to war against the Ammonites and Arameans, ending in an Israelite victory.

**Content:** When Nahash king of the Ammonites dies, David sends messengers to comfort his son Hanun, because Nahash had shown kindness to David. Hanun’s advisors suspect espionage, and Hanun humiliates the messengers by shaving off half their beards and cutting their garments. The Ammonites hire Aramean mercenaries for defense, but Joab and Abishai defeat them. The Arameans regroup under Hadadezer, but David himself leads Israel to victory, and the Arameans make peace and no longer dare to help the Ammonites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hesed (kindness/loyalty):** David wishes to repay the kindness Nahash showed him, an expression of covenant loyalty.
- **Shaving the beard:** Shaving the beard was a serious insult in ancient times, a symbol of shame and humiliation.
- **Mercenaries:** The Ammonites hire 33,000 Aramean soldiers, showing the scale of the conflict.
- **"The Lord will do what is good in his eyes":** Joab’s words (v.12) express trust in God’s sovereignty over the outcome of war.

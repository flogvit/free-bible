**Main Theme:** God gives Israel the Ten Commandments and foundational laws for worship at Sinai.

**Content:** God speaks directly to Israel and gives them the Ten Commandments, covering their relationship with God (no other gods, no idols, not misusing God's name, keeping the Sabbath) and their relationship with one another (honor parents, do not murder, do not commit adultery, do not steal, do not give false testimony, do not covet). The people are overwhelmed by fear at God's powerful presence and ask Moses to be their mediator. The chapter concludes with instructions on how the altar should be built.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Ten Commandments (עֲשֶׂרֶת הַדְּבָרִים):** God's fundamental covenant requirements that define Israel's relationship with Him and with each other.
- **The Sabbath:** The seventh day set apart for rest, grounded in God's creation.
- **A Jealous God (אֵל קַנָּא):** God demands undivided loyalty and does not tolerate idolatry.
- **Theophany:** God's powerful revelation accompanied by thunder, lightning, and smoke that causes the people to fear.
- **Altar of earth/unhewn stones:** Simple worship without human ornamentation, honoring God's holiness.

**Main Theme:** Jesus comforts his disciples before his departure by revealing himself as the way to the Father and promising the coming of the Holy Spirit.

**Content:** Jesus prepares his disciples for his leaving by assuring them that he is going to prepare a place for them in the Father's house. In response to questions from Thomas and Philip, he reveals his unity with the Father: "I am the way, the truth, and the life." He promises to send the Advocate (παράκλητος), the Spirit of Truth, who will be with them forever and teach them all things. Jesus links love for him with obedience to his commands, and concludes by giving them his peace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Father's house (μοναί):** The many rooms symbolize the eternal dwelling that Jesus is preparing for his followers.
- **The way, the truth, and the life:** Jesus' threefold self-revelation as the only access to the Father.
- **The Advocate (παράκλητος):** The Holy Spirit who will guide, teach, and remind the disciples of Jesus' words.
- **Jesus' peace (εἰρήνη):** An inner peace that is qualitatively different from what the world can give.

**Main Theme:** Nehemiah receives permission from King Artaxerxes to rebuild the walls of Jerusalem and mobilizes the people for the work.

**Content:** Nehemiah, who serves as cupbearer to King Artaxerxes, shares his grief over the destruction of Jerusalem. After praying to God, he obtains the king’s permission, letters, and resources for the journey. In Jerusalem, he inspects the walls at night, then gathers the people and tells them about God’s gracious hand and the king’s support. The people respond with a willingness to build but face opposition and mockery from Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God’s gracious hand:** Nehemiah attributes the king’s goodwill to God’s guidance and care (vv. 8, 18).
- **Nighttime inspection:** Nehemiah secretly examines the condition of the walls before taking action.
- **"Let us build":** The people’s unified response to Nehemiah’s challenge demonstrates the power of community.
- **Opposition:** Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem represent external hostility toward God’s people and their work.

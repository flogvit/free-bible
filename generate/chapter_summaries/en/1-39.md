**Main Theme:** God is with Joseph in adversity—both as a slave and as a prisoner in Egypt.

**Content:** Joseph is sold to Potiphar, captain of Pharaoh's bodyguard, and the LORD causes him to succeed in everything he does. Potiphar puts him in charge of his entire household. When Potiphar's wife tries to seduce Joseph, he refuses her out of loyalty to his master and to God. She falsely accuses him, and Joseph is thrown into prison. But even there the LORD is with him, and the prison warden gives him responsibility for all the prisoners.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The LORD was with Joseph** – This phrase is repeated four times and shows that God's presence is the source of Joseph's success.
- **The garment left behind** – Joseph flees from sin and leaves his garment, which is used as false evidence against him.
- **Temptation and faithfulness** – Joseph refuses to sin against God, even when it costs him his freedom.
- **From household manager to prison overseer** – The pattern repeats: Joseph is given responsibility and succeeds because God is with him.

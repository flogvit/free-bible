**Main Theme:** God meets the exhausted prophet Elijah with care and a renewed mission.

**Content:** After his victory over the prophets of Baal, Elijah flees from Queen Jezebel's threats and asks to die. God sends an angel who gives him food, and Elijah travels forty days to Mount Horeb. There, God reveals Himself not in wind, earthquake, or fire, but in a still, small voice. God gives Elijah new missions: to anoint Hazael, Jehu, and Elisha, and tells him that 7,000 in Israel remain faithful.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Still, Small Voice:** God reveals Himself not in the dramatic but in the quiet—a vital lesson on how God meets people.
- **Forty Days:** Elijah's journey to Horeb mirrors Moses' experience and marks a spiritual preparation.
- **The Mantle (אַדֶּרֶת):** Elijah casts his mantle over Elisha as a sign of calling and the transfer of the prophetic office.
- **The 7,000:** God's remnant of faithful ones who have not bowed to Baal—a sign that Elijah is not alone.

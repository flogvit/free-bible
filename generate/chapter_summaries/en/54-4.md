**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts Timothy to stand firm against false teaching and to be an example in service and conduct.

**Content:** The chapter warns that some will fall away from the faith, following deceiving spirits and doctrines of demons, including prohibitions on marriage and certain foods. Paul emphasizes that everything God has created is good and is made holy through God's word and prayer. Timothy is urged to train himself in godliness, be an example for believers, and faithfully steward the spiritual gift he received through the laying on of hands by the elders.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Godliness (εὐσέβεια):** Spiritual training that holds promise for both this life and the coming one, unlike physical exercise.
- **Example (τύπος):** Timothy is to be a model in speech, conduct, love, faith, and purity.
- **Spiritual Gift (χάρισμα):** The spiritual gift Timothy received through prophecy and the laying on of hands, which he must not neglect.
- **The Living God:** Savior of all people, especially believers—the foundation for the labor and hope of ministry.

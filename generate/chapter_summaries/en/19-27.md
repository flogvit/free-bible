**Main Theme:** A declaration of trust in God as light, salvation, and protection, combined with a longing for His presence.

**Content:** David proclaims his unwavering trust in the Lord amid hostile circumstances. His greatest desire is to dwell in God's house and behold the beauty of the Lord. In prayer he seeks God's face and asks for guidance and protection against enemies and false witnesses. The psalm concludes with an exhortation to wait on the Lord with strength and courage.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Light and salvation (v.1):** The Lord as the source of spiritual enlightenment and deliverance from danger.
- **God's house/tent (vv.4–5):** The temple as a place for fellowship with God and refuge in difficult times.
- **The rock (v.5):** God as a firm and exalted stronghold.
- **God's face (vv.8–9):** Seeking God's presence and favor in prayer.

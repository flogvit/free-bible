**Main Theme:** The prophet Ezekiel receives a powerful vision of God's glory by the Kebar River in Babylon.

**Content:** In the fifth year of King Jehoiachin's exile, the heavens opened to Ezekiel, and he saw a vision of God. From the north came a storm cloud with fire and brilliance, and within it were four living creatures with human-like forms, but each having four faces (human, lion, ox, eagle) and four wings. Beside them were wheels within wheels, full of eyes. Above them was a crystal-like expanse, and above this a sapphire-blue throne with a human figure surrounded by radiant light like a rainbow. This was the vision of the Lord's glory, and Ezekiel fell on his face.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Four Living Creatures (חַיּוֹת):** Heavenly beings with four faces and wings that carry God's throne and symbolize His omnipresence.
- **The Wheels (אוֹפַנִּים):** Mysterious wheels within wheels, full of eyes, showing God's ability to move in every direction.
- **The Glory of the Lord (כְבוֹד־יְהוָה):** God's visible presence, described with fire, light, and the colors of the rainbow.
- **Throne of Sapphire:** God's heavenly throne above the expanse, demonstrating His sovereignty even during the exile.

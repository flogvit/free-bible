**Main Theme:** A thanksgiving psalm that invites all the earth to praise the Lord with joy.

**Content:** The psalm calls all the earth to shout for joy before the Lord, serve him with gladness, and come into his presence with singing. It confesses that the Lord is God—he made us, and we are his people, the sheep of his pasture. The people are urged to enter his gates with thanksgiving and his courts with praise, because the Lord is good; his steadfast love endures forever, and his faithfulness to all generations.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Thanksgiving (תוֹדָה):** This psalm was written for use at a thank offering or thanksgiving service in the temple.
- **Joy and Shouting for Joy:** The worship is marked by joy (שִׂמְחָה) and shouting for joy (רְנָנָה)—not duty, but celebration.
- **Sheep of His Pasture:** A shepherd image expressing God's care and the people's belonging to him.
- **Gates and Courts:** Refers to the temple area where the people gather for worship.
- **Everlasting Steadfast Love (חֶסֶד):** God's faithful, covenantal love that lasts forever.

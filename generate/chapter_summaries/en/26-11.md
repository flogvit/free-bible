**Main Theme:** Judgment on the leaders of Jerusalem and the promise of spiritual renewal for the exiles.

**Content:** Ezekiel sees twenty-five leaders at the temple gate giving evil counsel, feeling secure like meat in a pot. God reverses the image: those killed are the meat, but the leaders will be led out and judged at Israel's borders. Pelatjah dies while Ezekiel is prophesying. God promises the exiles to be their "little sanctuary," gather them back, and give them a new heart of flesh instead of stone. The glory of God leaves the city and stops on the Mount of Olives.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Pot and Meat (vv. 3, 7, 11):** The leaders' false sense of security – they believe the city protects them, but God shows they will be led out.
- **New Heart and New Spirit (v. 19):** God's promise of inner renewal that enables the people to follow His commands.
- **Little Sanctuary (v. 16):** God Himself will be a refuge for the exiles in foreign lands.
- **The Glory of God Leaves the City (vv. 22–23):** The Lord's presence withdraws from the unclean Jerusalem.

**Main Theme:** King Asa’s betrayal of God by relying on human help rather than the Lord.

**Content:** When Israel’s king Baasha threatened Judah, Asa sought an alliance with Ben-Hadad, king of Aram, instead of trusting in God. The prophet Hanani rebuked him and warned of coming wars. Asa reacted with anger, imprisoned the prophet, and oppressed the people. In his illness, he turned to physicians rather than to the Lord, and died after 41 years of reign.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ramah:** The fortified city that Baasha built to block Judah’s borders, which triggered the conflict.
- **The eyes of the Lord:** God watches over all the earth to strengthen those whose hearts are fully devoted to him (v. 9).
- **Prison (mahpeket):** Asa imprisoned the prophet Hanani, demonstrating his harshness toward God’s word.
- **Physicians:** A symbol of Asa’s continued reliance on humans rather than on God, even in sickness.

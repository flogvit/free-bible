**Main Theme:** Haman plans the extermination of the Jews after Mordecai refuses to bow down before him.

**Content:** King Ahasuerus elevates Haman above all other princes, and everyone at the king's gate bows down to him—except for the Jew Mordecai. Filled with rage, Haman decides not only to destroy Mordecai but to exterminate all the Jews throughout the kingdom. He casts pur (lots), which fall on the 13th of Adar, and obtains the king's authority and signet ring to issue the decree. While the king and Haman sit drinking, the city of Susa is in confusion.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Haman the Agagite:** Haman's lineage connects him to Amalek, an ancient enemy of Israel.
- **Pur (lots):** The casting of lots determines the date for the massacre and later gives its name to the festival of Purim.
- **The king's signet ring:** Symbolizes full royal authority granted to Haman to carry out his plan.
- **Susa in confusion:** The contrast between the rulers' indifference and the city's despair highlights the brutality of the decree.

**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts wisdom and folly, especially through the power of words and the Lord's nearness to the righteous.

**Content:** Proverbs 15 emphasizes the power of the tongue—a gentle answer turns away wrath, and a healing tongue is like the tree of life. The eyes of the Lord are everywhere; he is close to the righteous but far from the wicked. The chapter also highlights the value of discipline and correction: whoever listens to reproof gains wisdom. Finally, we learn that reverence for God is instruction in wisdom, and humility comes before honor.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Healing tongue (מַרְפֵּא לָשׁוֹן):** Gentle and truthful words bring life and health, like the tree of life.
- **The eyes of the Lord:** God sees everything—both evil and good—and knows the human heart.
- **Discipline/Correction (מוּסָר/תּוֹכַחַת):** Accepting correction is the path to wisdom and life.
- **Reverence for God (יִרְאַת יְהוָה):** The foundation of wisdom and right living.
- **Humility (עֲנָוָה):** The prerequisite for honor—the humble will be exalted.

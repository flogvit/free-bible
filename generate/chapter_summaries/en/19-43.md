**Main Theme:** A prayer for justice, deliverance, and God's guidance back to His sanctuary.

**Content:** The psalmist cries out to God to defend his cause against a godless people and deceitful individuals, while also asking why God has rejected him. He asks God to send forth His light and truth to lead him to the holy mountain and God's dwelling place, where he will praise God with the harp at the altar. The psalm concludes with an exhortation to himself to hope in God rather than be discouraged.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Light and Truth (אוֹר וֶאֱמֶת):** God's guiding attributes that the psalmist asks to be led by.
- **The Holy Mountain (הַר־קָדְשְׁךָ):** Zion, the place of God's presence and worship.
- **The Altar (מִזְבַּח):** The goal of the psalmist's longing – meeting with God in worship and joy.
- **Why are you cast down, O my soul?:** A repeated refrain (also in Psalm 42) expressing inner struggle and hope.

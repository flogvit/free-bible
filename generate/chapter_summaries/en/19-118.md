**Main Theme:** A festival psalm that praises God’s eternal faithfulness and celebrates his deliverance from the threat of death.

**Content:** The psalm opens and closes with the exhortation, "Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; his love endures forever" (vv. 1, 29). The believer testifies to how God answered in distress and rescued from death (vv. 5–18). The psalm culminates in a processional liturgy at the temple with a prayer for deliverance ("Hosanna") and a blessing on the one who comes in the Lord’s name (vv. 25–26).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord’s Faithfulness (חֶסֶד):** God’s enduring, covenantal love, repeated as a refrain throughout the psalm.
- **The Cornerstone (v. 22):** The stone that the builders rejected became the chief cornerstone—a picture of God’s vindication of the despised, applied to Christ in the NT.
- **"This is the day the Lord has made" (v. 24):** A celebration of God’s saving act, used liturgically concerning the resurrection.
- **Hosanna (v. 25):** A Hebrew cry for help meaning "Save now!"—became the acclamation to Jesus on Palm Sunday.

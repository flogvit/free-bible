**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts believers to spiritual alertness in light of the Lord's day and gives practical instructions for church life.

**Content:** The Lord's day will come unexpectedly like a thief in the night, but believers are "children of light" and should be awake and sober, clothed with the breastplate of faith and love and the helmet of the hope of salvation. Paul gives short, concrete exhortations: respect your leaders, correct those who are disorderly, comfort the discouraged, help the weak, be patient, rejoice always, pray without ceasing, give thanks in all circumstances. They should not quench the Spirit or despise prophecies, but test everything and hold fast to what is good. The chapter concludes with a prayer that the God of peace may sanctify them completely.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Thief in the night:** The Lord's day comes suddenly and unexpectedly upon those who live in darkness.
- **Children of light:** Believers belong to the light and the day, not to darkness, and should live accordingly.
- **Spiritual armor:** The breastplate of faith and love and the helmet of the hope of salvation protect believers.
- **Pray without ceasing:** One of several short exhortations describing the fundamental attitude of the Christian life.
- **Do not quench the Spirit:** An exhortation to remain open to the Spirit's work and prophetic speech in the church.

**Main Theme:** The chapter gives laws concerning pure worship, the administration of justice, and royal leadership in Israel.

**Content:** Offerings to the Lord must be without defect. Anyone who worships other gods or the host of heaven shall be stoned based on the testimony of two or three witnesses, with the witnesses themselves casting the first stones. Difficult legal cases are to be brought to the priests and the judge at the place the Lord chooses, and their decision is binding. The king must be a brother from among the people; he must not accumulate horses, wives, or wealth, but should write out his own copy of God's law and read it daily in order to learn humility and obedience.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Blameless Offering:** Offerings must be without defect, for defective sacrifices are an abomination to the Lord.
- **Two or Three Witnesses:** No one can be sentenced to death on the testimony of only one witness—the witnesses themselves must cast the first stones.
- **Central Court:** Difficult cases are to be decided by priests and a judge at the place the Lord chooses, with authority from God.
- **The King's Scroll of the Law:** The king is to write his own copy of the law and read it daily in order to maintain reverence for God and humility.

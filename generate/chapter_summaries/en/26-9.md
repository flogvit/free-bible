**Main Theme:** God’s judgment on Jerusalem, where the righteous are marked for preservation while the rest are punished.

**Content:** In a vision, Ezekiel sees six men with weapons of destruction and one man clothed in linen carrying writing equipment. The man in linen is instructed to place a mark on the foreheads of all who mourn over Jerusalem’s sins. The six men are then to kill everyone without the mark—without mercy, regardless of age or gender—and begin at the sanctuary. God explains that the punishment results from the great sin of Israel and Judah, and finally, the man in linen reports that the task has been completed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Taw Mark:** A protective mark on the forehead that distinguishes the righteous from the wicked.
- **The Man in Linen:** A heavenly figure with writing tools who represents God’s mercy amid judgment.
- **The Six Men:** God’s instruments of punishment who carry out judgment against Jerusalem.
- **The Sanctuary:** Judgment begins at God’s house, a sign of how serious the apostasy is.

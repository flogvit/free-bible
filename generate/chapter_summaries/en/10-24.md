**Main Theme:** David's census leads to God's judgment, but ends with grace and the establishment of a place of sacrifice.

**Content:** The Lord incited David to take a census of Israel and Judah. Afterwards, David was convicted by his conscience and confessed his sin. The prophet Gad gave him a choice among three punishments, and David chose to fall into the Lord's hand rather than into human hands. A plague killed 70,000 people, but the Lord stopped the angel at the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite. David bought the site, built an altar, offered sacrifices, and the plague ceased.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Census:** David's action that triggered God's judgment—an expression of pride or lack of trust in God.
- **The Three Punishments:** Famine, war, or plague—David chooses the plague because God's mercy is great.
- **Araunah's Threshing Floor:** The place where the angel stopped and David built an altar—traditionally linked to the future location of the temple.
- **The Altar:** A symbol of atonement and worship—David refuses to offer anything that cost him nothing.

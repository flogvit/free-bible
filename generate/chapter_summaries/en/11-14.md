**Main Theme:** God’s judgment on the house of Jeroboam and Rehoboam’s failing rule in Judah.

**Content:** Jeroboam sends his disguised wife to the blind prophet Ahijah to inquire about their sick son Abijah. Ahijah recognizes her and prophesies that Jeroboam’s house will be wiped out because of idolatry, and that the son will die—but as the only one in the family to receive a honorable burial, since something good was found in him. In Judah, Rehoboam leads the people into sin with high places and Asherah poles, and Pharaoh Shishak plundered the temple treasures. Both kings die and are succeeded by their sons.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Disguise:** Jeroboam’s wife disguises herself, but God reveals the truth to the prophet—people cannot hide from God.
- **High Places and Asherah Poles:** Symbols of Judah’s apostasy and idolatry under Rehoboam.
- **Shields of Gold:** Solomon’s golden shields are plundered and replaced with bronze—an image of the kingdom’s spiritual and material decline.
- **Reed Swaying:** The prophecy that Israel will sway like a reed in water and be uprooted from the land (v.15).

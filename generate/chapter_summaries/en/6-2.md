**Main Theme:** Rahab hides the Israelite spies and confesses her faith in the LORD, leading to a covenant of protection for her and her family.

**Content:** Joshua sends two spies from Shittim to explore Jericho. They stay with Rahab, a prostitute, who hides them from the king's men. She confesses that the LORD is God in heaven above and on earth below, and that the entire land has lost heart because of Israel's victories. The spies promise to spare her and her family if she ties a scarlet cord in her window and keeps their agreement secret. The spies return safely and report to Joshua that the LORD has given the land into their hands.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rahab:** A Canaanite prostitute who shows faith in Israel's God and is saved along with her family.
- **The Scarlet Cord (תִּקְוַת הַשָּׁנִי):** The sign of the covenant between Rahab and the spies, which was to be hung in the window as a mark of protection.
- **Confession of Faith:** Rahab's acknowledgment that the LORD is God in heaven above and on earth below shows that the report of God's mighty acts had reached Canaan.
- **Chesed (חֶסֶד):** Steadfast love/loyalty – Rahab asks for kindness in return for the mercy she showed the spies.

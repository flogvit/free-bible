**Main Theme:** Wisdom is shown in speech, work, and conduct—and leads to life, while folly leads to ruin.

**Content:** The chapter contrasts the wise person and the foolish one in several areas of life: The wise accept guidance, guard their tongue, and work diligently, while the scoffer rejects correction. Righteousness preserves those who live uprightly, but wickedness brings sinners to destruction. Wealth gathered hastily disappears, but whoever gathers little by little gains abundance. Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise, but whoever associates with fools suffers harm.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tree of life (v.12):** A fulfilled longing is like the tree of life—a picture of blessing and vitality.
- **Light and lamp (v.9):** The light of the righteous burns brightly, while the lamp of the wicked goes out—contrasting what endures with what perishes.
- **Fountain of life (v.14):** The teaching of the wise is a fountain of life that helps one avoid the snares of death.
- **Discipline and love (v.24):** Whoever spares the rod hates their child—true love includes discipline.

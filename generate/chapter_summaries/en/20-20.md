**Main Theme:** The chapter offers wisdom words about righteous living, honesty, and trust in the Lord's guidance.

**Content:** Proverbs warns against wine and drunkenness, laziness, dishonest business practices, and revenge. The king is portrayed as the guardian of justice who separates out evil. No one can claim to have a pure heart, but the Lord's lamp—the human spirit—examines the innermost being. A person's steps are directed by the Lord, and the wise trust in Him rather than taking revenge themselves.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Wine as a mocker (v.1):** Intoxication leads to poor judgment and foolish behavior.
- **Deep waters (v.5):** The plans within a person's heart are like deep waters, but the understanding can draw them out.
- **False scales (vv.10, 23):** Dishonest weights and measures are an abomination to the Lord—honesty in business is required.
- **The Lord's lamp (v.27):** The human spirit is the Lord's lamp that searches the innermost parts of the heart.

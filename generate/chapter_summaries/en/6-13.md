**Main Theme:** God commands Joshua to distribute the remaining land, and the chapter describes the territories east of the Jordan that Moses had previously assigned.

**Content:** Although Joshua is old, much land remains to be taken. God lists the remaining areas and commands Joshua to allocate the land west of the Jordan to nine and a half tribes. The chapter then details the territories Moses gave to Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh east of the Jordan. The tribe of Levi received no land inheritance, for the Lord himself is their inheritance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Remaining Land:** Areas such as the cities of the Philistines and Lebanon that had not yet been conquered.
- **Inheritance (nachala):** The territory each tribe received as God's gift and permanent possession.
- **East of the Jordan:** The territories of Reuben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh, assigned by Moses before his death.
- **Levi's Inheritance:** Levi received no land because the Lord's offerings—and the Lord himself—were their inheritance.

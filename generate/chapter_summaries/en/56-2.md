**Main Theme:** Paul gives Titus instructions about sound doctrine and Christian living for various groups in the congregation, grounded in God's saving grace.

**Content:** Titus is to teach older men, older women, young women, young men, and slaves self-control, godliness, and good works, while also being an example himself. God's saving grace has been revealed to all people and trains us to live righteously as we await the glorious appearing of Jesus Christ. Christ gave himself to redeem us from lawlessness and to cleanse for himself a people who are zealous for good works.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sound doctrine (ὑγιαίνουσα διδασκαλία):** The true, life-giving teaching that shapes Christian life and character.
- **Self-control (σωφροσύνη):** A consistent virtue required of all groups—sound judgment and moderation.
- **God's grace (χάρις τοῦ θεοῦ):** God's saving grace that has been revealed and trains believers.
- **The blessed hope (μακαρία ἐλπίς):** The expectation of the glorious appearing of Jesus Christ, which motivates a holy life.
- **A people for his own possession (λαὸς περιούσιος):** Believers whom Christ has cleansed for himself, eager to do good works.

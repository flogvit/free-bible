**Main Theme:** Christ is the center of creation and salvation, and through him God has reconciled all things to himself.

**Content:** Paul and Timothy greet the church in Colossae and thank God for their faith, love, and hope, which they learned from Epaphras. The chapter contains a powerful hymn that proclaims Christ as the image of the invisible God, the firstborn over all creation, and the head of the church—all things were created through him and hold together in him. Through Christ's blood on the cross, all things have been reconciled to God, including the Colossians who were once enemies. Paul describes his ministry: to proclaim the mystery now revealed—Christ in you, the hope of glory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Firstborn (πρωτότοκος):** Christ is the firstborn over all creation and the firstborn from the dead—he holds the preeminent place in everything.
- **Image of God (εἰκών):** Christ is the visible representation of the invisible God, the perfect revelation of God's nature.
- **Reconciliation (ἀποκαταλλάσσω):** Through the blood of the cross, God has reconciled all things to himself, both on earth and in heaven.
- **Mystery (μυστήριον):** The secret that was hidden for ages but is now revealed: Christ in you, the hope of glory.

**Main Theme:** Proverbs 18 deals with the power of words and humanity's relationship with others—in speech, friendship, and justice.

**Content:** The chapter warns against foolishness and pride, emphasizing that our words hold power over life and death. The name of the Lord is a strong tower where the righteous find safety, in contrast to the rich who trust in their wealth. The chapter also highlights the value of marriage as a gift from the Lord, and that a faithful friend can be closer than a brother.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Strong Tower (v.10):** The name of the Lord is a secure fortress where the righteous can seek refuge and protection.
- **The Power of the Tongue (v.21):** Death and life are in the power of the tongue—our words carry great consequences.
- **Deep Water/Spring (v.4):** Words of wisdom flow like a fresh stream from the mouth of the wise.
- **A Friend Closer Than a Brother (v.24):** True friendship can be stronger than family ties.

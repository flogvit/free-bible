**Main Theme:** An exhortation to live as children of light in love, purity, and wisdom, with marriage as a picture of Christ and the church.

**Content:** Paul urges believers to imitate God and walk in love, just as Christ gave himself as an offering. They were once darkness, but are now light in the Lord and should live accordingly—by shunning impurity and greed, and instead being filled with the Spirit through songs of praise and thanksgiving. The chapter concludes with teaching on marriage: husbands are to love their wives as Christ loved the church, and the relationship between husband and wife reflects the mystery of Christ and the church.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Children of light (τέκνα φωτός):** Believers have moved from darkness into light and should bear the fruit of light: goodness, righteousness, and truth.
- **Be filled with the Spirit (πληροῦσθε ἐν πνεύματι):** In contrast to drunkenness, believers are to be filled with the Spirit, expressed through songs of praise and thanksgiving.
- **Christ and the church (Χριστὸν καὶ τὴν ἐκκλησίαν):** Marriage is a great mystery that points to the relationship between Christ and his church.
- **Make the most of every opportunity (ἐξαγοραζόμενοι τὸν καιρόν):** An exhortation to live wisely and make good use of time, because the days are evil.

**Main Theme:** God's judgment on Moab due to their pride and exaltation against the Lord, with a concluding promise of restoration.

**Content:** The prophecy declares total destruction over Moab's cities and people. Moab has lived securely and undisturbed like wine that has never been poured from one vessel to another, but now they will be emptied out and crushed. The god Chemosh and his priests will be led away into captivity, and Moab's pride and boasting will be put to shame. The chapter ends with a promise that the Lord will turn Moab's fate "in the last days."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Chemosh:** Moab's national god who will go into captivity along with his priests—a sign of total defeat.
- **Wine on its dregs (v.11-12):** Moab is compared to wine that has never been poured from one vessel to another—they have remained undisturbed, but now they will be emptied out.
- **Pride/arrogance (v.29):** Moab's boundless arrogance and self-exaltation are the main cause of judgment.
- **Eagle (v.40):** The enemy coming against Moab is described as an eagle spreading its wings—an image of inevitable judgment.

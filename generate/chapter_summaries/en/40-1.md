**Main Theme:** The origin and birth of Jesus Christ as the fulfillment of God's promises to Israel.

**Content:** The chapter opens with Jesus' genealogy from Abraham through David to Joseph, Mary's husband, structured in three periods of fourteen generations each. It then recounts the story of Jesus' birth: Mary became pregnant by the Holy Spirit while she was engaged to Joseph. An angel of the Lord revealed to Joseph in a dream that the child was conceived by the Spirit and that he should be named Jesus because he would save his people from their sins. Joseph obeyed and took Mary as his wife.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Son of David:** Jesus is the promised King from the line of David, heir to Israel's throne.
- **Virgin Birth:** Mary conceived by the Holy Spirit, a supernatural act fulfilling the prophecy in Isaiah 7:14.
- **Jesus (Ἰησοῦς):** The name means "the Lord saves" and points to his mission: to save his people from their sins.
- **Immanuel:** The prophetic name meaning "God with us," revealing Jesus' divine nature.

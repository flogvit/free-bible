**Main Theme:** An angel shows John the judgment upon the great harlot Babylon and explains the mystery of her and the beast on which she rides.

**Content:** John is carried away in the Spirit to a wilderness where he sees a woman clothed in purple and scarlet, sitting on a scarlet beast with seven heads and ten horns. She is drunk with the blood of the saints and bears the name "Babylon the Great, mother of harlots." The angel explains that the seven heads are seven mountains and seven kings, and the ten horns are ten kings who will give their power to the beast. These will make war against the Lamb, but the Lamb will overcome them. Finally, the beast and the ten kings will turn against the harlot and destroy her.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Great Harlot:** Identified as the great city that rules over the kings of the earth, drunk with the blood of the saints.
- **The Beast:** Was, is not, and is about to ascend from the abyss – symbolizing a power destined for destruction.
- **The Seven Heads:** Represent both seven mountains and seven kings, five of whom have fallen, one is, and one is yet to come.
- **The Ten Horns:** Ten kings who receive authority together with the beast for one hour, but who will ultimately destroy the harlot.
- **The Lamb:** Lord of lords and King of kings who will overcome the beast and the kings.

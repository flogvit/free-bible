**Main Theme:** Jacob arrives at Laban's house, marries Leah and Rachel, and Leah bears four sons.

**Content:** Jacob comes to Haran and meets Rachel at a well, where he rolls away the stone and waters her flock. He agrees with Laban to serve seven years for Rachel, but is deceived and receives Leah on the wedding night. Jacob must serve another seven years to get Rachel. The Lord sees that Leah is unloved and gives her four sons: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, while Rachel remains childless.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Well:** A meeting place where Jacob finds his future wife, a common motif in the patriarchal narratives.
- **Seven Years of Service:** Jacob's bride price for Rachel, which seemed like only a few days because of his love for her.
- **The Deception:** Laban substitutes Leah for Rachel, an ironic reflection of Jacob's own deception against Esau.
- **The Unloved Leah:** God sees her pain and blesses her with children, while Rachel is barren.

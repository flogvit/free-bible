**Main Theme:** David is anointed king over all Israel, conquers Jerusalem, and defeats the Philistines with God's help.

**Content:** All the tribes of Israel come to David in Hebron and acknowledge him as their leader. The elders make a covenant with him before the Lord, and David is anointed king over all Israel. He captures Jerusalem from the Jebusites and makes the fortress of Zion his capital, the City of David, where King Hiram of Tyre builds him a palace. The Philistines attack twice, but David seeks the Lord's guidance and wins both battles.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **City of David (עִיר דָּוִד):** Jerusalem/the fortress of Zion that David captured and made his capital and center of power.
- **Anointing (מָשַׁח):** The ritual act by which the elders confirmed David as God's chosen king over all Israel.
- **Baal-perazim (בַּעַל פְּרָצִים):** "Lord of Breakthroughs" – the place where the Lord broke through the enemies like a flood.
- **Seeking the Lord (שָׁאַל בַּיהוָה):** David's practice of asking God before taking action, which secured victory in both battles against the Philistines.

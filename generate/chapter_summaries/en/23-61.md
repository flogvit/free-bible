**Main Theme:** God’s anointed messenger brings freedom, comfort, and restoration for God’s people.

**Content:** The anointed one is equipped with the Lord’s Spirit to proclaim good news to the humble, liberty to captives, and to announce the year of the Lord’s favor and the day of God’s vengeance. Those who mourn in Zion will receive beauty instead of ashes, the oil of joy instead of mourning. The people will rebuild the ruined cities and be called priests of the Lord. The chapter ends with a song of praise in which the speaker rejoices over the salvation that God causes to spring forth for all nations.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The year of the Lord’s favor:** A time of God’s grace, deliverance, and blessing for his people.
- **Beauty instead of ashes:** A picture of total transformation from sorrow to joy and honor.
- **Oaks of righteousness:** God’s people standing firm, planted by the Lord for his glory.
- **Garment of salvation and robe of righteousness:** Symbols of God’s gift of salvation and righteousness.

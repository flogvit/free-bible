**Main Theme:** God declares his sovereignty over history and comforts Israel with promises of protection, while exposing the powerlessness of idols.

**Content:** God summons the nations to judgment and announces that he has raised up a conqueror from the east who will subdue peoples. Israel receives assurances that they are God's chosen servant and need not fear, for God will strengthen and help them. God promises to turn the desert into water-rich areas for the poor. Idols are challenged to prove their divinity by predicting the future, but they are revealed as emptiness and nothingness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Do not be afraid"** (v. 10, 13, 14): God's repeated assurance to Israel of his presence and help in adversity.
- **"The righteousness of my right hand"** (v. 10): A symbol of God's saving power and faithful support.
- **"Worm Jacob"** (v. 14): An image of Israel's weakness and insignificance, which will nevertheless become an instrument in God's hand.
- **"Water in the desert"** (v. 17–18): God's promise to provide for his people and transform wasteland into fertile land.
- **"Emptiness and wind"** (v. 29): A description of idols and their cast images as powerless and without substance.

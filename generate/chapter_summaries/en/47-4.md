**Main Theme:** Paul explains how God’s power preserves His servants through suffering and how temporary trouble leads to eternal glory.

**Content:** Paul does not lose heart in his ministry but proclaims the gospel openly and honestly. The gospel is hidden only for those who are perishing, blinded by the god of this age. God’s light has shone in the hearts of believers through Christ, who is the image of God. This treasure is carried in fragile clay jars so that it may be clear that the power comes from God. Suffering is light and momentary compared to the eternal glory that awaits.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Clay jars (ὀστρακίνοις σκεύεσιν):** Human frailty contrasted with God’s overwhelming power.
- **The god of this age (ὁ θεὸς τοῦ αἰῶνος τούτου):** Satan, who blinds the unbelieving so they cannot see the light of the gospel.
- **Outer and inner person:** The outer self is wasting away, but the inner self is being renewed day by day.
- **The visible versus the invisible:** What is seen is temporary; what is unseen is eternal—this guides the focus of believers.

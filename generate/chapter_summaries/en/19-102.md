**Main Theme:** A suffering person's prayer for God's intervention, with certainty of His eternal faithfulness to Zion.

**Content:** The psalm is a lament from one exhausted by suffering and feeling abandoned—his days vanish like smoke and his body withers like grass. In the midst of despair, the gaze turns to God who reigns forever, and hope is anchored in the promise that the Lord will restore Zion and hear the prayer of the helpless. The psalm culminates in the confession of God's eternity: heaven and earth shall perish, but God remains the same, and His years never end.

**Key Words/Images:**
- *Smoke and grass* (vv. 4–5, 12): Images of human life's transience and frailty.
- *Lonely birds* (vv. 7–8): The pelican in the wilderness and the bird on the roof illustrate the psalmist's isolation.
- *Restoration of Zion* (vv. 14–17): God's promise to have compassion on Jerusalem and reveal His glory.
- *God's eternity* (vv. 26–28): The creation ages like a garment, but God remains unchanged—a central testimony used in the NT concerning Christ.

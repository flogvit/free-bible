**Main Theme:** The Day of the Lord brings judgment upon the nations and establishes His universal kingdom from Jerusalem.

**Content:** All nations gather to wage war against Jerusalem; the city is captured, and half its population goes into exile. But the Lord intervenes and fights for His people. The Mount of Olives splits apart, and the Lord comes with all His holy ones. A terrible plague strikes the enemies, while Jerusalem is exalted and inhabited in safety without curse. The Lord will be king over all the earth, and all survivors from the nations shall come year after year to worship and celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles—those who refuse will face punishment.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Day of the Lord:** The eschatological day when God directly intervenes in history with judgment and salvation.
- **Mount of Olives:** The mountain east of Jerusalem that splits apart when the Lord stands upon it, creating a way of escape.
- **Living Water:** Flows from Jerusalem toward the east and west, symbolizing life and blessing from God's presence.
- **Feast of Tabernacles:** The annual feast all nations must celebrate in Jerusalem as a sign of submission to the Lord's kingdom.
- **"Holy to the Lord":** Even everyday items such as the bells on horses and cooking pots are to be consecrated—everything in Jerusalem belongs to God.

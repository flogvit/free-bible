**Main Theme:** Job longs for his former prosperity when he lived under God’s blessing and enjoyed great honor in society.

**Content:** Job recalls the time when God’s light shone upon him and the Almighty was with him. He describes how he was honored by all—from young people to elders, from princes to leaders—when he took his seat at the city gate. Job highlights his righteous way of life: he rescued the poor and fatherless, became eyes for the blind and feet for the lame. He expected to live a long life and die in prosperity, with continually renewed honor.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God’s light/lamp (v. 3):** A symbol of God’s presence and blessing that shone over Job’s life.
- **The city gate (v. 7):** The place for administering justice and public recognition where Job held a position of honor.
- **Eyes for the blind, feet for the lame (v. 15):** A poetic image illustrating Job’s practical care and righteousness.
- **Rooted like a tree and as numerous as sand (v. 18):** Symbols of Job’s expectation of security and a long life.
- **A king among his troops (v. 25):** An image of Job’s authority and leadership role in society.

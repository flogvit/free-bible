**Main Theme:** A psalm of thanksgiving in which David praises God for his grace, faithfulness, and answered prayers.

**Content:** The psalmist promises to praise God with all his heart and to worship before his holy temple. He gives thanks that God answered when he called and strengthened him with power. The psalm proclaims that all the kings of the earth shall praise the Lord when they hear his words, for the glory of the Lord is great. Though the Lord is exalted, he looks on the lowly, and he preserves the psalmist in the midst of trouble. The psalm concludes with confidence that the Lord's grace endures forever, and a prayer that God will not forsake the work of his hands.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's temple (הֵיכַל קָדְשְׁךָ):** The place of worship and God's holy presence.
- **Grace and faithfulness (חֶסֶד og אֱמֶת):** God's covenant love and reliability as the foundation for praise.
- **God's right hand (יְמִינֶךָ):** A symbol of God's saving power and protection.
- **The work of his hands (מַעֲשֵׂי יָדֶיךָ):** An expression referring to humanity as God's creation, which he cares for.

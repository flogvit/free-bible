**Main Theme:** Bezalel builds the holy furnishings of the tabernacle according to God's instructions.

**Content:** Bezalel makes the ark of the covenant from acacia wood, covers it with pure gold, and places two golden cherubim on the mercy seat with their faces turned toward each other. He also constructs the table for the bread of the Presence from acacia wood overlaid with gold, along with its utensils; the seven-branched lampstand made from a single piece of pure gold; and the altar of incense from acacia wood overlaid with gold. Finally, he prepares the holy anointing oil and the pure fragrant incense.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant:** A gold-covered chest of acacia wood with a mercy seat and cherubim—the place of God's presence.
- **Cherubim:** Two golden figures with outstretched wings that guard the mercy seat—symbolizing God's holiness.
- **Lampstand (menorah):** A seven-branched lampstand made of pure gold, decorated with knobs and flowers—providing light in the sanctuary.
- **Altar of Incense:** A gold-covered altar for burning incense—representing prayer and worship.

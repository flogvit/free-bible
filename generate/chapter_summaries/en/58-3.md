**Main Theme:** Jesus is greater than Moses and deserves faithful trust from God’s people.

**Content:** The chapter opens by calling Jesus "the apostle and high priest of our confession" and compares him with Moses. While Moses was faithful as a servant in God’s house, Christ is faithful as the Son over the house—and we are that house if we hold fast to our hope. Drawing on Psalm 95, it warns against hardening the heart as the Israelites did in the wilderness, which kept them from entering God’s rest. The readers are urged to encourage one another daily against the deceitfulness of sin and unbelief.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Apostle and High Priest:** Jesus is both God’s messenger and mediator for the confession of faith.
- **God’s House:** A picture of God’s people—Moses served in it, but Christ rules over it as Son.
- **Rest (κατάπαυσις):** The rest that Israel missed out on, which believers must seek through faith.
- **Hardened Heart:** A warning against shutting oneself off from God’s voice, as the wilderness generation did.
- **Today (Σήμερον):** Emphasizes that the time to hear and obey is now.

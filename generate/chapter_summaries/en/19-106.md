**Main Theme:** A historical confession psalm that praises God’s faithful love despite Israel’s repeated rebellion and sin.

**Content:** The psalm opens with praise for the Lord’s goodness and eternal grace (vv. 1–3), followed by a prayer for God’s favor (vv. 4–5). The main body reviews Israel’s history of sin: the ancestors’ rebellion at the Red Sea, the golden calf at Horeb, contempt for the promised land, worship at Baal-Peor, and idolatry in Canaan. Through all this, God shows faithful grace—he remembers his covenant and is moved by the people’s distress (vv. 44–46). The psalm concludes with a prayer for salvation and gathering from the nations (v. 47).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hesed (חֶסֶד – faithful love):** God’s eternal, covenantal love that endures despite the people’s unfaithfulness (vv. 1, 45).
- **Forgetfulness and Rebellion:** Israel’s repeated pattern of forgetting God’s deeds and rebelling against him (vv. 7, 13, 21).
- **Covenant (בְּרִית):** God remembers his covenant with Israel and acts in faithfulness toward it (v. 45).
- **Moses and Phinehas:** Intercessors who stand in the breach for the people and turn away God’s judgment (vv. 23, 30–31).

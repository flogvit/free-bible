**Main Theme:** The Table of Nations shows how all the nations of the earth descend from Noah's three sons after the flood.

**Content:** This chapter describes the descendants of Japheth (the island and coastal peoples), Ham (including Nimrod, the first mighty one on earth, who founded Babel in Shinar and built Nineveh in Assyria, as well as the sons of Canaan with their territorial boundaries) and Shem (the ancestor of Eber). From these three branches, the nations spread across the earth according to their clans, languages, and territories.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Table of Nations (תּוֹלְדֹת):** A genealogy showing the common origin of all nations from Noah.
- **Nimrod:** The son of Cush who became the first mighty one on earth, a mighty hunter before the Lord, and founder of Babel.
- **Peleg:** In his days "the earth was divided" – his name means 'division' and points to the scattering of the nations.
- **The Children of Eber:** Shem's lineage that points forward to the Hebrew people.

**Main Theme:** God's answer to Habakkuk's complaint: judgment on the oppressor and hope for the righteous.

**Content:** Habakkuk stations himself as a watchman to hear God's response, and the Lord commands him to write down the vision clearly. God reveals that the proud shall fall, while the righteous shall live by their faith. Five "woe" proclamations announce judgment against plundering, unjust gain, bloodshed, degradation, and idolatry. The chapter culminates with the promise that knowledge of the Lord's glory will fill the earth, and all the world will be silent before the Lord in his holy temple.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The righteous shall live by their faith"** – A foundational statement about the significance of faith for salvation and life (v.4).
- **The "Woe" Proclamations (הוֹי)** – Five prophetic words of judgment warning against sin and injustice.
- **"The earth will be filled with knowledge of the Lord's glory"** – A promise of God's universal revelation (v.14).
- **Silent Idols** – The contrast between lifeless idol images and the living God in his temple (vv.18–20).

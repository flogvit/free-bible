**Main Theme:** King Asa's religious reformation and the people's renewal of their covenant with God.

**Content:** The prophet Azariah, moved by the Spirit of God, meets King Asa with the message that the Lord is with them when they are with Him, but will leave them if they forsake Him. Inspired by this prophecy, Asa removes idols from Judah, Benjamin, and the conquered cities in Ephraim, and renews the altar of the Lord. He gathers the people—including many from the northern kingdom who had joined him—for a great festival in Jerusalem, where they enter into a covenant to seek the Lord with all their heart. Asa even removes his grandmother Ma'akah from her position because she had made an Asherah image. The result is peace and quiet throughout the land.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant Renewal:** The people solemnly commit themselves to seeking the Lord with all their heart and soul, with the death penalty for those who refuse.
- **Reformation:** Asa removes idols and renews the altar as an expression of returning to true worship.
- **"Seek and Find":** The core of the prophecy—God allows Himself to be found by those who seek Him, but abandons those who abandon Him.
- **Ma'akah's Removal:** Asa's radical action against his own grandmother demonstrates the seriousness and scope of the reformation.

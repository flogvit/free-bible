**Main Theme:** God promises cleansing from sin, the removal of false prophecy, and the refining of a faithful remnant through trials.

**Content:** On that day a fountain will be opened for the house of David and for the inhabitants of Jerusalem to cleanse them from sin and impurity. The Lord will remove idols and false prophets from the land—those who still prophesy falsely will be ashamed and even pierced by their own parents. The sword will awake against the Lord’s shepherd, and the flock will be scattered. Two-thirds will perish, but the remaining third will be refined like gold and silver in fire; they will call on the name of the Lord and become His people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Fountain (מָקוֹר):** An opened fountain for cleansing from sin and impurity for the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
- **False Prophets:** Prophets who speak lies in the Lord’s name will be ashamed and deny their prophetic role.
- **The Shepherd Struck:** God commands the sword to strike the shepherd, so that the flock is scattered—a prophecy Jesus applied to Himself.
- **Refining by Fire:** The remaining third will be tested as silver and gold are refined, and restored in covenant relationship with God.

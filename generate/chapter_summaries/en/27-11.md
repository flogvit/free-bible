**Main Theme:** Prophetic visions of future conflicts between kingdoms and the persecution of God's people by a wicked king.

**Content:** The chapter describes the fall of the Persian Empire and a mighty king whose kingdom is divided into four parts. This is followed by prolonged conflicts between the Northern Kingdom and the Southern Kingdom, involving wars, false alliances, and intrigues. A contemptible king from the north will arise, desecrate the sanctuary, remove the daily sacrifice, and set up "the abomination that causes desolation." Those who know their God will stand firm, but many will suffer. Finally, the king will come to his end with no one to help him.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Northern Kingdom and Southern Kingdom:** The two Hellenistic kingdoms (Seleucids and Ptolemies) that fought for power after Alexander's death.
- **The Holy Covenant:** God's covenant with His people, against which the wicked king directs his wrath.
- **The Abomination That Causes Desolation:** The desecration of the temple by removing the daily sacrifice and introducing idol worship.
- **The Wise Ones (maskilim):** The faithful who teach the people but suffer persecution through sword, fire, captivity, and plunder.

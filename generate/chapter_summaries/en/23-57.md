**Main Theme:** God's judgment on idolatry and the promise of healing for the humble.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the righteous dying and finding peace, while the people engage in idolatry, cultic prostitution, and child sacrifice in valleys and on high places. God confronts the people's unfaithfulness—they have abandoned Him for idols and do not fear Him. Yet God, who is exalted and holy, will still dwell with the broken and humble to give them life. The chapter concludes with peace for those who repent, while the wicked are like a troubled sea that cannot rest.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Death of the Righteous (v.1-2):** The faithful are gathered by God and find peace, shielded from coming disaster.
- **Idolatry Under Green Trees (v.5):** This image describes cultic unfaithfulness, including child sacrifice in the valleys.
- **God as Exalted and Near (v.15):** The Holy God dwells on high, yet also with the humble and brokenhearted.
- **The Wicked as a Troubled Sea (v.20):** The wicked have no peace; they are like a sea that constantly churns up mud and filth.

**Main Theme:** God’s sovereign protection of his church amid persecution and his judgment on arrogant rulers.

**Content:** King Herod Agrippa persecutes the church, kills the apostle James, and imprisons Peter during the Passover festival. While the church prays earnestly, an angel miraculously frees Peter from prison. Herod executes the guards and later dies by God’s judgment after receiving divine honor. The chapter concludes with the word of God growing, and Barnabas and Saul returning with John Mark.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Angel:** God’s messenger who both frees Peter from prison and strikes Herod dead.
- **The Chains Falling:** A symbol of God’s power to set free from human authority and persecution.
- **Eaten by Worms:** Herod’s gruesome death illustrates God’s judgment on those who take his glory.
- **The Church’s Prayer:** The believers’ perseverance in prayer shows the community’s role in God’s saving acts.

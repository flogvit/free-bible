**Main Theme:** Paul exhorts believers to live a transformed life in service to God and one another.

**Content:** Paul urges Christians to present their bodies as living sacrifices to God and to be renewed in mind rather than conformed to this world. He uses the image of the body to show that the church is one body in Christ, where each member has different spiritual gifts to be used for the good of the whole community. The chapter contains practical exhortations regarding sincere love, hospitality, humility, and peace with all people. Paul concludes by urging believers to overcome evil with good.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Living sacrifice (θυσίαν ζῶσαν):** The daily life of believers should be an act of worship in which the whole person is dedicated to God.
- **Renewal of the mind (ἀνακαίνωσις τοῦ νοός):** Transformation occurs from within as the way we think is renewed, enabling us to discern God's will.
- **One body, many members (ἓν σῶμα, πολλὰ μέλη):** The church functions like a human body where each member has a unique function and gift.
- **Sincere love (ἀγάπη ἀνυπόκριτος):** Love must be genuine and without hypocrisy, demonstrated through practical action.
- **Overcome evil with good:** The chapter culminates in the call not to repay evil for evil, but to conquer evil through goodness.

**Main Theme:** God calls and equips Ezekiel to be a prophet for rebellious Israel.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to stand up, and the Spirit enters him and raises him to his feet. God sends him to the people of Israel, a stubborn nation that has rebelled against God from the time of their ancestors until now. Ezekiel is to proclaim "Thus says the Lord God" whether they listen or not. He is urged not to fear the people, even though they are like thorns and scorpions. Finally, Ezekiel sees a hand reaching out to him with a scroll written on both sides with laments, sighs, and woes.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Son of Man (בֶּן־אָדָם):** God's address to Ezekiel that emphasizes his humanity before the holy God.
- **Rebellious House (בֵּית מְרִי):** A repeated description of Israel as a people in continuous rebellion against God.
- **The Scroll (מְגִלַּת־סֵפֶר):** A symbol of the message Ezekiel is to proclaim, filled with judgment and lamentation.
- **The Spirit (רוּחַ):** God's Spirit who equips and strengthens the prophet for service.

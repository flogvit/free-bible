**Main Theme:** The chapter demonstrates Jesus' power over death, nature, and illness, amidst opposition and human doubt.

**Content:** Herod beheads John the Baptist after a rash promise to his stepdaughter. Jesus feeds more than five thousand people with five loaves and two fish, then withdraws to pray. During the night, he walks on the water toward the disciples' boat; Peter attempts to do the same but sinks in doubt and is rescued by Jesus. The chapter concludes with the disciples confessing Jesus as the Son of God, and many are healed in Gennesaret.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Five loaves and two fish:** Jesus' miraculous care for the people reveals his power to provide abundantly.
- **Walking on water:** Jesus demonstrates divine authority over the forces of nature.
- **Ὀλιγόπιστε (little faith):** Jesus' rebuke of Peter highlights the connection between faith and keeping one's focus on Jesus.
- **Son of God:** The disciples' confession (v.33) is a Christological high point that affirms Jesus' identity.

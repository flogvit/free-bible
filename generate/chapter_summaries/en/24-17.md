**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts the curse on those who trust in humans with the blessing on those who put their confidence in the Lord, and concludes with an exhortation to keep the Sabbath.

**Content:** Judah's sin is engraved in their hearts and upon the horns of the altar. The prophet warns against trusting in people, comparing such a person to a shrub in the desert, while the one who trusts in the Lord is like a tree planted by water. The heart is deceitful, but the Lord searches it and rewards each according to their deeds. Jeremiah prays for healing and protection from his persecutors. The chapter ends with a strong exhortation to keep the Sabbath holy—obedience leads to blessing, disobedience to fire over the gates of Jerusalem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The iron stylus and diamond point (v.1):** Sin is permanently inscribed, impossible to erase.
- **The tree by water versus the shrub in the desert (vv.6–8):** Illustrates the contrast between trusting in humans and trusting in God.
- **The deceitful heart (vv.9–10):** The human heart is treacherous, but the Lord searches it and knows its thoughts.
- **The Sabbath (vv.19–27):** A test of obedience—keeping it holy brings blessing; breaking it brings judgment.

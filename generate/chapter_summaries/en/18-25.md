**Main Theme:** Bildad's brief speech on God's overwhelming greatness and humanity's total impurity.

**Content:** Bildad responds to Job by emphasizing that God has dominion and awe-inspiring power, creating peace in the heavens. He argues that no one can be righteous before God—even the moon does not shine clearly, and the stars are not pure in his eyes. How much less then is man, who is a maggot and a worm!

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dominion and Awe (הַמְשֵׁל וָפַחַד):** God's absolute power and awe-inspiring majesty.
- **God's Armies:** Countless heavenly hosts that underscore God's infinite resources.
- **The Moon and the Stars:** Even celestial bodies are not pure enough in God's eyes.
- **Maggot and Worm (רִמָּה/תּוֹלֵעָה):** Powerful images of human insignificance and frailty compared to God.

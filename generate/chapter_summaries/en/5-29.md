**Main Theme:** Moses renews the covenant between God and all Israel before they enter the promised land.

**Content:** Moses gathers the entire people—leaders, men, women, children, and foreigners—to enter into covenant with God. He reminds them of God’s mighty acts in Egypt, the journey through the wilderness where their clothes and sandals did not wear out, and the victories over the kings Sihon and Og. The covenant applies not only to those standing there but also to future generations. Moses strongly warns against apostasy and idolatry, describing how disobedience will lead to the land’s destruction like that of Sodom and Gomorrah. The chapter concludes with these words: "The secret things belong to the Lord our God, but the revealed things belong to us and to our children forever."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant (בְּרִית):** The binding agreement between God and Israel, renewed before entering the land.
- **A root that bears poison and wormwood (שֹׁרֶשׁ פֹּרֶה רֹאשׁ וְלַעֲנָה):** A picture of a person or group who may spread apostasy and sin among the people.
- **The secret and the revealed (הַנִּסְתָּרֹת... וְהַנִּגְלֹת):** The secret things belong to God, while the revealed things (the Torah) are given to Israel to follow.
- **Sodom and Gomorrah:** A symbol of total destruction as a consequence of breaking the covenant.

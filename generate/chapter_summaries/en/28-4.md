**Main Theme:** God brings a lawsuit against Israel for their faithlessness, lack of knowledge of God, and moral decay.

**Content:** God accuses the people of Israel because there is no truth, loyalty, or knowledge of God in the land (v.1). The priests are especially held responsible for rejecting knowledge and forgetting God's law, so God will reject them (v.6). The people have turned to idolatry and "fornication"—both literal and spiritual unfaithfulness toward God. As punishment, they will eat but not be satisfied and engage in prostitution without increasing in number (v.10). Israel is compared to a stubborn heifer, and Ephraim is bound to his idols (vv.16-17).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lawsuit (ריב):** God acts as the accuser in a covenant lawsuit against His people.
- **Knowledge of God (דעת אלהים):** The absence of knowledge of God is the root of the people's sin and destruction.
- **Fornication (זנות):** Used both for idolatry and sexual immorality—expressing unfaithfulness to the covenant.
- **Stubborn heifer:** Israel is compared to a stubborn cow that refuses to be led.
- **Ephraim bound to idols:** The northern kingdom is so firmly entrenched in idolatry that God lets them go their own way.

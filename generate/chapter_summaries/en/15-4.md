**Main Theme:** Opposition to the rebuilding of the temple in Jerusalem from local enemies and Persian authorities.

**Content:** When the enemies of Judah and Benjamin heard that the returned exiles were building the temple, they offered to join, but Zerubbabel and the leaders refused them, citing King Cyrus's decree. The enemies responded by intimidating the people, bribing advisors, and sending accusations to the Persian kings Ahasuerus and Artaxerxes. Artaxerxes ordered the work to stop, and construction remained halted until the second year of King Darius's reign.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **"The people of the land"** – Local ethnic groups settled in the area by the Assyrians who opposed the Jews' temple building.
- **Letter to the king** – Written accusations portraying Jerusalem as a rebellious city in order to stop the construction.
- **King Cyrus's decree** – The basis for the Jews' exclusive right to build the temple, used as an argument to reject cooperation.

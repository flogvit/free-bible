**Main Theme:** God creates a new heaven and earth where He dwells with His people in the new Jerusalem.

**Content:** John sees a new heaven and earth after the first creation has passed away—the sea is no more. The new Jerusalem comes down from heaven like a bride, and God Himself lives among humanity. He wipes away every tear, and death, mourning, and pain are gone forever. The city displays God’s glory; it needs neither sun nor moon, and has no temple—for God and the Lamb are its temple.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **New heaven and earth:** The old creation is completely replaced by God’s renewed work of creation.
- **The new Jerusalem:** The holy city that comes down from God, adorned with precious stones, gold, and gates made of pearl.
- **God’s dwelling (σκηνή):** God lives permanently with humanity—full restoration of fellowship.
- **Alpha and Omega:** God is the beginning and the end, the One who makes all things new.
- **The book of life:** Only those written in the Lamb’s book of life are granted entry into the city.

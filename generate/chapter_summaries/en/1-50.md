**Main Theme:** Jacob’s burial in Canaan and Joseph’s death in Egypt mark the end of the patriarchal era and point forward to deliverance.

**Content:** Joseph mourns his father Jacob and obtains Pharaoh’s permission to bury him in the cave of Machpelah in Canaan, which Abraham had purchased. After the burial, the brothers fear revenge, but Joseph comforts them with these words: "You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good." Joseph dies at the age of 110 in Egypt and makes the Israelites swear to carry his bones to Canaan when God visits them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Embalming (חֲנֻטִים):** An Egyptian practice used on both Jacob and Joseph, showing the Israelites’ integration into Egypt.
- **Cave of Machpelah:** The family burial site purchased by Abraham, symbolizing the promise of the land.
- **"God intended it for good":** Joseph’s theological interpretation of suffering as part of God’s plan of salvation.
- **Joseph’s oath:** Points forward to the Exodus and the fulfillment of the land promise made to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

**Main Theme:** God provides for His people through water from the rock and victory over Amalek.

**Content:** Israel arrives at Rephidim where there is no water, and the people complain to Moses and doubt God's presence. God commands Moses to strike the rock at Horeb, and water flows out. The place is called Massah and Meribah. Then Amalek attacks, and Israel prevails when Moses holds his hands up with support from Aaron and Hur. Moses builds an altar named "The Lord Is My Banner."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Rock at Horeb:** God gives water from the stone—His care surpasses nature's limits.
- **Massah and Meribah:** The names mean "testing" and "quarreling"—reminders of the people's doubt in God's presence.
- **Moses' Raised Hands:** Symbolize dependence on God in battle; victory comes from Him, not human strength.
- **JHVH Nissi (The Lord Is My Banner):** The altar proclaims that God Himself is Israel's banner of victory.

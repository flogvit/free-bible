**Main Theme:** David organizes the Levites to carry the ark of the covenant to Jerusalem in the proper manner, after the first attempt failed.

**Content:** David prepares a place and a tent for God's ark in Jerusalem and gathers the Levites, who alone have the right to carry it. He reminds them that God broke out against them the first time because they did not follow the instruction. The priests and Levites consecrate themselves, and the Levites carry the ark on their shoulders with carrying poles, just as Moses had commanded. The procession is accompanied by musicians and singers, and when the ark arrives in the City of David, David dances while Michal, Saul's daughter, despises him in her heart.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Levites' Task:** Only the Levites are to carry the ark—God has chosen them for this service forever (v. 2).
- **Consecration:** The priests and Levites must consecrate themselves before they can carry the ark, unlike the first attempt (vv. 12–14).
- **Carrying Poles on Shoulders:** The ark is carried according to Moses' instruction, on the Levites' shoulders with poles (v. 15).
- **Music and Joy:** Singers and musicians with cymbals, harps, and lyres accompany the procession (vv. 16, 28).
- **Michal's Contempt:** Saul's daughter sees David dancing and despises him—a contrast to the people's joy (v. 29).

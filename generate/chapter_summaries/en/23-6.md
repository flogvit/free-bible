**Main Theme:** Isaiah’s call to prophetic ministry through an encounter with the holy God.

**Content:** In the year King Uzziah died, Isaiah saw the Lord seated on a high throne, surrounded by seraphim who cried out, "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts." Confronted with God’s holiness, Isaiah recognized his own uncleanness, but was cleansed when a seraph touched his lips with a burning coal from the altar. Isaiah responded to God’s call with, "Here I am; send me!" and received the commission to proclaim judgment upon a people who would not repent—until the land lay desolate. Yet a holy remnant will remain, like a stump that still holds life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Seraphim:** Heavenly beings with six wings who praise God’s holiness and serve at his throne.
- **"Holy, holy, holy":** A triple declaration of praise that emphasizes God’s absolute holiness and majesty.
- **Burning coal:** Symbol of cleansing from sin, taken from the altar to purify Isaiah’s unclean lips.
- **"Here I am; send me":** Isaiah’s willing response to God’s call, an example of obedience.
- **Holy seed/stump:** A sign of hope amid judgment—a remnant will survive and carry forward the future.

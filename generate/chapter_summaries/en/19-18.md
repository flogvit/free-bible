**Main Theme:** A psalm of thanksgiving in which David praises God as his deliverer and rock after being saved from Saul and all his enemies.

**Content:** David begins by confessing the Lord as his rock, fortress, and deliverer (vv. 2-4). In his distress he cried out to God, who answered with a mighty theophany—the earth shook, fire and darkness surrounded him, and he rode on the winds to rescue David (vv. 5-20). David affirms his righteousness and God's faithfulness toward the upright (vv. 21-31), and praises God as the one who equips him for victory over his enemies and makes him head of the nations (vv. 32-46). The psalm concludes with praise and a promise of enduring grace to David and his descendants (vv. 47-51).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rock/Fortress (סֶלַע/מְצוּדָה):** God as an unchanging refuge and secure protection.
- **Theophany (vv. 8-16):** God's dramatic intervention in creation demonstrates his power over nature and his willingness to save.
- **Horn of Salvation (קֶרֶן־יִשְׁעִי):** A symbol of strength and victory that comes from God.
- **The Anointed One (מָשִׁיחַ):** David's kingdom points to God's eternal covenant and messianic promise.

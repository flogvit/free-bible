**Main Theme:** Moses prepares for the leadership transition to Joshua and ensures that God's law and a prophetic song are preserved as a testimony for future generations.

**Content:** Moses, 120 years old, announces that he will not cross the Jordan and presents Joshua as leader, urging him to be strong and courageous. He gives the Torah to the priests and the elders with the command to read it publicly every seventh year. God reveals Himself in the pillar of cloud, predicts that Israel will turn away from the covenant, and commands that a song be written as a witness. The book of the law is placed beside the ark of the covenant, and Moses recites the entire song before the assembly.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Be strong and courageous (חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ):** A repeated exhortation to Joshua and the people not to fear, because the Lord goes before them.
- **The pillar of cloud:** God's visible presence at the tent of meeting when He gave Joshua his commission and predicted the people's turning away.
- **The song as a witness:** A prophetic song commanded by God, to be preserved through generations and to testify against Israel when they turn away.
- **The Torah beside the ark:** The book of the law is placed next to the ark of the covenant as a permanent testimony to the covenant.

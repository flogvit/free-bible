**Main Theme:** Love (*agapē*) is the greatest and most enduring of all virtues and spiritual gifts.

**Content:** Paul shows that even the most impressive spiritual gifts—speaking in tongues, prophecy, faith that moves mountains—are worthless without love. He describes the characteristics of love: it is patient and kind, does not envy, does not boast, is not self-seeking, and rejoices in the truth. While prophecies and knowledge will cease when the perfect comes, love endures. Of faith, hope, and love, the greatest is love.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Resounding gong and clanging cymbal (v.1):** Illustrates that even the most spectacular gifts are empty noise without love.
- **The mirror and the riddle (v.12):** Our present understanding is incomplete, but one day we will see face to face.
- **The child becoming an adult (v.11):** The analogy shows the transition from partial to complete understanding.
- **Faith, hope, and love (v.13):** The three enduring virtues, of which love is the greatest.

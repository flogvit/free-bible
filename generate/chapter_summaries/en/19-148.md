**Main Theme:** A cosmic hymn of praise in which all creation—from heaven to earth—is called to exalt the Lord’s supreme name.

**Content:** The psalm begins by calling the angels of heaven, the sun, moon, and stars to praise God, who created them by His word and established eternal laws. Then, creatures of the earth—sea monsters, natural forces, mountains, trees, animals, and all people—are summoned to join in this same song of praise. The climax is that only God’s name is exalted, and He has raised up a horn for His people Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hallelujah:** An imperative that frames the psalm and calls all creation to praise.
- **Heavens of heavens:** A phrase referring to the highest heaven, where God’s throne is located.
- **Horn:** A symbol of strength and honor that God gives His people.
- **The people who are close to Him:** Israel as God’s chosen people with a special closeness to Him.

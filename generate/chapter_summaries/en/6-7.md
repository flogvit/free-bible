**Main Theme:** Achan's sin against the ban command led to Israel's defeat at Ai and required atonement through judgment.

**Content:** Israel suffered an unexpected defeat at Ai because Achan from the tribe of Judah had taken some of the devoted things from Jericho. God revealed the sin to Joshua and identified the sinner through a selection process from tribe to family to household. Achan confessed that he had taken a fine robe from Shinar, silver, and gold, and hidden them in his tent. He was brought to the Valley of Achor along with his family and all he owned, where they were stoned and burned, and the Lord's anger turned away from Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Ban (חֵרֶם):** That which was devoted to God and forbidden for the people to take; Achan's violation of this brought guilt upon the entire nation.
- **God's Selection:** The Lord actively "caught" (לכד) the guilty one through a systematic narrowing from tribe to individual.
- **The Valley of Achor (עֵמֶק עָכוֹר):** The place of judgment over Achan; the name means "Valley of Trouble" and plays on the words Joshua used: "Why have you brought trouble upon us?"
- **Collective Guilt:** One man's sin brought God's anger upon all Israel and led to military defeat.

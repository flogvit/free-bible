**Main Theme:** A praise of the Lord's greatness, deeds, and eternal covenant with his people.

**Content:** This acrostic psalm opens with a call to wholehearted praise within the assembly of believers. The psalmist extols the Lord's mighty works, his righteousness that endures forever, and his grace and mercy. God has sent redemption to his people and established an eternal covenant. The psalm culminates in the wisdom saying that the fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The deeds of the Lord (מַעֲשֵׂי יְהוָה):** God's great and mighty acts that are worth exploring and admiring.
- **Eternal covenant (בְּרִית לְעוֹלָם):** God's faithful promise to his people that stands firm forever.
- **Redemption (פְּדוּת):** God's saving act when he delivered his people.
- **The fear of the Lord (יִרְאַת יְהוָה):** Reverence and respect for God, which is the foundation of true wisdom.

**Main Theme:** Esther dares to appear before the king and invites him and Haman to a banquet, while Haman plots Mordecai’s death.

**Content:** On the third day, Esther puts on royal robes and enters the inner court, where the king extends his golden scepter as a sign of favor. The king offers her up to half of his kingdom, but Esther asks only that he and Haman come to a banquet. During the meal, she postpones her real request until the next day. Haman leaves rejoicing, but is filled with rage when Mordecai does not rise before him at the gate. At the advice of his wife Zeresh and his friends, Haman has a gallows fifty cubits high built to hang Mordecai.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Golden Scepter:** The king’s sign that Esther may live and speak, even though she came uninvited.
- **The Banquet:** Esther’s strategic approach to win the king’s favor before revealing her request.
- **The Gallows (50 cubits):** Haman’s proud plan to kill Mordecai, which ironically will lead to his own downfall.

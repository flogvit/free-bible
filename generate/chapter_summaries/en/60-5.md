**Main Theme:** Exhortation to humble service, spiritual alertness, and trust in God amid suffering.

**Content:** Peter exhorts the elders to shepherd the flock willingly and selflessly, as good examples, with the promise of the crown of glory when the Chief Shepherd returns. All are urged toward mutual humility and to cast their anxieties on God. They are warned against the devil and called to stand firm in faith, assured that God Himself will strengthen them after suffering.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Chief Shepherd (ἀρχιποίμην):** Christ as the supreme shepherd under whom the elders serve.
- **The unfading crown of glory:** The promise of eternal reward for faithful leaders.
- **Roaring lion:** A picture of the devil actively seeking to destroy believers.
- **God's mighty hand:** An appeal to humble oneself under God's sovereign care.

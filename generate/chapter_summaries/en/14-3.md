**Main Theme:** Solomon builds the Lord’s temple in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah.

**Content:** Solomon began building the temple in the second month of his fourth year as king, at the place where the Lord had appeared to David on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. The temple was richly decorated with cypress wood, gold from Parvaim, and precious stones. The Most Holy Place contained two gilded cherubim with a combined wingspan of twenty cubits. In front of the temple stood two pillars named Jachin and Boaz.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Moriah:** The place David had prepared for the temple, where the Lord had appeared to him.
- **The Most Holy Place:** The innermost room, 20 by 20 cubits, covered with 600 talents of gold.
- **The Cherubim:** Two gilded figures with a combined wingspan of 20 cubits, facing toward the temple hall.
- **Jachin and Boaz:** The two pillars in front of the temple, 35 cubits high, adorned with chains and pomegranates.

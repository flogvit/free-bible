**Main Theme:** God calls the unlikely Gideon to deliver Israel from Midianite oppression after the people turn back to Him in their distress.

**Content:** Israel does what is evil, and God gives them into the hand of the Midianites for seven years. When the people cry out to the Lord, He first sends a prophet with rebuke, then the angel of the Lord who meets Gideon at the winepress in Ophrah and calls him to service. Gideon, doubting his calling, receives confirmation through a sign involving fire and tears down his father's altar to Baal during the night. The Spirit of the Lord comes upon Gideon, and he gathers the tribes for battle, but first asks for confirmation through the sign of the fleece with dew.

**Key Words/Images:**
- *Angel of the Lord (מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה)*: A divine messenger who reveals God's presence and calling to Gideon under the terebinth tree.
- *Jerubbaal (יְרֻבַּעַל)*: Gideon's new name meaning "let Baal contend against him," given after he tore down Baal's altar.
- *The Fleece (הַגִּזָּה)*: Gideon's double sign with dew to confirm God's will and promise of victory.
- *Spirit of the Lord (רוּחַ יְהוָה)*: The Spirit who "clothed" Gideon and equipped him for leadership and battle.

**Main Theme:** God judges the divine beings who have failed in their responsibility for justice.

**Content:** God stands in His divine assembly and holds judgment over "gods" (elohim). He accuses them of judging unjustly and favoring the wicked, instead of protecting the weak, the poor, and the fatherless. Because they walk in darkness and fail in their calling, they shall die like humans. The psalm concludes with a prayer that God Himself will arise and judge all the earth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Assembly of God (עֲדַת־אֵל):** The heavenly council where God reigns as supreme Judge.
- **Elohim (אֱלֹהִים):** Divine beings or judges entrusted with upholding justice on earth.
- **The Weak (דַּל, יָתוֹם, עָנִי):** The poor, the fatherless, and the oppressed whom the judges were meant to protect.
- **Die like humans:** The warning that even divine beings lose their status through injustice.

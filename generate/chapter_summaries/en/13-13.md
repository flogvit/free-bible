**Main Theme:** David's first attempt to bring the ark of the covenant to Jerusalem fails due to a violation of God's holiness.

**Content:** David consults with the leaders and all Israel about bringing God's ark from Kiriath-Jearim, since it had not been sought during Saul's days. They transport the ark on a new cart with great celebration, but at the threshing floor of Kidon, Uzzah reaches out to steady the ark when the oxen stumble, and God strikes him dead. David becomes both angry and afraid, and leaves the ark in the house of Obed-Edom for three months, where God blesses his household.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of God (אֲרוֹן הָאֱלֹהִים):** The symbol of God's presence among the people, requiring reverence and proper handling.
- **Perez-Uzzah:** A place name that recalls God's breakthrough against Uzzah, serving as a warning about God's holiness.
- **The house of Obed-Edom:** Shows that the ark brings blessing when treated correctly.
- **New cart:** An incorrect method of transport that ignored God's instructions that the Levites were to carry the ark.

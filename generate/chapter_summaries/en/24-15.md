**Main Theme:** God’s irrevocable judgment against Judah and Jeremiah’s struggle between suffering and calling.

**Content:** God declares that even intercession by Moses or Samuel could not avert the judgment upon the people. Four fates await them: death, sword, famine, and captivity—all because of King Manasseh’s sins. Jeremiah laments his loneliness and suffering as a prophet, yet also confesses that God’s word became his joy. God responds by confirming Jeremiah’s calling and promising protection if he remains faithful.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Moses and Samuel** (v.1): Even the greatest intercessors in Israel’s history could not change God’s decision to bring judgment.
- **Four Devastations** (v.2–3): Death, sword, famine, and captivity represent complete judgment.
- **God’s Word as Food** (v.16): Jeremiah "ate" God’s word, and it became his joy—an image of receiving and living by God’s message.
- **Bronze Wall** (v.20): God promises to make Jeremiah an impregnable fortress against his opponents.

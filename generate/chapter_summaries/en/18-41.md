**Main Theme:** God describes the invincible sea monster Leviathan to demonstrate His own sovereign power over creation.

**Content:** God portrays Leviathan as a creature no human can tame or defeat—its scales are like tightly fitted shields, fire and smoke come from its mouth and nostrils, and weapons are powerless against it. The point is that if no one dares to rouse Leviathan, who then can stand before God Himself? Everything under heaven belongs to the Lord. Leviathan is king over all proud creatures, created without fear.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Leviathan:** A fearsome sea monster symbolizing the chaotic forces that only God has power over.
- **Fire and smoke:** Description of Leviathan's breath—torches from its mouth, smoke from its nostrils—emphasizes its terrifying nature.
- **Impenetrable armor:** Its scales fit so tightly that no air can pass between them, and no weapon can penetrate through.
- **King over the proud:** Leviathan rules over all arrogant creatures, yet bows before the Creator.

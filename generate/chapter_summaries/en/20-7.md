**Main Theme:** A warning against sexual immorality through a dramatic story about an inexperienced young man who is seduced by the foreign woman.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a father's exhortation to keep wisdom like a sister and hold fast to his commandments, in order to protect oneself from the foreign woman. Through a window, the author observes an inexperienced young man who, in the darkness of night, is lured by a seductive woman dressed as a harlot. She entices him with flattery, a perfumed bed, and promises that her husband is away. He follows her like an ox going to the slaughter—unaware that his life is at stake. The chapter concludes with a warning: her house is the road to the realm of the dead.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The foreign woman (אִשָּׁה זָרָה):** Represents the temptation of sexual immorality that deceives the ignorant.
- **Inexperienced/lacking understanding (חֲסַר־לֵב):** Describes the young man who lacks the wisdom to resist temptation.
- **Ox going to the slaughter (כְּשׁוֹר אֶל־טָבַח):** A picture of how the seduced person moves toward their own destruction without understanding the consequences.
- **Roads to the realm of the dead (דַּרְכֵי שְׁאוֹל):** Her house leads to death—a serious warning about the ultimate consequence of immorality.

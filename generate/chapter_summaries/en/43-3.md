**Main Theme:** Rebirth by the Spirit and faith in God’s Son as the way to eternal life.

**Content:** Nicodemus, a Pharisee and member of the ruling council, comes to Jesus at night and is told that one must be born from above of water and the Spirit to see the kingdom of God. Jesus compares his impending exaltation to Moses’ bronze serpent and proclaims that God sent his Son to save the world, not to condemn it – whoever believes has eternal life, while whoever does not believe is already condemned. John the Baptist testifies about Jesus as the bridegroom and declares: "He must increase, but I must decrease", confirming that whoever believes in the Son has eternal life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Born from above (ἄνωθεν):** Spiritual rebirth necessary to enter God’s kingdom.
- **The serpent in the wilderness:** A picture of the exaltation of the Son of Man (the crucifixion) for the salvation of all who believe.
- **Light and darkness:** The judgment consists in people loving darkness rather than light because of their evil deeds.
- **Bridegroom and friend:** John the Baptist describes himself as the bridegroom’s friend, rejoicing at Jesus’ voice.

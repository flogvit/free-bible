**Main Theme:** Warnings against idolatry and strict measures to preserve Israel’s faithfulness to the Lord.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the command to keep all of God’s words without adding to or taking away from them. It warns against false prophets who perform signs and wonders but lead the people to other gods—this is a test from the Lord to see whether the people love Him with all their heart. If anyone—a prophet, relative, or friend—urges idolatry, they must be put to death, and the accuser’s hand shall be the first in carrying out the execution. A whole city that has fallen into idolatry must, after thorough investigation, be devoted to destruction and completely burned. The chapter concludes with a promise of the Lord’s mercy upon obedience.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Test (נסה):** God permits false prophets to test whether the people truly love Him.
- **Devoted thing (חרם):** Total destruction of an apostate city and everything in it as an offering to the Lord.
- **"Remove the evil":** A repeated expression emphasizing the need to eliminate idolatry from society.
- **Signs and wonders (אות ומופת):** Miracles alone do not confirm a prophet’s truth; the message must align with God’s word.

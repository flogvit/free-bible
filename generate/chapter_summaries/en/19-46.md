**Main Theme:** God is the secure refuge and invincible protector of His people amidst the chaos of the world.

**Content:** The psalm, written by the sons of Korah, confesses that God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Even when the earth gives way and the mountains fall into the depths of the sea, we need not fear. A river with its streams makes glad the city of God, the dwelling place of the Most High. God puts an end to wars throughout the earth; He breaks the bow and shatters the spear. The refrain is repeated: "The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Refuge and strength (מַחֲסֶה וָעֹז):** God as a safe shelter and source of power in times of trouble.
- **City of God (עִיר־אֱלֹהִים):** Jerusalem/Zion as God's dwelling place, made secure by His presence.
- **The river (נָהָר):** Life-giving streams that contrast with the chaotic sea—a symbol of God's blessing.
- **The Lord of hosts (יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת):** God's title as ruler over heavenly and earthly armies.
- **Be still (הַרְפּוּ):** An invitation to let go, rest, and acknowledge God's sovereignty.

**Main Theme:** A father's exhortation to seek wisdom and choose the right path rather than the way of the wicked.

**Content:** A father passes on wisdom he himself received from his own father: Wisdom must be acquired and loved, for it gives life, honor, and protection. The chapter strongly warns against following the way of the wicked, contrasting the shining path of the righteous with the darkness of the ungodly. In conclusion, the son is urged to guard his heart, his speech, and his ways with care.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Wisdom as treasure:** Wisdom must be bought/acquired like a valuable possession and will bring honor and protection (vv. 5–9).
- **Light and darkness:** The way of the righteous is like the morning light that grows ever brighter, while the way of the ungodly is darkness (vv. 18–19).
- **Guardian of the heart:** The heart must be guarded above all else, for from it flow the springs of life (v. 23).
- **The right path:** Staying on the road without turning to the right or to the left symbolizes moral integrity (vv. 26–27).

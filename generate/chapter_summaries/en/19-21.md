**Main Theme:** A royal thanksgiving psalm that praises God for victory, blessing, and the protection of the king.

**Content:** The psalm opens with the king's joy over God's strength and salvation (vv. 2–3). God has richly blessed the king with a crown of gold, long life, and honor (vv. 4–7). The king trusts in the Lord and will not waver, while enemies will be struck by God's judgment (vv. 8–13). The psalm concludes with an exhortation to exalt the Lord and sing of His might (v. 14).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Crown of gold (פָּז):** A symbol of royal honor and God's abundant blessing upon the king (v. 4).
- **Eternal life:** The king asked for life and received days without end—an expression of God's faithful care (v. 5).
- **Fiery furnace:** Enemies will be consumed as in a burning furnace—a picture of God's judgment on the wicked (v. 10).
- **Trust (בֹּטֵחַ):** The king's unwavering trust in the Lord is the foundation of his stability (v. 8).

**Main Theme:** A psalm that calls the whole earth to praise God for his mighty works and faithfulness.

**Content:** The psalm begins with a universal call to all nations to praise God for his awe-inspiring deeds, including the miracle of the Exodus. It then describes how God tests and refines his people like silver in fire, yet leads them through to abundance. The psalmist gives personal testimony of fulfilled promises and answered prayers, praising God for his unfailing grace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **All the earth (כָּל־הָאָרֶץ):** A universal call to praise—God's glory concerns all peoples.
- **Refining silver (כִּצְרָף־כָּסֶף):** God's trials cleanse and purify his people.
- **Fire and water (בָאֵשׁ וּבַמַּיִם):** Symbols of life's trials through which God leads his people.
- **Promises and burnt offerings (עוֹלוֹת/נְדָרָי):** The psalmist's gratitude expressed through the fulfillment of vows made.

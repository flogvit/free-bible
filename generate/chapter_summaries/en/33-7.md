**Main Theme:** Micah laments moral decay but expresses trust in God as the one who forgives sin and keeps His promises.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a complaint that godly people have disappeared and corruption prevails—even family members have become enemies. Amidst the darkness, the prophet confesses his trust in the Lord as his light. He asks God to shepherd His people and show wonders like those at the Exodus from Egypt. The chapter concludes with a song of praise to God's unique forgiveness and faithful love toward Abraham and Jacob.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The Lord is my light"** (v.8): In the midst of darkness and fall, the prophet expresses unwavering trust in God.
- **"Cast into the depths of the sea"** (v.19): God's complete forgiveness—sins are removed forever.
- **"Who is a God like you?"** (v.18): A rhetorical question highlighting God's unique ability to forgive and show faithful love (חֶסֶד).
- **Family breakdown** (vv.5–6): The image that even the closest become enemies illustrates deep moral decay.

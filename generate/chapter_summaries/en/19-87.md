**Main Theme:** A praise of Zion as God's city, where peoples from all over the world receive their spiritual birth.

**Content:** The psalm, written by the sons of Korah, opens with the statement that Zion is founded on holy mountains and that the Lord loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. Glorious things are spoken about God's city. Peoples such as Rahab (Egypt), Babylon, Philistia, Tyre, and Cush will be counted as born in Zion. The Most High himself will establish the city, and the Lord will register the peoples as born there.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Zion:** God's chosen city, founded on holy mountains, loved by the Lord above all other places.
- **"Born there":** An expression repeated three times, pointing to spiritual citizenship in God's city for all nations.
- **The Nations:** Egypt, Babylon, Philistia, Tyre, and Cush represent the nations of the world who are included among God's people.
- **Sources:** The image in verse 7 that "all my springs are in you" expresses that life and joy have their source in Zion.

**Main Theme:** Christian conduct in marriage and society, with Christ's suffering as the example and basis for hope.

**Content:** Peter gives guidance for marriage: women are encouraged toward inner beauty and humility, men to show understanding and honor. All Christians are called to unity, compassion, and to bless rather than repay evil. They are urged to be ready to defend their hope with humility. Christ suffered for sinners and preached to the spirits in prison. Baptism, as an antitype to Noah's salvation through water, now saves through Christ's resurrection.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Inner Beauty:** True adornment is the hidden person of the heart—a gentle and quiet spirit that is precious to God.
- **Blessing:** Christians are called to bless those who persecute them, so that they themselves may inherit a blessing.
- **Defending Hope:** Believers should always be ready to give an answer for their hope, with gentleness and reverence.
- **Christ Suffered for Sinners:** The righteous suffered for the unrighteous to bring us to God—an example and basis for salvation.
- **Baptism as Antitype:** Just as eight souls were saved through water in the ark, baptism now saves as an appeal for a good conscience.

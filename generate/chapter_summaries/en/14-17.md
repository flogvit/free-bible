**Main Theme:** Jehoshaphat’s faithfulness to the Lord and Judah’s blessing with strength, wealth, and peace.

**Content:** King Jehoshaphat strengthened Judah militarily by placing troops in fortified cities and in the Ephraimite towns his father had conquered. He walked in the early ways of David, sought God, and removed the high places and Asherah poles from the land. In his third year, he sent leaders, Levites, and priests to teach the people from the Book of the Law of the Lord. The fear of God fell upon neighboring nations so that they did not wage war against him; instead, they brought tribute, and Jehoshaphat became powerful with an army of over one million men.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Ways of David** – Jehoshaphat followed David’s early example in faithfulness to God, rather than Israel’s idolatry.
- **Teaching the Law** – An organized effort by leaders, Levites, and priests to spread knowledge of God’s Word throughout Judah.
- **The Fear of the Lord** – God’s protection manifested as surrounding nations dared not attack.
- **Tribute from the Nations** – The Philistines and Arabs brought gifts and livestock as signs of Jehoshaphat’s power and God’s blessing.

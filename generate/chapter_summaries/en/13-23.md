**Main Theme:** David organizes the Levites for service in the coming temple before making Solomon king.

**Content:** The aging David gathers Israel's leaders and counts the Levites from 30 years of age and upward, totaling 38,000 men. These are assigned to various tasks: 24,000 for temple work, 6,000 as judges and overseers, 4,000 gatekeepers, and 4,000 singers. David explains that since the Lord will now dwell in Jerusalem forever, the Levites no longer need to carry the tabernacle. In his final instructions, David lowers the service age to 20 years.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Levi's three sons (Gershon, Kohath, Merari):** The basic structure for organizing the Levites by family lines.
- **The separation of Aaron:** Aaron and his descendants were set apart for the most holy service—to offer sacrifices, serve, and bless in the Lord's name.
- **From 30 to 20 years:** The service age is lowered because the Levites' duties change from carrying the tabernacle to permanent temple service.
- **Singing morning and evening:** The Levites were to stand each morning and evening to give thanks and praise the Lord.

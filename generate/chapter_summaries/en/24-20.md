**Main Theme:** Jeremiah’s persecution and inner struggle between prophetic calling and personal suffering.

**Content:** The priest Pashhur strikes Jeremiah and places him in the stocks at the Benjamin Gate because he prophesies judgment against Jerusalem. When Jeremiah is released, he gives Pashhur the name "Magor-Missabib" (Terror on Every Side) and prophesies exile to Babylon. This is followed by an intense lament in which Jeremiah feels overwhelmed by God and his calling, yet acknowledges that God’s word is like a burning fire he cannot hold back. The chapter ends darkly with Jeremiah cursing the day of his birth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Magor-Missabib (מָגוֹר מִסָּבִיב):** "Terror on Every Side" – the new name given to Pashhur, signaling the coming judgment.
- **Burning fire in my bones:** A metaphor for God’s word that Jeremiah cannot restrain, despite the suffering it brings.
- **The stocks (מַהְפֶּכֶת):** A symbol of the persecution the prophet endures for his message.
- **Cursing the day of birth:** An expression of deep despair, reminiscent of Job’s lament.

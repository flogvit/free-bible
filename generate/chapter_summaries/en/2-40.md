**Main Theme:** The Tabernacle is erected and filled with God's glory as a sign of His presence among the people.

**Content:** God commands Moses to set up the tabernacle on the first day of the first month in the second year, and to place all the articles in their proper positions. Moses carries out everything exactly as the Lord has commanded, anoints the tabernacle, and consecrates Aaron and his sons for priestly service. When the work is completed, the cloud covers the tent of meeting and the glory of the Lord fills the tabernacle—so intensely that even Moses cannot enter. The cloud becomes the people's guide: when it lifts up, they break camp; when it settles down, they remain.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tabernacle (משכן):** God's dwelling place among the people, carefully constructed according to His instructions.
- **Anointing (משחה):** The consecration of the tabernacle, its articles, and the priests for service to God.
- **The Glory of the Lord (כבוד יהוה):** God's visible presence that fills the tabernacle and confirms His dwelling among Israel.
- **The Cloud (ענן):** God's guidance made visible—cloud by day and fire by night—directing the people's journey.

**Main Theme:** A historical instructional psalm that exhorts God's people to learn from the fathers' disobedience and God's faithful deeds.

**Content:** Asaph urges the people to listen and pass on God's mighty works to future generations. The psalm reviews Israel's history, from the plagues in Egypt, through the wilderness journey with its miracles and rebellions, to settlement in the land. Despite God's care, the people repeatedly sinned by forgetting His deeds and testing Him. God responded with both judgment and grace, remembering that they were frail creatures. The psalm culminates with God's rejection of Ephraim and Shiloh, His choice of Judah and Zion, and the appointment of David as shepherd over Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Riddles from times long past (v. 2):** Wisdom words that reveal deep truths about God's actions in history.
- **A stubborn and rebellious generation (v. 8):** A description of the fathers' disobedience and fickle heart as a warning for new generations.
- **The Rock (vv. 15–16, 35):** An image of God as both provider (water from the rock) and refuge for His people.
- **Merciful (v. 38):** God's gracious character that forgives sin and holds back His full wrath.
- **David as shepherd (vv. 70–72):** God chose David from the sheepfolds to shepherd Israel with a sincere heart.

**Main Theme:** Caleb receives Hebron as an inheritance because of his faithfulness to the Lord.

**Content:** The chapter begins the division of the land in Canaan, led by Joshua, the priest Eleazar, and the tribal leaders. Caleb from the tribe of Judah steps forward and reminds Joshua of the promise Moses gave him at Kadesh-barnea 45 years earlier. Now 85 years old, yet still as strong as when he was 40, Caleb requests the hill country inhabited by the Anakim and their fortified cities. Joshua blesses him and gives Hebron to him as an inheritance because he "wholly followed the Lord."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lot-casting:** The method used to distribute the land fairly among the tribes according to the Lord's command.
- **Caleb:** The faithful spy who "wholly followed" the Lord and saw his promised inheritance fulfilled.
- **Hebron (Kiriath-arba):** The ancient city of the Anakim given to Caleb as a reward for his faithfulness.
- **Anakim:** A people of giants who lived in Hebron—Caleb did not fear them but trusted in the Lord's help.

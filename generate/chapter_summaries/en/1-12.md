**Main Theme:** God’s call to Abram and the promise of blessing to all peoples on earth.

**Content:** God calls Abram to leave his homeland and relatives to go to a land that God will show him, with the promise that he will become a great nation and a blessing to all. Abram (75 years old) obeys and sets out with Sarai and Lot to Canaan, where God reveals Himself and confirms the promise of the land. During a famine, they travel to Egypt, where Abram, out of fear, presents Sarai as his sister. Pharaoh takes Sarai into his household, but God afflicts him with plagues, and Pharaoh sends them away from the land.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lekh lekha ("go for yourself"):** God’s radical call to break with the past and trust in His guidance.
- **Blessing:** God promises to bless Abram and through him all the families of the earth—a universal plan of salvation.
- **Altar:** Abram builds altars at Shechem and Bethel as signs of worship and faithfulness toward God.
- **Fear and Deceit:** Abram’s lie in Egypt reveals human weakness, yet God still protects His promises.

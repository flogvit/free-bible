**Main Theme:** Jonah flees from God's call, but the Lord's sovereignty reaches him even in the middle of the sea.

**Content:** God calls Jonah to preach against Nineveh, but the prophet flees toward Tarshish to escape the presence of the Lord. The Lord sends a violent storm, and when casting lots reveals Jonah as the cause, he asks the sailors to throw him into the sea. When they do so, the storm immediately calms down, and the pagan sailors fear the Lord and offer sacrifices to Him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Flight (בָּרַח):** Jonah's attempt to flee from God's presence illustrates humanity's futile resistance against God's will.
- **The Storm (סַעַר):** God's instrument to stop Jonah and reveal His power over creation.
- **Casting Lots (גּוֹרָל):** Shows how God uses even pagan customs to reveal truth and advance His plan.
- **The Sailors' Fear (יִרְאָה):** Their development from crying out to their own gods to fearing the Lord and offering sacrifices to Him demonstrates God's universal sovereignty.

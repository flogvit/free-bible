**Main Theme:** Prophecy of God’s judgment on Egypt leading to repentance and a future fellowship of peace between Egypt, Assyria, and Israel.

**Content:** The Lord comes riding on a cloud to Egypt, and the land’s idols tremble. Egypt is struck by civil war, the drying up of the Nile, and the folly of its leaders. But "in that day" the Egyptians will turn to the Lord, build an altar for Him, and be healed. A road will connect Egypt and Assyria, and Israel will become the third in a blessed fellowship where the Lord calls Egypt "my people" and Assyria "the work of my hands."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord on the cloud (v.1):** God’s majestic coming as Judge causes the idols to waver and the hearts of the Egyptians to melt.
- **The drying up of the Nile (vv.5–10):** Symbolizes economic and social collapse when Egypt’s life source fails.
- **A spirit of confusion (v.14):** The Lord sends a spirit of dizziness that causes Egypt’s leaders to stagger like drunkards.
- **An altar and a pillar (v.19):** Signs of true worship of the Lord in the midst of Egypt—a radical repentance.
- **The third one (vv.24–25):** Israel, Egypt, and Assyria are united as the Lord’s blessed people—an eschatological vision of peace.

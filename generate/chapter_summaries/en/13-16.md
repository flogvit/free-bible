**Main Theme:** David establishes permanent praise before the Ark of the Covenant and organizes worship in Israel.

**Content:** The Ark of the Covenant was placed in the tent that David had pitched, and he offered burnt offerings and peace offerings. David appointed Asaph and the Levites to lead the singing of praise before the ark with music and song. The psalm itself (vv. 8–36) praises the Lord's great works, recalls the covenant with Abraham, and calls all the earth to worship God as Creator. David also organized a two-part worship service: Asaph and the Levites served at the ark in Jerusalem, while Zadok and the priests continued the daily sacrifices at the tabernacle in Gibeon.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ark of the Covenant:** God's visible presence among his people, now located in Jerusalem.
- **Praise and Music:** Worship with cymbals, harps, and trumpets as an expression of joy over God's presence.
- **Covenant:** God's eternal promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob regarding the land of Canaan.
- **"His mercy endures forever":** A recurring theme that emphasizes God's faithful love.

**Main Theme:** Paul explains how believers are freed from the dominion of sin through union with Christ in his death and resurrection.

**Content:** Paul rejects the idea of continuing in sin so that grace may increase—those who have died to sin can no longer live in it. Through baptism, believers are buried with Christ and raised to a new life. Paul urges them to consider themselves dead to sin and alive to God, and to offer themselves as servants to righteousness rather than to sin. The wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Baptism (βάπτισμα):** Symbolizes union with Christ's death and resurrection, where the old self dies and a new life begins.
- **Slavery and freedom (δοῦλος/ἐλευθερόω):** A person is either a slave under sin or under righteousness—freedom from sin means service to God.
- **Wages versus gift (ὀψώνια/χάρισμα):** The wages of sin is death, but eternal life is God's free gift—not something we deserve.

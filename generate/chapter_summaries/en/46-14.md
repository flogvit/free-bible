**Main Theme:** Paul gives guidance on the proper use of spiritual gifts in the church, emphasizing edification and order.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an exhortation to pursue love and eagerly desire spiritual gifts, especially prophecy. Paul explains that prophecy is more valuable than speaking in tongues in the assembly because it builds up the church, whereas speaking in tongues without interpretation benefits only the individual. He provides practical guidelines for worship: at most two or three should speak in tongues (with an interpreter), and two or three prophets should speak while the others weigh what is said. Everything must be done with dignity and order, for God is not a God of disorder but of peace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Edification (οἰκοδομή):** The overarching criterion for using spiritual gifts—everything must serve to build up the church.
- **Prophecy (προφητεία):** Speech from God that conveys edification, exhortation, and comfort to the assembly.
- **Speaking in tongues (γλῶσσα):** A spiritual gift in which one speaks mysteries to God; it requires interpretation to benefit the church.
- **Order (τάξις):** Worship should be marked by dignity and order, not chaos, because God is a God of peace.

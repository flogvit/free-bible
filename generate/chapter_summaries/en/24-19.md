**Main Theme:** God commands Jeremiah to break a clay jar as a symbol of the irreversible judgment against Jerusalem and Judah.

**Content:** God sends Jeremiah to the Valley of Hinnom near the Potsherd Gate with a clay jar, along with some of the elders of the people and the priests as witnesses. There he proclaims judgment upon Jerusalem because of idolatry, child sacrifice to Baal, and the shedding of innocent blood. Jeremiah breaks the jar as a sign that God will crush the people and the city just as irreparably. The place shall be called "Valley of Slaughter," and during the siege the famine will become so severe that people will eat their own children. Afterwards, Jeremiah repeats this message in the temple courtyard.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Clay Jar:** Symbolizes Jerusalem and Judah—once broken, it cannot be repaired, just as the judgment is final.
- **Valley of Hinnom/Topheth:** The site of child sacrifice that will become the "Valley of Slaughter"—a place for mass graves.
- **Child Sacrifice to Baal:** The central sin that provokes God's wrath—something God never commanded.
- **Stubbornness:** The people have "stiffened their necks" and refuse to listen to God's word.

**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself as the resurrection and the life by raising Lazarus from the dead, leading to both faith and opposition.

**Content:** Lazarus of Bethany becomes ill and dies, but Jesus intentionally waits two days before going there. When he arrives, Lazarus has been dead for four days. In conversation with Martha, Jesus declares, "I am the resurrection and the life," and Martha confesses him as the Christ, the Son of God. Jesus raises Lazarus from the tomb, which leads many to believe, but also causes the Jewish council to decide to kill Jesus. The high priest Caiaphas unintentionally prophesies that Jesus will die for the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"I am the resurrection and the life":** Jesus' declaration that he has power over death and gives eternal life to those who believe.
- **Four days in the tomb:** Emphasizes that Lazarus was truly dead, making the miracle undeniable.
- **Jesus' tears (v.35):** Shows Jesus' deep human compassion and love for his friends.
- **Caiaphas' prophecy:** The high priest's statement that one man should die for the people unintentionally points forward to Jesus' atoning death.

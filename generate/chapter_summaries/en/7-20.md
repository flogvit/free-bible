**Main Theme:** The tribes of Israel wage civil war against Benjamin to punish the wickedness in Gibeah.

**Content:** All Israel gathers at Mizpah to demand that the men who committed the crime in Gibeah be handed over, but Benjamin refuses and mobilizes for war. Israel seeks God’s guidance before each battle and suffers defeat in the first two engagements, losing 40,000 men. After fasting and offering sacrifices, the Lord promises victory. Through a surprise ambush, Israel crushes Benjamin: 25,100 Benjamites fall, and only 600 men flee to the rock of Rimmon.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mizpah** – The gathering place where the Israelites unite as "one man" to seek justice.
- **"Seeking the Lord"** – Israel repeatedly consults God, highlighting their dependence on divine guidance.
- **The Ambush** – The military tactic that turns the tide of battle, signaled by a smoke signal announcing victory.
- **The Rock of Rimmon** – The refuge where 600 Benjamites survive, ensuring the tribe’s continued existence.

**Main Theme:** Samson's downfall and final victory through his relationship with Delilah and his death in the temple of Dagon.

**Content:** The chapter begins with Samson in Gaza, where he escapes by carrying the city gates to a hill near Hebron. Later, he falls in love with Delilah, who is bribed by the Philistine rulers to uncover the secret behind his strength. After repeated attempts, Samson reveals that he is a Nazirite consecrated to God from birth and that his uncut hair is the sign of this covenant. While he sleeps, Delilah cuts his hair, and the Lord departs from him. The Philistines capture him, blind him, and force him into hard labor. During a festival for the god Dagon, Samson prays to God for strength one last time, pulls down the temple pillars, and kills more Philistines in his death than he did during his life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Nazirite:** Samson was consecrated to God from birth, and his uncut hair was the outward sign of this holy covenant.
- **Delilah:** The woman who, through persistent pressure, caused Samson to reveal the source of his strength.
- **The Lord's Presence:** Samson's strength came from God – when the Lord departed from him, he lost his power (v.20).
- **Temple of Dagon:** The site of Samson's final and greatest victory, where he toppled the pillars and killed thousands of Philistines.

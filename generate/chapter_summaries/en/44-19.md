**Main Theme:** The powerful advance of the gospel in Ephesus through Paul's ministry, despite opposition from both occult practices and idol worship.

**Content:** Paul arrives in Ephesus and baptizes twelve disciples in Jesus' name, after which they receive the Holy Spirit. He preaches in the synagogue for three months, then moves to the school of Tyrannus for two years, so that all Asia hears the word of the Lord. God performs extraordinary miracles through Paul, and following the incident where Scaeva's seven sons fail to cast out an evil spirit, many burn their magic books. The silversmith Demetrius incites a riot because the gospel threatens the cult of Artemis and his business, but the city clerk calms the crowd and disperses the assembly.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **John's baptism vs. Jesus' baptism:** The twelve disciples knew only John's baptism of repentance, but were baptized in Jesus' name and received the Spirit.
- **The school of Tyrannus:** The place where Paul taught daily for two years after leaving the synagogue due to opposition.
- **Scaeva's sons:** Jewish exorcists who tried to use Jesus' name without faith and were overpowered by the evil spirit.
- **Burning of books:** New converts burned their magic books, worth 50,000 silver coins, as a sign of repentance.
- **The riot against Artemis:** The economically motivated uprising by silversmiths against the gospel, which threatened idol worship in Ephesus.

**Main Theme:** God’s final judgment against Gog and the promise of Israel’s complete restoration.

**Content:** God will draw Gog and his army to the mountains of Israel, where they will fall and become food for birds and wild animals. The Israelites will use the enemies’ weapons as fuel for seven years and bury the dead over a period of seven months in the "Valley of Hamon-Gog" to cleanse the land. Through this judgment, all nations will recognize the Lord’s holiness. The chapter concludes with the promise that Israel will be gathered from exile, God will pour out His Spirit upon them, and He will never again hide His face.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gog:** The leader of the hostile army from the north, representing ultimate opposition to God and His people.
- **Valley of Hamon-Gog:** "The Valley of Gog’s Hordes," the burial place for the fallen, serving as a monument to God’s judgment.
- **Sacrificial Feast:** Birds and wild animals are summoned to a macabre feast upon the enemies’ corpses—an inverted sacrificial meal in which the enemy themselves become the offering.
- **God’s Spirit:** The promise that God will pour out His Spirit on Israel marks the final restoration of the relationship between God and His people.

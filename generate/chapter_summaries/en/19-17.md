**Main Theme:** A prayer for God's protection and righteous judgment against enemies.

**Content:** David asks God to listen to his sincere prayer and test his heart, which he claims is free from deceit. He describes how enemies surround him like predators on the hunt and pleads for God's intervention and protection. The psalm concludes with a declaration of trust: David expects to see God's face in righteousness and be satisfied by beholding His likeness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The apple of the eye (v.8):** A metaphor for something extremely precious that must be guarded—David prays to be as dear to God.
- **The shadow of your wings (v.8):** God's protection depicted as a bird covering its young.
- **A lion on the hunt (v.12):** The enemies are compared to a lion lying in wait to attack.
- **See God's face (v.15):** An expression for close fellowship with God and complete satisfaction in His presence.

**Main Theme:** Paul urges believers to seek righteousness in Christ alone, not in human achievements or works of the law.

**Content:** Paul warns against Judaizing opponents who demand circumcision and lists his own impressive Jewish credentials—which he now counts as "rubbish" in order to gain Christ and find righteousness through faith. He uses the image of a runner pressing toward the goal to seize what has already seized him. With tears, he warns against "enemies of the cross," who focus only on earthly things, and reminds believers that their citizenship is in heaven, where they await Christ, who will transform their bodies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Circumcision vs. mutilation (περιτομή/κατατομή):** A play on words showing that true circumcision is spiritual, not physical.
- **Loss and gain (ζημία/κέρδος):** Paul's radical reevaluation of everything he once relied upon.
- **The race toward the goal (σκοπός/βραβεῖον):** The image of an athlete stretching out to reach the prize.
- **Heavenly citizenship (πολίτευμα):** Believers belong to heaven, not this world.

**Main Theme:** The chapter provides rules for vows, valuation, and the consecration of people, animals, property, and tithes to the Lord.

**Content:** When someone makes a vow to dedicate a person to the Lord, their value must be set according to age, gender, and financial ability. Houses, land, and animals that are dedicated may be redeemed by paying their value plus one-fifth. Anything devoted (cherem) to the Lord is most holy and cannot be redeemed. The tithe of crops and livestock belongs to the Lord as holy. The book of Leviticus concludes with a reference to the commandments given on Mount Sinai.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Valuation (ערך):** The priest sets a monetary value based on age, gender, and ability to pay for what is dedicated to the Lord.
- **Redemption:** Most things can be bought back by adding one-fifth of their value.
- **Cherem (חרם):** Anything completely devoted to the Lord is most holy and can never be redeemed or sold.
- **Tithe:** A tenth part of crops and livestock belongs to the Lord as holy.

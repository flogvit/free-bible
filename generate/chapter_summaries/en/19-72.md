**Main Theme:** A prayer that God's righteousness and blessing may characterize the king's rule and extend to all peoples.

**Content:** The psalm, attributed to Solomon, prays that the king may judge the people with God's own righteousness and defend the poor and oppressed. It portrays a realm of peace where justice flourishes, the king's dominion reaches to the ends of the earth, and all nations bring him gifts and homage. The psalm concludes with a doxology praising the Lord and marking the end of David's prayers.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Righteousness (צֶדֶק/צְדָקָה):** The king is to judge with God's own righteousness, especially on behalf of the weak.
- **Rain upon the mown grass:** The king's rule should be refreshing and life-giving like rain over the grass (v.6).
- **From sea to sea:** The king's kingdom shall encompass the whole earth, from the Euphrates to the ends of the world (v.8).
- **Blessing for all peoples:** Through the king, all nations of the earth will be blessed—a promise with messianic overtones (v.17).

**Main Theme:** Jesus' resurrection is confirmed through witnesses and revelations that awaken faith.

**Content:** Mary Magdalene finds the tomb empty and informs the disciples. Peter and the other disciple see the linen cloths and the face cloth that had been around Jesus' head, folded separately. Jesus first reveals himself to Mary, then to the disciples behind locked doors, where he gives them the Holy Spirit and authority to forgive sins. Thomas confesses Jesus as "My Lord and my God" after seeing him. The chapter concludes with its purpose: that readers may believe and have life in Jesus' name.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Empty Tomb:** Evidence of the resurrection – the linen cloths were left behind, but the body was gone.
- **"Rabbuni":** Mary's recognition of Jesus when he calls her by name.
- **"Peace be with you":** Jesus' greeting to the fearful disciples, repeated three times.
- **Thomas' Confession:** "My Lord and my God" – one of the strongest Christological confessions in the New Testament.
- **Faith and Life:** The purpose of the Gospel is to create faith in Jesus as the Christ, the Son of God.

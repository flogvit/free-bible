**Main Theme:** A prayer for God's righteous judgment against false accusers and wicked enemies.

**Content:** David experiences how enemies repay his love with hatred, lies, and unjust accusations (vv. 1–5). He pronounces extensive curses upon his main adversary—including a short life, the ruin of his family, and the erasure of his memory (vv. 6–19). The psalmist confesses his own distress and weakness, pleading for deliverance for God's name's sake (vv. 21–26). The psalm concludes with confidence that God will bless His servant and stand at the right hand of the poor as their defender (vv. 28–31).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Satan/Accuser (v. 6):** An adversary who stands at the enemy's right hand in judgment—contrasting with God, who stands at the right hand of the poor (v. 31).
- **Curse as Clothing (vv. 18–19):** The enemy has clothed himself with a curse like a robe; it enters his inward parts like water and like oil.
- **Shadow That Fades Away (v. 23):** The psalmist describes his own weakness—he is driven away like a shadow and shaken off like a locust.
- **Love Repaid with Hatred (vv. 4–5):** The fundamental injustice of the enemy's conduct—they return evil for good.

**Main Theme:** The chapter provides laws to ensure justice and protect the innocent in Israel through cities of refuge, property boundaries, and witness testimony.

**Content:** Israel is to establish three cities of refuge for anyone who kills someone accidentally, with the possibility of adding three more if the land expands. Intentional murder is to be punished by death. The chapter forbids moving a neighbor's boundary markers and requires at least two witnesses to convict. False witnesses are to receive the same punishment they sought to impose on the accused.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Cities of Refuge:** Three cities that provide protection for someone who kills unintentionally, so that the innocent are not caught up in blood vengeance.
- **The Avenger of Blood (goel hadam):** The relative of the deceased who has the right to put the murderer to death in cases of intentional killing.
- **Two or Three Witnesses:** A legal requirement for multiple witnesses to ensure a fair judgment and prevent false accusations.
- **Lex talionis:** The principle of "an eye for an eye," ensuring that the punishment is proportional to the crime.

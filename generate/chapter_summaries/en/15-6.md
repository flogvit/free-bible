**Main Theme:** The temple in Jerusalem is completed with royal support, and the people celebrate its dedication and Passover.

**Content:** King Darius finds Cyrus's original decree and confirms that temple construction should continue with financial support from the treasury. Tattenai and his associates are ordered not to hinder the work. With the encouragement of the prophets Haggai and Zechariah, the Jews complete the temple on the third day of Adar in the sixth year of Darius. They celebrate the dedication with joy, followed by Passover and the Festival of Unleavened Bread.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Decree of Cyrus:** The rediscovered document that proves the Jews' right to build the temple and grants them royal legitimacy.
- **Temple Dedication:** A celebration involving sacrifices, including 12 male goats for all Israel, symbolizing national unity.
- **Passover Celebration:** The first Passover in the new temple, marking both spiritual cleansing and a fresh start for those who returned from exile.

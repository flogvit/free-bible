**Main Theme:** The ritual of the red heifer provides purification from impurity caused by contact with the dead.

**Content:** The Lord commands Israel to obtain a flawless red heifer that has never borne a yoke. Eleazar is to slaughter it outside the camp, sprinkle its blood seven times toward the Tent of Meeting, and burn the entire heifer together with cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet yarn. The ashes are stored to make water for purification. Anyone who touches a corpse becomes unclean for seven days and must be sprinkled with the water of purification on the third and seventh days—otherwise they will be cut off from the assembly because they have defiled the Lord's sanctuary.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Red Heifer (פָרָה אֲדֻמָּה):** A rare offering burned completely outside the camp, used for purification from the impurity of death.
- **Water of Purification (מֵי נִדָּה):** Water mixed with ashes from the heifer, used for ritual cleansing.
- **Seven Days:** The duration of uncleanness resulting from contact with a dead body, with sprinkling required on the third and seventh days.
- **Outside the Camp:** Both the slaughter and the storage of the ashes take place outside the camp, emphasizing the separation between impurity and holiness.

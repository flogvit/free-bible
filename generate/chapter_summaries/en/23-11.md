**Main Theme:** Prophecy about the Messiah from David's line who will bring righteous rule, universal peace, and the gathering of God's people.

**Content:** A shoot shall grow out from the stump of Jesse, and the Spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him with wisdom, understanding, and reverence for God. He shall judge righteously for the weak and strike the earth with the rod of his mouth. In his kingdom, predators and prey shall live peacefully together, and the earth shall be filled with knowledge of the Lord. The Lord shall gather the scattered ones from Israel and Judah from all nations and reunite the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Stump of Jesse:** The Davidic line that seemed cut off, but which will sprout new growth through the Messiah.
- **Spirit of the Lord:** The sevenfold endowment with wisdom, understanding, counsel, strength, knowledge, and reverence for God.
- **Wolf and lamb:** A picture of the radical peace in the Messiah's kingdom where natural enemies live in harmony.
- **Banner for the peoples:** The Messiah as a gathering point for both Israel and the nations.

**Main Theme:** The chapter presents the purpose of Proverbs and establishes that the fear of God is the foundation of all true wisdom.

**Content:** Proverbs 1 opens by describing the book's intent: to impart wisdom, discipline, and understanding to both the inexperienced and the wise. The fundamental principle is stated: "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge" (v. 7). A father warns his son against being enticed by sinners who lure with wealth gained through violence and robbery. Then Wisdom is personified as a woman crying out in the public square, warning people of the consequences of rejecting her: those who turn away will reap the fruit of their own ways, while whoever listens will dwell securely.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fear of God (יִרְאַת יְהוָה):** Reverence for the Lord is the starting point and foundation of all true knowledge and wisdom.
- **Wisdom as a woman:** Wisdom is personified as a woman who cries out publicly in the square and at the city gates, calling people to repentance.
- **The Way:** The image of two paths—the sinners' path leading to destruction, and the way of wisdom that brings security.
- **Father and mother as teachers:** Parents' instruction is compared to a beautiful garland and chains—precious ornaments for those who receive them.

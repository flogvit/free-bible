**Main Theme:** God presents the blessings of obedience and the consequences of disobedience to His covenant.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a prohibition against idolatry and a command to keep the Sabbath holy. Through obedience, God promises abundant blessings: rain in its season, fertility, peace, victory over enemies, and His presence among the people. Disobedience brings an escalating series of curses: disease, defeat, famine, and finally exile—during which the land will receive rest for its neglected Sabbaths. Yet God offers hope: if the people confess their sins, He will remember His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob and not utterly reject them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant** – God’s binding relationship with Israel as the foundation for both blessings and curses.
- **Sevenfold Punishment** – A repeated expression indicating that punishment intensifies through persistent disobedience.
- **The Land’s Sabbath Rest** – During exile, the land receives the Sabbatical years it was denied.
- **"I will walk among you"** – The promise of God’s presence as the highest blessing of the covenant.

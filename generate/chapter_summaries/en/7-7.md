**Main Theme:** God gives Israel victory over the Midianites through Gideon’s small army of 300 men to show that salvation comes from Him alone.

**Content:** God reduces Gideon’s army from 32,000 to 300 men through two tests—first by sending home those who were afraid, then through the water test at which only those who lapped the water were chosen. A Midianite soldier’s dream about a barley loaf that overturns a tent confirms God’s promise of victory. The 300 attack during the night with shofars, empty jars, and torches, shouting, "A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!" The Lord creates panic so that the Midianites turn their swords against each other and flee.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Chosen 300:** God’s deliberate reduction of the army shows that victory is His work, not human strength.
- **The Water Test:** The selection method at the spring of Harod where the way they drank determined who would fight.
- **The Barley Loaf in the Dream:** A symbol of Gideon overturning Midian’s camp—a confirmation of God’s promise.
- **Shofars and Jars:** Unconventional "weapons" that emphasize that the battle belongs to the Lord.

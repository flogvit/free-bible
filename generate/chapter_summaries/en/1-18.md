**Main Theme:** The LORD reveals Himself to Abraham with the promise of a son and discloses His plans for Sodom.

**Content:** The LORD appears to Abraham at the oaks of Mamre, where Abraham sees three men whom he welcomes with generous hospitality. They announce that Sarah will bear a son within a year, which she laughs at in unbelief—but the LORD asks: "Is anything too hard for the LORD?" Then the LORD reveals that He intends to investigate the sin of Sodom, and Abraham intercedes on its behalf. Through negotiation, Abraham moves from fifty down to ten righteous people, and the LORD promises to spare the city for their sake.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The oaks of Mamre:** The place where the LORD reveals Himself to Abraham, a sacred meeting spot.
- **Hospitality:** Abraham's swift and generous reception of the three men demonstrates his character.
- **Sarah's laughter:** Expresses human doubt regarding God's promise, yet is met with assurance of God's almighty power.
- **Intercession:** Abraham's bold prayer for Sodom highlights his role as an intermediary and reflects God's justice.

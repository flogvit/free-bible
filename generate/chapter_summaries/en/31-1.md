**Main Theme:** God's judgment against Edom for their pride and betrayal of their brother nation Jacob, and the promise of Israel's restoration.

**Content:** Obadiah delivers the Lord's judgment against Edom, who in their arrogance believed themselves invincible within their mountain strongholds. Edom betrayed their brother people when Jerusalem was plundered—they stood passively, rejoiced, participated in the looting, and prevented refugees from escaping. As punishment, Edom will be completely wiped out, while the house of Jacob will reclaim their land. The book concludes with the promise that saviors will ascend Mount Zion, and the kingdom will belong to the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Esau (הַר עֵשָׂו):** Edom's mountainous region, symbolizing their false security and pride.
- **The Day of the Lord (יוֹם־יְהוָה):** God's coming judgment against all nations, where retribution is given according to their own deeds.
- **Fire and Straw (אֵשׁ/לֶהָבָה/קַשׁ):** The house of Jacob as fire and the house of Joseph as a flame will consume the house of Esau like straw—a picture of total destruction.
- **Mount Zion (הַר צִיּוֹן):** The place of Israel's salvation and restoration, in contrast to Mount Esau.

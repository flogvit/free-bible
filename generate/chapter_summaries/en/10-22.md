**Main Theme:** David's song of praise to the Lord for deliverance from his enemies and for victory throughout his life.

**Content:** King David sings this psalm after the Lord has rescued him from all his enemies and from Saul. He praises God with rich imagery such as rock, fortress, and shield, describing how God intervened with cosmic power when David cried out in distress. David confesses that he has followed the Lord's ways, and God has rewarded him according to his righteousness. The psalm concludes with thanks for victory over the nations and a promise of enduring kindness to David and his descendants.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rock and Fortress (סַלְעִי וּמְצֻדָתִי):** God as an unshakable refuge and sure protection in danger.
- **Theophany (vv. 8–16):** God's dramatic revelation with earthquake, fire, darkness, and thunder, demonstrating His power to intervene.
- **Shield of Salvation (מָגֵן יִשְׁעֶךָ):** God's active protection that grants David victory in battle.
- **The Anointed One (מְשִׁיחוֹ):** David as God's chosen king, with a promise of enduring kindness to his descendants.

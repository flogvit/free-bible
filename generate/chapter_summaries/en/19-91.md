**Main Theme:** Psalm 91 is a psalm of trust that promises God's complete protection to those who take refuge in Him.

**Content:** The psalm begins by describing the secure state of the one who dwells in the shelter of the Most High and rests in the shadow of the Almighty. God is portrayed as a bird covering its young with its wings, and as a shield against the dangers of night and day—pestilence, arrows, and destruction. Angels are commissioned to protect the believer on all their paths. The psalm culminates with God's own words (vv. 14–16), where He promises to rescue, exalt, and grant long life to those who love Him and know His name.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shadow of the Almighty (צֵל שַׁדַּי):** A picture of close protection and safety in God's presence.
- **Wings (כְּנָפָיו):** Just as a bird protects its young, God covers the believer with His wings.
- **Watch of the Angels (מַלְאָכָיו):** God sends angels to carry and protect the believer on all their ways.
- **Treading upon lion and serpent (שַׁחַל וָפֶתֶן):** A symbol of victory over dangerous and evil powers.

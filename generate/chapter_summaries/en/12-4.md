**Main Theme:** Elisha performs four miracles that demonstrate God's care for people in distress.

**Content:** The chapter recounts four miraculous deeds. Elisha helps a poor widow, the wife of a prophet, by miraculously multiplying her oil so she can pay her debts and save her sons from slavery. He promises a childless, hospitable woman from Shunem that she will have a son; when the boy later dies, Elisha raises him from the dead through prayer. At Gilgal, he neutralizes poison in a pot for the company of prophets, and feeds one hundred men with twenty loaves of barley bread—leaving food left over.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Oil That Never Ran Out** – God's ability to provide beyond natural limits, conditioned on faith and obedience.
- **The Closed Door** – Both the widow's oil miracle and the resurrection take place behind closed doors, symbolizing an intimate space with God.
- **Man of God** (אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים) – This title is used repeatedly and emphasizes Elisha's role as God's representative.
- **Food Left Over** – Points forward to God's abundance and Jesus' miracle of the loaves (John 6).

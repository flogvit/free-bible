**Main Theme:** The Fall of Man and Its Consequences for Humanity.

**Content:** The serpent tempts the woman to eat from the tree of knowledge by casting doubt on God's word, and she also gives some to her husband. Their eyes are opened, and they discover their nakedness. God confronts them, and all three—the serpent, the woman, and the man—receive individual judgments. Humanity is driven out of the Garden of Eden, and cherubim are placed to guard the way to the tree of life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Serpent (הַנָּחָשׁ):** A cunning figure who tempts humanity into disobedience against God.
- **The Tree of Knowledge:** The tree in the middle of the garden representing the boundary between obedience and disobedience.
- **Opened Eyes:** A symbol of lost innocence and a new awareness of sin and shame.
- **Curse and Judgment:** God pronounces consequences upon the serpent, the woman, and the man—enmity, pain, and arduous labor.
- **Expulsion:** Humanity is sent out of the garden to prevent access to the tree of life, with cherubim and a flaming sword guarding the entrance.

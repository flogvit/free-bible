**Main Theme:** Civil war between Gilead and Ephraim, followed by three minor judges in Israel.

**Content:** The Ephraimites confronted Jephthah and threatened him because they had not been called to fight against the Ammonites. This led to a war in which Gilead defeated Ephraim and used the word "shibboleth" at the fords of the Jordan River to identify fleeing Ephraimites—42,000 were killed. After Jephthah's death (6 years as judge), three minor judges followed: Ibzan from Bethlehem (7 years), Elon from Zebulun (10 years), and Abdon from Pirathon (8 years).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shibboleth:** A test word that revealed the Ephraimites' dialect and was used to identify the enemy.
- **Fords of the Jordan:** A strategic point where Gilead captured fleeing Ephraimites.
- **The Minor Judges:** Ibzan, Elon, and Abdon—leaders known for their large families and peaceful rule.

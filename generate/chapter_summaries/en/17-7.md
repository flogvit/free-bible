**Main Theme:** Haman’s evil plan is exposed, and he is executed on his own gallows.

**Content:** During the second feast, Queen Esther asks the king to spare her life and the lives of her people, revealing that they have been sold for destruction. She identifies Haman as their enemy. The king goes out in anger into the garden, and when he returns, he finds Haman fallen over Esther’s couch. The court official Harbona mentions the gallows Haman had built for Mordecai, and the king orders Haman to be hanged there.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Feast (מִשְׁתֵּה הַיַּיִן):** A drinking banquet where truth is revealed and destinies are decided.
- **The Gallows (הָעֵץ):** Fifty cubits high, built for Mordecai but used against Haman—ironic justice.
- **The King’s Wrath (חֲמַת הַמֶּלֶךְ):** An expression of divine justice through human authority.

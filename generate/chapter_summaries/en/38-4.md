**Main Theme:** God's promise that the temple will be completed by his Spirit, not by human power.

**Content:** In a vision, Zechariah sees a golden lampstand with seven lamps and two olive trees on either side. The angel conveys the Lord's word to Zerubbabel: "Not by might nor by power, but by my Spirit." Zerubbabel's hands will complete the temple he has laid the foundation for, and the people are warned against despising "the day of small things." The two olive trees are identified as the two anointed ones who stand before the Lord of all the earth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Golden Lampstand:** Symbolizes God's presence and light, with seven lamps supplied by oil.
- **The Two Olive Trees:** The two anointed (בְנֵי־הַיִּצְהָר) who stand before the Lord of all the earth.
- **"Not by might, but by my Spirit":** God's work is accomplished through his Spirit, not human strength.
- **The Day of Small Things:** The warning against despising the humble beginnings of the temple construction.

**Main Theme:** God's kingdom is characterized by grace and servant leadership, not human hierarchy.

**Content:** Jesus tells the parable of the workers in the vineyard, where all receive equal pay regardless of hours worked—the last become first. He predicts his suffering, death, and resurrection in Jerusalem. When the mother of Zebedee's sons asks for places of honor for her children, Jesus teaches that greatness in God's kingdom means serving others, just as the Son of Man came to serve and give his life as a ransom. At Jericho, Jesus heals two blind men who call out to him as "Son of David."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Vineyard:** A picture of God's kingdom where grace surpasses human notions of fairness.
- **Ransom (λύτρον):** Jesus' life given to set many free—the core of his saving work.
- **Cup:** Symbol of Jesus' suffering, which the disciples will also share.
- **Servant/slave (διάκονος/δοῦλος):** True greatness in God's kingdom is serving others.
- **Son of David:** A messianic title used by the blind men in faith toward Jesus.

**Main Theme:** David is anointed king over Judah while Ish-Bosheth becomes king over Israel, and conflict arises between the two kingdoms.

**Content:** After Saul's death, David inquired of the Lord and went to Hebron, where the men of Judah anointed him king. At the same time, Abner, Saul's commander, made Ish-Bosheth king over the rest of Israel in Mahanaim. David sent a blessing to the men of Jabesh-Gilead who had buried Saul. At the pool of Gibeon, the forces of Joab and Abner met, and a bloody battle broke out in which David's men prevailed. During the pursuit, Abner killed Asahel, Joab's brother, known for his speed, before both sides withdrew.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hebron:** The city where David was anointed and reigned over Judah for seven and a half years.
- **The Pool of Gibeon:** The place where the forces met and the battle began with a deadly duel between twelve men from each side.
- **Helkath-Hazzurim (the field of sharp swords):** The name given to the place where the 24 warriors fell together.
- **Asahel:** Joab's brother, known for his speed, who was killed by Abner during the pursuit.

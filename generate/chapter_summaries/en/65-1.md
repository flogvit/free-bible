**Main Theme:** A warning against false teachers and an exhortation to contend for the Christian faith.

**Content:** Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and brother of James, writes to warn the church about ungodly people who have slipped in unnoticed and abuse God's grace. He recalls God's judgment on Israel in the wilderness, fallen angels, and Sodom and Gomorrah as cautionary examples. The false teachers are compared to Cain, Balaam, and Korah. The letter urges believers to build themselves up in their faith, pray in the Holy Spirit, and keep themselves in God's love. It concludes with a powerful doxology praising God, who is able to keep them from falling and present them blameless before his glory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Faith Once for All Delivered:** The Christian faith is a complete deposit that the saints must strive to preserve.
- **Wandering Stars:** The false teachers are like wandering stars for whom the deepest darkness has been reserved forever.
- **Love Feasts (agape):** The false teachers are blemishes or stains on the fellowship meals of the church.
- **Preservation:** God is able to keep believers from falling and to present them faultless before his glory.

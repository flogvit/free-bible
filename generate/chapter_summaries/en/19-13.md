**Main Theme:** A lament psalm in which David moves from a desperate cry to renewed trust in God’s faithfulness.

**Content:** David opens with a fourfold "How long?" cry that expresses his deep pain over God’s apparent absence and the threat of his enemies. He asks God to look upon him and give light to his eyes, so that he does not fall asleep in death. The psalm turns dramatically in its final verse, where David confesses his trust in God’s grace (חֶסֶד) and promises to sing to the Lord who has dealt kindly with him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"How long?" (עַד־אָנָה):** The repeated question (four times) emphasizes the intensity of the cry of lament.
- **Hiding the face:** A picture of God’s perceived absence and the believer feeling abandoned.
- **Give light to the eyes:** A prayer for vitality and hope—darkened eyes symbolize death and despair.
- **Grace/faithful love (חֶסֶד):** God’s covenantal love, which is the foundation of David’s trust amid suffering.

**Main Theme:** The psalm praises the blessings that follow those who fear the Lord and walk in his ways.

**Content:** Psalm 128 is a song of ascent that begins with a blessing on the godly person. It describes how the fruit of labor, a harmonious home, and a large family are signs of God's blessing. The psalm concludes with a prayer that the believer may see Jerusalem's prosperity and their grandchildren, and it wishes peace upon Israel.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fear of the Lord (יְרֵא יְהוָה):** Reverence for the Lord is the foundation of all blessing in the psalm.
- **Vine:** The wife is compared to a fruitful vine within the home—a picture of fertility and life.
- **Olive shoots:** Children around the table are likened to olive shoots—symbols of growth, hope, and continuity.
- **Zion:** The source of blessing is the Lord's holy place, linking family happiness to the covenant relationship.

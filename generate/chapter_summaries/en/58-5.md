**Main Theme:** Jesus is appointed by God as high priest in the order of Melchizedek, and readers are urged toward spiritual maturity.

**Content:** The chapter explains that a high priest is chosen from among people to represent them before God and offer sacrifices for sins. Jesus did not take this honor upon himself but was called by God and appointed as a priest in the order of Melchizedek. Through suffering, prayers with loud cries and tears, he learned obedience, became perfect, and thus became the source of eternal salvation for all who obey him. The author rebukes the readers for their spiritual immaturity—they still need milk instead of solid food.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **High Priest:** One chosen from among people to represent them before God and offer sacrifices for sins.
- **Order of Melchizedek:** The eternal priesthood to which Jesus belongs, in contrast to the Levitical priesthood.
- **Obedience through Suffering:** Jesus learned obedience through what he suffered and thereby became perfect.
- **Milk and Solid Food:** A picture of spiritual immaturity versus maturity—the ability to distinguish between good and evil.

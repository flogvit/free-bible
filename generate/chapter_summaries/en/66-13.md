**Main Theme:** Two beastly figures receive power from the dragon to deceive the world and persecute the saints.

**Content:** A beast rises out of the sea with ten horns and seven heads bearing blasphemous names, receiving its power and throne from the dragon. One of its heads appears to have received a fatal wound that was healed, causing the whole earth to marvel and worship the beast. Another beast rises out of the earth, performs great signs, and deceives people into making an image of the first beast. It institutes a mark (666) that everyone must receive on their hand or forehead in order to buy or sell. Here the endurance and faith of the saints are required.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Beast from the Sea:** A blasphemous power with ten horns and seven heads, given authority by the dragon to make war against the saints for 42 months.
- **The Beast from the Earth:** A deceptive figure with lamb-like horns but speaking like a dragon, who performs signs and compels worship of the first beast.
- **The Mark of the Beast (666):** A sign on the hand or forehead required for economic participation, linked to the name or number of the beast.
- **The Book of Life:** Those who worship the beast do not have their names written in the book of life belonging to the Lamb who was slain.

**Main Theme:** Manasseh’s sin, repentance, and God’s grace – in contrast to his son Amon’s unrepentant wickedness.

**Content:** King Manasseh reigned for 55 years and did evil by rebuilding high places, worshiping idols, sacrificing his sons in the fire, and practicing sorcery. God warned him, but he would not listen. The Assyrians captured him and took him to Babylon, where he humbled himself and prayed to God. The Lord heard his prayer and brought him back to Jerusalem, where he removed the idols and restored the altar of the Lord. His son Amon followed his father’s evil example but did not humble himself – he was killed after only two years, and Josiah became king.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Host of heaven:** The astral deities that Manasseh worshiped – an expression of his total apostasy from the Lord.
- **Valley of Hinnom:** The place where Manasseh sacrificed his sons in the fire – a symbol of the cruelty of idolatry.
- **Humiliation (הִכָּנַע):** Manasseh’s sincere repentance in captivity, which led to God’s forgiveness and restoration.
- **Return to Jerusalem:** A sign of God’s grace and confirmation that the Lord is the only true God.

**Main Theme:** Detailed measurements and descriptions of the temple building's inner structure and decoration.

**Content:** Ezekiel is brought into the temple and receives exact measurements of the sanctuary (heikhal) and the Most Holy Place (20x20 cubits). Around the temple are three stories of side chambers, 33 in each story. The walls are decorated with cherubim and palm trees, where each cherub has two faces—a human face and a lion's face. In the sanctuary stands a wooden altar which the angel calls "the table that stands before the Lord."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Most Holy Place (קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים):** The innermost room, 20x20 cubits, God's most holy dwelling.
- **Cherubim and palm trees:** Carvings on the walls symbolizing God's presence and paradise.
- **The two faces of the cherubim:** A human face and a lion's face turned toward the palms, symbolizing God's creation.
- **The wooden altar/table:** An altar made of wood called "the table before the Lord," connected to worship and sacrifice.

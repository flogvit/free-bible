**Main Theme:** The organization of the people of Israel and temple services after returning from Babylon, including genealogies and assignments of duties.

**Content:** This chapter lists the Israelites who settled in Jerusalem after the exile, divided among the tribes of Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh. The service duties of the Levites and priests are described in detail, including gatekeepers, singers, and those responsible for holy vessels and supplies. David and the seer Samuel originally established the gatekeeping service. The chapter concludes with Saul's genealogy from Gibeon.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Genealogies (התיחשׂו):** The people's identity and belonging were preserved through accurate family records.
- **Gatekeepers (שֹׁעֲרִים):** Levites who guarded the temple gates in all four directions, established by David and Samuel.
- **Faithfulness (בֶּאֱמוּנָה):** The temple servants carried out their duties with reliability and loyalty.
- **Saul's Lineage:** The genealogy from Gibeon to Saul and his sons prepares for the coming narrative about Israel's first king.

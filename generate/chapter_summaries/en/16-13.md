**Main Theme:** Nehemiah carries out reforms to restore purity in the temple, Sabbath holiness, and the people’s separation from foreigners.

**Content:** After being away at the Persian king’s court, Nehemiah returns to Jerusalem and discovers several problems. He drives Tobiah out of the temple room that the priest Eliashib had given him, restores support for the Levites, closes the city gates on the Sabbath to prevent trade, and confronts those who had married foreign women. He uses Solomon’s fall as a warning example and concludes with prayers asking God to remember his faithful service.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Temple Cleansing:** Nehemiah removes Tobiah’s belongings from the temple chamber and dedicates it again for holy use.
- **Sabbath Holiness:** The city gates are closed to prevent trade and desecration of the day of rest.
- **Mixed Marriages:** Marriages with foreign women threaten Israel’s identity and faithfulness to the covenant.
- **"Remember me, my God":** Nehemiah’s repeated prayer that frames his reform work as service to God.

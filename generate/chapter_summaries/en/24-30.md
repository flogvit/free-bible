**Main Theme:** God promises to restore Israel and Judah from captivity and heal their wounds.

**Content:** God commands Jeremiah to write down prophecies about the people's future restoration. Although "the time of Jacob's trouble" will be great, the people will be saved from it. God will break the yoke of foreign oppression, heal the nation's wounds, and raise up a ruler from David's line. The enemies themselves will be struck by judgment, while Israel will live securely as God's people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The time of Jacob's trouble** (v.7): A time of great distress for the people, but one that ends in salvation.
- **The yoke broken** (v.8): A picture of liberation from foreign rule and oppression.
- **Healing of wounds** (v.17): God will heal the nation's deep injuries despite serious sin.
- **David, their king** (v.9): A future ruler from David's line whom God will raise up.
- **The Lord's storm** (v.23-24): God's wrath that strikes the wicked in the last days.

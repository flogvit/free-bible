**Main Theme:** David’s flight from Saul and God’s guidance through repeated prayer and divine protection.

**Content:** After seeking the Lord’s will, David saves the city of Keila from the Philistines but must flee when God reveals that the people of the city intend to hand him over to Saul. In the Desert of Ziph, Jonathan visits him and confirms that David will become king, and they renew their covenant. The Ziphites betray David’s hiding place to Saul, who launches an intense pursuit, but at the last moment Saul is forced to abandon the chase due to a Philistine attack.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ephod:** The sacred instrument brought by the priest Abiathar, which David uses to seek God’s guidance.
- **Keila:** The city David saves, yet which would have betrayed him—showing that human loyalty is unreliable.
- **Desert of Ziph:** The area where David hides and where Jonathan strengthens his faith in God’s promises.
- **Sela Hammahlekot (Rock of Partings):** The place where Saul had to abandon the pursuit—a testimony to God’s intervention.

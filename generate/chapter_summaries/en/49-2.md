**Main Theme:** God’s grace saves both Jews and Gentiles and unites them into one people in Christ.

**Content:** Paul describes how people were spiritually dead in sin, but God, in His rich mercy, has made us alive with Christ. Salvation is by grace through faith, not by works, so that no one may boast. Christ has broken down the dividing wall between Jews and Gentiles and reconciled both to God through the cross. Believers are now fellow citizens with the saints, built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus as the cornerstone.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dead in trespasses (v.1,5):** The former condition of humanity without God, unable to save themselves.
- **Saved by grace through faith (v.8-9):** Salvation is God’s gift, not the result of human works.
- **The dividing wall (v.14):** The hostility between Jews and Gentiles that Christ tore down.
- **The cornerstone (v.20):** Christ Jesus as the foundation upon which the entire church is built.
- **God’s temple (v.21-22):** Believers growing together into a holy dwelling place for God in the Spirit.

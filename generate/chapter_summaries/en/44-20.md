**Main Theme:** Paul's farewell journey and his exhortation to the elders of the church in Ephesus.

**Content:** Paul travels through Macedonia and Greece, encouraging the churches. In Troas, he raises the young man Eutychus, who had fallen from the third story and died. He continues sailing toward Jerusalem and summons the elders from Ephesus to Miletus for a farewell address. There, he exhorts them to guard themselves and the church against false teachers, reminds them of his own ministry, and concludes with prayer and a tearful farewell.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shepherds and Flock:** The elders are appointed by the Holy Spirit as overseers to guard God's church against "savage wolves."
- **The Race Completed:** Paul describes his ministry as a race he intends to finish, regardless of what awaits him.
- **"It is more blessed to give than to receive":** A saying of Jesus that Paul cites as an example of self-sacrificing service.
- **God's Church, Bought with His Blood:** Emphasizes the value of the church and the foundation for shepherding ministry.

**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about the signs of the end times, the destruction of the temple, and his return, emphasizing watchfulness and readiness.

**Content:** On the Mount of Olives, Jesus gives his disciples a prophetic discourse about the future. He predicts the destruction of the temple and describes the signs of the end times: false messiahs, wars, famines, earthquakes, and persecution. Jesus warns of great tribulation and promises that the Son of Man will come visibly to all, with power and glory. He stresses that no one knows the day or the hour, and urges watchfulness through parables about the fig tree, the thief, and the faithful servant.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Abomination That Causes Desolation (βδέλυγμα τῆς ἐρημώσεως):** A prophetic sign from Daniel warning of the desecration of the holy place.
- **Birth Pangs (ἀρχὴ ὠδίνων):** Wars, famines, and earthquakes are described as the beginning of sorrows before the new age.
- **The Coming of the Son of Man (παρουσία):** Jesus' return will be visible like lightning and accompanied by cosmic signs.
- **The Fig Tree:** A parable about recognizing the signs of the times, just as the budding of the tree signals summer.
- **The Faithful and Wise Servant:** The picture of the watchful disciple who faithfully manages his responsibility while waiting for the Lord's coming.

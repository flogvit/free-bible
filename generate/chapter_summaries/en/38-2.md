**Main Theme:** God promises to restore and protect Jerusalem, and calls His scattered people home.

**Content:** Zechariah first sees four horns symbolizing the nations that scattered Judah, and four craftsmen who will cast them down. Then he sees a man with a measuring line who is about to measure Jerusalem, but is told that the city will be without walls because of the great multitude—God Himself will be a wall of fire around it. The people are urged to flee from Babylon, for God will punish the oppressors and dwell in the midst of His people. Many nations will join themselves to the Lord, and all creation is called to be silent before Him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Four Horns:** Symbolize the powers that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.
- **Four Craftsmen:** God's instruments who will overthrow the oppressing nations.
- **Measuring Line:** Shows that Jerusalem is to be measured for rebuilding and growth.
- **Wall of Fire:** God promises to be a protective wall of fire around Jerusalem and His glory in her midst.
- **Apple of His Eye:** He who touches Israel touches the apple of God's eye—an expression of God's deep care.

**Main Theme:** David shows covenantal loyalty by caring for Jonathan’s disabled son, Mephibosheth.

**Content:** David asks if anyone remains from Saul’s house to whom he can show kindness for Jonathan’s sake. The servant Ziba tells him about Mephibosheth, Jonathan’s son who is lame in both feet and lives in Lo-debar. David brings him back, returns all the land that belonged to Saul, and allows him to eat at the king’s table as one of his own sons. Ziba and his household are appointed to manage the land for Mephibosheth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hesed (חֶסֶד):** Covenantal loyalty and grace – David shows "God’s grace" to Jonathan’s family.
- **The King’s Table:** A symbol of restoration and full membership in the royal family.
- **Lame in both feet:** Mephibosheth was vulnerable and powerless, yet still honored.
- **Lo-debar:** The name may mean "nothing" – Mephibosheth is brought from obscurity to honor.

**Main Theme:** The chapter describes the resettlement of Jerusalem and Judah after the exile, focusing on who lived in "the holy city" and how the people were organized.

**Content:** The leaders settled in Jerusalem, while one out of ten from the rest of the people was chosen by lot to live there – those who volunteered were blessed by the people. The chapter lists family heads from Judah and Benjamin, as well as priests, Levites, temple servants, and gatekeepers with their duties. It also provides an overview of the cities in Judah and Benjamin where the rest of the people settled.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The holy city (עִיר הַקֹּדֶשׁ):** A designation for Jerusalem that emphasizes the city's religious significance.
- **Lot casting:** The method used to decide who would live in Jerusalem – one out of ten was chosen.
- **Priests and Levites:** Those who served in the temple and were responsible for worship and praise.
- **Gatekeepers:** Those who guarded the gates of the city and the temple – numbering 172.

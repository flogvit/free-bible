**Main Theme:** Peter’s defense of the mission to the Gentiles and the spread of the gospel to Antioch.

**Content:** Peter is criticized by the circumcised believers in Jerusalem for eating with Gentiles, but he defends himself by recounting his vision and how the Holy Spirit fell upon Cornelius’ household just as it had on the day of Pentecost. The church accepts this explanation and praises God that even the Gentiles have received repentance leading to life. In Antioch, the gospel spreads among Greek-speaking people, and Barnabas is sent there to strengthen the believers. He brings Saul from Tarsus, and together they teach for a year—it is here that the disciples are first called "Christians." The prophet Agabus predicts a famine, and the church sends relief to Judea.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Sheet with Animals:** God’s vision showing that no people should be called unclean or unholy.
- **The Gift of the Holy Spirit:** Proof that God accepts Gentiles on equal footing with Jews.
- **Christians:** The name first used for disciples in Antioch.
- **Antioch:** The center of the mission to the Gentiles and the first church comprising both Jews and Gentiles.

**Main Theme:** God sets the boundaries of Canaan and appoints leaders to distribute the land.

**Content:** God gives Moses detailed boundaries for the land of Canaan that Israel is to inherit. The southern boundary runs from the Wilderness of Zin along Edom to Kadesh-barnea and onward to the Brook of Egypt. The western boundary is the Mediterranean Sea; the northern boundary extends from the Mediterranean via Mount Hor to Hazar-enan; and the eastern boundary follows the Jordan River from the Sea of Galilee to the Dead Sea. Eleazar and Joshua, together with one leader from each of the nine and a half tribes that will settle west of the Jordan, are given responsibility for distributing the land.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Boundaries (גְּבוּל):** God’s concrete definition of the territory shows that the promise is geographically specific and not arbitrary.
- **Inheritance/Lot (נַחֲלָה/גוֹרָל):** The land is a gift from God, distributed by casting lots, which underscores God’s sovereignty.
- **Nine and a half tribes:** Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have already received their inheritance east of the Jordan, so only the remaining tribes will inherit in Canaan.
- **Tribal leaders (נָשִׂיא):** One leader from each tribe ensures fair representation in the distribution of the land.

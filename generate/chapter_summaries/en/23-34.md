**Main Theme:** God's universal judgment against the nations, with Edom as the primary example of total destruction.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a summons to all nations to hear about the Lord's wrath. God's sword will descend upon Edom like a sacrificial offering, and the land will be transformed into burning pitch and sulfur. This is "the day of vengeance" and the year of recompense for Zion's cause. The land will remain desolate forever, inhabited only by wild animals and terrifying creatures, and the Lord's book guarantees that all this will come to pass.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's sword (חֶרֶב לַיהוָה):** God's instrument of judgment, saturated with blood from a cosmic sacrifice.
- **The day of vengeance (יוֹם נָקָם):** The time of God's retribution, linked to his defense of Zion.
- **Edom/Bosrah:** Represents hostile nations struck by God's final judgment.
- **Wild animals and desolation:** Owls, ravens, jackals, and liliths take over the ruined land as signs of complete destruction.
- **The Lord's book:** The guarantee that the prophecy will be fulfilled—everything God has said will happen.

**Main Theme:** God promises to pour out His Spirit on all people and warns of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.

**Content:** God promises to pour out His Spirit on all people—sons and daughters will prophesy, old men will have dreams, and young men will see visions. Even male and female servants will receive the Spirit. Cosmic signs such as blood, fire, and pillars of smoke, along with the darkening of the sun and moon, will herald the day of the Lord. Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved, for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be deliverance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Outpouring of the Spirit:** God’s Spirit is given to all people regardless of age, gender, or social status.
- **Prophecy, dreams, and visions:** Signs of the Spirit’s work among God’s people.
- **The day of the Lord:** The great and dreadful day when God intervenes in history.
- **Cosmic signs:** The sun and moon are darkened as warnings of the day of the Lord.
- **Salvation through the name of the Lord:** The promise of deliverance for all who call on God.

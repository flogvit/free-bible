**Main Theme:** Hezekiah’s pride leads to a prophecy about the Babylonian exile.

**Content:** King Hezekiah receives envoys from Merodach-Baladan, king of Babylon, who has heard about his illness and recovery. Hezekiah proudly displays all his treasures and wealth. The prophet Isaiah confronts him and declares that everything he owns, along with his descendants, will be taken to Babylon. Hezekiah accepts the judgment and finds comfort in the promise that there will be peace during his lifetime.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Treasures:** Symbolize Hezekiah’s wealth and pride, which become the cause of future loss.
- **Babylon:** Introduced here as the future oppressor, a foreshadowing of the exile.
- **Sarisar (court officials):** Hezekiah’s descendants will serve as court officials in Babylon’s palace—a humiliating fate.
- **Peace in my days:** Hezekiah’s ambiguous response, showing both acceptance of God’s judgment and perhaps self-centered relief.

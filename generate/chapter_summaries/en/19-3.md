**Main Theme:** David's trusting prayer to God in the midst of life-threatening persecution from his own son, Absalom.

**Content:** The psalm is written in the context of David's flight from his son Absalom. David describes how many enemies have risen against him and mock that God will not save him. Yet he confesses his trust in the Lord as his shield and the one who lifts up his head. David can sleep safely because the Lord supports him, and he fears not even tens of thousands of foes. The psalm concludes with a proclamation that salvation belongs to the Lord, and a blessing upon God's people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shield (מָגֵן):** God as David's protector and defense against the enemies surrounding him.
- **Lifts my head:** A picture of the restoration of honor and dignity in the midst of humiliation.
- **God's holy mountain:** The place where God answers prayer, symbolizing God's nearness and faithfulness.
- **Sleep and wake:** Expresses total trust in God's care and protection through the night.
- **Salvation belongs to the Lord (לַיהוָה הַיְשׁוּעָה):** The climax of the psalm, affirming that all deliverance comes from God alone.

**Main Theme:** God’s judgment on disobedient Israel and the promise of a new creation for His servants.

**Content:** God has stretched out His hands toward a rebellious people who practice idolatry in gardens and sacrifice to foreign gods. He announces a sharp contrast: His servants shall eat, drink, and rejoice, while the disobedient shall hunger, thirst, and be put to shame. The chapter culminates in the promise of new heavens and a new earth, where Jerusalem will be a source of joy, death will lose its power, and peace will reign—even the wolf and the lamb shall graze together.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **New Heavens and New Earth (v.17):** God’s radical re-creation in which the old order is no longer remembered—a complete restoration.
- **My Servants (vv.13–15):** The faithful who stand in contrast to the disobedient and receive God’s blessings.
- **The Wolf and the Lamb (v.25):** A picture of eschatological peace, where nature’s enmity ceases on God’s holy mountain.

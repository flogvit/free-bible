**Main Theme:** James warns about the transience of wealth, encourages patience in suffering, and highlights the power of prayer within the community of believers.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a sharp warning to the rich who have hoarded treasures by withholding workers' wages—their wealth will testify against them on the day of judgment. Believers are urged to be patient like a farmer waiting for rain, because the Lord's coming is near. The prophets and Job are held up as examples of endurance. James warns against swearing oaths and instead calls for simple honesty. The chapter concludes with guidance on prayer for the sick, mutual confession, and restoring those who have strayed from the truth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rotten wealth (v.2-3):** Rust symbolizes that material prosperity is temporary and will testify against those who have accumulated it unjustly.
- **The Lord's coming/παρουσία (v.7-8):** Christ's imminent return is the basis for exhortations to patience and watchfulness.
- **Prayer of faith (v.15-16):** The earnest prayer of a righteous person has great power, illustrated by Elijah who prayed and the rain stopped.
- **The elders and anointing (v.14):** The church's elders are called to anoint the sick with oil and pray—an expression of the community's care.
- **Turning a sinner back (v.19-20):** Whoever brings a straying person back saves a soul from death and covers over many sins.

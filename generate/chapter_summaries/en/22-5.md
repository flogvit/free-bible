**Main Theme:** The chapter portrays longing, loss, and deep love through the woman's search for her beloved and her praise of him.

**Content:** The beloved comes to his garden and enjoys its fruits (v.1). The woman, in a state between sleep and wakefulness, hears her beloved knocking at the door, but when she opens it, he is gone. She searches for him throughout the city, where the watchmen beat and wound her. She asks the daughters of Jerusalem to tell him that she is sick with love. At their request, she describes him in a detailed poem of praise—from head to foot—and declares that he is her beloved and friend.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Garden:** A symbol of intimacy and loving fellowship between the lovers.
- **Myrrh:** A precious fragrant oil representing the preciousness and sensual dimension of love.
- **The Watchmen:** Represent obstacles and suffering on the path to finding the beloved.
- **"Sick with love":** An expression for the intense, all-consuming power of longing.
- **The Beloved's Beauty (vv.10–16):** A detailed description using gold, ivory, and precious stones that highlights his incomparable worth.

**Main Theme:** An appeal to the whole earth and all creation to praise the Lord as the only true God and righteous Judge.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a threefold call to sing a new song to the Lord, proclaiming his salvation and glory among all nations. It contrasts the Lord's greatness as Creator of heaven with the idols of the peoples. All nations are summoned to worship him in holy splendor and bring offerings to his courts. The psalm culminates in a vision of the entire creation—heaven, earth, sea, fields, and trees—rejoicing because the Lord is coming to judge the world with righteousness and faithfulness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **New song (שִׁיר חָדָשׁ):** Expresses renewal in worship as a response to God's saving acts.
- **Holy splendor (הַדְרַת־קֹדֶשׁ):** Describes the reverent attitude and beauty of worshiping the Lord.
- **Creation's rejoicing:** Heaven, earth, sea, fields, and trees are personified as participants in praise (vv. 11–12).
- **Righteous judgment (בְּצֶדֶק/בֶּאֱמוּנָה):** The Lord's coming judgment is characterized by righteousness and faithfulness.

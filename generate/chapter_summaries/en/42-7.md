**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals his authority through healing, raising the dead, and forgiving sins—and calls people to faith.

**Content:** Jesus heals a Roman officer’s servant from a distance and commends the officer’s exceptional faith. In Nain, he raises a widow’s only son from the dead, and the people praise God for having visited his people. When John the Baptist sends messengers to ask whether Jesus is the one who was to come, Jesus answers by pointing to his works: the blind see, the dead are raised, and the good news is preached to the poor. At the house of the Pharisee Simon, Jesus forgives a sinful woman and explains that her great love testifies to having been greatly forgiven.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Faith (πίστις):** The officer’s faith in Jesus’ authority and the woman’s saving faith highlight the central role of faith.
- **Forgiveness (ἄφεσις):** Jesus forgives sins with divine authority, which astonishes the onlookers.
- **"The One Who Comes" (ὁ ἐρχόμενος):** A messianic title that John inquires about, and which Jesus confirms through his works.
- **Compassion (σπλαγχνίζομαι):** Jesus’ deep compassion for the widow reveals God’s care for those who mourn and are marginalized.

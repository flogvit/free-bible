**Main Theme:** David prepares for the building of the temple and entrusts the task to Solomon, urging him to remain faithful to God.

**Content:** David gathers enormous quantities of materials for the temple—gold, silver, bronze, iron, cedar wood, and stone. God had forbidden David from building the temple because he had shed much blood in wars, but promised that his son Solomon, "the man of peace," would be the one to build it. David urges Solomon to be strong and courageous, and to keep the Lord's law in order to succeed. He also commands the leaders of Israel to support Solomon in this holy project.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The man of peace"** – Solomon (שְׁלֹמֹה) was to bring peace (שָׁלוֹם) and rest, in contrast to David's warfare.
- **Preparation** – David gathered materials "in my distress" (בְעָנְיִי), demonstrating his humility and zeal for God's house.
- **Blood and war** – The reason David was not allowed to build the temple; God's house required a builder of peace.
- **"Be strong and courageous"** – An exhortation echoing Joshua, emphasizing the need for faithfulness to the Torah.

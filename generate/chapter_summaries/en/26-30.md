**Main Theme:** God’s judgment on Egypt and its allies through Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon.

**Content:** Ezekiel prophesies about "the day of the Lord" – a day of darkness and judgment against Egypt. God will use Nebuchadnezzar as His instrument to destroy the land, shatter idols, and scatter the Egyptians among the nations. Egyptian cities such as Memphis, Thebes, and Zoan will be struck. The image of Pharaoh’s arm being broken while the king of Babylon’s arm is strengthened illustrates the shift in power. The purpose is that all may know that the Lord is God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The day of the Lord:** A day of judgment and darkness, described as "the day of clouds" and a time for the nations.
- **Pharaoh’s broken arm:** A symbol of Egypt’s shattered military power that cannot be healed.
- **Nebuchadnezzar:** The king of Babylon, chosen by God as His instrument to carry out judgment.
- **Egyptian cities:** Memphis, Thebes, Zoan, and others are named as targets of God’s judgment and fire.

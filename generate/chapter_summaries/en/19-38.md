**Main Theme:** A penitential psalm in which David confesses his sin and cries out to God for mercy amid physical and social suffering.

**Content:** The psalmist suffers under God's discipline and recognizes that his sin has led to physical pain and grievous wounds. Friends and relatives keep their distance, while enemies set traps and speak evil. Yet the psalmist remains silent before accusers and places his hope in the Lord alone, earnestly praying that God will not abandon him but come to his aid.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's arrows (v.3):** A picture of God's discipline striking the psalmist and causing suffering.
- **Heavy burden (v.5):** Sin is described as a load too heavy to bear.
- **Deaf and mute (vv.14–15):** The psalmist stays silent before the accusers' charges and entrusts his cause to God.
- **For remembrance (v.1):** A liturgical heading suggesting the psalm's use in prayer and worship.

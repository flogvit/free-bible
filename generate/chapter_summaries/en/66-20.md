**Main Theme:** Satan's binding and final defeat, the millennium, and the final judgment at the great white throne.

**Content:** An angel binds Satan in the abyss for a thousand years, while martyrs and the faithful are raised to reign with Christ (the first resurrection). After the thousand years, Satan is released, gathers Gog and Magog for battle against the holy ones, but fire from heaven consumes them, and Satan is cast into the lake of fire. At the great white throne, all the dead are judged according to their deeds; death and Hades are thrown into the lake of fire (the second death), along with everyone whose name is not written in the book of life.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Millennium (χίλια ἔτη):** The period during which Satan is bound and the holy ones reign with Christ.
- **The first resurrection:** The resurrection of believers to life and dominion; the second death has no power over them.
- **The great white throne:** God's judgment seat where all people are judged according to their deeds.
- **The book of life:** The book that determines final destiny; those not written in it are cast into the lake of fire.
- **The second death:** The lake of fire, the final punishment for Satan, death itself, and the ungodly.

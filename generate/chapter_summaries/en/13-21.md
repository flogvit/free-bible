**Main Theme:** David’s sin in the census and God’s grace through judgment and atonement at the threshing floor of Ornan.

**Content:** Satan incites David to number Israel, which provokes God’s wrath. The prophet Gad gives David a choice among three punishments, and David chooses to fall into God’s hands rather than human ones. A plague kills 70,000, but God stops the angel at the threshing floor of Ornan. David buys the site for 600 gold shekels, builds an altar, and offers sacrifices—and God answers with fire from heaven.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Satan as tempter:** For the first time in Chronicles, Satan is mentioned as the one who incites David to sin.
- **Census:** Symbolizes pride and self-reliance rather than trust in God.
- **God’s angel with a drawn sword:** A picture of God’s judgment halted by His mercy.
- **Threshing floor of Ornan:** The place that later becomes the temple site—a turning point from judgment to worship.
- **Fire from heaven:** A sign of God’s acceptance of David’s sacrifice and repentance.

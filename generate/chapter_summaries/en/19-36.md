**Main Theme:** A contrast between the wickedness of the ungodly and God's boundless grace and righteousness.

**Content:** The psalm, attributed to David, the Lord’s servant, opens with a description of the nature of sin in the heart of the ungodly—without fear of God, full of self-deception and evil. This is powerfully contrasted with God’s grace that reaches to the heavens, his faithfulness as high as the clouds, and his righteousness like mighty mountains. The psalmist seeks refuge under God’s wings and asks for continued grace, while the ungodly fall and cannot rise again.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Chesed (grace/faithful love):** God’s grace is described as infinitely precious and reaching to the heavens (v. 6, 8).
- **The Fountain of Life:** With God is the fountain of life, and in his light we see light (v. 10).
- **The Shadow of Wings:** A picture of God’s protection where people can seek refuge (v. 8).
- **The Depths and the Mountains:** God’s righteousness and judgment are compared to the highest mountains and the deepest sea (v. 7).

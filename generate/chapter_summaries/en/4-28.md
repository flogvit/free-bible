**Main Theme:** God gives Israel detailed regulations for the regular sacrifices that are to maintain worship throughout the year.

**Content:** The chapter establishes the daily burnt offerings (two lambs, morning and evening), the Sabbath offering (two additional lambs), and the new moon offering (bulls, rams, and lambs with a sin offering). Furthermore, it describes the offerings for Passover (the 14th day) and the Festival of Unleavened Bread (7 days from the 15th day of the first month), as well as the Day of Firstfruits/Feast of Weeks. All sacrifices are to be accompanied by grain and drink offerings in specified amounts.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tamid (תמיד):** The continual, daily sacrifice that ensures unbroken worship of God.
- **Fire Offering (אִשֶּׁה):** A sacrifice burned on the altar as a pleasing aroma to the Lord.
- **New Moon Offering:** Special sacrifices at the beginning of each month that mark the holy rhythm of time.
- **Sin Offering (חַטָּאת):** A male goat sacrificed to make atonement for the people.

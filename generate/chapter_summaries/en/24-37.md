**Main Theme:** Jeremiah is imprisoned after false accusations but holds fast to his message about the fall of Jerusalem.

**Content:** During King Zedekiah's reign, the king seeks counsel from Jeremiah while the Chaldeans besiege Jerusalem. God reveals that the Egyptian army, which had temporarily caused the Chaldeans to withdraw, will return to Egypt, and the Chaldeans will come back and burn the city. Jeremiah is arrested at the Benjamin Gate, falsely accused of intending to defect to the enemy, and placed in prison. The king secretly summons him and receives the same message: the city will be handed over to the king of Babylon. At Jeremiah's request, the king moves him to the guard court with a daily bread ration.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Guard Court (חֲצַר הַמַּטָּרָה):** The prison area in the palace where Jeremiah received better conditions than in Jonathan's dungeon.
- **Pharaoh's Army:** The Egyptian military force that gave false hope by temporarily easing the siege.
- **Benjamin Gate:** The city gate where Jeremiah was arrested while on his way to his homeland.
- **Secret Consultation:** Zedekiah's fear of his own men led him to seek God's word in secret.

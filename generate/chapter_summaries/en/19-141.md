**Main Theme:** A prayer for protection against sin and wicked people, with trust in God's righteous judgment.

**Content:** David cries out to God for help and asks that his prayer be like incense and the evening sacrifice. He asks God to guard his mouth and heart from evil and from fellowship with the ungodly. The psalmist prefers righteous correction over the flattery of the wicked, expressing confidence that God will protect him while the wicked fall into their own traps.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Incense and the evening sacrifice (v.2):** Prayer is compared to the holy offerings of temple service that rise up to God.
- **Guard over the mouth (v.3):** A prayer for self-control and protection from sinful words.
- **Righteous correction (v.5):** Correction from a righteous person is better than flattery from the wicked.
- **Traps and snares (vv.9–10):** The plans of the wicked against the psalmist will backfire on them.

**Main Theme:** God hears Hezekiah’s prayer and extends his life by fifteen years in response to his faithful appeal.

**Content:** King Hezekiah becomes gravely ill and is told by the prophet Isaiah that he will die. Hezekiah turns his face to the wall, prays to God, and weeps bitterly as he recalls his faithfulness. God hears his prayer and sends Isaiah back with a promise of fifteen additional years of life, deliverance from Assyria, and protection for Jerusalem. As a sign, the shadow on Ahaz’s sundial moves ten steps backward. Hezekiah expresses his gratitude in a psalm, vowing to praise God for the rest of his life.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Shadow Moving Backward:** A miraculous sign from God confirming the promise of healing and extended life.
- **Hezekiah’s Prayer and Tears:** Expresses his sincere appeal to God in a life-threatening crisis and recalls his faithful walk with Him.
- **Fifteen Years:** The specific period of time God adds to Hezekiah’s life in answer to his prayer.
- **Sheol/The Realm of the Dead:** In Hezekiah’s psalm, the silence of the dead is contrasted with the living singing praises to God.

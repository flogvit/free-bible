**Main Theme:** God fulfills his promise of a son to Abraham and Sarah, demonstrating his faithfulness toward both Isaac's and Ishmael's descendants.

**Content:** In their old age, Sarah gives birth to Isaac, and Abraham circumcises him on the eighth day as God commanded. When Sarah sees Hagar's son laughing, she demands that they be sent away; God comforts Abraham and promises to make Ishmael into a great nation as well. In the wilderness, God hears the boy's voice and opens Hagar's eyes so that she sees a well. Abraham makes a covenant with King Abimelech at Be'er-Sheba, plants a tamarisk tree, and calls upon the Lord as El Olam.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Isaac (יִצְחָק):** The name means "laughter" and recalls Sarah's joy and God's faithful fulfillment of his promise.
- **Circumcision:** The sign of the covenant between God and Abraham's offspring, performed on the eighth day.
- **The Well in the Wilderness:** A symbol of God's care for Hagar and Ishmael in their distress.
- **Be'er-Sheba:** Means "well of the oath" (from oath/שְׁבֻעָה) or "seven wells" (from the seven lambs), the place where Abraham and Abimelech swore a covenant.

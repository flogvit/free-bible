**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts the fate of the righteous and the wicked, showing how righteousness leads to life and blessing.

**Content:** Proverbs 11 teaches that righteousness saves from death, while wealth cannot help on the day of wrath. The chapter highlights how righteous people are a blessing to society, whereas the wicked bring destruction. Recurring themes include honesty in business, wisdom in speech, and the paradox of generosity: those who give receive more.

**Key Words/Images:**
- *False weights* (v.1): A symbol of dishonesty in trade—an abomination to Jehovah.
- *Righteousness* (צְדָקָה): The fundamental quality that saves from death and smooths the path of life.
- *Tree of life* (v.30): The fruit of the righteous gives life to others.
- *The city* (vv.10–11): Illustrates how the righteous and the wicked affect the entire community.

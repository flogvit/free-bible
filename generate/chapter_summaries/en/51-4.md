**Main Theme:** Paul concludes the letter with exhortations concerning prayer and wise conduct, along with personal greetings from his fellow workers.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an instruction to masters to treat their slaves justly. Paul urges perseverance in prayer and asks for intercession regarding his proclamation of the mystery of Christ. Believers are to act wisely toward outsiders and speak with grace. The chapter ends with greetings from coworkers such as Tychicus, Onesimus, Epaphras, and Luke, as well as practical instructions concerning the exchange of letters with Laodicea.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Prayer (προσευχή):** Perseverance in prayer with alertness and thankfulness is central to the Christian life.
- **Door for the word (θύραν τοῦ λόγου):** A metaphor for the opportunity to proclaim the gospel, which Paul requests.
- **Salt (ἅλατι):** Speech seasoned with salt symbolizes wisdom and relevance in communication with others.
- **Fellow workers (συνεργοί):** Paul's network of faithful coworkers demonstrates the fellowship and cooperation within the church.
- **Chains (δεσμῶν):** Paul's imprisonment for Christ's sake, which he asks the recipients to remember.

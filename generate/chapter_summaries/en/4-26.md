**Main Theme:** God orders a new census of Israel on the plains of Moab to prepare for the distribution of the promised land.

**Content:** Moses and Eleazar carry out a count of all men fit for war from twenty years old and upward. In total, 601,730 men are counted across the twelve tribes. The Levites are counted separately (23,000 from one month old and upward) and receive no inheritance share. The land is to be distributed according to the size of each tribe by casting lots. Of that generation counted at Sinai, only Caleb and Joshua remain—the others died in the wilderness as the Lord had said.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Census:** A mustering of all men fit for war as a basis for distributing the land.
- **Casting Lots:** The method used to distribute the land fairly among the tribes.
- **Generational Shift:** The entire wilderness generation has died except Caleb and Joshua, who demonstrated faith.
- **Daughters of Zelophehad:** Mentioned as an example of a family without sons, raising questions about inheritance rights.
- **Special Status of the Levites:** They are counted separately and receive no territorial inheritance.

**Main Theme:** A warning against false teachers who lead people away from the truth, and assurance that God judges the ungodly while saving the righteous.

**Content:** False teachers will arise in the church, driven by greed, leading many astray with destructive heresies. Peter uses three historical examples to show that God punishes sin: the angels in Tartaros, the flood (where Noah was preserved), and Sodom and Gomorrah (where Lot was rescued). These false teachers follow Balaam's way, promising freedom while they themselves are slaves of corruption. Those who turn back to sin after knowing the truth are like a dog returning to its vomit.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **False Teachers (ψευδοδιδάσκαλοι):** Those who spread destructive heresies and deny the Master who bought them.
- **Tartaros:** The deep darkness where sinful angels are held until judgment.
- **Balaam's Way:** A symbol of error motivated by love for unjust gain.
- **The Dog and the Pig:** Proverbs illustrating apostasy—returning to sin after knowing the way of righteousness.

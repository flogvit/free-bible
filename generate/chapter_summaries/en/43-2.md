**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals his glory through signs and proclaims his authority over the temple.

**Content:** At a wedding in Cana, Jesus performs his first sign by turning water into wine, which reveals his glory and strengthens the disciples' faith. Then Jesus goes to Jerusalem for the Passover celebration, where he cleanses the temple by driving out the merchants and declaring that they have made his Father's house a marketplace. When the Jews demand a sign of his authority, Jesus answers enigmatically about tearing down the temple and raising it in three days—a reference to his own death and resurrection. Many in Jerusalem came to believe because of the signs Jesus performed, but he did not entrust himself to them because he knew what was in man's heart.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sign (σημεῖον):** John uses this term for Jesus' miracles that reveal his divine glory and identity.
- **Water into wine:** Symbolizes the transition from the old to the new, from Jewish purification rituals to Jesus' abundance.
- **Temple/body (ναός):** Jesus identifies his own body as the true temple—the dwelling place of God among people.
- **My hour (ἡ ὥρα μου):** Refers to Jesus' impending suffering and glorification, a central theme in the Gospel of John.

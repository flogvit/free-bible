**Main Theme:** A psalm of trust that assures God's protection of the righteous and judgment upon the faithless.

**Content:** Those who trust in the Lord are unshakeable like Mount Zion. Just as the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the Lord surrounds his people forever. The wicked shall not hold dominion over the portion of the righteous, lest the righteous be led into wrongdoing. The psalm asks the Lord to do good to those who are good, while those who turn aside to crooked paths shall be led away with evildoers. The psalm concludes with the blessing "Peace upon Israel."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Zion:** A symbol of stability and permanence for those who trust in the Lord.
- **The mountains around Jerusalem:** An image of the Lord's protective presence surrounding his people.
- **The scepter of the wicked:** Represents foreign or oppressive rule that shall not have lasting power over the righteous.
- **Crooked paths:** An image of moral deviation and faithfulness leading to judgment.

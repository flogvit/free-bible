**Main Theme:** The chapter emphasizes God's sovereignty over human plans and ways, as well as the importance of wisdom, humility, and righteous speech.

**Content:** Humans make plans in their hearts, but the Lord directs their steps and has the final word (vv. 1, 9, 33). Pride leads to downfall, while humility and reverence for God lead away from evil (vv. 5, 18–19). The words we speak hold great power—kind words are like honey that brings healing, while malicious speech burns like fire (vv. 24, 27). Wisdom and understanding are more valuable than gold or silver (v. 16).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's Sovereignty:** The lot is cast, but the decision comes from the Lord—God has control over all things (v. 33).
- **Pride Before a Fall:** Arrogance precedes collapse, and a haughty spirit leads to ruin (v. 18).
- **Honeycomb:** Kind words are compared to sweet honey that brings health to the body (v. 24).
- **The Way That Leads to Death:** A path may seem right, but it ends in death—a warning against self-deception (v. 25).
- **Gray Hair as a Crown of Glory:** Old age walked in righteousness is a crown of glory (v. 31).

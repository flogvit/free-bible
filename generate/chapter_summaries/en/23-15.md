**Main Theme:** A prophecy of Moab's total destruction and the people's deep sorrow.

**Content:** Isaiah proclaims that Moab's cities, including Ar, Kir, and Dibon, will be destroyed in one night. The people will mourn with weeping, shaved heads, and sackcloth, while cries echo from city to city across the land. Even the prophet expresses compassion: "My heart cries out for Moab." Water sources will dry up, and Dimon's waters will be filled with blood, while a lion waits for the survivors.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Nightly Destruction:** The cities of Ar and Kir are destroyed in one night—symbolizing sudden and total judgment.
- **Mourning Rituals:** Shaved heads, cut-off beards, and sackcloth show extreme grief.
- **Dried-Up Water Sources:** Nimrim's waters are ruined—symbolizing economic and physical devastation.
- **Dimon's Blood:** The water is filled with blood, and a lion threatens the survivors—God's judgment is complete.

**Main Theme:** The vision of the four chariots patrolling the earth and the prophetic coronation pointing to the coming Branch.

**Content:** Zechariah sees four chariots with horses of different colors emerging between two bronze mountains—they represent the four winds of heaven going out from the Lord’s presence. The black and white horses go north, bringing God’s Spirit rest there, while the dappled ones go south. Then Zechariah is instructed to make crowns of silver and gold and place them on the head of the high priest Joshua, along with a prophecy about the "Branch" who will build the Lord’s temple, bear majesty, and unite the offices of king and priest in peace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Four Chariots:** Represent the four winds of heaven going out from the Lord, ruler over all the earth, to carry out his will.
- **Bronze Mountains:** Symbolize God’s heavenly dwelling or throne room, from which his messengers go forth.
- **Branch (צֶמַח):** A messianic title for the coming Savior who will build the temple and reign.
- **Crown:** A prophetic sign uniting the offices of king and priest, to be kept as a memorial in the temple.

**Main Theme:** God demonstrates His power over Egypt through three plagues while Pharaoh repeatedly hardens his heart.

**Content:** The chapter describes three of Egypt's plagues: frogs, gnats (כִּנִּם), and swarms of insects (עָרֹב). During the plague of frogs, Pharaoh asks Moses to intercede, but breaks his promise once the plague ends. Egypt’s magicians are unable to replicate the plague of gnats and acknowledge that this is "the finger of God". With the swarm of insects, God sets apart Goshen, where the Israelites live, as a sign that He rules even in the midst of the land.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Finger of God (אֶצְבַּע אֱלֹהִים):** The magicians’ recognition that the plagues surpass their own power and are divine works.
- **Goshen:** The Israelites’ land, spared from the swarm of insects—showing God’s distinction between His people and the Egyptians.
- **Hardening:** Pharaoh’s repeated pattern of promising release under pressure but reneging once the plague ends.
- **Intercession:** Moses prays to God on Pharaoh’s behalf, and God answers—demonstrating Moses’ role as mediator.

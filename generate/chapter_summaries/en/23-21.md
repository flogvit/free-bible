**Main Theme:** Three Oracles of Judgment against Babylon (the Desert Sea), Dumah (Edom), and Arabia.

**Content:** The prophet receives a harsh vision concerning the fall of Babylon, in which Elam and Media are called to attack, and he reacts with physical pain and anguish. A watchman proclaims: "Fallen, fallen is Babylon!" From Seir comes a question to the watchman during the night, but the answer remains ambiguous. The caravans of Dedan must seek refuge, and Tema shall help refugees fleeing from war. Within one year, the glory of Kedar will come to an end.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Watchman (הַמְצַפֶּה):** One who keeps watch and conveys God's message about coming judgment.
- **"Fallen, fallen is Babylon":** The central message of judgment concerning the downfall of Babylon.
- **Night and Morning:** Symbolize uncertainty about the future in the oracle concerning Dumah.
- **The Glory of Kedar:** The strength and prosperity of the Arab tribes that will vanish.

**Main Theme:** Joab uses cunning to bring David to fetch Absalom home from exile, but full reconciliation must wait.

**Content:** Joab perceives that David's heart longs for Absalom and sends a wise woman from Tekoa to the king with a fabricated story about a widow who risks losing her only surviving son. The story leads David to promise protection, after which the woman reveals that it actually concerns Absalom's situation. David allows Absalom to return to Jerusalem but refuses to see him for two years. Only after Absalom burns Joab's barley field to force action does Joab arrange a meeting where David finally kisses his son.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Wise Woman from Tekoa:** Used as a tool to present David's own situation through a parallel story.
- **The Coal (v.7):** The woman's image of the last son as the only hope for the family's future.
- **Water that flows away (v.14):** An image of life's transience and that death is irreversible – yet God seeks to bring back those who are exiled.
- **Joab's Barley Field:** Absalom's desperate act to break his isolation and force attention.
- **The King's Kiss (v.33):** A symbol of reconciliation between father and son after a long separation.

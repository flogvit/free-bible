**Main Theme:** Paul presents the gospel as God’s power for salvation and shows how God’s wrath is revealed against human ungodliness.

**Content:** Paul greets the church in Rome and expresses his desire to visit them. He declares that the gospel is God’s power for salvation to everyone who believes—to the Jew first, and also to the Greek—and that God’s righteousness is revealed in it from faith to faith. At the same time, God’s wrath is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and injustice of people who suppress the truth, for God has made himself known through creation. People who rejected God were given over to their own desires and a depraved mind, resulting in widespread sin and moral decay.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gospel (εὐαγγέλιον):** The good news about Jesus Christ, which is God’s power for salvation to everyone who believes.
- **God’s righteousness (δικαιοσύνη θεοῦ):** Revealed in the gospel "from faith to faith"—the foundation of salvation.
- **God’s wrath (ὀργὴ θεοῦ):** Revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and injustice among people who suppress the truth.
- **Natural revelation:** God’s invisible nature, eternal power, and divine character are clearly seen through creation, so that people are without excuse.
- **Given over (παρέδωκεν):** God gave those who rejected him over to their own desires, shameful passions, and a depraved mind.

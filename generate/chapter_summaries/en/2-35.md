**Main Theme:** Moses gathers the people of Israel to remind them of the Sabbath commandment and to invite voluntary offerings and labor for the Tabernacle.

**Content:** Moses assembles the entire congregation of Israel and begins with the command to keep the Sabbath holy—the seventh day is to be a complete rest for the Lord. He then urges everyone with a willing heart to bring freewill offerings of materials such as gold, silver, bronze, textiles, skins, oil, spices, and precious stones. Both men and women respond generously, and the women spin yarn with their own hands. God has chosen Bezalel from the tribe of Judah and Oholiab from the tribe of Dan, filling them with God's Spirit, wisdom, and skill to lead and teach in all kinds of craftsmanship.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sabbath (שַׁבָּת):** The weekly day of rest set apart for the Lord; kindling fire is forbidden on this day.
- **Freewill Offering (תְּרוּמָה/נְדָבָה):** Offerings given from a willing heart, forming the foundation of the entire Tabernacle project.
- **Wisdom in the Heart (חָכְמַת־לֵב):** God-given skill and insight that enable craftsmen to carry out holy work.
- **Bezalel and Oholiab:** The two chief craftsmen whom God appointed and filled with His Spirit to lead the construction.

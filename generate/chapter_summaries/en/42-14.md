**Main Theme:** Jesus teaches about humility, hospitality, and the cost of discipleship.

**Content:** Jesus heals a man with dropsy on the Sabbath and confronts the Pharisees. He instructs on humility at banquets and urges the host to invite the poor rather than the rich. In the parable of the great banquet, those originally invited refuse to come, so the master brings in the poor and outcasts. Jesus concludes by speaking about the cost of discipleship: bearing one's cross and giving up everything.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Great Banquet:** God's kingdom is open to all, especially those whom others reject.
- **Humility:** Those who exalt themselves will be humbled, and those who humble themselves will be exalted.
- **Cross:** Following Jesus requires bearing one's cross and counting the cost.
- **Salt:** Disciples who lose their flavor are worthless—a warning about faithfulness.

**Main Theme:** A lament expressing Israel's deep sorrow in exile and longing for Jerusalem, along with a cry for vengeance against the enemies.

**Content:** By the rivers of Babylon, the Israelites sit and weep as they remember Zion. They hang their harps on the willow trees and refuse to sing the Lord's songs in a foreign land, even when captors demand it. With strong self-curses, they swear loyalty to Jerusalem, and the psalm concludes with a prayer for vengeance against Edom and Babylon.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rivers of Babylon:** The place of exile where the Israelites mourn the loss of their homeland.
- **Harp on the willow trees:** A symbol that praise has fallen silent—joy is impossible in captivity.
- **Jerusalem as "the highest joy":** Expresses deep longing and the vow never to forget the holy city.
- **"Daughter of Babylon":** Personification of Babylon, who will receive retribution for her deeds against Israel.

**Main Theme:** An exhortation to act generously and decisively amid life's uncertainties, and to enjoy youth with wisdom.

**Content:** The chapter encourages giving generously and spreading risk, since no one can foresee the future (vv. 1–2). Whoever waits for perfect conditions will never act, for God's ways are as unfathomable as the wind and the mystery of life's origin (vv. 3–5). Therefore, one should work diligently and rejoice in youth, yet remember that God will bring every deed into judgment (vv. 6–9). Remove worry and sorrow, for youth is fleeting (v. 10).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Cast your bread upon the waters (v. 1):** A picture of generosity and trust—what you give returns to you.
- **Seven or eight (v. 2):** Spreading gifts and taking risks as wisdom in an uncertain world.
- **Wind and clouds (vv. 4–5):** Symbols of life's unpredictability and God's unfathomable ways.
- **The light is sweet (v. 7):** Life is a gift to be enjoyed with gratitude.

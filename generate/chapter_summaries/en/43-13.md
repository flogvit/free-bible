**Main Theme:** Jesus demonstrates serving love through foot washing and gives the disciples a new command to love one another.

**Content:** During the Passover meal, Jesus washes the disciples’ feet as an example of humble service, despite Peter’s objections. Jesus reveals that one of them will betray him, and after Judas receives the piece of bread, Satan enters him and he goes out into the night. Jesus then gives the new command about mutual love as a mark of discipleship, and predicts that Peter will deny him three times.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Foot Washing:** Jesus’ radical example of humble service—the Lord serves his disciples.
- **The New Commandment:** To love one another just as Jesus has loved them, as a sign of discipleship.
- **The Piece of Bread (ψωμίον):** Identifies the betrayer and marks the moment Satan enters Judas.
- **"It was night":** A symbolic expression of the spiritual darkness that follows Judas’ betrayal.

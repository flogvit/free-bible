**Main Theme:** God defends Moses' unique prophetic role against the rebellion of Miriam and Aaron.

**Content:** Miriam and Aaron criticize Moses for his Cushite wife and challenge his unique position: "Has the Lord spoken only through Moses? Has he not also spoken through us?" God calls them to the tent of meeting and declares that Moses is faithful in all God's house, speaking with him face to face, not in riddles. As punishment, Miriam is struck with leprosy. Moses prays for her, and after seven days of isolation outside the camp, she is brought back in.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Moses' humility:** Moses was the most humble person on earth and did not defend himself.
- **Face to face:** God speaks directly with Moses, not through visions or dreams as with other prophets.
- **Leprosy:** Miriam's skin disease symbolizes the seriousness of opposing God's chosen servant.
- **Seven days of exclusion:** Miriam is isolated outside the camp, and the people wait until she can return.

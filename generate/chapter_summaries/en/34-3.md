**Main Theme:** God’s final judgment on Nineveh for its bloodguilt, deceit, and spiritual seduction.

**Content:** The prophet pronounces woe upon the "city of blood," Nineveh, because of its lies, violence, prostitution, and sorcery. God Himself will disgrace the city and make her a terrifying example to the nations. Nineveh is compared with No-Amon (Thebes), which, despite its strength, was conquered. The city’s fortifications are like fig trees with ripe fruit—they fall at the slightest shake—and its leaders are like locusts that vanish when the sun rises.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **City of Blood (עִיר דָּמִים):** Nineveh is characterized by violence, oppression, and military brutality.
- **Prostitution and Sorcery:** Images representing Nineveh’s seductive power over other nations through trade and idolatry.
- **No-Amon (Thebes):** A great Egyptian city that was destroyed—a warning example that no city is invincible.
- **Locusts:** Symbolizing Nineveh’s leaders and merchants who disappear without a trace when destruction comes.

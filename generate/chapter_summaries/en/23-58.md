**Main Theme:** God rejects outward religiosity and demands genuine fasting expressed through justice, mercy, and Sabbath holiness.

**Content:** The prophet is commissioned to sound out the people's sin like a trumpet. God reveals that their fasting is hypocritical—they fast while oppressing others and seeking their own gain. True fasting means breaking unjust chains, sharing bread with the hungry, providing shelter for the homeless, and clothing the naked. Those who practice such justice and honor the Sabbath will experience God's blessing: light, healing, answered prayer, and rebuilding.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Trumpet/shofar** – Symbolizes the prophet's call to proclaim the people's sin without holding back.
- **Fasting** – True fasting is not about outward rituals but about practicing justice and caring for the oppressed.
- **Light breaking forth** – A picture of God's blessing and salvation coming to those who live righteously.
- **Sabbath** – The holy day should be honored as a delight, not used for self-interest.
- **Rebuilder of ruins** – The promise of restoration for those who follow God's will.

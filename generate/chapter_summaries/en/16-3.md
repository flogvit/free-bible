**Main Theme:** The people's collective effort to rebuild Jerusalem's wall despite the enemies' mockery.

**Content:** The chapter describes how various groups—priests, Levites, craftsmen, nobles, and ordinary citizens—worked side by side on different sections of the wall and gates. High Priest Eliashib led the work at the Sheep Gate. When Sanballat and Tobiah heard about the rebuilding, they mocked the Jews, saying that even a fox could tear down their wall. Nehemiah responded with prayer to God, asking Him to judge the enemies, and the people continued working with zeal until the wall was half completed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sheep Gate** – The gate where the priests began their work, consecrated by them (v. 1).
- **Gates and Bars** – Symbolize security and restored protection for the city.
- **Sanballat's Mockery** – The enemies' attempt to demoralize the rebuilders (vv. 33–35).
- **Prayer for Justice** – Nehemiah's appeal to God to punish those who mock His people (vv. 36–37).
- **The People's Heart** – The people were eager to work, and the wall was half completed (v. 38).

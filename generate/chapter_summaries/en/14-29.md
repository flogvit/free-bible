**Main Theme:** King Hezekiah's reopening and cleansing of the temple, with the restoration of true worship in Judah.

**Content:** Hezekiah became king at age 25 and immediately opened the doors of the temple that his father Ahaz had closed. He gathered the priests and Levites and commanded them to consecrate themselves and cleanse the temple of all uncleanness. The cleansing took 16 days, after which the rededication was celebrated with extensive sacrifices—including a sin offering for all Israel—accompanied by singing and music according to David's arrangement. The people rejoiced that God had made this possible so quickly.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Temple Cleansing:** Removal of all uncleanness from the sanctuary, symbolizing a reckoning with the ancestors' apostasy and idolatry.
- **Covenant with the Lord:** Hezekiah's desire to renew the covenant relationship in order to turn away God's wrath from the people.
- **Sin Offering for All Israel:** The sacrifice applied not only to Judah but to all Israel, demonstrating Hezekiah's vision of spiritual unity.
- **Davidic Worship:** Restoration of praise with instruments according to the instructions of David and the prophets.

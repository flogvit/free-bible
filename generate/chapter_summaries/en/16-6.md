**Main Theme:** Nehemiah overcomes his opponents' intrigues and completes the wall of Jerusalem with God's help.

**Content:** Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem repeatedly attempt to halt the wall-building through meeting invitations, false accusations of rebellion, and a bribed prophet who seeks to lure Nehemiah into the temple to discredit him. Nehemiah sees through all their plans and refuses to be distracted from his work. The wall is completed in 52 days, and the enemies acknowledge that God was behind this work. Nevertheless, Tobiah maintains influence through marriage connections with prominent Jewish families.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The wall completed in 52 days:** A testimony to God's blessing and the people's dedication despite constant opposition.
- **An open letter:** Sanballat's attempt to publicly damage Nehemiah's reputation with false accusations of rebellion and royal ambitions.
- **Shemaiah the false prophet:** Bribed to lure Nehemiah into sin by hiding in the temple, which would undermine his authority.
- **The enemies' fear:** When the wall was finished, everyone understood that God had done this work.

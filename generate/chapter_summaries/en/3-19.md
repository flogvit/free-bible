**Main Theme:** God calls Israel to holiness in all areas of life because He Himself is holy.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the fundamental call, "Be holy, for I, the Lord your God, am holy" (v.2). This is developed through specific commands regarding respect for parents, observance of the Sabbath, fair treatment of the poor and foreigners, honesty in business and legal matters. The command to love your neighbor as yourself (v.18) stands central, along with prohibitions against hatred, revenge, and slander. The chapter also forbids idolatry, divination, and contact with spiritists.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Holiness (קָדוֹשׁ):** God's own nature that Israel is called to reflect in ethical and ritual conduct.
- **"I am the Lord":** A repeated formula emphasizing that the commands carry divine authority.
- **Love your neighbor:** The foundational ethical principle summarizing our relationship with others.
- **Pe'ah (the corner of the field):** A specific command to leave part of the harvest for the poor and foreigners – social justice in practice.

**Main Theme:** A pilgrimage psalm that celebrates God's covenant with David and the choice of Zion as the Lord's eternal dwelling place.

**Content:** The psalm begins by reminding God of David's zeal and oath not to rest until he found a place for the ark of the covenant (vv. 1–5). It then recounts God's oath to David: his descendants shall sit on the throne forever, conditioned upon obedience to the covenant (vv. 11–12). God confirms that He has chosen Zion as His dwelling, where He will bless the people, clothe the priests with salvation, and cause David's horn to flourish (vv. 13–18).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mighty One of Jacob (אֲבִיר יַעֲקֹב):** A title for God that emphasizes His power and covenant faithfulness toward Israel's patriarch.
- **Ark of Your Strength (אֲרוֹן עֻזֶּךָ):** "The ark of your strength" – the symbol of God's presence and the center of the covenant.
- **Horn for David (קֶרֶן לְדָוִד):** An image of royal power and messianic hope that will sprout in Zion.
- **Lamp for My Anointed (נֵר לִמְשִׁיחִי):** A symbol of the continued existence of David's line and the messianic promise.

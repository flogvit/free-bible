**Main Theme:** The people's disobedience to God's word and their flight to Egypt.

**Content:** After Jeremiah delivers God's warnings, Azariah, Johanan, and the proud men reject the message and accuse the prophet of lying, claiming he was instigated by Baruch. They take the entire remnant of Judah, including Jeremiah and Baruch, to Egypt and arrive at Tahpanhes. There, God commands Jeremiah to hide large stones at the entrance of Pharaoh's palace as a sign that Nebuchadnezzar will come and strike Egypt with death, captivity, and the sword.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tahpanhes:** An Egyptian border city where the refugees settled and where Jeremiah performed his symbolic act.
- **The Hidden Stones:** A prophetic sign indicating the place where Nebuchadnezzar would set up his throne over conquered Egypt.
- **"Servant of the Lord" (Nebuchadnezzar):** God uses this pagan king as His instrument to bring judgment upon Egypt.
- **Pride/Disobedience:** The people's rejection of God's word in favor of their own fears and plans.

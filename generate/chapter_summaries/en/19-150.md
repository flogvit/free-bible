**Main Theme:** Psalm 150 is a concluding hymn of praise that calls on everything that lives to praise God with every available means.

**Content:** The psalm opens with an invitation to praise God in his sanctuary and in his mighty expanse of heaven. God is to be praised for his mighty deeds and overwhelming greatness. A variety of instruments and dancing are mentioned as expressions of this praise, before the psalm culminates in the universal call: "Let everything that has breath praise the Lord!"

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hallelujah (הַלְלוּ יָהּ):** An imperative call to praise Yahweh, framing the entire psalm.
- **The Sanctuary (קֹדֶשׁ):** God's dwelling place, where praise begins.
- **The Instruments:** Shofar, harp, lyre, tambourine, stringed instruments, flute, and cymbals represent the full range of musical worship.
- **Everything that has breath (כֹּל הַנְּשָׁמָה):** A final universal call that includes all creation in praise.

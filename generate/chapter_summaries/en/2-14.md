**Main Theme:** God delivers Israel at the Red Sea and reveals His power over Pharaoh and Egypt.

**Content:** God leads Israel to camp by the sea and hardens Pharaoh's heart so that he pursues them—all to glorify Himself and show that He is the LORD. When the people are afraid, Moses encourages them: "Do not be afraid; the LORD will fight for you." Moses stretches out his hand, the sea divides, and Israel crosses over on dry ground while the water stands like a wall on both sides. The Egyptians follow after, but God causes the waters to return and drowns the entire army. Israel sees God's mighty hand and puts their trust in the LORD and His servant Moses.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hardened heart:** God hardens Pharaoh's heart to complete His plan of salvation and reveal His power.
- **The pillar of cloud:** God's angel and the pillar of cloud move behind Israel, separating them from the Egyptians throughout the night.
- **The sea dividing:** The water stands like a wall on both sides—a picture of God's sovereign control over creation.
- **"The LORD will fight for you":** Moses' words that salvation comes from God alone, not human effort.

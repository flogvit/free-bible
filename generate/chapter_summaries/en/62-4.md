**Main Theme:** The chapter deals with testing the spirits and God's love as the foundation for mutual love among believers.

**Content:** John exhorts readers to test the spirits, since many false prophets have gone out into the world. The spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is from God, while the one that does not confess this has the spirit of antichrist. God is love, and he showed his love by sending his only Son as an atoning sacrifice for our sins. Because God loved us first, we are obligated to love one another—and perfect love casts out fear.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Test the spirits (δοκιμάζετε τὰ πνεύματα):** To examine whether a spirit's teaching confesses Jesus' incarnation.
- **God is love (ὁ θεὸς ἀγάπη ἐστίν):** God's nature is love, revealed through the sending of his Son.
- **Atonement (ἱλασμός):** Jesus' death as an atoning sacrifice for our sins.
- **Perfect love (τελεία ἀγάπη):** The love that is brought to completion in us and casts out fear of judgment.

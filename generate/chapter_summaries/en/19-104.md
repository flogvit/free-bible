**Main Theme:** Psalm 104 is a hymn of praise to God as the Creator and Sustainer of all life.

**Content:** The psalm opens and closes with "Bless the Lord, O my soul" and extols God's majesty and wisdom in creation. It describes how God has established the earth, set boundaries for the waters, and provides food for all creatures at the proper time. All life depends on God's open hand to live; when He hides His face, they perish. The psalm concludes with a wish that sinners should vanish from the earth, followed by "Hallelujah."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Light as a garment (v.2):** God clothes Himself in light and stretches out the heavens like a tent – an image of His glory and transcendence.
- **Leviathan (v.26):** The sea monster that God created to play with, demonstrating His sovereignty over chaotic forces.
- **God's Spirit/Breath (vv.29–30):** The source of life – when God sends forth His Spirit, life is created; when He takes it back, creatures die.
- **Wisdom (v.24):** Everything has been made with wisdom; the earth is full of God's possessions and displays His intelligent design.

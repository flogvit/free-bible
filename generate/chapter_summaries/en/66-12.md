**Main Theme:** The cosmic conflict between God and Satan, and God’s protection of His people.

**Content:** A woman clothed with the sun gives birth to a male child who is destined to rule all nations, but a great red dragon tries to devour the child. The child is caught up to God, and the woman flees into the wilderness where she is protected for 1,260 days. Michael and his angels cast the dragon—identified as Satan—down from heaven to earth. The believers overcome the accuser by the blood of the Lamb and their testimony. In his fury, the dragon makes war against the rest of the woman’s offspring: those who keep God’s commandments and hold to the testimony of Jesus.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Woman:** A heavenly sign representing God’s people, clothed with the sun, with a crown of twelve stars on her head.
- **The Dragon:** The great red dragon with seven heads and ten horns, identified as "the ancient serpent," the devil, and Satan.
- **The Male Child:** The child who will rule all nations with an iron scepter, caught up to God’s throne.
- **War in Heaven:** Michael and his angels cast Satan down to earth, initiating salvation and the victory of God’s kingdom.
- **The Blood of the Lamb:** The foundation for the believers’ victory over the accuser.

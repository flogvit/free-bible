**Main Theme:** Jesus overcomes temptation, begins his ministry in Galilee, and calls his first disciples.

**Content:** After 40 days of fasting in the wilderness, the devil tempts Jesus three times, but Jesus rebukes him by quoting Scripture. Jesus moves to Capernaum in Galilee and begins preaching: "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near!" He calls fishermen Peter, Andrew, James, and John to become "fishers of men." Jesus travels throughout Galilee, teaching in synagogues, proclaiming the gospel, and healing the sick, while large crowds follow him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Temptation in the Wilderness:** Jesus is tested in three ways (bread, jumping from the temple, dominion over the world) and demonstrates his obedience to God by responding with Scripture.
- **Fishers of Men:** Jesus' call for disciples to "fish for people" shows that their skills are to be used in God's service.
- **The Kingdom of Heaven is Near:** The core message of Jesus, marking the beginning of his preaching and calling people to repentance.
- **Light in Darkness:** Isaiah's prophecy that a people walking in darkness will see a great light, fulfilled through Jesus' ministry in Galilee.

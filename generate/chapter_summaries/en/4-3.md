**Main Theme:** The selection of the Levites as God's possession in place of Israel's firstborn, and the organization of their service at the tabernacle.

**Content:** The chapter opens with Aaron's sons, where Nadab and Abihu died after offering unauthorized fire, while Eleazar and Ithamar continued the priestly service. The Levites are given to Aaron and the priests as assistants at the tabernacle. The three Levitical clans—Gershon, Kohath, and Merari—are counted and assigned specific tasks and camp locations around the sanctuary. God takes the Levites (22,000) in place of all the firstborn in Israel (22,273), and the 273 excess are redeemed with five shekels each.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Firstborn (בְּכוֹר):** Belong to God since the exodus from Egypt; the Levites replace them as the Lord's special possession.
- **Unauthorized fire (אֵשׁ זָרָה):** The sin of Nadab and Abihu that led to their death—a warning about holiness in worship.
- **The three Levitical clans:** Gershon (tent coverings, west), Kohath (holy objects, south), Merari (framework, north).
- **Redemption price (פִּדְיוֹם):** Five shekels for each of the 273 firstborn who exceeded the number of Levites.

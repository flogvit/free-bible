**Main Theme:** On the eighth day, the consecration of the priestly service is completed as God's glory is revealed to the people.

**Content:** Moses calls together Aaron, his sons, and the elders of Israel and gives instructions concerning offerings. Aaron brings a sin offering and a burnt offering for himself, then a sin offering, a burnt offering, a grain offering, and a peace offering for the people. After all the offerings are completed and Aaron blesses the people, the glory of the Lord appears, and fire from the Lord consumes the sacrifice on the altar. The people shout for joy and fall face down in awe.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The eighth day:** Marks the completion of the seven days of consecration and the beginning of regular priestly service.
- **The glory of the Lord:** God's visible presence shown to the entire congregation as confirmation that He accepts their service.
- **Fire from the Lord:** Divine fire that consumes the sacrifice—a sign that God accepts the offerings and consecrates the altar.
- **The blessing:** Aaron lifts his hands and blesses the people after completing the sacrifices—the first priestly blessing in the tabernacle service.

**Main Theme:** The chapter depicts the escalation of sin from Cain's fratricide to Lamech's violent boasting, but also hope through Seth's lineage.

**Content:** Cain and Abel bring offerings to God, but only Abel's offering is accepted. Despite God's warning that sin is lying in wait, Cain kills his brother and is cursed to be a fugitive—but God places a protective mark on him. Cain's descendants develop civilization (cities, livestock, music, metalwork), yet Lamech's glorification of violence reveals moral decay. Adam and Eve have Seth as a replacement for Abel, and with his son Enosh, people begin to call upon the name of the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sin is lying in wait (חַטָּאת רֹבֵץ):** Like a predator at the door, sin waits to overpower anyone who does not do good.
- **Cain's mark (אוֹת):** A sign from God that protects the murderer, demonstrating God's grace amid judgment.
- **The blood cries out (דְּמֵי אָחִיךָ צֹעֲקִים):** Abel's blood cries out from the ground—violence cannot be hidden from God.
- **To call upon the name of the Lord (לִקְרֹא בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה):** With Enosh, worship of YHWH begins, a new beginning for humanity.

**Main Theme:** Two visions of God’s judgment on sin and the removal of evil from the land.

**Content:** Zechariah first sees a flying scroll that represents the curse against all who steal and swear falsely in God’s name—it enters their houses and consumes them. Then he sees an ephah (a measuring basket) with a woman inside, called "Wickedness." Two women with wings like storks lift the ephah and carry it to Shinar (Babylon), where a house is to be built for it.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Flying Scroll:** Symbolizes God’s curse that actively seeks out and judges sinners.
- **The Ephah with the Woman:** Represents evil being gathered and removed from God’s people.
- **Shinar (Babylon):** The place where evil is carried away, symbolically representing exile and separation from the holy land.

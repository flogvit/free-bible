**Main Theme:** A lament in which the suffering one prays for deliverance from enemies and disgrace, trusting in God’s faithfulness and promising praise.

**Content:** The psalmist describes his distress with the image of sinking into deep waters, while enemies hate him without cause. His zeal for God’s house has brought him mockery, and his foes give him gall and vinegar. He asks God for righteous judgment against his persecutors and for rescue. The psalm ends with praise and a promise that God will save Zion and give the land to the descendants of his servants.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Deep waters (מַעֲמַקֵּי־מַיִם):** A picture of life-threatening distress and chaos threatening to swallow the psalmist.
- **Zeal for God’s house (קִנְאַת בֵּיתְךָ):** The psalmist’s burning devotion to God has made him an object of scorn.
- **Gall and vinegar (רֹאשׁ/חֹמֶץ):** The cruel treatment by his enemies—they give poison instead of food and vinegar for his thirst.
- **Book of life (סֵפֶר חַיִּים):** A prayer that the enemies be blotted out from the list of those who share in life with God.

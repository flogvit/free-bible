**Main Theme:** God announces judgment over Israel for their spiritual unfaithfulness, with exile and loss of fertility as punishment.

**Content:** Israel has committed spiritual adultery by forsaking the Lord, and therefore their joy and prosperity will come to an end. The people will be taken back to Egypt and Assyria, and their glory shall vanish—no births, no children. At Gilgal they showed their wickedness, and God will reject them. As the fathers sinned at Baal-Peor, so the present generation has plunged into sin as in the days of Gibeah.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Baal-Peor:** A historical example of Israel's early apostasy when they devoted themselves to the god of shame (v.10).
- **The Days of Gibeah:** A reference to moral corruption in Israel's past, which is now repeating itself (v.9).
- **Ephraim's Glory Flies Away:** An image of the loss of fertility and descendants as divine punishment (v.11).
- **Gilgal:** The place that symbolizes Israel's wickedness and where God began to hate them (v.15).
- **Wanderers Among the Nations:** Israel's fate as exiles because they did not listen to God (v.17).

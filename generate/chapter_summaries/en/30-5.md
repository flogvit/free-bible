**Main Theme:** God laments over the fall of Israel and calls them to repentance through justice rather than empty worship.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a lament over "virgin Israel," who has fallen. God urges the people to seek Him and live, warning against the shrines at Bethel, Gilgal, and Beersheba. Israel is accused of oppressing the poor and perverting justice. God rejects their festivals and sacrifices, demanding instead that "justice roll down like waters." The chapter concludes with a threat of exile beyond Damascus.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lament (קִינָה):** The prophet sings a funeral song over Israel as if they have already fallen—a shocking rhetorical device.
- **The Day of the Lord:** Warns against longing for this day, for it will be darkness, not light—an unexpected message to the self-assured.
- **Justice rolling down like waters (v. 24):** The famous image of justice as a constantly flowing stream, in contrast to empty rituals.
- **Virgin Israel:** A picture of the nation as young and vulnerable, now lying abandoned on its own land.

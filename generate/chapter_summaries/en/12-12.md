**Main Theme:** King Joash’s Temple Restoration and His Compromise with Aram.

**Content:** Joash became king at the age of seven and reigned for forty years in Jerusalem. Under the guidance of the priest Jehoiada, he did what was right in the Lord’s eyes, although the high places were not removed. When the priests could not repair the temple, Joash introduced a new system using a collection chest, and the money went directly to the craftsmen. When Hazael, king of Aram, threatened Jerusalem, Joash paid him off with treasures from the temple. Eventually, Joash was killed by his own servants.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Temple Repair (בֶּדֶק הַבַּיִת):** The main project during Joash’s reign, which required a new organization of collections.
- **The Collection Chest:** Jehoiada made a chest with a hole in its lid and placed it beside the altar to collect money for temple work.
- **The High Places (הַבָּמוֹת):** Continued to be used, showing the limitation of Joash’s religious reform.
- **Hazael’s Threat:** The king of Aram captured Gath and threatened Jerusalem, leading Joash to give away the temple treasures.

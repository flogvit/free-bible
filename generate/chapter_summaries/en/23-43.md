**Main Theme:** God assures Israel of his faithful love, salvation, and forgiveness despite the people's failures.

**Content:** God declares that he has created, saved, and called Israel by name, promising to be with them through water and fire. He will gather his scattered people from all directions and proclaim himself as the only God and Savior—Israel is his witness. God criticizes the people for neglecting worship and burdening him with their sins, yet promises to blot out their transgressions for his own sake.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Redemption (גְאַלְתִּיךָ):** God has bought Israel free and calls them by name as his possession.
- **Water and Fire:** Images of trials and dangers through which God promises to preserve his people.
- **Witnesses (עֵדַי):** Israel is called to testify that the Lord alone is God and Savior.
- **"Something new" (חֲדָשָׁה):** God promises a new salvation that surpasses even the exodus from Egypt.
- **Forgiveness:** God blots out the people's sins for his own sake, not because of their merit.

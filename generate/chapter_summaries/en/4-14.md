**Main Theme:** Israel’s unbelief and rebellion against God lead to judgment: 40 years of wilderness wandering.

**Content:** After the negative report from the spies, the people lose faith and wish to return to Egypt. Joshua and Caleb urge trust in the Lord, but the people threaten to stone them. God proposes striking down the nation and making Moses into a greater people, but Moses intercedes on their behalf. God forgives, yet decrees that all over 20 years old will die in the wilderness—except for Caleb and Joshua. The ten spies who spread the evil report die in a plague. The people repent and attempt to enter the land against God’s will anyway, but are defeated by the Amalekites and Canaanites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Complaint and Unbelief** – The people’s weeping and desire to die in Egypt reveal their lack of trust in God’s promises.
- **Intercession** – Moses appeals to God’s reputation among the nations and His mercy to save Israel.
- **40 Years in the Wilderness** – The punishment corresponds to one year for each day the spies explored, teaching the people the consequences of disobedience.
- **Caleb and Joshua** – These two faithful spies, who had "a different spirit" and followed God wholeheartedly, are spared.
- **Presumption** – The people’s attempt to enter the land without God shows that late repentance does not cancel God’s judgment.

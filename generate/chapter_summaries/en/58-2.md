**Main Theme:** Jesus had to become human in order to be a merciful high priest who saves his people.

**Content:** The chapter warns against neglecting the salvation that was proclaimed by the Lord and confirmed with signs and wonders. The coming world is not subject to angels, but to the Son of Man—fulfilled in Jesus, who was humbled, suffered death, and is now crowned with glory. By sharing human nature, he could through his death destroy the devil and free those who lived in slavery under the fear of death. As high priest, he atones for the people's sins and can help those who are tempted.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **High Priest (ἀρχιερεύς):** Jesus had to become like his brothers to be a faithful and merciful high priest who atones for sin.
- **Author of Salvation (ἀρχηγός):** Jesus is the leader who brings many sons to glory, having been made perfect through suffering.
- **Brothers (ἀδελφοί):** Jesus is not ashamed to call believers brothers because they share the same origin.
- **Power of Death:** Jesus defeated the devil, who held the power of death, and freed people from the fear of death.

**Main Theme:** God promises to give new life to His people and reunite Israel under one king with an everlasting covenant of peace.

**Content:** Ezekiel sees a valley full of dry bones symbolizing Israel's hopelessness in exile. At God's command, he prophesies over the bones, which then receive tendons, flesh, skin, and breath—and rise up as a great army. Then Ezekiel is instructed to join two sticks together as a sign that Judah and Israel will become one people. God promises to gather them to their land, cleanse them, and set His servant David as their eternal prince over them, with His sanctuary in their midst.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Valley of Dry Bones:** Symbolizes Israel's spiritual death and hopelessness in exile, but also God's power to give new life.
- **The Spirit/Ruach:** The life-giving power from God that makes the dead alive—both physical and spiritual restoration.
- **The Two Sticks:** Represent the reunification of the northern kingdom (Ephraim/Josef) and the southern kingdom (Judah) into one people.
- **My Servant David:** The future Messianic king who will rule over reunited Israel forever.
- **Everlasting Covenant of Peace:** God's promise of a lasting relationship with His people, with His sanctuary in their midst.

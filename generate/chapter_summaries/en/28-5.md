**Main Theme:** God announces judgment over Israel, Ephraim, and Judah for their unfaithfulness and idolatry, and will withdraw until they acknowledge their guilt.

**Content:** God addresses the priests, the royal house, and the people, accusing them of becoming a snare and a net that leads the nation astray. Both Ephraim and Judah will stumble in their sin; even if they seek the Lord with sacrificial animals, they will not find him. When they turn to Assyria for help instead of turning to God, it will bring no healing. God compares himself to a lion that tears apart, declaring he will withdraw until the people acknowledge their guilt and seek his face.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Snare and net (v.1):** The leaders have become traps that lead the people away from God.
- **Fornication/unfaithfulness (vv.3–4):** Spiritual unfaithfulness through idolatry has made Israel unclean.
- **Moth and rot (v.12):** God acts like a slow destruction from within because of the people's sin.
- **Lion and young lion (v.14):** God as a predator that tears apart, with no one able to rescue.
- **Assyria (v.13):** A symbol of futile human help that cannot heal spiritual sickness.

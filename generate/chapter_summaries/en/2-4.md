**Main Theme:** Moses' Preparation for the Mission – God Overcomes Moses' Objections with Signs, Promises, and a Helper.

**Content:** Moses doubts that the people will believe him, so God gives him three signs: the staff turning into a snake, the hand becoming leprous, and water turning to blood. When Moses objects that he is slow of speech, God promises to be with his mouth and sends Aaron as his spokesman. Moses travels to Egypt with his family, and on the way Zipporah intervenes and circumcises their son, after which the Lord lets him go. Moses and Aaron gather the elders of Israel, perform the signs, and the people believe and worship God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Staff (מַטֶּה):** Moses' shepherd's staff is transformed into a snake and back again – a sign of God's power meant to convince the people.
- **Slow of Speech (כְבַד־פֶּה):** Moses' objection about his speech difficulties is met with God's promise to be with his mouth and to send Aaron as his spokesman.
- **Firstborn Son (בְּנִי בְכֹרִי):** God calls Israel His firstborn – a key concept for the deliverance and the conflict with Pharaoh.
- **Bridegroom of Blood (חֲתַן־דָּמִים):** Zipporah's words at the circumcision – a mysterious episode that underscores the seriousness of the covenant.

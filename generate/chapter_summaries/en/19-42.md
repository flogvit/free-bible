**Main Theme:** A lament expressing deep longing for God's presence amid suffering and the mockery of enemies.

**Content:** The psalmist, one of the sons of Korah, compares his soul's thirst for God to a deer that longs for flowing water. With sorrow he recalls how he once led festive processions to God's house with joy and praise. Now he is far away, in the region of Jordan, overwhelmed by suffering while enemies mockingly ask: "Where is your God?" Yet he exhorts himself to hope in God, for he will still praise him as his salvation.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The deer at the water brook** (v. 2): Expresses the soul's intense, life-giving longing for the living God.
- **"Where is your God?"** (vv. 4, 11): The enemies' repeated mockery that intensifies the psalmist's pain and isolation.
- **Deep calls to deep** (v. 8): A picture of overwhelming suffering, like waves and breakers sweeping over him.
- **Hope in God** (vv. 6, 12): The refrain where the psalmist speaks to his downcast soul and confesses faith in future salvation.

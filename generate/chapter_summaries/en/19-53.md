**Main Theme:** Psalm 53 is a teaching psalm by David that contrasts the fool's denial of God with God's sovereign oversight and coming salvation.

**Content:** The psalm opens with the fool's claim that there is no God, followed by a description of humanity's total moral decay—no one does good. God looks down from heaven and finds that all have turned away. The godless devour God's people, but they themselves will be struck with fear and shame when God judges them. The psalm concludes with a longing for Israel's salvation from Zion.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The fool (naval):** Not intellectual stupidity, but moral and spiritual blindness that leads to the denial of God.
- **God's gaze from heaven:** Describes God's omniscience and sovereign assessment of humanity.
- **Salvation from Zion:** Expresses the hope for God's final intervention to restore and save His people.

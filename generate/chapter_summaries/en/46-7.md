**Main Theme:** Paul gives guidance on marriage, celibacy, and remaining in one's calling in light of the shortness of time.

**Content:** Paul answers the congregation's questions about marriage and sexuality. He recommends the unmarried state for those who have that gift, but emphasizes that marriage is good and that spouses have mutual responsibilities toward each other. For mixed marriages (believer/non-believer), the rule is that the believer should not leave the spouse, but is free if the non-believer departs. A recurring principle is that each person should remain in the condition they were in when called to faith, whether it concerns civil status, circumcision, or slavery.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Calling (κλῆσις):** God calls people in various life situations, and they are to serve Him where they are.
- **The time is short (ὁ καιρὸς συνεσταλμένος):** The eschatological expectation shapes how believers should relate to earthly circumstances.
- **Mutual obligation:** In marriage, husband and wife have authority over each other's bodies—a radically equal view.
- **Sanctification:** The believing spouse sanctifies the non-believer and the children in a mixed marriage.

**Main Theme:** God equips craftsmen to build the tabernacle and gives the Sabbath commandment as a sign of the covenant.

**Content:** God calls Bezalel (from Judah) and Oholiab (from Dan) and fills them with God's Spirit, wisdom, and skill to make the tabernacle with all its equipment. Then God commands the Sabbath as an eternal sign of the covenant between him and Israel—anyone who dishonors it shall die. The chapter concludes with God giving Moses the two stone tablets, written by the finger of God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's Spirit (רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים):** God equips the craftsmen with spiritual gifts for practical service.
- **Sabbath as a sign (אוֹת):** The Sabbath is a visible sign of the covenant between God and Israel for all generations.
- **The Stone Tablets:** The two tablets of testimony, written by the finger of God, symbolize the divine law.

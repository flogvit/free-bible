**Main Theme:** God promises to restore Israel and Judah through a new covenant characterized by forgiveness and inner renewal.

**Content:** God declares His eternal love and promises to gather the people from exile and bring them back with joy. Rachel weeps over her lost children but receives the promise that they will return. Ephraim repents of his sin, and God responds with deep compassion. The central prophecy concerns a new covenant—unlike the Sinai covenant—in which the law is written on hearts, all will know God, and sins are forgiven. The chapter concludes with the promise of Jerusalem’s rebuilding as holy to the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The New Covenant (v.31-34):** A covenant in which God’s law is written on the heart, not on stone tablets, and all will know the Lord.
- **Rachel Weeping (v.15-17):** An image of Israel’s sorrow over exile, yet with the promise of their children’s return.
- **Ephraim Repenting (v.18-20):** Ephraim (the Northern Kingdom) is portrayed as a son who turns back, and God responds with parental love.
- **Everlasting Love (v.3):** God’s unchanging love as the foundation for restoration.

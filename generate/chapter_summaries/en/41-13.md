**Main Theme:** Jesus' eschatological discourse on the destruction of the temple, tribulations before his return, and the call to watchfulness.

**Content:** On the Mount of Olives, Peter, James, John, and Andrew ask Jesus when the temple will be destroyed and what signs will precede it. Jesus warns against false messiahs and prophets, describing tribulations such as wars, earthquakes, and famines – "the beginning of birth pains." The disciples will be persecuted, but the Holy Spirit will give them words to speak. The Son of Man will come in the clouds with power and glory to gather his elect. No one knows the day or the hour – therefore all must watch.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Abomination of Desolation (v. 14):** A warning sign of destruction requiring immediate flight from Judea.
- **Birth Pains (v. 8):** An image indicating that tribulations are the beginning of something new, not the end.
- **The Son of Man in the Clouds (v. 26):** Jesus' return in power and glory to gather the elect.
- **The Fig Tree (vv. 28–29):** A parable about reading the signs of the times – when its branches become tender, summer is near.
- **Watch! (vv. 33–37):** A repeated call to watchfulness since no one knows the time of the Lord's coming.

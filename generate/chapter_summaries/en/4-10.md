**Main Theme:** God gives instructions about silver trumpets, and Israel breaks camp from Sinai under the Lord's leadership.

**Content:** The Lord commands Moses to make two silver trumpets for gathering the people and signaling departures. The trumpets are also to be used in war and during festivals as a reminder before God. In the second year, the cloud lifts from the tabernacle, and Israel journeys in orderly formation from Sinai to the wilderness of Paran. Moses asks his father-in-law Hobab to join them as a guide. When the ark sets out, Moses prays: "Arise, O Lord, let your enemies be scattered!"

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Silver Trumpets:** Instruments for assembling the congregation, signaling departures, and being remembered by God in war and during festivals.
- **The Cloud:** God's visible guidance showing when the people should break camp or set up camp.
- **Ark of the Covenant:** Goes before the people to find resting places; a symbol of God's presence and protection.
- **Orderly Journey:** The twelve tribes break camp in a specific order with their leaders, demonstrating God's order among His people.

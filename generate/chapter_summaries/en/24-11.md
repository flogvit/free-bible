**Main Theme:** God's judgment on Judah for breaking the covenant and conspiring against Jeremiah from his own hometown.

**Content:** God reminds the people of the covenant made at the Exodus and the curse upon those who break it. Both Israel and Judah have broken the covenant by worshiping Baal and other gods—they have as many gods as cities. God forbids Jeremiah to pray for the people, for He will not hear them in their distress. The chapter concludes with God revealing to Jeremiah that men from Anathoth plan to kill him, but God promises to punish them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Covenant (הַבְּרִית):** The Sinaitic covenant from the Exodus, containing promises and obligations between God and Israel.
- **The Iron Furnace (כּוּר הַבַּרְזֶל):** A picture of Egypt as a place of suffering and purification before deliverance.
- **The Green Olive Tree (זַיִת רַעֲנָן):** Israel was called to be a fruitful tree, but its branches will be destroyed by fire because of wickedness.
- **The Lamb Led to Slaughter (כְּכֶבֶשׂ אַלּוּף יוּבַל לִטְבוֹחַ):** A picture of Jeremiah's innocent ignorance of the murder plots against him.

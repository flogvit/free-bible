**Main Theme:** Mordecai's exalted position and his service to the Jewish people.

**Content:** King Ahasuerus imposed a tax on the land and the coastal regions. Mordecai the Jew was second in command under the king, highly esteemed among the Jews and respected by most of his brothers. He sought the best for his people and spoke peace for all his descendants. His greatness is recorded in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Media and Persia.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mishne (second-in-command):** Mordecai held the second-highest position in the empire, right under the king.
- **Sought the good for his people:** Mordecai actively used his position to promote the welfare of the Jews.
- **Spoke peace:** He worked for harmony and security for the entire Jewish nation.
- **The Book of Chronicles:** Mordecai's greatness was officially documented in the empire's historical annals.

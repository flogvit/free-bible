**Main Theme:** David's military victories over the Ammonites and the Philistine giants.

**Content:** Joab led the army against the Ammonites, besieged and destroyed their capital city of Rabbah. David took the king’s crown and much war booty. This is followed by three battles against Philistine giants: Sibbekai killed Sippai at Gezer, Elhanan killed Lahmi (Goliath’s brother), and Jonathan killed a giant with twenty-four fingers and toes in Gath. All these giants fell before David and his men.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rabbah:** The capital of the Ammonites, which was besieged and destroyed by Joab.
- **Rephaim:** A race of giants that the Philistines had among their ranks.
- **The king’s crown:** Symbolizes complete victory—David took it from the Ammonite king.
- **David’s men:** Heroes such as Sibbekai, Elhanan, and Jonathan who performed these feats on David’s behalf.

**Main Theme:** John the Baptist prepares the way for the Messiah through preaching repentance, and Jesus is baptized and confirmed as God's Son.

**Content:** The chapter places John's ministry in a precise historical context under Emperor Tiberius. John preaches a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins, fulfilling Isaiah's prophecy, and gives practical ethical exhortations to the people, tax collectors, and soldiers. He points forward to a mightier one who will baptize with the Holy Spirit and fire. At Jesus' baptism, the heavens open, the Spirit descends like a dove, and God's voice declares Jesus as his beloved Son. The chapter concludes with Jesus' genealogy tracing back to Adam and God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Repentance (μετάνοια):** A radical change of mind that must be demonstrated in concrete action and fruit, not merely in religious affiliation.
- **Voice in the wilderness:** John fulfills Isaiah's prophecy as the forerunner who clears the way for the Lord.
- **Baptism with Spirit and fire:** The coming Messiah will bring a more powerful baptism than John's water baptism—one leading to salvation or judgment.
- **The Dove:** The Holy Spirit descends in bodily form like a dove at Jesus' baptism, a visible confirmation from heaven.
- **Genealogy:** Jesus' lineage is traced back to Adam and God, underscoring his universal significance for all humanity.

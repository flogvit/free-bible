**Main Theme:** Moses sets before Israel the choice between life and death, blessing and curse.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the promise that God will restore Israel from exile if they turn to Him with all their heart. God promises to circumcise their hearts so that they may love Him fully. Moses emphasizes that the command is not too difficult or far away, but is near—in their mouth and in their heart. With heaven and earth as witnesses, he urges the people to choose life by loving God and holding fast to Him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Circumcision of the heart (v.6):** God Himself will transform the heart so that the people can love Him completely.
- **The word is near (v.14):** God's command is not inaccessible, but is in their mouth and heart—ready to be obeyed.
- **Life and death (v.19):** The fundamental choice Israel faces, with blessing or curse as the consequence.
- **Heaven and earth as witnesses (v.19):** Cosmic witnesses that underscore the seriousness and irrevocable nature of the covenant.

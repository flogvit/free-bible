**Main Theme:** Belief in Jesus as God’s Son gives certainty of eternal life and victory over the world.

**Content:** Whoever believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God and loves both God and his children. To love God means to keep his commands, which are not burdensome, for faith gives victory over the world. Three witnesses confirm the truth about Jesus: the Spirit, the water, and the blood. Whoever has the Son has life; whoever does not have the Son does not have life. John writes these things so that believers may know they have eternal life and can pray with confidence according to God’s will.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Faith (πίστις):** Belief in Jesus as God’s Son is the foundation for victory over the world and certainty of salvation.
- **Testimony (μαρτυρία):** God’s testimony about his Son through the Spirit, the water, and the blood is greater than human testimony.
- **Eternal life (ζωὴ αἰώνιος):** Life is found in the Son—those who have him have life.
- **Confidence in prayer (παρρησία):** Believers can pray with assurance, and God hears prayers that align with his will.
- **Idols (εἴδωλα):** A final exhortation to stay away from anything that takes the place of God.

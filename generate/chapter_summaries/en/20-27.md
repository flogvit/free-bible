**Main Theme:** The chapter offers wisdom on friendship, self-knowledge, and diligent work.

**Content:** Proverbs 27 warns against boasting and encourages letting others praise you. The chapter highlights the value of honest friendship—the wounds from a friend are trustworthy, and iron sharpens iron. It cautions against jealousy, a quarrelsome wife, and humanity's insatiable desires. Finally, it urges diligent care for livestock and farming as the foundation for daily sustenance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Iron sharpens iron (v.17):** Friendship and community shape and refine character.
- **A neighbor near rather than a brother far away (v.10):** Practical closeness is more valuable than distant family ties in times of need.
- **Open rebuke (vv.5–6):** Honest criticism from a friend is better than hidden affection.
- **The grave is never satisfied (v.20):** Human eyes and desires are insatiable, serving as a warning against greed.
- **Watch over your flock (vv.23–27):** Diligence and care for what you are responsible for bring lasting prosperity.

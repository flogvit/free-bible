**Main Theme:** David recovers everything the Amalekites took, by seeking the Lord's guidance and demonstrating righteous leadership.

**Content:** When David and his men return to Ziklag, they find the city burned and their families taken captive by the Amalekites. While the people threaten to stone him, David strengthens himself in the Lord and asks God through the priest Abiathar, using the ephod, whether he should pursue the enemy. With God's promise of victory, he attacks the Amalekites, rescues all the captives, and takes great plunder. David establishes a lasting rule that the plunder is to be shared equally between those who fought and those who guarded the equipment, and sends gifts to his friends in Judah.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ephod:** The sacred object used by the priest to seek God's will in important decisions.
- **Strengthened himself in the Lord:** David's response to crisis – seeking strength and guidance from God rather than giving up.
- **Equal share:** The principle that all who participate in God's work deserve equal reward, regardless of their role.
- **Gifts to the elders of Judah:** David's strategic and generous action that builds alliances before he becomes king.

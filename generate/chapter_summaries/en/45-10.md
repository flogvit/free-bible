**Main Theme:** Righteousness by faith in Christ is available to all who confess and believe, but Israel has rejected this despite having heard.

**Content:** Paul expresses his heartfelt longing for Israel's salvation, yet notes that they have zeal for God without correct knowledge—they seek to establish their own righteousness instead of submitting to God’s. Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to everyone who believes. Salvation comes by confessing Jesus as Lord and believing that God raised him from the dead, and this applies to both Jews and Gentiles. Paul emphasizes the necessity of preaching so that people can hear and believe, but points out that Israel has heard without obeying.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **End of the law (τέλος νόμου):** Christ is the goal and fulfillment of the law, so that righteousness now comes by faith, not by works of the law.
- **Mouth and heart:** Confession with the mouth and belief in the heart are the two sides of saving faith (v.9-10).
- **Chain of preaching:** A logical chain from sending to preaching to hearing to believing to calling shows the spread of the gospel (v.14-15).
- **Outstretched hands:** The image of God stretching out his hands all day long toward a disobedient people illustrates his patience and Israel’s stubbornness (v.21).

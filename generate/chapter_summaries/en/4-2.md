**Main Theme:** The Lord gives instructions on how the twelve tribes of Israel are to be organized in camp and during departure around the tent of meeting.

**Content:** The tribes are arranged into four groups of three tribes each, positioned in their respective cardinal directions around the tent of meeting: the Judah group in the east, the Reuben group in the south, the Ephraim group in the west, and the Dan group in the north. The Levites with the tent of meeting are placed in the center of the camp. When breaking camp, the groups follow a specific order: Judah first, then Reuben, followed by the Levites with the tent of meeting, then Ephraim, and finally Dan. In total, 603,550 men fit for battle are counted, while the Levites are excluded from this count.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Banner/Standard (דֶּגֶל):** Each tribal grouping has its own banner as a rallying point and mark of identity.
- **The Tent of Meeting in the Center:** God's dwelling is placed in the middle of the camp, expressing that the Lord lives among his people.
- **Order and Structure:** The detailed organization shows that Israel's God is a God of order who gives clear instructions for community life.
- **Obedience:** The Israelites do everything just as the Lord commanded Moses (v.34).

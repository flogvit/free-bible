**Main Theme:** The Lord is king over all the earth, and his majestic presence brings joy to the righteous and shame to idolaters.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a proclamation of the Lord's kingship that causes the earth and its islands to rejoice. His throne is established on righteousness and justice, and his theophany is described with clouds, fire, lightning, and melting mountains. Idolaters will be put to shame, while Zion and Judah rejoice over God's judgments. Those who love the Lord hate evil, and light and joy are sown for the righteous.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's Kingship:** God's sovereign rule over all the earth brings joy and righteousness.
- **Theophany (clouds, fire, lightning):** Dramatic natural phenomena reveal God's majesty and power.
- **Mountains melting like wax:** Even the most solid parts of creation bow before the Lord's presence.
- **Light sown for the righteous:** God's blessing and future hope are planted for those who live rightly.

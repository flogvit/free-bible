**Main Theme:** God gives Judah victory over Israel when they trust in him and hold fast to true worship.

**Content:** King Abijah of Judah goes to war against Jeroboam of Israel, despite being outnumbered (400,000 versus 800,000). Before the battle, Abijah delivers a speech reminding them of God’s eternal covenant with David’s house and accuses Israel of idolatry with the golden calves and of rejecting the Levitical priests. When Judah cries out to Jehovah and the priests blow their trumpets, God strikes down the enemy, and 500,000 Israelites fall. Jeroboam never regains his strength and is struck down by Jehovah.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Covenant of Salt** – The eternal, unbreakable covenant God made with David’s royal house (v. 5).
- **Golden Calves** – Symbols of Israel’s apostasy and Jeroboam’s sin that led the people away from Jehovah (v. 8).
- **Trumpets** – The priests’ signal horns marking this as Jehovah’s war and calling for his intervention (vv. 12, 14).
- **Relying on Jehovah** – The key to Judah’s victory; trust in God rather than military might (v. 18).

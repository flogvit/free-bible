**Main Theme:** Joel 1 describes a devastating locust plague that warns of the Day of the Lord and calls the people to repentance.

**Content:** The prophet Joel receives the word of the Lord and delivers it to the inhabitants of Judah. A catastrophic locust invasion has destroyed the crops—vines, fig trees, and all vegetation have been consumed. The devastation also affects temple worship, as grain offerings and drink offerings are cut off from the house of the Lord. Joel calls the priests and all the people to fast, mourn, and cry out to the Lord, for the Day of the Lord is near.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Locusts (גָּזָם, אַרְבֶּה, יֶלֶק, חָסִיל):** Four types of locusts that in waves devour everything—a picture of total destruction.
- **The Day of the Lord (יום יהוה):** A day of judgment and destruction that is approaching, signaled through this natural disaster.
- **Grain offerings and drink offerings (מִנְחָה וָנֶסֶךְ):** Temple worship has stopped due to a lack of grain and wine—a sign of the spiritual crisis.
- **Call to repentance:** The people are urged to fast, lament, and pray in the house of the Lord as their response to the disaster.

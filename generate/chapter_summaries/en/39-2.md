**Main Theme:** God rebukes the priests for faithlessness and condemns the people’s betrayal of their spouses and of the covenant.

**Content:** The chapter begins with a warning to the priests who dishonor God’s name—their blessings will become curses. God reminds them of the covenant with Levi, which was characterized by life, peace, and the law of truth. The priests have abandoned the right path and caused many to stumble. Then God condemns Judah’s faithlessness: they have married worshippers of foreign gods and betrayed the wives of their youth. God hates divorce, and the people have wearied Him by claiming that He takes pleasure in the wicked.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Covenant with Levi** – The original covenant promised life and peace, and required reverence for God and truthful teaching from the priests.
- **The Law of Truth (תּוֹרַת אֱמֶת)** – The ideal priest had the law of truth on his lips and turned many away from sin.
- **Faithlessness (בָּגַד)** – A recurring theme; both the priests and the people have acted faithlessly toward God and toward one another.
- **The Wife of Your Youth** – God condemns those who betray their first wife, your companion and the wife of your covenant.

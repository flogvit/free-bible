**Main Theme:** Moses exhorts Israel to keep God's commands and warns against idolatry, based on God's unique revelation at Horeb.

**Content:** Moses urges the people to obey God's laws in order to live and enter the promised land, reminding them of the revelation at Horeb where God spoke from the fire and gave the Ten Commandments. He strongly warns against making images of gods, since they saw no form when God spoke. Disobedience will lead to exile, but God will allow Himself to be found by those who seek Him wholeheartedly. The chapter concludes with Moses designating three cities of refuge east of the Jordan.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Horeb Revelation:** God spoke from the fire without any visible form and gave the Ten Commandments on stone tablets.
- **Devouring Fire:** The Lord is a jealous God who demands undivided loyalty and punishes idolatry.
- **The Iron Furnace:** Egypt is described as an iron furnace from which God brought Israel out.
- **Cities of Refuge:** Three cities east of the Jordan are designated as places of refuge for anyone who kills another unintentionally.

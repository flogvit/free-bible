**Main Theme:** Jesus is confronted by religious leaders and teaches about authority, obedience, and the resurrection.

**Content:** In the temple, the chief priests, scribes, and elders challenge Jesus' authority, but he responds with a counter-question about John's baptism. Through the parable of the vineyard owner, he warns of Israel's rejection of God's messengers and his Son. Jesus teaches to give Caesar what belongs to Caesar, answers the Sadducees concerning the resurrection, and questions how the Messiah can be both David's son and David's Lord. He warns against the hypocrisy and love of honor among the scribes.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Vineyard:** Symbolizes Israel; the vineyard owner is God, the servants are the prophets, and the son is Jesus who is rejected and killed.
- **The Cornerstone:** Jesus quotes Psalm 118 about the stone that the builders rejected but which became the chief cornerstone—a picture of his rejection and exaltation.
- **Caesar's Coin:** Illustrates the principle of giving to earthly authorities what belongs to them, and to God what belongs to him.
- **The Living God:** Jesus explains that God is the God of the living, not of the dead, as proof of the resurrection.

**Main Theme:** Peter prepares believers for the Lord's day by warning against scoffers and urging holy living in light of God's patience and the promise of a new creation.

**Content:** Peter warns against scoffers who doubt Jesus' return and claim that everything continues as it always has. He explains that God is not slow, but patient—he desires that all should come to repentance. The Lord's day will come unexpectedly like a thief, and the present heavens and earth will be consumed by fire. But believers await new heavens and a new earth where righteousness dwells. Peter therefore urges holy living and growth in grace and knowledge of Christ.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **The Day of the Lord:** The coming day of judgment when heaven and earth will pass away and all things will be made new.
- **God's Patience:** God delays judgment not out of slowness, but out of mercy so that more may repent.
- **New Heavens and a New Earth:** The promise of a renewed creation where righteousness dwells permanently.
- **Like a Thief:** A picture indicating that the Day of the Lord will come unexpectedly and without preparation for many.

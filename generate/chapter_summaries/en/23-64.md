**Main Theme:** A penitential prayer in which the people confess their sins and ask God to intervene and restore His devastated nation.

**Content:** The prophet describes God's powerful presence that causes mountains to melt and nations to tremble. The people confess that they have all become unclean, their righteousness is like filthy garments, and their sins have driven them away like the wind. They appeal to God as Father and Potter, praying that He not remain angry forever, pointing out that the holy cities lie desolate, the temple has been burned down, and asking whether the Lord can hold back from this.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fire and melting mountains:** God's powerful presence that causes nature to react and enemies to tremble.
- **Filthy garments (בֶגֶד עִדִּים):** A picture showing that even the people's best deeds are unclean before God.
- **The Potter's clay:** God is the Creator who shapes His people; they are entirely dependent on His grace.
- **Temple burned down:** The holy house where the fathers praised God lies in ruins, a symbol of total destruction.

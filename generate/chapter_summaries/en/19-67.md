**Main Theme:** A prayer for God's blessing upon Israel, with the purpose that all nations may know His salvation and fear Him.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a prayer that God would be gracious and let His face shine upon His people (v. 2). The aim is that God's way and salvation may become known among all nations (v. 3). The nations are called to praise God because He judges righteously and guides the nations (vv. 4-6). The psalm concludes with the earth yielding its increase, and God's blessing leading all the ends of the earth to fear Him (vv. 7-8).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's face shines (v. 2):** An image of God's grace and favor, drawn from the Aaronic blessing (Num 6:25).
- **All peoples/nations (vv. 4-6):** A repeated refrain that underscores the psalm's universal, missional perspective.
- **The earth's increase (v. 7):** God's concrete blessing as a sign of His goodness and faithfulness.
- **The ends of the earth (v. 8):** Expresses the ultimate goal: that the whole world should fear and honor God.

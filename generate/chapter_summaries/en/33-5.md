**Main Theme:** Prophecy of an eternal ruler from Bethlehem who will bring peace, and God’s cleansing of Israel from idolatry.

**Content:** From the small town of Bethlehem shall come a ruler whose origins are from eternity, who will shepherd his people in the strength of the Lord and be peace. The remnant of Jacob shall be among the nations like refreshing dew and like an invincible lion. God will cleanse Israel by removing military power, sorcery, idols, and everything they have relied on instead of Him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bethlehem:** The insignificant town that will give birth to the eternal ruler over Israel.
- **Dew and Lion:** The remnant of Jacob shall be a blessing like dew, but also bring victory like a lion among the nations.
- **Cleansing:** God removes horses, chariots, idols, and sorcery—everything that competes with trust in Him.
- **Peace (shalom):** The coming ruler Himself will be peace when Assyria threatens.

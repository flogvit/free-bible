**Main Theme:** God warns Jerusalem of impending judgment from an enemy from the north due to the people’s stubborn disobedience and moral decay.

**Content:** Jeremiah proclaims that a cruel enemy is coming from the north to besiege Jerusalem because the people have rejected God’s word and refused to listen to the watchmen. God urges them to stand by the roads and seek "the ancient paths" – the good way – but the people answer, "we will not walk in it" (v. 16). From the least to the greatest, all are greedy for gain; prophets and priests preach a false peace. Jeremiah is appointed to test the people as a refiner tests metal, but they are rejected silver – the purification fails because wickedness cannot be separated out.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The enemy from the north** – A cruel army with bow and spear coming to carry out God’s judgment on Jerusalem.
- **The ancient paths** – God’s call to return to the well-tested way that brings rest for the soul.
- **"Peace, peace" – but no peace** – The superficial comfort of false prophets that hides the real danger.
- **Rejected silver** – The people are like metal that cannot be purified; God has rejected them.

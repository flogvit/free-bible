**Main Theme:** God announces that the end has come for Israel because of their oppression of the poor.

**Content:** Amos sees a basket of summer fruit (קָיִץ), a wordplay warning that the end (קֵץ) has come for Israel. God condemns the rich who impatiently wait for holy days to be over so they can cheat with false weights and exploit the poor. As punishment, the festivals will turn to mourning, the sun will be darkened at midday, and a famine of hearing the word of the Lord will strike the land.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Basket of summer fruit:** Symbol that Israel is "ripe" for judgment; a wordplay between "summer fruit" and "end" in Hebrew.
- **Darkening at noon:** A cosmic upheaval as a sign of God's judgment and sorrow over the land.
- **Spiritual famine:** The people will seek the word of the Lord from sea to sea, but not find it—a punishment worse than physical hunger.

**Main Theme:** God is the only true God who has chosen Israel, and idol worship is meaningless.

**Content:** God reaffirms His relationship with Israel and promises to pour out His Spirit upon the descendants of the people. He declares Himself as the First and the Last—the only God. The chapter contains a detailed satire on idolatry, in which the same tree is used both for fuel and to carve an image of a god. God promises to forgive Israel’s sins and rebuild Jerusalem, and He names Cyrus as His shepherd who will carry out this plan.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Jeshurun** – A term of endearment for Israel meaning "the upright one," used to emphasize God's love for His people.
- **The First and the Last** – God’s self-designation, highlighting His eternal existence and sovereignty over history.
- **Satire on Idolatry** – A detailed description of a craftsman who uses half of a tree for fuel and the rest to make an idol.
- **Cyrus (Koresh)** – The Persian king whom God calls "my shepherd" and who will command that Jerusalem and the temple be rebuilt.

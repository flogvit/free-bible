**Main Theme:** Hezekiah's religious reforms and the restoration of temple service, met with the people's generous response.

**Content:** After celebrating Passover, the Israelites destroy idolatrous sites throughout Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh. Hezekiah organizes the duties of the priests and Levites and urges the people to bring their firstfruits and tithes so that the priesthood can devote themselves fully to the Law of the Lord. The people respond with great generosity, and gifts are gathered in heaps from the third month through the seventh. The high priest Azariah testifies that the Lord has blessed the people with abundance. Hezekiah establishes storehouses and an orderly system for distributing provisions to all who serve in the temple.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Idolatrous Sites (מצבות, אשׁרים, במות):** Stone pillars, Asherah poles, and high places that were destroyed throughout the land.
- **Firstfruits and Tithes (ראשׁית, מעשׂר):** The people's offerings of grain, wine, oil, and honey for temple service.
- **Heaps (עֲרֵמוֹת):** The large quantities of gifts gathered, a visible sign of God's blessing.
- **Faithfulness (אֱמוּנָה):** The principle that characterized both the collection and distribution of offerings.
- **Prosperity (הִצְלִיחַ):** Hezekiah's success because he sought God with all his heart.

**Main Theme:** A deep penitential prayer in which David asks for forgiveness and inner renewal after his sin with Bathsheba.

**Content:** David implores God for mercy and asks to be cleansed from his sin, which he acknowledges is always before his eyes. He prays for a pure heart and a new spirit, and that God should not take His Holy Spirit from him. The psalm culminates in the recognition that God does not desire external sacrifices, but a broken and repentant heart.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hyssop:** A plant used in cleansing rituals; symbolizes David's desire for spiritual purification from sin.
- **Pure heart:** Expresses the need for inner renewal and transformation, not merely external forgiveness.
- **Broken spirit:** God's preferred "sacrifice" – humility and repentance are more valuable than external rituals.
- **Holy Spirit:** David asks to retain God's abiding Spirit, which gives strength to live rightly.

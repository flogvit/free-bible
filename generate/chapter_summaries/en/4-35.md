**Main Theme:** Establishment of Levitical cities and a refuge system to protect against blood guilt and preserve the purity of the land.

**Content:** The Lord commands Israel to give the Levites 48 cities with surrounding pastureland from the inheritance portions of the tribes. Six of these are to be cities of refuge where someone who has killed another unintentionally can seek protection from the avenger of blood. Intentional murder requires the death penalty, while unintentional killing requires remaining in a city of refuge until the death of the high priest. Blood guilt defiles the land where the Lord dwells, and only the blood of the murderer can make atonement for it.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **City of Refuge (עִיר מִקְלָט):** A place where someone who has killed unintentionally can find protection from the avenger of blood.
- **Avenger of Blood (גֹּאֵל הַדָּם):** A relative with the right to kill the one who took a family member's life.
- **Death of the High Priest:** Marks the time when the murderer may leave the city of refuge and return home.
- **Purity of the Land:** Blood guilt defiles the land where the Lord dwells and requires atonement.

**Main Theme:** God gives Jonah a second chance, and Nineveh turns from its wickedness and is spared from judgment.

**Content:** The Lord calls Jonah again to preach in Nineveh, the great city. This time Jonah obeys and proclaims that the city will be destroyed in forty days. The people of Nineveh believe in God, fast, and put on sackcloth—from the greatest to the least. Even the king leaves his throne, removes his royal robe, sits in ashes, and commands that both people and animals must fast, cry out to God, and turn from their evil ways. God sees their repentance and relents from the disaster he had planned to bring upon them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sackcloth and ashes:** External signs of grief, humility, and sincere repentance before God.
- **Forty days:** A deadline for judgment that allows time for repentance and mercy.
- **Repentance (שׁוב):** Turning away from one’s evil path—the decisive action that leads to salvation.
- **God’s relenting (וַיִּנָּחֶם):** God changes his course of action in response to human repentance, an expression of his mercy.

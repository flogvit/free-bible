**Main Theme:** Paul concludes the letter with exhortations about mutual care and warnings against those who promote circumcision.

**Content:** The chapter opens with calls to bear one another's burdens and fulfill Christ's law, while each must also carry their own load. Paul then uses the image of sowing and reaping to warn against following the flesh rather than the Spirit. He criticizes those who impose circumcision to avoid persecution and declares that only the new creation matters. Paul ends by mentioning the marks of Jesus he bears on his body and wishes grace upon the congregation.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bear burdens** (v. 2, 5): Mutual care within the congregation that fulfills Christ's law.
- **Sow and reap** (v. 7-8): The one who sows to the flesh will reap corruption; the one who sows to the Spirit will reap eternal life.
- **New creation** (v. 15): Neither circumcision nor uncircumcision means anything—only new life in Christ matters.
- **The cross** (v. 14): Paul's only reason for boasting, through which the world has been crucified to him.
- **Marks of Jesus** (v. 17): The physical scars Paul bears as a testimony to his service for Christ.

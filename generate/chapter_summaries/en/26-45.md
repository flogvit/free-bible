**Main Theme:** The chapter provides regulations for the holy land distribution, fair trade, and festival offerings in restored Israel.

**Content:** God commands that a holy area be set apart for the temple, the priests, and the Levites, while the prince receives land on both sides. The princes are urged to cease violence and oppression and to practice justice. Detailed rules are given for honest weights and measures, as well as regulations for the offerings of the people and the prince at Passover and the Feast of Tabernacles.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Holy area (terumah):** A parcel of land dedicated to the Lord for the temple and the priesthood.
- **The Prince (nasi):** The leader who receives his own territory and is responsible for providing the festival offerings.
- **Just measures:** Requirements for honest weights and units of measure as an expression of a just social order.
- **Purification of the temple:** Ritual sacrifices at the new moon to cleanse the sanctuary from sin.

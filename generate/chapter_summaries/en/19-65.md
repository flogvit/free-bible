**Main Theme:** A psalm of praise that celebrates God as the one who hears prayers, forgives sins, and richly blesses the earth with fertility.

**Content:** The psalm opens with reverent silence as an act of worship to God in Zion, where promises are fulfilled and prayers are heard. God forgives sin and blesses those he chooses for fellowship in his temple. As the hope of all the earth, God displays his power by establishing the mountains and silencing the roar of the seas. The psalm culminates in a celebration of God's care for the earth through rain and growth, where all creation bursts forth in joy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Silence as praise (דֻמִיָּה תְהִלָּה):** Reverent silence as an expression of worship in God's presence.
- **God's courts:** Those whom God chooses are allowed to dwell near him and be satisfied with the blessings of his house.
- **A river of God full of water (פֶּלֶג אֱלֹהִים):** A picture of God's abundant provision that gives fertility to the earth.
- **The year crowned with goodness:** God's blessing through the seasons, bringing abundance and joy in nature.

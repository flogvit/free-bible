**Main Theme:** God's judgment against false prophets and prophetesses who mislead Israel with deceptive messages.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to prophesy against Israel's false prophets, who follow their own spirit without having seen anything from God. They proclaim peace when there is no peace, and are compared to foxes among ruins that fail to defend the people. The prophets build a wall covered with weak plaster—a picture of false security that God will tear down with storm and hail. Women who practice divination are also condemned for trapping souls with magical bands and veils in exchange for payment. God will deliver His people and expose the deceivers.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Foxes among ruins (v.4):** The false prophets are like jackals that feed on destruction instead of protecting the people.
- **Wall covered with weak plaster (vv.10–15):** A picture of false security—the prophets give empty promises of peace that will not hold when God's judgment comes.
- **Bands and veils (vv.18–21):** Magical objects used by prophetesses to "trap souls"—symbolizing spiritual manipulation.
- **"Peace, peace—but there is no peace" (vv.10, 16):** The core of the false prophets' lie: they promise safety in the midst of God's coming judgment.

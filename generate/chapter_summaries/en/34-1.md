**Main Theme:** A prophecy of God's judgment against Nineveh that reveals his zeal toward enemies and his protection of those who take refuge in him.

**Content:** The chapter begins by proclaiming the Lord's character: he is jealous and avenging toward his foes, yet patient and a refuge for those who trust in him. God's majesty is depicted through powerful images of nature—storms, clouds, dried-up seas, and trembling mountains. Nineveh, from which evil and godless plans have emanated, will be struck by total destruction. At the same time, God promises to break the yoke upon his people and deliver them from oppression.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Jealous and Avenging God (קַנּוֹא וְנֹקֵם):** The Lord settles accounts with his adversaries and does not leave the guilty unpunished.
- **Refuge in Time of Trouble (מָעוֹז בְּיוֹם צָרָה):** God knows and protects those who seek shelter in him, standing in contrast to the judgment upon his enemies.
- **Theophany in Nature:** Storms, clouds like dust beneath his feet, and mountains that tremble display God's overwhelming power.
- **Breaking of the Yoke:** God promises to break Assyria's yoke and shatter the bonds that bind his people.

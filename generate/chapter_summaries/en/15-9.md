**Main Theme:** Ezra’s grief and confession of sin regarding the people’s intermarriage with pagan nations.

**Content:** Ezra learns that the Israelites, including priests and Levites, have married women from neighboring peoples—and that the leaders were the first to commit this unfaithfulness. Ezra tears his clothes, pulls hair from his head and beard, and sits speechless until the evening sacrifice. He offers a heartfelt prayer in which he confesses the people’s guilt, acknowledges that God has nevertheless shown mercy by preserving a remnant and giving them a foothold in His holy place, and asks whether they can continue to break God’s commands without being completely wiped out.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Holy Seed (זֶרַע הַקֹּדֶשׁ):** Israel’s unique status as God’s chosen people, now mixed with neighboring nations.
- **Remnant/Escapees (פְּלֵיטָה):** Those who survived the exile, a sign of God’s grace amid judgment.
- **Mourning Rituals:** Ezra tears his clothes and hair as an expression of deep sorrow over sin and covenant breach.
- **Unfaithfulness/Breach (מַעַל):** The Hebrew word describes the people’s infidelity toward their covenant with God.

**Main Theme:** A prayer for protection from enemies, with praise of God as refuge and defender.

**Content:** David asks God for deliverance from enemies who seek his life, linked to the time when Saul sent men to guard his house. He asserts his innocence and describes the enemies as dogs that prowl around the city. The psalm alternates between lament and declarations of trust, concluding with praise of God's strength and grace.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dogs that prowl (vv. 7, 15):** The enemies are compared to hungry, unchained dogs that wander in the evening – an image of threat and restlessness.
- **Refuge/stronghold (vv. 10, 17–18):** God is David's high fortress and secure refuge in times of trouble.
- **Sword in their lips (v. 8):** The enemies' words are like sharp weapons – they speak evil and think no one hears.
- **God's laughter (v. 9):** The Lord laughs at the enemies and mocks the nations – an expression of God's sovereignty.

**Main Theme:** The renewal of the covenant between God and Israel after the sin of the golden calf, with new stone tablets and God's revelation of His attributes.

**Content:** Moses hews two new stone tablets and goes up Mount Sinai, where God reveals Himself and proclaims His name and attributes: merciful, gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness. God renews the covenant with Israel and gives instructions not to make covenants with the people of Canaan but to destroy their altars for idols, along with commands regarding festivals, the Sabbath, and the firstfruits. Moses is on the mountain for 40 days without food or drink, and when he comes down, his face shines so brightly that he must cover it with a veil.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Stone Tablets** – The new tablets replacing those Moses broke symbolize the renewal of the covenant.
- **God's Attributes (v. 6–7)** – The central proclamation of God's nature: merciful, gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness.
- **A Jealous God (אֵל קַנָּא)** – God demands undivided loyalty and tolerates no idolatry.
- **Moses' Shining Face** – A visible sign of encountering God's glory, which required him to wear a veil before the people.

**Main Theme:** Jeremiah is thrown into a pit for his preaching, but is rescued and gives King Zedekiah one final choice between obedience and destruction.

**Content:** The officials accuse Jeremiah of weakening the people's will to resist and throw him into a muddy cistern. Ebed-Melech, an Ethiopian court official, obtains the king's permission to rescue the prophet. Afterwards, King Zedekiah has a secret conversation with Jeremiah, who advises him to surrender to the Babylonians in order to save the city and his own life. The king fears the consequences and does not follow the advice, but asks Jeremiah to keep the conversation hidden.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Pit (הַבּוֹר):** A symbol of mortal danger and humiliation; Jeremiah sinks into the mud, but God provides rescue.
- **Ebed-Melech:** A foreigner who acts righteously when God's own people fail, becoming an example of faith in action.
- **The Choice:** Zedekiah faces a clear choice between obedience to God's word (life) and disobedience (destruction).
- **Secrecy:** The king's desire to conceal the conversation reveals his fear and lack of leadership.

**Main Theme:** Isaiah 60 is a prophetic vision of Jerusalem's future glory, where God's light shines over the city and draws all nations to it.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an invitation to Jerusalem to rise up because the Lord's glory shines upon the city, while darkness covers the rest of the earth. Nations and kings will stream to Zion with their wealth, and Israel's scattered children will return. The city will experience lasting peace and righteousness, and the Lord Himself will be its eternal light. Those who oppressed Israel will humbly acknowledge Jerusalem as the Lord's city.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Light and darkness:** God's glory shines over Jerusalem while darkness covers the nations – a picture of salvation and God's presence.
- **Open gates:** The city's gates stand always open to receive the wealth of the nations – symbolizing prosperity and security.
- **The Lord as eternal light:** The sun and moon are replaced by God Himself as the source of light – expressing God's eternal presence and glory.
- **Zion/The Lord's city:** Jerusalem is rebuilt as a holy center where all peoples come to worship the Holy One of Israel.

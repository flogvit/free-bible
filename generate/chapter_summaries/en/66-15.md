**Main Theme:** The preparation for God’s final judgment through the seven last plagues.

**Content:** John sees a great sign in heaven: seven angels with the seven last plagues, which complete God’s wrath. Standing by a sea of glass mixed with fire are those who have overcome the beast, singing the song of Moses and the Lamb. From the heavenly temple come the seven angels, and one of the four living creatures gives them seven golden bowls filled with God’s wrath. The temple is filled with smoke from God’s glory, and no one can enter until the plagues are completed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Seven Last Plagues:** The final fulfillment of God’s judgment upon evil.
- **Sea of Glass Mixed with Fire:** Symbolizes both God’s holiness and the trials believers have endured.
- **The Song of Moses and the Lamb:** Unites deliverance from Egypt with Christ’s ultimate work of salvation.
- **Smoke in the Temple:** An expression of God’s glory and power filling the sanctuary during judgment.

**Main Theme:** God's Presence and Moses' Intercession After the Golden Calf Sin.

**Content:** God commands Moses to lead the people to Canaan but refuses to go with them because of their stubbornness. The people mourn and remove their jewelry. Moses sets up the tent of meeting outside the camp, where he meets God face to face. Through Moses' heartfelt intercession, God promises that His presence will accompany them. Moses asks to see God's glory but is only allowed to see God's back from a cleft in the rock.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Stiff-necked people** (עַם־קְשֵׁה־עֹרֶף): Describes Israel's stubbornness that hinders God's presence.
- **Tent of meeting** (אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד): The place outside the camp where Moses met God face to face.
- **Cloud pillar** (עַמּוּד הֶעָנָן): God's visible presence that descended at the entrance of the tent when Moses entered.
- **God's face/back** (פָּנִים/אֲחֹרָי): Humans cannot see God's full glory and live, but Moses is allowed to see God's back as a sign of grace.

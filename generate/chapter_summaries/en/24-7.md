**Main Theme:** God condemns Judah's false trust in the temple and demands genuine repentance rather than empty religious practice.

**Content:** Jeremiah proclaims at the temple gate that the people cannot rely on the temple as a guarantee of security while they live in sin. God requires justice, care for vulnerable groups, and faithfulness to the covenant—not merely outward rituals. The people have turned the temple into a "den of robbers" and worship foreign gods, including child sacrifices in the Valley of Hinnom. God points to the destruction of Shiloh as a warning and forbids Jeremiah from praying for the people, since judgment is unavoidable.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The temple of the Lord"** (v.4): The false sense of security the people found in repeating this phrase, as if the temple automatically protected them.
- **Den of robbers** (v.11): The temple has become a refuge for sinners who continue their evil deeds.
- **Shiloh** (vv.12–14): The former sanctuary that God destroyed because of Israel's sin—a warning about Jerusalem's fate.
- **Queen of Heaven** (v.18): A fertility goddess whom entire families participated in worshipping, to God's provocation.
- **Topheth/Valley of Hinnom** (vv.31–32): The place of child sacrifices that will become the "Valley of Slaughter"—a symbol of coming judgment.

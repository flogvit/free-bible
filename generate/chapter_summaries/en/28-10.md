**Main Theme:** God's judgment on Israel for idolatry and false trust, with an appeal to repentance.

**Content:** Israel is compared to a fruitful vine that uses its prosperity for pagan altars and idols. The calf image at Beth-Aven will be carried off to Assyria, and the people will be ashamed of their idol. The prophet reminds them that Israel has sinned since the days of Gibeah. God urges them to sow righteousness and plow new ground in seeking Him, but because they have plowed wickedness, the devastation of war will strike them, and their king will be destroyed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Vine (גֶּפֶן):** Israel as a fruitful vine that misuses its blessing for idolatry.
- **The Calf at Beth-Aven:** An idol symbolizing Israel's religious apostasy, which will be carried away.
- **Sowing and Reaping:** A metaphor illustrating the connection between action and consequence—they have sown wickedness and will reap accordingly.
- **Gibeah:** A historical reference to Israel's deep and long-standing sin.

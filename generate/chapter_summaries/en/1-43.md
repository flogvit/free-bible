**Main Theme:** Jacob's family is forced by famine to send Benjamin to Egypt, where they are reunited with Joseph without knowing who he is.

**Content:** The famine forces Jacob to send his sons back to Egypt for grain. Judah persuades his father to let Benjamin go and personally takes responsibility for him. In Egypt, the brothers fear punishment for the money found in their sacks, but Joseph's steward reassures them and brings Simeon out to meet them. Joseph is deeply moved upon seeing Benjamin and must step away to weep. He hosts a feast where Benjamin receives a portion five times larger than that of his brothers.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Judah's Guarantee:** Judah takes personal responsibility for Benjamin's safety and guarantees it with his own life.
- **El Shaddai:** Jacob calls upon God Almighty, asking for mercy, while resignedly saying: "If I have lost my children, then I have lost them."
- **Joseph's Hidden Tears:** Joseph must hide his weeping, which reveals his inner conflict between love and his plan.
- **The Feast:** The three separate tables (Joseph, the brothers, the Egyptians) show cultural divisions, while Benjamin's large portion suggests Joseph's special affection.

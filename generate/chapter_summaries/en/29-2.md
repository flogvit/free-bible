**Main Theme:** Joel 2 describes the Day of the Lord with judgment and a call to repentance, followed by God's promises of restoration and blessing.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an alarm about the Day of the Lord—a mighty army is described as destroying everything in its path. God calls the people to heartfelt repentance through fasting and mourning, reminding them of His gracious character. In response to the people's prayer, the Lord promises to remove the threat, restore the crops, and replace the years that the locusts consumed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Day of the Lord:** A day of darkness and judgment that demands reconciliation with God.
- **Shofar/trumpet:** Blown to warn of danger and call for a holy assembly.
- **Locust army:** A devastating force described as God's army, moving with military precision.
- **Tear your hearts, not your garments:** God's demand for genuine, inner repentance rather than outward rituals.
- **Early rain and late rain:** Symbols of God's blessing and the restoration of fertility.

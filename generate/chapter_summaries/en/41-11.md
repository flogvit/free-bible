**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals his messianic authority through his entry, the cleansing of the temple, and teaching about faith.

**Content:** Jesus rides into Jerusalem on a donkey while the crowd hails him as a descendant of David. He curses a fig tree that bears no fruit and cleanses the temple of merchants, calling it a house of prayer that has become a den of robbers. Using the withered fig tree as a starting point, he teaches about the power of faith and the necessity of forgiveness. The religious leaders challenge his authority, but Jesus disarms them with a counterquestion concerning John's baptism.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Donkey:** Symbol of the King of Peace and fulfillment of Zechariah's prophecy.
- **The Fig Tree:** Image of judgment over spiritual barrenness, framing the critique of the temple.
- **Cleansing of the Temple:** Jesus asserts his authority over worship and criticizes corruption.
- **Forgiveness:** A prerequisite for prayers to be heard by God.

**Main Theme:** Saul is anointed and then publicly chosen as Israel's first king.

**Content:** Samuel anoints Saul in private and gives him three signs confirming God's choice, including that he will prophesy when the Spirit of the Lord comes upon him. God gives Saul a new heart, and all the signs are fulfilled on the same day. At the assembly at Mizpah, Saul is chosen by lot, but he must be brought forward from among the baggage where he has hidden himself. The people shout "Long live the king!", yet some godless men despise him.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Anointing** – Samuel pours oil over Saul's head as a sign that God has chosen him to lead His people.
- **The Three Signs** – Prophetic confirmations showing that God's word through Samuel is reliable.
- **A New Heart** – God transforms Saul inwardly and equips him with His Spirit for the task.
- **Lot Casting** – A public method of selection demonstrating that it is God who chooses the king, not humans.
- **Hidden Among the Baggage** – Saul's reserve at his selection stands in contrast to his impressive physical appearance.

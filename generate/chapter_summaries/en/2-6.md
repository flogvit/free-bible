**Main Theme:** God confirms his covenant and his name YHWH, and renews the call to Moses and Aaron to lead Israel out of Egypt.

**Content:** God reveals himself to Moses with the name YHWH and reminds him of the covenant made with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob to give them the land of Canaan. He promises to deliver the Israelites with a mighty hand and great judgments, and to take them as his own people. The Israelites do not listen to Moses because of their discouragement and hard labor, and Moses doubts his ability because he has "uncircumcised lips." The chapter also includes a genealogy of the tribe of Levi that places Moses and Aaron within their ancestral line.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **YHWH (vv. 2–3):** God's covenant name now revealed in its full meaning, distinct from El Shaddai known by the patriarchs.
- **Uncircumcised lips (vv. 12, 30):** Moses' expression of his sense of inadequacy as a speaker.
- **Mighty hand (v. 6):** A picture of God's powerful intervention to deliver his people.
- **Genealogy of Levi (vv. 14–25):** Documents the ancestry of Moses and Aaron and validates their leadership.

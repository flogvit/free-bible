**Main Theme:** Manasseh and Amon’s evil reigns lead to God’s judgment on Judah.

**Content:** Manasseh reigned for 55 years and did what was evil: he rebuilt the high places, erected altars to Baal and to the host of heaven in the temple, made his son pass through the fire, practiced sorcery, and shed much innocent blood. Through the prophets, the Lord declared that He would bring disaster upon Jerusalem because of these sins. Amon’s brief two-year reign followed the same evil pattern; he was killed by his servants, but the people executed the conspirators and installed his son Josiah as king.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **High places (bamot):** Illegal worship sites that Manasseh rebuilt after his father Hezekiah had removed them.
- **Host of heaven:** Astral idolatry (sun, moon, stars) that Manasseh introduced into the temple itself.
- **Innocent blood:** Manasseh’s violent oppression filled Jerusalem with innocent blood from one end to the other.
- **People of the land (am ha-arets):** The group that intervened after Amon’s murder to ensure the dynasty continued through Josiah.

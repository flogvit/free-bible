**Main Theme:** God promises to restore and strengthen His people after judging the false leaders who led them astray.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an exhortation to ask the Lord for the spring rain, in contrast to teraphim and diviners who spoke only lies. The Lord is angry with the bad shepherds and will Himself care for His flock, Judah. From Judah shall come a cornerstone, a tent peg, and a battle bow. The Lord promises to strengthen both Judah and Joseph (Ephraim), gather them from Egypt and Assyria, and bring them back to Gilead and Lebanon. The people shall walk in the name of the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Spring rain (malqosh):** A symbol of God's blessing and provision that the people are to ask for.
- **Teraphim and diviners:** False religious sources that spoke emptiness and lies, in contrast to the Lord.
- **Cornerstone and tent peg:** From Judah shall come stability and leadership—possible messianic images.
- **Flock without a shepherd:** Describes the people's condition under bad leaders, but God will Himself become their shepherd.

**Main Theme:** Jesus concludes the Sermon on the Mount with an exhortation to right living, sound judgment, and obedience to his words.

**Content:** Jesus warns against hypocritical judging and encourages self-examination before correcting others. He promises answers to prayer from the heavenly Father and gives the Golden Rule as a summary of the Law and the Prophets. The chapter contrasts two paths (narrow and wide), two types of trees (good and bad), and two builders (wise and foolish). Jesus warns against false prophets and emphasizes that entry into God's kingdom requires obedience to God's will, not merely confession.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The speck and the plank:** Illustrates hypocrisy in criticizing others' small faults while ignoring one's own great sins.
- **The narrow gate:** Symbolizes the demanding path to eternal life that few find, as opposed to the wide road to destruction.
- **The tree and its fruit:** Shows that people are known by their deeds, just as a tree is known by its fruit.
- **The house on rock and sand:** Illustrates the difference between hearing and doing Jesus' words (a solid foundation) versus merely hearing (an unstable foundation).

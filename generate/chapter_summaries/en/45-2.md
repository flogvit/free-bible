**Main Theme:** God’s righteous judgment is impartial and strikes all who judge others while doing the same things themselves.

**Content:** Paul warns against judging others when one does the same oneself, and explains that God’s kindness is meant to lead people to repentance. God judges impartially according to deeds—first the Jew, then the Greek. Gentiles who do not have the law still have the work of the law written on their hearts, with their conscience bearing witness. The true Jew is not one with outward circumcision, but one whose heart has been circumcised by the Spirit.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God’s judgment (κρίμα):** God’s righteous judgment is based on truth and deeds, not external status.
- **Repentance (μετάνοια):** God’s kindness and patience are intended to lead people to repentance.
- **Impartiality (οὐ προσωπολημψία):** God shows no partiality—both Jew and Greek are judged alike.
- **Circumcision of the heart (περιτομὴ καρδίας):** The true Jew is circumcised in the heart by the Spirit, not merely in the flesh.

**Main Theme:** God reveals Ephraim's (Israel's) sin and unfaithfulness, warning of judgment because the people refuse to turn back to Him.

**Content:** When God seeks to heal Israel, their sin and betrayal are exposed—they live in lies, theft, and political intrigue. The leaders rejoice in evil, and the people burn with passion like an oven. Instead of seeking God, Ephraim turns to Egypt and Assyria for help. Despite God's desire to save them, they lie about Him and do not cry out to Him from the heart.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Burning Oven (vv. 4–7):** Symbolizes the people's burning desires and political conspiracies that consume their own leaders.
- **Unturned Cake (v. 8):** Ephraim is half-baked and useless—mixed with the nations and without a distinct identity.
- **Silly Dove Without Sense (v. 11):** Israel flutters naively between the great powers, Egypt and Assyria, instead of trusting in God.
- **Faulty Bow (v. 16):** The people are unreliable and turn away from God, leading to their destruction.

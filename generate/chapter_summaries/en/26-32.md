**Main Theme:** A double lament over Pharaoh and Egypt announcing their fall and descent to the realm of the dead.

**Content:** God commands Ezekiel to deliver a lament over Pharaoh, who is compared to a lion and a sea monster that will be caught and cast ashore as food for birds and beasts. The king of Babylon is the instrument of God's judgment, and cosmic signs such as the darkening of the sun, moon, and stars will accompany Egypt's fall. Egypt will descend into the realm of the dead where they meet other fallen powers—Assyria, Elam, Meshech-Tubal, Edom, and Sidon—all lying uncircumcised, slain by the sword.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sea monster (תַּנִּים):** Pharaoh is portrayed as a mighty creature in the rivers that stirs up the waters, but whom God will catch in His net.
- **Cosmic darkening:** The sun, moon, and stars will be darkened as signs of God's judgment and Egypt's fall.
- **Realm of the dead (שְׁאוֹל):** The underworld where Egypt will lie together with other fallen nations, uncircumcised.
- **Uncircumcised (עֲרֵלִים):** A term for the dishonored dead who lie in shame, without honor in death.

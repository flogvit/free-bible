**Main Theme:** God’s faithful love for Israel illustrated through Hosea’s redemption of an unfaithful wife.

**Content:** God commands Hosea to love a woman who is unfaithful, just as God loves Israel despite their idolatry. Hosea buys her for fifteen pieces of silver and some barley, and she must wait many days without being unfaithful. This symbolizes that Israel will be without king, prince, sacrifice, or religious objects for a long time, before they turn back to the Lord and to David their king in the last days.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Love for an unfaithful wife:** Symbolizes God’s unconditional love for wayward Israel.
- **Many days:** A period of waiting and purification, both for the woman and for Israel in exile.
- **Without king or sacrifice:** Israel’s loss of political and religious institutions during the exile.
- **David their king:** Messianic hope of restoration under a Davidic ruler.
- **The last days:** Eschatological promise of Israel’s final repentance and blessing.

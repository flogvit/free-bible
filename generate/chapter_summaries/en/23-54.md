**Main Theme:** God promises the barren Zion complete restoration, eternal love, and unshakable protection.

**Content:** The childless woman is urged to rejoice, for she will have more children than the married woman. God, who is Israel's husband and Redeemer, abandoned His people only for a moment but will gather them with everlasting kindness. As He swore after Noah's flood, He now swears never again to be angry. Jerusalem will be built with precious stones, and no weapon formed against the people shall prosper—this is the heritage of the Lord's servants.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The barren woman who bears children** – Zion, which was desolate, will have more children than the one who had a husband.
- **God as husband (בֹעֲלַיִךְ)** – The Lord is Israel's Creator and Redeemer, taking back His "forsaken wife."
- **The Noahic covenant** – Just as God swore never to send another flood, He now swears everlasting kindness.
- **The city of precious stones** – Jerusalem will be built with sapphires, rubies, and costly stones as a sign of God's glory.
- **No weapon shall prosper** – God's unshakable protection is the heritage and righteousness of His servants.

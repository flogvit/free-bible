**Main Theme:** Warning against apostasy in the last days and exhortation to hold fast to Scripture.

**Content:** Paul warns Timothy about difficult times in the last days, characterized by people who are selfish and have a form of godliness but deny its power. He reminds Timothy of his own teaching, faith, and sufferings, emphasizing that all who want to live godly lives in Christ will be persecuted. Timothy is urged to hold fast to what he has learned from childhood, for all Scripture is inspired by God and useful to equip the believer for every good work.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The last days (ἐσχάταις ἡμέραις):** A time marked by moral decay and false piety.
- **Form of godliness (μόρφωσιν εὐσεβείας):** Outward religiosity without inner power or true reverence for God.
- **Jannes and Jambres:** Traditional names of Egyptian magicians who opposed Moses – an example of resistance to the truth.
- **Theopneustos (θεόπνευστος):** Scripture is "inspired by God" – the basis for its authority and usefulness.

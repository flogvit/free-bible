**Main Theme:** Job repents and is exalted, and God restores all that he had lost—double for his loss.

**Content:** Job acknowledges God’s almighty power and admits that he has spoken about things he did not understand; he repents in dust and ashes. God rebukes Job’s three friends because they did not speak rightly about Him, and commands them to offer burnt offerings while Job prays for them. When Job prays for his friends, God turns his fate around and gives him back double all that he had lost. Job receives seven sons and three daughters, lives 140 years after this, and dies old and full of days.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dust and ashes (עָפָר וָאֵפֶר):** A symbol of deep humility and repentance before God.
- **Burnt offering:** The friends must offer seven bulls and seven rams to be forgiven for their false words about God.
- **Double (לְמִשְׁנֶה):** God restores Job’s wealth in double measure—a sign of complete restoration.
- **Four generations:** Job sees his descendants through four generations, a sign of blessing and a full life.

**Main Theme:** God’s protection of His people and the heavenly worship of the saved.

**Content:** Four angels hold back the winds of the earth until 144,000 from the twelve tribes of Israel are sealed on their foreheads. Then John sees an innumerable multitude from every nation, clothed in white robes and holding palm branches, standing before the throne and the Lamb. They cry out that salvation belongs to God and the Lamb, and the angels offer sevenfold praise. One of the elders explains that this multitude has come out of the great tribulation and has washed their robes white in the blood of the Lamb.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The 144,000 sealed ones:** God’s servants from the twelve tribes of Israel who receive God’s protective seal on their foreheads before judgment.
- **The great multitude:** An uncountable crowd from all nations who have come through the great tribulation.
- **White robes:** A symbol of purity and righteousness, washed in the blood of the Lamb.
- **The Lamb as Shepherd:** The Lamb will shepherd them and lead them to springs of living water, and God will wipe away every tear.

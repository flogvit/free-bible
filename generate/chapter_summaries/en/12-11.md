**Main Theme:** The priest Jehoiada leads a coup against Queen Athaliah and restores the Davidic line by crowning the young Joash.

**Content:** After King Ahaziah's death, his mother Athaliah seizes power by killing the entire royal family, but Jehosheba saves the infant Joash and hides him in the temple for six years. In the seventh year, the priest Jehoiada organizes a coup with military leaders, brings out Joash, and crowns him king with the crown and the testimony of the covenant. Athaliah is executed outside the temple, Baal's temple is destroyed, and Jehoiada renews the covenant between the Lord, the king, and the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Athaliah** – The only female ruler in Judah, representing the influence of Baal worship from the northern kingdom.
- **The Temple (בֵּית יְהוָה)** – Serves as a refuge for the legitimate heir and the site of the rightful coronation.
- **The Covenant (בְּרִית)** – Jehoiada renews the covenant between God, the king, and the people, restoring Judah's identity as the Lord's people.
- **The Crown and the Testimony (נֵזֶר/עֵדוּת)** – Symbols of legitimate Davidic kingship and the king's commitment to God's law.

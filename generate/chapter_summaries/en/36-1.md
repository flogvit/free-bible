**Main Theme:** The day of the Lord will bring total judgment upon Judah and Jerusalem because of their apostasy and sin.

**Content:** The prophet Zephaniah, who ministered during the reign of King Josiah, proclaims that God will wipe out all life from the earth and especially strike Judah. Judgment is directed against Baal worshippers, those who worship the host of heaven on the rooftops, those who mix Yahweh-worship with idolatry, and those who have turned away from the Lord. The day of the Lord is described as near and coming quickly—a day of wrath, distress, devastation, darkness, and alarm. Neither silver nor gold can save anyone from God’s burning zeal.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Day of the Lord (יוֹם יְהוָה):** The coming day of judgment described with intense imagery of darkness, distress, and destruction.
- **Sacrifice of the Lord (זֶבַח):** God has prepared a sacrifice where the invited guests are instruments of judgment, and Judah is the offering.
- **Search with lanterns:** God will thoroughly search through Jerusalem to find the indifferent who say, "The Lord does neither good nor evil."
- **Idolatry:** The cult of Baal, star worship, and mixed religion are the main causes of the judgment.

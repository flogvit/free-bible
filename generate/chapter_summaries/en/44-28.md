**Main Theme:** Paul's arrival in Rome after a shipwreck and his bold proclamation of the kingdom of God.

**Content:** After the shipwreck, Paul and his companions are stranded on Malta, where they experience hospitality and Paul heals the sick. After three months, the journey continues to Rome, where believers come out to meet them. Paul is allowed to live under house arrest and gathers the Jewish leaders to explain his situation and proclaim Jesus from the Scriptures. Some believe, while others reject the message, and Paul declares that salvation has been sent to the Gentiles. For two years he proclaims the kingdom of God with boldness and without hindrance.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Snake on Malta** – Paul is bitten by a venomous snake but suffers no harm, demonstrating God's protection and causing the people to change their view of him.
- **Healings** – Paul heals Publius' father and then other sick people on the island, showing the power of the gospel in action.
- **The Hope of Israel** – Paul explains that he bears chains because of his hope in the Messiah and the resurrection.
- **The Isaiah Quotation** – Paul quotes Isaiah 6:9–10 concerning the hardened hearts of the people to explain the Jews' rejection.
- **Unhindered Proclamation** – The chapter concludes with Paul proclaiming the kingdom of God "with all boldness and without hindrance" (μετὰ πάσης παρρησίας ἀκωλύτως).

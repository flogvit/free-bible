**Main Theme:** The seventh seal is opened, initiating the first four trumpet judgments that affect the earth.

**Content:** When the seventh seal is broken, there is silence in heaven for about half an hour. Seven angels receive seven trumpets, while another angel carries the prayers of the saints up to God with incense, before casting fire from the altar onto the earth. The first four trumpets each strike one-third of creation: land and vegetation, sea and marine life, fresh water (poisoned by the star Wormwood), and heavenly bodies. An eagle flies across the sky and cries out three "woe" warnings as a foretelling of the remaining three trumpets.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Silence in heaven:** A half-hour silence marks a solemn pause before judgment begins.
- **The altar of incense:** Symbolizes the prayers of the saints rising to God, followed by God's response in judgment.
- **One-third:** A recurring motif showing that the judgments are severe yet still limited.
- **Wormwood (Ἄψινθος):** The star that poisons fresh water and causes death among people.
- **The eagle with woe cries:** Warns that the three coming trumpets will bring even greater suffering.

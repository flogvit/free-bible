**Main Theme:** The construction of Solomon's temple in Jerusalem with its magnificent design and God's conditional promise of presence.

**Content:** Temple construction began 480 years after the exodus from Egypt and took seven years to complete. The temple had three main parts: the porch, the holy place (heikhal), and the most holy place (debir), where the ark of the covenant was to stand. The entire interior was lined with cedar wood and gold, and decorated with carved cherubim, palm trees, and flowers. God gave Solomon a promise: if he kept God's commands, God would dwell among the Israelites and never abandon his people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Temple (הַבַּיִת):** The house of the Lord, built as a permanent dwelling for God's presence among his people.
- **The Most Holy Place (דְּבִיר):** The innermost room where the ark of the covenant was to be placed, lined with pure gold.
- **Cherubim (כְּרוּבִים):** Two large figures made of olive wood overlaid with gold, positioned in the most holy place with their wings spread out.
- **Cedar and Gold:** The primary materials that covered the entire interior of the temple, symbolizing splendor and holiness.
- **God's Promise:** Conditional on obedience – God will dwell among Israel and not forsake them if Solomon follows his commands.

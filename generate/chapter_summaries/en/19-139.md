**Main Theme:** A hymn about God's perfect knowledge of man, His omnipresence, and His wonderful works of creation.

**Content:** David expresses awe that God knows all his thoughts, words, and ways completely (vv. 1–6). He acknowledges that it is impossible to flee from God's presence—not in heaven, not in the realm of the dead, nor at the farthest reaches of the sea (vv. 7–12). The psalm praises God as the Creator who formed man in his mother's womb and knew all his days before they began (vv. 13–16). David also expresses his hatred for those who hate God, before concluding with a prayer that God would search his heart and lead him on the everlasting way (vv. 19–24).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Search (חקר):** God's thorough, intimate knowledge of man's innermost being.
- **Wings of the dawn:** A poetic image for the farthest place—even there, God is present.
- **Formed in the mother's womb:** God as Creator who weaves man together with care and purpose.
- **Book of life:** God's foreknowledge—all of man's days were written before they began.
- **The everlasting way (דרך עולם):** God's path to life and righteousness, which David asks to be led upon.

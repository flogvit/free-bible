**Main Theme:** God reveals to Ezekiel the abominable idolatrous practices taking place in Jerusalem’s temple.

**Content:** In the sixth year, Ezekiel is brought in a vision to the temple in Jerusalem, where God shows him four progressively worse forms of idol worship: the image of jealousy at the north gate, seventy elders burning incense before carved images in the darkness, women weeping for the god Tammuz, and twenty-five men worshipping the sun with their backs turned toward the temple. God declares that He will act in wrath without sparing them.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Image of Jealousy (סֵמֶל הַקִּנְאָה):** An idol at the temple gate that provokes God’s holy jealousy.
- **The Seventy Elders:** Israel’s leaders who secretly worship idols, believing that the Lord does not see them.
- **Weeping for Tammuz:** Women mourning over the Mesopotamian god of fertility—a pagan practice within God’s house.
- **Sun Worship:** Men turning their backs on the Lord’s temple to worship the sun—the ultimate contempt for God.

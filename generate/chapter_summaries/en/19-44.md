**Main Theme:** A national lament in which Israel calls upon God amidst inexplicable suffering, despite their faithfulness to the covenant.

**Content:** The psalmist recalls God's mighty acts of the past, when He drove out nations and planted Israel in the land (vv. 2–9). Now the people experience defeat, dispersion, and disgrace among the nations, even though they have not broken the covenant or forgotten God (vv. 10–23). The psalm concludes with a fervent plea for God to awake and save them for the sake of His faithfulness.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sheep for slaughter** (vv. 12, 23): The people are compared to helpless animals, symbolizing innocent suffering for God's sake.
- **Shadow of death** (צַלְמָוֶת, v. 20): A picture of deep distress and despair, where God has crushed them.
- **Covenant faithfulness** (vv. 18–22): The people assert their innocence—they have not forgotten God or worshiped foreign gods.
- **God's face** (vv. 4, 25): God's gracious presence once brought victory; now He hides His face.

**Main Theme:** The chapter contrasts the victory of the redeemed with God’s judgment on the ungodly through messages from angels and imagery of harvest.

**Content:** John sees the Lamb on Mount Zion with 144,000 who bear the name of God and the Lamb and sing a new song. Three angels proclaim: the first announces the eternal gospel, the second declares the fall of Babylon, and the third warns against worshiping the beast. Those who die in the Lord are called blessed. The chapter concludes with two harvests—one of grain and one of grapes crushed in God’s winepress of wrath.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The 144,000:** God’s chosen ones who follow the Lamb, are blameless, and have God’s name on their foreheads.
- **The three angels:** Deliver messages about the gospel, the fall of Babylon, and a warning against the mark of the beast.
- **Babylon:** A symbol of the world’s rebellion against God and spiritual unfaithfulness.
- **The harvest:** A dual image of the earth ripening for judgment—both grain and grapes representing God’s final reckoning.
- **The winepress:** God’s wrathful judgment on the ungodly, with blood flowing over an enormous area (1,600 stadia).

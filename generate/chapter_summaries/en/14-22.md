**Main Theme:** Ahaziah's short, ungodly reign and his death as God's judgment on the influence of Ahab's house over Judah.

**Content:** Ahaziah becomes king over Judah and reigns for only one year. He follows the evil ways of the family of Ahab, influenced by his mother Athaliah as an advisor. He allies with King Joram of Israel against the Arameans at Ramoth in Gilead, and his visit to the wounded Joram in Jezreel becomes from God a cause for his downfall—there Jehu kills him. After Ahaziah's death, Athaliah destroys the royal family, but Jehosheba rescues the infant Joash and hides him in the temple for six years.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ahab's house:** Ahaziah follows the ways of this evil dynasty through his mother's influence and counsel.
- **God's judgment (תְּבוּסָה):** Ahaziah's downfall was directed by God as punishment for his alliance with the ungodly house of Ahab.
- **Jehu:** God's anointed instrument to destroy Ahab's house, which also strikes down Ahaziah.
- **Jehosheba:** The king's daughter and wife of a priest who rescues Joash and preserves the Davidic line.
- **Athaliah:** Ahaziah's mother who seizes power and represents the continuing threat of Ahab's house to Judah.

**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself as the Messiah during the Feast of Tabernacles in Jerusalem, creating deep division among the people and opposition from the religious leaders.

**Content:** Jesus travels secretly to Jerusalem for the Feast of Tabernacles after his brothers, who do not believe in him, challenge him to show himself publicly. In the midst of the feast, he teaches in the temple and claims that his teaching comes from the Father. On the last day of the feast, Jesus invites all who are thirsty to come to him to receive living water—a promise of the Holy Spirit. This creates division: some believe he is the Messiah, others reject him, and the leaders seek to arrest him. Nicodemus cautiously defends Jesus before the Pharisees.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Feast of Tabernacles (σκηνοπηγία):** A Jewish autumn festival with water rituals that form the background for Jesus' promise of living water.
- **Living Water (ὕδατος ζῶντος):** An image of the Holy Spirit that Jesus promises to all who believe in him.
- **Jesus' Hour/Kairos (ὁ καιρός/ἡ ὥρα):** God's appointed time for Jesus' suffering and glorification, which had not yet come.
- **Division (σχίσμα):** The people's divided response to Jesus—some believe, others reject him as the Messiah.

**Main Theme:** A hymn of praise extolling the Lord's greatness, power, and faithfulness toward Israel, in contrast to powerless idols.

**Content:** The psalm begins with a call for the Lord's servants in the temple to praise him because he is good and has chosen Israel as his own possession. It recalls God's sovereign power over creation and his acts of deliverance: the plagues in Egypt, the victory over kings Sihon and Og, and the gift of the land to Israel. Idols made of silver and gold are contrasted with the living God—they cannot speak, see, or hear, and those who trust in them become like them. The psalm concludes with calls for all Israel to bless the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Servants of the Lord (עַבְדֵי יְהוָה):** Those who stand in the temple and are called to praise God.
- **Treasured possession (סְגֻלָּה):** Israel as God's chosen, precious possession.
- **Signs and wonders (אֹתוֹת וּמֹפְתִים):** God's miraculous interventions in Egypt that demonstrated his power.
- **Powerlessness of idols:** The gods of the nations are lifeless objects without life, in sharp contrast to the living God.

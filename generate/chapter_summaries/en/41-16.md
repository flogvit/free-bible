**Main Theme:** Jesus' resurrection is confirmed by the empty tomb and his appearances to the disciples, who are commissioned to preach the gospel to the whole world.

**Content:** After the Sabbath, Mary Magdalene, Mary, and Salome go to the tomb to anoint Jesus' body. They find the stone rolled away and meet a young man in white clothing who proclaims that Jesus has risen. The women flee in fear and tell no one anything. In the longer ending (verses 9-20, which is absent from the earliest manuscripts), Jesus first appears to Mary Magdalene, then to two disciples, and finally to the eleven, whom he rebukes for their unbelief. He gives them the Great Commission, is taken up into heaven, and the disciples preach everywhere while the Lord confirms the word with signs.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Empty Tomb:** The proof of the resurrection – the stone has been rolled away and Jesus is not there.
- **"He has risen" (ἠγέρθη):** The core message proclaimed by the young man to the women.
- **Fear and Silence:** The women's reaction (τρόμος καὶ ἔκστασις) shows the overwhelming nature of the resurrection.
- **The Great Commission:** The assignment to preach the gospel to all creation (πάσῃ τῇ κτίσει).
- **God's Right Hand:** Jesus is exalted to a position of power and glory after his ascension.

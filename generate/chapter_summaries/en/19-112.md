**Main Theme:** Psalm 112 praises the blessings that follow the godly and righteous person, in contrast to the fate of the wicked.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a blessing on the one who fears the Lord and finds delight in his commands. The righteous will experience blessings such as prosperity, honor, and descendants who become powerful. Such a person is characterized by generosity toward the poor and a steady heart that does not fear bad news. The psalm concludes with a contrast: the wicked see this with anger, and their desires will come to nothing.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Fear of God (יָרֵא אֶת־יְהוָה):** The foundation of all blessing—reverence and obedience toward God.
- **Righteousness that endures (צִדְקָתוֹ עֹמֶדֶת לָעַד):** The character and deeds of the righteous have lasting value.
- **Light in darkness (זָרַח בַּחֹשֶׁךְ אוֹר):** God gives hope and guidance to the upright even in difficult times.
- **Steadfast heart (נָכוֹן לִבּוֹ):** The one who trusts in the Lord has inner stability and fearlessness.

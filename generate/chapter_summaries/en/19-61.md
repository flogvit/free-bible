**Main Theme:** A prayer for protection and trust in God as a secure refuge.

**Content:** The psalmist cries out to God from the ends of the earth with an overwhelmed heart, asking to be led to the rock that is higher than himself. He expresses confidence in God, who has been his refuge and strong tower against the enemy. The psalm also includes a prayer for the king's long life under God’s faithfulness, and concludes with a promise of eternal praise and daily fulfillment of vows.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Rock (צוּר):** A symbol of God’s supremacy and unshakable nature, giving the psalmist security.
- **Refuge and Tower (מַחְסֶה, מִגְדַּל־עֹז):** Images of God’s protection against enemies.
- **The Shelter of Your Wings (סֵתֶר כְּנָפֶיךָ):** An expression of God’s close care and protection.
- **Vows (נְדָרִים):** The psalmist’s commitment to honor God with praise and faithfulness.

**Main Theme:** The people's leaders, priests, and Levites seal a written covenant to live according to God's law.

**Content:** Nehemiah, the priests, the Levites, and the heads of the people set their seals on a written covenant. The people commit themselves to living by the Law of Moses, not marrying foreign nations, observing the Sabbath and the sabbatical year, and supporting temple service through contributions, firstfruits, tithes, and wood offerings. They conclude with this pledge: "We will not neglect our God's house."

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sealed Covenant (אֲמָנָה):** A binding written agreement signed by the leaders that obligated the entire people.
- **Tithes and Firstfruits:** The people promise to give a tenth of their crops to the Levites and the firstfruits to the priests to maintain temple service.
- **Sabbath and Sabbatical Year:** A commitment to keep the day of rest holy and let the land rest every seventh year.
- **God's House:** The temple as the center of worship, which the people promise not to neglect.

**Main Theme:** God gives David victory over the Philistine giant Goliath and shows that He saves Israel without sword or spear.

**Content:** The Philistine giant Goliath mocks the army of Israel and challenges them to single combat for forty days, while all Israel is filled with fear. The young shepherd David comes to the camp and volunteers, declaring that he comes in the name of the LORD. With a sling and a stone, he strikes down Goliath and cuts off his head with the giant’s own sword. The Philistines flee, and Israel pursues them, winning a great victory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Goliath** – The Philistine giant who mocked the army of the living God for forty days and created fear in Israel.
- **Sling and stone** – David’s simple weapons, showing that victory comes from God, not human strength.
- **"In the name of the LORD"** – David’s confession, standing in contrast to Goliath’s sword, spear, and javelin.
- **The living God** – David defends God’s honor against the pagan’s mockery and shows that Israel’s God is real.

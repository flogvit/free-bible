**Main Theme:** God preserves the line of Judah through Tamar’s bold action when Judah fails to fulfill his obligations.

**Content:** Judah has three sons with a Canaanite woman: Er, Onan, and Shelah. Er marries Tamar but dies childless because he was evil in the Lord’s sight. Onan refuses to fulfill the levirate duty and also dies. When Judah does not give her Shelah as promised, Tamar disguises herself and becomes pregnant by Judah himself. When the truth is revealed through the pledge she took (seal, cord, and staff), Judah acknowledges: "She is more righteous than I." She gives birth to the twins Perez and Zerah.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **Levirate marriage (levirate duty):** The custom whereby a brother was to provide offspring for his deceased brother’s widow in order to continue the family line.
- **The pledge (seal, cord, and staff):** Judah’s personal items of identification that Tamar demanded, which later proved his paternity.
- **"She is more righteous than I":** Judah’s acknowledgment of his own failure and Tamar’s right to secure her place in the family line.
- **Perez:** The twin who was born first and became an ancestor in the lineage leading to King David and Jesus Christ.

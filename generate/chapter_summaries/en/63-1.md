**Main Theme:** An exhortation to walk in truth and love, and a warning against accepting false teachers who deny the incarnation of Christ.

**Content:** "The Elder" writes to "the chosen lady and her children," expressing joy that they are walking in the truth. He reminds them of the original commandment to love one another and warns against deceivers who do not confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh—these are called antichrists. The congregation is urged not to receive such people, for whoever remains in the teaching of Christ has both the Father and the Son.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Truth (ἀλήθεια):** A recurring theme describing both Christian doctrine and right conduct.
- **Love (ἀγάπη):** The original commandment regarding mutual love, demonstrated through obedience to God's commands.
- **Antichrist (ἀντίχριστος):** A designation for deceivers who deny that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh.
- **Abide in the teaching (μένων ἐν τῇ διδαχῇ):** Holding fast to Christ's teaching is essential for fellowship with God.

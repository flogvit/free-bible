**Main Theme:** God's judgment against the powerful who oppress the people, and the promise of future gathering.

**Content:** Micah pronounces woe upon those who plot evil during the night and seize others' fields and houses by force (vv. 1-2). God warns of punishment from which they cannot escape (vv. 3-5). False prophets will silence Micah, while the people prefer those who promise wine and intoxication (vv. 6-11). The chapter concludes with a promise that God will gather the remnant of Israel like a flock, and the Lord Himself will lead them forth (vv. 12-13).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Woe (הוֹי):** A prophetic exclamation introducing judgment upon those who do evil.
- **Fields and houses:** Symbolize what the greedy take from others through violence and power.
- **The yoke on the neck:** An image of the judgment from which they cannot free themselves (v. 3).
- **The sheep in the fold (בָּצְרָה):** A picture of God's future gathering of His people.
- **The one who breaks through (הַפֹּרֵץ):** The Lord going before His people and opening the way out.

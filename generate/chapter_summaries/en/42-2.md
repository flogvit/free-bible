**Main Theme:** Jesus' birth, childhood, and early revelation as God's Son and Savior.

**Content:** Under Emperor Augustus' census, Joseph and Mary travel to Bethlehem, where Jesus is born and laid in a manger. Angels announce the birth to shepherds, who find the child and praise God. At Jesus' presentation in the temple, Simeon and Anna prophesy about his significance for Israel and all nations. As a twelve-year-old, Jesus is found in the temple among the teachers, where he first refers to God as his Father.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Manger (φάτνη):** The sign given by the angels to the shepherds – The Savior comes in humility, not in palaces.
- **Shepherds:** The first witnesses of the Messiah's birth represent the humble who receive the gospel.
- **Simeon's Song:** Confirms Jesus as a light for the Gentiles and the glory of Israel, fulfilling God's promise.
- **"My Father's house":** Jesus' words at age twelve reveal his awareness of his unique relationship with God.

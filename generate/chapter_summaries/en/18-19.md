**Main Theme:** Job defends himself against his friends' accusations and expresses his hope in a Redeemer despite deep suffering and isolation.

**Content:** Job accuses his friends of humiliating him and crushing him with words (vv. 2-3). He describes how God has surrounded him like an enemy and taken everything from him—honor, hope, and relationships (vv. 6-19). In the midst of despair, Job expresses his famous confession of faith: "I know that my Redeemer lives" and that he will see God (vv. 25-27). He concludes with a warning to his friends about God's judgment (vv. 28-29).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Redeemer (גֹּאֵל):** Job confesses faith in a living Redeemer who will arise and defend his cause.
- **God's siege (v. 12):** God is portrayed as a warring enemy who surrounds Job with His troops.
- **Skin and bones (v. 20):** A picture of Job's extreme physical suffering—he has barely escaped death.
- **Written in stone (vv. 23-24):** Job wishes that his words might be carved into the rock forever as a testimony.

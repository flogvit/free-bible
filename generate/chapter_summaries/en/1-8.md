**Main Theme:** God remembers Noah and ends the flood, and Noah responds with worship.

**Content:** God remembers Noah and all the animals, and sends wind over the earth so that the waters recede. The ark rests on the mountains of Ararat, and Noah sends out a raven and then a dove to check whether the ground is dry—the dove returns with an olive leaf as a sign of new life. Noah, his family, and the animals leave the ark, and Noah builds an altar and offers sacrifices to the Lord. God promises in His heart never again to curse the earth or destroy all life, and confirms that the cycles of nature will continue forever.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God remembers (זכר):** The turning point in the story—God’s faithfulness toward Noah and all creation.
- **The dove with an olive leaf:** A symbol of hope and the renewal of life on earth.
- **The altar and the sacrifice:** Noah’s gratitude and worship as a response to deliverance.
- **God’s promise:** God commits Himself to preserving the earth and its natural order.

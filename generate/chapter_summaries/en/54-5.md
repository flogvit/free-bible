**Main Theme:** Paul gives Timothy guidance on how the church should treat various groups and maintain good order.

**Content:** The chapter begins with instructions to treat all age groups with respect as family. This is followed by detailed rules for supporting widows—"true widows" over 60 years old are to receive the church’s help, while younger widows are encouraged to marry. Elders who lead well deserve double honor, especially those who work in preaching and teaching. Accusations against elders require two or three witnesses, and Timothy is warned against hasty laying on of hands and showing partiality.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **True widow (ὄντως χήρα):** A widow who stands alone without family to support her and places her hope in God through prayer.
- **Double honor (διπλῆς τιμῆς):** Elders who lead well deserve both respect and financial support, based on Scripture.
- **Laying on of hands (χεῖρας ἐπιτίθει):** The act of ordination that should not be done hastily, to avoid sharing in the sins of others.
- **Without prejudice (χωρὶς προκρίματος):** Timothy is to act impartially and without favoritism in all decisions.

**Main Theme:** A wisdom psalm that encourages trust in the Lord rather than envying the apparent success of the wicked.

**Content:** The psalm contrasts the fate of the righteous and the wicked: The wicked will wither like grass and vanish, while the meek shall inherit the land and enjoy peace. The believer is urged to trust in the Lord, do good, wait patiently, and put away anger. God knows the days of the upright, supports them when they fall, and never forsakes His faithful ones.

**Key Words/Images:**
- *Inherit the land* (יִירְשׁוּ־אָרֶץ): A recurring promise to the righteous and meek of lasting security and blessing.
- *Wither like grass* (כֶחָצִיר יִמָּלוּ): An image of the fleeting success of the wicked that quickly passes away.
- *The meek* (עֲנָוִים): The humble who wait on the Lord and will experience an abundance of peace.
- *Roll your way upon the Lord* (גּוֹל עַל־יְהוָה דַּרְכֶּךָ): To entrust one's plans and future into God's hands.

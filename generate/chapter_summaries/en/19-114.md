**Main Theme:** A song of praise celebrating God's mighty deeds at the Exodus from Egypt and the journey through the wilderness.

**Content:** When Israel went out of Egypt, Judah became God's sanctuary and Israel his dominion. Nature reacted to God's presence: the sea fled, the Jordan turned back, and the mountains skipped like rams. The psalm asks rhetorically why nature trembles, answering that all the earth should quake before the Lord's presence—he who turns the rock into a pool of water.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sanctuary (קֹדֶשׁ):** Judah became God's holy possession, a chosen people.
- **The sea fled:** The miracle at the Reed Sea where the waters parted for Israel.
- **Mountains that skip:** A poetic image of nature's awe before the Creator.
- **Rock into a pool of water:** God's care in the wilderness when he provided water from the rock.

**Main Theme:** A detailed genealogy of the sons of Israel with special focus on the descendants of the tribe of Judah down to King David.

**Content:** The chapter opens with a list of the twelve sons of Israel, before providing an extensive review of Judah's descendants. Through Perez and Hezron, the lineage is traced via Ram to David, the seventh son of Jesse. The chapter also mentions Hezron's other sons Jerahmeel and Caleb, as well as their many descendants and the cities they founded.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Er and God's Judgment:** Judah's firstborn was evil in the Lord's sight and was put to death by Him – demonstrating God's holiness and judgment against sin.
- **Achan (Achar):** Referred to as "the one who brought trouble on Israel" due to his faithlessness regarding the ban – a warning about sin among the people.
- **The Lineage of David:** From Judah through Perez, Hezron, Ram, to Jesse and David – establishing the royal line.
- **Caleb and City Founders:** Many of Caleb's descendants are identified as "father of" various cities, showing the tribe's territorial significance in Judah.

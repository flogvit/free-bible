**Main Theme:** Paul thanks God for the Thessalonians' living faith and their testimony that has spread far and wide.

**Content:** Paul, Silas, and Timothy greet the congregation and thank God for their work of faith, labor of love, and endurance of hope. The gospel came to them not only in word but also in power and in the Holy Spirit, and they received it with joy amid great suffering. They became imitators of the apostles and of the Lord, and an example to believers in Macedonia and Achaia. Their turning from idols to the living God, and their expectation of the Son's return from heaven, has become known everywhere.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Election (ἐκλογή):** God has chosen the Thessalonians, confirmed by the power of the gospel in their lives.
- **Work of faith, labor of love, endurance of hope:** A triad describing the three foundational pillars of Christian life expressed in active obedience.
- **Imitators (μιμηταί):** The Thessalonians became imitators of Paul and the Lord by receiving the Word with joy amid suffering.
- **The coming wrath (ὀργὴ ἐρχομένη):** Jesus rescues believers from God's coming judgment, a central element of Christian hope.

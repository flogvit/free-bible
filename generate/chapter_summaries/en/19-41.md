**Main Theme:** A prayer from a sick person who experiences betrayal by friends, yet places their trust in God’s grace and protection.

**Content:** The psalm opens with a blessing on the one who cares for the weak—the Lord will deliver him in times of trouble. The psalmist himself is ill and prays for mercy and healing while confessing his sin. Enemies, and even a close friend, have betrayed him, but he trusts that God will raise him up and sustain him in his innocence. The psalm concludes with a doxology marking the end of the first book of Psalms.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The weak (dal):** The poor or needy person whom the blessed one cares for.
- **Bed of sickness:** A picture of the psalmist’s suffering, where God turns his bed into a place of healing in his illness (v.4).
- **The friend who betrays:** The trusted one who ate his bread but lifted his heel against him (v.10)—a deep betrayal.
- **Integrity (tom):** The psalmist’s innocence, which God upholds him in (v.13).

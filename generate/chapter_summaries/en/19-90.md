**Main Theme:** A prayer by Moses that contrasts God's eternity with human transience, asking for wisdom and grace.

**Content:** The psalm opens with the confession of God as the people's refuge through all generations, the eternal God before creation. Human life is compared to grass that withers in a day, and our days are short—70 or 80 years filled with toil. Moses asks God to teach us to number our days so that we may gain wisdom, and concludes with a prayer that God's grace and blessing may rest upon the people and their work.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Refuge/Dwelling (מָעוֹן):** God has been Israel's secure dwelling through all generations.
- **The grass that withers:** Human life is brief like grass that springs up in the morning and withers by evening.
- **A thousand years as one day:** God's perspective on time is radically different from humanity's.
- **Number our days:** A prayer for wisdom to live rightly in light of the brevity of life.

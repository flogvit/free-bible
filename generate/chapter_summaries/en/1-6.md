**Main Theme:** God's judgment on humanity due to widespread evil, and his plan of salvation through Noah and the ark.

**Content:** Humanity multiplies on earth, but wickedness grows—God sees that every thought in people's hearts is only evil. God regrets having made humankind and decides to wipe out all life with a flood. Noah, who is righteous and walks with God, finds favor and receives the command to build an ark. God makes a covenant with Noah and instructs him to take his family and pairs of every kind of animal to preserve life. Noah obeys everything God commands.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sons of God (בְנֵי־הָאֱלֹהִים):** Mysterious figures who take human daughters as wives, contributing to the corruption on earth.
- **Nephilim (הַנְּפִלִים):** Mighty and renowned men from ancient times, associated with the pre-flood era.
- **The Ark (תֵּבָה):** The vessel Noah is to build to save his family and animal life from the flood—an image of God's salvation amid judgment.
- **Covenant (בְּרִית):** God's promise to Noah, the first covenant mentioned in the Bible, guaranteeing deliverance for those inside the ark.
- **Violence/חָמָס (chamas):** Describes the condition of the earth—filled with violence and corruption, which is the cause of God's judgment.

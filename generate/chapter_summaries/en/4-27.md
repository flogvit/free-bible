**Main Theme:** Inheritance rights for daughters and the transition of leadership from Moses to Joshua.

**Content:** The daughters of Zelophehad claim inheritance rights because their father died without sons, and God upholds their case—this becomes a lasting inheritance law for Israel. God tells Moses that he will see the Promised Land from the mountain but not enter it due to the rebellion at Meribah. At Moses' request, God appoints Joshua as the new leader, and Moses transfers authority to him through the laying on of hands before the people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Daughters of Zelophehad:** Five women who courageously step forward and establish inheritance rights for daughters in Israel.
- **Mount Abarim:** The place where Moses is to see the land he will not enter, as a consequence of disobedience.
- **Laying on of hands:** A ritual act by which Moses transfers authority and leadership to Joshua.
- **Urim:** The means that the priest Eleazar is to use to seek God's will on behalf of Joshua and the people.

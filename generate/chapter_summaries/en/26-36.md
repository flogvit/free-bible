**Main Theme:** God promises to restore Israel and sanctify His name among the nations.

**Content:** God prophesies against the mountains of Israel and promises that the land will be restored after enemies have mocked it. Israel had profaned God’s holy name among the nations through their impurity and sins. Therefore, God – not for Israel’s sake, but for the sake of His holy name – will gather the people from exile, cleanse them with pure water, give them a new heart, and put His Spirit within them. The land will once again become fruitful and be like the garden of Eden, so that the nations may know that the Lord is God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The mountains of Israel:** The land is personified and addressed directly as the recipient of God’s promises of restoration.
- **God’s holy name:** God acts primarily to sanctify His name, which Israel had dishonored among the nations.
- **A new heart and a new spirit:** God promises inner renewal – the heart of stone is removed and replaced with a heart of flesh and God’s Spirit.
- **Pure water:** A symbol of spiritual cleansing from all impurity and idolatry.
- **The garden of Eden:** The devastated land will become so fruitful that it is compared to the garden of paradise.

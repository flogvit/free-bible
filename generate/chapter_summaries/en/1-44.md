**Main Theme:** Joseph puts his brothers to a decisive test of loyalty by planting his silver cup in Benjamin’s sack.

**Content:** Joseph orders his steward to fill the brothers’ sacks with grain and money, and to place the silver cup in Benjamin’s sack. After they depart, the brothers are overtaken and accused of theft. When the cup is found in Benjamin’s possession, Joseph demands that he be kept as a slave. Judah delivers a moving speech in which he offers himself as a slave in Benjamin’s place, to spare his aging father from deadly grief.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Silver Cup (גְּבִיעַ הַכֶּסֶף):** Joseph’s personal drinking cup, used as the instrument of the test; Joseph also claims to use it for divination (נַחֵשׁ).
- **The Guarantee (עָרַב):** Judah has personally guaranteed Benjamin’s safety to his father.
- **Tearing Their Garments (וַיִּקְרְעוּ שִׂמְלֹתָם):** An expression of deep grief and despair when the cup is discovered.
- **Substitute Sacrifice:** Judah’s offer to become a slave in Benjamin’s place shows a radical transformation from the brother who once sold Joseph.

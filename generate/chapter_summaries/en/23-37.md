**Main Theme:** God delivers Jerusalem from Assyria through Hezekiah's prayer and Isaiah's prophecy.

**Content:** King Hezekiah responds to the Assyrian threat by tearing his clothes, putting on sackcloth, and seeking the house of the Lord. He sends messengers to the prophet Isaiah and asks for intercession. When Sennacherib sends a threatening letter, Hezekiah lays it before the Lord in prayer. Isaiah conveys God's answer: Assyria will not enter Jerusalem but will return by the same way they came. The angel of the Lord strikes down 185,000 Assyrian soldiers, and Sennacherib returns home to Nineveh, where he is killed by his own sons.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sackcloth (שַׂק):** A symbol of mourning, humility, and repentance that Hezekiah and the priests put on.
- **The Virgin Daughter of Zion:** A poetic image of Jerusalem mocking the fleeing Assyrian king.
- **The Angel of the Lord:** God's supernatural intervention that kills 185,000 soldiers in one night.
- **Root and Fruit:** An image of how the remnant of Judah will survive and flourish again (verse 31).

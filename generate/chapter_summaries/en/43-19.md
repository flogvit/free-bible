**Main Theme:** Jesus' suffering, death, and burial – the fulfillment of God's plan of salvation.

**Content:** Pilatus has Jesus flogged and mocked by soldiers with a crown of thorns and a purple robe, yet repeatedly declares that he finds no guilt in him. Under pressure from the Jews who cry out, "We have no king but Caesar," he nevertheless hands Jesus over to be crucified. At Golgotha, Jesus is crucified between two others, and Pilatus sets up an inscription reading "Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews" in three languages. After entrusting his mother to the disciple whom he loved, Jesus says, "It is finished," and dies. Joseph of Arimathea and Nicodemus bury him in a new tomb.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Behold the man!" (Ecce homo):** Pilatus' words when he presents the flogged and mocked Jesus, unintentionally proclaiming his true humanity.
- **The crown of thorns:** The soldiers' mockery ironically becomes a symbol of Jesus' true kingship through suffering.
- **"It is finished" (Tetelestai):** Jesus' final words marking that the work of salvation is completed – not a defeat, but a victory.
- **Blood and water:** From Jesus' pierced side flow blood and water, testimony to his real death and symbol of salvation and cleansing.

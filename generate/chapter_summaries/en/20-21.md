**Main Theme:** God's sovereignty over all human plans and actions, and the importance of righteousness over outward religiosity.

**Content:** The chapter opens with God directing kings' hearts like watercourses and emphasizes that justice and right conduct are more valuable than sacrifice (vv. 1–3). Proverbs warns against pride, dishonesty, laziness, and quarrelsomeness, contrasting the fate of the righteous and the wicked. The chapter concludes by stating that no wisdom can stand against the Lord, and that victory belongs to Him alone (vv. 30–31).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Watercourses (v. 1):** God's sovereign guidance of kings' hearts illustrated through the image of water being directed where He wills.
- **Righteousness rather than sacrifice (v. 3):** Doing what is right is more important to God than religious rituals.
- **The quarrelsome wife (vv. 9, 19):** A repeated image warning against strife in the home; it is better to live on a rooftop or in the wilderness.
- **The horse and victory (v. 31):** The horse is prepared for battle, but victory comes from the Lord—an image of God's ultimate control.

**Main Theme:** David is humbled during his flight from Absalom, while Absalom consolidates his power in Jerusalem.

**Content:** During David's flight from Jerusalem, he first meets Ziba, the servant of Mephibosheth, who brings supplies and claims that Mephibosheth has betrayed David—David gives him all of Mephibosheth's property. Then Shimei, from Saul's family, curses David and throws stones at him, but David refuses to let his men kill him and accepts it as possibly coming from the Lord. In Jerusalem, David's friend Hushai arrives and declares apparent loyalty to Absalom. At Ahithophel's advice, Absalom lies with David's concubines on the roof, visible to all Israel, to demonstrate the final break with his father.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Ziba and the Supplies** – Represents opportunism and doubt about who remains loyal to David during the crisis.
- **Shimei's Curse** – David interprets it as possible divine discipline and shows humility in suffering.
- **Absalom on the Roof** – A public act symbolizing the complete takeover of his father's kingdom and women.
- **Ahithophel's Advice** – Described as being like asking God himself; demonstrates his enormous influence on both sides.

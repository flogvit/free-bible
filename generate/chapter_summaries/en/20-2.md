**Main Theme:** The chapter is a father's exhortation to his son to seek wisdom actively, because it brings reverence for God, protection from evil, and a good life.

**Content:** The son is urged to accept words of wisdom and seek understanding as if searching for hidden treasures (vv. 1–4). Those who do this will understand reverence for God and find knowledge of God, for the Lord gives wisdom (vv. 5–6). Wisdom protects against evil people and the adulterous woman, whose path leads to death (vv. 12–19). The result is that the righteous will dwell in the land, while the wicked will be cut off (vv. 21–22).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hidden treasure (מַטְמוֹנִים):** Wisdom must be sought actively and intensely, like one digging for silver and treasures.
- **The adulterous woman (אִשָּׁה זָרָה):** Represents unfaithfulness and paths of apostasy that lead to death and the realm of the dead.
- **Reverence for God (יִרְאַת יְהוָה):** The goal of seeking wisdom—to know and honor God rightly.
- **Shield (מָגֵן):** God protects those who walk in uprightness, using His wisdom as a shield.

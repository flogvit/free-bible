**Main Theme:** Paul explains the believer's freedom from the law and describes the inner struggle of humanity between wanting to do good and doing evil.

**Content:** Through a marriage analogy, Paul shows that believers have died to the law through Christ's body and now belong to him who was raised from the dead. The law is holy and good, but it reveals sin without being able to overcome it. Paul describes his inner conflict: he wants to do what is right, but the sin living in him causes him to do what is wrong. Deliverance from this condition comes only through Jesus Christ.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Marriage Analogy (vv. 1–4):** Just as a woman is freed from the marriage law when her husband dies, so believers are released from the law through Christ's death.
- **The Law as Holy (v. 12):** The law is not sin, but holy, righteous, and good—it reveals sin.
- **Sin Dwelling in Me (vv. 17, 20):** Sin is described as a power that lives within a person and acts against their will.
- **The Inner Person (vv. 22–23):** Paul distinguishes between the mind that delights in God's law and the members that are captive to the law of sin.

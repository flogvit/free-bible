**Main Theme:** God demonstrates His power over Egypt through three plagues to reveal that there is none like Him.

**Content:** The chapter describes the fifth, sixth, and seventh plagues: livestock disease, boils, and hail with fire. Each time, God distinguishes between Egypt and Israel—the livestock and land of the Israelites in Goshen remain unharmed. God declares that the purpose of the plagues is for Pharaoh to know that there is no one like the Lord throughout all the earth. After the plague of hail, Pharaoh confesses his sin, but hardens his heart again once the plague ceases.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Livestock Disease (דֶּבֶר):** The fifth plague that kills Egypt's livestock but spares that of the Israelites.
- **Boils (שְׁחִין):** The sixth plague affecting both people and animals, so severe that even the magicians could not stand before Moses.
- **Hail with Fire (בָּרָד וְאֵשׁ):** The seventh plague, the worst in Egypt's history, destroying crops and killing those who did not seek shelter.
- **Hardened Heart:** Pharaoh's repeated stubbornness despite God's mighty signs and his own confession of sin.

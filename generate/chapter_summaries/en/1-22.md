**Main Theme:** God tests Abraham’s faith by commanding him to sacrifice Isaac, but intervenes and confirms His covenant.

**Content:** God commands Abraham to offer his beloved son Isaac on a mountain in Moriah. After a three-day journey, Abraham binds Isaac on the altar, but at the last moment, the angel of the Lord stops him. A ram is sacrificed in Isaac’s place, and Abraham names the place "The Lord will provide." God swears by Himself and renews His covenant with promises of numerous descendants and blessing for all nations. The chapter concludes with a genealogy of Nahor’s children, mentioning Rebekah.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Test (נִסָּה):** God tests Abraham’s faith and obedience through the ultimate challenge.
- **The Lord will provide (יְהוָה יִרְאֶה):** Abraham names the place prophetically—God sees the need and provides the sacrifice.
- **The ram in the thicket:** God Himself supplies a substitute offering, pointing forward to salvation.
- **Covenant renewal:** God swears by Himself and confirms His promises of descendants and blessing for all peoples.

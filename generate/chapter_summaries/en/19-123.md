**Main Theme:** A prayer for grace from God in the face of contempt and mockery from arrogant people.

**Content:** Psalm 123 is a song of ascent where the psalmist lifts their eyes toward God who reigns in heaven. Just as servants look to their master's hand in expectation, so believers wait for the Lord's grace. The psalm is a heartfelt plea for mercy because the people have had enough of contempt from the self-assured and proud.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lifting eyes toward heaven:** An expression of prayer and dependence on God who reigns on high.
- **Servants' eyes toward their master's hand:** A picture of humble expectation and trust in God's care and intervention.
- **Grace (חָנֵּנוּ):** The central cry of the psalm, repeated—an appeal to God's mercy.
- **Contempt and mockery:** Describes the suffering inflicted by arrogant people from whom believers seek deliverance.

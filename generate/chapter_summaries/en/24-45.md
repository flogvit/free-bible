**Main Theme:** God's comfort and exhortation to Baruch amid his despair over the coming judgment.

**Content:** In the fourth year of Jehoiakim, Jeremiah delivers a message from God to his scribe Baruch, who laments sorrow, pain, and exhaustion. God explains that He Himself tears down what He has built and uproots what He has planted throughout the earth. Baruch is exhorted not to seek greatness for himself but receives the promise that his life will be spared wherever he goes.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Building and tearing down, planting and uprooting:** God's sovereign power to both create and judge – now the time of judgment has come.
- **Seeking greatness:** The warning against personal ambitions in a time of universal judgment.
- **Life as spoil of war:** The promise that Baruch will keep his life as a gift amid catastrophe.

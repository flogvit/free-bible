**Main Theme:** Ezekiel 7 is an intense prophecy of judgment in which God declares that the end has come upon the land of Israel because of the people's sins and idolatry.

**Content:** The Lord proclaims that the end has now come upon the four corners of the land, and His wrath will strike the people without mercy. Catastrophe will arrive in the form of sword, pestilence, and famine—no one shall escape. Wealth will lose its value; silver and gold cannot save anyone on the day of wrath. Foreign nations will plunder the land and desecrate God's sanctuary, while kings, princes, priests, and prophets stand powerless. Finally, the people will know that He is the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The End (הַקֵּץ):** Repeated several times to emphasize that judgment is inevitable and imminent.
- **Silver and gold cast aside:** The wealth that has been a stumbling block will become worthless on the day of judgment.
- **Limp hands and knees turning to water:** Describes the people's total powerlessness and fear when judgment strikes.
- **Doves in the valleys:** The survivors will mourn over their sin, like cooing doves on the mountains.

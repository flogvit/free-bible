**Main Theme:** A declaration of innocence in which the psalmist asks God to examine his integrity and separate him from the wicked.

**Content:** David claims to have walked with uprightness and trust in the Lord, asking God to test and search his inner being. He distances himself from liars, hypocrites, and the ungodly, declaring his love for God's house and altar. The psalm concludes with a promise to praise the Lord among the assembly, standing on firm ground.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Integrity/Innocence (תֻּמִּי):** The psalmist's claim to have lived righteously and sincerely before God.
- **Testing (בְּחָנֵנִי/נַסֵּנִי/צָרְפָה):** A prayer that God would examine the kidneys and heart—the innermost parts of a person.
- **Washing hands in innocence:** Ritual purity as a symbol of moral integrity when worshiping at the altar.
- **God's house (מְעוֹן בֵּיתֶךָ):** The temple as the place where God's glory dwells, which the psalmist loves.

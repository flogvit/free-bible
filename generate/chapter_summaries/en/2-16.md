**Main Theme:** God provides Israel with manna and quail in the wilderness and institutes the Sabbath rest.

**Content:** The Israelites complain about a lack of food in the Wilderness of Sin, and God promises to send bread from heaven as a test of their obedience. In the evening, quail arrive, and in the morning, manna falls—a white, honey-sweet bread. The people are instructed to gather only enough for each day, but double on the sixth day, because the seventh day is the Sabbath when no manna will appear. An omer of manna is preserved as a testimony for future generations, and Israel eats manna for forty years.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Manna** – Bread from heaven that God gives daily; symbolizes God’s faithful provision.
- **Sabbath** – The seventh day set apart for rest; the first time the Sabbath command is put into practice.
- **Test** – The instructions regarding manna test whether the people will obey God’s torah.
- **Omer** – A measure for the daily portion; an omer is kept as a reminder for future generations.
- **Grumbling/Complaining** – The people’s discontent reveals their lack of trust in God and Moses.

**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself as the Messiah and Savior of the world through encounters that break social and ethnic barriers.

**Content:** Jesus meets a Samaritan woman at Jacob's well and offers her "living water" that gives eternal life. He reveals his knowledge of her life and teaches that true worship takes place in spirit and truth, not in a specific location. The woman testifies in the village, and many Samaritans come to faith and confess Jesus as the "Savior of the world." Back in Galilee, Jesus heals an official's son from a distance—the man believes Jesus' word before seeing the miracle, and his entire household comes to faith.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Living water:** Symbolizes the Holy Spirit and the eternal life that Jesus gives, in contrast to physical water which only temporarily quenches thirst.
- **Worship in spirit and truth:** The new worship that is not bound to Jerusalem or Gerizim, but takes place in the Spirit and in truth.
- **The harvest:** A picture of missionary work—the fields are white for harvest, and the disciples are sent out to reap what others have sown.
- **Savior of the world:** The Samaritans' confession showing that salvation through Jesus applies to all people, not only the Jews.

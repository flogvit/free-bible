**Main Theme:** Paul reveals the mystery of the full participation of the Gentiles in Christ and prays that the church may comprehend the boundless love of Christ.

**Content:** Paul, a prisoner for Christ, explains how God has shown him by revelation the mystery: that the Gentiles are fellow heirs, one body with the Jews, and sharers in the promise in Christ. He has been given grace to proclaim the unsearchable riches of Christ, so that through the church the manifold wisdom of God may now be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places. Paul prays that believers may be strengthened by the Spirit, rooted and grounded in love, to grasp how wide and long and high and deep is the love of Christ, which surpasses all knowledge. The chapter concludes with a doxology to God who is able to do far more than we ask or understand.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mystery (μυστήριον):** God's plan of salvation now revealed—the Gentiles are fully included in Christ together with the Jews.
- **Manifold wisdom of God (πολυποίκιλος σοφία):** The rich and varied wisdom of God made known through the church to the heavenly powers.
- **Unsearchable riches of Christ (ἀνεξιχνίαστον πλοῦτος):** The infinite and unfathomable riches of the gospel that Paul is called to proclaim.
- **Width, length, height, and depth:** A poetic image of the incomprehensible dimensions of Christ's love into which believers are invited to explore.

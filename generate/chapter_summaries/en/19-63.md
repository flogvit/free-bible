**Main Theme:** A longing prayer from David in the wilderness, where he expresses deep thirst for God's presence and trust in His protection.

**Content:** David is in the wilderness of Judah and describes his longing for God with images of thirst and a parched land. He recalls how he has seen God's power and glory in the sanctuary, and declares that God's steadfast love is better than life itself. The psalm expresses deep trust in God as helper and protector, while enemies who seek David's life will come to ruin.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Thirst (צָמְאָה):** The soul's intense longing for God is compared to physical thirst in a waterless wilderness landscape.
- **Shadow of your wings (צֵל כְּנָפֶיךָ):** An image of God's protection and care where David finds safety and can rejoice.
- **Your right hand (יְמִינֶךָ):** Symbolizes God's support and power that upholds David.
- **Steadfast love/loyal love (חֶסֶד):** God's faithful love which David values more than life itself.

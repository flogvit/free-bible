**Main Theme:** The chapter establishes that Israel is to worship the Lord only at the place He Himself chooses, in contrast to the many sacrificial sites of the nations.

**Content:** The Israelites are to completely destroy all Canaanite cultic sites and gather their worship at the one place the Lord chooses. There they must bring their sacrifices, tithes, and offerings, and rejoice together with their entire households and the Levites, who have no inherited portion. Ordinary meat may be eaten anywhere, but blood must never be consumed, for the blood is the life. The chapter strongly warns against imitating the customs of the nations, which included child sacrifice.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Chosen Place:** The place where the Lord chooses to have His name dwell – the center of all legitimate worship.
- **The Blood Is the Life (nefesh):** The theological basis for the prohibition against consuming blood; life belongs to God alone.
- **The Levites:** They are to be included in the festive meals because they have no inherited portion in the land.
- **Destruction of Idolatrous Sites:** Altars, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles must be destroyed to prevent syncretism.

**Main Theme:** A detailed genealogy of the tribe of Benjamin, with special emphasis on Saul's royal lineage.

**Content:** The chapter lists Benjamin's descendants through several branches, including the families of Bela, Ehud, Shaharajim, and Elpaal. Several family heads settled in cities such as Geba, Ono, Lod, Aijalon, and Jerusalem. The most important genealogical line runs from Ner to Kish to Saul, and further through Jonathan to his descendants. The chapter concludes with the sons of Ulam, described as brave archers with 150 descendants.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Family Heads (רָאשֵׁי אָבוֹת):** Leaders who organized the tribe's structure and settlement in various cities.
- **Saul's Lineage:** The royal line from Benjamin that establishes the tribe's historical significance for Israel's first kingdom.
- **Archers (דֹּרְכֵי קֶשֶׁת):** Ulam's descendants are described as brave warriors, highlighting Benjamin's military strength.

**Main Theme:** God's judgment on the nations for their atrocities and transgressions.

**Content:** Amos, a shepherd from Tekoa, receives his visions concerning Israel during the reigns of King Uzziah and Jeroboam II, two years before an earthquake. The LORD roars from Zion and proclaims judgment against Israel's neighboring peoples: Damascus, the Philistines (Gaza), Tyre, Edom, and Ammon. Each nation is condemned for specific atrocities—from war crimes to slave trading and breaches of covenant relationships—with fire destroying their strongholds.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"For three transgressions... yes, for four":** The judgment formula that introduces each oracle, emphasizing that the measure of sin is full.
- **The LORD roars from Zion:** The image of God as a roaring lion signals coming judgment and shows Jerusalem as His place of revelation.
- **Fire against the strongholds:** A recurring motif symbolizing God's total judgment on the centers of power among the nations.
- **Gilead:** The region brutally treated by Damascus and Ammon, representing the victims of the neighboring peoples' cruelty.

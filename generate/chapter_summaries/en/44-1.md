**Main Theme:** Jesus’ ascension and the church’s preparation for the coming of the Holy Spirit.

**Content:** After his resurrection, Jesus appeared to the apostles over a period of 40 days and taught them about God’s kingdom. He commanded them to wait in Jerusalem for the Father’s promise—the baptism with the Holy Spirit that would give them power to be witnesses from Jerusalem to the ends of the earth. After ascending from the Mount of Olives, two angels promised that Jesus would return in the same way. The disciples gathered together in prayer along with the women and Mary, the mother of Jesus, and chose Matthias to replace Judas as an apostle.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Promise of the Holy Spirit:** Jesus promises that the disciples will be baptized with the Holy Spirit and receive power to be his witnesses.
- **The Ascension:** Jesus was lifted up while the disciples watched, and a cloud took him out of their sight.
- **Witnesses to the Ends of the Earth:** The mission extends from Jerusalem through Judea and Samaria to the whole world.
- **The Choice of an Apostle:** Matthias was chosen by casting lots to fill Judas’ place among the twelve apostles.

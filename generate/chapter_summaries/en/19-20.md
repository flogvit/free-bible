**Main Theme:** A prayer from the people for the king's victory, placing trust in God’s name rather than military strength.

**Content:** The psalm is a collective prayer in which the congregation asks that God answer the king on his day of distress and send help from His sanctuary and Zion. The people express confidence that the Lord saves His anointed one and answers him from His holy heaven. While some trust in chariots and horses, God’s people call upon the name of the Lord—and those who trust in Him will stand firm while their enemies fall.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord’s Anointed (משיח):** The king as God’s chosen representative, with messianic overtones.
- **God’s Name:** God’s character and power, which His people call upon and trust instead of relying on military means.
- **The Sanctuary and Zion:** God’s dwelling place as the source of help and support for the king.
- **Chariots and Horses:** Symbols of human military might, contrasted with trust in God.

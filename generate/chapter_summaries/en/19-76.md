**Main Theme:** The psalm praises God's majestic power that crushes enemies and saves the humble.

**Content:** God is known in Judah and Israel, and his dwelling place is in Salem (Jerusalem) and Zion. From there he has broken the enemy's weapons—bow, shield, and sword. When God rises up to judge from heaven, the earth trembles and becomes still, for he saves all the humble of the earth. The psalm calls everyone to make vows and bring gifts to the Lord, who is awesome in the sight of the kings of the earth.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Salem and Zion:** God's dwelling place and holy site, the center of his power and presence.
- **Broken weapons:** Symbolize God's total victory over all hostile powers.
- **The humble of the earth (anve-erets):** The oppressed whom God rises up to save through his judgment.
- **Awesome (nora):** God's nature that inspires awe and causes no one to stand before his wrath.

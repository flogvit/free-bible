**Main Theme:** God heals the Syrian army commander Naaman through the prophet Elisha, while Gehasi is punished for greed.

**Content:** Naaman, a powerful Syrian army commander suffering from leprosy, is sent to the prophet Elisha on the advice of an Israelite slave girl. Through a messenger, Elisha instructs him to wash seven times in the Jordan—a humiliating command that Naaman initially rejects, but his servants persuade him to obey. After being healed, Naaman confesses that the God of Israel is the only true God and asks for soil from Israel so he can offer sacrifices to the Lord. Elisha’s servant Gehasi runs after Naaman and lies in order to obtain gifts, but is exposed and punished with Naaman’s leprosy.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Jordan River:** The place of Naaman’s cleansing, symbolizing obedience and humility over human pride.
- **Seven times:** The number seven symbolizes perfection and complete cleansing in biblical tradition.
- **Leprosy (צָרַעַת):** A skin disease that serves as both Naaman’s trial and Gehasi’s punishment—demonstrating God’s power to heal and judge.
- **Israelite soil:** Naaman’s request to take soil shows his new faith and commitment to worship only the God of Israel.
- **Gehasi’s greed:** A warning against exploiting God’s grace for personal gain.

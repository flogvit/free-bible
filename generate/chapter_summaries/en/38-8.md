**Main Theme:** God promises to restore Jerusalem and make the city a spiritual center where all nations will seek Him.

**Content:** God declares His burning zeal for Zion and promises to return and dwell in Jerusalem, which will be called "the City of Truth". He paints a picture of peace where elderly people and playing children fill the streets, and the land yields its harvest. The people are urged to speak truth, judge righteously, and not plan evil. The four fasts of mourning will be transformed into joy and celebration, and many nations will come to Jerusalem to seek the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **City of Truth (עִיר־הָאֱמֶת):** Jerusalem's new name expressing its character under God's presence.
- **Elderly and children in the streets:** A picture of security, peace, and blessing—a community where all generations thrive.
- **Fasts become joy:** The four fasts of mourning will be transformed into festivals of joy when restoration comes.
- **Ten men grasp a garment corner:** A picture of the future seeking of Israel's God by the nations—ten men from every language group will seize hold of one Jew and wish to follow him.

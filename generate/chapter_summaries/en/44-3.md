**Main Theme:** Peter heals a lame man in Jesus’ name and proclaims Jesus as the Messiah and the author of life.

**Content:** Peter and John meet a man who had been lame from birth at the temple’s Beautiful Gate. Peter heals him in the name of Jesus Christ, and the man enters the temple while praising God. When the people gather in Solomon’s Colonnade, Peter explains that the healing occurred through faith in Jesus’ name—he whom they handed over and denied, but whom God raised from the dead. Peter calls for repentance so that sins may be wiped out and times of refreshing may come from the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Beautiful Gate (Ὡραία Πύλη):** The temple gate where the lame man sat daily begging for alms, the site of this public miracle.
- **Jesus’ name (ὄνομα Ἰησοῦ):** The basis for the healing and the believer’s authority—it is through faith in his name that the man was made well.
- **Author of life (ἀρχηγὸς τῆς ζωῆς):** A Christological title emphasizing Jesus as the source of life, in contrast to their choice of a murderer.
- **Repentance (μετάνοια):** Peter’s call to the people to turn back, linked with the promise that sins will be wiped out and the Messiah will return.

**Main Theme:** God’s guidance to the exiles in Babylon to settle down and His promise of future restoration.

**Content:** Jeremiah sends a letter from Jerusalem to the Jewish exiles in Babylon with God’s instructions: they are to build houses, plant gardens, marry, and seek the welfare of the city. God warns against listening to false prophets and diviners who give false promises of a quick return. After 70 years, God will bring them back and give them a future and hope. The chapter also contains God’s judgment against the false prophets Ahab, Zedekiah, and Shemaiah, who prophesied lies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **70 years:** God’s appointed time for the exile in Babylon before restoration.
- **Future and hope (אַחֲרִית וְתִקְוָה):** God’s promise of a good future for His people despite their present suffering.
- **Seek the welfare of the city:** An encouragement to pray for and work for the best interests of Babylon, because the well-being of the exiles is tied to that host city.
- **False prophets:** The warning against those who prophesy lies in God’s name and give the people false hope.

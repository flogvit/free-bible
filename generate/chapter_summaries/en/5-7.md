**Main Theme:** God commands Israel to remain separate from the peoples of Canaan and promises blessing for obedience.

**Content:** When Israel enters Canaan, they are to destroy the seven nations there and demolish all their cultic objects. They must not form alliances or intermarry with them, so as to avoid being led into idolatry. God chose Israel as his treasured possession—not because they were numerous, but out of love and faithfulness to the promise made to their ancestors. If they keep God's commands, he promises blessing, protection from disease, fertility, and victory over their enemies.

**Key Terms/Images:**
- **עַם סְגֻלָּה (am segullah) – treasured possession:** Israel is God's chosen treasure among all the peoples of the earth.
- **חֵרֶם (cherem) – ban:** The total destruction of the Canaanite peoples and their cultic objects, consecrated to God.
- **בְּרִית וְחֶסֶד (berit ve-chesed) – covenant and faithful love:** God keeps his covenant for a thousand generations with those who love him.

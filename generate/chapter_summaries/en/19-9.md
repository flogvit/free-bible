**Main Theme:** A hymn of praise that exalts God as a righteous Judge who defends the poor and judges the nations.

**Content:** David praises the Lord with all his heart for His wonderful works and righteous judgment against enemies. God reigns on Zion as the Judge of the world and is a refuge for the oppressed in times of trouble. The psalm concludes with a prayer that God would arise and show the nations that they are merely human.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Righteous Judge (שׁוֹפֵט צֶדֶק):** God sits on His throne and judges the world with justice and the peoples with righteousness.
- **Refuge/Stronghold (מִשְׂגָּב):** The Lord is a fortress for the oppressed, a shelter in times of distress.
- **Zion (צִיּוֹן):** God's dwelling place from which His mighty deeds are proclaimed among the nations.
- **The Grave (שְׁאוֹל):** The place to which the wicked and those who forget God will return.

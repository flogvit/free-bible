**Main Theme:** Christ's resurrection is the foundation of the Christian faith and the guarantee of believers' own resurrection.

**Content:** Paul reminds the Corinthians of the core of the gospel: Christ died for our sins, was buried, and rose again on the third day according to the Scriptures, confirmed by many witnesses. He argues that without the resurrection, faith would be futile and those who have fallen asleep in Christ would be lost. But Christ has been raised as the firstfruits, and just as all die in Adam, so all will be made alive in Christ. At the last trumpet, the dead will be raised with imperishable, spiritual bodies, and death will be swallowed up in victory.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Firstfruits (aparche):** Christ is the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep—the guarantee that believers too will be raised.
- **Adam and Christ:** The contrast between the two: through Adam came death, through Christ comes resurrection and life.
- **Spiritual body (soma pneumatikon):** The resurrected body is not the same as the earthly one, but a transformed, imperishable, and glorious body.
- **Victory over death:** The resurrection means that death, the sting of sin, has been defeated through Jesus Christ.

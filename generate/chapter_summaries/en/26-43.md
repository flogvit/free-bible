**Main Theme:** The Lord's glory returns to the temple from the east, and the altar is consecrated for sacrifices.

**Content:** Ezekiel sees the Lord's glory coming from the east with a voice like the sound of many waters, filling the temple. God declares the temple as His eternal dwelling place and throne, requiring Israel to remove their sexual immorality and idolatry. Ezekiel receives detailed measurements for the altar and instructions for its seven-day consecration, after which priests from the line of Zadok are to offer burnt offerings and peace offerings.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's glory (כְּבוֹד יְהוָה):** God's visible presence coming from the east and filling the temple, a sign of restored fellowship.
- **The East Gate:** The direction from which God's glory arrives, the same path it took when leaving the temple earlier in Ezekiel.
- **The Altar:** The central place for sacrifices, with precise measurements and a seven-day consecration ritual.
- **Zadok's sons:** The priestly lineage alone permitted to serve in the Lord's presence in the future temple.

**Main Theme:** God calls his people to repentance and promises to return to Jerusalem with mercy.

**Content:** In the second year of Darius, Zechariah receives a word from the Lord with an exhortation: "Turn back to me," says the Lord, "and I will turn back to you." The people are warned not to be like their ancestors, who did not listen to the prophets. In a night vision, Zechariah sees a man on a red horse standing among myrtle trees, with other horses behind him. These have been sent by the Lord to patrol the earth and report that all the earth is at rest. The Lord responds with comforting words: He is burning with zeal for Jerusalem; he is angry with the arrogant nations, and he promises that the temple will be rebuilt and Jerusalem chosen once again.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Turn back to me"** – God's repeated call to repentance, with the promise that he will then turn toward his people.
- **The rider among the myrtle trees** – A heavenly figure who leads the Lord's messengers as they patrol the earth.
- **The 70 years** – The period of God's anger against Jerusalem, now coming to an end.
- **The Lord's zeal for Zion** – God's passionate love for his people and his city, which motivates restoration.

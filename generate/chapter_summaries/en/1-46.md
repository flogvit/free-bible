**Main Theme:** Jacob’s family moves to Egypt after God’s confirmation and promise of blessing.

**Content:** Jacob leaves Canaan and stops at Beer-Sheba, where he offers sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac. In a night vision, God confirms that Jacob may safely go to Egypt and promises that his descendants will become a great nation there. Jacob and all his relatives—70 persons in total—arrive in Egypt, where Joseph meets his father with tears. Joseph instructs his family to present themselves as shepherds to Pharaoh so they can settle in Goshen.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Beer-Sheba:** A holy place where Jacob offers sacrifices and receives God’s confirmation before leaving the Promised Land.
- **Night Vision:** God’s revelation to Jacob, promising His presence and future greatness for the people.
- **Seventy Souls:** The number of Jacob’s descendants who came to Egypt, symbolizing a complete nation.
- **Goshen:** The region in Egypt where the family was settled, chosen because shepherds were detestable to the Egyptians.

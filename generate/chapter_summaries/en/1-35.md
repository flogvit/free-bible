**Main Theme:** Jacob returns to Bethel where God confirms the covenant and gives him the name Israel, while the chapter also marks important deaths and family changes.

**Content:** God commands Jacob to go to Bethel and build an altar. Before the journey, Jacob purifies his household of foreign gods. At Bethel, God reveals Himself as El Shaddai, gives Jacob the name Israel, and renews the promises concerning land and descendants. Rachel dies while giving birth to Benjamin, and Reuben dishonors his father by sleeping with Bilhah. Isaac dies at 180 years old and is buried by Esau and Jacob.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bethel** – The place where God revealed Himself to Jacob both during his flight and upon his return, and where Jacob builds an altar.
- **Israel** – Jacob's new name, marking his identity as the patriarch of God's people.
- **Foreign gods** – The idols that the household had to give up and bury before they could approach God.
- **Benjamin** – Jacob's youngest son, born at Rachel's death; she named him Ben-Oni ("son of my sorrow"), but Jacob called him Benjamin ("son of my right hand").

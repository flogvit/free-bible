**Main Theme:** The genealogies of the eastern tribes (Reuben, Gad, and half-Manasseh) and the lineage of Levi, with a focus on faithfulness and apostasy.

**Content:** Reuben lost his birthright to Joseph's sons because he defiled his father's bed. The three eastern tribes won a great victory over the Hagrites because they cried out to God and trusted in Him during battle. Later, due to idolatry, they were taken into exile by the Assyrians. The chapter concludes with the lineage of Levi and the high priestly line from Aaron to Jehozadak, who was exiled to Babylon.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Birthright (בְּכֹרָה):** Reuben's loss of his birthright shows that privileges can be forfeited through sin.
- **Trust in God:** The tribes' victory over the Hagrites came because they cried out to God and trusted in Him (v. 20).
- **Exile (גֹּלָה):** Both the eastern tribes and Jehozadak were taken into exile as a result of sin and apostasy.
- **High Priestly Line:** The genealogy from Aaron to Jehozadak confirms the legitimate priestly line through Eleazar.

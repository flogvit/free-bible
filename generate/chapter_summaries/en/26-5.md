**Main Theme:** God announces through a symbolic action that Jerusalem will be punished with pestilence, famine, the sword, and scattering because of the people’s rebellion and idolatry.

**Content:** Ezekiel is commanded to shave his head and beard with a sharp sword and divide the hair into three parts: one part is burned, one is struck with the sword, and one is scattered to the wind. A small remnant is bound in his cloak, but even some of these are thrown into the fire. God explains that Jerusalem has been more rebellious than the surrounding nations, and therefore the punishment will be uniquely severe—including cannibalism during the siege. God will pour out His wrath through pestilence, famine, the sword, and wild animals, so that the people will recognize that the Lord has spoken.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hair divided into three parts:** Symbolizes Jerusalem’s population who will be struck by pestilence/famine, the sword, and scattering among the nations.
- **The razor:** The sharp sword represents God’s judgment that will strike the people completely.
- **Jerusalem in the midst of the nations:** God placed Jerusalem at the center, yet the city became more rebellious than the surrounding nations.
- **"I, the Lord, have spoken":** A repeated formula emphasizing the inevitability of judgment and God’s sovereignty.

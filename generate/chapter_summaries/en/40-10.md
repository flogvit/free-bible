**Main Theme:** Jesus sends out the twelve apostles with authority and instructions, preparing them for opposition and persecution.

**Content:** Jesus calls his twelve disciples and gives them power to drive out evil spirits and heal the sick. He first sends them only to the lost sheep of Israel to proclaim that the kingdom of heaven is near. Jesus warns of coming persecution but urges them to fear God alone and trust in his care. He emphasizes that following him requires radical loyalty—taking up one's cross—and promises reward for those who receive the disciples.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Sheep among wolves:** The disciples are sent out defenseless into a hostile world, yet called to be wise as serpents and innocent as doves.
- **Not peace, but a sword:** Jesus' message will create division, even within families, requiring that loyalty to him come first.
- **Take up your cross:** Following him means accepting suffering and self-denial for his sake.
- **Sparrows and hairs:** God has complete knowledge and care for his own; therefore, the disciples should not fear people.

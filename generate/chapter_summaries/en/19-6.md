**Main Theme:** A lament in which David, in deep distress, pleads for God's mercy and healing, ending with the assurance that his prayer has been heard.

**Content:** David implores God not to rebuke him in anger but to show mercy because he is weak and his soul is in anguish. He describes his suffering with vivid imagery—nightly weeping that wets his bed and eyes weakened by sorrow. The psalm turns from complaint to certainty: the Lord has heard his cry, and all evildoers must turn away. His enemies will be put to shame.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **God's anger and mercy (חֵסֶד):** David asks to be spared God's discipline in wrath and appeals to His faithful love.
- **The realm of the dead (שְׁאוֹל):** In death, no one praises God—an argument for why He should deliver him.
- **Tears and weeping:** Intense images of nighttime sorrow where the bed is soaked with tears, expressing deep suffering.
- **Enemies:** Those who do wrong must flee, for God has heard David's prayer—they will be put to shame.

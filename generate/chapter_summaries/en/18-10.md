**Main Theme:** Job laments his distress to God and asks why God, who created him with care, now pursues him.

**Content:** Job expresses his bitterness and asks God to explain why He is bringing a case against him (vv. 1-2). He reminds God that he was formed with care—shaped like clay, clothed with skin and bones, and given life and grace (vv. 8-12). Yet Job feels that God watches him closely to find sin and punishes him whether he is guilty or innocent (vv. 13-17). Job wishes he had never been born and asks for a moment of peace before he goes to the land of darkness and the shadow of death (vv. 18-22).

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Clay and dust (v. 9):** Job reminds God of human frailty—formed from clay and destined to return to dust.
- **Milk and curds (v. 10):** A poetic image for fetal development, showing God's intimate role in creation.
- **Land of darkness (vv. 21-22):** The realm of the dead is described as a place of total darkness, without order, where Job will soon go.

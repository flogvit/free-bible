**Main Theme:** A genealogy of King David's descendants from his sons to the generations after the exile.

**Content:** The chapter first lists David's six sons born in Hebron and then nine sons born in Jerusalem, including Solomon. David reigned for 7.5 years in Hebron and 33 years in Jerusalem. Then the royal line of Solomon is traced through the kings of Judah down to Josiah and his sons. The chapter concludes with the descendants of Jeconiah (taken into exile) and Zerubbabel, who led the return from Babylon.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The House of David:** The royal lineage to whom God promised an eternal kingdom.
- **Hebron and Jerusalem:** The two cities where David reigned and had his sons—first as king over a tribe, then as king over the whole nation.
- **Zerubbabel:** A descendant of David who led the Jews back from exile, a sign that the lineage continued despite disaster.

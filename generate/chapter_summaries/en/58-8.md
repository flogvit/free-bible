**Main Theme:** Jesus is the high priest of a new and better covenant that replaces the old, inadequate covenant.

**Content:** Jesus serves as high priest in the heavenly sanctuary—the true tabernacle that the Lord himself has set up. His service is far superior to the earthly one because he is the mediator of a better covenant, established on better promises. The old covenant was flawed, and God promised through the prophet a new covenant in which he will write his laws in people’s minds and hearts, forgive their sins, and be known by all. By calling this covenant "new," God has made the first one obsolete.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **High Priest in Heaven:** Jesus sits at God’s right hand and serves in the true, heavenly sanctuary.
- **The New Covenant:** A better covenant with better promises, where God writes his laws on the heart and completely forgives sin.
- **Shadow and Copy:** The earthly sanctuary was only a shadow of the heavenly reality.
- **Forgiveness:** Under the new covenant, God promises no longer to remember people’s sins.

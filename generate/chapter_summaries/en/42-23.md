**Main Theme:** Jesus’ suffering, death, and burial as the innocent and righteous one who gives his life.

**Content:** Jesus is brought before Pilate and Herod, both of whom find him innocent, but Pilate yields to the crowd’s demand and hands Jesus over for crucifixion. On the way to Golgotha, Jesus warns the daughters of Jerusalem about coming judgment, and Simon of Cyrene carries the cross. At the cross, Jesus asks forgiveness for his executioners and promises paradise to the repentant thief. At Jesus’ death, darkness covers the land and the temple curtain is torn; the centurion confesses that Jesus was righteous. Joseph of Arimathea buries Jesus, and the women prepare to anoint him after the Sabbath.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Barabbas:** A rebel and murderer whom the people choose over the innocent Jesus, illustrating humanity’s wrong choice.
- **Temple Curtain:** Torn at Jesus’ death, symbolizing that the way to God is now open for all.
- **Paradise:** Jesus promises the repentant thief that he will be with him in paradise on that same day, showing salvation as a gift.
- **The Righteous One:** The centurion’s confession confirms Jesus’ innocence and fulfills Luke’s portrayal of Jesus as the suffering righteous one.
- **The Tomb:** A new, unused tomb where Jesus’ body is laid, pointing forward to the resurrection.

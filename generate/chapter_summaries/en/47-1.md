**Main Theme:** Paul praises God for comfort in suffering and defends his integrity before the church in Corinth.

**Content:** Paul begins by thanking God, "the Father of all comfort," who comforts us in all our troubles so that we can comfort others. He shares about extreme suffering in Asia which taught him to rely on God, who raises the dead. Paul defends his changed travel plans and emphasizes that God's promises find their "Yes" in Christ. God has confirmed believers by anointing them, sealing them, and giving the Spirit as a guarantee in their hearts.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Comfort (παράκλησις):** God's comfort in suffering that enables us to comfort others.
- **Suffering (θλῖψις/παθήματα):** Trials that serve spiritual growth and fellowship with Christ.
- **Yes and Amen:** All of God's promises are confirmed in Christ—God is faithful.
- **Guarantee of the Spirit (ἀρραβών):** The Holy Spirit is God's down payment and guarantee of our final redemption.

**Main Theme:** God reveals the future through a dream about the course of earthly kingdoms and His own eternal kingdom.

**Content:** King Nebuchadnezzar has a disturbing dream and demands that his wise men tell him both the dream and its interpretation—under threat of death. When no one can answer, God reveals the secret to Daniel after prayer. The dream shows a statue made of different metals, which is shattered by a stone. Daniel explains that it represents successive earthly kingdoms that will ultimately be replaced by God's eternal kingdom. The king exalts Daniel and acknowledges his God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Statue:** Represents four successive earthly kingdoms with decreasing value from gold to iron/clay.
- **The Stone:** God's kingdom, which shatters all human kingdoms and fills the whole earth.
- **The Secret (raza):** Only God can reveal what is hidden, not human wisdom.
- **The God of Heaven:** Daniel confesses God as the One who controls times, removes kings, and sets up kings.

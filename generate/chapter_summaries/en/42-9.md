**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals his identity as the Messiah and teaches what true discipleship entails.

**Content:** Jesus sends out the twelve with power to preach and heal, then feeds five thousand with five loaves and two fish. Peter confesses Jesus as God's Messiah, and Jesus predicts his suffering, death, and resurrection. On the mountain, Jesus is transfigured alongside Moses and Elijah, and God declares him to be his chosen Son. After healing a possessed boy, Jesus sets his face toward Jerusalem, and the chapter concludes with radical demands for discipleship.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Kingdom of God:** The central message the disciples are sent out to proclaim, and which Jesus himself teaches about.
- **Take up your cross daily:** Jesus' call to self-denial and radical discipleship for all who wish to follow him.
- **The Transfiguration:** Jesus is revealed in glory together with Moses and Elijah, who speak of his "exodus" in Jerusalem.
- **Son of Man:** Jesus' self-designation that emphasizes both his humanity and his coming suffering.

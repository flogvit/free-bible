**Main Theme:** The chapter gives instructions regarding the sanctuary service and establishes laws concerning blasphemy and just retribution.

**Content:** The Lord commands that pure olive oil be used for the lampstand, which is to burn continuously, and that twelve loaves of showbread be placed before the Lord’s presence every Sabbath. A man with an Israelite mother and an Egyptian father blasphemes God’s name and is stoned according to the Lord’s judgment. The chapter establishes the law of retribution—life for life, eye for eye—and emphasizes that this same law applies equally to both native-born Israelites and foreigners.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lampstand (menorah):** A symbol of God’s enduring presence, with lamps burning continuously before the Lord.
- **Showbread:** Twelve loaves representing the twelve tribes, placed as a perpetual covenant.
- **Blasphemy:** To revile or curse God’s name results in the death penalty by stoning.
- **Eye for eye (lex talionis):** A principle of proportional retribution ensuring just punishment.
- **One law for all:** The same legal principles apply to Israelites and foreigners—God judges with justice.

**Main Theme:** Isaiah 12 is a song of praise that honors God for his salvation and comfort after his anger has turned away.

**Content:** The chapter consists of two songs of praise: first, an individual thanksgiving for the comfort and salvation God has given (vv. 1–3), followed by a collective call to praise the Lord and proclaim his mighty deeds among the nations (vv. 4–6). The inhabitants of Zion are called to rejoice because the Holy One of Israel is great in their midst.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Wells of Salvation** (v. 3): The image of drawing water with joy symbolizes an abundance of salvation and blessing from God.
- **The Holy One of Israel** (v. 6): A central concept in Isaiah that describes God's holiness and his presence among his people.
- **God’s Anger Turned Away** (v. 1): The transition from judgment to comfort—God’s anger is temporary, but his grace endures.
- **Proclaim Among the Nations** (v. 4): The call to make God’s mighty deeds known to all nations.

**Main Theme:** Ruth meets Boaz in the field and experiences God's care through his kindness and protection.

**Content:** Ruth goes out to glean grain and happens upon the field of Boaz, a wealthy relative of Elimelech. Boaz notices her, shows her unusual kindness, and protects her among his workers. He blesses her for her loyalty to Naomi and asks that the God of Israel reward her. Ruth gathers an entire ephah of barley, and when Naomi hears that it was Boaz, she praises the Lord's faithfulness and reveals that he is a kinsman-redeemer for the family.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Gleaning (לקט):** A right granted to the poor and foreigners in Israel, demonstrating God's social care within the law.
- **God's wings (כְּנָפָיו):** Boaz's image of God's protection under which Ruth has sought refuge.
- **Kinsman-redeemer (גֹּאֵל):** A close relative responsible for protecting the family's property and honor—pointing forward to God's salvation.
- **Hesed (חֶסֶד):** God's faithful love, which Naomi sees at work through Boaz's kindness toward both the living and the dead.

**Main Theme:** Peter’s confession of Jesus as the Messiah and Jesus’ teaching on discipleship.

**Content:** The Pharisees and Sadducees demand a sign, but Jesus rejects them and warns his disciples against their teaching. At Caesarea Philippi, Peter confesses that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of the living God, and Jesus promises to build his church on this rock and give Peter the keys to the kingdom of heaven. Jesus then predicts his suffering, death, and resurrection, and rebukes Peter for trying to prevent him. Finally, Jesus teaches about the demands of discipleship: self-denial, taking up one’s cross, and being willing to lose one’s life for his sake.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Leaven:** A picture of the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees that spreads and influences negatively.
- **The Rock (petra):** The foundation on which Jesus will build his church, connected to Peter’s confession.
- **The Keys to the Kingdom of Heaven:** Authority given to Peter to bind and loose, with heavenly confirmation.
- **The Cross:** A symbol of self-denial and the radical discipleship that Jesus requires of his followers.

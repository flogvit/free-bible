**Main Theme:** The confrontation between the true prophet Jeremiah and the false prophet Hananiah regarding Babylonian domination.

**Content:** In the fourth year of King Zedekiah's reign, Hananiah prophesied in the temple that God would break the yoke of Babylon within two years and restore the temple articles and the exiles. Jeremiah responded that prophets have traditionally proclaimed judgment, and a prophecy of peace is only confirmed when it comes to pass. Hananiah symbolically broke the wooden yoke from Jeremiah's neck, but God replied that the wooden yoke would be replaced by an iron yoke. Jeremiah declared that Hananiah would die because he had spoken rebellion against the Lord, and Hananiah died that same year in the seventh month.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Yoke (mottah):** A symbol of Babylonian domination; the wooden yoke that was broken is replaced by an iron yoke as a sign that submission will become heavier.
- **False Prophet:** Hananiah spoke in the Lord's name without being sent by Him, giving the people false hope.
- **Two Years:** Hananiah's deadline for deliverance, which would not be fulfilled.
- **True Prophecy:** Jeremiah teaches that a prophecy of peace is confirmed only by its fulfillment, whereas prophecies of judgment have a long tradition.

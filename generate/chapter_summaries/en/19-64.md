**Main Theme:** A prayer for protection against enemies' hidden attacks, with the certainty that God will intervene and turn their evil back upon them.

**Content:** David asks God to preserve his life from enemies who sharpen their tongues like swords and shoot bitter words like arrows at the innocent in secret. They plot evil and think no one sees them. But God shoots His arrow against them, and their own tongue leads to their downfall. All people will see this, fear, and proclaim God's deeds, while the righteous rejoice in the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Tongue like a sword / arrows:** Describes the enemies' malicious words as weapons intended to harm the innocent.
- **Hidden snares:** The enemies operate in secret and believe they are invisible to God.
- **God's arrow:** God's sudden and unexpected judgment against the wicked, mirroring their own attacks.
- **The righteous rejoice:** God's intervention leads those who are sincere of heart to praise Him and take refuge in Him.

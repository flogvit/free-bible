**Main Theme:** The seven bowls of wrath are poured out as God's final judgment upon the earth and the followers of the beast.

**Content:** Seven angels receive command from the temple to empty God's bowls of wrath over the earth. The plagues strike those who bear the mark of the beast: sores, blood in the sea and rivers, a scorching sun, darkness over the kingdom of the beast, and the drying up of the Euphrates River to prepare the way for the kings from the east. Demonic spirits gather the kings of the world for battle at Armageddon. The seventh bowl triggers an unprecedented earthquake, the fall of Babylon, and enormous hail – yet instead of repenting, people mock God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bowls of Wrath:** The seven bowls represent the fulfillment of God's wrath against evil.
- **Armageddon:** The place where the kings of the world gather for the great battle against God.
- **Babylon:** The great city which God remembers to give her the cup of His wrath.
- **Blasphemy Without Repentance:** Despite the plagues, people repeatedly mock God and refuse to repent.

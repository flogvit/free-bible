**Main Theme:** A prayer of David for God's grace, protection, and guidance in the face of enemies.

**Content:** David cries out to God as poor and needy, asking for grace and protection. He confesses God's uniqueness—there is no god like the Lord, and all nations will one day worship Him. Amidst the threat from arrogant enemies, David holds fast to God's mercy and faithfulness, praying for an undivided heart that fears the name of the Lord.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **חֶסֶד (grace/faithful love):** God's abundant and enduring goodness toward those who call on Him (vv. 5, 13, 15).
- **עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן (poor and needy):** The psalmist's humble self-description as the basis for prayer (v. 1).
- **יַחֵד לְבָבִי (unite my heart):** A prayer for wholeness and focus in reverence for God (v. 11).
- **אֵל רַחוּם וְחַנּוּן (merciful and gracious God):** The description of God's character from Exodus 34:6 (v. 15).

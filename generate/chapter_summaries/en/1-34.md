**Main Theme:** The Rape of Dinah and the Brothers' Revenge on Shechem.

**Content:** Dinah, daughter of Jacob and Leah, is raped by Shechem, son of the Hivite Hamor, the prince of the land. Shechem wishes to marry Dinah, and Hamor negotiates with Jacob and his sons. Jacob's sons respond with deceit and demand that all the men in the city be circumcised. On the third day, while the men are suffering from pain, Simeon and Levi kill all the men of the city and take Dinah back. Jacob's sons plunder the city. Jacob fears the consequences, but his sons defend themselves: "Should he treat our sister like a prostitute?"

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rape (עִנָּה):** Shechem's assault on Dinah – the text uses a verb meaning to "dishonor" or "humiliate" her, a serious violation against the family.
- **Circumcision:** Jacob's sons deceitfully use the sign of the covenant as a weapon for revenge.
- **Deceit (מִרְמָה):** The brothers respond "with deceit" – a dark echo of Jacob's own history.
- **Shame and Honor:** The conflict centers on the family's honor and "outrage in Israel" (נְבָלָה).

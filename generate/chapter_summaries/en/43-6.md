**Main Theme:** Jesus reveals himself as the bread of life from heaven—those who believe in him and receive him will have eternal life.

**Content:** Near the time of Passover, Jesus feeds five thousand men with five loaves and two fish, then walks on water to reach his disciples. In Capernaum, he teaches the crowd that he is the true bread from heaven who gives eternal life, in contrast to the manna their ancestors ate yet still died. Jesus' words about eating his flesh and drinking his blood cause many of his disciples to leave him, but Peter confesses that Jesus has the words of eternal life and is the Holy One of God.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Bread of Life (ὁ ἄρτος τῆς ζωῆς):** Jesus uses the miracle of the loaves to reveal himself as the spiritual bread that gives eternal life, in contrast to physical food that perishes.
- **To eat and drink (τρώγων/πίνων):** The image of receiving Jesus completely—his flesh and blood represent full participation in his work of salvation.
- **Ἐγώ εἰμι (I am):** Jesus' self-revelation using words that recall God's name, used both on the sea and in the bread discourse.
- **The Father draws (ἑλκύσῃ):** No one can come to Jesus unless the Father draws them—the gift of salvation comes from God.

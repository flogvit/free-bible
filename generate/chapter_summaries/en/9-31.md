**Main Theme:** Saul's tragic death in battle against the Philistines marks the end of his reign as king.

**Content:** The Israelites suffer a crushing defeat by the Philistines at Mount Gilboa, where Saul's three sons—Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua—are killed. Severely wounded by archers, Saul takes his own life to avoid being dishonored by the enemy. The Philistines hang their bodies on the wall of Beth Shan, but the men of Jabesh Gilead retrieve them during the night, burn the bodies, bury the bones under the tamarisk tree, and fast for seven days.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Mount Gilboa:** The site of Israel's defeat and Saul's fall, a turning point in Israel's history.
- **The Sword:** Symbolizing Saul's desperate choice to take his own life rather than be dishonored.
- **The Wall of Beth Shan:** Where the Philistines hung the bodies as a sign of victory and humiliation.
- **Jabesh Gilead:** The city that showed gratitude and loyalty by risking their lives to give Saul a dignified burial.

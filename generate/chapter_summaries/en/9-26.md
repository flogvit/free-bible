**Main Theme:** David spares Saul's life a second time and shows respect for "the Lord's anointed."

**Content:** The Ziphites warn Saul of David's hiding place, and Saul pursues him with 3,000 men. David and Abishai sneak into the camp at night while a deep sleep from the Lord has fallen upon everyone. David refuses to kill Saul because he is "the Lord's anointed," taking only the spear and water jug as proof. From a hilltop, David confronts Abner and Saul, who confesses his sin and blesses David.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord's anointed (משיח יהוה):** David's theological basis for sparing Saul—no one may lay hands on the one God has anointed without incurring guilt.
- **The spear and water jug:** Concrete evidence of David's mercy and self-control, taken from beside Saul's head.
- **A deep sleep from the Lord (תַּרְדֵּמַת יְהוָה):** God's intervention that made David's access to Saul possible.
- **The Ziphites:** Local inhabitants who betray David's hiding place to Saul.

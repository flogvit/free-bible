**Main Theme:** A lament about the transience of life and a prayer for God's grace amid suffering, acknowledging human frailty.

**Content:** The psalmist resolves to keep his tongue in check while the wicked are near, but his inner pain and unrest burn until he must speak. He asks God to help him understand how short and fleeting life is—as brief as a handbreadth, like a breath, or a shadowy image. He acknowledges that God's discipline for sin wears down humanity and prays for deliverance from his transgressions. As a stranger and guest on earth, he asks God for relief before he departs.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Hevel (הֶבֶל) – breath/vanity:** Human life is compared to a breath—fleeting and transient.
- **Shadowy image (צֶלֶם):** Humanity walks like a shadow, without substance or permanence.
- **Stranger and guest (גֵר/תּוֹשָׁב):** The psalmist sees himself as a temporary resident with God, having no permanent home on earth.
- **Fire (אֵשׁ):** Inner pain and thoughts that burn until they must be expressed.

**Main Theme:** The priest Jehoiada leads a successful uprising against Queen Athaliah and restores the Davidic line by installing the young Joash as king.

**Content:** In the seventh year, Jehoiada gathers military leaders, Levites, and family heads and makes a covenant with them in the house of God. He organizes guard duty around the temple, arms the officers with King David's weapons, and brings forward Prince Joash, who is crowned and anointed as king. When Athaliah hears the shouting and sees the king, she cries out "Treason!", but is led out and executed at the Horse Gate. Jehoiada makes a covenant between the Lord, the king, and the people, and Baal's temple along with its altar and idols is destroyed.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Covenant:** Jehoiada makes several covenants—first with the leaders, then between God, the king, and the people—which mark a renewal of the relationship with the Lord.
- **David's Weapons:** The weapons from King David symbolize continuity in the Davidic line and legitimacy for the new king.
- **The Temple:** God's house is the center of the coronation and stands in contrast to Baal's temple, which is torn down.
- **The Crown and the Testimony:** Joash receives both the royal crown and the testimony (the law), showing that the king must rule under God's word.

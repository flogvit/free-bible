**Main Theme:** God makes a formal covenant with Abram, promising him numerous descendants and a land from the river of Egypt to the Euphrates.

**Content:** God reveals Himself to Abram in a vision and promises to be his shield and great reward. Abram expresses concern about his childlessness—his servant Eliezer would inherit him—but God promises descendants as numerous as the stars. Abram believed the Lord, who counted it to him as righteousness. God predicts that his descendants will be strangers and oppressed for 400 years but will return in the fourth generation. The covenant is confirmed when God, symbolized by a smoking oven and a flaming torch, passes between the divided animals.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Stars:** A symbol of the countless descendants God promises Abram (v.5).
- **Righteousness by Faith:** Abram's faith in God's promise was counted to him as righteousness (v.6)—a central verse in biblical theology.
- **Covenant Ritual:** The animals are divided, and God passes between them as a smoking oven and a flaming torch—a unilateral covenant where God alone binds Himself (v.17).

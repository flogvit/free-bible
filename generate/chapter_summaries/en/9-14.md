**Main Theme:** Jonathan’s bold attack in faith gives Israel victory, while Saul’s unwise oath creates a crisis.

**Content:** Jonathan and his armor-bearer attack the Philistine outpost in faith that God can save "with few or many." God grants victory, and panic spreads through the enemy camp. Saul’s oath to fast leads to Jonathan unknowingly breaking it, and the people sin by eating meat with blood. Saul intends to execute Jonathan, but the people rescue him. The chapter concludes with an overview of Saul’s family and his successful wars.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"With few or many"** – Jonathan’s confession of faith that God is not limited by numbers (v.6).
- **The trembling of God (חֶרְדַּת אֱלֹהִים)** – A supernatural panic that struck the Philistines (v.15).
- **The Oath and the Honey** – Saul’s unwise oath that harmed his own son and exhausted the people.
- **Casting Lots (תָמִים)** – Used to reveal God’s will and find the "guilty" one (vv.41–42).

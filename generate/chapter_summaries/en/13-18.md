**Main Theme:** David expands his kingdom through military victories granted by the Lord.

**Content:** David defeats the Philistines, Moabites, King Hadadezer of Zobah, the Arameans from Damascus, and Edom. He takes large amounts of bronze from the cities of Tibhath and Kun, as well as golden shields from Hadadezer's men. King Tou of Hamat sends gifts to congratulate David on his victory. David dedicates all the war spoils to the Lord, and the text repeatedly emphasizes that "the Lord gave David victory wherever he went." The chapter concludes with an overview of David's officials.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"The Lord gave victory"** – A repeated statement (vv. 6, 13) showing that David's military success came from God.
- **Tribute and subjugation** – The peoples became "servants" who paid tribute, a sign of David's dominion.
- **Dedication of war spoils** – David consecrated the gold, silver, and bronze to the Lord, which were later used for the temple.
- **David's administration** – The list of officials (vv. 14–17) shows a well-organized kingdom characterized by justice and righteousness.

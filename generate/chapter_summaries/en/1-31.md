**Main Theme:** Jacob breaks with Laban and makes a covenant with him at Gilead.

**Content:** God commands Jacob to return to Canaan after Laban’s attitude has changed. Jacob flees with his wives and all that he owns, while Rachel secretly steals her father’s household gods (teraphim). Laban pursues them but is warned by God in a dream. At Gilead, Laban confronts Jacob, but after a heated exchange of words they make a non-aggression pact, marked by a heap of stones.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Teraphim (household gods):** Religious objects Rachel steals from Laban, possibly connected to inheritance rights or protection.
- **The Heap of Stones at Gilead:** A witness to the covenant between Jacob and Laban, called "Gal-Ed" (the heap of witness).
- **The Covenant (berit):** A mutual agreement not to cross the boundary to harm one another, with God as witness.
- **The Fear of Isaac (pachad Yitschak):** A divine name by which Jacob swears, referring to God’s protection over Isaac’s descendants.

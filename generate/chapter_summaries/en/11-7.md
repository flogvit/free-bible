**Main Theme:** Solomon completes the construction of his palace and equips the Lord's temple with skillfully crafted bronze and gold articles.

**Content:** Solomon builds his palace over 13 years, including the House of the Forest of Lebanon, the Hall of Pillars, and the Hall of Judgment. He brings Hiram from Tyre, a master craftsman in bronze, who makes the two large pillars named Jakin and Boaz, the bronze sea supported by twelve bronze oxen, ten stands with basins for washing, and various other utensils. Solomon also makes sacred gold articles: the golden altar, the table for the bread of the Presence, and ten lampstands. All the work is completed, and David's holy gifts are placed in the treasury of the temple.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Jakin and Boaz:** The two bronze pillars at the entrance to the temple, 18 cubits high, symbolizing God's strength and stability.
- **The Bronze Sea:** A large round basin supported by twelve bronze oxen, used for the priests' cleansing.
- **Hiram of Tyre:** The skilled craftsman filled with wisdom and knowledge to carry out all the bronze work.
- **The Gold Articles:** The altar, the lampstands, and the table for the bread of the Presence made of pure gold demonstrate the holiness of the temple.

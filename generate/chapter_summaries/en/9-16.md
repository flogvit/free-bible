**Main Theme:** God chooses and anoints David as the new king over Israel, while Saul loses God’s Spirit.

**Content:** After rejecting Saul, God sends Samuel to Bethlehem to anoint a new king from among Jesse’s sons. Samuel learns that God looks at the heart, not outward appearance, and anoints the youngest son, David, a shepherd. God’s Spirit comes upon David, while an evil spirit torments Saul. David is brought to Saul’s court to play the lyre and soothe his distress.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Anointing:** Samuel anoints David with oil from a horn, as a sign of God’s choice for the kingship.
- **"God looks at the heart":** God rejects the older sons and shows that His criteria are inner character, not outward appearance.
- **God’s Spirit:** The Spirit leaves Saul and comes upon David—a shift in power showing whom God supports.
- **Lyre playing:** David’s music drives away the evil spirit from Saul and brings him into the king’s presence.

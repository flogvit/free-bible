**Main Theme:** Christians are called to live holy lives, serve one another with spiritual gifts, and face suffering with joy as a participation in Christ's sufferings.

**Content:** Peter urges believers to break away from their former pagan lifestyle and live according to God's will. Since the end is near, they should be sober-minded in prayer, show sincere love and hospitality, and serve one another with their spiritual gifts for God's glory. They should not be surprised by the fiery trial of suffering but rejoice in sharing Christ's sufferings. Judgment begins with the household of God, and those who are suffering should entrust their souls to the faithful Creator.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Arm yourselves (ὁπλίσασθε):** Christians must equip themselves with the same mindset as Christ, who suffered in the flesh.
- **Fiery trial (πύρωσις):** Suffering is not something foreign but a test that purifies faith.
- **Stewards of God's grace (οἰκονόμοι χάριτος):** Every believer has received spiritual gifts to be used in serving others.
- **Judgment begins with the household of God:** Believers will be judged first, which underscores the seriousness of living a holy life.

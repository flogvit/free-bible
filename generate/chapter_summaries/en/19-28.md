**Main Theme:** David cries out to God for help and expresses his trust in him as rock and shield.

**Content:** David implores the Lord not to be silent but to hear his cry when he lifts his hands toward the sanctuary. He asks to be separated from the wicked who speak peace yet have evil in their hearts, and that they may receive recompense for their deeds. The psalm turns from lament to praise as David confesses that the Lord has heard him, and concludes with a prayer for salvation and blessing for God's people.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Rock (צור):** God as a firm and unchanging refuge (v. 1)
- **Shield (מגן):** God as protector of those who trust in him (v. 7)
- **The Most Holy Place (דביר):** The place of God's presence where prayer is directed (v. 2)
- **The Anointed One (משיח):** The Lord is salvation for his anointed, pointing to the king and the Messiah (v. 8)

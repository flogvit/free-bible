**Main Theme:** A prayer psalm in which David, in deep distress, pleads for God’s faithful help, guidance, and deliverance from his enemies.

**Content:** The psalmist begins by invoking God’s faithfulness and righteousness, acknowledging that no human being can stand justified before Him. Persecuted by enemies and driven into darkness, he reminds himself of God’s past deeds and reaches out to God as dry ground longs for water. He asks for swift help, guidance on the right path, and that God in His mercy would destroy his enemies—for the psalmist is God’s servant.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Dry ground (v.6):** The psalmist’s soul longs for God as parched earth thirsts for rain.
- **The place of darkness (v.3):** His enemy has driven him into darkness, like those who have long been dead.
- **Level ground (v.10):** God’s good Spirit will lead him on safe and straight paths.
- **Morning mercy (v.8):** The psalmist asks to hear of God’s faithful love in the morning.

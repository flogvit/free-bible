**Main Theme:** Joshua organizes the survey of the remaining land and assigns the tribe of Benjamin their inheritance by casting lots at Shiloh.

**Content:** Israel gathers at Shiloh where the Tabernacle is set up. Joshua rebukes the seven tribes that have not yet taken possession of their allotted land, and sends out 21 men (three from each tribe) to survey the land into seven parts. After the survey, Joshua casts lots for the Lord at Shiloh. The chapter then describes Benjamin's borders in detail—between Judah to the south and the tribes of Joseph to the north—and lists the tribe's 26 cities, including Jericho, Bethel, and Jerusalem.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Shiloh:** The place where the Tabernacle is set up and becomes Israel's religious center for the casting of lots.
- **Casting Lots:** The method used to distribute the land fairly "before the Lord," showing that the distribution is divinely guided.
- **Benjamin:** The first of the seven remaining tribes whose inheritance is described, strategically located between Judah and Ephraim.
- **Surveying:** The sent-out men write down the land in a book, a systematic process for fair distribution.

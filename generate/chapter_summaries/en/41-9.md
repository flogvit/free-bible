**Main Theme:** Jesus' divine glory is revealed, and he teaches about faith, servanthood, and radical discipleship.

**Content:** The chapter opens with the Transfiguration on the mountain, where Jesus is transfigured before Peter, James, and John, and God's voice confirms him as "my Son, the beloved." Jesus heals a demon-possessed boy after the disciples fail to do so, emphasizing that all things are possible for those who believe. He predicts his death and resurrection for the second time, but the disciples do not understand. Jesus teaches that whoever wants to be greatest must be everyone's servant, and he strongly warns against causing others to stumble.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Transfiguration (μετεμορφώθη):** Jesus reveals his divine glory on the mountain, confirmed by God's voice from the cloud.
- **"I believe; help my unbelief!":** The father's honest prayer shows the struggle between faith and doubt, and Jesus' gracious response.
- **Servant (διάκονος):** Whoever wants to be greatest in God's kingdom must be everyone's servant and welcome the least.
- **Gehenna (γέενναν):** A serious warning about destruction, used to emphasize the importance of radical repentance.

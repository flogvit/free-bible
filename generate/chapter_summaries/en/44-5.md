**Main Theme:** The early church's growth and trials through God's judgment on hypocrisy, the apostles' powerful ministry, and opposition from religious leaders.

**Content:** Ananias and Sapphira died after lying to the Holy Spirit by withholding part of the proceeds from a property sale, which brought great fear upon the church. The apostles performed many signs and wonders, and people came from surrounding cities with sick individuals who were all healed. When the apostles were imprisoned by the high priest, an angel freed them and instructed them to continue preaching in the temple. The Pharisee Gamaliel persuaded the council to release them, but they were still beaten before being set free—and left the council rejoicing that they had been counted worthy to suffer dishonor for the name of Jesus.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Lying to the Holy Spirit:** The sin of Ananias and Sapphira was not withholding money, but lying to God and testing the Lord's Spirit.
- **Signs and wonders:** The apostles' healings confirmed the power of the gospel and drew large crowds.
- **Gamaliel's counsel:** The principle that God's work cannot be stopped by humans, while human projects will fail on their own.
- **Joy in suffering:** The apostles considered it an honor to suffer dishonor for the name of Jesus.
- **Obedience to God:** Peter's declaration "We must obey God rather than men" expresses the apostles' priority.

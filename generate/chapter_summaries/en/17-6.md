**Main Theme:** God’s hidden providence turns Haman’s evil plan on its head through a sleepless night.

**Content:** King Ahasuerus cannot sleep and has the royal chronicles read to him, recounting how Mordecai saved him from an assassination attempt—without receiving any reward. Eventually, Haman arrives at the palace to request Mordecai’s execution, but the king first asks him how a man the king wishes to honor should be honored. Haman, assuming the honor is meant for himself, suggests royal attire and a public procession—but must personally carry this out for Mordecai. Humiliated, Haman returns home, and his advisors warn that he will fall before the Jew Mordecai.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The King’s Sleeplessness:** An apparently random occurrence becomes the turning point in the story—God’s invisible hand at work in events.
- **The Royal Chronicles:** Written documentation of Mordecai’s loyalty becomes the instrument of his vindication.
- **Royal Honor:** Horse, robe, and public proclamation—the honor Haman coveted, he must bestow upon his enemy.
- **Haman’s Humiliation:** The irony when the proud one must serve the one he despised—a dramatic reversal in the balance of power.

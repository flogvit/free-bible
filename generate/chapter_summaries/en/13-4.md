**Main Theme:** Genealogies of the tribes of Judah and Simeon, focusing on settlements and conquests.

**Content:** The chapter first presents the descendants of Judah, including Jabez, who prays a remarkable prayer for blessing and protection. Then the descendants of Simeon are listed, along with their cities and villages up to the time of David. The chapter concludes with the Simeonites' conquests of the land of the Hamites and the Amalekites.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Jabez's Prayer (vv. 9–10):** A short but powerful prayer in which Jabez asks for blessing, expanded territory, and protection from harm—and God grants his request.
- **Genealogies:** Detailed lists of families and clans that document Israel's identity and their inheritance rights to the land.
- **Simeonite Settlements (vv. 28–33):** An overview of the cities where the Simeonites lived, showing the tribe's geographical distribution.
- **Conquest (vv. 39–43):** The Simeonites captured fertile grazing land from the Hamites and defeated the Amalekites at Mount Seir during the reign of King Hezekiah.

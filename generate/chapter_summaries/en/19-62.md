**Main Theme:** A declaration of trust that the soul finds rest in God alone, who is the only source of salvation and security.

**Content:** David confesses that his soul is silent before God alone—from him comes salvation and protection. He warns against enemies who seek to overthrow him, and against trusting in oppression, plunder, or wealth. Instead, he encourages the people to pour out their hearts before God and trust in him at all times, for power and lovingkindness belong to God, who rewards each according to their deeds.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **"Only" (אַךְ):** Repeated six times to emphasize exclusive trust in God alone.
- **Rock and fortress (צוּר, מִשְׂגָּב):** Images of God as a solid and impregnable refuge.
- **Silence (דּוּמִיָּה):** The soul's quiet rest and waiting on God in trust.
- **Humans as breath (הֶבֶל):** A warning against trusting in people, who are empty and light on the scales.

**Main Theme:** The organization of Israel's military forces, tribal leaders, and King David's administration.

**Content:** This chapter describes the army divided into twelve rotations of 24,000 men each, serving one month at a time under their respective commanders. It then lists the leaders of Israel's twelve tribes. David did not count those under twenty years old because the Lord had promised to make Israel as numerous as the stars in the sky. The chapter concludes with administrators over the king's property and his closest advisors.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **Monthly Rotations:** Military organization where each division serves one month of the year, ensuring continuous readiness.
- **Tribal Leaders (נָגִיד):** Leaders appointed over each of Israel's tribes to represent them.
- **Administrators:** Those responsible for the king's treasuries, agriculture, vineyards, olive groves, and livestock.
- **Advisors:** Ahithophel and Hushai served as the king's advisors, with Joab as commander-in-chief of the army.

**Main Theme:** A suffering man's lament that moves from deep despair through hope in God’s faithfulness to a prayer for deliverance and justice.

**Content:** The chapter opens with an intense description of suffering under God’s discipline, where the speaker feels trapped in darkness and pursued like prey. In the midst of despair comes a turning point: the Lord’s grace never ends; his mercies are new every morning, and his faithfulness is great. The author encourages waiting patiently for the Lord and examining one’s ways to turn back. The chapter concludes with a prayer that God would see injustice and repay the enemies.

**Key Words/Images:**
- **The Lord’s steadfast love (חַסְדֵי יְהוָה):** God’s faithful love that never ends, the foundation of hope in the midst of suffering.
- **New every morning:** God’s mercies are renewed daily—an expression of God’s inexhaustible care.
- **The Lord is my portion:** The sufferer confesses that God himself is his inheritance and hope, not external circumstances.
- **Let us examine our ways:** An invitation to self-examination and repentance in response to suffering.
- **Bear and lion:** Images of God as a predator in persecution, expressing the experience of God’s wrath.

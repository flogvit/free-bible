Heavenly Father,

In Your infinite love You gave Your only Son for us,
So that we, through faith in Him, may find eternal hope and salvation.
Fill our hearts with gratitude for this incomprehensible gift,
And let our faith in Jesus be firm and unshakable.
Give us the strength to live according to Your love,
And help us to share the message of Your salvation with all we meet.
Thank You for Your promise of eternal life with You.

In Jesus' name,
Amen.

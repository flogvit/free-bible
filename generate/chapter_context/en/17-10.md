## People

The chapter mentions Mordecai in his final role as "mishne" (מִשְׁנֶה), which means "second" or "second-in-command" after the king. This title was an official Persian position equivalent to what we would today call a vizier or prime minister.

## Events and Customs

The chapter’s reference to the king having "imposed a tax on the land and the islands of the sea" (verse 1) emphasizes the extent of the empire. The expression "islands of the sea" (אִיֵּי הַיָּם) was a standard term for coastal lands and islands, especially in the western Mediterranean region. This note serves to highlight the king’s power, which now also benefits the Jews through Mordecai’s position.

## Key Terms

The expression "sefer divrei ha-yamim" (סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים) – "the book of the events of the days" – refers to the official Persian royal chronicles. The reference to these gives the narrative historical authority.

The expressions "doresh tov" (דֹּרֵשׁ טוֹב) – "seeks what is good" – and "dover shalom" (דֹבֵר שָׁלוֹם) – "speaks peace" – in verse 3 describe Mordecai’s role as spokesperson and protector for the Jewish people. These expressions summarize his character as a leader who actively works for the welfare of his people.
